ACT (Action Chunking with Transformers) is a natural first policy to reproduce when getting started with robot imitation learning.

ACT（Action Chunking with Transformers）是任何人可以复现的第一个机器人模仿学习策略。

Loss:

Loss：

$$L_{\mathrm{ACT}}=L_{\mathrm{L1}}+\beta L_{\mathrm{KL}}.$$

L1 measures action prediction error; KL constrains the difference between the latent posterior and prior.

L1 衡量动作预测的偏差，KL约束潜变量后验与先验的差异。

The image $I_t$, robot state $s_t$, and latent variable $z$ enter the backbone, which predicts an action chunk of length $K$:

图像 $I_t$、机器人状态 $s_t$ 和潜变量 $z$ 输入主干网络，一次预测长度为 $K$ 的动作块：

$$\hat A_t=f_\theta(I_t,s_t,z),\qquad A_t=(a_t,\ldots,a_{t+K-1}).$$

observation.state records the follower’s joint positions; action records target joint positions supplied by the leader. Adding a mobile base gives discrete velocity values for x.vel, y.vel, and theta.vel, making cross-entropy a natural choice.

observation.state 记录从臂的关节位置，action 记录主臂给出的目标关节位置。如果加上底盘移动的离散的速度值 x.vel、y.vel 和 theta.vel，很容易想到引入交叉熵。

x.vel, y.vel−0.15 m/s00.15 m/s
theta.vel−45 deg/s045 deg/s

For x.vel, a demonstrated speed of 0.15 m/s becomes the label “positive.” A linear classification head reads the ACT features and assigns a score to each class. These scores are logits, $u_k$; softmax converts them into probabilities $R_k$.

以 x.vel 为例，示范速度为 0.15 m/s 时，标签就是“正向”。接在 ACT 主干后的线性分类头给每个类别输出一个分数，称为 logit，记作 $u_k$；softmax 再把分数转换为概率 $R_k$。

$$R_k=\frac{e^{u_k}}{\sum_j e^{u_j}},\qquad \ell_{\mathrm{CE}}=-\ln R_y.$$

$y$ is the demonstrated class. Giving it a higher probability lowers the loss. PyTorch’s CrossEntropyLoss accepts logits directly and computes log-softmax internally.

$y$ 是示范类别。模型给它的概率越高，损失越小。PyTorch 的 CrossEntropyLoss 直接接收 logits，内部计算 log-softmax。

Representation信息如何表示

The negative logarithm has a meaning in Shannon’s information theory. Independent event probabilities multiply, while their information adds. The negative logarithm connects the two:

负对数在香农信息论中对应信息量。独立事件的概率相乘，信息量相加，负对数把这两个关系联系起来：

$$I(p)=-\log_2p,\qquad I(pq)=I(p)+I(q).$$

One head from a fair coin carries 1 bit of information; two consecutive heads carry 2 bits. Less likely outcomes carry more information. Training uses the natural logarithm and measures information in nats: multiply bits by $\ln 2$.

公平硬币一次正面携带 1 bit 信息，连续两次正面携带 2 bit。越少见的结果，信息量越大。训练使用自然对数，单位为 nat，数值等于 bit 乘以 $\ln 2$。

{{figure:information}}

Averaging information over a target distribution $P$ gives its entropy. Evaluating the same outcomes using a predicted distribution $R$ gives cross-entropy:

对目标分布 $P$ 的信息量求平均，得到熵；用预测分布 $R$ 计算同一批结果的信息量，得到交叉熵：

$$H(P)=-\sum_kP_k\ln P_k,\qquad H(P,R)=-\sum_kP_k\ln R_k.$$

Their difference is KL divergence. With a fixed target, reducing cross-entropy brings the prediction closer to that target. For a one-hot label, target entropy is zero and cross-entropy reduces to $-\ln R_y$.

两者的差就是 KL 散度。目标固定时，降低交叉熵会让预测分布接近目标分布。单条 one-hot 标签的目标熵为零，交叉熵就简化为 $-\ln R_y$。

$$H(P,R)=H(P)+D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P\Vert R).$$
{{figure:entropy}}

Autoregressive language models use cross-entropy to raise the probability of each demonstrated next token. For example, a training sequence can teach the model to continue “the robot should” with “stop.”

自回归语言模型也用交叉熵提高示范中下一个 token 的概率。例如，训练序列可以让模型在“机器人应该”之后更可能生成“停止”。

Training a velocity classification head with cross-entropy produces a class distribution for each step in the action chunk. At inference, argmax selects a speed.

用交叉熵训练速度分类头，为动作块中的每一步预测类别分布，推理时通过 argmax 选取速度。

Yet SmolVLA and π0.5 learned base motion more readily from the same task demonstrations. Several factors may contribute to this difference: pretrained representations help identify visual conditions during motion; flow matching helps represent different possible action sequences; model capacity and feature interactions determine how fully that information can be used.* Not fully verified.

但同样的任务数据给 SmolVLA 和 π0.5 训练时，却更容易学到路径移动。这种差异可能来自几方面的共同作用：预训练表示帮助模型识别移动中的视觉条件，flow matching 帮助表达整段动作的不同可能，网络容量与交互方式影响这些信息能否被充分利用。* 尚未完全验证。

Learning损失与训练

Adding cross-entropy gives:

引入交叉熵损失：

$$L=L_{\mathrm{L1}}+\lambda_{\mathrm{disc}}L_{\mathrm{CE}}+\beta L_{\mathrm{KL}}.$$

L1 is the absolute difference between a demonstrated action component $a$ and its prediction $\hat a$. A prediction above the target is pushed down; one below it is pushed up. The regression loss averages these errors over the selected action dimensions and valid steps.

L1 是示范动作分量 $a$ 与预测值 $\hat a$ 的绝对差。预测偏大，梯度推动它减小；预测偏小，就推动它增大。回归损失对选定的动作维度和有效时间步求平均。

$$\ell_{\mathrm{L1}}=|\hat a-a|,\qquad \frac{\partial\ell_{\mathrm{L1}}}{\partial\hat a}=\operatorname{sign}(\hat a-a)\quad(\hat a\ne a).$$
{{figure:l1}}

KL compares distributions. The training posterior $q$ uses demonstrations to encode z; the prior $p$ must be available without them. Minimizing $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(q\Vert p)$ keeps the two distributions close.

KL 比较的是分布。训练后验 $q$ 利用示范动作编码 z，先验 $p$ 则不依赖示范动作。降低 $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(q\Vert p)$，使两种分布接近。

$$L_{\mathrm{KL}}=\mathbb E_{z\sim q}\left[\ln\frac{q(z)}{p(z)}\right].$$

CE raises the probability of the demonstrated class. Without class weighting, its gradient with respect to logit $u_k$ is the predicted probability minus the target indicator:

CE 提高示范类别的概率。不加类别权重时，它对 logit $u_k$ 的梯度是预测概率减去目标指示值：

$$\frac{\partial\ell_{\mathrm{CE}}}{\partial u_k}=R_k-\mathbf1[k=y].$$

These losses act on one forward pass. Images become visual features; state and z are projected into tokens. ACT combines them and predicts an action chunk. The action heads produce joint targets and velocity logits, which are compared with the demonstrations.

这些损失来自同一次前向计算。图像变成视觉特征，state 和 z 经过投影，ACT 融合这些输入并预测一个动作块。动作头输出关节目标和速度 logits，再与示范比较。

{{figure:training}}

Backpropagation carries L1 and CE gradients through the action heads and ACT backbone to the trainable input networks. Reparameterization also lets action gradients reach the posterior through z; KL acts directly on the distribution parameters. The demonstrations stay fixed while the network parameters are updated using these gradients.

反向传播将 L1 和 CE 的梯度沿动作头、ACT 主干传回可训练的输入网络。重参数化使动作梯度也能沿 z 到达后验；KL 直接作用于分布参数。示范保持不变，训练根据这些梯度更新网络参数。

Vision视觉特征

State, image features, and latent z enter the backbone together. In this training setup, the continuous state representation is more readily used to reduce L1 than the changing image features.

主干网络中，state、image 特征与潜变量 z 同时传入。训练中，连续的 state 表示比图像变化的特征更容易用于降低 L1 损失。

State is strongly correlated with subsequent actions and may provide a prediction shortcut. Too few stopping examples, invisible visual cues, or timing misalignment can produce similar behavior. Adjacent joint targets often change little, so continuing the current state fits part of the action sequence; identifying a stopping point requires task-relevant changes in the image.

状态与后续动作高度相关，可能形成预测捷径；停止样本不足、视觉线索不可见或时间错位也可能产生类似表现。相邻关节目标往往变化很小，延续当前状态就能拟合一部分动作；识别停止位置，还需要从画面中提取与任务有关的变化。

ResNet-18 comes from He et al.’s residual networks. The $F(x)+x$ connection learns a correction to an existing representation. ImageNet-pretrained weights are used to extract image features.

ResNet-18来自 He 等人的残差网络，通过 $F(x)+x$ 的连接，让网络学习对已有表示的修正。用 ImageNet 预训练权重处理图像特征。

ResNet-18 has a small computational footprint, suitable for multiple cameras and frequent inference. Removing the image classification head leaves a spatial feature map, retaining where features occur rather than reducing the image to a class.

ResNet-18 规模较小，能以较低计算量提取空间特征，适合多相机输入和频繁推理。去掉图像分类头后，输出仍是一张特征图，物体在画面中的位置没有被直接压成一个类别。

Latent Space潜空间

KL divergence is another special loss. It was introduced by Kullback and Leibler in 1951 and is commonly used to compare probability models and constrain distributions in variational inference.

KL 散度是另一个特殊的损失。它由 Kullback 与 Leibler 在 1951 年提出，常用于比较概率模型，以及变分推断中的分布约束。

Why introduce latent z? The same scene can admit different ways of completing a task. Regressing one action may give a compromise between demonstrations. L1 does not guarantee choosing one of them: with equally likely −1 and +1, every prediction in [−1,1] has the same expected L1 loss.

为什么还需要潜变量z？同一个场景可能有不同的完成方式。直接回归一个动作，可能得到两种示范之间的折中。L1 也不能保证选中其中一种：当 −1 和 +1 等概率出现时，[−1,1] 内的预测具有相同的期望 L1 损失。

A VAE encoder produces a distribution with parameters such as $\mu$ and $\sigma$. A decoder reconstructs the sample from z drawn from that distribution. A CVAE adds a condition: observations describe the scene, while z can represent variation among actions for that scene.

VAE 编码器输出由 $\mu$、$\sigma$ 等参数描述的分布，解码器根据从中采样的 z 重建样本。CVAE 加入条件：观测描述场景，z 可以表示同一场景下的动作差异。

In ACT, the posterior reads robot state $s$ and the demonstrated action chunk $A$. The action network reads images, state, and z. Reconstruction encourages useful action information in z; KL keeps the posterior close to a standard normal prior.

ACT 的后验读取机器人状态 $s$ 和示范动作块 $A$，动作网络读取图像、状态和 z。动作重建促使 z 携带有用的动作信息，KL 则使后验接近标准正态先验。

$$q_\phi(z\mid s,A)=\mathcal N(\mu_q,\operatorname{diag}\sigma_q^2),\qquad p(z)=\mathcal N(0,I).$$

The green dashed curve is the fixed posterior; the blue solid curve is the adjustable prior. The mean determines the center and the standard deviation determines the width. When both parameters match, the Gaussian curves coincide and KL is zero.

绿色虚线是固定的后验，蓝色实线是可调的先验。均值决定曲线中心的位置，标准差决定曲线的宽窄；这两个参数都相同时，两条高斯曲线重合，KL 为 0。

{{figure:gaussian}}

In the experiment, however, KL became very small. With a fixed prior, that can accompany an ignored latent variable: the model reconstructs actions from observations, while the posterior passes little additional information through z.

但在实际训练中，KL 损失很小。固定先验时，这可能伴随潜变量被忽略：模型只靠观测就能降低重建损失，后验不再需要通过 z 传递多少额外信息。

The inference implementation fixes z at zero. Rollout does not sample different latent values:

推理实现将 z 统一置为 0，rollout 中不再采样不同的潜变量：

latent_sample = torch.zeros([bs, self.latent_dim], dtype=torch.float32).to(qpos.device)
latent_input = self.latent_out_proj(latent_sample)

Transformer decoder 有输出索引问题，issue #25。

hs = self.transformer(src, None, self.query_embed.weight, pos,
                      latent_input, proprio_input,
                      self.additional_pos_embed.weight)[0]

The multi-camera path also lacks camera identity embeddings. Tokens at the same local coordinates receive the same positional label across cameras, so the position encoding cannot identify which camera a feature comes from.

以及多相机图像的处理：没有单独的相机身份编码，不同相机中相同局部坐标的 token 使用相同的位置标记，位置编码无法说明特征来自哪台相机。

Each image passes through the same ResNet-18, followed by a 1×1 projection to change the feature channels. Camera feature maps and their per-image position encodings are concatenated along the width, then flattened into tokens. Each token represents one spatial location in an image. With the default ResNet-18 stride, an $H\times W$ image produces roughly $(H/32)\times(W/32)$ tokens.

每张图先经过同一个 ResNet-18，再用 1×1 投影调整特征通道。各相机的特征图与各自的二维位置编码沿宽度拼接，随后展平成 tokens，每个 token 对应图像中的一个空间位置。按默认 ResNet-18 的步幅，一张 $H\times W$ 图像约得到 $(H/32)\times(W/32)$ 个 tokens。

$$I^{(c)}\longrightarrow F^{(c)}\in\mathbb R^{D\times H'\times W'}\longrightarrow T^{(c)}\in\mathbb R^{H'W'\times D}.$$

After fixing these issues together, the CVAE posterior encoder was changed to a ResNet-18 image-action network, following the earlier strong results with vision-only control.

把这些问题一次性改掉后，又沿用之前表现不错的 vision-only 思路，将 CVAE 的后验编码器换成 ResNet-18 的 image-action 网络。

An image-conditioned prior was also added to obtain z from the current image during rollout. KL trains its distribution to stay close to the training posterior.

为了在 rollout 时根据图像得到 z，又加入了条件先验，通过 KL 学习与训练后验接近的分布。

After these changes, the multimodal task (left/right grasping) still failed. 😞

这些修改完成后，多模态任务（区分左右的抓取任务）依然失败了。😞

To check whether z actually affected the actions and whether the CVAE changes had helped, the image was held fixed while z was resampled several times. The predicted actions barely changed.

为了看看 z 是否真的有用，以及这次 CVAE 修改有没有起作用，又固定同一张图像，多次重新采样 z，比较输出动作。结果几乎没有变化。

$$q_\phi(z\mid V,A),\qquad p_\psi(z\mid V).$$

$V$ is pooled visual information for the latent networks. During training, z is sampled from the posterior; during rollout, it comes from the prior, either by sampling or by taking its mean. ACT receives the spatial image features in both cases.

V 是供潜变量网络使用的视觉汇总。训练时从后验采样 z，rollout 时改从先验采样，或取先验均值。两种情况下，ACT 都同时接收保留空间信息的图像特征。

Reparameterization lets the action loss update the posterior through z. KL updates both posterior and conditional prior. A small KL now means they agree for the same image; both can still change with the image.

重参数化让动作损失沿 z 更新后验，KL 同时更新后验和条件先验。此时 KL 小，表示两者在同一图像条件下接近；换一张图像，两者仍可一起改变。

$$z=\mu_q+\sigma_q\odot\epsilon,\qquad\epsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,I).$$
{{figure:network}}

The ACT experiments cover six real-world tasks and two simulated tasks. The real-world tasks use teleoperation data; the simulated tasks use scripted or human demonstrations. The project page shows both teleoperation demonstrations and learned-policy rollouts. How far do these fine-grained behaviors generalize, and could they be overfitting the demonstrated trajectories?

ACT 的论文实验包括 6 个真机任务和 2 个仿真任务。真机任务使用遥操数据，仿真任务使用脚本示范或人类遥操示范。项目页面同时展示了遥操视频和学习策略的 rollout。这些精细操作能泛化到多大范围，会不会也过拟合了示范轨迹？

Data Collection如何采集数据

  • Smooth actions. Reduce accidental jitter while keeping necessary pauses and corrections, so incidental movements do not obscure the task.

    动作平稳。 减少无意抖动，保留必要停顿与纠偏，避免示范中的偶然动作干扰学习。

  • Camera views. Keep the target and contact point visible, and align images with actions so the relevant visual information is available.

    相机视角。 让目标与接触点可见，并对齐图像和动作，保证判断所需的信息没有缺失。

  • Varied conditions. Vary starting and target positions, and include recovery actions so the policy can handle more states.

    覆盖变化。 改变起点和目标位置，补充偏离轨迹后的恢复动作，让策略适应更多状态。

Inference如何推理

chunk_size sets the predicted horizon $K$ during training. n_action_steps, written $n$, sets how many actions execute before reading a new observation. (A model trained with $K=30$ cannot produce a 100-step chunk simply by setting $n=100$.)

训练时的 chunk_size 决定预测长度 $K$，n_action_steps 记为 $n$，决定执行多少步后重新读取观测。（训练时 $K=30$ 的模型，不能仅靠设置 $n=100$ 就预测出 100 步动作。）

Executing 100 steps at a time showed pauses between chunks. Reducing n used only the beginning of each prediction, but sometimes made the pauses more obvious or left the robot still. Frequent replanning can repeatedly execute an initial waiting segment, or expose inference latency.

一次执行 100 步时，动作块之间出现明显卡顿。把 n 调小，只执行每个预测块的前一部分，卡顿有时更明显，甚至停住不动。更频繁重规划可能反复执行预测块开头的等待部分，也可能暴露推理延迟。

Temporal ensembling with coefficient 0.01 made motion smoother and corrections quicker, though occasional stops remained. It combines predictions from different chunks for the same execution time t, with i = 0 denoting the oldest prediction:

开启时序融合，系数设为 0.01，动作变得平滑，纠正偏差也更及时，但偶尔仍会静止。它把不同动作块中对应同一执行时刻 t 的预测加权平均，i = 0 表示最旧预测：

$$\bar a_t=\frac{\sum_{i=0}^{N-1}e^{-mi}\hat a_t^{(i)}}{\sum_{i=0}^{N-1}e^{-mi}},\qquad i=0,\ldots,N-1.$$
{{figure:temporal}}

Increasing the coefficient to 0.5 produced repeated actions. In the current indexing order, a larger positive coefficient favors older predictions. With only two predictions, m = 0.5 assigns about 62% to the older one and 38% to the newer one, rather than half to each.

把系数调到 0.5 后，出现了明显动作反复。按当前实现的编号顺序，正系数越大，越偏向旧预测。只有两个预测时，$m=0.5$ 的旧、新权重约为 62% 和 38%，并非各占一半。

Similar images can still produce conflicting predictions for t: an old chunk predicts further into the future, while a new chunk responds to the current observation. Averaging an old forward command with a new correction can delay that correction; opposing velocity predictions can also cancel.

画面相似，对 t 的预测仍可能冲突：旧动作块预测的是更远的一步，新动作块则根据当前观测纠偏。旧的前进指令与新的纠偏指令混合，可能拖延纠正；方向相反的速度预测也可能相互抵消。

Latent and action caches潜变量与动作缓存

sample + phase reuses a sampled z. reset() or refresh_latent() clears the latent and pending predictions. Without temporal ensembling, a nonempty action queue also skips a new network call.

sample + phase 会复用已采样的 z。reset() 或 refresh_latent() 清空潜变量和未执行的预测。不开启时序融合时，动作队列未耗尽也不会重新调用网络。

To compare different z values, run fresh forward passes on the same observation and compare continuous actions and logits. argmax can hide probability changes that leave the winning class unchanged. Sampling at every chunk also clears previous ensemble state in this implementation.

比较不同 z 时，对同一观测重新前向，直接比较连续动作和 logits。类别概率变化但最大项不变时，argmax 会隐藏这种差异。当前实现每个 chunk 重新采样时，也会清空旧融合状态。
