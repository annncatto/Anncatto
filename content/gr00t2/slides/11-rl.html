After supervised fine-tuning, contact-rich skills remain sensitive to small alignment errors. The report therefore evaluates real-world RL using task-level success feedback. Self-imitation reaches 88%, 98%, 94% and 96% on the four tasks.监督微调后，接触密集技能仍对小幅对齐误差敏感。因此报告进一步用任务级成败反馈进行实机 RL。自模仿在四项任务上分别达到 88%、98%、94%、96%。
