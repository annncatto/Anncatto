1 · Single-view video1 · 单视角视频预测

Adapt Cosmos to embodied interactions. Predict future video from history using human video supplemented by robot observations.将 Cosmos 适配到具身交互。以人类视频为主，加入机器人观测，从历史画面预测未来视频。

→

2 · Multi-view video + action2 · 多视角视频—动作联合训练

Add embodiment modules. Predict video and actions jointly; human hand-pose trajectories and robot actions provide valid action supervision.加入本体模块，联合预测视频与动作；人类手部姿态轨迹和机器人动作提供有效的动作监督。

→

3 · Upweight robot data3 · 提高机器人数据权重

Continue the joint objective with more robot demonstrations, especially YAM, while retaining human and dexterous-hand data.继续联合训练，提高机器人示范、特别是 YAM 的权重，同时保留人类与灵巧手数据。
