The target robot has no teleoperated demonstration of these tasks. Demonstrations specify the task; compatible body experience and joint world–action learning support transfer to the target robot.目标机器人没有这些任务的遥操作示范。示范提供任务内容，本体相关经验与世界—动作联合学习支持模型把技能迁移到目标机器人。

Task knowledge任务知识

Task demonstrations come from humans or capture devices, rather than the target robot.任务由人类或采集设备演示，而非目标机器人。

Embodiment knowledge本体知识

Human/UMI transfer uses task-agnostic target-robot data. A matched DexUMI hand can use a direct joint mapping.人类／UMI 迁移使用目标机器人的非任务特定数据；匹配的 DexUMI 手可直接映射关节。

Transfer evidence迁移结果

Filter-paper setup: 65%; UMI→YAM truck assembly: 60%. Conveyor bars measure sorted items, not completed trials.滤纸装配 65%；UMI→YAM 卡车组装 60%。传送带柱形统计已分拣物品比例。
