The target robot has no teleoperated demonstration of these tasks. Demonstrations specify the task; compatible body experience and joint world–action learning support transfer to the target robot.目标机器人没有这些任务的遥操作示范。示范提供任务内容，本体相关经验与世界—动作联合学习支持模型把技能迁移到目标机器人。

The demonstrations may come from motion-captured humans, handheld UMI, DexUMI or a human-to-simulation pipeline. For human and handheld-UMI transfer, task-agnostic data from the target robot supply its appearance and action statistics. A DexUMI exoskeleton matched to the target hand provides a direct joint mapping instead.示范可以来自人体动捕、手持 UMI、DexUMI，或人类到仿真的数据管线。人体与手持 UMI 迁移使用目标机器人的非任务特定数据提供本体外观与动作统计；与目标手匹配的 DexUMI 外骨骼则可以直接映射关节。
