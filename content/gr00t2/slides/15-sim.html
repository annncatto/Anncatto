Real videos, scans and images are used to reconstruct geometry, appearance and collisions; asset tests and demonstration replay validate the environments (§4.5.1). Policies trained on real data are then evaluated directly in these environments. On 25 YAM tasks and 14 checkpoints, Pearson correlations reach 0.92 for success and 0.93 for Elo (§4.5.2).由实机视频、扫描和图像重建几何、外观与碰撞，再通过资产测试和示范重放校验环境（§4.5.1）。真实数据训练的策略随后直接进入这些环境评估；25 项 YAM 任务、14 个检查点的成功率和 Elo Pearson 相关性分别为 0.92、0.93（§4.5.2）。
