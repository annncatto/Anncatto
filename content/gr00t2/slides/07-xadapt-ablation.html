消融组的训练条件Training in the ablation

仍用 GR00T 2 网络，具身预训练只使用目标机器人数据；绿色组使用完整跨本体混合，预训练预算匹配。The GR00T 2 architecture remains. Target-only robot data replace the full cross-embodiment mixture at a matched pretraining budget.

后训练条件相同Matched post-training

两组都使用重定向人体示范与非任务机器人 play，按 1∶1 采样，步数相同。Both use retargeted human demonstrations and task-agnostic robot play, sampled 1:1 for the same number of steps.

跨本体经验的作用Cross-embodiment experience

论文的解释：跨本体经验帮助共享骨干把同一种任务联系到不同身体。完整混合同时增加了本体、人类数据与规模。The paper attributes transfer to learning the same task across bodies; the full mixture jointly adds embodiments, human data and scale.
