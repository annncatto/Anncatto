The frozen Wan 2.1 VAE compresses video 4× in time and 8× along each spatial axis. At 224×320 resolution, patches yield 280 tokens per latent frame and camera; view embeddings distinguish cameras. The frozen Cosmos-Reason1 encoder supplies language features through cross-attention.冻结的 Wan 2.1 VAE 将视频时间压缩 4 倍、空间每轴压缩 8 倍；224×320 图像分块后，每相机每潜帧得到 280 个 token，并加入相机视角嵌入。冻结的 Cosmos-Reason1 编码器通过交叉注意力提供语言条件。

Video, state and action tokens are processed by the same 2B Transformer, initialized from Cosmos-Predict2.5-2B. Small embodiment-specific MLPs encode each robot’s state/actions and decode its action coordinates; action tokens can read both observed and predicted video features.视频、状态与动作 token 由同一个 2B Transformer处理，骨干从 Cosmos-Predict2.5-2B 初始化。本体专属小型 MLP 编码各机器人的状态／动作并解码动作坐标；动作 token 可以读取历史观测和预测视频的特征。
