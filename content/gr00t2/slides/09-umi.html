For UMI→YAM (§4.4.2), camera slots and action semantics are aligned before co-training with task-agnostic YAM data. From the same YAM start frame, UMI-only training predicts human arms and UMI fingers; adding YAM experience predicts YAM’s own arms. The co-training data supply both body appearance and action statistics.UMI→YAM（§4.4.2）先对齐相机槽位与动作语义，再与 YAM 非任务特定数据联合训练。同一 YAM 起始画面下，仅 UMI 训练会预测人类手臂和 UMI 手指，加入 YAM 经验后预测YAM 自己的手臂；联合训练数据同时提供本体外观与动作统计。

The following subsections use other capture routes: a DexUMI exoskeleton matches the target hand’s joints (§4.4.3), while human-to-sim transfer reconstructs one demonstration and trains an RL expert to generate randomized simulations (§4.4.4). The latter achieves 9/10 real hammering trials.后续小节分别采用其他采集路线：DexUMI 外骨骼与目标手关节匹配（§4.4.3）；人类到仿真路线则从单个示范重建动作和资产，用 RL 专家生成随机化仿真示范（§4.4.4），最终完成 9/10 次实机钉钉子试验。
