The operator wears head/wrist cameras at robot-matched viewpoints, PICO trackers for 6-DoF wrist poses and Manus gloves for finger joints. Offline IK retargets wrist motion to the 7-DoF arms and hand motion to 22-DoF Sharpa hands, preserving fingertip pinch geometry. The human video remains unchanged.操作者佩戴与机器人视角匹配的头部／腕部相机，以 PICO 跟踪腕部六自由度位姿、Manus 手套跟踪手指关节。离线 IK 将动作重定向到七自由度手臂和 22 自由度 Sharpa 手，保持指尖捏持几何。人体视频保持原样。

Retargeted human actions and robot actions train one shared action head. Task-agnostic robot play is mixed 1:1 with human demonstrations; at deployment, the robot receives its own observation and the instruction and predicts future frames together with executable actions.重定向的人体动作与机器人动作监督同一动作头；机器人非任务特定 play 与人体示范按 1:1 混合。部署时，模型接收机器人自身的观测与指令，联合预测未来画面及可执行动作。
