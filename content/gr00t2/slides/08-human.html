采集路线决定动作标签怎样接入目标机器人；目标机器人的非任务经验提供本体外观与运动统计。The capture route determines how action labels connect to the robot; task-agnostic robot experience supplies body appearance and motion statistics.

人体重定向保留原视频，与机器人 play 按 1∶1 混合，共同监督目标动作头。Human retargeting preserves the original video; human data and robot play are mixed 1:1 to supervise the target action head.

任务示范提供交互过程，动作对齐将其转为目标动作坐标；机器人 play 补充目标本体经验，二者在后训练中结合。Task demonstrations supply the interaction; alignment maps it to target actions, while robot play adds target-body experience for post-training.
