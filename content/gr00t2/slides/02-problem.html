From physical experience to robot skills研究问题与核心工作

02 · §1 · p. 3 ↗

A general-purpose robot must learn from heterogeneous physical experience, acquire skills with little task supervision, improve through interaction, and expose those skills to reasoning systems. GR00T 2 connects these requirements through joint prediction of future observations and actions.通用机器人需要从异构的物理经验中学习，用少量任务监督获得新技能，通过交互提高可靠性，并把操作能力提供给推理系统。GR00T 2 以未来视觉观测与动作的联合预测连接这些需求。

“predicts both how the scene will unfold and the robot’s actions.”

Transfer physical skills迁移物理技能

Human and device demonstrations supply the task; experience with the target body supports execution on that body.人类与采集设备提供任务示范，目标机器人的经验帮助模型在其本体上执行。

Adapt and improve适应并提高可靠性

Few-shot adaptation learns new tasks; reinforcement learning improves contact-rich manipulation.少样本微调适应新任务，强化学习提高接触密集操作的可靠性。

Compose and execute组合技能并实时执行

A reasoning agent composes skills; efficient inference and chunk continuity support real robots and humanoids.推理智能体组合技能；高效推理与动作块连续性支持实机及人形机器人。
