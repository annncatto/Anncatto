From physical experience to robot skills研究问题与核心工作

02 · §1 · p. 3 ↗

A general-purpose robot must learn from heterogeneous physical experience, acquire skills with little task supervision, improve through interaction, and expose those skills to reasoning systems. GR00T 2 connects these requirements through joint prediction of future observations and actions.通用机器人需要从异构的物理经验中学习，用少量任务监督获得新技能，通过交互提高可靠性，并把操作能力提供给推理系统。GR00T 2 以未来视觉观测与动作的联合预测连接这些需求。

“predicts both how the scene will unfold and the robot’s actions.”
