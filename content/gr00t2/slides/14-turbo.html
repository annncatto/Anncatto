Turbo keeps the 2B joint video–action Transformer but replaces Wan 2.1 VAE with Wan 2.2 VAE. Spatial compression rises from 8× to 16× per axis, reducing the spatial latent grid to one quarter while retaining the action horizon.Turbo 保留 2B 视频—动作联合 Transformer，将 Wan 2.1 VAE 换为 Wan 2.2 VAE；空间每轴压缩从 8 倍增至 16 倍，使潜变量网格缩到四分之一，动作预测窗口不变。

Turbo predicts 1.6 s / 80-token motion chunks and replans about once per second. SONIC turns these latent whole-body commands into joint targets at 50 Hz, and the robot’s PD controller tracks them at 1000 Hz. Turbo runs on Jetson AGX Thor; SONIC runs on Orin NX.Turbo 预测 1.6 秒／80 token 的运动块，约每秒重规划一次；SONIC 将全身动作潜变量解码为 50 Hz 的关节目标，机器人 PD 控制器以 1000 Hz 跟踪。Turbo 运行于 Jetson AGX Thor，SONIC 运行于 Orin NX。
