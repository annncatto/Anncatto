Across 50 tasks absent from Dexter data, zero-shot success is 40%. One demonstration raises it to 71%; five raise it to 95%. Each LoRA adaptation takes about 30 minutes on one RTX 5090.50 项任务未出现在 Dexter 训练数据中。零样本成功率 40%，一条示范微调后 71%，五条后 95%；单张 RTX 5090 上每次 LoRA 适应约 30 分钟。

The suite selects tasks reaching at least 80% after five-shot adaptation. This is the protocol for the reported 95%, rather than an arbitrary task sample.任务集选取五样本适应后至少达到 80% 的任务；这是 95% 结果对应的任务筛选条件。
