With the architecture and target-robot post-training data held fixed, increasing human video from 0% to 100% raises real-robot success from 18% to 41% and task progress from 50% to 70%. The 1%, 10% and 100% conditions use matching epochs of their human-data share; the 0→1% step also introduces a shared pretraining stage.在架构和目标机器人后训练数据相同的条件下，人类视频从 0% 增至 100%，实机成功率从 18% 提高到 41%，任务进度从 50% 提高到 70%。1%、10%、100% 按各自数据规模训练相应 epoch；0→1% 还包含共同预训练阶段的加入。

For checkpoint selection (§4.3), GearArena compares policies in randomized order on matched real scenes and fits Bradley–Terry ratings. The current suite contains 6,035 comparisons across 122 campaigns.检查点选择采用 §4.3 的 GearArena：在匹配实机场景中随机排序比较策略，再拟合 Bradley–Terry 评分。当前套件累计 122 轮活动、6,035 次比较。
