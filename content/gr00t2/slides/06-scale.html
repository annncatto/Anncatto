Controlled data scaling数据规模对照

All models share the architecture and target-robot post-training data. Increasing human video from 0% to 100% raises real-robot success from 18% to 41% and task progress from 50% to 70%.架构和目标机器人后训练数据保持一致。人类视频从 0% 增至 100% 时，实机成功率从 18% 提高到 41%，任务进度从 50% 提高到 70%。

The 0→1% comparison also adds a shared human-pretraining stage; larger shares are trained for matching epochs rather than equal update counts.0→1% 还包含共同的人类视频预训练阶段；不同规模按相应 epoch 训练，并非固定更新步数。
