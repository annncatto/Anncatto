01

Mass and its location质量有多少，又放在哪里

$$m=\sum_i m_i,\qquad m_{\rm member}=\rho A L_{\rm cut},\qquad \mathbf r_C=\frac{\sum_i m_i\mathbf r_i}{m}.$$

Member mass uses the actual cut length. Joint housings, actuators, feet, the tool and the payload remain in the assembly budget. A denser material may still be useful if its stiffness permits a smaller section.杆件质量按实际下料长度计算，关节壳体、电机、足端、工具和负载仍计入装配质量。选材需要同时改变密度与模量，再检查截面能否满足要求。

For a thin tube with fixed outer diameter and free wall thickness, the stiffness index is E / ρ. For geometrically similar beam sections allowed to scale, it is √E / ρ; with a shape factor, √(φE) / ρ. The present sliders set dimensions directly, so stress and deflection checks still decide whether a material change is acceptable.定外径、壁厚自由的薄壁圆管，刚度选材指数为 $E/\rho$；几何相似截面可自由缩放的受弯梁，对应 $\sqrt E/\rho$，计入形状后为 $\sqrt{\phi E}/\rho$。当前滑块直接规定尺寸，选材结果仍需通过应力与变形校核。

02

Inertia of the driven chain被驱动链的惯量

$$J_L=\sum_i\left[\mathbf a^T R_iI_{Ci}R_i^T\mathbf a+m_i\left(\|\mathbf r_i\|^2-(\mathbf a^T\mathbf r_i)^2\right)\right].$$

The selected joint axis is a. Every downstream body's central inertia tensor and perpendicular COM distance enter this sum. Moving a knee or elbow actuator proximally reduces its lever arm, while the additional belt, pulleys and supports also enter the model.$\mathbf a$ 是所选关节轴；逐项计入下游构件的质心惯量张量与质心到该轴的垂距。将膝部或肘部电机移向近端，可以缩短力臂；新增同步带、带轮与支承也同时计入模型。

03

The cost of reduction减速比带来的惯性代价

$$J_{\rm ref}=N^2J_m,\qquad J_{\rm total}=J_L+J_{\rm ref},\qquad \tau_{\rm inertial}=J_{\rm total}\alpha.$$

N is motor speed divided by output speed. Doubling N quadruples reflected rotor inertia. Moving the motor housing and changing its reduction affect different terms: relocation changes J_L; gearing changes N²J_m.$N$ 为电机转速与输出转速之比，减速比加倍会让反射惯量增至四倍。移动电机壳体与改变减速比作用于不同项：前者改变 $J_L$，后者改变 $N^2J_m$。

04

Deflection, resonance and target bandwidth从变形、共振到目标带宽

$$C_{\rm tip}=\int_{\rm member}\frac{(L-s)^2}{EI(s)}\,ds+\frac{L^2}{k_\theta},\qquad\delta=FC_{\rm tip}.$$
$$m_{\rm eff}\simeq\int\rho A\phi(s)^2ds+\sum_i m_i\phi_i^2+J_{\rm ref}\theta_j^2,\qquad f_n\simeq\frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{1}{C_{\rm tip}m_{\rm eff}}}.$$

The cut member bends; its end fixtures are treated as rigid, and the transmission is a root torsional spring. The static displacement shape, normalized to unit tip motion, estimates the first mode by Rayleigh's method. Downstream parts are lumped at the end. This is a selected-member estimate with stated connection flexibility.下料杆件参与弯曲，两端连接段视为刚体，传动以根部转动弹簧表示。将静挠曲线归一化为单位端部位移，用 Rayleigh 法估算第一模态；下游组件集中于端部。这是所选构件与连接柔性的近似模型。

The displayed interval is f_n / f_b, where f_b is the requested control bandwidth. Its screening threshold is editable. Actual closed-loop bandwidth also depends on damping, sensing, delay and the controller; these calculations do not identify it.显示的间隔是 $f_n/f_b$，其中 $f_b$ 为目标控制带宽，初筛间隔可自行设置。实际闭环带宽还取决于阻尼、测量、延迟与控制器，需要另外辨识或验证。

05

Check stress and actuator demand校核应力与执行器需求

$$\sigma_{\max}=\frac{M_{\max}c}{I},\qquad n\sigma_{\max}\le\sigma_{\rm lim},\qquad\delta\le\delta_{\rm lim}.$$
$$\tau_{\rm req}\approx|\tau_g|+FL+(J_L+N^2J_m)\alpha+\tau_{\rm break},\qquad\tau_{\rm available}=\eta N\tau_m s.$$

F is the transverse verification force entered at right. The torque screen adds magnitudes for the stated acceleration, gravity, external force and friction. The motor class factor s scales the base torque for the selected joint. The screen excludes Coriolis coupling and contact-force allocation; a full motion/contact analysis must supply final load histories.$F$ 是右侧输入的横向校核力。力矩初筛将给定角加速度、重力、外力和摩擦的幅值相加，电机规格系数 $s$ 将基准转矩换算到所选关节，未求解科氏耦合与接触力分配；最终动载荷历程仍需由完整运动与接触分析给出。

06

Backdriving and contact反向驱动与接触

$$\tau_{\rm break}\approx\tau_{f,g}+\frac{N\tau_{f,m}}{\eta_{\rm reverse}},\qquad E_{\rm rot}=\frac12(J_L+N^2J_m)\omega^2.$$

The breakaway estimate assumes compensated gravity and the entered motor/gear friction and reverse efficiency. Lower reduction can reduce both reflected inertia and the friction reflected from the motor, but more direct motor torque is then required. Gear geometry, seals and preload still matter.反驱门槛按重力已补偿、所填电机及减速器摩擦和反向效率估算。降低减速比可以同时减小反射惯量和折算电机摩擦，但需要电机直接提供更多转矩。齿轮结构、密封和预紧仍会影响实际反驱能力。

Model and verification scope模型与校核范围

Geometry follows a joint tree with explicit member lengths, actuator envelopes and end clearances, following the modelling organization of the open-source Draft project. Blue structures, dark actuators and warm-coloured feet/tools identify the mass that moves. Motion uses inverse kinematics with fixed stance feet and a checked support polygon. The solid-envelope scan samples 32 frames and excludes intended adjoining structural interfaces; separate actuator packages are always checked. Structural and drive results remain analytical screening calculations.几何参考开源 Draft 项目的组织方式，用关节树明确连杆长度、电机包络与端部退让。蓝色结构、深色执行器和暖色足端／工具，帮助区分随动质量。运动预览采用逆运动学、支撑足约束与支承多边形检查。包络扫描抽取 32 帧，排除相邻结构的预定连接界面，独立电机包络始终参与检查；结构与驱动结果用于解析初筛。
