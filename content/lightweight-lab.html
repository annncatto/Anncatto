    Model and assumptions计算模型与假设

    Mass sums all limbs and the stated body/accessory budget. Inertia and static torque refer to one two-link limb at the displayed pose. Bending, stress and frequency use the longest individual member as a linear cantilever, with a transverse tip test load. The frequency includes an assumed tip mass; it is not the complete robot’s first mode. Proximal transmission adds 0.16 kg per limb. Rotor inertia is fixed at 2.5×10⁻⁵ kg·m² and reflected through an ideal constant ratio. Material properties are representative teaching values; CFRP uses an equivalent laminate. Animation is kinematic: the legs follow a treadmill foot cycle and the arm uses planar two-link inverse kinematics. Joint limits, collisions and balance are not solved. URDF exports continuous joints without motor ratings, armature or contact tuning.总质量包含全部肢体及给定的机身与附件预算。惯量与静态力矩针对显示姿态中的一条二连杆肢体。挠度、应力与频率将最长单根杆件视为线性悬臂梁，施加横向末端测试载荷；频率包含假定的端部质量，并非整机第一阶模态。近端传动每条肢体增加 0.16 kg。转子惯量固定为 2.5×10⁻⁵ kg·m²，按理想恒定减速比折算。材料采用教学标称值；CFRP 使用等效层合板参数。运动采用运动学：腿部跟随原地步态轨迹，机械臂使用平面二连杆逆解；未求解关节限位、碰撞与平衡。URDF 导出连续关节，未指定电机额定参数、转子 armature 和接触调参。
