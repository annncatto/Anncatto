Motion loads and whole-body control运动载荷与全身控制
$$M(q)\ddot q+b(q,\dot q)=S^T\tau+J_c^Tf,\qquad f_z\ge0,\quad |f_x|+|f_y|\le\mu f_z.$$

For a prescribed motion, this equation relates the robot’s acceleration to motor torque and ground reactions. The generalized position $q$ contains joint coordinates and, for a walking robot, body position and orientation; dots denote time derivatives. $M$ is the inertia matrix, and $b$ collects gravity and velocity-dependent forces. $S^T$ maps joint torques $\tau$ into the full-body equations; the contact Jacobian $J_c$ maps the ground forces $f$ into the same coordinates.给定一段运动后，这个方程将整机加速度与关节转矩、地面反力联系起来。广义位置 $q$ 包含关节坐标；对行走机器人，还包含机身的位置和姿态，上方的点表示对时间求导。$M$ 是惯性矩阵，$b$ 汇总重力及与速度有关的力。$S^T$ 将关节转矩 $\tau$ 写入整机方程，接触雅可比矩阵 $J_c$ 则把地面反力 $f$ 换算到同一组坐标。

At each stance contact, $f_z$ is the upward normal force and $f_x,f_y$ are the horizontal components. The entered friction coefficient $\mu$ sets the available tangential force through the inequality above. For a walking robot, MuJoCo first computes the generalized force required by the reference motion. A constrained solve then allocates ground reactions that balance the six unactuated body equations, and the remaining joint equations give the required output torques. For a fixed-base arm, inverse dynamics directly gives the joint torques. The displayed balance residual measures how closely the contact solution satisfies those equations.每个支撑接触点的 $f_z$ 为向上的法向力，$f_x$、$f_y$ 为水平分力；输入的摩擦系数 $\mu$ 通过上述不等式限定可提供的切向力。四足与人形的计算先由 MuJoCo 求出参考运动所需的广义力，再分配地面反力，使没有直接驱动的机身六个自由度满足平衡，最后由关节方程得到输出转矩。固定基座机械臂则直接由逆动力学求各关节转矩。显示的力与力矩残差，就是接触力解与平衡要求之间的差值。

The motion envelope records extrema over the sampled reference trajectory. When a beam section is defined, the same loads also give member stress and deflection. In the closed-loop test, the walking controller instead uses the current simulated state to choose bounded torques while tracking the body, feet and joint posture. MuJoCo integrates those torques to produce the replay; tracking error and contact slip are measured on that simulated motion.参考动作包络记录抽样轨迹中的载荷极值；已定义梁截面时，同一组载荷也用于计算杆件应力和变形。闭环测试中，行走控制器根据当前仿真状态，在动力学、摩擦和关节限值内求解转矩，并跟踪躯干、足端与关节姿态。MuJoCo 对这些转矩积分生成回放，跟踪误差和接触滑移由实际仿真轨迹统计。

Assembly mass and centre of mass装配质量与质心
$$m=\sum_i m_i,\qquad m_{\rm member}=\rho A L_{\rm cut},\qquad \mathbf r_C=\frac{\sum_i m_i\mathbf r_i}{m}.$$

Assembly mass $m$ is the sum of all component masses $m_i$. For a parametric member, density $\rho$ (kg/m³), section area $A$ (m²) and cut length $L_{\rm cut}$ (m) give its mass. Motors, joint fittings, feet, tools and payload are then added. The assembly centre of mass $\mathbf r_C$ is the mass-weighted average of component COM positions $\mathbf r_i$, expressed in the same coordinate frame.装配质量 $m$ 是各部件质量 $m_i$ 之和。参数化杆件由密度 $\rho$（kg/m³）、截面积 $A$（m²）和实际下料长度 $L_{\rm cut}$（m）求得质量，再计入电机、连接件、足端、工具与负载。将各部件质心位置 $\mathbf r_i$ 表示在同一坐标系中，按质量加权即可得到整机质心 $\mathbf r_C$。

Material selection also depends on which dimensions may change. With a fixed outer diameter and adjustable thin wall, bending stiffness per mass follows $E/\rho$; when similar beam sections may scale in all directions, the index is $\sqrt E/\rho$. Here $E$ is Young’s modulus. Including the section shape factor $\phi=12I/A^2$ gives $\sqrt{\phi E}/\rho$, where $I$ is the area second moment about the bending axis. The material comparison therefore offers separate fixed-dimension and constraint-sized calculations.材料比较还取决于哪些尺寸允许改变。外径固定、薄壁厚度可调时，受弯刚度对应的选材指数为 $E/\rho$；几何相似截面允许整体缩放时，对应 $\sqrt E/\rho$，其中 $E$ 为杨氏模量。计入截面形状系数 $\phi=12I/A^2$ 后，指数为 $\sqrt{\phi E}/\rho$，$I$ 是弯曲轴对应的截面二次矩。因此，材料比较分别提供“保持尺寸”和“满足约束”两种计算方式。

Driven-chain inertia随动链惯量
$$J_L=\sum_i\left[\mathbf a^T R_iI_{Ci}R_i^T\mathbf a+m_i\left(\|\mathbf r_i\|^2-(\mathbf a^T\mathbf r_i)^2\right)\right].$$

This calculation gives the inertia $J_L$ of the bodies moved by the selected joint, evaluated at the design pose. The unit vector $\mathbf a$ defines the joint axis. For each downstream body, $I_{Ci}$ is its inertia tensor about its own COM; $R_i$ rotates that tensor into world coordinates. The vector $\mathbf r_i$ runs from the joint origin to that COM, and $m_i$ is the body mass. The first term projects the COM inertia onto the joint axis; the second adds the parallel-axis contribution, equal to mass times squared perpendicular distance.这里计算设计姿态下，所选关节带动的所有下游构件对该轴的转动惯量 $J_L$。单位向量 $\mathbf a$ 表示关节轴方向；$I_{Ci}$ 是第 $i$ 个构件绕自身质心的惯量张量，$R_i$ 将其旋转到世界坐标系。$\mathbf r_i$ 从关节原点指向该构件质心，$m_i$ 为构件质量。括号中第一项是质心惯量在关节轴上的投影，第二项按平行轴定理加入“质量 × 到轴垂距的平方”。

Reduction and reflected inertia减速比与反射惯量
$$J_{\rm ref}=N^2J_m,\qquad J_{\rm total}=J_L+J_{\rm ref},\qquad \tau_{\rm inertial}=J_{\rm total}\alpha.$$

The reduction ratio $N$ is motor speed divided by joint output speed. If the output turns at $\omega$, the rotor turns at $N\omega$; expressing its kinetic energy at the output gives the reflected inertia $J_{\rm ref}=N^2J_m$, where $J_m$ is rotor inertia. Adding the driven-chain inertia $J_L$ gives $J_{\rm total}$. Multiplying by the prescribed output acceleration $\alpha$ gives the corresponding inertial torque, in N·m.减速比 $N$ 定义为电机转速与关节输出转速之比。输出端以 $\omega$ 转动时，转子转速为 $N\omega$；将转子的动能折算到输出端，便得到反射惯量 $J_{\rm ref}=N^2J_m$，其中 $J_m$ 为转子惯量。再加上随动链惯量 $J_L$，得到总惯量 $J_{\rm total}$；乘以给定的输出角加速度 $\alpha$，即得到相应的惯性力矩，单位为 N·m。

Compliance and natural frequency结构柔度与固有频率
$$C_{\rm tip}=\int_{\rm member}\frac{(L-s)^2}{EI(s)}\,ds+\frac{h^2}{k_\theta},\qquad\delta=FC_{\rm tip}.$$
$$m_{\rm eff}\simeq\int\rho A\phi(s)^2ds+\sum_i m_i\phi_i^2+J_{\rm ref}\theta_j^2,\qquad f_n\simeq\frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{1}{C_{\rm tip}m_{\rm eff}}}.$$

A transverse force $F$ acts at the end of the selected member, at distance $L$ from the driving axis. The coordinate $s$ is measured along this span. Only the cut member bends; the end fittings are rigid, and the output transmission is a rotational spring of stiffness $k_\theta$ (N·m/rad). Here h is the transverse-force moment arm about the actual joint axis. Its spring compliance h²/kθ adds to the beam term, which uses Young’s modulus $E$ and the bending-axis area second moment $I(s)$. Total tip compliance $C_{\rm tip}$ is displacement per unit force (m/N), so the loaded displacement is $\delta=FC_{\rm tip}$.横向力 $F$ 作用在所选杆件末端，该处距驱动轴为 $L$，$s$ 表示沿这段跨距的位置。计算中，下料杆件参与弯曲，两端连接段视为刚体；输出传动用转动刚度为 $k_\theta$（N·m/rad）的弹簧表示。h 表示横向力对实际关节轴的力臂，传动柔度为 h²/kθ。梁的柔度由杨氏模量 $E$ 和弯曲轴对应的截面二次矩 $I(s)$ 决定，再与传动柔度相加，得到端部柔度 $C_{\rm tip}$（m/N）。乘以载荷 $F$ 后得到端部变形 $\delta$。

To estimate the first natural frequency $f_n$, the static deflection curve is normalized to unit tip displacement and used as the mode shape $\phi(s)$. Distributed member mass $\rho A\,ds$ and concentrated masses $m_i$ are weighted by squared modal displacement; $\phi_i$ is the mode-shape value at each concentrated mass. The reflected inertia $J_{\rm ref}$ is weighted by $\theta_j^2$, the squared joint rotation per unit tip displacement. Rigid downstream bodies also contribute the kinetic energy of rotation about their own centres of mass. Together these terms give modal mass $m_{\rm eff}$. The ratio $f_n/f_b$ then compares this structural estimate with the entered target control bandwidth $f_b$ and the requested frequency separation.估算一阶固有频率 $f_n$ 时，将静挠曲线按单位端部位移归一化，作为振型 $\phi(s)$。杆件的分布质量 $\rho A\,ds$ 和各集中质量 $m_i$ 按振型位移的平方加权，$\phi_i$ 为各集中质量所在处的振型值；反射惯量 $J_{\rm ref}$ 则乘以 $\theta_j^2$，其中 $\theta_j$ 为单位端部位移对应的关节转角。下游刚体还计入绕自身质心转动的动能，不把整个构件压缩成端部点质量。相加得到等效模态质量 $m_{\rm eff}$，与端部柔度一起求出频率。最后用 $f_n/f_b$ 将这一结构频率估计与输入的目标控制带宽 $f_b$ 比较，检查设定的频率间隔。

Strength and output torque强度与驱动力矩
$$\sigma_{\max}=\frac{M_{\max}c}{I},\qquad n\sigma_{\max}\le\sigma_{\rm lim},\qquad\delta\le\delta_{\rm lim}.$$
$$\tau_{\rm req}\approx|\tau_g|+F|h|+(J_L+N^2J_m)\alpha+\tau_{\rm break},\qquad\tau_{\rm available}=\eta N\tau_m s.$$

The manual load case applies the entered transverse force $F$ to the selected member. In the bending-stress formula, $M_{\max}$ is the largest bending moment, $c$ is the distance from the neutral axis to the outer fibre, and $I$ is the area second moment. For a rectangular section loaded about both axes, the implementation adds the two bending-stress contributions. The safety factor $n$ multiplies the calculated stress before comparison with the entered material strength $\sigma_{\rm lim}$; tip displacement $\delta$ is checked against its limit $\delta_{\rm lim}$.手动工况将输入的横向校核力 $F$ 施加在所选杆件末端。弯曲应力式中，$M_{\max}$ 为最大弯矩，$c$ 为中性轴至最外缘的距离，$I$ 为截面二次矩；矩形截面同时绕两轴受弯时，程序叠加两向弯曲应力。计算应力乘以安全系数 $n$ 后，与输入的材料强度 $\sigma_{\rm lim}$ 比较；端部变形 $\delta$ 则与允许值 $\delta_{\rm lim}$ 比较。

For the manual torque estimate, $\tau_g$ is the gravity moment of the driven bodies, $FL$ is the transverse-load moment (using the actual moment arm h), and $(J_L+N^2J_m)\alpha$ is the inertia term at the entered acceleration. Their magnitudes are added to the friction estimate $\tau_{\rm break}$. Available output torque uses motor shaft torque $\tau_m$, reduction $N$, forward efficiency $\eta$ and the selected joint’s motor-size factor $s$. The reference-motion envelope instead obtains each joint torque from the full motion equation, including coupled motion and ground reactions.手动力矩估算中，$\tau_g$ 是随动构件的重力矩，$F|h|$ 是横向载荷对实际驱动轴的力矩，$(J_L+N^2J_m)\alpha$ 是给定角加速度下的惯性项；将这些幅值与摩擦估计 $\tau_{\rm break}$ 相加，得到所需转矩。可用输出转矩由电机轴转矩 $\tau_m$、减速比 $N$、正向效率 $\eta$ 和所选关节的电机规格系数 $s$ 相乘求得。参考动作包络采用整机运动方程，逐帧计入耦合运动和地面反力后求关节转矩。

Backdrive torque and kinetic energy反向驱动力矩与动能
$$\tau_{\rm break}\approx\tau_{f,g}+\frac{N\tau_{f,m}}{\eta_{\rm reverse}},\qquad E_{\rm rot}=\frac12(J_L+N^2J_m)\omega^2.$$

With gravity compensated, an external torque must overcome both gearbox and motor friction to start reverse motion. Here $\tau_{f,g}$ is gearbox friction expressed at the output, $\tau_{f,m}$ is friction at the motor shaft, and $\eta_{\rm reverse}$ is reverse transmission efficiency. The motor term is reflected through reduction $N$ and added to the gearbox term to estimate breakaway torque $\tau_{\rm break}$. Dividing by lever arm $L$ gives the perpendicular end force required by this model.重力得到补偿后，外部转矩需要克服减速器和电机摩擦，才能使关节反向转动。$\tau_{f,g}$ 是输出端的减速器摩擦转矩，$\tau_{f,m}$ 是电机轴摩擦转矩，$\eta_{\rm reverse}$ 是反向传动效率。电机摩擦经减速比 $N$ 折算后，与减速器摩擦相加，得到启动反向运动所需的转矩 $\tau_{\rm break}$；再除以力臂 $L$，可求得末端垂直施力的估计值。

Rotational energy $E_{\rm rot}$ uses the driven-chain and reflected rotor inertias at the entered output speed $\omega$ (rad/s). Both friction and inertia affect manual backdriving: reducing $N$ lowers the reflected motor-friction term linearly and the rotor-inertia term quadratically, while increasing the motor torque needed for a given output load.转动动能 $E_{\rm rot}$ 按随动链惯量、转子反射惯量和输入的输出角速度 $\omega$（rad/s）计算。摩擦与惯量共同影响外力推动关节时的表现：减小 $N$ 会线性降低折算电机摩擦，并按平方关系降低反射惯量；在相同输出负载下，电机自身则需要提供更大的轴转矩。
