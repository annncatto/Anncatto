Fast-weight view: splitting SmolVLA attention into parallel MLPs快权重视角：把 SmolVLA 注意力拆成并行 MLP

Why did stalling the 96.6M BF16 expert body push 2.5× larger updates specifically into odd CA k_proj/v_proj, and why do L01 and L15 fail hardest under layer swaps? Following the fast-weight framework of MiTA [12], we derive SmolVLA’s alternating CA/SA directly as input-induced two-layer MLPs and test compression versus routing on the frozen 100k checkpoints.

为什么 BF16 主体停滞后，更新会集中流向奇数 CA 层的 k_proj/v_proj（2.5 倍），且替换 L01 与 L15 代价最高？借用 MiTA [12] 的快权重框架，我们可以把 SmolVLA 的交替 CA/SA 直接推导成输入生成的两层 MLP，并在冻结的 100k 权重上实测压缩与路由的效果。

Worked derivation: odd CA versus even SA结构推导：奇数 CA 与偶数 SA 的快权重拆解

For one query $\boldsymbol{q}\in\mathbb{R}^d$ and key–value memory $\boldsymbol{K},\boldsymbol{V}\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times N}$, scaled dot-product attention takes the form of an $N$-wide two-layer MLP whose weights are generated on the fly by the input (with query-dependent softmax normalizer $Z(\boldsymbol{q})=\sum_{j=1}^N\exp(\hat{\boldsymbol{k}}_j^\top\boldsymbol{q})$):

对单个 Query $\boldsymbol{q}\in\mathbb{R}^d$ 与键值矩阵 $\boldsymbol{K},\boldsymbol{V}\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times N}$，标准注意力可写成一个隐层宽度为 $N$、权重由当前输入即时生成的两层快权重 MLP（其中归一化分母 $Z(\boldsymbol{q})=\sum_{j=1}^N\exp(\hat{\boldsymbol{k}}_j^\top\boldsymbol{q})$ 依赖 $\boldsymbol{q}$）：

$$\operatorname{Atten}(\boldsymbol{q},\boldsymbol{K},\boldsymbol{V})=\hat{\boldsymbol{V}}\exp\!\left(\hat{\boldsymbol{K}}^\top\boldsymbol{q}\right),\qquad \hat{\boldsymbol{K}}=\frac{\boldsymbol{K}}{\sqrt{d}},\quad \hat{\boldsymbol{V}}=\frac{\boldsymbol{V}}{Z(\boldsymbol{q})}.$$

How does this map onto SmolVLA’s alternating layers ($P=305$ prefix tokens, $H=50$ action queries, $d=64$, 5 KV heads expanded to 15 Q heads)? Because $\exp(\cdot)$ acts elementwise, an odd cross-attention layer (L01–L15) cleanly splits its 305-wide observation MLP into six parallel source sub-MLPs sharing $1/Z(\boldsymbol{q}_t)$:

把它落到 SmolVLA 的交替层（前缀 $P=305$、动作 $H=50$、$d=64$、5 个 KV head 扩至 15 个 Q head）会看到什么？因为 $\exp(\cdot)$ 逐元素作用，每个奇数交叉注意力层（L01–L15）先用专家独有的 FP32 矩阵重编码冻结 VLM 缓存，再自然拆成 6 路共享分母 $Z(\boldsymbol{q}_t)$ 的并行子 MLP：

$$\tilde{\boldsymbol{K}}_{\mathrm{obs}}^{(l)}=W_K^{(l)}\boldsymbol{K}_{\mathrm{obs}}^{(l)},\qquad \tilde{\boldsymbol{V}}_{\mathrm{obs}}^{(l)}=W_V^{(l)}\boldsymbol{V}_{\mathrm{obs}}^{(l)},$$
$$\operatorname{CA}_h(\boldsymbol{q}_t)=\frac{1}{Z(\boldsymbol{q}_t)}\!\left(\underbrace{\sum_{j\in\text{4 cams}}}_{4\times 64}+\underbrace{\sum_{j\in\text{text}}}_{48\,(23\text{ valid})}+\underbrace{\exp\!\left(\hat{\boldsymbol{k}}_{\text{state}}^\top\boldsymbol{q}_t\right)\boldsymbol{v}_{\text{state}}}_{1}\right).$$

  • One fixed 305-wide observation MLP reused across 10 Euler steps: Prefix keys/values are computed once per control call, so all 50 action queries × 10 denoising steps pass through the same scene-induced MLP. Despite occupying 1/305 positions (0.3%), the single state unit receives 20.4% of CA mass (40% at L01), and wrist_right receives 25.2%.

    10 步去噪全程复用同一张 305 宽观测网络：前缀只预计算一次，50 个动作 Query 在 10 步 Euler 积分中反复穿过同一张场景专属 MLP。仅占 1/305（0.3%）的 state 单元却承接 20.4% 的 CA 权重（L01 达 40%），wrist_right 占 25.2%。

  • Odd CA $W_K, W_V$ are the only trainable observation remappers: When BF16 rounding stalls the 96.6M slow-weight body, AdamW updates concentrate in these 16 FP32 matrices (17.3% vs 7.0%), remapping the observation MLP to match the stalled body—making boundary layers L01 (1.97×) and L15 (1.81×) most sensitive under swaps.

    奇数 CA 的 $W_K, W_V$ 是唯一可训练的观测快权重重编码器：混合存储的 BF16 舍入锁住 96.6M 慢权重主体后，更新只能大幅改写这 16 个 FP32 矩阵（17.3% 对 7.0%），重新映射观测网络以适配停滞主体——因此首尾边界层 L01（1.97×）与 L15（1.81×）跨模型替换时升幅最大。

  • Even SA builds a $(305+t+1)$-wide composite MLP: Even layers (L00–L14) have no expert prefix $W_K, W_V$; they concatenate the frozen 305-wide VLM prefix (68–95% of SA mass) with a causal $(t+1)$-wide action sub-MLP (5–33%, peaking at L10) that updates every Euler step. By contrast, π0.5 makes the 50-step action block bidirectional, while SmolVLM2’s pixel shuffle acts as static upfront compression ($1024\to 64$).

    偶数 SA 是 $(305+t+1)$ 宽的复合 MLP：偶数层（L00–L14）不设专家前缀 $W_K, W_V$，直接拼接冻结 VLM 的 305 宽前缀（仍占 SA 权重的 68–95%）与随去噪步更新的 $t+1$ 宽因果动作子网（占 5–33%，L10 达峰）；而 π0.5 的动作块双向可见，SmolVLM2 的 pixel shuffle 则属于前置静态压缩（$1024\to 64$）。

Zero-retraining probes: compression vs routing on the 305-token prefix免重训探针：305 维前缀上压缩与路由谁更有效？

Borrowing MiTA’s algorithmic-generalization test (Appendix C), we freeze both 100k checkpoints and shrink odd-CA prefix width at inference via three schemes: Route-only (Top-$k$), Compress-only (Agent-style temporal pooling of 50 action queries into $m$ landmarks), and MiTA ($m$ shared summaries + $k$ routed raw K/V).

借鉴 MiTA 附录 C 的免重训算法泛化测试，我们冻结两套 100k 权重，仅在推理时缩小奇数 CA 的 305 维前缀宽度，对照三种方式：纯路由（Top-$k$）、纯压缩（把 50 步动作 Query 按时序池化成 $m$ 个地标摘要，Agent 风格）与 MiTA（$m$ 个共享摘要 + $k$ 个路由原始 K/V）。

  • 4 reusable experts match 50 private Top-$k$ sets: Early (0–24) and late (25–49) action queries share 0.66–0.77 Top-16 mIoU; replacing 50 private Top-16 sets (FP32 0.0151) with 4 shared temporal landmarks gives 0.0152—confirming MiTA’s shared-expert premise.

    4 个共享地标专家即可替代 50 套私有 Top-$k$：前 25 步与后 25 步动作的 Top-16 重叠 mIoU 达 0.66–0.77；将 50 套私有 Top-16（FP32 0.0151）减为 4 个共享地标仅升至 0.0152，直接印证了 MiTA 复用 top-$k$ 集合的思路。

  • Compression beats hard routing on already-compressed tokens: Unlike high-res ViTs (MiTA Table 6), a single global summary fails (m=1: +123%), yet just 4 compressed landmarks hold FP32 at 0.0129 (+1.5%), whereas hard Top-16 raises loss by 18% (12% in Mixed). Combining both in MiTA wins at every budget: budget 16 (m=4, k=12) reaches 0.0126 (below Full 305), and budget 64 matches Full MAE (0.0283).

    在已压缩前缀上，压缩显著优于硬路由截断：与高分辨分类 ViT（MiTA 表 6）相反，单个全局摘要会失效（m=1 升 123%），但仅 4 个时序压缩摘要就能把 FP32 稳在 0.0129（+1.5%），而硬 Top-16 截断反而使 loss 升 18%（混合升 12%）。结合两者的 MiTA 在同预算下全面占优：预算 16（m=4, k=12）即降至 0.0126（低于全量 305），预算 64 的 MAE 与全量持平（0.0283）。

  • Reverse depth sparsity (“narrow shallow, wide deep”): Opposite to image-classification ViTs (MiTA Fig. 4), shallow CA locks onto 2.6–4.8 anchor tokens (40–52% of visual patches never enter Top-16), then fans out to 53.7 at L15 in FP32 versus 45.1 in Mixed—explaining why Mixed loses less under Top-16 truncation.

    深度稀疏规律与分类 ViT 相反（浅窄深宽）：浅层 CA（L01–L05）仅盯 2.6–4.8 个锚点（40–52% 视觉 patch 从未进入 Top-16），到末层 L15 展开至 53.7（FP32）对 45.1（混合）——混合 L15 更窄，也解释了它受 Top-16 截断影响更小。
