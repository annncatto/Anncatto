Fast-weight view: splitting SmolVLA attention into parallel MLPs快权重视角：把 SmolVLA 注意力拆成并行 MLP

Why did stalling the 96.6M BF16 body redirect 2.5× larger updates specifically into odd CA k_proj/v_proj, and why do boundary layers L01 and L15 degrade hardest under swaps? Rewriting SmolVLA’s alternating CA and SA layers as input-induced two-layer MLPs—following MiTA’s fast-weight formulation [12]—answers both directly and links them to inference-time compression and routing.

为什么 BF16 主体停滞后，更新会集中流向奇数 CA 层的 k_proj/v_proj（2.5 倍），且首尾层 L01 与 L15 跨模型替换代价最高？借用 MiTA [12] 的快权重框架，将 SmolVLA 的交替 CA 与 SA 层改写为输入即时生成的两层 MLP，可以直接回答这两个问题，并自然过渡到推理期的压缩与路由探针。

Worked derivation: odd CA versus even SA结构推导：奇数 CA 与偶数 SA 的快权重拆解

Slow weights vs fast weights: Expert MLPs and projection matrices $W_K, W_V$ are slow weights learned across training; the 305 projected key–value pairs $\tilde{\boldsymbol{K}}_{\mathrm{obs}},\tilde{\boldsymbol{V}}_{\mathrm{obs}}$ act as a scene-specific fast-weight MLP generated on the fly per observation.

慢权重 vs 快权重：动作专家主体与投影矩阵 $W_K, W_V$ 是跨示教训练得到的慢权重；每帧前缀经 $W_K, W_V$ 生成的 305 组键值 $\tilde{\boldsymbol{K}}_{\mathrm{obs}},\tilde{\boldsymbol{V}}_{\mathrm{obs}}$ 则是随当前场景即时生成的快权重 MLP。

For one query $\boldsymbol{q}\in\mathbb{R}^d$ and key–value memory $\boldsymbol{K},\boldsymbol{V}\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times N}$, scaled dot-product attention is an $N$-wide two-layer MLP normalized by a query-dependent denominator $Z(\boldsymbol{q})$:

对单个 Query $\boldsymbol{q}\in\mathbb{R}^d$ 与键值矩阵 $\boldsymbol{K},\boldsymbol{V}\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times N}$，标准缩放点积注意力可写为隐层宽 $N$、以依赖 $\boldsymbol{q}$ 的分母 $Z(\boldsymbol{q})$ 归一化的两层 MLP：

$$\operatorname{Atten}(\boldsymbol{q},\boldsymbol{K},\boldsymbol{V})=\hat{\boldsymbol{V}}\exp\!\left(\hat{\boldsymbol{K}}^\top\boldsymbol{q}\right),\qquad \hat{\boldsymbol{K}}=\frac{\boldsymbol{K}}{\sqrt{d}},\quad \hat{\boldsymbol{V}}=\frac{\boldsymbol{V}}{Z(\boldsymbol{q})},\quad Z(\boldsymbol{q})=\sum_{j=1}^N\exp\!\left(\hat{\boldsymbol{k}}_j^\top\boldsymbol{q}\right).$$

Because $\exp(\cdot)$ acts elementwise, each odd cross-attention layer (L01–L15, $P=305$, $H=50$, $d=64$) remaps the frozen VLM cache via trainable FP32 $W_K^{(l)}, W_V^{(l)}$ and splits into six parallel source sub-MLPs sharing $1/Z(\boldsymbol{q}_t)$:

由于 $\exp(\cdot)$ 逐元素作用，每个奇数交叉注意力层（L01–L15，$P=305, H=50, d=64$）先用专家独有的 FP32 矩阵重编码冻结 VLM 缓存，再自然拆成 6 路共享分母 $Z(\boldsymbol{q}_t)$ 的并行子 MLP：

$$\tilde{\boldsymbol{K}}_{\mathrm{obs}}^{(l)}=W_K^{(l)}\boldsymbol{K}_{\mathrm{obs}}^{(l)},\qquad \tilde{\boldsymbol{V}}_{\mathrm{obs}}^{(l)}=W_V^{(l)}\boldsymbol{V}_{\mathrm{obs}}^{(l)},$$
$$\operatorname{CA}_h(\boldsymbol{q}_t)=\frac{1}{Z(\boldsymbol{q}_t)}\!\left(\underbrace{\sum_{j\in\text{4 cams}}}_{4\times 64}+\underbrace{\sum_{j\in\text{text}}}_{48\,(23\text{ valid})}+\underbrace{\exp\!\left(\hat{\boldsymbol{k}}_{\text{state}}^\top\boldsymbol{q}_t\right)\boldsymbol{v}_{\text{state}}}_{1}\right).$$
  • One reused observation MLP, anchored by state and wrist_right10 步去噪复用同一观测网络，state 与 wrist_right 占比最高

    All 50 queries × 10 Euler steps pass through the same 305-wide prefix. Despite holding 1/305 positions (0.3%), the single state unit takes 20.4% of CA mass (40% at L01), and wrist_right takes 25.2%.

    50 步动作 × 10 步 Euler 积分全程复用同一张 305 宽前缀网络。仅占 1/305（0.3%）的 state 承接 20.4% 的 CA 权重（L01 达 40%），wrist_right 占 25.2%。

  • Odd CA $W_K, W_V$ are the sole trainable observation remappers奇数 CA 的 $W_K, W_V$ 是唯一可训练的观测快权重重编码器

    Once BF16 rounding stalls the 96.6M slow-weight body, updates concentrate in these 16 FP32 matrices (17.3% vs 7.0%), remapping the observation MLP to compensate for the frozen body and making boundary layers L01 (1.97×) and L15 (1.81×) hardest to swap.

    BF16 慢权重主体停滞后，更新集中改写这 16 个 FP32 矩阵（17.3% 对 7.0%），重新映射观测快权重以适配停滞主体，使首尾边界层 L01（1.97×）与 L15（1.81×）跨接代价最高。

  • Even SA forms a $(305+t+1)$-wide composite MLP偶数 SA 构成 $(305+t+1)$ 宽的复合 MLP

    Without expert prefix $W_K, W_V$, even layers (L00–L14) pair the frozen VLM prefix (68–95% of SA mass) with a causal $(t+1)$-wide action sub-MLP (5–33%, peaking at L10), whereas π0.5 applies bidirectional attention across the action block.

    偶数层（L00–L14）不设专家前缀 $W_K, W_V$，将冻结 VLM 前缀（占 SA 权重 68–95%）与每步更新的因果动作子网（5–33%，L10 达峰）拼接，而 π0.5 的动作块双向可见。
