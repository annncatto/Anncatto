加入双玩偶数据：任务分布发生了变化Adding two-toy demonstrations changes the task distribution

后续又补充了 120 条双玩偶数据，覆盖三种物体相对布局，并在此基础上改变摆放位置。下面展示三种采集布局和两段失败表现。现有记录没有把这次混合数据与前三轮检查点完整关联，因此不把“新增 120 条”自动换算为某个确定总数据量，也不把它合并成 150 条继续训练的同一次实验。

A later extension adds 120 two-toy demonstrations with three relative layouts and placement variations. The layouts and two failure clips appear below. Available records do not fully link this mixture to the earlier checkpoints, so the additions are not assigned an assumed total or merged into the 150-episode training stage.

补充后，在已测试的单玩偶和双玩偶场景中都出现失败。双玩偶并不只是把单玩偶轨迹重复两次：策略要选择先抓谁，抓完第一个后重新判断剩余目标，还要区分“当前抓取结束”与“全部收集结束”。两种玩偶的外观、空间关系、抓取顺序与轨迹阶段共同构成了新的条件分布。

Both single- and two-toy failures were observed after the extension. Two-toy collection is not simply replaying a single-toy trajectory twice: the policy selects a target, reinterprets the scene after the first deposit, and distinguishes one completed grasp from complete collection. Appearance, layout, order, and phase jointly change the conditioning distribution.

若同样的图像与状态附近，在示教中既有“伸向左边”又有“伸向右边”的合理动作，条件分布可能变成多峰。流匹配可以表达多样动作，但这不保证有限数据下的目标选择与长程阶段转换都能学好。应先标注首选目标、抓取顺序、抓起后重观察和第二次接近这些节点，检查相似条件下的示教是否具有可辨别的上下文；不能直接把失败解释成动作平均或模型容量不足。

Nearby observations may admit distinct demonstrated actions, such as reaching left or right. Flow matching can represent multimodal behavior, but that does not guarantee reliable target selection and long-horizon transitions with limited data. Annotate first target, grasp order, re-observation, and the second approach; check whether context distinguishes the choices before attributing failure to action averaging or insufficient capacity.

验证顺序应先解决接口与时间对应，再讨论模型能力。若相机顺序、归一化、复位状态或动作单位不一致，再大的模型也在解决错误的问题。确认输入输出成套之后，数值更新探针能回答“参数有没有有效改变”；固定场景测试再回答“这种改变有没有保留旧能力、学到新能力”。

Verify interfaces and temporal correspondence before interpreting model capability. Camera order, normalization, reset, and units determine whether the policy is solving the intended problem. Once those are consistent, update probes establish whether parameters actually change; fixed-scene evaluations establish whether those changes preserve old behavior and acquire new behavior.

下一轮：让每次改动都有可比较的结果Next experiment: make each change comparable

  • 固定测试条件。预先确定复位姿态、底盘起点、玩偶相对位置、照明和任务结束条件。分别建立旧场景、距离变化、左右位置变化和双玩偶布局组，给每次运行分配编号。
  • 按阶段评分。记录接近、稳定夹持、抬起、入盒、全部收集，以及完成时间、重试和人工干预。保留总尝试数；失败阶段与失败原因不要使用同一个字段。
  • 先做最小对照。在相同样本、种子、学习率、统计量和执行方式下，比较可训练权重 FP32 与历史存储方式；再单独对照旧/新归一化和不同数据混合比例。不要同时改变这些条件后只看最终视频。
  • 记录真实部署时间。保存观察采样、发送、推理起止、chunk 返回、每步执行时刻，以及 raw action、覆盖后目标和带命令编号的反馈。由此才可计算观察年龄、队列耗尽和配对跟踪误差。
  • 双玩偶单独验收。先确认单玩偶基线不回归，再检查第一抓与第二抓。必要时补充中间状态恢复、第二次接近和收集结束的示教，而不是只增加相似起始画面。
  • Fix evaluation conditions.Specify reset pose, base origin, relative toy placement, lighting, and termination. Maintain old-scene, distance-shift, lateral-shift, and two-toy groups, with a run ID for every attempt.
  • Score stages.Record approach, secure grasp, lift, deposit, full collection, completion time, retries, and interventions. Preserve denominators and separate failure phase from inferred cause.
  • Start with minimal comparisons.Match samples, seed, rate, statistics, and execution when comparing FP32 trainable storage with the historical recipe. Then separately test normalization and mixture ratios.
  • Log deployment time.Capture observation, transfer, inference, chunk return, and command timestamps, with raw actions, modified targets, and command-linked feedback.
  • Evaluate two-toy collection separately.First preserve the single-toy baseline, then test first and second grasps. Add recovery, second-approach, and termination demonstrations where coverage is missing.

当前最明确的结果是：小规模示教已经能让 SmolVLA 完成具体任务；继续训练的收益没有随数据量单调增加；π0.5 在已记录场景中表现更连贯。模型架构解释了信息怎样用于动作生成，数值类型解释了更新怎样被保存，异步机制解释了动作怎样赶上真实时间。把这三条链条和实验记录逐一对应，才能进一步判断下一次该改数据、训练，还是执行系统。

The clearest findings are that limited demonstrations already enable SmolVLA to complete the task, more data and continued training do not monotonically improve behavior, and the recorded π0.5 rollout appears more continuous. Architecture explains information flow, dtypes explain update preservation, and asynchrony explains execution timing. Linking each chain to evidence makes the next change actionable.
