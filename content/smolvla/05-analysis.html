  • Transition coverage: 54.10% of shoulder labels lie below −95 and 89.22% of gripper labels below 5. Prioritize lift, reopening, deposit and recovery states alongside these frequent low/closed states.

    阶段覆盖：54.10% 的肩抬标签低于 −95，89.22% 的夹爪标签低于 5。除常见的低位与闭合状态外，优先补充抬升、重新开爪、投放和恢复状态。

  • Training configuration: the FP32 recipe improves matched fit, and swaps show that experts and projectors adapt together. Track weight storage, Adam state and compute precision as separate configuration fields.

    训练配置：FP32 方案改善配对拟合，模块交换则显示专家与投影共同适配。配置记录分别列出权重存储、Adam 状态和计算精度。

  • Execution diagnosis: score demonstration fit, output validity and task completion separately. Align raw predictions, accepted commands and subsequent feedback when locating a failure stage.

    执行诊断：示范拟合、输出有效性与任务完成分别计分。定位失败阶段时，对齐原始预测、已接收命令和后续反馈。
