Implications实验启发

  • Training precision: Keep trainable weights and AdamW moments in FP32 (configured and logged separately from BF16 autocast compute) so low-lr updates do not stall the 96.6M expert body.

    训练精度：可训练权重与 AdamW 矩估计保持 FP32（与 BF16 autocast 计算精度分开配置与登记），避免衰减期 96.6M 专家主体停滞。

  • Data skew & normalization: In the 150 demos, 54% of shoulder_lift labels sit below −95 and 89% of right-gripper labels below 5; add lift/reopen/recovery demos (with right-wrist views) and add an std floor to near-constant channels such as the left gripper (std 0.22).

    数据与归一化：150 条数据中 54% 的 shoulder_lift 标签低于 −95、89% 的右夹爪标签低于 5；应补采抬升、漏抓重开与恢复示范（含右腕视角），并对近常量左夹爪（std 0.22）设置标准差下限。

  • Chunk handoff & evaluation: Separate offline fit, output bounds, chunk-handoff continuity (overlap aggregation / RTC), and stage-level live success, logging raw chunks and command IDs.

    块交接与评测：将离线拟合、输出合法性、块交接连续性（重叠聚合 / RTC）与分阶段真机成功率拆开计分，并同步记录原始动作块与命令 ID。
