Implications实验启发

Putting the training, data, and live-execution findings together explains why fixing precision alone improved offline fit without eliminating closed-loop failure. Three distinct bottlenecks acted at three different layers of the stack:

把训练精度、数据分布与真机执行三段证据拼合起来，就能回答整章的核心问题：为什么 FP32 把训练拟合误差降了一半，真机仍会在后期陷入锯齿卡死？因为三层瓶颈同时存在，各自需要对应手段解决：

  • Training precision: Mixed BF16/FP32 storage does not merely slow convergence—it silently locks the 96.61M expert body during cosine decay and forces 26 FP32 interface and CA K/V tensors into 1.6–2.5× compensatory drift. Keep trainable parameters and AdamW moments in FP32 (with BF16 autocast kept separate as a compute choice), and log weight storage, optimizer state, and compute dtype independently.

    训练精度层：BF16/FP32 混合存储不只是拖慢收敛，而是在学习率衰减期锁死 96.61M 专家主体、把 26 个 FP32 接口与奇数 CA K/V 张量推向 1.6–2.5 倍的过度代偿。应将可训练权重与 AdamW 矩估计保持为 FP32（与 BF16 autocast 计算精度分开配置），并在实验记录中分别登记三项 dtype。

  • Data skew and state conditioning: In the 150-demo dataset, 54.10% of right shoulder_lift labels sit below −95 and 89.22% of right-gripper labels sit below 5, while the static box-holding left gripper has a tiny standard deviation (0.220) that amplifies small hardware variations into 49σ standardized spikes. Prioritize demonstrations covering lift, reopening, deposit, and recovery transitions—especially right-wrist viewpoints around missed grasps—and audit near-constant channels before z-score normalization.

    数据分布与归一化层：150 回合数据中，54.10% 的右臂 shoulder_lift 标签低于 −95、89.22% 的右夹爪标签低于 5，而近常量的左夹爪（标准差 0.220）会把轻微物理差异放大为 49σ 的输入尖峰。后续采集应重点补足抬升、漏抓后重新开爪、投放与后退恢复示范（尤其是漏抓时的右腕视角），并对近常量状态通道设置标准差下限保护。

  • Chunk handoff and interface bounds: Both the 120-demo wrist_roll oscillation (42.9% of TV on 25-frame boundaries) and the FP32 shoulder_lift 200-second sawtooth (cliff drops every ~0.9 s) occur at replanning handoffs, while unbounded linear heads still cross −100 near boundary targets. Evaluate demonstration fit, output legality, chunk-handoff smoothness (overlap aggregation / RTC), and closed-loop stage success separately, logging raw chunks and command IDs alongside accepted targets and feedback.

    块交接与接口边界层：120 条模型的 wrist_roll 25 帧振荡（占 TV 的 42.9%）与 FP32 模型 200 秒的 shoulder_lift 锯齿断崖（每约 0.9 s 发生一次），均集中在动作块交接时刻；无界输出头在贴边目标处也仍会越界。评测必须将示范拟合、输出合法性、块交接平滑度（重叠聚合 / RTC）与分阶段真机成功率拆开计分，并在日志中同步保留原始动作块与命令序号。
