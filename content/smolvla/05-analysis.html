Implications实验启发

Combining the training, data, and live-execution evidence explains why switching to FP32 lowered offline loss without eliminating closed-loop failure. Three distinct factors operated at three layers of the system:

将训练精度、数据分布与真机执行三部分证据合并来看，FP32 虽然使离线 flow loss 下降 52.3%，但闭环仍受另外两层因素制约，三层问题需要分别处理：

  • Training precision: Mixed BF16/FP32 storage stalls the 96.61M expert body during cosine decay and concentrates weight drift (1.6–2.5× larger than in B) into 26 FP32 interface and CA K/V tensors. Keep trainable parameters and AdamW moments in FP32 (with BF16 autocast configured separately as a compute choice), and log weight storage, optimizer state, and compute dtype independently.

    训练精度：BF16/FP32 混合存储在学习率衰减期使 96.61M 专家主体基本停止更新，并使 26 个 FP32 接口与奇数 CA K/V 张量的偏移达到 B 的 1.6–2.5 倍。应将可训练权重与 AdamW 矩估计保持为 FP32（与 BF16 autocast 计算精度分开配置），并在实验记录中分别登记三项 dtype。

  • Data skew and state normalization: In the 150-demo dataset, 54.10% of right shoulder_lift labels sit below −95 and 89.22% of right-gripper labels sit below 5, while the nearly static box-holding left gripper has a small standard deviation (0.220) that turns moderate hardware differences into a 49σ standardized input. Prioritize demonstrations covering lift, reopening after missed grasps, deposit, and recovery—including right-wrist viewpoints—and apply a standard-deviation floor to near-constant channels.

    数据分布与归一化：150 回合数据中，54.10% 的右臂 shoulder_lift 标签低于 −95、89.22% 的右夹爪标签低于 5，而近常量的左夹爪（标准差 0.220）会把轻微物理差异放大为 49σ 的标准化输入。后续采集应补足抬升、漏抓后重新开爪、投放与后退恢复示范（含对应右腕视角），并对近常量状态通道设置标准差下限。

  • Chunk handoff and interface bounds: Both the 120-demo wrist_roll oscillation (42.9% of TV on 25-frame boundaries) and the FP32 shoulder_lift sawtooth oscillation across 95–290 s (step drops every ~0.9 s) occur at replanning handoffs, while unbounded linear heads still cross −100 near boundary targets. Evaluate demonstration fit, output legality, chunk-handoff continuity (overlap aggregation / RTC), and stage-level live success separately, logging raw chunks and command IDs alongside accepted targets and feedback.

    块交接与接口边界：120 条模型的 wrist_roll 25 帧振荡（占 TV 的 42.9%）与 FP32 模型 95–290 s 的 shoulder_lift 锯齿振荡（每约 0.9 s 阶跃回落），都集中在动作块交接时刻；无界输出头在贴边目标处也仍会越界。评测应将示范拟合、输出合法性、块交接连续性（重叠聚合 / RTC）与分阶段真机成功率拆开计分，并在日志中同步保留原始动作块与命令序号。
