实验启发Implications

  • 采集应覆盖关键状态转换：150 回合中，右肩抬标签约 54.10% 位于 −95 以下，右夹爪约 89.22% 位于 5 以下。除常见的低位与闭合状态，还应增加抬起、重新张开、投放与失败恢复的条件覆盖。

    Cover critical transitions: about 54.10% of shoulder labels are below −95 and 89.22% of gripper labels below 5 in the 150 episodes. Add coverage of lifting, reopening, deposit and recovery alongside common low/closed states.

  • 先核对实际更新，再解释 loss：本轮 FP32 方案的权重更新与固定输入拟合都有改善；专家与投影又通过交换试验证实共同适配。参数存储、动量、计算精度和初始化应分别登记，避免把不同配方的收益归入单一开关。

    Inspect real updates before interpreting loss: the FP32 recipe improves both weight changes and matched-input fit, while swaps establish expert–projection co-adaptation. Record storage, moments, compute and initialization separately to attribute recipe effects correctly.

  • 训练拟合、动作合法性和执行结果分层评价：示范 MAE、标定范围外预测、Host 接受目标、请求前反馈各自对应不同环节。配合动作块边界和条件替换，才能把一次失败定位到生成、约束、交接或观测适应。

    Score fit, validity and execution separately: demonstrated-action MAE, out-of-range predictions, Host-accepted targets and pre-request feedback concern different stages. Chunk boundaries and condition substitutions connect failures to generation, constraints, handoff or observation adaptation.

  • 训练精度：固定 FP32 权重和动量、初始化、数据、步数与采样方式，只切换 FP32 计算和 BF16 autocast；配对比较 loss、参数变化、耗时与显存，拆开存储和计算的作用。

    Training precision: fix FP32 weights/moments, initialization, data, steps and sampling, then vary only FP32 versus BF16-autocast computation. Compare matched loss, weight changes, time and memory to separate storage from compute.

  • 内部补零维：固定模型、观测和噪声，对照常规 32 维积分与逐步固定 14 个补零维，观察真实 18 维的拟合和越界数量。

    Padded dimensions: fix model, observations and noise; compare ordinary 32-dimensional integration with clamping the 14 padded dimensions each step, measuring fit and crossings in the 18 real dimensions.

  • 任务与交接：固定复位、场景和结束条件，A/B 各测试原位置、距离变化、横向变化和双玩偶布局；记录抓取、抬起、投放、后退与人工干预。保留原始动作块和命令序号，按相同观测对齐重规划交接。

    Task and handoff: match reset, scene and termination while testing A/B at original, distance-shifted, lateral-shifted and two-toy layouts. Score grasping, lifting, deposit, retreat and interventions; retain raw chunks and command IDs for aligned replanning analysis.
