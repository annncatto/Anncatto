类型、范围与精度是三个层次Format, range, and precision

FP32、FP16、BF16 是浮点数格式，也常被用作张量 dtype 的名称。“数值类型”适合描述它们如何存储，“数值精度”则描述可区分数值的细密程度。两者有关，但位数相同并不意味着范围和精度相同：FP16 与 BF16 都占 16 bit，却把这些位分配给了不同的部分。

FP32, FP16, and BF16 name floating-point formats and tensor dtypes. A type specifies storage; numerical precision describes how finely values can be distinguished. Equal bit counts do not imply equal range or precision: FP16 and BF16 allocate their 16 bits differently.

$$x=(-1)^s\left(1+\frac{m}{2^p}\right)2^e\qquad\text{(normal numbers)}.$$

$s$ 是符号位，$m$ 是 fraction 的整数编码，$p$ 是 fraction 位数，$e$ 是去掉偏置后的指数。指数决定大致覆盖范围，fraction 决定同一数量级内的间距。上式只写正规数；零、非正规数、无穷与 NaN 另有编码。可结合 IEEE 754 与 bfloat16 的格式说明阅读。

Here $s$ is the sign, $m$ the stored fraction integer, $p$ the fraction width, and $e$ the unbiased exponent. Exponents largely determine range; fraction bits determine spacing within a magnitude. The formula covers normal values; zero, subnormals, infinity, and NaN have separate encodings. See IEEE 754 and bfloat16.

BF16 保留接近 FP32 的指数范围，因此较不容易因量级大而溢出，却只有 7 个显式 fraction 位；FP16 在同一数量级内更细，但范围窄。格式的特点不能简单排成“BF16 全面优于 FP16”或“FP32 才能训练”：矩阵乘法、归一化、loss 归约和权重更新，对范围、误差与速度的要求并不一样。

BF16 preserves an exponent range close to FP32 but has only seven explicit fraction bits. FP16 is finer within a magnitude but has a narrower range. Neither format is universally superior. Matrix multiplication, normalization, loss reduction, and weight updates impose different range, accuracy, and speed requirements.

一次训练中，dtype 分布在哪些地方Where dtypes live during training

“用 BF16 训练”至少可能指两件事：把参数本体永久存成 BF16，或保持参数为 FP32、仅让适合的算子在 BF16 下计算。后者是常见的自动混合精度做法。PyTorch autocast 为算子选择计算类型，不会自动给每个低精度参数增设 FP32 主副本；GradScaler 也不能代替主副本。

“BF16 training” may mean permanently storing parameters in BF16, or keeping FP32 parameters while computing suitable operations in BF16. Autocast implements operator-level mixed precision. It does not automatically create an FP32 master copy for every low-precision parameter, and GradScaler does not replace one. PyTorch autocast.

在 SmolVLA 中，视觉主干、动作专家和几个投影层可能来自不同的构造路径，因而具有不同 dtype。读取一条顶层配置，或查看一个参数，都不足以证明整网的安排。尤其要分开检查 frozen 与 trainable 两组：冻结主干使用 BF16，并不妨碍动作专家用 FP32 存储。推理检查点是 BF16，也不能单独证明训练时没有 FP32 主副本，因为导出时可能转换过格式。

SmolVLA’s backbone, expert, and projectors can acquire different dtypes through different constructors. One top-level flag or one inspected parameter cannot characterize the whole policy. Audit frozen and trainable groups separately. A BF16 exported checkpoint alone also does not prove that training lacked an FP32 master copy: export may convert weights.

梯度非零，为什么权重可能不变Why a nonzero gradient may leave a weight unchanged

$$w_{k+1}=\operatorname{round}_{\mathrm{dtype}}(w_k+\Delta w_k).$$

对于正规 BF16 数，在 $[2^e,2^{e+1})$ 内，相邻数间距为 $\operatorname{ULP}=2^{e-7}$。若更新小于朝该方向舍入边界的距离，写回后仍是原值。离二次幂边界较远时，这个边界约为半个 ULP；边界两侧间距不一定相等，恰在中点还要考虑 ties-to-even。ULP 解释的是存储分辨率，不是梯度是否正确。

Within a normal BF16 binade $[2^e,2^{e+1})$, spacing is $\operatorname{ULP}=2^{e-7}$. An update smaller than the directional rounding boundary writes back as the same value. Away from powers of two the boundary is approximately half an ULP; spacing can be asymmetric at a binade boundary, with ties-to-even at exact midpoints. ULP concerns storage resolution, not gradient correctness.

例如，$0.01$ 转成 BF16 后为 $0.010009765625$，附近的 ULP 为 $2^{-14}\approx6.10\times10^{-5}$。每次尝试减去 $10^{-5}$，小于半个 ULP，直接写回 BF16 就不会改变它。重复同一个更新，也不能凭空累积被舍掉的余量。若另有 FP32 权重保存累计值，即使一两步后的 BF16 计算副本没变，累计足够多步后仍会跨过可表示数的边界。

For example, $0.01$ becomes $0.010009765625$ in BF16, with nearby ULP $2^{-14}\approx6.10\times10^{-5}$. A decrement of $10^{-5}$ is below half an ULP, so direct BF16 writes leave it unchanged. Repeating that update cannot recover discarded residuals. FP32 storage retains the accumulated value, eventually crossing a boundary visible in a BF16 compute copy.

The chart deliberately fixes the update to isolate rounding. Actual AdamW steps depend on gradient moments and are not simply equal to the learning rate:

$$\Delta w_k=-\eta_k\frac{\widehat m_k}{\sqrt{\widehat v_k}+\varepsilon}-\eta_k\lambda w_k.$$

因此，不能仅凭 $\eta=10^{-5}$ 就判定某个参数一定不动；应比较实际更新与该参数附近的间距。反过来，继续微调时降低学习率虽然可以减小扰动，也可能让更多更新落到低精度写回的舍入边界以内。这给后文的低学习率继续训练提出了一个可检验的机制，而不是已经确认的实验原因。AdamW 更新定义。

A learning rate of $10^{-5}$ alone cannot determine whether a weight moves. Compare its actual update with local spacing. Lowering the learning rate may reduce disruptive changes while also pushing more updates below low-precision writeback boundaries. This supplies a testable mechanism for continued fine-tuning, rather than a confirmed explanation of these experiments. AdamW update definition.

一项针对 VLA 微调的数值审计A numerical audit of VLA fine-tuning

Full-Weight VLA Fine-Tuning Without an fp32 Master Copy Silently Erases Most Updates（2026）检查了可训练权重的实际存储，并在 LIBERO-Object 上对照低精度写回与 FP32 参数方案。作者在特定 LeRobot 版本中发现：SmolVLA 的动作专家会继承 BF16 配置，而外部投影层仍为 FP32，顶层设置并不完整代表运行时类型。

Full-Weight VLA Fine-Tuning Without an fp32 Master Copy Silently Erases Most Updates (2026) audits trainable-weight storage and compares low-precision writeback with FP32 storage on LIBERO-Object. In the inspected LeRobot version, SmolVLA’s expert inherits BF16 configuration while external projectors remain FP32; a top-level flag therefore does not fully describe runtime dtypes.

其实现路线是固定数据、训练与评分条件，改变可训练权重的存储；每隔 100 步，在优化器更新前后比较参数，测量没有改变的元素比例。默认学习率下，SmolVLA 执行 50 步开环动作块时，两种方案的成功率为 59.3% 与 69.5%；重训并改为每 10 步重规划后，差距缩为 88.4% 与 92.6%。这些是该论文内部对照，不能与其他 LIBERO 分数直接排名。

The study changes trainable-weight storage under controlled training and scoring, probing unchanged parameter elements around optimizer steps every 100 steps. At the default rate, SmolVLA scores 59.3% versus 69.5% with 50-action open-loop execution. Retraining and replanning every ten actions narrows the comparison to 88.4% versus 92.6%. These are within-study comparisons, not a cross-paper leaderboard.

修正原则是让可训练参数保有 FP32 累计值，再用 autocast 降低合适算子的计算成本。标准 PyTorch 方案中，FP32 参数本身就承担主副本的作用，无需再复制一套完全相同的 FP32 参数。若使用显式低精度计算副本、分片优化器或其他框架，则要核对其具体更新路径。上述论文的 SmolVLA 评分复现有未解决的限制，低学习率实验也处于欠训练区间；它提供了数值机制和对照证据，不足以诊断本次实机失败。论文限制。

The repair preserves FP32 accumulation for trainable parameters while autocast lowers eligible compute costs. Ordinary FP32 parameters already serve as the master values; they do not require a redundant FP32 clone. Explicit compute copies, sharding, and other frameworks need their own update-path audit. The paper reports unresolved SmolVLA scoring-reproduction limitations and undertraining at low rates. Its mechanism does not diagnose these real-robot failures. Study limitations.

把数值设置变成可核对的实验条件Making numerical settings auditable

下面是独立说明更新路径的 PyTorch 片段。真实策略需要先确定冻结范围，并在加载权重、建立优化器和恢复训练之后重新检查类型；这不是直接替换历史运行配置的命令。

The following PyTorch sketch illustrates the update path. A real policy must first establish freezing rules and recheck dtypes after loading and resuming. It is not a drop-in command for historical runs.

trainable = [p for p in policy.parameters() if p.requires_grad]
for p in trainable:
    p.data = p.data.float()

optimizer = torch.optim.AdamW(trainable, lr=1e-4)
optimizer.zero_grad(set_to_none=True)
with torch.autocast("cuda", dtype=torch.bfloat16):
    loss = policy(batch)[0]
loss.float().backward()
torch.nn.utils.clip_grad_norm_(trainable, 10.0)
optimizer.step()

在 FP16 autocast 下，通常还需要 GradScaler 来处理小梯度下溢，且梯度裁剪应在 unscale 之后；BF16 指数范围较宽，通常不需要同样的缩放。两者都不能保证网络里的每个算子自动采用最合适的类型。还应记录各模块的参数与梯度 dtype、优化器一二阶矩的 dtype、非零梯度覆盖率，以及一次更新前后真正改变的参数比例。

FP16 autocast commonly needs GradScaler for small gradients, with clipping after unscaling. BF16’s wider exponent range usually avoids that need. Neither guarantees that every operation uses the desired dtype. Record module-level parameter and gradient dtypes, optimizer moment dtypes, nonzero-gradient coverage, and the proportion of parameters actually changed by a step.

假设有 100M 个 FP32 可训练参数，参数、梯度、AdamW 两份矩估计分别约为 0.4、0.4、0.8 GB，合计约 1.6 GB；这还没有计入冻结主干、激活、缓存与临时张量。这个估算解释了为什么保留 FP32 累计值需要额外资源，也解释了为什么冻结主干的小模型可以优先把有限显存用在可训练部分的数值可靠性上。

For 100M trainable FP32 parameters, weights, gradients, and two AdamW moments occupy approximately 0.4, 0.4, and 0.8 GB: about 1.6 GB before frozen weights, activations, caches, and temporaries. This explains both the cost of reliable accumulation and why a small frozen-backbone policy can prioritize FP32 storage for its trainable component.
