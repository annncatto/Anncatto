底盘小速度：四组数值沿两条路径对齐Small base velocities: aligning four streams across two branches

同一数据集在两个不同生成模型上出现类似问题时，当前优先排查顺序是：启停条件不可辨识或时间对齐 → 停止帧占比与阶段分布 → 连续生成的中间值及动作块执行 → 归一化实现错误。这是基于现象提出的工作假设，尚未通过真实输出日志确认各项的影响。

When two generative models show similar issues on the same data, the current working order is: start/stop observability or temporal alignment → stopped-frame and phase distribution → intermediate generated values and chunk execution → normalization implementation errors. This is a diagnostic priority, not a confirmed causal ranking.

四组数值应来自两条对应路径：示教参考动作及其归一化值，模型在同一观察条件下预测的归一化动作及其反归一化值。推理时，示教未来动作并不作为网络输入。图中将它们分开画，避免把“原始标签 → 归一化标签 → 模型预测”误读为实际部署的数据流。

The four streams follow two branches: reference actions and their normalization; model predictions under the same observation and their restoration. Future demonstrated actions are not inference inputs. The diagram separates the branches to avoid suggesting that normalized future labels are fed to the deployed model.

Replay demonstration observations spanning stop, forward, and stop, record full chunks, and align by intended action time $t+j/f$. Compare raw references/restored predictions in one panel and normalized references/predictions in the other. Preserve observation ID, chunk ID, within-chunk index, camera/state timestamps, and processor-statistics version. Once real-robot states diverge from demonstrations, a separate demonstration is no longer framewise ground truth.

  • 标签分支先异常：实际标准化结果与公式不符，或启停标签相对图像、状态错位，优先检查字段、统计、采样与时间对齐。
  • 归一化预测已经偏向停止或中间值：偏差在反归一化之前已存在，继续检查启停信息、阶段覆盖、生成分布和采样；这仍不能只凭曲线断言数据或网络谁负责。
  • 归一化预测正确，反归一化结果却不符合已记录统计：检查动作字段顺序、错误统计、重复反归一化、dtype 与后处理路径。
  • 反归一化值合理，最终发送值或实际动作异常：另存覆盖、限幅后的发送值，结合过时动作跳过、队列交接和命令编号定位执行环节。
  • Reference branch is already inconsistent: check fields, statistics, sampling, and alignment if normalized labels disagree with the formula or start/stop timing disagrees with observations.
  • Normalized predictions already favor stop/intermediate values: investigate observability, phase coverage, the conditional distribution, and sampling before restoration. Curves alone do not isolate data from model causes.
  • Normalized predictions are appropriate but restoration disagrees with logged statistics: check field order, wrong statistics, duplicate restoration, dtypes, and postprocessing.
  • Restored predictions are appropriate but sent commands or motion are not: log post-limit targets, stale-step skipping, queue transitions, and command-linked feedback.

现有历史评估只留下了可能经过覆盖与限幅的单步目标，没有保存上述两路网络输出，因此目前无法补齐真实的四流预测曲线。前文展示的只是可复算的示教与标准化结果。这组诊断能够先区分“转换是否按约定工作”与“预测何时开始偏离”，而启停条件本身是否可辨识，仍要配合相应图像、状态和必要的历史观察判断。
