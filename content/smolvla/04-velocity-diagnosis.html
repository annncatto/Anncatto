Base velocity底盘速度

Turning to the base channels of the same October 23 record, main forward travel spans 0.833–5.633 s ($|a_x|>0.05$) and the main turn spans 22.500–23.167 s ($|a_\theta|>5$). Three stopping windows—after forward travel, after turning, and during raised-arm transfer—examine whether small residual commands explain base creep.

继续检查同一条 10 月 23 日记录的底盘通道：主要前进区间为 0.833–5.633 s（$|a_x|>0.05$），主要转向区间为 22.500–23.167 s（$|a_\theta|>5$）。下面选取前进后、转向后和抬臂转移三个停车窗口，核查小幅非零指令的来源与量级。

Across the 420 stopping frames, 90.0%–97.5% of forward commands stay within $|a_x|\leq0.001$, yet neither forward nor angular commands ever hit exact zero; during arm transfer, angular commands hold a sustained negative median of −0.058434. Meanwhile, lateral velocity y.vel is commanded strictly zero across all 2,936 frames, yet lateral feedback still reports P95 values of 0.003221–0.004832, separating sensor/odometry floor noise from commanded motion.

在 420 帧停车窗口中，90.0%–97.5% 的前进指令落在 $|a_x|\leq0.001$ 以内，但前进与转向指令从未归零；抬臂搬运阶段，转向指令还保持 −0.058434 的持续负向偏置。与此同时，侧移速度指令 y.vel 在全部 2,936 帧严格为零，侧移反馈的绝对值 P95 仍达 0.003221–0.004832，说明零指令下反馈本身也带有小幅非零读数。

Using AlohaMini’s Processor and the 120-demo statistics on CPU, a zero stop label normalized and restored in FP32 yields an error of $5.59\times10^{-9}$; rounding the normalized label to BF16 before FP32 restoration yields $-3.63\times10^{-6}$.

使用 AlohaMini 的 Processor 与 120 条示教统计在 CPU 上复算：零值停车标签在 FP32 下归一化再还原的误差为 $5.59\times10^{-9}$；若先将标准化标签舍入为 BF16 再以 FP32 还原，误差为 $-3.63\times10^{-6}$。

The observed stopping-command P95 is 230–310 times larger than one BF16 label round-trip error. Single-step label quantization is therefore negligible compared with what the network actually predicts—which redirects our numerical investigation from label conversion to weight updates during training.

实测停车指令的 P95 达到单次 BF16 标签往返误差的 230–310 倍，排除了“单纯标签量化导致停车微动”的解释。数值精度真正产生决定性影响的环节不在标签转换，而在训练时的权重写入——这正是下一节精度对照要检验的核心。
