The recorded absolute forward-command P95 values are about 230–310 times larger than this single BF16-label round-trip error. Their magnitude therefore calls for tracing policy predictions and postprocessing. The nonzero lateral-feedback readings require an independent displacement reference to separate actual motion from velocity-estimation error.
