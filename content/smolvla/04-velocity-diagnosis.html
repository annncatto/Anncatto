Stop-label arithmetic check停止标签的算术检查

The CPU arithmetic check uses AlohaMini’s numeric Processor and the 120-demo statistics. With BF16 autocast, the statistics and restored output remain FP32: a stop label restores to a residual of $5.59\times10^{-9}$, or $-3.63\times10^{-6}$ if the normalized label is first quantized to BF16.
