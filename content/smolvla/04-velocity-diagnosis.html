指令残余的数值量级Residual command scale

按 $\sigma_x=0.055243$ 折算，三个停止窗口的前向指令绝对值 P95 对应约 0.015–0.020 的标准化等效偏差。独立算术检查中，停止标签量化与反归一化的残差比这些实测指令残余小约 230–310 倍。这项比较将动作输出偏离停止值的量级与单次逆变换的舍入量级区分开。

Dividing by $\sigma_x=0.055243$, the forward-command absolute-value P95 in the three stopping windows corresponds to an equivalent standardized deviation of about 0.015–0.020. In the independent arithmetic check, stop-label quantization and restoration leave residuals about 230–310 times smaller. This separates the scale of the stopping-command deviations from single-transform rounding.

停止标签的算术检查Stop-label arithmetic check

使用 AlohaMini 的数值处理器 Processor 和 120 条数据的统计量，单独检查停止标签的归一化与反归一化。该 CPU 算术检验开启 BF16 autocast，统计量和恢复输出均为 FP32。保持输入停止标签为 FP32 时，恢复残差为 $5.59\times10^{-9}$；先将标签量化为 BF16 时，恢复残差为 $-3.63\times10^{-6}$。

An independent CPU arithmetic check uses AlohaMini’s numeric Processor and the 120-demo statistics to normalize and restore a stop label. BF16 autocast is enabled; statistics and restored outputs remain FP32. An FP32 input stop label restores with residual $5.59\times10^{-9}$; quantizing the label to BF16 first gives $-3.63\times10^{-6}$.
