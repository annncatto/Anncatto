底盘速度Base velocity

底盘小速度的诊断依次检查启停条件与时间对齐、停止帧和任务阶段分布、动作生成及 chunk 执行、归一化映射。两个生成模型在同一数据集上出现相似现象，将排查重点指向共有的数据条件与执行接口。

Base-velocity diagnosis examines start/stop observability and timing, stopped-frame and phase distributions, action generation and chunk execution, then normalization. Similar behavior from two generative models on the same data prioritizes shared data conditions and execution interfaces.

诊断记录包括示教参考动作、标准化参考标签、模型的标准化预测和反归一化预测。前两项描述监督目标，后两项描述同一观察条件下生成的动作。

The diagnostic record contains raw demonstrated actions, normalized references, normalized model predictions, and restored predictions. The first pair represents supervision; the second represents generated actions under the same observation.

对包含静止、前进和停止的片段进行观察重放，保存完整 chunk，并按目标时刻 $t+j/f$ 对齐。原始动作面板对照示教与反归一化预测，标准化面板对照标签与网络输出。记录字段包括 observation ID、chunk ID、块内步号、相机与状态时间戳，以及统计量版本。

Replay observations across stop, forward, and stop, save full chunks, and align by intended action time $t+j/f$. Compare demonstrated and restored actions in the raw-domain panel, and reference/prediction values in the normalized panel. Preserve observation IDs, chunk IDs, indices, camera/state timestamps, and statistics versions.

  • 标签转换：检查标准化结果与统计公式的一致性，以及启停标签和观察的时间对应。
  • 网络生成：统计预测在停止、前进和中间区域的分布，并按任务阶段与初始条件分组。
  • 动作恢复：按记录的均值、标准差和字段顺序复算反归一化结果。
  • 部署执行：对照恢复后的预测、限幅后发送值和命令反馈，定位过时动作、队列等待与块间跳变。
  • Label transformation: check numerical consistency and start/stop alignment with observations.
  • Generation: measure prediction distributions around stop, forward, and intermediate values, grouped by phase and initial condition.
  • Restoration: recompute outputs with recorded statistics and field order.
  • Execution: compare restored predictions, limited commands, and feedback to identify stale actions, waiting, and boundary jumps.
