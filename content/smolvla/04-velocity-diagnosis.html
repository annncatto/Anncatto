The main forward segment ends around 6 s and the turn ends before approximately 24 s. Base targets return near zero after both. Camera views at 76–80 s show the raised right arm transferring the toy toward the box, while base traces retain small residuals.

因此，策略已经表现出从前进、转向切换到近零速度的启停行为，但前向与转向目标仍有残余。横向目标全程为零，横向反馈却持续波动，说明执行与测量环节也参与了静止阶段的反馈残差。

The policy therefore switches from forward motion and turning to near-zero targets, but retains residual forward and angular commands. Lateral targets remain zero while lateral feedback fluctuates, identifying an additional execution or measurement contribution to stopping residuals.

使用相同统计量在 CPU 上调用反归一化处理器，FP32 停止标签恢复后的残差为 $5.59\times10^{-9}$；将该标签先转换为 BF16，恢复残差为 $-3.63\times10^{-6}$。两者均小于记录中 $v_x$ 残余目标的典型量级。单次反归一化舍入不足以解释主要残余指令；记录反映的是近零动作预测偏差与执行、测量残差共同存在。

Using the same statistics in the inverse processor on CPU yields a $5.59\times10^{-9}$ residual for the FP32 stop label and $-3.63\times10^{-6}$ after first converting that label to BF16. Both are below the typical recorded $v_x$ residual. Rounding in the inverse operation is insufficient to explain the principal residual commands; near-zero prediction deviations coexist with execution and measurement residuals.

动作数据流Action data flow {{smolvla:normalization}}

示教动作与标准化标签构成监督路径，网络输出与反归一化动作构成预测路径。按相同观察、动作块步号和目标执行时刻对齐后，$\delta v_x=\sigma_x\delta z_x$ 将两个数值域中的偏差联系起来。

Demonstrated and standardized actions form the supervision path; network outputs and restored actions form the prediction path. Alignment by observation, chunk index, and intended execution time relates their errors through $\delta v_x=\sigma_x\delta z_x$.
