反归一化误差Inverse-normalization error

在 CPU 的 BF16 autocast 下调用实验代码中的 LeRobot MEAN_STD 处理器，并使用 120 条数据的统计量，FP32 停止标签恢复后的残差为 $5.59\times10^{-9}$；先将同一标签转换为 BF16，再恢复，残差为 $-3.63\times10^{-6}$。后者的绝对值仍比三个停止窗口的前向目标绝对值 P95 小约 230–310 倍。单次停止标签量化与反归一化舍入不足以解释主要残余目标。

With the experimental code’s LeRobot MEAN_STD processor under CPU BF16 autocast and the 120-demo statistics, an FP32 stop label restores with a residual of $5.59\times10^{-9}$. Casting that label to BF16 before restoration yields $-3.63\times10^{-6}$. Even the latter magnitude is about 230–310 times below the forward-target absolute-value P95 in the stopping windows. Quantizing the stop label once and rounding the inverse operation cannot account for the principal residual targets.

主运动结束后，目标已降到近零，但仍未严格归零；机械臂目标还存在周期跳变。这份文件保存后处理动作与状态反馈，归一化域的网络输出未被记录。数据直接指向的两项改进是停止输出精度和动作块交接连续性。

Targets fall near zero after the main motions but do not reach exact zero; arm targets also show periodic jumps. The file stores postprocessed actions and state feedback, without normalized network outputs. The data directly identifies stopping-output precision and action-handoff continuity as improvement targets.

动作数据流Action data flow {{smolvla:normalization}}

示教动作与标准化标签构成监督路径，网络输出与反归一化动作构成预测路径。按相同观察、动作块步号和目标执行时刻对齐后，$\delta v_x=\sigma_x\delta z_x$ 将两个数值域中的偏差联系起来。

Demonstrated and standardized actions form the supervision path; network outputs and restored actions form the prediction path. Alignment by observation, chunk index, and intended execution time relates their errors through $\delta v_x=\sigma_x\delta z_x$.
