记录包含 2,936 帧，时间覆盖 0–97.833 s，录制频率为 30 Hz。约 15–25 s，肩部平转和腕部滚转目标出现成组往返；随后肩肘目标与夹爪开合持续反复调整，末段腕部偏航仍有小幅周期阶跃。蓝色目标曲线本身带有这些变化，橙色反馈大体随目标变化，震荡首先表现为指令侧反复修正。

The record contains 2,936 frames over 0–97.833 s at 30 Hz. Around 15–25 s, shoulder-pan and wrist-roll targets repeatedly reverse. Shoulder/elbow targets and gripper openings continue to cycle, with small periodic wrist-yaw steps near the end. These changes already appear in blue targets, which orange feedback broadly follows, locating the oscillation first in repeated command revisions.

事件表为右臂七个通道各保留三次最大相邻目标变化，共 21 条，全部落在 25 帧整数倍的位置。在 30 Hz 的记录坐标中，这一网格的间隔为 $25/30=0.833$ s。较大的目标跳变具有明确的刷新节律。

The event table retains three largest adjacent target changes for each of seven right-arm channels. All 21 occur at multiples of 25 frames, giving a $25/30=0.833$ s grid in the 30 Hz recording coordinates. Large target jumps therefore follow a distinct refresh rhythm.

这组证据将主要抖动定位在发送目标的连续性上：同一操作阶段反复给出不同目标，执行器随之往返。夹爪的大幅开合体现重复抓取尝试，腕部接近方波的小阶跃则体现短周期指令抖动；两者叠加在较慢的接近与转移轨迹上。

The evidence locates the principal jitter in command continuity: targets repeatedly change during the same task phase and the actuators follow. Large gripper cycles reflect repeated grasp attempts; smaller square-like wrist steps reflect short-period command jitter, superimposed on the slower approach and transfer trajectory.

25 帧对齐的跳变与动作块刷新处的接缝相符。每次重规划基于新观察和噪声生成候选动作块，块内 SA 建立的关系覆盖当前预测。新旧预测在相同执行时刻的目标差异，会在切换时形成跳变；跨块连续性需要在队列交接时处理，可采用重叠动作融合或 RTC 的待执行前缀约束。

The 25-frame-aligned jumps are consistent with discontinuities at action refreshes. Replanning generates a candidate chunk from a new observation and noise sample; within-chunk SA operates over the current prediction. Different old/new targets for the same execution time create a discontinuity at handoff. Queue handoff can preserve continuity through overlapping-action aggregation or RTC committed-prefix constraints.
