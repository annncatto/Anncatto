Periodic oscillation and 25-frame command jumps周期振荡与每 25 帧指令跳变

Right wrist_roll (gripper forearm rotation) peaks at ~0.60 Hz (~50 frames per full reversal—twice the 25-frame replanning period). Measuring cumulative command variation via Total Variation ($\mathrm{TV}$) across all 2,935 adjacent pairs shows that the 117 multiples of 25 (4% of steps) exhibit 5–18× larger jumps across all seven right-arm channels, accounting for 43% of wrist_roll TV and all 21 largest per-channel jumps:

右臂 wrist_roll（夹爪绕前臂轴旋转）主峰约 0.60 Hz（约 50 帧往返一次，正好是 25 帧重规划周期的两倍）。以累计总变差（$\mathrm{TV}$）将全部 2,935 对相邻采样按是否落在 25 帧整数倍分组（117 对，仅占 4%），七个右臂通道在边界处的平均跳变都是其余位置的 5–18 倍，贡献了 wrist_roll 43% 的累计变化，全部 21 个最大单步跳变也均落在此网格上：
