Trajectory analysis轨迹分析

Before looking inside model weights, we inspect what the deployed 120-demo / 100k SmolVLA policy actually commanded on the robot. The following arm and base analyses use episode 0 recorded on October 23 at 10:32:19: 2,936 frames spanning 97.833 s on a nominal 30 Hz timeline.

在拆解模型权重之前，先核对 120 条 / 100k SmolVLA 模型在真机上实际发出的控制指令。以下双臂与底盘分析基于 10 月 23 日 10:32:19 记录的 episode 0，共 2,936 帧，按标称 30 Hz 时间轴覆盖 97.833 s。

  • Policy command (Target): action stores the single-step control request after denormalization and postprocessing, specifying target joint positions or base velocities.
  • Measured feedback (Pre-request feedback): observation.state stores the robot-reported joint positions and base velocities sampled immediately before the policy call and command send.
  • 策略指令（Target）：action 记录动作经反归一化与后处理后的单步控制请求，表示要求关节到达的目标位置或底盘速度。
  • 实测反馈（Pre-request feedback）：observation.state 记录机器人上报的关节位置与底盘速度，采样早于本次策略调用与指令发送。

Because each control loop reads state, pulls either a fresh or queued action, and then transmits it, one recorded row pairs the pre-request state with the upcoming command; the robot’s physical response appears in subsequent rows.

每轮控制循环先读取当前状态，再取出新预测或队列中的动作并发送。因此，同一行配对的是调用前状态与下一条指令，机械臂的实际响应体现在后续帧中。

Throughout approach and transfer, shoulder, elbow, and gripper commands repeatedly reverse direction. The right wrist_roll joint rotates the gripper around the forearm axis; its target switches back and forth between two nearby plateaus while physical feedback tracks a few frames behind.

接近与搬运期间，肩、肘与夹爪指令反复折返修正。右臂 wrist_roll 关节控制夹爪绕前臂轴线旋转，其目标指令在两个相近平台间规律切换，物理反馈延迟数帧跟随。

Periodic oscillation周期振荡

Right wrist_roll commands and feedback share dominant spectral peaks at 0.60 Hz (16–26 s) and 0.583 Hz (38–50 s), and wrist_yaw peaks at 0.608 Hz (88–97.833 s). Every peak corresponds to roughly 50 frames per full back-and-forth cycle—twice the 25-frame vertical grid drawn above.

右臂 wrist_roll 的指令与反馈在 16–26 s 共同出现 0.60 Hz 主峰、在 38–50 s 为 0.583 Hz，wrist_yaw 在 88–97.833 s 的主峰为 0.608 Hz。三个峰值均对应约每 50 帧完成一次完整往返，正好是图中 25 帧灰色网格的两倍。

Command discontinuities指令跳变

To test whether these reversals lock onto a fixed replanning cadence across the entire episode, we partition all 2,935 adjacent frame pairs by the later frame index: the 117 pairs landing on multiples of 25 (25, 50, 75, …) versus the remaining 2,818 pairs, measuring absolute step-to-step command change and Total Variation (TV):

为检验这种反转是否贯穿整段轨迹并锁定在固定重规划节拍上，我们将全部 2,935 对相邻采样按后一帧序号分为两组——落在 25 帧整数倍（25、50、75……）的 117 对与其余 2,818 对，统计单步指令变化绝对值及其整段累加和 Total Variation（TV）：

$$\mathrm{TV}(a)=\sum_{t=1}^{T-1}|a_t-a_{t-1}|.$$

On right wrist_roll, those 117 boundary pairs make up just 4.0% of all transitions yet account for 42.9% of total variation. Across all seven right-arm channels, mean command jumps at every 25th frame are 5.2–18.1 times larger than at intermediate frames.

在右臂 wrist_roll 上，这 117 对边界采样仅占全部相邻对的约 4.0%，却贡献了整段累计变化的 42.9%。七个右臂通道在每 25 帧处的平均跳变幅度，都是其他位置的 5.2–18.1 倍。

Every one of the 21 largest per-channel jumps also falls on this 25-frame grid. At 30 Hz, one chunk transition occurs every 0.833 s, and alternating directional revisions across consecutive chunks produce the ~50-frame oscillation—showing that the jitter originates on the command side at chunk handoff rather than from motor resonance. Overlap aggregation and RTC prefix conditioning directly target this boundary discontinuity.

各通道选出的 21 个最大单步跳变也全部落在这个 25 帧网格上。30 Hz 下，每 0.833 s 发生一次动作块切换，相邻两块交替改向便形成了约 50 帧的完整往返周期——说明抖动源自指令侧的块交接重规划，而非电机机械共振。重叠动作聚合与 RTC 前缀约束正是针对这一交接断层。
