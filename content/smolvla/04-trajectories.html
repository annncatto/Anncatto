Trajectory analysis轨迹分析

The following arm and base analyses use episode 0 recorded on October 23 at 10:32:19. The model was trained on 120 demonstrations for 100k steps; its 2,936 frames span 97.833 s on a nominal 30 Hz timeline.

以下双臂与底盘分析使用 10 月 23 日 10:32:19 记录的 episode 0。模型在 120 条示教上训练 100k 步；记录共 2,936 帧，按标称 30 Hz 时间轴覆盖 97.833 s。

  • Policy command (Target): action records a single-step control request after denormalization and other postprocessing, specifying requested joint positions or base velocities.
  • Measured feedback (Pre-request feedback): observation.state contains robot-reported joint positions, base velocities, and other state, sampled before the current policy call and command transmission.
  • 策略指令（Target）：action 记录策略动作经反归一化等后处理后的单步控制请求，表示要求关节到达的位置或底盘达到的速度。
  • 实测反馈（Pre-request feedback）：observation.state 记录机器人上报的关节位置、底盘速度等状态，采样早于本次策略调用与指令发送。

Each loop reads feedback, obtains a new or queued policy action, then sends the command. A row therefore pairs the pre-request state with the next command; later feedback contains the response.

每轮循环先读取反馈，再生成或取出队列动作，最后发送命令。因此，同一行配对的是调用前状态与下一条指令，响应出现在后续反馈中。

Repeated shoulder, elbow and gripper revisions accompany approach and transfer. The right wrist_roll joint rotates the gripper about the forearm axis; its command alternates between nearby plateaus, with feedback following.

接近与搬运阶段，肩、肘和夹爪指令反复修正。右臂 wrist_roll 关节控制夹爪绕前臂轴线旋转，其指令在相近平台间交替，反馈随后跟随。

Periodic oscillation周期振荡

wrist_roll command and feedback both peak at 0.60 Hz in 16–26 s and 0.583 Hz in 38–50 s. wrist_yaw peaks at 0.608 Hz in 88–97.833 s; each corresponds to roughly 50 frames per full cycle.

16–26 s 的wrist_roll指令与反馈均在 0.60 Hz 处出现峰值，38–50 s 则为 0.583 Hz。88–97.833 s 的wrist_yaw峰值为 0.608 Hz，三者均约为每 50 帧一个完整周期。

Command discontinuities指令跳变

Group the 2,935 adjacent sample pairs by the later frame index: 25, 50, 75, … (117 pairs), versus the remaining 2,818 pairs. Each comparison uses the absolute change in the same joint’s policy command.

将 2,935 对相邻采样按后一帧序号分组：第 25、50、75……帧对应 117 对，其余位置为 2,818 对。比较量是同一关节两帧策略指令之差的绝对值。

Total variation (TV) sums these absolute changes over the whole record. Here $a_t$ is one joint’s policy command and $T$ is the number of recorded frames:

Total variation（TV）是整段记录中相邻指令变化绝对值的总和。$a_t$ 表示某个关节第 $t$ 帧的策略指令，$T$ 为记录帧数：

$$\mathrm{TV}(a)=\sum_{t=1}^{T-1}|a_t-a_{t-1}|.$$

For right wrist_roll, the 117 selected pairs contribute 42.9% of this sum, despite representing only about 4.0% of all pairs. Across joint channels, their mean absolute command change is 5.2–18.1 times larger than elsewhere.

对右臂 wrist_roll，这 117 对采样仅占全部相邻对的约 4.0%，却贡献了指令累计绝对变化的 42.9%。各关节通道在这些位置的平均变化幅度，为其他位置的 5.2–18.1 倍。
