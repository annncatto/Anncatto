轨迹分析Trajectory analysis

以下轨迹及下一节底盘速度均取自 SmolVLA 第一轮模型（120 条示教、训练 100k 步）在 2026-10-23 的评估，记录为 10:32:19 这一条的 episode 0。全段包含 2,936 帧，覆盖 0–97.833 s，录制时间按 30 Hz 编排。

These trajectories and the following base-velocity analysis use episode 0 of the 2026-10-23 evaluation record identified by 10:32:19, from the first SmolVLA checkpoint trained for 100k steps on 120 demonstrations. It contains 2,936 frames over 0–97.833 s on a 30 Hz recording timeline.

  • 策略指令（Target）：action 记录策略动作经反归一化等后处理后的单步控制请求，表示要求关节到达的位置或底盘达到的速度。
  • 实测反馈（Pre-request feedback）：observation.state 记录机器人上报的关节位置、底盘速度等状态，采样早于本次策略调用与指令发送。
  • Policy command (Target): action records a single-step control request after denormalization and other postprocessing, specifying requested joint positions or base velocities.
  • Measured feedback (Pre-request feedback): observation.state contains robot-reported joint positions, base velocities, and other state, sampled before the current policy call and command transmission.

采集顺序为读取状态、调用策略生成或取出队列动作、发送指令。同一行中的两条信号分别描述当前状态和接下来请求的动作，响应体现在后续反馈中。

The loop reads state, calls the policy to generate or dequeue an action, then sends the command. A row pairs the current state with the next requested action; its response appears in subsequent feedback.

肩部、肘部与夹爪在接近、抓取和转移过程中多次调整；腕部同时出现持续往返的小阶跃。指令曲线本身已有周期变化，反馈随指令往返。

Shoulder, elbow, and gripper commands change repeatedly during approach, grasping, and transfer, while the wrist shows persistent small alternating steps. Periodicity is already present in the commands, followed by the feedback.

周期振荡Periodic oscillation

16–26 s 的腕部滚转指令与反馈，频谱主峰都为 0.60 Hz，对应一个完整往返约 50 帧。38–50 s 的同一通道主峰为 0.583 Hz，末段 88–97.833 s 的腕部偏航主峰为 0.608 Hz，均接近 50 帧一周期。局部轨迹在两个平台间交替，指令变化先于反馈响应，体现了指令侧的周期修正。

The wrist-roll command and feedback in 16–26 s both peak at 0.60 Hz, or about 50 frames per full cycle. The same channel peaks at 0.583 Hz in 38–50 s; wrist yaw peaks at 0.608 Hz in 88–97.833 s. Both remain close to 50 frames per cycle. Local commands alternate between plateaus before the feedback responds, showing periodic command revisions.

指令跳变Command discontinuities

将全段 2,935 次相邻指令变化分为两组：帧号为 25 整数倍的 117 次变化，以及其余 2,818 次变化。前者只占 4.0%，各关节的平均跳变量却达到其他位置的 5.2–18.1 倍；腕部滚转有 42.9% 的累计绝对变化集中在这些位置。七个通道各自最大的三次变化，共 21 次，也全部落在这组网格上。

Of all 2,935 adjacent command changes, 117 occur at frame indices divisible by 25 and 2,818 elsewhere. The grid accounts for only 4.0% of changes, yet its mean jump is 5.2–18.1 times larger across channels. It carries 42.9% of wrist-roll total variation. All 21 events selected as the three largest changes per channel also lie on this grid.

25 帧网格对应 0.833 s 的刷新间隔，约 50 帧对应一次完整振荡，两者分别描述指令切换与往返周期。这种对齐关系与动作块交接处的不连续相符：新观察生成的指令与上一块末端指令存在差异，切换时便出现阶跃。块内自注意力建立当前预测序列的关系，跨块连续性还取决于重规划与队列交接。重叠动作融合或 RTC 的待执行前缀约束，直接针对这一衔接问题。

The 25-frame grid corresponds to a 0.833 s refresh interval; approximately 50 frames span a full oscillation. These describe command switching and the round-trip period, respectively. Their alignment is consistent with chunk-handoff discontinuities: a newly observed plan differs from the previous chunk at handoff, creating a step. Within-chunk self-attention models the current sequence; continuity between chunks also depends on replanning and queue handoff. Overlapping-action aggregation or RTC committed-prefix constraints address this junction.
