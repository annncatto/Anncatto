Experimental record实验记录

AlohaMini picks plush toys from the floor and deposits them into a box held by its left arm. Even after expanding the dataset from 120 to 150 demonstrations and training for another 100k steps, recorded rollouts still failed during approach, grasp establishment, retreat, and two-toy transitions.

AlohaMini 的任务是从地面抓起毛绒玩偶，放进左臂托住的盒子。将示教从 120 条扩充到 150 条并继续训练 100k 步后，真机记录仍在接近、建立抓取、后退以及转向第二个玩偶时失败。

The analysis follows one diagnostic sequence: first locating command discontinuities in the historical trajectories, then testing whether BF16 writeback rounding stalled continued training, and finally inspecting why an FP32 model with lower training loss still oscillated without completing the task on the robot.

本章按一条排查顺序展开：先从历史轨迹定位指令交接与停车偏差，再检验 BF16 写入舍入是否导致继续训练停滞，最后检查 FP32 模型的真机记录，定位训练 loss 下降后闭环仍持续振荡的原因。

Key findings关键发现

  • BF16 writeback rounding stalls the expert body during lr decay: Between 50k and 100k steps under cosine decay, mixed-storage model A lowers matched flow loss by only 1.8% (0.02753 to 0.02702), while its 96.58M BF16 expert body changes by 4.46% in relative L2 norm from initialization (0.08267% of BF16 elements change in the final 5k steps; all 32 RMSNorm weights stay at 0.00%). Training all weights, AdamW moments, and compute in FP32 (model B) updates the 16-layer expert body by 13.71% in relative L2 norm, lowers loss another 39.4% after 50k, and finishes 52.3% below A under uniform FP32 replay (0.01276 vs 0.02673).

    BF16 写入舍入导致动作专家主体在衰减期停滞：在 50k–100k 余弦学习率衰减期，混合存储模型 A 的配对 flow loss 仅下降 1.8%（0.02753 至 0.02702），占主体的 96.58M BF16 专家参数相对初始化的 L2 变化率仅为 4.46%（末 5k 步仅 0.08267% 的 BF16 元素改变，全部 32 个 RMSNorm 权重变化率为 0.00%）。将全部可训练参数、AdamW 矩估计与计算改为 FP32（模型 B）后，16 层专家主体相对 L2 变化率达 13.71%，50k–100k 期间 loss 继续下降 39.4%，100k 统一 FP32 回放比 A 低 52.3%（0.01276 对 0.02673）。

  • Different update distributions under mixed storage raise swap loss: While A’s BF16 body changes little, its 26 FP32 tensors change 1.6–2.5× more than in B—external projections (1.64M, relative L2 change 7.88% vs B’s 4.81%) and all eight odd cross-attention layers’ k_proj/v_proj (1.64M, 17.31% vs B’s 7.03%, reaching 24.6% at L15). Recombining external projections from one model with the expert from the other raises loss to 0.07326 and 0.06058; among single-layer swaps, replacing L01 or L15 raises loss the most.

    混合存储改变了各组参数的变化幅度，跨模型替换后 loss 明显升高：A 的 BF16 主体变化很小时，其余 26 个 FP32 张量相对初始化的 L2 变化是 B 的 1.6–2.5 倍——外部投影为 7.88%（B 为 4.81%），8 个奇数交叉注意力层的 k_proj/v_proj 为 17.31%（B 为 7.03%，L15 达 24.6%）。将两套模型的外部投影与动作专家跨组拼接，loss 升至 0.07326 与 0.06058；逐层替换中，更换 L01 和 L15 引起的 loss 升幅最大。

  • Start-window crossings and different active dimensions across projections: Right shoulder_lift (upper-arm pitch) crosses its −100 Host lower bound only in start windows where demonstrations sit at −99.74 to −98.98. FP32 120k model C reduces crossings from 390 to 153 out of 4,500 positions, while offline clipping lowers overall MAE by only 0.64%. Weight audits also show state_proj and action_out_proj change on exactly 56.25% (18/32) of elements, whereas action_in_proj changes on 100% because Gaussian noise and 10-step Euler sampling cover all 32 internal dimensions.

    贴边起始窗口越界与输入/输出投影更新维度不同：右臂 shoulder_lift（控制上臂抬放）低于 Host 下限 −100 的越界全部发生在示范位于 −99.74 至 −98.98 的起始窗口。FP32 120k 模型 C 将越界从 390/4,500 降至 153/4,500，而离线裁剪仅将整体 MAE 降低 0.64%。权重核对显示，state_proj 与 action_out_proj 恰好只有 56.25%（18/32）的元素发生变化，而 action_in_proj 为 100%，因为训练加噪与推理 Euler 积分覆盖了全部 32 维。

  • Chunk-handoff oscillation and phase-dependent input sensitivity: In the 120-demo record, command jumps concentrate at every 25th frame (contributing 42.9% of right wrist_roll total variation). In model B’s 300-second live run, shoulder_lift enters a sustained sawtooth oscillation across 95–290 s, dropping 60–75 units every ~0.9 s at chunk handoffs while the gripper stays closed. Controlled input substitutions show that sensitivity varies by phase: while the base moves (10.76 s), swapping base velocity changes arm output most (3.386); during oscillation (251.12 s), swapping only the wrist_right image shifts output by 53.75 Host units—nearly matching all four cameras combined (53.15).

    块交接振荡与不同阶段的输入响应差异：在 120 条模型记录中，指令跳变集中在每 25 帧处（占右臂 wrist_roll 累计变化的 42.9%）。在模型 B 的 300 秒真机记录中，shoulder_lift 在 95–290 s 持续出现锯齿振荡，每约 0.9 s 块交接处阶跃回落 60–75 单位，而夹爪保持闭合。受控观测替换显示，不同阶段对输出影响最大的输入不同：底盘前进时（10.76 s）替换底盘速度引起的变化最大（3.386），而振荡阶段（251.12 s）仅替换 wrist_right 单路图像就使输出变化 53.75 单位，与替换全部四路图像（53.15）相近。
