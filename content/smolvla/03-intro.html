Experimental record实验记录

AlohaMini picks toys from the floor and deposits them in a box held by its left arm. Recorded failures involve approach, grasp establishment, retreat and the transition to a second toy.

AlohaMini 的任务是从地面抓起玩偶，放进左臂托住的盒子。记录中的失败涉及接近目标、建立抓取、后退，以及转向第二个玩偶。

The analysis connects these behaviors to three stages: command generation and handoff, numerical adaptation during training, and the response to real-robot observations. Each stage uses its own recording or controlled comparison.

分析沿三个环节展开：动作指令的生成与交接、训练中的数值适配，以及模型对真机观测的响应。每个环节使用对应的运行记录或受控对照。

Key findings关键发现

  • Training precision: at the same 100k-step budget, the FP32 recipe lowers matched training-window flow loss by 52.3% relative to the recipe storing most trainable weights in BF16.

    训练精度：相同 100k 步预算下，FP32 方案的训练窗口配对 flow loss，比以 BF16 保存多数可训练权重的方案低 52.3%。

  • Module compatibility: exchanging experts or projectors between the two trained models raises loss, indicating co-adaptation of the expert and its interfaces.

    模块兼容：在两版模型之间交换专家或投影层会提高 loss，说明动作专家与输入输出接口存在共同适配。

  • Output bounds: the right shoulder_lift joint raises and lowers the upper arm. Its predicted targets fall below the interface lower bound of −100 less often in the FP32 120k model than in the mainly-BF16-weight 100k model.

    输出边界：右臂 shoulder_lift 关节控制上臂抬放。其预测目标低于接口下限 −100 的次数，FP32 120k 模型少于以 BF16 保存多数可训练权重的 100k 模型。

  • Command timing: in the historical 30 Hz record, average command jumps are larger at frame indices 25, 50, 75 and so on than at other adjacent samples.

    指令时序：在历史 30 Hz 记录中，第 25、50、75……帧处的平均指令跳变，比其他相邻采样位置更大。

  • Input replacements: re-predict actions after changing camera images or robot state at three saved times. The size of the action change differs across the original inputs and selected training-frame replacements.

    输入替换：在三个保存的真机时刻，替换相机图像或机器人状态后重新预测动作。替换前后的动作差值，在不同起始输入和所选训练参考帧组合下幅度不同。
