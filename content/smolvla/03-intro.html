Experimental record实验记录

AlohaMini picks plush toys from the floor and deposits them into a box held by its left arm. Even after expanding the dataset from 120 to 150 demonstrations and training for another 100k steps, recorded rollouts still failed during approach, grasp establishment, retreat, and two-toy transitions.

AlohaMini 的任务是从地面抓起毛绒玩偶，放进左臂托住的盒子。将示教从 120 条扩充到 150 条并继续训练 100k 步后，真机记录仍在接近、建立抓取、后退以及转向第二个玩偶时失败。

The analysis follows one diagnostic sequence: first locating command discontinuities in the historical trajectories, then testing whether BF16 writeback rounding stalled continued training, and finally inspecting why an FP32 model with lower training loss still oscillated without completing the task on the robot.

本章按一条排查顺序展开：先从历史轨迹定位指令交接与停车偏差，再检验 BF16 写入舍入是否让继续训练趋于停滞，最后回到 FP32 真机记录，分析训练 loss 明显下降后闭环执行仍持续振荡的原因。

Key findings关键发现

  • BF16 writeback stalling versus FP32 adaptation: During the 50k–100k cosine decay phase, mixed-storage model A plateaus near flow loss 0.027 (−1.8%) as its 96.61M BF16 expert body barely moves (4.46% L2 drift from initialization; 0.08267% changed in the final 5k steps; all 32 RMSNorms 0.00%). Promoting trainable weights, AdamW moments, and compute to FP32 (model B) restores adaptation across the expert body (13.71% drift), lowering loss another 39.4% after 50k and finishing 52.3% below A under uniform FP32 replay (0.01276 vs 0.02673).

    BF16 写入停滞与 FP32 主体更新：在 50k–100k 余弦学习率衰减期，混合存储模型 A 的 flow loss 基本停止下降（0.02753 至 0.02702，仅降 1.8%），占主体的 96.61M BF16 专家参数变化很小（相对初始化 L2 漂移 4.46%，末 5k 步仅 0.08267% 改变，32 个 RMSNorm 保持 0.00%）。改用全 FP32（模型 B）后，16 层专家主体恢复充分更新（L2 漂移 13.71%），50k–100k 期间继续下降 39.4%，100k 统一 FP32 回放比 A 低 52.3%（0.01276 对 0.02673）。

  • Mixed-storage drift asymmetry explains swap degradation: While A’s BF16 body barely shifts, its 26 FP32 tensors absorb much larger updates—external projections (1.64M, drifting 7.88% vs B’s 4.81%) and all eight odd cross-attention layers’ k_proj/v_proj (1.64M, drifting 17.31% vs B’s 7.03%, reaching 24.6% at L15). Crossing modules pairs differently adapted representations (loss rises to 0.07326 and 0.06058), and single-layer swaps degrade most strongly at the L01 entry and L15 readout cross-attention layers.

    混合存储造成的偏移差异导致换模块失配：A 的 BF16 主体变化很小时，其余 26 个 FP32 张量产生了远大于 B 的偏移——外部投影（1.64M，漂移 7.88% 对 B 的 4.81%）以及全部 8 个奇数交叉注意力层的 k_proj/v_proj（1.64M，漂移 17.31% 对 B 的 7.03%，L15 达 24.6%）。跨模型组合使两套接口与主体的中间表示不兼容（loss 升至 0.07326 与 0.06058），逐层替换则在 L01 入口和 L15 出口两个交叉注意力层恶化最明显。

  • Boundary crossings and 32D/18D asymmetry: Right shoulder_lift (upper-arm pitch) crosses its −100 Host lower bound only in start windows where demonstrations sit at −99.74 to −98.98. FP32 120k model C reduces crossings from 390 to 153 out of 4,500 positions, yet unbounded linear flow integration still produces boundary overshoots. Weight audits further show state_proj and action_out_proj update on exactly 56.25% (18/32) of elements, whereas action_in_proj updates 100% because Gaussian noise enters all 32 internal dimensions during training and Euler sampling.

    无界输出越界与 32/18 维不对称：右臂 shoulder_lift（控制上臂抬放）低于 Host 下限 −100 的越界全部发生在示范贴边（−99.74 至 −98.98）的起始窗口。FP32 120k 模型 C 将越界从 390/4,500 降至 153/4,500，但无界线性积分本身不执行关节限位。权重核对同时显示，state_proj 与 action_out_proj 恰好仅更新 56.25%（18/32）的元素，而 action_in_proj 更新 100%，说明 14 个无监督补零维在加噪与 Euler 采样中仍参与每步输入。

  • Chunk-handoff oscillation and phase-dependent input response: In the 120-demo record, command jumps concentrate at every 25th frame (contributing 42.9% of right wrist_roll total variation). In model B’s 300-second live run, shoulder_lift enters a sustained sawtooth oscillation across 95–290 s, dropping 60–75 units every ~0.9 s at chunk handoffs while the gripper stays closed. Controlled input substitutions show that dominant conditioning shifts with phase: base velocity produces the largest arm change while moving (10.76 s: 3.386), whereas during the oscillation phase (251.12 s), replacing only the wrist_right image shifts output by 53.75 Host units—nearly matching all four cameras combined (53.15).

    块交接振荡与分阶段输入响应：在 120 条模型记录中，指令跳变集中在每 25 帧处（占右臂 wrist_roll 累计变化的 42.9%）。在模型 B 的 300 秒真机记录中，shoulder_lift 在 95–290 s 持续处于锯齿振荡，每约 0.9 s 块交接处阶跃回落 60–75 单位，而夹爪保持闭合。受控观测替换显示条件响应随阶段改变：底盘前进时（10.76 s）底盘速度替换引起的动作变化最大（3.386），而振荡阶段（251.12 s）仅替换 wrist_right 单路图像就使输出变化 53.75 单位，与替换全部四路图像（53.15）相近。
