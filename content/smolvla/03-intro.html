Experimental record实验记录

AlohaMini picks plush toys from the floor into a box held by its left arm. Expanding training data from 120 to 150 demonstrations for another 100k steps still left failures during approach, grasp, retreat, and two-toy transitions, so we traced command discontinuities, training precision, cross-attention fast weights, and closed-loop FP32 behavior in order.

AlohaMini 的任务是从地面抓起毛绒玩偶放进左臂托盒。将示教从 120 条扩至 150 条再训 100k 步后，真机仍在接近、抓取、后退及双玩偶衔接处失败。本章按顺序核查：轨迹跳变 → 混合存储与 FP32 对照 → 注意力快权重探针 → FP32 真机闭环表现。

Even at 52% lower loss, FP32 oscillated 195 s live while gripper stayed closed.即使 loss 低 52%，FP32 真机仍在块交接处持续锯齿振荡 195 s。

  • FP32 converges faster, not slower, once lr decays: Full FP32 already beats mixed storage by 20k steps and continues falling through 100k while mixed storage plateaus near 0.027; extending FP32 to 120k stays flat at 0.013, confirming 100k had converged.

    FP32 并非更慢收敛，而是避免了衰减期停滞：全 FP32 在 20k 步已低于混合存储，50k–100k 继续下降 39%，混合存储则停在 0.027；将 FP32 续训至 120k 后曲线走平在 0.013，说明 100k 已基本收敛。

  • Mixed storage redirects updates into 26 FP32 tensors: With the 96.6M BF16 expert body barely changing, external projections and odd cross-attention k_proj/v_proj change 1.6–2.5× more than in FP32, so crossing modules between the two models raises loss to 0.061–0.073.

    混合存储使更新集中到 26 个 FP32 张量：BF16 主体变化很小时，外部投影与奇数 CA 层 K/V 的相对 L2 变化是 FP32 的 1.6–2.5 倍，因此将两套模型的外部投影与专家跨组拼接后 loss 升至 0.061–0.073。

  • Lower training loss still leaves chunk-handoff oscillation on the robot: Offline error and boundary crossings drop substantially in FP32, yet closed-loop execution still enters a 195-second sawtooth cycle driven by chunk handoffs and right-wrist visual conditioning.

    训练 loss 减半后，闭环仍受块交接与现场视角制约：FP32 大幅降低了离线误差与贴边越界，但真机仍出现 195 秒块交接锯齿振荡，且该阶段输出对 wrist_right 单路图像高度敏感。

  • Fast-weight probes explain both the weight split and live sensitivity: Rewriting odd CA as a 305-wide observation MLP shows that 4 compressed temporal summaries retain 98% of full-CA accuracy without retraining, while live 0.4 s start underflow breaks the L01 state anchor (40% → 0.4%) and 251 s oscillation routes heavily through wrist_right.

    快权重探针打通了权重变化与真机归因：将奇数 CA 拆成 305 宽观测 MLP 后，免重训仅 4 个时序压缩摘要即可保住 98% 精度；真机白盒追踪显示，0.4 s 起步越界打断了首层 state 锚点（40% → 0.4%），251 s 振荡期则高度依赖 wrist_right 路由。
