实验记录Experimental record

AlohaMini 的任务是从地面抓起玩偶，放入左臂托住的盒子。原 120 回合模型能完成部分摆放条件下的任务；增加到 150 回合继续训练后，记录中仍出现漏抓和未能后退。

AlohaMini must pick toys from the floor and place them in the box held by its left arm. The original 120-episode model completes the task in some layouts; after continuation on 150 episodes, recorded failures still include missed grasps and failure to retreat.

本轮围绕两个相连的问题展开：训练中的小更新是否真正写入了权重，以及拟合改善后，动作为什么仍会越界或在真机观测下改变。对照包括两份 150 回合、100k 步模型的精度方案，另跟踪 FP32 继续训练到 120k 的结果。

Two linked questions motivate this analysis: whether small optimizer updates actually reach stored weights, and why better-fitting actions can still cross bounds or change under robot observations. The precision comparison uses two 150-episode, 100k-update models, followed by an FP32 continuation to 120k.

关键发现Key findings

  • 更新与拟合：同一组 96.61M 权重元素在最后 5k 步起止检查点中数值不同的比例，A 为 0.08267%，B 为 99.99785%；统一 FP32 回放后，B 的 flow loss 比 A 低 52.3%。

    Updates and fit: between the checkpoints bounding the final 5k updates, 0.08267% of the same 96.61M weight elements differ in A versus 99.99785% in B. Under uniform FP32 replay, B has 52.3% lower flow loss.

  • 模块共同适配：把一版动作专家与另一版投影层直接组合，loss 升至 0.07326 或 0.06058，高于两套原配模型。专家和输入输出接口形成了共同适配。

    Module co-adaptation: combining one version's expert with the other's projections raises loss to 0.07326 or 0.06058, above both native models. The expert and its interfaces are co-adapted.

  • 输出边界：FP32 120k 的 153 个越界预测全部集中在起始窗口，对应目标本身已接近 −100。线性速度头、Euler 更新与均值方差还原保留连续输出，边界约束由接口另行处理。

    Output boundaries: all 153 crossings by FP32 120k occur in start windows whose targets are already near −100. The linear velocity head, Euler updates and mean/std restoration retain continuous outputs; interface constraints are separate.

  • 真机输入与执行：FP32 100k 的 300 秒记录仍出现肩抬越界请求与大幅跳变。对保存观测重新预测时，图像与状态替换都能改变输出，作用随观测变化。

    Robot inputs and execution: the FP32 100k model still produces shoulder requests outside bounds and large jumps in a 300-second record. Re-predicting saved observations shows effects from both image and state substitutions that vary with the observation.
