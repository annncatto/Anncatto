Experimental record实验记录

AlohaMini picks plush toys from the floor into a box held by its left arm. Expanding training data from 120 to 150 demonstrations still left failures during approach, grasp, retreat, and two-toy transitions. Below we first present what works and fails on the real robot—including the FP32 100k 300-second live run—then trace the underlying training-precision, fast-weight, and live-input mechanisms.

AlohaMini 的任务是从地面抓起毛绒玩偶放进左臂托盒。将示教从 120 条扩至 150 条再训 100k 步后，真机仍在接近、抓取、后退及双玩偶衔接处失败。本章先呈现真机表现与实测结果（含全 FP32 模型的 300 秒闭环验证），再逐层拆解轨迹跳变、混合存储更新机制、快权重探针与现场输入归因。
