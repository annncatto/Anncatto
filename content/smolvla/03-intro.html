Experimental record实验记录

AlohaMini picks plush toys from the floor and deposits them into a box held by its left arm. Even after expanding the dataset from 120 to 150 demonstrations and training for another 100k steps, recorded rollouts still failed during approach, grasp establishment, retreat, and two-toy transitions.

AlohaMini 的任务是从地面抓起毛绒玩偶，放进左臂托住的盒子。即便把示教从 120 条扩充到 150 条并继续训练 100k 步，真机记录仍在接近、建立抓取、后退以及转向第二个玩偶时失败。

The investigation traces these failures along one diagnostic chain: first locating command discontinuities in the historical trajectories, then testing whether BF16 writeback rounding stalled continued training, and finally checking why an FP32 model with half the training loss still stalled in closed-loop execution.

本章沿一条排查链展开分析：先从历史轨迹定位指令交接与停车偏差，再检验 BF16 写入舍入是否让继续训练陷入停滞，最后回到 FP32 真机记录，追问拟合误差减半后闭环执行为何依然卡死。

Key findings关键发现

  • BF16 writeback stalling versus FP32 adaptation: During the 50k–100k cosine decay phase, mixed-storage model A stalls near flow loss 0.027 because its 96.61M BF16 expert body barely moves (4.46% L2 drift from initialization; 0.08267% changed in the final 5k steps; all 32 RMSNorms 0.00%). Promoting trainable weights, Adam moments, and compute to FP32 (model B) moves adaptation back into the expert body (13.71% drift) and lowers matched flow loss by 52.3% (0.02673 to 0.01276).

    BF16 写入停滞与 FP32 主体适应：在 50k–100k 余弦学习率衰减期，混合存储模型 A 的 flow loss 停滞在 0.027 附近，因为占主体的 96.61M BF16 专家参数几乎锁死（相对初始化 L2 漂移仅 4.46%，末 5k 步仅 0.08267% 改变，32 个 RMSNorm 为 0.00%）。改用全 FP32（模型 B）后，适应回到 16 层专家主体（L2 漂移 13.71%），同预算配对 flow loss 下降 52.3%（0.02673 降至 0.01276）。

  • Precision-split over-compensation breaks module swaps: Because A’s BF16 body stalls, optimization pressure concentrates in its 26 FP32 tensors—external projections (1.64M, drifting 7.88% vs B’s 4.81%) and all eight odd cross-attention layers’ k_proj/v_proj (1.64M, drifting 17.31% vs B’s 7.03%, reaching 24.6% at L15). Swapping experts and projections pairs incompatible coordinate frames (loss rises to 0.07326 and 0.06058), and single-layer swaps spike hardest at the L01 entry and L15 readout cross-attention layers.

    精度分裂引发的过代偿导致换模块失配：A 的 BF16 主体停滞后，更新压力全部涌入仅有的 26 个 FP32 张量——外部投影（1.64M，漂移 7.88% 对 B 的 4.81%）以及全部 8 个奇数交叉注意力层的 k_proj/v_proj（1.64M，漂移 17.31% 对 B 的 7.03%，L15 达 24.6%）。跨模型拼接使失配坐标相撞（loss 升至 0.07326 与 0.06058），逐层替换则在 L01 入口和 L15 出口两个交叉注意力边界层恶化最剧烈。

  • Boundary crossings and 32D/18D asymmetry: Right shoulder_lift (upper-arm pitch) crosses its −100 Host lower bound only in start windows where demonstrations sit at −99.74 to −98.98. FP32 120k model C cuts crossings from 390 to 153 out of 4,500 positions, yet unbounded linear flow integration still leaves boundary overshoots. Weight audits further show state_proj and action_out_proj update on exactly 56.25% (18/32) of elements, whereas action_in_proj updates 100% because Gaussian noise feeds all 32 internal dimensions during training and Euler sampling.

    无界输出越界与 32/18 维不对称：右臂 shoulder_lift（控制上臂抬放）低于 Host 下限 −100 的越界全部发生在示范贴边（−99.74 至 −98.98）的起始窗口。FP32 120k 模型 C 将越界从 390/4,500 压低至 153/4,500，但无界线性积分仍无法自行守住关节限位。权重统计同时揭示，state_proj 与 action_out_proj 恰好仅更新 56.25%（18/32）的元素，而 action_in_proj 更新 100%，说明 14 个无监督补零维在加噪与 Euler 采样中仍在每步参与输入。

  • Closed-loop chunk handoff and visual lock-in: In the 120-demo record, command jumps concentrate at every 25th frame (contributing 42.9% of right wrist_roll total variation). In model B’s 300-second live run, shoulder_lift enters a 200-second sawtooth stall (95–290 s) that drops 60–75 units every ~0.9 s at chunk handoffs while the gripper stays closed. Controlled input substitutions show phase-dependent conditioning: base velocity dominates while moving (10.76 s: 3.386), whereas during the stall (251.12 s), replacing only the wrist_right image shifts output by 53.75 Host units—matching all four cameras combined (53.15).

    闭环块交接振荡与右腕视觉锁定：在 120 条模型记录中，指令跳变集中在每 25 帧处（贡献右臂 wrist_roll 累计变化的 42.9%）。在模型 B 的 300 秒真机记录中，shoulder_lift 在 95–290 s 陷入近 200 秒的锯齿卡死，每约 0.9 s 块交接瞬间断崖跌落 60–75 单位，而夹爪全程闭合。受控观测替换进一步表明条件依赖随阶段切换：底盘移动时（10.76 s）底盘速度影响最大（3.386），而卡死阶段（251.12 s）仅替换 wrist_right 单路图像即可引发 53.75 单位的动作改变，与替换全部四路图像（53.15）几乎相等。
