Model B cut matched training-window loss by 52.3% and reduced offline boundary crossings by more than half. Deploying B (FP32 100k) on the robot on November 9 for 299.880 s (5,832 frames; predict 50, execute 25) tests whether fixing training precision alone resolves closed-loop execution.

模型 B 将训练窗口配对 loss 降低了 52.3%，并大幅减少了离线越界。11 月 9 日将其部署到真机运行 299.880 s（5,832 帧；预测 50 步、执行 25 步），正好检验“只解决训练精度”能否打通闭环执行。

  • Start underflow and base approach (0–20 s): Policy requests dip as low as −119.629 at 0.36 s while Host clips accepted targets at −100. Nearly half of all 320 underflow frames (149 frames) happen before 20 s, mirroring the boundary-adjacent start behavior seen in offline replay.

  • Active grasp attempts (20–75 s): Right shoulder_lift rises cleanly to +84.25 and the right gripper opens repeatedly up to 45.987 (feedback 44.794), confirming that the FP32 policy retains both lifting and gripper-opening behaviors on hardware.

    有效抬臂与开爪尝试（20–75 s）：右臂 shoulder_lift 顺利抬升至 +84.25，右夹爪多次张开至 45.987（反馈 44.794），说明 FP32 模型在真机上仍能正常发出抬臂与开爪动作。

Observation substitutions: what drove the stall?观测替换：是什么锁住了策略输出？

To identify which input modality drove the low-target and sawtooth predictions, we selected three saved timestamps representing each phase marked above—0.36 s (worst start underflow, request −119.63), 10.76 s (base moving forward at x.vel = 0.145, request −102.51), and 251.12 s (deep inside the sawtooth stall, request −100.96 right before snapping up to −74.1, with a toy still visible on the floor in wrist_right). For each timestamp, we paired the saved observation with its nearest demonstrated arm-state frame and swapped individual cameras or state channels while holding weights, instruction, and three matched noise seeds fixed (117 predictions total, averaging absolute change over the first 25 shoulder_lift steps).

为定位究竟是哪一路输入在驱动越界和锯齿预测，我们从上图三个典型阶段各取一个保存时刻——0.36 s（起步最深越界，请求 −119.63）、10.76 s（底盘前进中，x.vel = 0.145，请求 −102.51）以及 251.12 s（锯齿卡死段内，请求 −100.96 随即跳回 −74.1，右腕画面仍见地上玩偶）。在每个时刻匹配双臂状态最接近的训练参考帧，固定权重、指令与三个随机种子，逐项替换单路相机或分通道状态，共做 117 次重预测，统计前 25 步右臂 shoulder_lift 输出的平均绝对变化。

  • At 251.12 s (sawtooth stall), wrist_right alone dictates the arm output: Replacing all four cameras shifts shoulder_lift by 53.151 Host units, and replacing only wrist_right shifts it by 53.746—nearly 20–60× larger than swapping forward (2.877), chest (1.935), or wrist_left (0.878), and 12× larger than swapping the entire 18D state (4.481). Inside the stall loop, right-wrist visual feedback overwhelmingly controls whether the arm lifts or drops back down.

    251.12 s 锯齿卡死段，wrist_right 单路视觉几乎完全主导抬臂输出：替换全部四路图像引起 53.151 单位变化，仅替换 wrist_right 单路就达到 53.746——远高于单换 forward（2.877）、chest（1.935）和 wrist_left（0.878），也是替换全部 18 维状态（4.481）的 12 倍。这说明在卡死循环中，右腕视角画面主导了策略在“抬臂”与“回落”间的剧烈切换。

  • At 10.76 s (base moving), cross-channel base velocity overrides vision: While the chassis advances at x.vel = 0.14507, swapping all four images changes shoulder_lift by only 0.439, whereas swapping only base velocity state (to donor x.vel = 0) changes arm output by 3.386. The policy uses base motion state to suppress premature arm lifting during approach.

    10.76 s 底盘移动阶段，跨通道底盘速度压过视觉：当车体以 x.vel = 0.14507 前进时，替换全部四路图像仅让 shoulder_lift 变化 0.439，仅把底盘状态换为静止参考帧（x.vel = 0）却引起 3.386 的手臂输出变化。策略在接近阶段依赖底盘速度状态抑制机械臂过早抬起。

  • Start-time offset and retained opening capability: At 0.36 s, wrist_right (3.723), forward (2.611), and chest (2.268) contribute together while state swaps change output by less than 0.2. Notably, the live initial left-gripper state reads 13.26 versus a training mean of 2.41 and standard deviation of 0.220 (a 49.27σ excursion caused by the left gripper holding the box nearly statically in demonstrations). Meanwhile, replaying 30 demonstrated maximum-opening windows under 0.7×, 1.0×, and 1.3× brightness yields 30/30 gripper openings—showing that the policy did not forget how to open the gripper, but failed to reach the visual–pose trigger conditions during the live stall.

    起步偏移与开爪能力保留：0.36 s 时 wrist_right（3.723）、forward（2.611）与 chest（2.268）共同影响输出，状态替换变化则低于 0.2。此外，真机初始左夹爪读数为 13.26，而训练中左夹爪一直稳态托盒（均值 2.41、标准差仅 0.220），导致标准化输入偏离均值高达 49.27 个标准差。与此同时，在 30 个示范最大开度窗口中测试 0.7、1.0、1.3 倍亮度，模型均 30/30 生成张开动作——说明策略并未遗忘开爪技能，而是在真机卡死循环里未能进入触发开爪的视觉与位姿组合。
