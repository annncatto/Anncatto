模型 B 将训练窗口配对 flow loss 降低了 52.3%，模型 C 也将离线越界减少了 60.8%。11 月 9 日将模型 B（FP32 100k）部署到真机运行 299.880 s（5,832 帧；预测 50 步、执行 25 步），检验仅改进训练精度是否足以稳定闭环执行。

  • Active grasp attempts (20–75 s): Right shoulder_lift rises to +84.25 and the right gripper opens repeatedly up to 45.987 (feedback 44.794), confirming that the FP32 policy retains both lifting and gripper-opening behaviors on hardware.

    抬臂与开爪尝试（20–75 s）：右臂 shoulder_lift 抬升至 +84.25，右夹爪多次张开至 45.987（反馈 44.794），说明 FP32 模型在真机上保留了抬臂和开爪动作。

    持续锯齿振荡（95–290 s）：在随后约 195 秒内，右夹爪保持闭合在 2 附近，shoulder_lift 则持续周期性振荡。150–160 s 放大图显示，每约 0.9 s（约 18 个记录帧，对应一次重规划交接），模型请求先平滑上升至 −10 附近，随后阶跃回落 60–75 单位至 −79…−98（例如 151.62 s 从 −10.92 降至 −79.10）；Host 接受目标在交接处滞后约 2 帧，物理臂来不及跟随如此大幅的往返指令，只能在 −50 至 −80 之间反相摆动。

Observation substitutions: which inputs drive the predictions?观测替换：哪些输入主导了策略输出？

To examine which input channels drive the low-target and oscillating predictions, we selected three saved timestamps from the phases marked above—0.36 s (deepest start underflow, request −119.63), 10.76 s (base advancing at x.vel = 0.145, request −102.51), and 251.12 s (inside the sawtooth oscillation, request −100.96 right before stepping to −74.1, with a toy visible on the floor in wrist_right). At each timestamp, we paired the saved observation with its nearest demonstrated arm-state frame and substituted individual cameras or state channels while holding weights, instruction, and three matched noise seeds fixed (117 predictions overall, averaging absolute change over the first 25 shoulder_lift steps).

为定位具体是哪些输入通道在影响越界与振荡预测，我们从上图三个阶段各选一个保存时刻——0.36 s（起步最深越界，请求 −119.63）、10.76 s（底盘前进中，x.vel = 0.145，请求 −102.51）以及 251.12 s（锯齿振荡期内，请求 −100.96 随后阶跃至 −74.1，右腕画面仍见地上玩偶）。在每个时刻匹配双臂状态最接近的训练参考帧，固定权重、指令与三个随机种子，逐项替换单路相机或分通道状态，共做 117 次重预测，统计前 25 步右臂 shoulder_lift 输出的平均绝对变化。

  • At 251.12 s (oscillation phase), wrist_right dominates the arm output: Replacing all four cameras shifts shoulder_lift by 53.151 Host units, and replacing only wrist_right shifts it by 53.746—far larger than swapping forward (2.877), chest (1.935), or wrist_left (0.878), and 12× larger than swapping the entire 18D state (4.481). During the oscillation phase, right-wrist visual input is the primary driver of the arm-target swings.

    251.12 s 振荡期，wrist_right 单路视觉主导抬臂输出：替换全部四路图像引起 53.151 单位变化，仅替换 wrist_right 单路即达 53.746，远大于单换 forward（2.877）、chest（1.935）或 wrist_left（0.878），也是替换全部 18 维状态（4.481）的 12 倍。这说明在振荡阶段，右腕相机画面主导了抬臂目标的大幅切换。

  • At 10.76 s (base advancing), cross-channel base velocity outweighs image swaps: While the chassis moves at x.vel = 0.14507, swapping all four images changes shoulder_lift by only 0.439, whereas swapping only base velocity state (to donor x.vel = 0) changes arm output by 3.386. During approach, base-motion state plays a larger role than images in keeping the arm lowered.

    10.76 s 底盘前进期，跨通道底盘状态变化大于图像替换：当车体以 x.vel = 0.14507 前进时，替换全部四路图像仅使 shoulder_lift 变化 0.439，仅将底盘状态换为静止参考帧（x.vel = 0）则引起 3.386 的手臂输出变化。接近阶段中，底盘速度状态对抑制机械臂提前抬起的作用大于图像。

  • Start-time offset and retained opening behavior: At 0.36 s, wrist_right (3.723), forward (2.611), and chest (2.268) contribute together while state swaps change output by less than 0.2. In addition, the live initial left-gripper state reads 13.26 versus a training mean of 2.41 and standard deviation of 0.220 (a 49.27σ offset resulting from the left gripper holding the box nearly statically in demonstrations). Replaying 30 demonstrated maximum-opening windows under 0.7×, 1.0×, and 1.3× brightness yields 30/30 gripper openings, showing that the policy retains gripper opening on demonstrated geometry even though closed-loop visual and pose conditions during the 95–290 s oscillation did not trigger it.

    起步偏移与开爪能力保留：0.36 s 时 wrist_right（3.723）、forward（2.611）与 chest（2.268）共同影响输出，状态替换变化低于 0.2。此外，真机初始左夹爪读数为 13.26，而训练中左夹爪一直稳态托盒（均值 2.41、标准差仅 0.220），导致标准化输入偏离均值 49.27 个标准差。在 30 个示范最大开度窗口测试 0.7、1.0、1.3 倍亮度，模型均 30/30 生成张开动作，说明策略保留了示范中的开爪行为，但在 95–290 s 振荡阶段的现场视角与位姿组合下未能触发开爪。
