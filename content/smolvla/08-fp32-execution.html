观测条件怎样影响动作How observation conditions affect actions

在 0.36、10.76、251.12 s 三个保存观测附近，从训练集寻找双臂状态最接近的参考帧。固定三个随机种子，分别替换图像、全部状态或部分通道，比较重新生成动作前 25 步的右肩抬平均绝对变化，共 39 个组合、117 次预测。

At saved observations near 0.36, 10.76 and 251.12 s, find a training-frame donor with nearby arm state. Under three matched seeds, replace images, all state or selected channels and compare the first 25 re-generated shoulder actions: 39 combinations and 117 predictions.

这里的“平均绝对变化”以同一保存观测重新预测的基线为参照，量测输入替换的局部输出响应。原执行请求可能取自较早生成的缓存动作块，因此本组重新预测单独建立匹配基线。

Mean absolute change uses re-prediction of the same saved observation as its baseline and measures local response to input replacement. Executed requests may come from earlier cached chunks, so these re-predictions establish their own matched baseline.

  • 251.12 s 对右腕图像替换响应突出：仅换右腕图像，平均变化 53.746；换全部状态为 4.481。该观测下，具体视觉参考对肩抬生成产生明显作用。

    At 251.12 s, the wrist-image replacement has a large effect: right-wrist-only replacement changes output by 53.746, versus 4.481 for all state. This specific visual donor strongly affects shoulder generation at that observation.

  • 10.76 s 对底盘状态也有响应：仅换底盘状态，变化 3.386；当时供体把 x.vel 从 0.14507 换成 0。相同关节的输出依赖跨通道条件，响应大小随观测与供体而变化。

    At 10.76 s, base state also matters: base-only replacement changes output by 3.386, with donor x.vel changing from 0.14507 to 0. The same joint depends on cross-channel conditions, with effects varying by observation and donor.

  • 训练中的张开动作仍能生成：30 个示范最大开度窗口在原亮度、0.7 倍和 1.3 倍亮度下均有 30/30 预测张开。该对照保持几何与状态，用于量测亮度倍率变化；真机的视觉、状态、动作历史同时变化，需沿条件链继续定位。

    Demonstrated openings remain generatable: all 30 maximum-opening windows predict opening at original brightness and factors 0.7 and 1.3. Geometry and state are fixed in this brightness test; robot vision, state and action history vary jointly and require further condition-specific analysis.

三组自身肩抬状态替换量分别为 −0.792、+0.176、−4.398；初始左夹爪状态按训练统计标准化后为 49.27。输入偏离训练中心的程度与输出对某次替换的响应，是两项分别记录的量，图中各格比较其具体供体输入。

The three shoulder-state substitutions are −0.792, +0.176 and −4.398; initial left-gripper state is 49.27 training standard deviations from its mean. Input displacement from the training center and output response to a specific substitution are measured separately, with each cell tied to its donor.
