Continuing from the 120-demo checkpoint onto 150 demonstrations changed three training conditions at once, and the final training loss plateaued near 0.026 rather than dropping below stage one (0.018):

从 120 条检查点续训至 150 条示教同时改变了三项条件，且末端训练 loss 停在 0.026 附近，反而高于第一阶段（0.018）：

  • Data: keep the original 120 demonstrations, add 30 new episodes, and run another 100k updates at a lower peak learning rate ($2\times10^{-5}$ decaying to $5\times10^{-7}$).
  • Coordinates: recompute mean and standard deviation across all 150 demonstrations, shifting the numerical coordinates inherited by the stage-one weights.
  • Optimizer: reinitialize AdamW so moment estimates rebuild from zero under the new coordinate scale.
  • 数据与学习率：保留原 120 条、补充 30 条示教，再训练 100k 步，同时峰值学习率降至 $2\times10^{-5}$、末端衰减至 $5\times10^{-7}$。

Deployment timing results部署计时结果

A separate October 27 timing test measures about 106 ms per prediction. Executing 25 actions at 30 Hz with synchronous replanning gives $25/(25/30+0.106)\approx26.6$ Hz, matching the observed 26–27 Hz command rate.

另一次 10 月 27 日部署计时测得单次预测约 106 ms。按 30 Hz 执行 25 步后同步重规划计算，平均发送频率为 $25/(25/30+0.106)\approx26.6$ Hz，与实测的 26–27 Hz 一致。

Compared with SmolVLA, π0.5 exhibits far fewer back-and-forth alignment retries. Inside a single chunk, π0.5’s bidirectional action attention coordinates future steps jointly, while across chunks the synchronous replanning boundary determines how cleanly successive targets connect.

与 SmolVLA 相比，π0.5 的反复微调对准明显更少。单块内部，π0.5 的双向动作注意力能前后协同各步预测；跨块衔接则由同步重规划边界决定新旧目标能否平滑接续。
