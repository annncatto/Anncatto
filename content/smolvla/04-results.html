初始状态Initial conditions

{{svmedia:initial-pose-a|initial-pose-b}}

改变初始位置后，两次运行均未完成抓取。物体相对位置变化影响图像中的目标位置、尺度和接近距离；关节复位姿态变化影响 state token 表示的构型、机械臂可达空间及抓取路径。动作专家需要将视觉目标与当前构型匹配，生成相应的关节动作。

Neither run completes grasping after the initial placement changes. Relative object placement affects image position, scale, and approach distance; joint reset pose affects the configuration represented by the state token, reachability, and grasp path. The expert matches visual targets to the current configuration to generate joint actions.

The evaluation contains 2,936 rows over dataset time 0–97.833 s at 30 Hz. During 10–20 s the right arm unfolds; during 20–60 s joint targets and gripper openings repeatedly change; during 65–75 s several joints return toward their initial ranges before another movement.
