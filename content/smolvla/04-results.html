Deployment timing results部署计时结果

A separate October 27 timing test measures about 106 ms per prediction. With 25 executed actions at 30 Hz and a synchronous replan, $25/(25/30+0.106)\approx26.6$ Hz, matching the recorded 26–27 Hz.

另一次 10 月 27 日部署测试测得单次预测约 106 ms。按每块执行 25 步、控制频率 30 Hz 和同步重规划计算，平均频率为 $25/(25/30+0.106)\approx26.6$ Hz，与记录的 26–27 Hz 一致。

The clip shows fewer repeated alignment corrections than the SmolVLA examples. π0.5’s bidirectional action attention coordinates positions within a chunk; continuity across chunks also depends on the execution schedule.

该片段中的反复对准比 SmolVLA 示例更少。π0.5 的双向动作注意力协调块内各位置，跨块连续性还取决于执行调度。

The timing test quantifies waits at replanning boundaries. Average command rate describes this overhead, while trajectory continuity describes how the old and new targets connect.

计时测试量化了重规划边界的等待开销。平均指令频率描述这项开销，轨迹连续性则描述新旧目标怎样接上。
