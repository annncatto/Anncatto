Deployment timing results部署计时结果

Offline timing measures ~106 ms per prediction; executing 25 actions at 30 Hz with synchronous replanning yields an average loop rate matching the observed 26–27 Hz:

离线计时测得单次预测约 106 ms；按 30 Hz 同步执行 25 步计算平均控制频率，与实测 26–27 Hz 一致：

$$f_{\mathrm{avg}} = \frac{25}{25 / 30 + 0.106} \approx 26.6\text{ Hz}.$$
