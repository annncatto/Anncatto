初始数据由 125 条记录剔除前五条后构成。第二轮增加 30 条示教，以全部 150 条数据重新计算归一化统计，重建优化器，并从第一轮检查点继续训练。两轮 SmolVLA 均更新完整模型参数，π0.5 使用前视、胸部与双腕四路输入。

The initial dataset retains 120 of 125 records after excluding the first five. Stage two adds 30 demonstrations, recomputes normalization on all 150, rebuilds the optimizer, and continues from the first checkpoint. Both SmolVLA stages update the complete model. π0.5 uses forward, chest, and both wrist views.
