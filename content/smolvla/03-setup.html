任务与数据：从地面收集玩偶Task and data: collecting toys from the floor

Pick up all the plush toys from the floor and place them into the box held by the left arm.

机器人需要根据物体相对位置调整底盘和右臂，接近并抓住玩偶，再抬起、移动到左臂所持的盒子上方，松开夹爪完成投放。这个任务把移动、双臂协调与视觉定位连在一起：单次夹爪闭合并不等于完成任务，玩偶是否被真正提起、是否落入盒中也要分别记录。

The robot adjusts its base and right arm to approach a toy, grasps it, lifts it over the box held by the left arm, and releases it. This couples mobility, bimanual coordination, and visual localization. Closing the gripper does not establish task completion: lifting and depositing must be recorded separately.

训练记录的口径与待核实项目Record differences and unresolved configuration details

同一任务另有一份 SmolVLA 表单记录：batch 8、200k 步、学习率 $10^{-5}$、loss 0.016，训练方式简写为 full。这些数值与上表的运行汇总不完全一致，尚未建立对应检查点，因此单独保留，不用于覆盖前两轮参数。full 还需要区分“可训练模块的全权重微调”与“整个 VLM 一起更新”，它本身不能证明没有冻结主干。

A separate SmolVLA form for the same task lists batch 8, 200k steps, learning rate $10^{-5}$, loss 0.016, and the shorthand full. These differ from the summarized runs and are not linked to a checkpoint, so they do not overwrite the first two stages. Full may mean updating all weights within the trainable module, rather than also unfreezing the VLM.

π0.5 的相机记录尚有差异：实验报告描述四路输入，表单只列了前视与双腕三路。后面的推理计时明确测试四路图像，但它不能反向证明每条视频或训练都使用相同映射。因此，比较前应补齐实际检查点配置与相机顺序，避免把“数据里有四路”“模型配置四路”和“该次推理使用四路”写成同一件事。

π0.5 camera records differ: the report describes four inputs, while the form lists forward and two wrist views. The later timing test explicitly uses four views, but does not prove the mapping for every rollout or training run. Distinguish available dataset streams, checkpoint configuration, and cameras actually used in a run.

步数也不能直接横比。按记录中的 batch 变化，第一轮累计采样约 $5000\times8+95000\times16=1.56$ M 个训练窗口，相当于总帧数的约 12.7 倍；相邻窗口大量重叠，不能视为 1.56 M 段独立经验。π0.5 的 10k 步与 SmolVLA 的 100k 步，还具有不同的初始化、可训练参数范围和数据处理方式，因此“步数更少”本身不是模型效率的严格证明。

Step counts are not directly comparable. The first stage samples about $5000\times8+95000\times16=1.56$ M training windows, approximately 12.7 times the frame count. Overlapping windows are not independent experiences. π0.5’s 10k steps and SmolVLA’s 100k also differ in initialization, trainable scope, and processing, so fewer steps alone do not establish superior efficiency.
