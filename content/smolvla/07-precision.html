Replay all models with FP32 weights and computation, disabling autocast and TF32. The test uses 16 training windows with two fixed noise/time draws each (32 paired evaluations), scoring flow loss on the 18 real action dimensions.

回放统一使用 FP32 权重与计算，关闭 autocast、TF32。在 16 个训练窗口上各固定两组噪声与时间抽样，共 32 次配对计算，只对 18 个真实动作维度计 flow loss。

  • Matched budget: B reduces flow loss by 52.3% relative to A under the same replay arithmetic. The learned weight values therefore retain the training-recipe difference.

Boundary-adjacent targets and output crossings贴边目标与越界预测

Boundary replay tests whether improved fitting also reduces shoulder predictions below −100. All crossings occur in start windows whose demonstrations are already near the limit, between −99.7447 and −98.9787.

边界回放检验拟合改善是否也减少右肩抬低于 −100 的预测。全部越界都位于起始窗口，对应示范目标在 −99.7447 至 −98.9787 之间，已贴近下限。

The shoulder channel has $\mu=-53.0387$ and $\sigma=60.9626$, placing −100 at standardized value −0.77033. The linear velocity head and Euler integration produce continuous predictions; restoration by $a=\mu+\sigma z$ rescales them without enforcing joint limits.

右肩抬通道的 $\mu=-53.0387$、$\sigma=60.9626$，下限 −100 对应标准化值 −0.77033。线性速度头与 Euler 积分生成连续预测，$a=\mu+\sigma z$ 只恢复尺度，不执行关节限位。

  • Fewer crossings: A crosses the lower limit at 390 of 4,500 positions (8.67%); C at 153 (3.40%). C’s mean overshoot is 0.235 and its maximum is 1.219.

    越界减少：4,500 个预测位置中，A 有 390 个越界（8.67%），C 有 153 个（3.40%）。C 的平均超出量为 0.235，最大为 1.219。

  • Limited effect of clipping: clipping only C’s shoulder to [−100,100] changes MAE from 1.2488 to 1.2408, a 0.64% improvement. Range enforcement and accurate trajectory generation address separate requirements.

    限幅的作用有限：仅将 C 的右肩抬裁剪到 [−100,100]，MAE 从 1.2488 变为 1.2408，改善 0.64%。保证数值范围与生成准确轨迹是两项独立要求。

  • Internal action width: the sampler integrates all 32 dimensions, while training loss supervises 18. The 14 padded coordinates remain a distinct candidate for the controlled test listed in Next comparisons.

    内部动作维度：采样器积分全部 32 维，训练 loss 则监督 18 维。其余 14 个补零坐标是下一轮对照中可单独检验的内部通路。
