In the 150-demo continued run above, training loss stalled near 0.026 as the learning rate decayed from $2\times10^{-5}$ to $5\times10^{-7}$. To test whether BF16 writeback rounding was erasing small optimizer increments, we trained model B from the exact same 120-demo checkpoint and 150-episode dataset, promoting all trainable weights, AdamW moments, and training arithmetic to FP32, and continued B for another 20k steps at $5\times10^{-7}$ as model C.

Stalling under decay and internal precision split低学习率停滞与内部精度分裂

Tracking matched 16-window flow loss across checkpoints exposes exactly when A and B diverge. At 5k steps (high learning rate), A (0.03962) even edges out B (0.04284), and both drop steeply through 20k. Once cosine decay drives the learning rate down after 50k steps, however, A hits a hard plateau (0.02753 → 0.02719 → 0.02702), whereas B cuts its loss nearly in half again between 50k and 100k (0.02104 → 0.01342 → 0.01276).

追踪 5k 至 100k 多个检查点的 16 窗口配对 flow loss，可以直接看到 A 与 B 发生分叉的时刻。第 5k 步高学习率阶段，A（0.03962）甚至略低于 B（0.04284），前 20k 步两者都在快速下降；但 50k 步进入余弦学习率衰减期后，A 彻底撞墙（0.02753 → 0.02719 → 0.02702），B 却在 50k–100k 期间继续把 loss 近乎腰斩（0.02104 → 0.01342 → 0.01276）。

Inspecting the parameter inventory reveals why. In A, 129 trainable tensors (96,607,440 elements) are stored in BF16 and only 26 tensors (3,273,552 elements) are FP32. Those 26 FP32 tensors are not scattered at random—they consist of the 10 external projection tensors (1.64M) plus all 16 k_proj/v_proj matrices inside the eight odd cross-attention layers (1.64M) that read the frozen VLM prefix.

核对逐张量权重清单，停滞机理一目了然。模型 A 的可训练参数被拆成了截然不同的两套精度：129 个张量（96,607,440 元素）以 BF16 存储，仅有 26 个张量（3,273,552 元素）为 FP32。这 26 个 FP32 张量精确分布在两处——10 个外部输入输出与时间投影（1.64M），以及全部 8 个奇数交叉注意力层中读取冻结 VLM 前缀的 16 个 k_proj/v_proj 矩阵（1.64M）。

  • A’s BF16 body freezes while its FP32 channels over-compensate: Across the entire 100k stage, A’s 96.58M BF16 body drifts only 4.46% from initialization (with only 0.08267% of the 96.61M BF16 elements changing between 95k and 100k, and all 32 RMSNorm weights staying strictly 0.00% changed). Because loss stays elevated while the body cannot absorb small increments (Update rounding), optimization pressure drains into the 26 FP32 tensors, pushing external projections 1.64× farther (7.88% vs 4.81%; action_time_mlp_out reaches 12.12% vs 6.08%) and odd CA k_proj/v_proj 2.46× farther (17.31% vs 7.03%, peaking at 24.63% in L15 v_proj).

    A 的 BF16 主体锁死，FP32 通道被迫 1.6–2.5 倍过代偿：整个 100k 步训练里，A 的 96.58M BF16 专家主体相对初始化仅漂移 4.46%（95k→100k 末 5k 步仅 0.08267% 的元素不同，全部 32 个 RMSNorm 权重更是 0.00% 纹丝不动）。由于主体在低学习率下被写入舍入锁死（见更新舍入），残余梯度持续涌入 26 个 FP32 张量，迫使外部投影漂移比 B 大 1.64 倍（7.88% 对 4.81%，其中 action_time_mlp_out 达 12.12% 对 6.08%），奇数 CA 层的 k_proj/v_proj 更比 B 大 2.46 倍（17.31% 对 7.03%，L15 v_proj 高达 24.63%）。

  • B reverses the division of labor: With every trainable tensor and AdamW moment in FP32, B’s 16-layer expert body carries the bulk of adaptation (13.71% L2 drift, 3.07× A’s stalled body, with 99.99785% of elements still updating over 95k–100k). Its external projections and CA K/V gates stay comparatively compact (4.81% and 7.03%). Model C continues this pattern over 115k–120k (99.99773% changed) while holding flow loss near B (0.01284).

    B 反转了内部分工：全部可训练权重与 AdamW 矩估计升为 FP32 后，B 的 16 层专家主体承担了核心适应（L2 漂移 13.71%，达到 A 停滞主体的 3.07 倍，95k→100k 仍有 99.99785% 元素持续更新），外部接口与 CA K/V 反而保持温和（4.81% 与 7.03%）。续训 20k 步的模型 C 在 115k→120k 仍保持 99.99773% 变化率，配对 loss 与 B 接近（0.01284）。

Expert–projection co-adaptation and layer sensitivity精度分裂下的共同适配与逐层敏感性

Because A and B adapted through opposite parameter groups—A over-shifting its FP32 interfaces around a stalled body, B moving its 16-layer expert around compact interfaces—we swapped modules and individual layers between memory copies under the same 16-window FP32 test to see how tightly those representations lock together.

既然 A 与 B 走出了截然相反的适配路径——A 靠过漂的 FP32 接口迁就停滞主体，B 靠深度更新的 16 层专家配合温和接口——我们在同一 16 窗口 FP32 测试下对内存副本做整组与逐层替换，直接检验两套内部表征的耦合程度。

Boundary-adjacent targets and 32D/18D output path贴边目标、越界预测与 32/18 维通路

Even with FP32 updates lowering trajectory MAE, the model’s architecture imposes no hard joint bounds. In the 90-window replay (4,500 predicted positions per model), every right shoulder_lift prediction below the Host −100 lower bound occurs in the 30 start windows—where demonstrated targets already press against −99.7447 to −98.9787—while gripper-open and raised-shoulder windows record zero crossings.

即使 FP32 训练明显压低了轨迹误差，模型输出通路本身并不带硬限位。在 90 窗口回放（每模型 4,500 个预测位置）中，右臂 shoulder_lift 低于 Host 下限 −100 的越界全部集中在 30 个起始窗口——这些窗口的示范目标本身紧贴 −99.7447 至 −98.9787——而夹爪张开与抬臂最高两类窗口的越界数均为 0。

For right shoulder_lift ($\mu=-53.0387$, $\sigma=60.9626$), the −100 physical limit maps to standardized value −0.77033. Because the linear velocity head and 10-step Euler integration generate unbounded continuous values and affine restoration $a=\mu+\sigma z$ only rescales them, even a small negative error around −99.6 crosses −100.

对右臂 shoulder_lift（$\mu=-53.0387$，$\sigma=60.9626$），物理下限 −100 对应标准化值 −0.77033。线性速度头与 10 步 Euler 积分输出无界连续值，仿射还原 $a=\mu+\sigma z$ 只恢复尺度而不做截断，因此在 −99.6 附近的微小负向预测误差便会跨过下限。

  • Tighter fit shrinks mid-chunk crossings by ~75%: A crosses −100 at 390 of 4,500 positions (8.67%, deepest −102.40) uniformly across all five horizon bins (66–91 per bin). C tightens the distribution to 153 crossings (3.40%, mean overshoot 0.235, deepest −101.22), cutting mid-horizon bins 10–39 down to 21 each while leaving residual crossings at chunk start (0–9: 53) and far tail (40–49: 37).

    拟合收紧使中段越界减少约 75%：A 在 4,500 个位置中越界 390 次（8.67%，最深 −102.40），均匀散布于 50 步块的全段（每 10 步 66–91 次）。C 将越界压低至 153 次（3.40%，平均超出 0.235，最深 −101.22），其中第 10–39 步中段各降至 21 次，残留越界集中在块首（0–9 步：53 次）与块尾（40–49 步：37 次）。

  • Clipping protects interfaces without fixing trajectories: Clipping C’s right shoulder_lift to [−100, 100] offline changes MAE only from 1.2488 to 1.2408 (−0.64%). Hard interface limits prevent illegal commands, while trajectory accuracy depends on learning the motion itself.

    限幅保障接口合法，不能替代轨迹改进：离线仅将 C 的右臂 shoulder_lift 裁剪到 [−100, 100]，MAE 只从 1.2488 变为 1.2408（降幅 0.64%）。输出限幅负责守住硬件合法范围，轨迹精度仍取决于模型本身。

  • Weight audits expose the 32D/18D gradient asymmetry: Both state_proj.weight and action_out_proj.weight update on exactly 56.25% (= 18/32) of elements in A and B, confirming zero gradients on the 14 zero-padded state inputs and 14 unsupervised action outputs. Yet action_in_proj.weight updates on 100% of elements because flow matching injects Gaussian noise across all 32 dimensions and the sampler integrates all 32 coordinates at every Euler step. Clamping those 14 padded coordinates during sampling is therefore a clean controlled test in Next comparisons.

    权重变化直接暴露 32/18 维不对称：在 A 与 B 中，state_proj.weight 和 action_out_proj.weight 恰好均只有 56.25%（= 18/32）的元素更新，证实后 14 个补零维在状态输入与损失端确实零梯度；但 action_in_proj.weight 却 100% 都在更新——因为流匹配向全部 32 维注入高斯噪声，且推理采样器每步 Euler 更新都会把这 14 个无监督坐标送回输入投影。因此在下一轮对照中，逐步固定 14 个补零维是定位其影响的直接受控实验。
