Loss curves and where weights updated收敛曲线与权重更新分布

Matched 16-window replay across 5k–120k shows FP32 does not converge more slowly: after a slightly higher 5k point (0.043 vs 0.040), FP32 already beats mixed storage by 20k (0.026 vs 0.031). During 50k–100k lr decay, mixed storage drops only 1.8% while FP32 drops another 39%; extending FP32 to 120k stays flat at 0.013, confirming convergence by 100k.

5k–120k 的 16 窗口配对回放曲线表明，FP32 并不是收敛更慢：仅第 5k 步略高（0.043 对 0.040），到 20k 步 FP32 已低于混合存储（0.026 对 0.031）。50k–100k 衰减期，混合存储仅降 1.8%，FP32 继续下降 39%；续训至 120k 后曲线走平在 0.013，说明 100k 时已基本收敛。

  • Mixed storage stalls its BF16 body and shifts 26 FP32 tensors 1.6–2.5× more: Once step sizes fall below half a BF16 ULP (Update rounding), the 96.6M BF16 expert body changes only 4.5% (0.08% changed in final 5k). Cumulative updates concentrate in the 26 FP32 tensors: external projections change 7.9% vs 4.8% and odd CA k_proj/v_proj change 17.3% vs 7.0%.

    混合存储中 BF16 主体停滞，26 个 FP32 张量变化达 FP32 的 1.6–2.5 倍：低学习率下 BF16 更新小于半个步长被舍入回原值（见更新舍入），96.6M 主体相对 L2 变化仅 4.5%（末 5k 仅 0.08% 变）。持续梯度改由 26 个 FP32 张量承接：外部投影为 7.9% 对 4.8%，奇数 CA 层 K/V 为 17.3% 对 7.0%。

  • Full FP32 puts updates into the 16-layer expert body: With all trainable weights and AdamW moments in FP32, the expert body reaches 13.7% relative L2 change (3.1× Mixed, >99.99% elements updating in final 5k), while external projections and CA K/V change less.

    全 FP32 的更新集中在 16 层动作专家主体：全部可训练参数与 AdamW 矩估计改为 FP32 后，专家主体相对 L2 变化达 13.7%（混合的 3.1 倍，末 5k 步 >99.99% 元素在更新），外部投影与 CA K/V 的变化幅度低于混合存储。

Boundary-adjacent targets and 32D/18D output path贴边目标越界与 32/18 维通路

Across 90 replay windows (4,500 steps), every right shoulder_lift prediction below the Host −100 lower bound occurs in the 30 start windows—where demonstrated targets already sit at −99.7 to −99.0—because linear velocity prediction, 10-step Euler integration, and affine scaling $a=\mu+\sigma z$ enforce no joint bounds.

在 90 窗口（4,500 步）离线回放中，右臂 shoulder_lift 低于 −100 的越界全部发生在示范紧贴 −99.7 ~ −99.0 的 30 个起始窗口；线性输出头、10 步 Euler 积分与仿射还原 $a=\mu+\sigma z$ 均不执行硬限位。

Offline clipping vs model fit: Clipping to [−100, 100] only truncates out-of-range tails (MAE 1.25 → 1.24, −0.6%); full FP32 improves in-range accuracy directly (crossings 390 → 153, −61%; MAE −29% to −38%).

离线限幅 vs 轨迹拟合改进：仅把预测裁剪到 [−100, 100] 只截断越界尾段（MAE 1.25 → 1.24，仅 −0.6%）；全 FP32 改善的是边界以内的整体拟合（越界 390 → 153，−61%；MAE 降 29–38%）。

On the 32D/18D path, state_proj and action_out_proj change on exactly 56% (18/32) of weights, whereas action_in_proj changes 100% because Gaussian noise and 10-step Euler sampling feed all 32 dims back at every step (tested by clamping those 14 dims in Next comparisons).

在 32/18 维通路上，state_proj 与 action_out_proj 恰好只更新 56%（18/32） 的权重，而 action_in_proj 为 100%，因为流匹配加噪与 10 步 Euler 积分覆盖全部 32 维（见下一轮对照的置零检验）。
