动作归一化Action normalization

SmolVLA 使用 MEAN_STD 对连续状态和动作逐维标准化。状态与动作分别采用各自的均值 $\mu_i$ 和标准差 $\sigma_i$，网络在标准化坐标中学习动作，输出时恢复原始尺度。

SmolVLA standardizes continuous states and actions per dimension with MEAN_STD. Each uses its own mean $\mu_i$ and standard deviation $\sigma_i$. The network learns actions in standardized coordinates, then restores the original scale at output.

LeRobot MEAN_STD 实现：

LeRobot MEAN_STD implementation:

$$z_i=\frac{a_i-\mu_i}{\sigma_i+10^{-8}},\qquad \widehat a_i=\sigma_i\widehat z_i+\mu_i.$$

其中 $a_i$ 为原始示教动作，$z_i$ 为训练标签，$\widehat z_i$ 为网络预测，$\widehat a_i$ 为恢复后的控制量。分母中的 $10^{-8}$ 用于处理零标准差。

Here $a_i$ is the demonstrated action, $z_i$ its training target, $\widehat z_i$ the network prediction, and $\widehat a_i$ the restored control. The $10^{-8}$ denominator term handles zero standard deviation.

The 120 demonstrations contain 122,475 frames. Base $v_x$ targets take only two values: 0 in 102,656 frames (83.82%) and 0.15 in 19,819 frames (16.18%). Their statistics are $\mu_x=0.02427308$ and $\sigma_x=0.05524300$.
