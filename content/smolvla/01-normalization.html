Data preprocessing数据预处理

State and action channels combine joint positions, base velocities and lift position. SmolVLAProcessor calls AlohaMini’s shared Processor to normalize each channel with its own mean $\mu_i$ and standard deviation $\sigma_i$, and restores predicted actions to their original scale.

状态与动作通道包含关节位置、底盘速度和升降位置。SmolVLAProcessor 调用 AlohaMini 的共享 Processor，使用各通道自己的均值 $\mu_i$、标准差 $\sigma_i$ 进行归一化，并把预测动作还原到原尺度。

$$z_i=\frac{a_i-\mu_i}{\sigma_i+10^{-8}},\qquad \widehat a_i=\sigma_i\widehat z_i+\mu_i.$$

$a_i$ is a demonstrated action, $z_i$ its normalized target, $\widehat z_i$ the model prediction and $\widehat a_i$ the restored command. The denominator’s $10^{-8}$ term handles zero standard deviation.

$a_i$ 是示范动作，$z_i$ 是标准化标签，$\widehat z_i$ 是模型预测，$\widehat a_i$ 是还原后的指令。分母中的 $10^{-8}$ 用于处理标准差为零的情况。

In the 120-demo dataset, forward targets take two values: 0 in 102,656 frames and 0.15 in 19,819 frames, or 83.82% and 16.18% of the 122,475 frames. Their mean and standard deviation are $\mu_x=0.02427308$ and $\sigma_x=0.05524300$.

120 条示教中，前进标签有两个值：102,656 帧为 0，19,819 帧为 0.15，分别占总计 122,475 帧的 83.82%、16.18%。对应均值为 $\mu_x=0.02427308$，标准差为 $\sigma_x=0.05524300$。

For this channel, a standardized prediction error $\delta z_x=0.01$ becomes a raw-command error of approximately $5.52\times10^{-4}$. The standard deviation sets the conversion scale:

对该通道，标准化预测误差 $\delta z_x=0.01$ 会还原成约 $5.52\times10^{-4}$ 的指令误差。标准差决定这项尺度换算：

$$\delta v_x=\sigma_x\delta z_x.$$

The training path normalizes demonstration actions; the prediction path restores generated actions. State follows a separate normalization path before conditioning the policy.

训练路径归一化示范动作，预测路径反归一化生成动作。状态使用独立的归一化统计，再作为策略条件输入。
