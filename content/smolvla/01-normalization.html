动作归一化：底盘小速度要在哪个域里看Action normalization: interpreting small base velocities

SmolVLA 发布配置对状态和动作默认采用逐维 MEAN_STD 标准化；状态使用状态统计，动作用动作统计。以动作的第 $i$ 维为例，训练目标 $a_i$ 与归一化目标 $z_i$、模型输出 $\widehat z_i$ 与反归一化结果 $\widehat a_i$ 的关系为：

SmolVLA’s release configuration applies per-dimension MEAN_STD normalization to state and actions, using separate statistics for each. For action dimension $i$, reference $a_i$, normalized target $z_i$, model prediction $\widehat z_i$, and restored prediction $\widehat a_i$ are related by:

$$z_i=\frac{a_i-\mu_i}{\sigma_i+\varepsilon_n},\qquad \widehat a_i=\sigma_i\widehat z_i+\mu_i,\qquad \varepsilon_n=10^{-8}.$$

这是核对的 LeRobot MEAN_STD 实现：正向分母加 $\varepsilon_n$ 防止除零，反向乘 $\sigma_i$ 后加 $\mu_i$。忽略这个很小的稳定项时，两式互为逆。图像在该 processor 中的 IDENTITY 设置，不代表视觉编码器不再进行像素预处理。实际训练与部署还需核对各自的 processor 配置。

This matches the inspected LeRobot MEAN_STD implementation: epsilon is added in the forward denominator, while the inverse multiplies by standard deviation and adds the mean. Ignoring the small stabilizer, they are inverses. IDENTITY for visual fields in this processor does not remove image preprocessing inside the vision pipeline. 这次 120 条数据的原始 $v_x$ 标签只有两个取值：0 与约 0.15。122,475 帧中，停止为 102,656 帧（83.82%），前进为 19,819 帧（16.18%）。动作统计为 $\mu_x=0.02427308$、$\sigma_x=0.05524300$。因此归一化域的零不等于底盘停止：

The 120-episode dataset has only two raw $v_x$ labels: zero and approximately 0.15. Among 122,475 frames, 102,656 (83.82%) are stopped and 19,819 (16.18%) move forward. Action statistics are $\mu_x=0.02427308$ and $\sigma_x=0.05524300$. Thus zero in normalized coordinates does not mean a stopped base:

A normalized prediction near zero restores to a small positive velocity, and a small error near the normalized stop target also produces a nonzero residual. This explains coordinates; it does not imply that a generative model necessarily predicts zero or a conditional mean. Correct forward target 2.2759 restores to approximately 0.15 under the same statistics.

这段示教在约 2.367 s 开始前进、8.300 s 停止。使用数据集统计量按上式做 float64 离线往返，最大误差约 $2.28\times10^{-8}$；它不是实机混合精度测量，却足以检查这套公式与统计量是否存在明显缩放错误。历史运行是否使用了相同字段顺序、相同统计量，仍需真实日志确认。另如 $y$ 向动作的标准差为零，使用这套反归一化时该维输出恒为均值；它与标准差非零的 $v_x$ 是不同情况。

Forward motion begins at approximately 2.367 s and stops at 8.300 s. An offline float64 round trip using these statistics has maximum error around $2.28\times10^{-8}$. This is not a measurement of mixed-precision robot inference, but checks the formula/statistics for gross scaling errors. Historical field order and statistics still require logs. A zero-standard-deviation dimension, such as lateral action here, instead restores to its mean regardless of prediction; that differs from nonconstant $v_x$.

停止帧占多数是已测事实，但其成因包含正常的任务结构：机器人操作玩偶期间底盘应当停止。要判断它是否妨碍学会前进，需要检查不同阶段、不同观察条件下的覆盖，尤其是“何时开始走、何时停止”能否从输入辨别。归一化 MSE 中，不同维度还按自身标准差缩放误差；不能把原始单位下的幅度小，直接等同于训练中不重要。

The large stopped fraction is measured, but partly reflects legitimate task structure: the base should remain still during manipulation. Its effect on learning depends on phase-conditioned coverage and whether inputs distinguish when to start or stop. Normalized losses also rescale errors by each dimension’s standard deviation; small raw amplitudes do not automatically mean negligible training importance.
