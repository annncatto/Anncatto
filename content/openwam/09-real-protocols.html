$$S_{\mathrm{final}}=\frac{N_{\mathrm{success}}}{N_{\mathrm{trial}}}\times100\%,\qquad S_{\mathrm{process}}=\frac{\sum_i n_i}{\sum_i m_i}\times100\%.$$

$n_i$ counts completed elements in trial $i$ and $m_i$ the elements present. Shuttlecock, tower and clothing progress count placed items. Progress is aggregated over all elements, so when trials contain different item counts it differs from an unweighted average of per-trial percentages.

$n_i$ 是第 $i$ 次试验中完成的操作要素数，$m_i$ 是该次试验的要素总数。收集任务按放入筒内的羽毛球计分，套塔按插入的圆盘计分，收衣按进入篮子的衣物计分。Progress Score 按全部要素汇总，因此不同试验包含的物体数不同时，不能直接用每次进度百分比的简单平均替代。

Cap-removal progress records whether the cap is removed; final success also checks stable grasp. ID uses ten trials per task. OOD uses ten tower, fifteen shuttlecock, twenty clothing and twenty cap trials, aggregating each task’s variation conditions.

拧瓶盖的进度只统计是否拧开，最终成功还检查稳定抓持。ID 各任务 10 次；OOD 套塔 10 次、收羽毛球 15 次、收衣与拧瓶盖各 20 次，各任务的 OOD 列汇总其所有变化条件。

Bimanual Real-Robot Experiments

RoboDojo-Real uses the official platforms and unified task protocol, covering six tasks on each of three embodiments. Tasks probe generalization, precision, long horizons, memory and open settings, reporting both progress and final success.

RoboDojo-Real 使用官方平台与统一任务协议，覆盖三种本体、每种六个任务。任务分别涉及泛化、精细操作、长时序、记忆与开放场景，结果同时报告阶段进度与最终成功。
