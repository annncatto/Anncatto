Inheriting Upstream World Knowledge

World knowledge has two main carriers: generative priors in the video backbone and representations defined by the visual encoder. The former supplies learned visual-dynamics prediction; the latter defines what is predicted and in which feature coordinates. Separate experiments vary these two carriers.

世界知识有两个主要载体：视频骨干参数中学到的生成先验，以及视觉编码器定义的表征。前者决定模型已有怎样的视觉动态预测能力，后者决定模型预测的对象和坐标系。两组实验分别改变这两个载体。

768-D and 1,024-D denote feature channels per visual location. With $N$ spatiotemporal locations, write $F\in\mathbb R^{N\times C}$: $N$ counts locations and $C$ the width of each description. Reducing 768 to 48 contracts channels; temporal pooling, spatial downsampling and patch embedding separately affect time, spatial grids and token organization.

768-D 与 1,024-D 指每个视觉位置的特征通道数。令编码后共有 $N$ 个时空位置，则特征可写为 $F\in\mathbb R^{N\times C}$，$N$ 表示位置数量，$C$ 表示每个位置的描述宽度。把 $C$ 从 768 压到 48，改变的是通道维；时间平均、空间下采样与 Patch Embedding 分别改变时间长度、空间网格及 Token 的组织。

DiT projects latent patches into Transformer width $D$ and predicts patches of the original size at its output. A patch covering $p_t\times p_h\times p_w$ locations has raw width $C p_t p_h p_w$. Latent channels, token count and hidden width are distinct quantities. The original DiT recipe was built around compact reconstructive VAE latents; high-dimensional representations change its input, output and denoising target.

DiT 把潜变量 Patch 投影到 Transformer 的隐藏宽度 $D$，并在输出端预测同样大小的潜变量 Patch。若一个 Patch 覆盖 $p_t\times p_h\times p_w$ 个位置，其原始宽度为 $C p_t p_h p_w$。因此“潜变量通道数”“Token 数量”和“网络隐藏宽度”是三个不同的量。原始 DiT 围绕重建 VAE 的低维潜空间建立训练配方，更换为高维表征后，输入、输出与去噪目标都会改变。

Rich high-dimensional semantic features also carry noise in all their channels once Gaussian noise is added. RAE studies adapt model width and dimension-dependent noise schedules, showing that high-dimensional representations can support generation directly. OpenWAM instead learns compact feature coordinates before generation.

高维语义特征可以包含丰富信息，但加入高斯噪声后，模型也必须处理这些通道上的噪声分量。RAE 的研究据此调整 DiT 宽度与维度相关的噪声调度，证明高维表征可以直接用于生成；OpenWAM 采用另一条路线，在生成前先学习紧凑的特征坐标。

$$F\xrightarrow{\ E_s\ }q_\psi(z_s\mid F),\qquad z_s\in\mathbb R^{N\times48},\qquad\hat F=D_s(z_s).$$
$$\begin{aligned}\mathcal L_{S\text{-VAE}}={}&\operatorname{MSE}(\hat F,F)\\&+\gamma\,\mathbb E_n[1-\cos(\hat F_n,F_n)]\\&+\beta\,D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\left(q_\psi(z_s\mid F)\,\|\,\mathcal N(0,I)\right).\end{aligned}$$

S-VAE’s encoder $E_s$ maps features to a 48-D Gaussian posterior; decoder $D_s$ reconstructs them. The official implementation combines MSE, cosine reconstruction and KL on standardized features: MSE retains values, cosine retains directional relationships, and KL regularizes the latent distribution; $\gamma$ and $\beta$ weight the cosine and KL terms. The generator then learns 48-D targets from the frozen encoder, reducing prediction dimensionality while reconstruction preserves information.

S-VAE 的编码器 $E_s$ 将高维特征映射到 48 维高斯后验，解码器 $D_s$ 再重建原特征。官方实现在标准化特征上结合 MSE、余弦重建与 KL 正则：MSE 保留特征数值，余弦项保留方向关系，KL 约束潜空间分布，$\gamma$ 与 $\beta$ 分别控制余弦项和 KL 项的权重。生成器随后直接学习冻结编码端得到的 48 维目标，减轻高维预测负担，同时通过重建约束保留原表征中的信息。

This follows the semantic VAE approach: learn a compact generative latent instead of truncating to 48 channels. S-VAE improves DINOv3 by 13.76 points and V-JEPA 2.1 by 7.55. Matching 48 channels does not reproduce Wan-VAE coordinates; Figure 7 still uses a reinitialized generator.

这种压缩延续了语义 VAE的思路：学习适合生成的紧凑潜空间，而非直接截取前 48 个通道。DINOv3 经 S-VAE 后提高 13.76 个百分点，V-JEPA 2.1 提高 7.55 个百分点。48 维只对齐通道宽度，不会自动得到与 Wan-VAE 相同的特征坐标；图 7 的生成骨干仍按实验设置重新初始化。

Temporal compression determines how fewer latent frames retain changes across frames. For DINOv3 frame features, the current frame $f_0$ is retained separately and subsequent groups of four are averaged:

时间压缩则决定较少的潜帧怎样保留多帧变化。以 DINOv3 的帧特征为例，当前帧 $f_0$ 单独保留，其余四帧一组取平均：

$$\bar f_0=f_0,\qquad\bar f_j=\frac14\sum_{r=1}^{4}f_{4(j-1)+r},\quad j\ge1.$$

Averaging is insensitive to permutations of the same input features. Scalar sequences $(0,1,2,3)$ and $(3,2,1,0)$ both average to 1.5, eliminating their opposite directions. Context already encoded in features may retain motion information, but the pooling operator does not learn which temporal differences to preserve.

平均算子对同一组输入特征的排列不敏感。例如，标量特征序列 $(0,1,2,3)$ 与 $(3,2,1,0)$ 的均值都是 1.5，变化方向在平均后消失。编码器已有的上下文可能保留一部分运动信息，但平均运算本身不会学习哪些跨帧差异需要保留。

Wan2.2-VAE uses causal 3D convolutions and learned temporal downsampling, trained through video reconstruction before WAM learning. Ordered frames and cached history inform what its limited latent channels retain; those parameters remain frozen during WAM training. S-VAE channel contraction and temporal encoding address different axes. The authors attribute the remaining 0.12-point gap to Wan2.2-VAE’s native temporal compression and backbone compatibility.

Wan2.2-VAE 使用因果三维卷积与学习式时序下采样，压缩参数在视频 VAE 预训练中由重建目标学得。它可以依据帧的先后关系与历史特征组织潜变量，用有限通道保留有助于恢复视频的变化；进入 WAM 训练后，这些参数保持冻结。S-VAE 的通道压缩与这种时间压缩分别处理特征宽度和跨帧组织。作者将 DINOv3 + S-VAE 与 Wan2.2-VAE 剩余的 0.12 个百分点差距，联系到原生时间压缩和骨干适配这两项优势。

A WAM inherits upstream world knowledge most effectively through a sufficiently capable generative backbone and a compact, information-rich visual representation space.

WAM 通过具有足够能力的生成骨干，以及紧凑、信息丰富的视觉表征空间，能够最有效地继承上游世界知识。

OpenWAM, Finding 1

Both reconstructive and representation encoders can support effective control. The important properties are retention of control-relevant information and a latent structure the generator can model efficiently. Wan2.2-VAE currently combines native temporal compression with backbone compatibility, so it remains the final model’s encoder.

这也解释了重建型与表征型编码器之间的取舍：两类编码器都可以支持有效控制，关键是保留控制所需信息，并让生成网络能够高效建模这些特征。Wan2.2-VAE 当前同时具备原生时间压缩和骨干适配优势，最终模型因而仍选用它。

Consolidating Knowledge Across Domains

Problem Setup and Evaluation Protocol

Earlier ablations start downstream learning from pretrained video weights. This experiment adds embodied pretraining on egocentric human data and robot trajectories before target-task fine-tuning. From Scratch means without this additional embodied stage; it still retains the chosen upstream video initialization.

先前消融从视频预训练权重开始学习下游任务。这里增加的是具身预训练：在目标任务微调之前，再使用人类第一视角数据和机器人轨迹学习世界—动作联系。表中的 From Scratch 指没有这一步额外的具身预训练，仍保留所选视频骨干的上游初始化。

The four runs keep backbones, optimization budget and synchronized inference fixed. Embodied pretraining uses 600 hours: Robot-only uses RoboCOIN throughout; mixed recipes use 350 hours of EgoDex and 250 hours of RoboCOIN, either sequentially or jointly in one stage. Identical downstream fine-tuning follows.

Joint mixed pretraining raises OOD from 14.50% to 26.62%, a 12.12-point gain, while ID rises by 0.68 points. Robot-only has the strongest ID result, whereas both mixed recipes transfer better OOD. 混合共同训练将 OOD 从 14.50% 提高到 26.62%，增加 12.12 个百分点；ID 仅增加 0.68 个百分点。Robot-only 的 ID 最高，但两种混合配方的 OOD 都更强。结果与两类数据的监督差异一致：机器人轨迹建立可执行动作与观测的对应，人类视频扩大场景、物体与交互过程的覆盖。

One-stage co-training exceeds sequential training by 0.58 points ID and 0.12 points OOD; the recipes are close. The authors choose co-training for its slightly stronger result and simpler single-stage workflow. The larger benefit comes from including both data sources, rather than their precise ordering.

单阶段相对两阶段在 ID 高 0.58 个百分点、OOD 高 0.12 个百分点，两者表现接近。作者选择单阶段共同训练，既取得略高结果，也省去数据课程切换。这组更明显的收益来自纳入两种数据，而非改变它们的训练顺序。

The proposed mechanism is that embodied data establishes bidirectional correspondence: future visual features support action, while action helps the world stream represent manipulator motion. The measured result is the consistent preference for Mutual in these pretrained comparisons; the bidirectional-grounding account explains that result. Mutual becomes the final default.
