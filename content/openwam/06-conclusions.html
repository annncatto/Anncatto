Conclusions

OpenWAM decomposes transfer from video priors to robot actions into testable design choices. Generative backbones and visual encoders carry knowledge; dedicated action capacity and world-to-action paths expose it to control; joint denoising maintains feature exchange; mixed human/robot data broadens coverage while preserving action grounding.

OpenWAM 将视频先验到机器人动作的迁移拆成可检验的设计环节。生成骨干与视觉编码器分别携带知识；动作专属容量和世界到动作的信息通路，使这些知识进入控制计算；联合去噪在推理中持续交换特征；人类视频与机器人轨迹的混合，则扩大场景覆盖并维持动作监督。

The most informative evidence comes from separated comparisons: backbone changes, encoder changes under a reinitialized generator, mask-only changes, schedule-only changes, and data recipes under a fixed 600-hour budget. Composing these findings yields a strong multi-embodiment baseline. Camera/noise losses on LIBERO-Plus identify compact robust representations and diverse grounded data as continuing priorities.

其最有解释力的证据来自分开的对照：换骨干、固定随机初始化的生成器比较编码器、只换注意力掩码、只换推理路径、固定 600 小时预算比较数据配方。OpenWAM-α 将这些结果组合后，在多本体仿真与真机任务中成为较强基线。LIBERO-Plus 的相机与噪声损失又将后续重点指向紧凑且稳健的表征，以及覆盖目标环境的高质量具身数据。
