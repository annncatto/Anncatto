OpenWAM-α: From Principles to a Pretrained Model

OpenWAM-α scales the controlled-study recipe into a pretrained model for downstream fine-tuning. Starting from Wan video priors, it jointly learns future visual latents and robot actions, then adapts through supervised fine-tuning on each benchmark.

OpenWAM-α 将受控研究的配方扩展为一个可微调的预训练模型：以 Wan 视频先验为起点，同时学习未来视觉潜变量与机器人动作，再在各个下游基准上进行监督微调。

A $T$-frame input becomes $T^\prime=1+(T-1)/4$ latent frames under the causal VAE. The first latent depends only on the current observation and remains clean. A $(1,2,2)$ patch embedding produces world tokens with temporal and spatial position encoding. The action chunk $a\in\mathbb R^{H\times80}$ is linearly embedded into one token per time step.

输入窗口有 $T$ 帧，因果 VAE 将其压缩为 $T^\prime=1+(T-1)/4$ 个潜帧。第一潜帧只依赖当前观测，始终保持干净。视觉潜变量经 $(1,2,2)$ Patch Embedding 变成带时间、高度、宽度位置编码的 Token；动作块 $a\in\mathbb R^{H\times80}$ 经线性映射变为按动作时刻排列的 Token。

World and action residual widths are 3,072 and 1,024. Stream-owned projections map them to shared attention, then return outputs to their respective paths. AdaLN injects each stream’s noise time; observed-frame tokens stay at $t_v=1$. Prediction heads output both velocity fields. Separate parameters retain direct token exchange at every bridge layer.

世界流宽度为 3,072，动作流宽度为 1,024；它们通过各自的投影进入共同的注意力空间，交互后返回自己的处理路径。每层的 AdaLN 注入该流的去噪时间，首帧 Token 的时间固定为 $t_v=1$。预测头输出速度场，完成一次联合前向计算。参数可以分开，Token 仍能在每个桥接层直接交换信息。

Training

One-stage co-training updates Video DiT, ActionDiT and the proprioception encoder, while freezing Wan2.2-VAE and umT5. Video and action loss weights are both one, with a bell-shaped time weight emphasizing intermediate noise. Human videos have no robot action labels: their action and proprioception channels are masked, leaving world-prediction supervision.

单阶段混合训练更新 Video DiT、ActionDiT 与本体状态编码器，冻结 Wan2.2-VAE 与 umT5。世界与动作损失权重均为 1，时间权重采用在中间噪声水平较高的钟形函数。对于没有机器人动作标签的人类视频，动作与本体状态通道全部屏蔽，仅保留世界预测监督。

$$t_v=1-f_\rho(u_v),\qquad t_a=1-f_\rho(u_a),\qquad u_v,u_a\overset{\mathrm{iid}}\sim\mathcal U[0,1],\qquad\rho=5.$$

The times are independent and share a warp that increases sampling density at high noise. Training therefore exposes both streams to combinations of different feature cleanliness. Synchronized inference follows one path through that support.

两种时间独立采样，且使用相同的时间变换，增加高噪声区间的采样密度。独立的时间关系让两条流在训练时接触不同清晰度的特征组合；同步推理只是这些组合中的一条路径。

Deployment

$$t^i=1-f_\rho(1-i/N),\qquad i=0,\ldots,N,\quad N=10,\quad\rho=5,\quad t_v^i=t_a^i=t^i.$$

Inference starts from Gaussian noise and uses ten coupled Euler steps, re-clamping the clean first frame after every update. Both streams become clean together; actions return through the inverse slot map and denormalization. All reported simulation and real-robot evaluations use synchronous serving, predicting a fresh chunk after the buffer empties. The accelerated loop takes about 170 ms per chunk on an RTX 5090.

推理从高斯噪声开始，使用 10 步耦合 Euler 更新，每步都重新固定干净首帧。视频与动作同步变干净，最终动作经统一空间的逆映射与反归一化返回本体。所有后续仿真和真机结果均采用同步执行：旧缓冲耗尽后才根据当前观测生成新块。加速后，论文报告 RTX 5090 上每块约 170 ms。

The raw egocentric collection contains 71,600 recordings lasting 0.25–6 minutes across 3,006 everyday manipulation tasks. Whole episodes are retained under source budgets, then sampled proportionally so each retained sample is visited once per epoch.

第一视角原始采集包含 71,600 段、每段 0.25–6 分钟的视频，覆盖 3,006 类日常操作任务。真实机器人数据覆盖 17 个物理平台，提供动作与视觉的准确对应；仿真数据补充单臂、双臂技能与环境变化。各来源先按预算保留完整 episode，再按实际样本数量比例训练，每个保留样本每轮访问一次。

Data Curation

Visual curation screens freezing, blur and exposure defects. Robot-signal curation checks command–state consistency, temporal alignment and leading/trailing inactivity. State changes are then checked against video to distinguish motion from sensor jitter.

视觉清洗处理冻结、模糊与曝光问题；机器人信号清洗检查动作—状态一致性、时序对齐和首尾空闲。状态变化再与画面对照，区分真实运动与传感器抖动。

  • Vision: remove decoding failures, frozen/duplicate or solid-color frames, exposure defects, flicker, blur and abrupt jumps. Human videos also exclude hands-out-of-view segments and invalid language.视觉层：剔除无法解码、冻结或重复、纯色、过曝欠曝、闪烁、模糊与突跳；人类视频另去除手离开视野的片段及无效语言标注。
  • Integrity: discard episodes with state/command amplitude ratio ≥3 and per-axis correlation <0.5, or video–signal misalignment affecting over 2% of frames.信号完整性：状态相对指令振幅比 ≥3 且逐轴相关性 <0.5，或视频—信号错位超过 2% 帧时，删除整段。
  • Idle footage: trim leading/trailing inactivity using state thresholds, confirmed by average EEF translation <2 cm/s and rotation <5°/s. Preserve internal pauses to avoid splicing nonadjacent moments.静止段：以状态为主识别首尾空闲，并用末端平均平移速度 <2 cm/s、旋转速度 <5°/s 确认；中途停顿保留，避免拼接本不相邻的时刻。
  • Outliers and cross-checks: median–MAD screens state spikes; changing state with static views is checked for sensor jitter; genuine arm motion with frozen capture causes episode rejection.异常与互检：用 median–MAD 检查状态尖峰；状态在变而全部画面静止时排查传感器抖动；确有机械臂运动但视频冻结时删除该 episode。
  • Episode removal: discard episodes losing over 70% of frames or frozen for at least 90% of their duration.整段删除：清洗去除超过 70% 帧，或视频冻结占比 ≥90% 时，丢弃整个 episode。

The paper observes two advantage regions. Video-latent supervision gives WAMs an additional training signal for ID fitting. Under unseen visual conditions, predicting future visuals adds modeling difficulty, and the selected strong VLAs lead several OOD comparisons. These are results for the tested model/data combinations; pretraining coverage, representation and optimization still shape performance.

论文观察到两种优势区间：WAM 的视频潜变量监督在动作标签之外提供训练信号，有助于拟合域内任务；在未见视觉条件下，未来视觉预测增加了建模难度，本文选取的强 VLA 在多项 OOD 对照中更有优势。该比较描述的是这些模型与数据组合的表现，最终能力还取决于预训练覆盖、表征与优化配方。

“Embodied pretraining mainly improves OOD” in the 600-hour study and “WAMs fit ID better than some VLAs” compare different things. The former varies embodied data within a WAM; the latter compares complete models across paradigms. Together they indicate that video priors help task learning while diverse, accurately action-labeled data determines how broadly that capability transfers.

600 小时实验中“具身预训练主要改善 OOD”，与此处“WAM 相对某些 VLA 更擅长 ID”是两个不同对照。前者在同一 WAM 内比较有没有额外具身数据，后者比较不同范式的完整模型。两者共同指向：强视频先验可以降低任务学习难度，而多样且带准确动作监督的数据决定这种能力能覆盖多少真实变化。
