Introduction

OpenWAM studies how world knowledge acquired through video generation becomes useful for robot control. Its three connected questions concern where knowledge is inherited, how world prediction supports action generation, and how that benefit transfers across scenes and embodiments. The main contribution is a controlled experimental system and a pretraining recipe accumulated through that system.

OpenWAM 研究视频生成预训练中的世界知识怎样转化为机器人控制能力。论文将这一过程拆成三个相连的问题：知识从哪里继承，世界预测怎样帮助动作生成，以及这种帮助怎样延伸到新场景与新本体。其主要贡献是一套可比较的实验体系，以及由实验逐步确定的预训练配方。

Its central hypothesis is that world prediction and action generation can be learned in synergy: world modeling supplies structured knowledge of states, dynamics, and possible futures that can inform action, while action learning focuses the model on changes that matter for control.

其核心假设是，世界预测与动作生成能够协同学习：世界建模提供关于状态、动力学和可能未来的结构化知识，以支持动作；动作学习则让模型关注与控制有关的变化。

OpenWAM, §1

Videos show how objects and scenes change but usually lack executable robot action labels. Robot trajectories provide action supervision, with coverage constrained by hardware and collection costs. WAMs inherit visual-dynamics priors from video generators and learn control through embodied experience. VLAs usually inherit semantic and linguistic priors from vision–language pretraining. Both map observations to actions; their inherited priors and supervision differ.

视频记录了物体与场景怎样变化，但通常没有机器人的可执行动作标签；机器人轨迹提供动作监督，采集规模与环境覆盖却受到硬件和人工成本限制。WAM 继承视频生成模型的视觉动态先验，再用具身交互数据学习控制。VLA 则通常从视觉语言预训练继承语义与语言知识。两者都要将观测映射为动作，区别在于继承的先验和训练监督。

Monolithic systems often implement the encoder, video backbone, action module, attention and sampling procedure as one fixed design. Changing one part may also change others, obscuring the source of a gain. OpenWAM exposes these choices as configuration variables. Software modularity reduces implementation coupling; statistical dependencies between a pretrained backbone and its latent space still require appropriate experimental controls.

单体式系统（monolithic systems）常把编码器、视频骨干、动作模块、注意力方式与采样流程写成一套固定实现。更换其中一项时，其他部分往往也随之改变，使性能差异难以归因。OpenWAM 将这些选择暴露为配置变量。软件模块化降低了实现耦合；编码器潜空间与预训练骨干之间的统计依赖仍然存在，因此替换模块时还要相应设计对照实验。
