A · Limitations and Future Work

  • Training phases: embodied pretraining is the main focus; post-training and adaptation remain separate design spaces.训练阶段：主要研究具身预训练，后训练与新任务适配仍有独立的设计空间。
  • Fusion: current variants emphasize cross-modal attention and hard-routed MoE; earlier token fusion and soft routing are further candidates.模态融合：当前主要采用跨模态注意力与硬路由 MoE，后续可比较更早的 Token 融合和软路由专家。
  • Data: the mixture does not include UMI-style collection, which could combine scene diversity with executable actions.数据来源：当前混合没有 UMI 类数据。此类采集接口有望同时提供场景多样性与可执行动作，连接人类视频的覆盖优势和机器人监督。
  • Encoder: Wan2.2-VAE balances temporal compression and backbone compatibility. Viewpoint, noise and scene results motivate compact, robust, information-rich latent spaces.视觉编码器：Wan2.2-VAE 是时间压缩与骨干适配的现实折中。视角、噪声与场景扰动的结果支持继续研究紧凑、稳健且保留环境信息的潜空间。
