Independent training → joint sampling独立噪声训练 → 同步采样

Training uses $\lambda_v=\lambda_a=1$, bell-shaped loss weights and independent $t_v,t_a=1-f_5(u)$, $u\sim U[0,1]$, emphasizing high-noise samples.训练取 $\lambda_v=\lambda_a=1$，损失权重在中间噪声处较高；独立抽样 $t_v,t_a=1-f_5(u)$，$u\sim U[0,1]$，增加高噪声样本。

With $f_5(s)=5s/(1+4s)$, inference uses 10 synchronized Euler steps, $t_i=1-f_5(1-i/10)$. Re-pin clean O each step, then gather slots and inverse-normalize.$f_5(s)=5s/(1+4s)$；推理采用 10 步同步 Euler 积分，$t_i=1-f_5(1-i/10)$。逐步固定干净 O，结束后取回本体槽位并反归一化。

Benchmarks use synchronous serving; no video decode in the control path. Accelerated inference takes ≈170 ms/chunk on RTX 5090.基准采用同步服务，控制路径不解码视频；加速后在 RTX 5090 上约 170 ms／动作块。
