The training dots cover both axes: independently draw $u_v,u_a\sim U[0,1]$, then set $t_v=1-f_5(u_v)$ and $t_a=1-f_5(u_a)$. With $f_5(s)=5s/(1+4s)$, samples favor high noise.训练散点覆盖二维平面：独立抽取 $u_v,u_a\sim U[0,1]$，再取 $t_v=1-f_5(u_v)$、$t_a=1-f_5(u_a)$。$f_5(s)=5s/(1+4s)$ 使抽样偏向高噪声。

The inference path follows the diagonal: $t_v^i=t_a^i=1-f_5(1-i/10)$, $i=0,\ldots,10$. Ten Euler steps jointly refine both streams; current-observation latents are restored to their unnoised values at every step.推理沿对角线前进：$t_v^i=t_a^i=1-f_5(1-i/10)$，$i=0,\ldots,10$。10 步 Euler 更新共同细化两流，每步将当前观测潜变量恢复为原始编码值。

Loss weighting is separate: $\lambda_v=\lambda_a=1$ balances the two tasks; $w(t)$ assigns larger loss weight to intermediate noise levels.损失加权是另一项设置：$\lambda_v=\lambda_a=1$ 等权组合两项任务，$w(t)$ 则使中间噪声的误差获得较高权重。
