14 · §5.1–5.2 · Training and deployment

OpenWAM-α: Data, Action Space and SamplingOpenWAM-α：数据、动作空间与采样

  • One-stage co-training uses 518.5M frames / 6,369 hours of human egocentric and robot data. Human video supervises world prediction; unavailable action coordinates are masked.单阶段联合训练使用 5.185 亿帧／6,369 小时人类第一视角与机器人数据。人类视频监督世界预测，缺失动作坐标被掩码屏蔽。
  • The 80-D action space has two 34-D arm blocks and 12 reserved slots. Each arm contains xyz 3, rotation 6, gripper 1 and hand 24.80 维空间由两个 34 维手臂区块和 12 维预留组成；单臂包含位置 3、旋转 6、夹爪 1、手部 24 维。
  • Training samples the two noise times independently. Inference uses 10 synchronous Euler steps, re-clamping the observed latent after each update.训练独立抽样两种噪声时间；推理采用 10 步同步 Euler 积分，每一步更新后重新固定观测潜变量。
  • The native action map is inverted after generation. Benchmark evaluations use synchronous serving and skip VAE video decoding in the control path.生成完成后逆映射回本体动作空间；报告评测使用同步服务，控制路径省去 VAE 视频解码。
