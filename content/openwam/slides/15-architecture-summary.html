The Architectural Idea核心架构思想

1 · Preserve a useful prior1 · 保留可用的先验

A matched visual encoder and pretrained video generator supply an organized latent prediction space and knowledge of visual dynamics. A dedicated action network adds the capacity needed for control.配套的视觉编码器与预训练视频生成器提供有组织的潜变量预测空间，以及视觉动态知识；专属动作网络补充控制所需的参数容量。

2 · Couple representations2 · 耦合中间表征

Attention bridges make world features available to action tokens. During learning, the coupled objective adapts the trainable system; during generation, both variables exchange intermediate features at every denoising step.注意力桥接让动作 token 读取世界特征。训练时，联合目标适配整个可训练系统；生成时，两类变量在每个去噪步骤交换中间表征。

3 · Ground the model in actions3 · 用动作监督落地

Human video expands dynamic coverage, robot labels supply executable action targets, and the unified interface transfers the model across embodiments. α demonstrates the recipe in simulation and on real robots.
