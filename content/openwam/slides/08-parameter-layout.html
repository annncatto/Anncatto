Vanilla and modality-routed MoEVanilla 与按模态路由的 MoE

$$[H'_v;H'_a]=\operatorname{Transformer}_{\theta_s}([H_v;H_a])$$

Vanilla shares attention and FFN weights. MoE shares attention but routes each modality to its own FFN; routing is fixed by token type.Vanilla 指共享骨干的基础版本，注意力投影与 FFN 共用权重。MoE 保留共享注意力，但按 token 模态固定分配 FFN 专家。

$$\operatorname{FFN}(h_j)=\begin{cases}\operatorname{FFN}_{v}(h_j),&j\in V\\\operatorname{FFN}_{a}(h_j),&j\in A\end{cases}$$

Separate backbones and understanding独立骨干与理解流

$$Q_v=H_vW_{Q,v},\qquad Q_a=H_aW_{Q,a}$$

Dual systems keep separate attention, FFN and normalization weights. Their own Q/K/V projections interact through masked joint attention.双系统分别拥有注意力、FFN 与归一化参数；联合自注意力使投影后的特征按掩码交互，$K,V$ 也分别生成。

A frozen VLM feeds a trainable understanding stream. World/action streams read it; it reads only itself. Flow matching still predicts world and action.三系统由冻结 VLM提供特征，再由可训练理解流处理；世界／动作流可读取理解流，理解流只读取自身。联合流匹配仍以世界和动作为预测任务。

Ownership ≠ gradient origin: action loss may update video weights through cross-stream links; detachment blocks that path.参数归属不等于梯度来源：跨流连接可使动作损失的梯度进入视频参数；是否分离梯度是另一项独立控制。
