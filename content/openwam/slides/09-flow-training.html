09 · Training objective

Joint Flow-Matching Training联合流匹配训练

$$z^{t_v}=t_vz+(1-t_v)\epsilon_v$$

$z$ is the future visual target and $\epsilon_v$ is Gaussian noise. The derivative of this linear path is $z-\epsilon_v$. The world network predicts a field velocity $\hat v_v$ with target $z-\epsilon_v$. The clean observed frame is provided separately and excluded from this future-prediction loss.$z$ 是未来视觉标签，$\epsilon_v$ 是高斯噪声。这条线性路径对噪声时间的导数为 $z-\epsilon_v$。世界网络预测向量场速度 $\hat v_v$，目标为 $z-\epsilon_v$；干净观测帧单独作为条件，不计入未来预测损失。

$$a^{t_a}=t_aa+(1-t_a)\epsilon_a$$

$a$ is the action-chunk target. The action network predicts $\hat v_a$ toward $a-\epsilon_a$. The two noise times are sampled independently, teaching the coupled model to operate at many combinations of world and action noise levels. Training predicts these local directions; inference will integrate them repeatedly.$a$ 是动作块标签。动作网络预测 $\hat v_a$，目标为 $a-\epsilon_a$。两种噪声时间独立抽样，使耦合模型能够处理世界与动作具有不同噪声水平的多种组合。训练拟合局部变化方向，推理再沿这些方向反复积分。

$$\mathcal L=\mathbb E\!\left[\lambda_v w(t_v)\|\hat v_v-(z-\epsilon_v)\|^2+\lambda_a w(t_a)\|m\odot[\hat v_a-(a-\epsilon_a)]\|^2\right]$$

Here $m$ masks valid robot coordinates and $w$ weights noise levels. α uses $\lambda_v=\lambda_a=1$ and emphasizes intermediate-noise losses. Field velocity describes change in sample space; integrating it produces a video latent or action sample.$m$ 屏蔽无效机器人坐标，$w$ 对噪声水平加权。α 取 $\lambda_v=\lambda_a=1$，在损失中强调中间噪声区域。这里的速度描述样本空间中的变化，积分后得到视频潜变量或动作样本。

Training learns how to move noisy world and action samples toward their paired data targets.训练学习如何把带噪的世界与动作样本推向成对的数据目标。
