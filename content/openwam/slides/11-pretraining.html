11 · §4.3 · Consolidating embodied knowledge

Cross-Domain Pretraining and Visibility跨域预训练与信息流

Strong single-domain performance can reflect target-distribution fitting. Keeping the architecture, optimization budget and inference fixed, the transfer study compares 600-hour robot-only pretraining with two mixtures of 350 hours EgoDex + 250 hours RoboCOIN, followed by identical downstream fine-tuning.单域表现好可能只是拟合了目标分布。迁移实验固定架构、优化预算与推理流程，比较 600 小时机器人预训练，以及350 小时 EgoDex + 250 小时 RoboCOIN的两种混合方式，再进行相同的下游微调。

Robot-only pretraining retains stronger ID fitting; mixed human–robot data raises OOD success from 14.50% to 26.62%. Sequential training and one-stage co-training are close; co-training is kept as the simpler default.机器人预训练更有利于分布内拟合；人类—机器人混合数据使 OOD 成功率由 14.50% 提高到 26.62%。顺序训练与单阶段联合训练接近，最终采用流程更简单的联合训练。

With embodied pretraining, mutual visibility becomes preferable in all reported settings: +0.16 pp on Full, +0.70 on C2R-ID and +0.72 on C2R-OOD. The final recipe carries forward one-stage human–robot co-training with mutual attention.具身预训练后，所报告设置均更倾向互通：Full 增加 0.16、C2R-ID 增加 0.70、C2R-OOD 增加 0.72 个百分点。最终配置采用单阶段人类—机器人联合训练与互通注意力。
