Earlier design ablations train and evaluate on RoboTwin2.0-Full tasks. This study adds embodied pretraining, then fine-tunes every variant on the same RoboTwin target-task demonstrations with the same training settings.前面的设计对照在 RoboTwin2.0-Full 任务上训练、评测。本组增加具身预训练，随后各组用相同 RoboTwin 目标任务示教与训练设置微调，比较迁移收益。

EgoDex: human egocentric manipulation. RoboCOIN: real multi-robot bimanual trajectories. Compare robot-only 600 h, ego 350 h → robot 250 h, and one-stage mixing.EgoDex：人类第一视角操作；RoboCOIN：真实多机器人双臂操作轨迹。对照为机器人 600 小时、EgoDex 350 小时 → RoboCOIN 250 小时，以及两者单阶段混合。

Clean-only fine-tuning → Clean evaluation (ID) ／ Randomized evaluation (OOD)仅用 Clean 微调 → Clean 评测（ID）／ Randomized 评测（OOD）

Robot-only pretraining gives the highest ID score (88.50%). Human–robot mixtures yield stronger OOD transfer: 26.50% sequential, 26.62% joint, versus 23.80% robot-only. One-stage co-training becomes the practical default.仅用机器人数据预训练的 ID 最高（88.50%）；混合人类数据的 OOD 更好：顺序训练 26.50%、联合训练 26.62%，仅机器人数据为 23.80%。后续采用单阶段联合训练。

Before embodied pretraining, action-reads-video (92.39%) and mutual (92.15%) differ by only 0.24 pp. Repeating that comparison after pretraining, mutual wins by 0.16 pp on Full, 0.70 on C2R-ID and 0.72 on C2R-OOD. The final recipe therefore uses co-training + mutual visibility.具身预训练前，动作读取视频（92.39%）与互通（92.15%）仅差 0.24 个百分点。预训练后再次对照：互通在 Full、C2R-ID、C2R-OOD 分别提高 0.16、0.70、0.72 个百分点。最终配置采用联合预训练＋互通可见性。
