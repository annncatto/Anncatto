Transfer experiments retain the dual system and synchronized denoising, then compare 600 h robot-only pretraining with 350 h EgoDex + 250 h RoboCOIN, sequentially or jointly, before identical fine-tuning.前面的对照得到单域默认配置；迁移实验保留双系统与同步去噪，比较600 小时机器人单域预训练与350 小时 EgoDex＋250 小时 RoboCOIN的顺序／联合训练，再进行相同下游微调。

Clean-only fine-tuning → Clean evaluation (ID) ／ Randomized evaluation (OOD)仅用 Clean 微调 → Clean 评测（ID）／ Randomized 评测（OOD）

Robot-only pretraining gives the highest ID score (88.50%). Human–robot mixtures yield stronger OOD transfer: 26.50% sequential, 26.62% joint, versus 23.80% robot-only. One-stage co-training becomes the practical default.机器人单域预训练的 ID 最高（88.50%）；混合人类数据的 OOD 更好：顺序训练 26.50%、联合训练 26.62%，机器人单域为 23.80%。后续采用单阶段联合训练。

The earlier one-way/mutual comparison is repeated after pretraining. Mutual now wins by 0.16 pp on Full, 0.70 on C2R-ID and 0.72 on C2R-OOD. The final recipe therefore uses co-training + mutual visibility.预训练后重新比较此前接近的单向与互通掩码：互通在 Full、C2R-ID、C2R-OOD 分别提高 0.16、0.70、0.72 个百分点。最终配置采用联合预训练＋互通可见性。
