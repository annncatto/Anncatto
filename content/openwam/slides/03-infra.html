To test world–action synergy, the source of each gain must be identifiable. Monolithic WAMs bind six design dimensions; changing an architecture can also change its representation, training data and sampler. Infra exposes these choices through explicit interfaces.检验世界—动作协同，需要辨明收益来自哪项设计。单体式 WAM 常将六个设计维度绑定；更换架构时，表征、数据与采样过程也可能同时变化。Infra 通过明确的接口组织这些选择。

Six coupled design dimensions六个容易耦合的设计维度
Design dimension设计维度Dependency依赖关系
Generative backbone生成骨干Pretrained video DiTs are adapted to particular latent spaces, noise parameterizations and generation tasks.预训练视频 DiT 适配特定潜空间、噪声参数化与生成任务。
Visual representation视觉表征Changing the encoder changes latent dimensions, distributions and prediction targets.更换编码器会改变潜变量的维度、分布与预测目标。
Model architecture模型架构Shared backbones, experts and separate streams allocate computation and parameters differently.共享骨干、专家与独立流决定视频和动作如何计算、分配参数。
Information flow信息流Attention visibility and gradient paths depend on how the streams interact.注意力可见性、参数共享与梯度路径依赖流之间的交互方式。
Inference procedure推理过程Denoising schedules use the world/action noise-state relationships learned during training.去噪调度依赖训练中学到的世界／动作噪声状态关系。
Training data训练数据Human videos and robot trajectories supply different action labels and supervision.人类视频、机器人轨迹及动作标注影响训练目标与动作空间。

Technical dependence模型的技术依赖

A video backbone is pretrained in its encoder’s latent space. Replacing a VAE with DINOv3 changes feature distributions, semantics and targets; matching tensor dimensions cannot preserve the original prior by itself.视频骨干在与编码器匹配的潜空间中预训练。将 VAE 换成 DINOv3，会改变特征分布、语义与预测目标；仅用投影层对齐张量维度，仍不足以保留原有先验。

Experimental confounding实现绑定与实验混杂

If the encoder, action expert and data recipe change together, a higher success rate cannot isolate any one contribution. Infra separates model composition, training, deployment and evaluation, supporting controlled changes to individual design choices.若同时更换编码器、动作专家与数据配方，成功率提高后便难以区分各项贡献。Infra 拆分模型组合、训练、部署与评测，使单项设计能够在共用流程中受控比较。

$$C(\mathcal E,\mathcal S,\mathcal M)$$

$\mathcal E$: visual encoder · $\mathcal S$: stream backbones · $\mathcal M$: visibility mask. The parameter-free compositor runs prepare → layer steps → finalize.$\mathcal E$：视觉编码器；$\mathcal S$：流骨干；$\mathcal M$：可见性掩码。无参数的组合器统一组织输入准备 → 逐层计算 → 输出预测。
