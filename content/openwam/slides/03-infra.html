$\mathcal E$ · Visual encoder$\mathcal E$ · 视觉编码器

Maps observations to the latent sequences predicted by the world stream; kept frozen.将观测映射为世界流预测的潜变量序列，始终保持冻结。

$\mathcal S$ · Stream backbones$\mathcal S$ · 流骨干

Process world/action tokens, with shared weights, action FFNs or an independent ActionDiT; a third stream adds VLM features.世界／动作可共享参数、设置动作专属 FFN，或使用独立 ActionDiT；三系统另加 VLM 理解流。

$\mathcal M$ · Visibility mask$\mathcal M$ · 可见性掩码

Specifies which tokens can attend to which, within and across streams.规定流内与流间哪些 token 可以相互读取。

$$C(\mathcal E,\mathcal S,\mathcal M)$$

The compositor adds no neural-network weights. It organizes prepare → layer steps → finalize; stream backbones and their projections perform the learned computation.组合器不额外引入神经网络权重，只组织输入准备 → 逐层计算 → 输出预测；各流骨干及其投影层承担学习。
