$C(E,S,M)$ combines a frozen visual encoder $E$, stream backbones $S$ and attention visibility $M$. The parameter-free composition coordinates prepare → layer steps → finalize, allowing the same training and deployment interfaces to run different architectures.$C(E,S,M)$ 组合冻结视觉编码器 $E$、流骨干 $S$ 与注意力可见性 $M$。组合器本身不持有参数，通过 prepare → 逐层计算 → finalize 组织前向过程，使不同架构共用训练与部署接口。
