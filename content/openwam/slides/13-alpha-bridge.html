$$\operatorname{Attn}(Q,K,V)=\operatorname{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^{\mathsf T}}{\sqrt{128}}+M\right)V$$

World width 3072 and action width 1024 are projected by stream-owned Q/K/V into 24 heads × 128 dimensions. Concatenation is along the token axis; attention outputs split back into separate output projections, residuals and FFNs.世界流 3072 维、动作流 1024 维，经过各自的 Q/K/V 投影映射到 24 头 × 128 维。两流沿 token 轴拼接，注意力结果再拆回各自的输出投影、残差和前馈网络。

Each stream injects its own noise time through AdaLN: $\gamma(t)\odot\operatorname{LN}(x)+\beta(t)$. Observed tokens use $t_v=1$; future video and actions use $t_v,t_a$.各流通过 AdaLN 注入自身噪声时间：$\gamma(t)\odot\operatorname{LN}(x)+\beta(t)$。观测 token 取 $t_v=1$，未来视频与动作分别取 $t_v,t_a$。

Text/state enters each block’s cross-attention. The joint self-attention mask instead controls reading between observation, future video and action tokens.文本／状态进入各层交叉注意力；联合自注意力中的掩码则控制观测、未来视频与动作 token 之间的读取。
