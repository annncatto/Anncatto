The inputs are intermediate token features, as in the earlier dual-system diagram. α uses widths 3072 and 1024; its own Q/K/V projections map both to 24 × 128 attention dimensions; tokens are concatenated, attended and split back into the original streams.输入与前面的双系统示意图一样，均为 token 中间特征。α 的世界／动作流宽度为 3072／1024；各自的 Q/K/V 投影映射到 24 × 128 维注意力空间；沿 token 轴拼接计算，再按原序列拆回两流。

AdaLN embeds each stream’s noise time to produce scale $\gamma(t)$ and shift $\beta(t)$, modulating normalized features: $\gamma(t)\odot\operatorname{LN}(x)+\beta(t)$. Observation tokens are never noised, so their time stays at $t_v=1$. Text and robot state enter separately through cross-attention.AdaLN 将各流的噪声时间嵌入映射为缩放 $\gamma(t)$ 与偏移 $\beta(t)$，调节归一化特征：$\gamma(t)\odot\operatorname{LN}(x)+\beta(t)$。观测 token 从未加噪，其时间始终为 $t_v=1$；语言与本体状态则通过交叉注意力进入。
