Monolithic WAMs bind six design dimensions: an architecture change can also alter representation, data and sampling. Infra exposes these choices through explicit interfaces for controlled comparison.检验世界—动作协同，需要辨明收益来自哪项设计。单体式 WAM 常将六个设计维度绑定；更换架构时，表征、数据与采样过程也可能同时变化。Infra 通过明确的接口组织这些选择。

Six coupled design dimensions六个容易耦合的设计维度
Design dimension设计维度Dependency依赖关系
Generative backbone生成骨干Pretrained video DiTs are adapted to particular latent spaces, noise parameterizations and generation tasks.预训练视频 DiT 适配特定潜空间、噪声参数化与生成任务。
Visual representation视觉表征Changing the encoder changes latent dimensions, distributions and prediction targets.更换编码器会改变潜变量的维度、分布与预测目标。
Model architecture模型架构Shared backbones, experts and separate streams allocate computation and parameters differently.共享骨干、专家与独立流决定视频和动作如何计算、分配参数。
Information flow信息流Attention visibility and gradient paths depend on how the streams interact.注意力可见性、参数共享与梯度路径依赖流之间的交互方式。
Inference procedure推理过程Denoising schedules use the world/action noise-state relationships learned during training.去噪调度依赖训练中学到的世界／动作噪声状态关系。
Training data训练数据Human videos and robot trajectories supply different action labels and supervision.人类视频、机器人轨迹及动作标注影响训练目标与动作空间。

Four components with explicit interfaces四个具有明确接口的组件

  • Composable model: encoders, stream backbones and visibility masks.组合模型：接入编码器、流骨干与可见性掩码。
  • Training runtime: one trainer for all supported compositions.训练运行时：同一训练器处理不同模型组合。
  • Deployment runtime: one policy server serves the resulting checkpoints.部署运行时：同一策略服务器加载并服务模型检查点。
  • Evaluation protocol: benchmarks access that server through a common protocol.评测协议：各基准通过统一协议访问该服务器。

Control over pretrained initialization预训练初始化的控制

  • Matched encoder: reuse the pretrained video DiT with its native visual encoder.原生匹配：使用视频骨干配套的视觉编码器，直接复用预训练 DiT 参数。
  • Representation ablation: swap encoders and randomly initialize the base DiT to isolate visual-representation gains (§4.1.2).表征对照：替换编码器并随机初始化基础 DiT，单独检验视觉表征带来的收益（§4.1.2）。
