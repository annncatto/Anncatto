Earlier WAMs organize model, training, serving and evaluation around a particular design. These coupled choices complicate controlled comparison. Cosmos Policy and Motus illustrate how the six dimensions enter computation.现有 WAM 的模型、训练、部署与评测往往围绕特定设计组织，组件之间的绑定使各项设计难以独立比较。Cosmos Policy 与 Motus 展示了六个维度如何落实为具体计算。

Model architecture模型架构Parameter sharing and action-specific capacity.参数共享范围与动作专属容量。One shared video Transformer processes all modalities.各模态共享同一个视频 Transformer。Separate video, action and understanding experts; action and video have equal depth.视频、动作、理解各有专家；动作专家与视频专家等深。
Information flow信息流Which features each token can read.每类 token 可以读取哪些特征。Self-attention across the interleaved latent-frame sequence.通过交错潜在帧序列中的自注意力交换信息。Expert-owned Q/K/V interact through joint attention.各专家生成自己的 Q/K/V，再通过联合注意力交换特征。
Inference procedure推理过程Known conditions, generated variables and update order.已知条件、生成变量及去噪更新顺序。Condition/target masks select tasks; parallel or sequential decoding.条件帧与目标帧决定策略／世界／价值预测；可并行或顺序解码。Video/action noise times select five modes, including VLA and world modeling.视频、动作的噪声时间切换 VLA、世界模型等五种模式。
Training data训练数据Supervision, action space and training stages.可用监督、动作表示与训练阶段。One-stage robot post-training predicts actions, future states and values.目标机器人示教监督动作、未来状态与价值，单阶段后训练。Video adaptation → optical-flow latent-action pretraining → real-action fine-tuning.视频适配 → 光流潜动作预训练 → 真实动作微调。

Infra makes these choices composable, while reusing training, serving and evaluation interfaces.Infra 将这些选择组织为可组合模块，并复用训练、部署与评测接口。
