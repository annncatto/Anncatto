Monolithic WAMs bind six design dimensions: an architecture change can also alter representation, data and sampling. Infra exposes these choices through explicit interfaces for controlled comparison.检验世界—动作协同，需要辨明收益来自哪项设计。单体式 WAM 常将六个设计维度绑定；更换架构时，表征、数据与采样过程也可能同时变化。Infra 通过明确的接口组织这些选择。
