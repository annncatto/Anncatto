The policy server separates when a new action chunk is predicted from how video and action are denoised within that prediction. These are independently configurable choices.策略服务分别配置何时预测新的动作块，以及一次预测内部如何对视频与动作去噪。两项选择彼此独立。

Action execution modes动作执行方式

Synchronous: pause when the buffer empties. Asynchronous: infer before it empties, then skip the stale prefix. $H$ denotes predicted chunk length; $n$ denotes the executed horizon.同步：缓冲耗尽后暂停，等待新预测。异步：提前预测，再跳过过时前缀。$H$ 为预测动作块长度，$n$ 为执行窗口。

α uses synchronous execution in every reported simulation and real-robot benchmark: exhaust the buffer, predict a fresh chunk, then execute it. §5.1 ↗α 的仿真与实机评测均采用同步执行：缓冲耗尽后暂停，依据当前观测预测新动作块，再执行。§5.1 ↗

Video–action denoising schedules两流去噪调度

At each step, the model receives noise times $(t_v,t_a)$ and predicts two velocities. The sampler applies Euler updates; the schedule sets the two times: synchronized, video-leading or action-leading.每步按 $(t_v,t_a)$ 预测两流速度。采样器统一执行 Euler 更新，调度规定两个时间如何推进：可同步，也可让视频或动作领先。

$$\begin{aligned}z_{i+1}&=z_i+\Delta t_v\hat v_z,\\a_{i+1}&=a_i+\Delta t_a\hat v_a\end{aligned}$$

α uses synchronized denoising: $t_v^i=t_a^i$, with 10 coupled Euler steps. Neither stream leads. This choice is separate from synchronous execution. §5.1 ↗α 采用同步去噪：$t_v^i=t_a^i$，执行 10 步联合 Euler 更新，两流均不领先。这与动作块的同步执行是独立的选择。§5.1 ↗
