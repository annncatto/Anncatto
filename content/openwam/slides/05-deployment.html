OpenWAM-Infra: Generation and ExecutionOpenWAM-Infra：生成与执行

Serving: each prediction fills an action buffer. Synchronous serving waits when it empties; asynchronous serving predicts early and skips the stale prefix. $H$ is predicted chunk length and $n$ is the executed horizon.服务调度：预测结果写入动作缓冲区；同步服务在缓冲耗尽后等待，异步服务提前预测并跳过过时前缀。$H$ 是预测动作块长度，$n$ 是执行窗口长度。

Generation: future latents and actions start from noise. Each joint forward predicts velocities, and Euler integration updates both samples for $N$ steps. Serving timing and the world/action noise schedule are independent choices.生成过程：未来潜变量与动作从噪声出发，每次联合前向预测向量场速度，Euler 积分更新两类样本，共进行 $N$ 步。服务调度与世界／动作的噪声调度是两个独立选择。
