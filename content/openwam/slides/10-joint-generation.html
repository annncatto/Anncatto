Joint Denoising and Action Generation联合去噪与动作生成

One coupled generation loop一个耦合的生成循环

Start future latents $z_0$ and actions $a_0$ from independent Gaussian noise. A joint forward pass predicts both velocities, followed by the Euler updates below. α uses 10 synchronous steps: $t_v=t_a$ throughout. The observed latent is clamped and re-pinned after every step.未来潜变量 $z_0$ 与动作 $a_0$ 从独立高斯噪声出发。联合前向过程预测两个向量场速度，再按下式进行 Euler 更新。α 使用 10 步同步去噪，全程 $t_v=t_a$；观测潜变量在每一步后重新固定。

$$z_{i+1}=z_i+\Delta t_i\hat v_v,\qquad a_{i+1}=a_i+\Delta t_i\hat v_a$$

World features improve as actions refine世界特征与动作共同细化

Every forward pass includes all 30 paired bridge layers. Actions are repeatedly refined using the current world representation; the world stream can also read the evolving action representation. The final actions can be returned without decoding the video into pixels.每次前向过程都经过全部 30 层配对桥接。动作反复利用当前世界表征细化，世界流也可以读取逐渐形成的动作表征；最终返回动作时，无需把视频潜变量解码为像素。
