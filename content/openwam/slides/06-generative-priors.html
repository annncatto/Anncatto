  • Wan2.2-TI2V-5B reaches 92.39%, 1.40 pp below 14B. Its dense backbone and 4×16×16 VAE compression provide the practical default.Wan2.2-TI2V-5B 达到 92.39%，比 14B 低 1.40 个百分点；采用稠密骨干与 4×16×16 VAE 压缩，成为后续对照的默认配置。
