06 · §4.1.1 · Generative world priors

Inherited Video-Generation Priors继承视频生成先验

What transfers from the upstream model?上游模型中的什么知识能够迁移？

  • The controlled WAM uses separate video and action modules connected through joint attention. Video-module parameters are copied from each pretrained generator.对照使用视频与动作模块分离、通过联合注意力连接的 WAM；视频模块继承各预训练生成器的参数。
  • Across the four backbones, success rises from 90.14% to 93.79%. Backbone choice affects the resulting policy.四种骨干对应的平均成功率由 90.14% 上升至 93.79%，骨干选择会影响最终策略。
  • The 5B model reaches 92.39%, only 1.40 points below 14B. The study keeps 5B for the remaining controls, balancing performance and cost.5B 模型达到 92.39%，仅比 14B 低 1.40 个百分点；后续对照采用 5B，平衡性能与成本。
  • The backbones differ in more than size. The other inheritance channel is the latent representation defined by the visual encoder.骨干之间的区别还包括架构、数据和目标。另一条先验继承通道是视觉编码器定义的潜变量表征。
