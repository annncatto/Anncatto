α combines frozen Wan2.2-VAE, pretrained Wan2.2-TI2V-5B, a dedicated 1B ActionDiT, mutual attention and synchronized denoising. Video, action and state-projection parameters train end to end; only VAE and umT5 remain frozen.α 组合冻结 Wan2.2-VAE、预训练 Wan2.2-TI2V-5B、专属 1B ActionDiT、互通注意力与同步去噪。视频、动作与状态投影共同端到端训练，仅 VAE 与 umT5 冻结。
