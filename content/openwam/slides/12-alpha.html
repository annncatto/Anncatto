Each action step becomes a 1024-D token; 1D RoPE marks its position in the action chunk. umT5 maps the instruction to 4096-D text features; the projected 80-D robot state adds a context token. Both streams read this context through cross-attention.每个动作步投影为 1024 维 token，一维 RoPE 标明它在动作块中的顺序。umT5 将指令编码为 4096 维文本特征，80 维本体状态另投影成一个条件 token；两流通过交叉注意力读取这些条件。
