Dedicated action capacity专门的动作容量

Single shares a Transformer; MoE adds action FFNs. Dual separates video/action backbones. Tri adds an understanding stream fed by frozen VLM features.单系统共享 Transformer，MoE 增加动作专属前馈网络；双系统分离视频／动作骨干；三系统增加读取冻结 VLM 特征的理解流。

Forward access and gradient paths前向读取与梯度通路

Detached cross-attention still reads video features but stops action gradients at that interface. Joint attention permits two-way exchange; the next experiment controls its visibility mask.分离梯度交叉注意力仍读取视频特征，但在接口处阻断动作梯度。联合注意力允许双向交换，下一项实验用可见性掩码控制交换方向。

The study selects Dual + joint attention to balance performance and complexity while isolating world–action interaction.双系统＋联合注意力兼顾表现与复杂度，并将世界—动作交互与 VLM 理解特征的作用分开。
