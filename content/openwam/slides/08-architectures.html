Joint prediction needs an effective connection联合预测需要有效的连接

  • Single systems share a Transformer; MoE adds action-specific FFNs. Dual systems separate video/action backbones. Tri systems add an understanding stream fed by frozen VLM features.单系统共享 Transformer，MoE 为动作增加专属前馈网络；双系统分离视频与动作骨干；三系统加入读取冻结 VLM 特征的理解流。
  • The study keeps the dual system to balance complexity and isolate world–action interaction from VLM understanding features.研究保留双系统，以平衡复杂度，并把世界—动作交互与 VLM 理解特征的影响分开。
  • Joint attention permits exchange; the visibility mask determines its direction.联合注意力提供交换接口，可见性掩码进一步决定交换方向。

Detached cross-attention reads video features but blocks action gradients at that interface; visibility and gradient flow differ.分离梯度的交叉注意力保留前向的视频特征读取，在该接口阻断动作梯度；注意力可见性与梯度通路是不同的选择。
