“Joint attention” covers the stream connections below. Q comes from tokens being updated; K supplies matches and V the aggregated features. Self-attention takes them from one sequence; cross-attention takes Q and K/V from different sequences.“联合注意力”概括下列流间连接。Q来自待更新的 token，K用于匹配，V提供被加权汇总的特征。自注意力的三者来自同一序列；交叉注意力的 Q 与 K/V 来自不同序列。

Joint self-attention联合自注意力 · Joint self-attention

Each stream projects its own Q/K/V. Join along tokens, attend, and split. “Self” refers to the joint sequence, while projection weights remain separate. Masks control cross-stream access.两流分别投影 Q/K/V，沿 token 轴拼接，计算后拆回各流。“自”指联合序列内部读取，投影参数仍分别拥有；掩码决定跨流可见方向。

Joint cross-attention联合交叉注意力 · Joint cross-attention

Both streams retain self-attention. An extra bridge lets Qₐ query video Kᵥ/Vᵥ to update actions. Text/state cross-attention uses the same operator, with K/V from context c.两流保留流内自注意力；动作流另以Qₐ 查询视频 Kᵥ/Vᵥ，通过交叉注意力接收世界特征。语言／状态条件也用交叉注意力，但 K/V 来自上下文 c。
