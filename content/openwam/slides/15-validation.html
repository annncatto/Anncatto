LIBERO-Plus visual perturbationsLIBERO-Plus 的视觉扰动

α’s LIBERO-fine-tuned checkpoint is evaluated directly on LIBERO-Plus. Average success is 69.2%: camera 33.8%, noise 39.8%, light 97.0%. Other WAMs score much higher on camera/noise. α’s single-arm pretraining draws only on DROID and single-arm InternData-A1 (1,100+ h). The authors link its weakness to limited coverage and disturbance-sensitive multi-frame pixel prediction.
