Compact Visual Representation Priors紧凑的视觉表征先验

07 · §4.1.2 · Visual representation priors
  • To isolate representation effects, the study keeps the Wan2.2-TI2V-5B architecture but randomizes its weights, then compares four visual encoders.为分离表征的作用，保留 Wan2.2-TI2V-5B 架构，但随机初始化网络权重，再比较四种视觉编码器。
  • Raw DINOv3 and V-JEPA features are high-dimensional. An S-VAE contracts them into 48-D latents suitable for the DiT.DINOv3 与 V-JEPA 原始特征维度较高，S-VAE 将其压缩为适合 DiT 处理的 48 维潜变量。
  • DINOv3 improves from 76.42% to 90.18% with S-VAE, close to WanVAE’s 90.30%. Compactness and world information matter more than the encoder category alone.DINOv3 加入 S-VAE 后由 76.42% 提高到 90.18%，接近 WanVAE 的 90.30%。关键在于紧凑性与世界信息，而非编码器类别本身。
  • WanVAE learns temporal compression; the other encoders average four-frame features. Latent channels, token count and Transformer hidden width have different roles.WanVAE 原生学习时序压缩，其余编码器采用四帧特征平均。潜变量通道数、token 数与 Transformer 隐藏宽度分别承担不同作用。

The cited PS-VAE framework regularizes semantic and pixel reconstruction. OpenWAM tests the representation-compression idea with a 48-D bottleneck.所引 PS-VAE 框架联合正则语义与像素重建；OpenWAM 对照采用表征压缩思路与 48 维瓶颈。
