From high-dimensional features to compact latents高维特征与紧凑潜空间

Direct DINOv3 features yield 76.42%. Adding S-VAE raises success to 90.18%, close to WanVAE’s 90.30%. The representation-encoder prior becomes effective after its features are adapted to the generator’s prediction space.直接使用 DINOv3 高维特征时成功率为 76.42%；加入 S-VAE 后达到 90.18%，接近 WanVAE 的 90.30%。表征编码器的先验在适配生成骨干的预测空间后，同样能够支持高性能 WAM。

The authors attribute WanVAE’s remaining edge to its backbone-compatible latents and learned temporal compression, compared with imposed four-frame averaging.作者将 WanVAE 的剩余优势归因于其与视频骨干的适配，以及原生学习的时序压缩；其他编码器使用的是外加的四帧特征平均。

Finding 1 · Inherit a capable generator and compact, information-rich visual latents. Reconstruction versus representation learning alone does not determine the best prediction space.发现 1：继承足够强的生成骨干，以及紧凑且信息丰富的视觉潜空间。选择编码器应考察潜变量的实际性质，而非仅按重建／表征类别选型。
