From Generated Chunks to Robot Control从动作块到机器人闭环

Three different step counts三种不同的步数

$N$ counts denoising iterations inside one prediction; $H$ counts the actions predicted in a chunk; $n$ counts the actions executed before observing again. Increasing $N$ changes generation cost, while increasing $n$ delays the next perception-based correction.$N$ 是一次预测内部的去噪积分次数，$H$ 是预测动作块的长度，$n$ 是重新观测前执行的动作数。增加 $N$ 改变生成开销，增加 $n$ 则延后下一次基于感知的纠正。

The final action sample is gathered and inverse-normalized into native robot commands. VAE decoding is skipped in the control path because action generation already consumed latent world features.最终动作样本取回有效坐标并反归一化为本体指令。控制路径省去 VAE 解码，因为动作生成已在潜变量层利用世界特征。

Two meanings of synchronization两种不同层面的同步

Synchronous denoising means $t_v=t_a$ within the model. Synchronous serving means robot execution waits for a fresh chunk when the buffer empties. α uses both in its reported evaluations.同步去噪表示模型内 $t_v=t_a$；同步服务表示动作缓冲区耗尽后，机器人等待新动作块。α 的报告评测同时采用这两种同步。

Infra also supports asynchronous serving: prediction begins before the buffer ends, and the stale prefix is skipped when the new chunk arrives. This changes scheduling around the model, independently of its world/action denoising path. Fig. 3 ↗原图 3 ↗Infra 还支持异步服务：缓冲区结束前启动预测，新动作块到达时跳过已过时的前缀。这改变的是模型外部的执行调度，可以独立于世界与动作的去噪路径设置。Fig. 3 ↗原图 3 ↗

Joint denoising produces the chunk; repeated observation and replanning turn it into closed-loop control.联合去噪产生动作块，重复观测与重新规划使其形成闭环控制。
