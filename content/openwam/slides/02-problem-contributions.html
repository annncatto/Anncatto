The Problem and Three Contributions研究问题与三项贡献

Videos of the changing world are abundant, whereas robot trajectories with executable action labels remain comparatively scarce. Monolithic systems couple representations, backbones, architecture, information flow, inference and data, making design choices difficult to compare systematically.世界变化的视频数据丰富，带有可执行动作标签的机器人轨迹却相对稀缺。现有单体 WAM 系统把生成骨干、视觉表征、模型架构、信息流、推理过程与训练数据组成紧密耦合，使各项设计的作用难以被系统地比较。

Research question研究问题Finding对应发现
Which world knowledge should be inherited?应当继承哪些世界知识？A capable video generator and compact, information-rich latents transfer upstream priors effectively.足够强的生成骨干与紧凑、信息丰富的视觉潜变量最有利于继承上游先验。
How should world and action learning interact?如何形成世界与动作学习的协同？Dedicated action capacity, world-to-action visibility and synchronized denoising support synergy.协同依赖专门的动作容量、世界到动作的信息流，以及推理时同步联合去噪。
How can the synergy transfer across domains?如何把这种协同扩展到不同领域？Embodied pretraining mainly improves OOD generalization; human video and robot trajectories contribute complementary knowledge.具身预训练主要改善分布外泛化；人类视频扩展世界覆盖，机器人轨迹提供可执行动作知识。
