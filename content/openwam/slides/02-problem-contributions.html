The Problem and Three Contributions研究问题与三项贡献

02 · §1 · Introduction

Videos of the changing world are abundant, whereas robot trajectories with executable action labels remain comparatively scarce. Monolithic systems couple representations, backbones, architecture, information flow, inference and data, making design choices difficult to compare systematically.世界变化的视频数据丰富，带有可执行动作标签的机器人轨迹却相对稀缺。现有单体 WAM 系统把生成骨干、视觉表征、模型架构、信息流、推理过程与训练数据组成紧密耦合，使各项设计的作用难以被系统地比较。

1. Capable video backbones and compact, informative latents.发现 1：强生成骨干与紧凑、信息丰富的潜变量。

2. Dedicated action capacity, world-to-action access and synchronized denoising.发现 2：专门动作容量、世界到动作信息流与同步去噪。

3. Human–robot pretraining primarily improves OOD generalization.发现 3：人类—机器人联合预训练主要改善分布外泛化。
