Human Video and Robot Actions人类视频与机器人动作

A visual objective across domains跨域共享视觉预测目标

Human egocentric video offers diverse object interactions and scene changes, even when robot commands are absent. It trains the world-prediction objective. Robot clips additionally pair those changes with state and action labels, connecting visual dynamics to executable control.人类第一视角视频包含多样的物体交互与场景变化，即使没有机器人指令，也能训练世界预测目标。机器人片段额外提供状态与动作标签，把视觉动态与可执行控制联系起来。

For human-only video, action/state coordinates are masked. Missing commands are not treated as demonstrations of zero action. α combines both data types in one end-to-end pretraining stage.只有人类视频的样本屏蔽动作与状态坐标，缺失的指令不会被当作零动作示教。α 在单阶段端到端预训练中联合使用两类数据。

One semantic action space统一的动作语义空间

Native robot actions are normalized and scattered into 80 fixed slots: two 34-D arm blocks plus 12 reserved dimensions. Each arm contains position 3, rotation 6, gripper 1 and dexterous hand 24; validity mask $m$ selects supervised coordinates.各机器人原始动作归一化后映射到固定的 80 个槽位：两个 34 维手臂区块加 12 维预留。单臂包含位置 3、旋转 6、夹爪 1、灵巧手 24 维；有效性掩码 $m$ 选择参与监督的坐标。
