Expert structure ↔ attention interface专家结构 ↔ 注意力接口

Wan2.2-5B and an equally deep action expert exchange features through tri-model joint attention. A trainable understanding expert reads frozen Qwen3-VL-2B features. Experts retain separate parameters.Wan2.2-5B 与等深动作专家经三模态联合注意力交换特征；可训练理解专家读取冻结 Qwen3-VL-2B 的特征，各专家保留独立参数。

Noise conditions ↔ modeling mode噪声条件 ↔ 任务模式

A UniDiffuser-style scheduler assigns video/action noise levels, switching among VLA, world models, inverse dynamics, video generation and joint prediction.UniDiffuser 式调度分配视频／动作噪声水平，使同一模型切换 VLA、世界模型、逆动力学、视频生成与联合预测五类模式。

Latent actions ↔ training data潜动作 ↔ 训练数据

Optical-flow latent actions supervise action pretraining on video. Training proceeds through video-model adaptation → latent-action joint pretraining → target-robot action fine-tuning.光流潜动作使视频参与动作预训练；训练依次进行视频模型适配 → 潜动作联合预训练 → 目标机器人动作微调。
