05 · Architecture families

Shared Capacity or Separate Backbones共享容量与独立骨干

Single-System单系统

One Transformer processes video, action and state tokens. Vanilla shares attention and feed-forward networks (FFNs); the MoE variant routes action tokens to a dedicated FFN while retaining shared attention.一个 Transformer 处理视频、动作与状态 token。Vanilla共享注意力和前馈网络；MoE让动作 token 进入专属前馈网络，同时保留共享注意力。

The two tasks compete within largely shared capacity.两项任务主要使用同一套参数容量。

Dual-System双系统

A video DiT and an ActionDiT retain separate parameters. Joint attention mixes their tokens at bridge layers; cross-attention instead lets action queries read video features.视频 DiT 与 ActionDiT 保留独立参数。联合注意力在桥接层混合两类 token；交叉注意力则让动作 Query 读取视频特征。

The separate IDM variant generates future video before inferring actions.独立的 IDM 变体先生成未来视频，再由视频推断动作。

Tri-System三系统

Frozen VLM features feed a trainable understanding stream. World and action streams can read it; understanding tokens read only themselves, providing read-only semantic context.冻结 VLM 的特征进入可训练的理解流。世界流与动作流可以读取理解流，理解 token 只读取自身，形成只读语义上下文。

α selects the dual joint-attention design.α 采用双系统联合注意力设计。

Separate backbones specialize their capacity; bridge attention supplies the connection between tasks.独立骨干为两项任务保留专门容量，桥接注意力建立任务间的联系。
