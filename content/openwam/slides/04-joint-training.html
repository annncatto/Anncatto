Joint Flow-Matching Training联合流匹配训练

04 · §3.2 · Training runtime

All architectures use the same sample contract and joint flow-matching objective: $(\ell,o_{1:T},a_{1:H},q,m)$ denotes instruction, video window, action chunk, optional robot state and valid-coordinate mask.所有架构使用同一数据接口与联合流匹配目标。样本 $(\ell,o_{1:T},a_{1:H},q,m)$ 分别包含指令、视频窗口、动作块、可选本体状态和有效坐标掩码。

$$\begin{aligned}z^{t_v}&=t_vz+(1-t_v)\epsilon_v,& a^{t_a}&=t_aa+(1-t_a)\epsilon_a,\\(\hat v_z,\hat v_a)&=v_\theta(z^{t_v},a^{t_a},t_v,t_a,c)\end{aligned}$$

Conditions: the observed frame stays clean; language and optional state form $c$. The independently sampled $t_v,t_a$ range from noise at 0 to data at 1. Future video and actions are training labels.已知条件：观测帧保持干净，语言与可选状态组成 $c$。$t_v,t_a$ 独立抽样，0 表示噪声，1 表示数据；未来视频与动作来自训练标签。

Network outputs: $\hat v_z$ and $\hat v_a$ approximate $z-\epsilon_v$ and $a-\epsilon_a$, the derivatives of the linear noise–data paths. Weighted squared errors supervise them; action loss uses $m$, and the clean frame is excluded from video loss.网络输出：$\hat v_z$、$\hat v_a$ 拟合线性噪声—数据路径的导数 $z-\epsilon_v$、$a-\epsilon_a$。加权平方误差监督两流；动作损失使用 $m$，干净观测帧不计入视频损失。

Flow matching regresses a vector field along a chosen noise–data path. The trained field is then integrated by an ODE solver to generate samples.流匹配沿选定的噪声—数据路径拟合向量场，生成时再用 ODE 求解器积分。
