04 · §3.2 · Training runtime

OpenWAM-Infra uses one sample contract $(\ell,o_{1:T},a_{1:H},q,m)$: instruction, video, action chunk, optional state and coordinate mask. Flow matching learns the velocity along a noise–data path.OpenWAM-Infra 的统一样本 $(\ell,o_{1:T},a_{1:H},q,m)$ 包含指令、视频、动作块、可选状态与坐标掩码。流匹配学习噪声—数据路径上的向量场速度。

$$\begin{aligned}\mathcal L={}&\lambda_v\mathbb E\!\left[w(t_v)\|\hat v_z-(z-\epsilon_v)\|_2^2\right]\\&+\lambda_a\mathbb E\!\left[w(t_a)\|m\odot(\hat v_a-(a-\epsilon_a))\|_2^2\right]\end{aligned}$$

Conditions and noised targets条件与加噪标签

$z^{t_v}=t_vz+(1-t_v)\epsilon_v$; $a^{t_a}=t_aa+(1-t_a)\epsilon_a$. The observed frame stays clean; language and state form $c$.$z^{t_v}=t_vz+(1-t_v)\epsilon_v$；$a^{t_a}=t_aa+(1-t_a)\epsilon_a$。观测帧保持干净，语言与状态组成 $c$。

Independent $t_v,t_a$ cover the joint noise plane: 0 is noise, 1 is data.独立抽样 $t_v,t_a$，覆盖联合噪声平面：0 为噪声，1 为数据。

Joint vector-field supervision联合向量场监督

One forward predicts $(\hat v_z,\hat v_a)$. Their targets $z-\epsilon_v$ and $a-\epsilon_a$ are path derivatives; $\lambda_v,\lambda_a$ weight streams and $w(t)$ weights noise levels.一次前向预测 $(\hat v_z,\hat v_a)$，目标 $z-\epsilon_v$、$a-\epsilon_a$ 是路径导数；$\lambda_v,\lambda_a$ 控制两流权重，$w(t)$ 控制噪声级别权重。

$m$ selects valid action coordinates. Clean conditioning frames are excluded from video loss.$m$ 仅保留有效动作坐标，视频损失排除干净条件帧。
