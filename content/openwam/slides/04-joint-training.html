All architectures expose the same sample $(\ell,o_{1:T},a_{1:H},q,m)$: instruction, video, actions, state and valid coordinates. The architecture prepares inputs and predicts both vector fields; Infra’s shared trainer applies the same loss.所有架构共用样本 $(\ell,o_{1:T},a_{1:H},q,m)$：指令、视频、动作、状态与有效坐标。架构负责准备输入和预测两流向量场，Infra 的统一训练器据此计算同一损失。

$$\begin{aligned}\mathcal L={}&\lambda_v\mathbb E\!\left[w(t_v)\|\hat v_z-(z-\epsilon_v)\|_2^2\right]\\&+\lambda_a\mathbb E\!\left[w(t_a)\|m\odot(\hat v_a-(a-\epsilon_a))\|_2^2\right]\end{aligned}$$

Common flow-matching supervision共同的流匹配监督

$z^{t_v}=t_vz+(1-t_v)\epsilon_v$ and $a^{t_a}=t_aa+(1-t_a)\epsilon_a$. Independent $t_v,t_a$ cover noise (0) to data (1); the targets are the path velocities.$z^{t_v}=t_vz+(1-t_v)\epsilon_v$、$a^{t_a}=t_aa+(1-t_a)\epsilon_a$。独立抽样 $t_v,t_a$，覆盖噪声（0）到数据（1）；监督目标是路径速度。

$\lambda_v,\lambda_a$ weight streams; $w(t)$ weights noise levels. $m$ selects valid action coordinates; clean conditioning frames are excluded from video loss.$\lambda_v,\lambda_a$ 控制两流权重，$w(t)$ 控制噪声级别权重。$m$ 选择有效动作坐标，视频损失排除干净条件帧。

Shared training utilities共用训练运行时

  • Accelerate + DeepSpeed ZeRO-1/2; BF16 by default, gradient accumulation and clipping.Accelerate＋DeepSpeed ZeRO-1/2；默认 BF16，支持梯度累积与裁剪。
  • Activation checkpointing, optional CPU activation offload and optimizer-state offload.梯度检查点，可选激活与优化器状态 CPU 卸载。
  • Pretrain, fine-tune from a checkpoint, or resume optimizer, scheduler and data position.支持预训练、检查点微调，以及恢复优化器、调度器与数据位置。
