One shared trainer optimizes both predictions with the same form of flow-matching objective, then saves model and training state.前图的模型组合通过统一接口返回两流预测；训练器复用同一形式的流匹配目标，负责反向传播、参数更新与检查点管理。

Two flow-matching paths两条流匹配路径

$$\begin{aligned}z^{t_v}&=t_vz+(1-t_v)\epsilon_v,\\a^{t_a}&=t_aa+(1-t_a)\epsilon_a.\end{aligned}$$

Sample $t_v,t_a$ independently from noise (0) to data (1), with Gaussian $\epsilon_v,\epsilon_a$.独立抽样 $t_v,t_a$，从噪声（0）到数据（1）；$\epsilon_v,\epsilon_a$ 为高斯噪声。

Sample $(\ell,o_{1:T},a_{1:H},q,m)$: instruction, video, actions, optional proprioceptive state and an action-dimension validity mask.样本 $(\ell,o_{1:T},a_{1:H},q,m)$：指令、视频、动作、可选本体状态与动作维度有效性掩码。

Two vector fields and a weighted objective双向量场与加权目标

$$(\hat v_z,\hat v_a)=F_\theta(z^{t_v},a^{t_a},t_v,t_a,c)$$
$$\mathcal L=\lambda_v\mathcal L_v+\lambda_a\mathcal L_a$$

$c$ contains language/state; $\lambda_v,\lambda_a$ weight the tasks and $w(t)$ the noise levels. $m$ excludes unlabeled action coordinates; clean frames have no video loss.$c$ 提供语言及可选本体状态等条件。$\lambda_v,\lambda_a$ 控制两项任务权重，$w(t)$ 控制噪声级别权重。$m$ 屏蔽无标签动作维度，干净条件帧不计入视频损失。

Adding losses does not establish cross-stream interaction; the network connections and visibility mask determine feature exchange.损失相加本身不构成信息交换；跨流交互需由网络连接及可见性掩码实现。

$$\begin{aligned}\mathcal L={}&\lambda_v\mathbb E\!\left[w(t_v)\|\hat v_z-(z-\epsilon_v)\|_2^2\right]\\&+\lambda_a\mathbb E\!\left[w(t_a)\|m\odot(\hat v_a-(a-\epsilon_a))\|_2^2\right]\end{aligned}$$

Runtime: default BF16; Accelerate/ZeRO-1/2, accumulation, clipping, activation checkpointing and optional CPU offload.训练支持：默认 BF16；Accelerate＋ZeRO-1/2、梯度累积与裁剪、激活检查点及可选 CPU 卸载。
