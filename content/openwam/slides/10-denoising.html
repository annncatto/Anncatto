10 · §4.2.3 · Inference-time information flow

Inference-Time Information Flow推理时的信息流

  • After establishing world-to-action access in training, the study tests whether video-leading generation gives actions better guidance.训练中建立世界到动作的信息通路后，研究进一步检验：让视频在生成时领先，能否更好地指导动作。
  • Mutual visibility stays fixed. Variance-shift and linear-offset schedules let either stream lead; independently sampled training noise times cover these joint states.固定互通掩码，分别用方差偏移与线性错位调度使某一流领先；训练时独立抽样的噪声时间覆盖这些联合状态。
  • The synchronized baseline is 93.0%, and the paper adopts synchronized denoising. The relative ranking of video-leading and action-leading paths differs between the two schedule families.同步基线为 93.0%，论文最终采用同步去噪。两类非同步调度对“视频领先”和“动作领先”的相对排序不同。
  • The result supports a representation-learning account of WAM synergy, rather than requiring a completed future video followed by inverse dynamics.结果支持 WAM 协同的表征学习解释，无需先完成未来视频，再按逆动力学顺序产生动作。
