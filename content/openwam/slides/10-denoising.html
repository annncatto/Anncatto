The mask experiment shows that actions need world features. Study next tests whether world prediction should denoise ahead of action generation, using Infra’s common sampler with different schedules.掩码实验表明动作需要世界特征。接着检验推理时是否应让世界预测先于动作生成去噪：在 Infra 的统一采样器上比较不同调度。

What a schedule changes调度改变的变量

The common forward pass takes noisy video and actions. Their times $t_v,t_a$ specify denoising progress: 0 = pure noise, 1 = unnoised data. A schedule sets both values at each Euler step.一次前向同时接收加噪的视频与动作。$t_v,t_a$ 分别表示两流的去噪程度，0 为纯噪声，1 为未加噪数据；调度规定每次 Euler 更新使用哪一对时间。

For $t_v>t_a$, actions read more-denoised video features; for $t_a>t_v$, actions lead. Sync uses $t_v=t_a$. The model, mutual mask and training remain fixed; training samples both times independently.$t_v>t_a$ 时，动作读取去噪更充分的视频特征；$t_a>t_v$ 时动作领先；同步取 $t_v=t_a$。固定模型、互通掩码与训练，仅改变推理调度；训练时两时间独立抽样。

Two families, different relative rankings两类路径与排序差异

  • Variance shift: both streams progress, with unequal relative noise levels; video-leading exceeds action-leading in this family.方差偏移：两流持续推进，改变相对噪声水平；该族中视频领先优于动作领先。
  • Linear offset: the lagging stream stays at noise initially, then catches up; action-leading exceeds video-leading in this family.线性错位：滞后流初段保持纯噪声，再开始追赶；该族中动作领先优于视频领先。

The synchronized baseline is 93.0%; the paper adopts it thereafter. Leading order and the specific noise-time path must be considered together.同步基线为 93.0%，论文后续采用同步调度。领先方向与具体噪声路径共同影响结果。

Retained design: synchronized joint denoising. The authors connect this result to the representation-learning hypothesis: video supervision helps learn features used for action prediction, without requiring video generation to finish first.设计选择：同步联合去噪。作者据此支持表征学习假说：视频监督在训练中帮助形成动作预测可用的特征，推理可让两流共同细化，无需先完成视频预测。
