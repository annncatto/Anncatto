Noise progress controls conditioning去噪进度改变读取条件

Within one generation, 0 is noise and 1 is clean data. Video-leading means $t_v>t_a$: action updates read a more-denoised world stream. Action-leading reverses this relation; synchronized sampling uses $t_v=t_a$.在同一次生成中，0 为噪声，1 为干净数据。视频领先指 $t_v>t_a$，动作更新时读取去噪更充分的世界流；动作领先则相反，同步取 $t_v=t_a$。

Keep mutual visibility fixed. Independently sampled training times cover the joint $(t_v,t_a)$ noise plane; inference then chooses a path through that plane.固定互通掩码。训练时独立抽样两流噪声时间，覆盖 $(t_v,t_a)$ 联合噪声平面；推理时选择经过该平面的路径。

Two families, different relative rankings两类路径与排序差异

  • Variance shift: both streams progress, with unequal relative noise levels; video-leading exceeds action-leading in this family.方差偏移：两流持续推进，改变相对噪声水平；该族中视频领先优于动作领先。
  • Linear offset: the lagging stream stays at noise initially, then catches up; action-leading exceeds video-leading in this family.线性错位：滞后流初段保持纯噪声，再开始追赶；该族中动作领先优于视频领先。

The synchronized baseline is 93.0%; the paper adopts it thereafter. Leading order and the specific noise-time path must be considered together.同步基线为 93.0%，论文后续采用同步调度。领先方向与具体噪声路径共同影响结果。

Authors’ interpretation: representation-learning synergy. World prediction and action learning shape useful features together; synchronized inference refines both streams at each step, rather than requiring a completed future video before action generation.作者将其解释为表征学习的协同：世界预测与动作学习共同塑造有用特征，同步推理在每一步细化两流，动作生成无需等待完整未来视频。
