Inference-order comparison生成顺序对照

Training established that actions need world features. The inference study tests whether video-leading, action-leading or synchronized denoising makes better use of them.训练结果表明动作需要世界特征；推理实验继续比较视频领先、动作领先与同步去噪，检验生成顺序的作用。

Fix mutual visibility and independently sampled training noise times. Vary only the inference path: variance shift or linear offset. Linear offset initially holds the lagging stream at pure noise.固定互通掩码，训练独立抽样两流噪声时间；推理仅改变方差偏移或线性错位路径。线性错位使滞后流在初段保持纯噪声。

Selected schedule and interpretation采用的调度与结果解释

The synchronized baseline is 93.0%, and the paper carries forward synchronized denoising. The ranking of video-leading and action-leading paths changes across the two schedule families.同步基线为 93.0%，论文后续采用同步去噪；视频领先与动作领先的相对排序随调度族改变。

Visibility and denoising progress control different conditions. The mask determines which world features actions can read. This comparison fixes mutual visibility and changes only the two noise-time paths, testing the same information route at different noise levels.可见性与去噪进度控制的是不同条件。掩码决定动作能读取哪些世界特征；本组固定互通掩码，仅改变两流噪声时间路径，比较同一信息通路在不同噪声阶段下的作用。

The retained default is synchronized joint denoising. The authors interpret the evidence as representation-learning synergy: world prediction and action learning shape useful features together. Actions need not wait for a completed future video; α refines both streams at each step.最终采用同步联合去噪。作者将结果解释为表征学习的协同：世界预测与动作学习共同塑造有用特征，动作生成不必等待完整未来视频；α 据此在每一步同时细化两流。
