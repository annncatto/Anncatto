Each GPU processes 24 clips, giving a global batch of 3,072 across 128 GPUs without gradient accumulation. The 33-frame window becomes nine latent frames after 4× causal compression. Video stride four subsamples the source recording; VAE compression acts within that sampled window. Both determine the temporal scale seen by the model.

每张 GPU 放置 24 个 clip，128 张卡组成 3,072 的全局 batch，无梯度累积。33 帧窗口经 4 倍因果时间压缩后得到 9 个潜帧；Video Stride 4 指原视频的采样间隔，VAE 时间压缩则发生在采样后的窗口内。两种操作共同决定网络接触的时间尺度。
