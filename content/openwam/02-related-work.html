Related Work

World–Action Models. World models learn predictive structure from observations for planning, model-based reinforcement learning or policy evaluation. WAMs connect prediction directly to a policy, learning executable actions alongside future visual states. OpenWAM focuses on the mechanisms of prior inheritance and cross-modal interaction.

World–Action Models. 世界模型通过观测学习环境的预测结构，可用于规划、基于模型的强化学习或策略评估。WAM 将这种预测能力直接连接到策略，在生成未来视觉状态的同时学习可执行动作。本文重点比较知识继承与跨模态交互的机制。

DINO-WM learns action-conditioned dynamics over frozen DINOv2 spatial features and optimizes action sequences toward goal images, showing how feature prediction supports control. Unified World Models (UWM) couples video and action diffusion in one Transformer with independent modality timesteps, learning from robot trajectories and action-free videos. OpenWAM further separates the effects of priors, capacity, visibility, sampling paths and data recipes.

DINO-WM 已在冻结的 DINOv2 空间特征上学习动作条件动力学，并通过优化动作序列到达目标图像，展示表征预测可以支持控制。Unified World Models（UWM） 在同一 Transformer 中联合建模视频与动作扩散，分别控制两种模态的噪声时间，并利用带动作轨迹和无动作视频训练。OpenWAM 在这些研究方向上进一步分离先验、容量、可见性、推理路径与数据配方的影响。

Open Research Ecosystems for Generalist Robot Policy Learning. OpenVLA provides an open-weight pretrained baseline, StarVLA organizes modular policies, and XPolicyLab standardizes evaluation and deployment. OpenWAM extends composable models and shared runtimes to joint video–action generation.

Open Research Ecosystems for Generalist Robot Policy Learning. OpenVLA 提供开放权重的预训练基线，StarVLA 组织模块化策略实现，XPolicyLab 统一评估与部署。OpenWAM 将可组合模型与共享运行时扩展到视频—动作联合生成，使不同 WAM 能通过同一套接口比较。

Towards a Scientific Understanding of Model Design. Representation choice, modality-specific capacity, data mixtures and knowledge flow have been studied in multimodal learning. OpenWAM places these variables within one WAM system, derives a recipe through controlled studies, and tests it at pretraining scale. Robot success and transfer experiments establish which earlier ideas carry over to control.

Towards a Scientific Understanding of Model Design. 表征选择、模态专属容量、数据混合和知识流动已在多模态研究中被讨论。OpenWAM 的增量在于将这些变量放入同一 WAM 体系，先进行受控实验，再检验配方在大规模预训练中的表现。原有概念在机器人控制中是否成立，需要成功率与迁移实验给出证据。
