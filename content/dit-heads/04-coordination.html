底盘与机械臂Base–arm coupling

移动操作中，底盘动作同时改变相机视角、目标相对位置和机械臂可达区域。底盘尚未稳定时，按旧相对位置生成的抓取轨迹会面对新的几何条件。联合输出 18 维动作使这些量处于同一预测问题，模型内部还需要学习它们之间的约束。

Base motion changes camera views, target-relative position, and arm reachability. A grasp planned against an earlier relative pose encounters changed geometry as the base moves. Jointly predicting 18 action dimensions places these controls in one problem while leaving their constraints to be learned.

底盘运动条件Mobility conditioning

AC-DiT 先以底盘动作为监督训练轻量 mobility head，学习运动隐表示；再冻结该分支，将生成的隐特征提供给全身动作头。全身动作头继续输出底盘与机械臂动作，底盘分支承担运动先验。AC-DiT mobility runner。

AC-DiT first trains a mobility head on base actions, then freezes it and supplies its latent features to whole-body generation. The body head predicts both base and arm actions, conditioned on the mobility prior. AC-DiT mobility runner.

$$F_m=\operatorname{MobilityDiT}(Z_m,O,\ell),\qquad\hat A=\operatorname{BodyDiT}(Z,O,\ell,F_m)$$

运动特征取自底盘分支五次去噪中最后一个 DiT block 的动作 token，在最终 MLP 读出之前收集，再沿序列维拼接并加入全身动作头的跨注意力条件。这一流程将运动先验显式放到操作预测之前。推理时先得到底盘分支的隐特征，再运行全身生成，计算开销涉及两个生成分支。实际控制仍需要在底盘运动、手臂接近与夹爪动作之间安排时序。

Mobility features concatenate pre-MLP tokens from the final DiT block at five denoising steps into whole-body cross-attention conditions. The motion prior explicitly precedes manipulation prediction. Inference first obtains mobility features and then runs body generation, incurring computation in both branches. Execution still coordinates base motion, arm approach, and gripper timing.

多模态权重Multimodal weighting

PMA 将外部、左右腕图像和点云特征投影到与语言可比较的空间，用余弦相似度生成权重，再加权视觉 token。宽视角有利于观察底盘与目标关系，腕部局部视角和几何信息有利于接近与抓取；公开实现将余弦相似度线性映射为 0.5–1.5 的正权重，随当前观察与语言条件变化。AC-DiT PMA implementation。

PMA projects external-view, wrist-view, and point-cloud features into a space comparable to language, computes cosine-related weights, and reweights visual tokens. Wide views support base–target relations; local views and geometry support approach and grasping. The implementation maps cosine similarity to positive weights in 0.5–1.5. AC-DiT PMA implementation.
