CLIP 表征CLIP representations

CLIP以图像与文本配对训练两个编码器，将对应样本的投影特征拉近、非对应样本拉开。对比损失同时进行图像到文本与文本到图像分类，预训练后的视觉特征可表达物体、场景及语言关联。

CLIP trains separate image and text encoders on paired data, bringing matched projected features together and separating unmatched pairs through bidirectional contrastive classification. Radford et al..

$$s_{ij}=\frac{u_i^\top v_j}{\tau},\qquad\mathcal L_{\mathrm{CLIP}}=\frac12\big[\operatorname{CE}(S,\operatorname{diag})+\operatorname{CE}(S^\top,\operatorname{diag})\big]$$

其中 $u_i,v_j$ 为归一化后的图像与文本投影向量，$\tau$ 为温度，$\operatorname{diag}$ 表示批内图文配对的索引。ViT-B/16 将 $224\times224$ 图像分为 $14\times14=196$ 个图块，再加一个 CLS token，形成 197 个视觉 token；视觉 Transformer 的隐藏宽度为 768。CLIP 对比空间还包含输出投影，MTD 直接提取视觉主干的 768 维 CLS 特征。

Here $u_i,v_j$ are normalized projected embeddings, $\tau$ is temperature, and $\operatorname{diag}$ denotes paired image/text indices within the batch. ViT-B/16 produces $14\times14=196$ patches plus a CLS token from a $224\times224$ image, with hidden width 768. CLIP’s contrastive embedding also uses an output projection; MTD takes the backbone’s 768-dimensional CLS feature directly.

MTD 共享各相机的视觉编码器，每个观察时刻、每路相机得到一个全局向量。文本编码器输出 512 维池化特征，经可训练投影进入条件；文本编码器冻结，视觉编码器以主学习率的 0.1 倍更新。多相机提供不同视角，全局 CLS 汇总决定后续 DiT 实际接收到的视觉信息。

MTD shares the vision encoder across cameras, producing one global vector per camera and observation time. A frozen text encoder supplies pooled 512-dimensional features through a trainable projection. The vision encoder uses 0.1 times the main learning rate. Multi-view coverage and CLS summarization determine the visual information supplied to the DiT. ObservationEncoder.

每个 block 依次执行调制后的自注意力和前馈网络。自注意力在 32 个动作位置之间交换信息；六组调制量分别控制两个子层的偏置、尺度和残差门控。条件向量广播到所有动作位置，位置关系通过 RoPE 编入 Q/K。输出投影恢复为 $[B,32,18]$，本次 DDPM 配置将其解释为噪声预测。

Each block applies modulated self-attention followed by a feed-forward network. Attention connects 32 action positions; six modulation groups control shifts, scales, and residual gates for both sublayers. The condition broadcasts across positions, with RoPE encoding position in Q/K. The output returns to $[B,32,18]$, interpreted as predicted noise in this DDPM configuration.

$$(\beta_a,\gamma_a,\alpha_a,\beta_f,\gamma_f,\alpha_f)=\operatorname{Linear}(\operatorname{SiLU}(\tilde c))\in\mathbb R^{B\times6d}$$

观察与执行窗口Observation and execution windows

两帧观察包含前一时刻与当前时刻 $[o_{t-1},o_t]$。这份实现的训练动作窗口与观察起点对齐，32 个位置对应 $[a_{t-1},a_t,\ldots,a_{t+30}]$。执行队列从索引 $T_o-1=1$ 开始，跳过历史位置，再取 $n$ 个动作。

Two observations are $[o_{t-1},o_t]$. This implementation aligns the 32-action training window with the observation start: $[a_{t-1},a_t,\ldots,a_{t+30}]$. Execution starts at index $T_o-1=1$, skips the historical position, and takes $n$ actions.

$$A_{\mathrm{exec}}=A[:,\,T_o-1:T_o-1+n],\qquad n\le H-T_o+1=31$$

按 30 Hz 计，25 步覆盖 0.833 s，30 步覆盖 1 s，多执行的 5 步为 0.167 s。延长窗口会保留更多当前预测，同时延后下一次观察驱动的重规划。若关键松爪动作位于被截去的尾部，队列更新可能持续将它推迟；实验中窗口变化的作用需要结合实际松爪目标与重规划时刻解释。

At 30 Hz, 25 actions span 0.833 s and 30 span 1 s; the extra five cover 0.167 s. A longer window preserves more of the current prediction and delays observation-driven replanning. A release placed in a discarded tail can be repeatedly postponed. The experiment therefore relates window length to release targets and replanning timing.

状态与阶段条件State and phase conditioning

玩偶任务的语言指令在各阶段保持相同。开始移动、停止、抓取和释放的区别来自视觉与状态条件：物体相对位置、手臂构型、夹爪状态和底盘运动共同决定当前可执行的动作。两帧观察在 30 Hz 下相隔约 33 ms，提供短时变化；条件向量同时保留各路相机及关节状态，学习过程中需要建立它们与动作阶段的联系。

The toy instruction stays constant across phases. Vision and state distinguish starting, stopping, grasping, and releasing through target-relative position, arm configuration, gripper state, and base motion. Two observations are about 33 ms apart at 30 Hz, providing short-term change; the condition vector retains camera and joint-state features for phase-dependent action generation.
