策略与动作头Policy and action head

机器人策略根据观察 $O$ 与任务指令 $\ell$ 生成动作。观察编码器 $g_\phi$ 提取条件 $c$，动作生成器 $G_\theta$ 从随机初值 $Z$ 生成动作块 $A$，执行队列按控制周期送出动作。图像、语言和状态进入条件路径，带噪动作在生成路径中反复更新。

A robot policy generates actions from observations $O$ and instruction $\ell$. The observation encoder $g_\phi$ produces condition $c$; the generator $G_\theta$ transforms a random initial tensor $Z$ into an action chunk $A$; the execution queue sends controls at the control rate. Images, language, and state enter the conditioning path, while noisy actions are repeatedly updated in the generation path.

$$c=g_\phi(O,\ell),\qquad A=G_\theta(Z;c),\qquad a_t=\operatorname{Queue}(A)_t$$

动作头描述模块在完整策略中的作用，DiT 描述该模块内部的 Transformer 结构。动作头可以包含多层注意力、前馈网络和输入输出投影。视觉语言模型作为策略主干时，DiT 接收其特征并承担动作生成；在动作网络内部，DiT 又是反复参与采样的计算主体。动作头的参数规模与计算开销取决于其具体结构；同一 DiT 骨架可配合噪声预测或流匹配目标训练。

An action head names a module’s role within a policy; DiT names the Transformer architecture inside that module. A head can contain many attention and feed-forward layers plus input/output projections. When a VLM supplies the policy backbone, the DiT generates actions from its features. Within the action network, that same DiT is the main computation repeatedly evaluated during sampling. Its architecture can support noise prediction or a flow-matching objective.

Diffusion Policy already separated visual encoding from action denoising. Octo feeds multimodal Transformer readouts into a diffusion head; CogACT connects a pretrained VLM to a DiT action module. These illustrate a clearer division between representation learning and action generation, alongside continued development of standalone diffusion policies.

扩散模型Diffusion models

Sohl-Dickstein 等（2015）受非平衡热力学启发，以逐步加噪破坏数据结构，再学习逆过程恢复数据分布。DDPM（2020）将这一过程发展为有效的图像生成方法，常用训练目标是预测加到样本上的噪声。DiT（2022 年预印本，ICCV 2023）在图像隐空间中以 Transformer 替换去噪 U-Net。迁移到机器人时，被生成的张量变为一段动作，图像与机器人状态作为条件。

Sohl-Dickstein et al. (2015) introduced a non-equilibrium-thermodynamics-inspired process that destroys data structure with noise and learns its reverse. DDPM (2020) developed effective image synthesis with a commonly used noise-prediction objective. DiT (2022 preprint, ICCV 2023) replaced the image-latent denoising U-Net with a Transformer. Robot policies generate action tensors conditioned on images and robot state.

对动作块 $A\in\mathbb R^{H\times D_a}$，$H$ 是动作序列长度，$D_a$ 是每步动作维度。加噪后的张量与 $A$ 形状相同：模型在一次网络计算中处理整段动作，再通过多次采样更新得到最终结果。采样过程的时间索引表示噪声程度，与动作块中的真实控制时刻分开。

For $A\in\mathbb R^{H\times D_a}$, $H$ counts action positions and $D_a$ is the action width. Corruption preserves the tensor shape: one network evaluation processes the whole chunk, and repeated sampling updates produce the final result. Generative time tracks noise level separately from physical action time.

DDPMDDPM

DDPM包含正向加噪、去噪网络训练和逆向采样三个部分。训练目标规定网络应预测什么，采样规则规定如何利用预测更新动作。采用相同噪声预测目标训练的网络，可以使用 DDPM 的逆向更新，也可以使用 DDIM 更新。

DDPM includes forward corruption, denoiser training, and reverse sampling. The objective specifies what the network predicts; the sampling rule specifies how predictions update actions. A network trained with the same noise-prediction objective can use DDPM reverse updates or DDIM updates.

设干净动作 $A_0=A$，噪声等级为 $k=1,\ldots,K$。设每级噪声方差为 $\beta_k$，$\alpha_k=1-\beta_k$，累计系数为 $\bar\alpha_k=\prod_{i=1}^{k}\alpha_i$。训练时从示教中取动作块，随机选择 $k$，再独立采样标准高斯噪声 $\epsilon$，直接构造该噪声等级的样本：

Let $A_0=A$ be clean actions and $k=1,\ldots,K$ index noise levels. For noise variance $\beta_k$, define $\alpha_k=1-\beta_k$ and $\bar\alpha_k=\prod_{i=1}^{k}\alpha_i$. Training samples a demonstration chunk, a random level $k$, and standard Gaussian noise $\epsilon$, then constructs that noisy sample directly:

$$A_k=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_k}A_0+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_k}\epsilon,\qquad \epsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$$
$$\mathcal L_{\mathrm{DDPM}}=\mathbb E_{A_0,c,k,\epsilon}\!\left[\|\epsilon_\theta(A_k,k,c)-\epsilon\|_2^2\right]$$

网络接收带噪动作、噪声等级和观察条件，输出同形状的噪声估计。梯度下降使预测接近本次加入的噪声；一个训练样本只需计算随机选中的等级。不同等级共享同一套网络参数。

The network receives noisy actions, noise level, and observation conditions, and predicts a same-shaped noise tensor. Gradient descent fits the sampled noise target. Each training example evaluates its sampled level, with network weights shared across levels.

$$A_{k-1}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha_k}}\left(A_k-\frac{\beta_k}{\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_k}}\epsilon_\theta(A_k,k,c)\right)+\sigma_k\xi_k$$

采样从 $A_K\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$ 开始，按 $k=K,\ldots,1$ 反复运行网络与更新公式。$\sigma_k$ 是采样噪声尺度，$\xi_k$ 为新采样的标准高斯噪声，末步通常取零。训练提供各噪声等级的监督，采样将这些局部预测串成完整生成过程。最终动作块经过反归一化和执行窗口选择后发送给机器人。

Sampling starts at $A_K\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$ and repeatedly evaluates the network and update for $k=K,\ldots,1$. Here $\sigma_k$ is the sampling-noise scale and $\xi_k$ fresh standard Gaussian noise, usually omitted at the final step. Training supervises individual noise levels; sampling composes their predictions into a generated chunk, which is then denormalized and sliced for execution.

DDIM 采样DDIM sampling

DDIM（Denoising Diffusion Implicit Models）原论文提出一类与 DDPM 共享训练目标、具有不同生成过程的模型。实际部署时，DDIM 通常指使用已有噪声预测网络的采样方法：先由当前 $A_k$ 估计干净动作，再更新到较低噪声等级 $j<k$。选取稀疏等级可减少采样步数。确定性配置 $\eta=0$ 的更新为：

DDIM (Denoising Diffusion Implicit Models) introduces a model family with the same training objective as DDPM and a different generative process. In deployment, DDIM commonly refers to sampling with an existing noise predictor: estimate clean actions from $A_k$, then advance to a lower level $j<k$. A sparse schedule reduces sampling steps. With deterministic sampling, $\eta=0$:

$$\hat A_0=\frac{A_k-\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_k}\,\epsilon_\theta(A_k,k,c)}{\sqrt{\bar\alpha_k}}$$
$$A_j=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_j}\,\hat A_0+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_j}\,\epsilon_\theta(A_k,k,c)$$

跳步减少网络调用次数，原噪声预测权重可以复用。确定性配置在给定条件与初始噪声后确定整条采样轨迹，改变初始噪声仍可得到不同动作样本。DDIM 的一般形式还允许通过 $\eta$ 调节额外随机噪声；上式展示其零噪声配置。

Skipping levels reduces network evaluations while reusing the noise-prediction weights. For fixed conditions and initial noise, the deterministic configuration fixes the sampling trajectory; different initial noise can still produce different actions. General DDIM updates use $\eta$ to control additional stochastic noise; the equations show the zero-noise configuration.

流匹配Flow matching

Flow Matching通过回归向量场，学习将简单噪声分布输送到数据分布。以噪声 $Z$ 与示教动作 $A$ 之间的线性路径为例，生成时间 $\tau=0$ 对应噪声，$\tau=1$ 对应动作。训练随机抽取一对 $(Z,A)$ 及时间 $\tau$，构造中间样本 $X_\tau$，用路径导数 $A-Z$ 监督网络：

Flow matching learns a vector field transporting noise to data. For a linear path between noise $Z$ and demonstrated actions $A$, generative time $\tau=0$ denotes noise and $\tau=1$ denotes data. Training samples $(Z,A)$ and $\tau$, constructs $X_\tau$, and regresses the path derivative $A-Z$:

$$X_\tau=(1-\tau)Z+\tau A,\qquad u_\tau=A-Z$$
$$\mathcal L_{\mathrm{FM}}=\mathbb E_{A,c,Z,\tau}\!\left[\|v_\theta(X_\tau,\tau,c)-(A-Z)\|_2^2\right]$$

流匹配规定向量场的训练目标；采样还需要选择常微分方程（ODE）的数值积分器。从新噪声 $X_0$ 出发，固定观察条件，用 Euler 法沿已学习的向量场积分，可得：

Flow matching specifies how to train the vector field; sampling additionally requires a numerical ODE solver. Starting from fresh noise $X_0$ with fixed observation conditions, Euler integration gives:

$$\frac{\mathrm dX_\tau}{\mathrm d\tau}=v_\theta(X_\tau,\tau,c),\qquad X_{\tau+\Delta\tau}=X_\tau+\Delta\tau\,v_\theta(X_\tau,\tau,c)$$

这里的“速度场”表示整个动作张量在生成时间内的变化率；张量中的各位置仍分别对应机器人未来的控制时刻。上式对应 MTD 在 $\sigma_{\min}=0$ 时的路径；SmolVLA 使用相反的生成时间方向，路径导数与积分方向也随之调整。训练学习场，推理沿场生成动作，两者分别使用示教样本与新采样的初始噪声。

This velocity field is the rate of change of the entire action tensor in generative time; its positions still represent future robot controls. The equations match MTD with $\sigma_{\min}=0$. SmolVLA reverses the generative-time orientation together with the target and integration direction. Training fits the field using demonstrations; inference follows it from newly sampled noise.

采样与执行时序Sampling and execution timing

一次预测同时涉及三个数量：采样迭代次数 $N$、预测动作数 $H$、实际执行动作数 $n$。若 $N=10,H=32,n=25$，网络被调用十次，每次更新整段 32 步动作，完成后队列保留其中 25 步。控制频率 $f=30\,\mathrm{Hz}$ 时，这段执行窗口覆盖 $25/30=0.833\,\mathrm s$；其间是否接收新观察，由同步或异步执行方式决定。

A prediction involves sampling iterations $N$, predicted actions $H$, and executed actions $n$. With $N=10,H=32,n=25$, the network runs ten times, refining all 32 action positions each time; the queue then retains 25 actions. At $f=30\,\mathrm{Hz}$, this window spans $25/30=0.833\,\mathrm s$. Observation refresh during execution depends on the synchronous or asynchronous execution scheme.

$$T_{\mathrm{sample}}\approx T_{\mathrm{condition}}+N\,T_{\mathrm{network}},\qquad T_{\mathrm{exec}}=\frac{n}{f}$$

视觉条件在一次采样内通常可以缓存，动作网络则随噪声等级重复计算。减少 $N$ 主要缩短生成延迟，改变 $n$ 主要改变重规划频率和开环执行长度，改变 $H$ 则改变单次预测的动作范围。三者分别决定生成成本、反馈时序与预测范围。

Observation conditions can usually be cached within one sample, while the action network is evaluated at successive noise levels. Reducing $N$ primarily reduces generation latency; changing $n$ changes replanning frequency and open-loop duration; changing $H$ changes the predicted action range.

宽度、头数与层数Width, heads, and depth

编码器与解码器描述模块的输入输出作用，MLP、线性层和 Transformer 描述实现结构。MTD 的动作输入投影将每步 18 维动作变为 512 维特征，输出投影再将其变回 18 维噪声预测。Transformer 内的前馈 MLP 分别变换各位置的通道，自注意力负责动作位置之间的信息交换。

Encoder and decoder name input/output roles; an MLP, linear layer, or Transformer specifies implementation. MTD projects each 18-dimensional action to width 512 and projects the final features back to 18-dimensional noise predictions. Feed-forward MLPs transform channels at each position; self-attention exchanges information between action positions.

设动作特征为 $X\in\mathbb R^{B\times H\times d}$。$B$ 为批量，$H$ 为动作位置数，$d$ 为每个位置的隐藏维度。注意力头数 $h$ 决定每头宽度 $d_h=d/h$；层数 $L$ 表示 Transformer block 重复的次数。一个动作头可包含六层 Transformer，每层再包含八个注意力头。MTD 的 $H=32,d=512,h=8,L=6$ 对应每头 64 维。

For $X\in\mathbb R^{B\times H\times d}$, $B$ is batch size, $H$ action positions, and $d$ hidden width. With $h$ heads, each has width $d_h=d/h$; $L$ counts blocks. One action head can contain six Transformer blocks, each with eight attention heads. MTD uses $H=32,d=512,h=8,L=6$, giving 64 dimensions per head.

$$Q_j=XW_j^Q,\ K_j=XW_j^K,\ V_j=XW_j^V,\qquad W_j^{Q,K,V}\in\mathbb R^{512\times64}$$
$$\operatorname{head}_j=\operatorname{softmax}\!\left(\frac{Q_jK_j^\top}{\sqrt{64}}\right)V_j,\qquad Y=\operatorname{Concat}(\operatorname{head}_1,\ldots,\operatorname{head}_8)W^O$$

每头投影都读取完整 512 维输入，学习不同的特征组合。Q、K、V 分头后形状为 $[B,8,32,64]$，每头得到 $32\times32$ 的动作关系矩阵；拼接与输出投影再混合各头特征。头数改变关系建模的子空间，隐藏维度改变每个 token 的容量，增加层数则增加连续变换的深度。

Each projection reads all 512 input features. Q/K/V reshape to $[B,8,32,64]$, producing a $32\times32$ action-relation matrix per head; concatenation and output projection mix the results. Head count changes relational subspaces, width changes token capacity, and depth adds successive transformations.

条件注入Conditioning

Cross-Attention 以动作特征作 query，以图像、语言等条件 token 作 key/value。每个动作位置根据自身 query 读取不同条件信息。adaLN 在层归一化中将条件向量映射为通道尺度、偏置与残差门控，并广播到动作位置。两者可以组合使用，例如跨注意力读取多模态内容，同时由噪声水平调制归一化。

Cross-attention uses action queries and condition keys/values, allowing each action position to read relevant condition content. adaLN maps a condition vector in layer normalization to channel scales, shifts, and residual gates shared across action positions. They can coexist: cross-attention reads multimodal content while noise level modulates normalization.

$$\operatorname{CA}(X,C)=\operatorname{softmax}\!\left(\frac{XW_Q(CW_K)^\top}{\sqrt{d_h}}\right)CW_V$$
$$\operatorname{adaLN}(x;c)=(1+\gamma(c))\odot\operatorname{LN}(x)+\beta(c),\qquad x^+=x+\alpha(c)\odot F(\operatorname{adaLN}(x;c))$$

adaLN-Zero 将残差门控等调制层的末层初始化为零，使 block 从接近恒等映射开始训练。MTD 将视觉、状态、语言和扩散时间拼成条件向量，逐层生成六组调制量；MDT、GR00T 和 Spirit 还使用跨注意力保留条件 token 的读取路径。

adaLN-Zero initializes modulation outputs to zero, starting blocks near identity. MTD forms a vector from vision, state, language, and diffusion time to produce six modulation groups per block. MDT, GR00T, and Spirit also use cross-attention to retain condition-token access.

层数与模型规模Depth and model size

$$P_{\mathrm{total}}=P_{\mathrm{vision}}+P_{\mathrm{language}}+P_{\mathrm{action}}+P_{\mathrm{projection}},\qquad P_{\mathrm{block}}\approx12d^2+P_{\mathrm{condition}}$$

上式的 $12d^2$ 对应四组注意力投影与扩张比为 4 的前馈层，仅作结构量级估算。条件投影、跨注意力及不同宽度会改变系数。层数描述变换深度，宽度决定矩阵大小；完整模型参数还包括视觉语言主干。

The $12d^2$ estimate covers four attention projections and an expansion-4 feed-forward layer. Conditioning and cross-attention add terms. Total parameters also include the backbone, so depth, width, head parameters, and complete-model size should be listed separately.
