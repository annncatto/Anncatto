DiT 将带噪动作序列作为 Transformer 的处理对象，视觉、语言与机器人状态作为条件。完整策略包括观察编码、动作生成与执行队列；DiT action head 指其中生成动作的网络。不同方法主要改变条件的组织方式、生成网络的结构及采样开销。

DiT processes noisy action sequences with a Transformer conditioned on vision, language, and robot state. A complete policy includes observation encoding, action generation, and execution queues; its DiT action head is the action-generating network. Methods differ in conditioning, network structure, and sampling cost.
