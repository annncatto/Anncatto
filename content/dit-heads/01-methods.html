扩散模型已从图像生成扩展到机器人轨迹与动作生成。Diffuser以去噪合成状态—动作轨迹，并在采样时加入目标或奖励约束；Diffusion Policy根据当前观察联合生成一段动作，表达多种可行动作方案及其时间关联。接入 VLA 后，视觉语言主干提供物体、任务与场景表征，专门的动作头生成高维动作序列；CogACT采用这一分工，使视觉语言预训练能力与机器人动作建模结合。

Diffusion has expanded from image synthesis to robot trajectories and actions. Diffuser denoises state–action trajectories with goals or rewards guiding sampling; Diffusion Policy jointly generates action chunks conditioned on observations, representing multimodal choices and temporal relationships. In a VLA, the vision-language backbone supplies object, task, and scene features while a dedicated head generates high-dimensional actions, as in CogACT.

流匹配通过回归向量场训练生成器，常用的线性插值路径便于采用少步积分；它所支持的高斯概率路径也包括扩散路径。两者都可生成多模态动作，实际延迟还取决于网络大小、采样器和迭代次数。扩散方案保留了复用已训练噪声预测器、调整 DDIM 采样，以及进一步蒸馏和剪枝的部署路径：CogACT 的仿真评测使用 10 步 DDIM，LightDP联合压缩网络与采样过程。选择流匹配或扩散时，需要比较具体任务上的成功率与延迟，以及现有权重的复用成本；流匹配的发展扩充了方法选择，扩散策略也持续通过采样和结构优化改善效率。

Flow matching trains a generative vector field; commonly used linear interpolation paths facilitate few-step integration. Its Gaussian path family also includes diffusion paths. Both can model multimodal actions; latency depends on network size, sampler, and iteration count. Diffusion retains deployment routes through existing noise predictors, DDIM, distillation, and pruning: CogACT uses ten DDIM steps in simulation, and LightDP compresses both the network and sampling process. Task success, latency, and checkpoint-reuse cost therefore guide the choice alongside the training formulation.
