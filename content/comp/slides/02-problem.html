Why test-time epistemic UQ is hard for flow VLAs流匹配 VLA 的测试期认知不确定性痛点

Pre-trained flow policies ($\pi_{0.5}$, GR00T, SmolVLA) integrate an ODE to output action chunks without signaling OOD failure, while training deep ensembles does not scale.预训练流匹配策略（$\pi_{0.5}$、GR00T、SmolVLA）经 ODE 积分输出动作块，却无法提示分布外失效；训练多份独立集成代价过高。

Why post-hoc BayesDiff breaks down单模型后验方法（BayesDiff）的人为矛盾

BayesDiff propagates last-layer Laplace moments but marginalizes weights per step: finer ODE grids (larger $T$) improve trajectories yet drive reported uncertainty to zero.BayesDiff 沿采样链传播末层 Laplace 矩，但每步独立边缘化权重：步数 $T$ 越细、ODE 解越准，不确定性反而趋近于零。

“This creates an artificial tradeoff: finer integration better approximates the ODE solution, yet drives the reported uncertainty to zero.”“这制造了人为矛盾：更细的积分网格能更精确逼近 ODE 解，却把报告的不确定性压向零。”

1Prove $O(1/T)$ collapse证明 $O(1/T)$ 方差坍缩

BayesDiff obeys $\operatorname{tr} P_T \le e^{2L}\bar\sigma_{\max}^2/T$ even with full $P_i$ and on diffusion SDEs (Thm. 1, Cor. 1–2).证明总方差受 $e^{2L}\bar\sigma_{\max}^2/T$ 约束，满协方差与扩散 SDE 亦无法避免（定理 1、推论 1–2）。

2CoMP-EKF & CoMP-UKF提出 CoMP-EKF 与 UKF

Carry cross-covariance $C_i=\operatorname{Cov}(\delta x_i,\delta\theta)$ for an $O(1)$ bound (Thm. 2) via autodiff or sigma points.显式维护互协方差 $C_i=\operatorname{Cov}(\delta x_i,\delta\theta)$ 得到 $O(1)$ 上界（定理 2），给出两种实现。

3Augmented head $c(o)$零重训增强末层后验

Append VLM embeddings $c(o)$ with zero weights—preventing feature collapse without altering MAP actions.零权重拼接已有嵌入 $c(o)$，不重训、不改动作即缓解特征坍缩，并同步提升各类基线。
