Variogram discrepancy (target = 1)变差函数误差（衡量跨步相关）

Measures pairwise moment error across action-chunk coordinates. BayesDiff is 13–16× worse than reference (misreporting cross-step correlation along with scale); CoMP stays within 10%.度量动作块坐标间两两相关性误差。BayesDiff 达参考值的 13–16 倍（尺度与跨步相关性同时失真）；CoMP 保持在参考值 10% 以内。

Expert-action NLL (lower is better)专家动作负对数似然（越低越好）

Collapsed Gaussians assign near-zero density to real expert chunks (BayesDiff NLL $>10^9$). CoMP achieves well-calibrated NLLs—and switching to the augmented head lowers CoMP NLL by a further 8%–10%.坍缩后的高斯分布对真实专家动作几乎赋予零密度（BayesDiff 达 $>10^9$）。CoMP 似然大幅下降，换用增强头后还可再降 8%–10%。
