3 · Next step for real-time & WAMs3 · 面向实时与 WAM 的演进

Low-rank covariance propagation (avoiding $2D+1$ sigma points for large chunks, $H\ge 32$) is the missing piece for 20–30 Hz closed-loop failure monitors.对 $H\ge 32$ 的大动作块与世界—动作模型，低秩协方差传播是打通 20–30 Hz 实时闭环监控的关键下一步。

Take-home message: Quantifying epistemic uncertainty in pre-trained flow policies requires coherent propagation across ODE steps ($C_i$) and uncollapsed observation features ($c(o)$)—fixing both turns a single frozen VLA into a calibrated failure detector and data curator.总结：让预训练流匹配策略给出可信的认知不确定性，必须同时解决“跨步传播不相干（补回 $C_i$）”与“末层特征坍缩（拼接 $c(o)$）”两道根因；二者齐备后，单个冻结 VLA 即可胜任故障检测与数据筛选。
