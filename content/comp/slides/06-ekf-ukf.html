$$y_j = h_i(\chi_j),\quad (P_i+\varepsilon_i I)A_i^\top = \Sigma_i^{xy},\quad P_{i+1} = \Sigma_i^{yy} + \text{cross} + B_i\Sigma_\theta B_i^\top$$
$$y_j = h_i(\chi_j),\quad (P_i+\varepsilon_i I)A_i^\top = \Sigma_i^{xy},\quad P_{i+1} = \Sigma_i^{yy} + \text{交叉项} + B_i\Sigma_\theta B_i^\top$$
  • Places $2D+1$ deterministic sigma points $\chi_j$ around $(m_i, P_i)$ and evaluates them in one forward batch through the nonlinear map $h_i$.围绕 $(m_i, P_i)$ 构造 $2D+1$ 个确定性 sigma 点 $\chi_j$，单批前向穿过非线性欧拉映射 $h_i$，保留二阶曲率 $\Sigma_i^{yy}$。
  • Key gain: No backward passes; analytic $B_i$ at $m_i$ means no sigma points are needed for weights $\theta$.优势：无需反向传播；权重项 $B_i$ 仍在 $m_i$ 处解析计算，因此无需对权重 $\theta$ 采样 sigma 点。
  • Cost & score: 1.85 s on $\pi_{0.5}$, 3.38 s on SmolVLA (3.2× faster than EKF); leads LIBERO-90 (TWA 0.643).实测代价：$\pi_{0.5}$ 仅 1.85 s，SmolVLA 3.38 s（快于 EKF 3.2 倍）；LIBERO-90 全面第一（TWA 0.643）。

Scalar uncertainty scores (App. A.5, Eq. 28)标量不确定性分数定义（附录 A.5, Eq. 28）

$$U_{\mathrm{chunk}} = \operatorname{tr} P_T,\qquad U_{\mathrm{endpoint}} = \operatorname{tr}\operatorname{Cov}\!\left(\sum_{k=1}^H a_k\right) = \operatorname{tr}\!\left(\sum_{k,k'=1}^H [P_T]_{kk'}\right)$$

Why $U_{\mathrm{endpoint}}$ matters in control为何操作任务使用末端协方差 $U_{\mathrm{endpoint}}$

$U_{\mathrm{chunk}}$ sums only diagonal per-step variances (used in Q1 to match BayesDiff). $U_{\mathrm{endpoint}}$ incorporates off-diagonal cross-step blocks $[P_T]_{kk'}$, capturing accumulated displacement uncertainty across the $H$-step chunk (used in Q2–Q4).$U_{\mathrm{chunk}}$ 只累加对角单步方差（Q1 与 BayesDiff 直接对比）；$U_{\mathrm{endpoint}}$ 计入非对角跨步协方差块 $[P_T]_{kk'}$，度量整个 $H$ 步动作块累积末端位移的不确定性（用于 Q2–Q4）。
