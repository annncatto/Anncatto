Last-layer Laplace posterior (§2, Eq. 4)末层 Laplace 后验（§2, Eq. 4）

$$p(\theta\mid\mathcal{D}) \approx \mathcal{N}(\theta_{\mathrm{MAP}}, \Sigma_\theta),\qquad \Sigma_\theta = \big(\nabla^2_\theta \mathcal{L}(\mathcal{D};\theta)\big|_{\theta_{\mathrm{MAP}}}\big)^{-1}$$

Freeze the backbone at $\theta_{\mathrm{MAP}}$ and fit KFAC curvature only on the linear output head over $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{fit}}$. A single posterior draw shifts weights by $\delta\theta \triangleq \theta - \theta_{\mathrm{MAP}} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \Sigma_\theta)$ and is held fixed across all $T$ steps.冻结主干权重，仅对线性输出头在拟合集 $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{fit}}$ 上估计 KFAC 曲率；单个后验样本的权重偏离 $\delta\theta \triangleq \theta - \theta_{\mathrm{MAP}} \sim \mathcal{N}(0,\Sigma_\theta)$ 在整个 $T$ 步积分中保持同一组权重不变。

BayesDiff moment recursion (§2, Eq. 6–7)BayesDiff 矩递推（§2, Eq. 6–7）

$$P_{i+1} = P_i + 2\Delta t\,\operatorname{diag}\operatorname{Cov}(x_i, u_i) + \Delta t^2\operatorname{diag}\operatorname{Cov}(u_i)$$

Starts from $(m_0, P_0) = (x_0, 0)$ and uses $S$ Monte Carlo particles $x_i^{(s)} \sim \mathcal{N}(m_i, P_i)$ under $p(u\mid x_i, t_i, \mathcal{D})p(x_i)$. Because weights are marginalized out before each step, the recursion forgets that $x_i$ was generated by the same $\theta$.从 $(m_0, P_0)=(x_0, 0)$ 出发，每步在积分布 $p(u\mid x_i,t_i,\mathcal{D})p(x_i)$ 下采样 $S$ 个粒子估计交叉项；由于每步先将权重边缘化，递推丢失了 $x_i$ 本身由同一 $\theta$ 生成的关联。
