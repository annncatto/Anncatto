Why scale-invariant thresholds cannot save BayesDiff为何“尺度不变”阈值也救不了 BayesDiff

One might ask: if collapse merely divides every score by $T$, wouldn’t quantile thresholds or top-$k$ selection stay unaffected? Figure 7 refutes this: BayesDiff ranks at most 40% correctly (falling to 23% at $T=400$) and reorders observations at every $T$; full-$\Sigma$ BayesDiff reaches at most 53% with the same crossing trajectories.若坍缩只是给所有样本同比例除以 $T$，保形分位阈值或 top-$k$ 选数本应不受影响。但图 7 证明：BayesDiff 排序正确率最高仅 40%（$T=400$ 跌至 23%），且随 $T$ 剧烈交叉换位；满协方差版本也仅 ≤53%。

CoMP keeps rankings accurate and invariant to $T$CoMP 在所有步数下保持高准确率且排序锁定

Both CoMP-EKF (77% / 87%–90%) and CoMP-UKF (77% / 93% identically at every $T$) cleanly separate high- and low-uncertainty observations with horizontal tracks—explaining why CoMP retains downstream gains across $T$ while BayesDiff degrades in Q2 and Q4.CoMP-EKF（77% / 87%–90%）与 CoMP-UKF（全部步数稳居 77% / 93%）将高低不确定样本彻底分开、连线几乎水平，直接解释了后文 Q2 与 Q4 中步数增加后 BayesDiff 失效而 CoMP 稳定的现象。
