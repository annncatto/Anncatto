Controlled wording protocol (same image & state)受控指令变体（固定首帧图像与本体状态）

Fit both Laplace heads once on ≤10 successful specific rollouts + 50k DROID frames. At test time, vary only text—e.g., “Grasp the yellow banana and place it inside the red bowl on the table” → “Pick up the banana and place it in the bowl” → “Put the fruit in the bowl” (Table 3).两种后验均仅在 ≤10 条成功具体回合 + 50k DROID 帧上拟合一次；测试首帧只改指令措辞——例如从“抓起黄色香蕉放进桌上的红碗”逐步模糊到“把水果放进碗里”（表 3）。

Augmented head unlocks semantic sensitivity; CoMP preserves it增强头恢复语义敏感度，CoMP 保证跨步数不衰退

On the plain head, all estimators remain flat while success drops sharply. On the augmented head, every estimator except DECU (saturated at $\ln M$) climbs monotonically with ambiguity—and unlike BayesDiff, CoMP retains full responsiveness up to $T=50$.朴素头上所有估计器几乎走平；增强头上除 DECU（触及互信息代理上限 $\ln M$ 饱和）外，所有估计器随语言模糊度单调攀升——且仅 CoMP 在 $T=50$ 下不衰退。
