Why plain last-layer Laplace misses observation shifts为何朴素末层 Laplace 会出现特征坍缩

  • Feature collapse: A last layer trained only to regress velocities $\phi(x,t)$ discards visual/language nuances not needed in-distribution, making OOD inputs look falsely familiar.特征坍缩：末层特征 $\phi(x,t)$ 仅受速度回归监督，会过度压缩训练分布内用不到的视觉与语言差异，导致分布外输入看似“熟悉”。
  • Advantage over LULA: LULA trains extra units on an OOD dataset unavailable to pre-trained policies; setting $\zeta = c(o)$ reuses embeddings the VLA already computes, and for a linear head the Gram matrix depends only on $\psi$—giving the exact curvature covariance at $\bar\theta_{\mathrm{MAP}}$.相对 LULA 的改进：LULA 依赖额外 OOD 数据训练新单元；令 $\zeta=c(o)$ 直接复用 VLA 已算好的视觉—语言特征，且线性头 Gram 矩阵仅依赖 $\psi$，在 $\bar\theta_{\mathrm{MAP}}$ 处曲率协方差与后验众数完全一致。
