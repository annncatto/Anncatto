1Linear last-layer assumption依赖线性末层假设

Analytic weight Jacobians $J_i^\theta$ and fast KFAC curvature rely on a linear final layer; extending Laplace posteriors into deeper transformer layers remains expensive.解析权重雅可比 $J_i^\theta$ 与高效 KFAC 曲率依赖线性输出层；向更深层 Transformer 扩展后验仍代价高昂。

2$O(D^2)$ covariance storage$O(D^2)$ 状态协方差存储

Storing and factorizing $P_i \in \mathbb{R}^{D\times D}$ scales quadratically with the flattened chunk dimension $D = H d_a$, motivating future low-rank propagation.存储与分解 $P_i\in\mathbb{R}^{D\times D}$ 随展平动作块维度 $D=Hd_a$ 呈平方增长，指向未来低秩协方差传播方向。

3Cost grows linearly with $D$推理开销随 $D$ 线性增长

UKF batches $2D+1$ sigma points per Euler step (on $\pi_{0.5}$ $D=70$: 1.85 s ≈ VFD 1.80 s; on SmolVLA $D=350$: 3.38 s = 3× VFD). EKF’s $D$ backward passes hit 10.98 s on SmolVLA.UKF 每步前向 $2D+1$ 个 sigma 点（$\pi_{0.5}$ 1.85 s ≈ VFD 1.80 s；SmolVLA 5 倍维度升至 3.38 s），EKF 反向微分达 10.98 s。

Real-time UQ remains open across all methods: Even the fastest post-hoc baseline (DEUA) takes $\ge 0.50\text{ s}$/decision on A100. Today, CoMP is directly practical for offline demonstration curation and safety-critical monitoring where missed failures outweigh latency.实时不确定性估计仍是全行业未解问题：即便最快的基线 DEUA 在 A100 上也需 $\ge 0.50\text{ s}$/次决策。CoMP 当前最适合离线微调数据筛选与安全关键监控，未来经低秩化可扩展至 WAM 与长时序装配。
