Scalar trace recursion (Eq. 8) & Theorem 1 (Eq. 9)迹递推恒等式（Eq. 8）与方差坍缩定理（Eq. 9）

$$\operatorname{tr} P_{i+1} = \underbrace{\operatorname{tr}\operatorname{Cov}_x\![h_i(x_i)]}_{\le\,(1+\Delta t\,L)^2\operatorname{tr} P_i} + \underbrace{\Delta t^2\,\bar\sigma_i^2}_{\text{epistemic injection}}$$
$$\operatorname{tr} P_{i+1} = \underbrace{\operatorname{tr}\operatorname{Cov}_x\![h_i(x_i)]}_{\le\,(1+\Delta t\,L)^2\operatorname{tr} P_i} + \underbrace{\Delta t^2\,\bar\sigma_i^2}_{\text{单步认知方差注入}}$$
$$\operatorname{tr} P_T \;\le\; \frac{\bar\sigma_{\max}^2}{T}\!\left(1+\frac{L}{T}\right)^{2T} \;\le\; e^{2L}\frac{\bar\sigma_{\max}^2}{T} \;=\; O\!\left(\frac{1}{T}\right)$$
  • Two standard conditions: (A1) $L$-Lipschitz MAP velocity $u_{\theta_{\mathrm{MAP}}}$; (A2) bounded summed predictive variance $\sum_d\gamma_d^2(x,t) \le \bar\sigma_{\max}^2$.两项常规假设：(A1) MAP 速度场对状态 $L$-Lipschitz；(A2) 预测方差和一致有界 $\sum_d \gamma_d^2(x,t) \le \bar\sigma_{\max}^2$。
  • Why it vanishes: $T$ steps inject $T$ variances of size $\Delta t^2 = 1/T^2$; even after maximum $(1+\Delta t L)^2$ stretching, $T \cdot (1/T^2) = 1/T \to 0$.坍缩根源：$T$ 步共注入 $T$ 次 $\Delta t^2 = 1/T^2$ 量级方差；即便每步按最大系数 $(1+\Delta t L)^2$ 放大，总和仍按 $T\cdot(1/T^2)=1/T \to 0$ 消失。
  • Corollaries 1 & 2 (App. A.1): Carrying full state covariance $P_i$ does not fix the decay; on diffusion SDEs, the epistemic term still decays as $O(1/T)$ while Brownian noise stays $O(1)$.推论 1 与 2（附录 A.1）：保留满状态协方差 $P_i$ 无法消除衰减；扩散 SDE 中认知项仍为 $O(1/T)$，仅布朗噪声偶然项维持 $O(1)$。
