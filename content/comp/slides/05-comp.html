$$C_{i+1} = A_i C_i + B_i \Sigma_\theta$$
$$P_{i+1} = \underbrace{A_i P_i A_i^\top}_{\text{state spread}} + \underbrace{A_i C_i B_i^\top + B_i C_i^\top A_i^\top}_{\text{state–weight cross terms}} + \underbrace{B_i \Sigma_\theta B_i^\top}_{\text{step injection}}$$
$$P_{i+1} = \underbrace{A_i P_i A_i^\top}_{\text{状态扩散}} + \underbrace{A_i C_i B_i^\top + B_i C_i^\top A_i^\top}_{\text{状态—权重交叉项}} + \underbrace{B_i \Sigma_\theta B_i^\top}_{\text{单步注入}}$$
Theorem 2 (Step-count invariance): Carrying $C_i$ adds the $T$ contributions of size $\Delta t$ coherently as amplitudes before squaring ($(T\Delta t)^2 = 1$), whereas setting $C_i \equiv 0$ adds their squares as independent variances ($T\Delta t^2 = 1/T$). For a linear last layer ($\pi_{0.5}$, GR00T, SmolVLA), $J_i^\theta$ and $B_i$ are analytic.定理 2（与步数无关的常数上界）：携带 $C_i$ 使 $T$ 步 $\Delta t$ 量级的权重扰动先按振幅相干叠加再平方（$(T\Delta t)^2=1$）；丢弃 $C_i$ 则是先平方再按独立方差累加（$T\Delta t^2=1/T$）。对线性输出头（$\pi_{0.5}$、GR00T、SmolVLA），$J_i^\theta$ 与 $B_i$ 均可解析计算。
