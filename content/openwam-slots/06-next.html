视频—动作耦合Video–action coupling

OpenWAM 的视频与动作分支各经过 30 个配对 Transformer 层，每层分别产生 Q、K、V，再拼接进行联合注意力。Q 决定当前 token 读取哪些位置，K 用于计算匹配程度，V 携带被汇入输出的特征；“视频到动作”指视频 K/V 被动作 Q 读取的信息流。

OpenWAM runs video and action streams through 30 paired Transformer layers. Each produces Q, K and V before joint attention: Q queries positions, K determines their matching scores, and V carries the features aggregated into the output. Video-to-action denotes the information carried by video K/V into action Q.

图中的视频、动作残差宽度分别是 3072、1024。各自的 Q/K/V 投影把它们映射到相同的 24 头 × 128 维注意力空间，沿 token 轴拼接；计算后再分回两条分支。

Video and action residual widths are 3072 and 1024, respectively. Their Q/K/V projections map them into a shared 24-head × 128-coordinate attention space for concatenation along the token axis, after which outputs are split back into the two streams.

图中的 392 个位置属于视频—动作联合注意力序列。文本和状态通过另一组条件注意力进入两个分支，所以保留为单独的上下文输入。

The 392 positions belong to joint video–action attention. Text and state enter both streams through separate conditional attention and are therefore shown as a distinct context input.

注意力采用 $P=\operatorname{softmax}(QK^\top/\sqrt{d_h}+M_G)$，输出为 $PV$，其中 $d_h$ 是每个注意力头的维度。选定层中，$M_G[Q_a,K_v]=-\infty$，使对应概率归零，其余允许位置的概率重新归一化。

35.05% 的计算对象What the 35.05% measures

这里的动作速度场是网络指导动作逐步去噪的更新量。单步试验将未来示范视频、示范动作与固定噪声各混合 50%，在时间 500 比较一次前向；当前观测视频前缀保持固定。

The action velocity field is the network output that guides successive denoising updates. The one-step probe mixes demonstrated future video and actions equally with fixed noise, compares one forward pass at time 500, and keeps the observed-video prefix clamped.

对窗口 $i$，记正常输出为 $v_i^{(0)}$，屏蔽层组 $G$ 后的输出为 $v_i^{(G)}$，二者均取 32 步、双臂含夹爪的前 14 个归一化动作通道。每个窗口先计算输出差的 Frobenius 范数与基线范数之比，再平均四个窗口：

For window $i$, let $v_i^{(0)}$ be normal output and $v_i^{(G)}$ the output after masking group $G$, both restricted to 32 steps and the first 14 normalized arm/gripper channels. Compute the Frobenius norm of the difference relative to the baseline norm within each window, then average four windows:

$$\Delta_G=\frac{1}{4}\sum_{i=1}^{4}\frac{\lVert v_i^{(G)}-v_i^{(0)}\rVert_F}{\lVert v_i^{(0)}\rVert_F}\times100\%.$$

A 的 35.05% 表示：在这四个状态和这个去噪时间，切断 25–29 层的视频到动作连接后，最终速度场的改变量平均相当于基线向量范数的 35.05%。完整 10 步去噪后的关节变化则另用 Host 坐标单位统计，两种度量分别描述更新量与最终动作。

A's 35.05% means that, at these four states and this denoising time, cutting the video-to-action connections at layers 25–29 changes final velocity by an average 35.05% of the baseline norm. Joint changes after complete 10-step denoising are measured separately in Host units; the metrics describe the update field and the resulting actions, respectively.

生成中的未来视频参与动作形成Generated future video contributes to actions

完整 10 步生成只输入当前观测，其余未来视频与动作共同去噪。只屏蔽未来视频到动作的连接后，A/B 的动作变化为 16.92/18.46；屏蔽全部视频到动作则为 17.08/18.18，说明模型生成中的未来视频通路实际影响动作形成。

Full 10-step generation receives only current observations; future video and actions are denoised jointly. Masking only future-video-to-action connections changes A/B actions by 16.92/18.46, compared with 17.08/18.18 for masking all video-to-action connections, establishing a functional role for generated future-video pathways.

单步基线中，用 Q/K 在 FP32 下重建的联合注意力，平均分给当前视频约 35%、未来视频约 42%、动作约 23%，对应 120、240、32 个 token。注意力分配描述读取位置，屏蔽试验量测切断读取后的局部功能作用；softmax 的重新分配与后续非线性计算共同决定干预结果。

FP32 reconstruction from baseline Q/K gives approximately 35% joint attention to observed video, 42% to future video and 23% to actions, across 120, 240 and 32 tokens. Attention mass describes reading locations, while masking measures the local effect of cutting those reads; renormalization and downstream nonlinear computation jointly determine the intervention outcome.
