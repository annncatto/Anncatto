1. Trajectory fit and fine-tuning stagnation1. 轨迹拟合与微调参数停滞

The live gap between Restarted 10k and Fine-tuned 10k raises four concrete diagnostic checks: release-window trajectory fit, BF16 weight movement, lift-axis scaling, and text/color/video pathways. Both 10k routes share the identical 149-train / 6-val split (40 added episodes = 22,338 frames, 28.2% of training) and identical normalization stats: Fine-tuned 10k resumes the 115-episode model at lr 1×10⁻⁵ (cumulative 20k), while Restarted 10k starts from official OpenWAM-α 154k with reinitialized 18D projections at lr 5×10⁻⁵ [17].

真机复测中重训 10k 能无介入纠正跨侧绿块、微调 10k 仍反复错篮，接下来分三部分定位原因：松爪拟合与权重停滞、升降归一化与文本/颜色探针，以及 30 层视频—动作耦合。两条 10k 路线共用完全相同的 149 训练 / 6 验证划分（新增 40 条共 22,338 帧，占训练帧 28.2%）和同一份归一化统计：微调 10k 承接原 115ep 模型（lr 1×10⁻⁵，累计 20k），重训 10k 从官方 OpenWAM-α 154k 重建 18 维投影（lr 5×10⁻⁵）[17]。

Release-window trajectory fit: why gains concentrate in ep. 115 & 154松爪窗口拟合：优势为何集中在 ep. 115 与 ep. 154

Because all 155 episodes share one fixed prompt (encoded by frozen umT5), basket choice is taught solely through demonstrated transport and release trajectories. To test absorption at the decision moment, we automatically select four 32-step windows at the largest upcoming right-gripper opening—one old validation (ep. 3 f211) and three added corrective episodes (ep. 115 f32, ep. 135 f66, ep. 154 f155)—and measure 12-arm trajectory MAE under identical observations and two fixed noise seeds (17 & 29).

155 回合共用同一句指令（冻结 umT5 编码），选篮行为完全由搬运和松爪轨迹监督。为检验模型在“选篮松爪”时刻是否真正学进纠偏轨迹，我们按未来 20–31 步相对 0–7 步的右夹爪最大张开量自动截取 4 个 32 步诊断窗口——1 条旧验证（ep. 3）与 3 条新增示范（ep. 115 / 135 / 154），在相同图像、状态与双固定种子（17、29）下比较 12 关节预测 MAE（越低越贴近示范）。

微调前 100 步动作 loss 低 48 倍（0.029 对 1.38）；至 10k 时，6 条旧验证的 flow loss（0.019 对 0.017）和 12 关节 MAE（4.14 对 4.26）均接近，旧窗 ep. 3 三者持平（5.9 / 6.1 / 6.1）。

State conditioning projects normalized states via $h_s = W_s N(s) + b_s$, with relative L2 shift $\Delta_H = \|H_{\mathrm{new}}-H_{\mathrm{old}}\|_F / \|H_{\mathrm{old}}\|_F$ across 320 added states. Because the lift axis stays virtually stationary during tabletop sorting, applying tight dataset-driven min/max to [−1, +1] turns 0.2 mm of homing jitter into a massive coordinate jump. Why Fine-tuned 10k suffers harder为何微调 10k 受冲击更大

Restoring old lift stats shifts final-layer action residuals by 31.4% / 25.4% and 12-arm actions by 5.0 / 3.0. Fine-tuned 10k takes the larger hit because 99.3% of its state_proj weights never re-adapted to the shifted coordinates.

仅恢复旧升降统计就使末层动作残差偏离 31.4% / 25.4%、12 关节偏移 5.0 / 3.0。微调 10k 的 state_proj 99.3% 保持旧值却收到偏移坐标，因此受冲击更重。

Engineering rule: Never apply raw dataset min/max to fixed or near-constant channels (lift height, stationary base). Enforce physical hardware range floors (e.g. 0–600 mm lift stroke) and keep existing normalization stats frozen when fine-tuning a checkpoint.

工程结论：对固定高度或静止通道绝不能使用数据驱动的紧 min/max，应强制采用硬件物理行程下限（如 0–600 mm 行程），并在承接旧权重微调时冻结原归一化统计而非重算。

3. Where actions read video across 30 paired layers3. 动作分支在 30 层中何时读取视频

OpenWAM projects video (360 tokens × 3072) and action (32 tokens × 1024) streams into a shared 24-head × 128-dim joint attention sequence of 392 positions, while text and state enter via separate cross-attention. With weights fixed, we block action queries from reading video keys/values (the 32×360 sub-block) in just one five-layer group at a time, run all 30 layers, and measure how much the final predicted 32×14 action-velocity field deviates from the normal baseline [18].

OpenWAM 将视频（360×3072）与动作（32×1024）投影到 24 头 × 128 维、共 392 位置的联合注意力中，文本与状态走独立交叉注意力。保持权重固定，我们每次只在连续五层中切断“动作 Q 读取视频 K/V”的 32×360 子块，跑完完整 30 层后量测末端 32×14 动作去噪速度场相对正常基线的偏离幅度 [18]。

Generated future video directly drives execution生成中的未来视频直接决定轨迹与松爪

In full 10-step rollouts given only current observations, cutting only generated future video → action shifts 12-arm actions by 16.9 / 18.5 and right gripper by 39.4 / 35.7—virtually identical to cutting all video (17.1 / 18.2). Baseline FP32 joint attention places ~42% mass on future video (240 tok), ~35% on observed frames (120 tok), and ~23% on actions (32 tok).

仅输入当前观测的完整 10 步去噪中，只切断生成中的未来视频 → 动作，12 关节就偏离 16.9 / 18.5、右夹爪偏离 39.4 / 35.7，几乎等同切断全部视频（17.1 / 18.2）。单步基线联合注意力约 42% 落在未来视频（240 tok）、35% 落在当前观测（120 tok）、23% 落在动作（32 tok）。

Architectural takeaway: OpenWAM does not simply read observations once at the input; its action stream fuses visual context at mid-depth (10–19) and pre-readout (25–29), actively tracking the model’s own imagined future frames. Any training or inference shortcut that drops future-video generation directly degrades arm trajectories and gripper timing.

结构结论：OpenWAM 并非只在浅层看一次输入画面，而是会在中段（10–19 层）与读出前（25–29 层）两度集中融合视频，并直接依据模型自己想象出的未来视频帧来生成机械臂轨迹与松爪时机。
