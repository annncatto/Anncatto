补充数据 · 任务纠偏Additional demonstrations · correcting task behavior

前面的误投记录已经完成抓取、抬起、搬运和释放，错误发生在绿色积木的目标篮子选择。新增示范要补足的正是这一任务目的：物体出现在与原示教差异较大的条件下，仍按“蓝色进左篮、绿色进右篮”执行。

The preceding failed rollout completes grasping, lifting, transport, and release; the error is the destination chosen for a green block. The additional demonstrations target this task requirement: under conditions that differ substantially from the original demonstrations, blue objects should still go left and green objects right.

因此，本轮比较关注的是补充数据能否改写目标选择，同时保留已经学到的抓放能力。训练收敛速度、轨迹拟合和目标篮子正确率分别描述这三个过程中的不同结果。

The comparison asks whether additional data can change destination selection while retaining learned manipulation skills. Convergence speed, trajectory fit, and correct-basket rate describe different outcomes in that process.

纠偏监督Corrective supervision

任务指令经冻结的 umT5 编码，状态经线性投影变为一个条件 token；视频和动作 DiT 读取这组条件，并通过联合注意力交换信息。两条训练路线都更新两个 DiT，因此纠偏可以发生在条件读取、视觉—动作关联和动作生成三个环节。

Frozen umT5 encodes the instruction, and a linear projection converts state into one context token. Both routes update the video and action DiTs, allowing correction through condition reading, visual–action associations, and action generation.

  • 监督内容：155 回合共用一句固定分拣指令，正确篮子体现在示范的搬运轨迹和释放位置中。新增数据主要教同一规则在新条件下的执行。

    Supervision: all 155 episodes share one sorting instruction; demonstrated transport and release encode the intended basket. Added data primarily teaches execution of that rule under new conditions.

  • 训练分布：40 条新增示范占训练帧的 28.24%，其余为原数据。A 还继承了原 115 回合的 10k 步任务历史，B 则直接从最终混合分布建立任务适配。[17]

    Training distribution: the 40 added episodes supply 28.24% of training frames. A also inherits 10k updates on the original 115 episodes, whereas B learns task adaptation directly from the final mixture. [17]

状态尺度如何进入动作How state scaling reaches actions

状态条件为 $h_s=W_sN(s)+b_s$。保持真实状态与旧投影权重不变，每回合均匀取 8 帧，只替换归一化统计：在 320 个新增训练状态上，整个状态 token 的相对 L2 变化为 51.33%，单独替换升降统计就达到 49.47%。

State conditioning is $h_s=W_sN(s)+b_s$. Holding real states and original projection weights fixed, eight states per episode are sampled while replacing only normalization statistics: across 320 added-training states, the full state token changes by 51.33% in relative L2; replacing lift statistics alone gives 49.47%.

视频与动作在哪些层交换信息Where video and action exchange information

另在同样四个窗口进行单步网络探测：将真实未来视频和动作与固定噪声各混合 50%，在去噪时间 500 比较动作速度场。逐层记录干预引起的残差变化，并每次只屏蔽连续五层的视频到动作注意力；层号从 0 开始。

A separate one-step probe mixes demonstrated future video and actions equally with fixed noise and compares action velocity at denoising time 500. It records residual changes by layer and masks video-to-action attention in one five-layer group at a time; layer indices start at zero.

  • 视频到动作的桥接作用集中在中段与末段：A 的 10–19 层分组干预使速度场变化约 25%–27%，25–29 层达到 35.05%；5–9 层仅为 3.61%。B 对应约 28%–30%、32.50% 和 3.90%，旧模型也呈类似分布，显示不同深度的信息交换承担不同程度的局部作用。

    Video-to-action bridging is most consequential in middle and final groups: A changes by about 25%–27% when groups within layers 10–19 are masked, 35.05% at 25–29, and only 3.61% at 5–9. B gives about 28%–30%, 32.50%, and 3.90%; the original model has a similar pattern, showing unequal local effects across depth.

  • 生成中的未来视频参与了动作形成：完整 10 步生成只输入当前观测；屏蔽未来视频到动作后，A/B 的输出变化为 16.92/18.46，与屏蔽全部视频通路的量级相近。动作分支因此依赖联合去噪中形成的视频信息，未来视频通路是纠偏需要触及的内部环节。

    Generated future video participates in action formation: Full 10-step generation receives only current observations; masking future-video-to-action attention changes A/B outputs by 16.92/18.46, comparable in scale to masking all video-to-action attention. Actions therefore depend on video information formed during joint denoising, making this pathway relevant to correction.

  • 颜色扰动经多层交换进入动作表征：A 的动作残差差异从第 0 层 0.19% 增至末层 2.33%，B 从 0.24% 增至 2.42%。这把“换颜色后动作变了”进一步定位到两条分支逐层交换信息的过程。

    Color perturbations propagate into action representations across layers: A's action-residual difference rises from 0.19% at layer 0 to 2.33% at the final layer; B rises from 0.24% to 2.42%. This locates the color-induced action change within the inter-branch information exchange.

单步基线中，动作查询平均把约 35% 的联合注意力分给当前视频、42% 分给未来视频、23% 分给动作，其中 token 数分别为 120、240、32。注意力分配描述读取位置，屏蔽实验量测该通路对输出的局部作用；多个通路相互耦合，各组效应也随后续计算变化。

In the one-step baseline, action queries allocate about 35% of joint attention to observed video, 42% to future video, and 23% to actions, across 120, 240, and 32 tokens. Attention mass describes where the model reads, while masking measures local output dependence; coupled pathways and downstream computation shape each intervention effect.
