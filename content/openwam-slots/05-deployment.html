反馈与指令Feedback and commands

Using the same 517-frame fixed-base rollout (all three base velocity targets fixed at 0; lift target 599.6 mm), Host reconstructs base x/y velocity and yaw rate directly from the three wheel-speed registers (kinematic replay error < 2×10⁻¹⁰ m/s).

沿用上节同一份 517 帧固定底盘记录（三维速度目标固定为零，升降目标 599.6 mm），Host 通过三轮速度寄存器经底盘运动学换算出 x、y 线速度与偏航角速度（重算误差 < 2×10⁻¹⁰ m/s）。

  • Green feedback spikes come from ±50 tick/s discrete wheel quantization, not base drift绿线尖峰源于 ±50 tick/s 离散轮速量化跳变，并非底盘持续滑动

    Out of 1,551 wheel-speed readings (517 frames × 3 wheels), 1,350 are zero and 194 of the 201 nonzero readings are exactly ±50 ticks/s (4.83 mm/s rim speed); base kinematics projects a single-wheel tick simultaneously onto x, y, and yaw.

    517 帧共 1,551 个轮速读数中 1,350 个为零，201 个非零读数中有 194 个（96.5%）恰好为 ±50 tick/s（轮缘 4.83 mm/s），单轮跳变经运动学合成后同时出现在三路速度曲线上。

  • Wheel positions stay within ±1 tick (> 99%) with identical start and end counts三轮位置 99% 以上停留在初始值 ±1 tick，首尾读数一致

    Every wheel keeps over 99% of position samples within one tick of its start value and finishes on the exact starting tick, ruling out sustained wheel rotation under zero velocity commands (separating sub-tick micro-motion from register jitter requires an external displacement sensor).

    三只轮子各有超过 99% 的位置读数落在初始值 ±1 tick 内且回合首尾完全相同，零速指令下未见持续轮转（极微物理蠕动与寄存器抖动需独立外部位移测量区分）。
