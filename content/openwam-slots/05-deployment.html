Host 读取三只轮子的速度寄存器，再通过底盘运动学换算出 x、y 线速度与偏航角速度。用保存的三轮读数重算，x、y 与报告值的差均小于 $2\times10^{-10}$ m/s，偏航角速度的差小于 $4\times10^{-8}$ deg/s，确认绿线来自轮速反馈。蓝线则是实际提交的零速度目标。

Host reads the three wheel-speed registers and converts them into x/y linear velocity and yaw rate through base kinematics. Reconstructing these values from the saved wheel readings matches the report within $2\times10^{-10}$ m/s for x/y and $4\times10^{-8}$ deg/s for yaw, confirming the source of the green curves. The blue curves are the submitted zero-velocity targets.

图中的小尖峰来自轮速反馈在零附近的离散跳变。517 帧包含 1551 个轮速读数，其中 1350 个为零；201 个非零读数中，有 194 个恰好是 ±50 tick/s，其余为 ±100 或 +150 tick/s。按 Host 使用的 4096 tick/转、轮半径 0.063 m 换算，50 tick/s 对应约 4.832 mm/s 的轮缘线速度。三轮的读数合成底盘速度后，同一个轮速跳变会同时出现在多个通道：例如仅左轮为 +50 tick/s 时，x、y、偏航分别为 +2.790 mm/s、−1.611 mm/s、+0.473 deg/s。

The small spikes originate in discrete wheel-speed feedback changes around zero. The 517 frames contain 1,551 wheel-speed readings: 1,350 are zero, and 194 of the 201 nonzero readings are exactly ±50 ticks/s. The remaining readings are ±100 or +150 ticks/s. Using Host's 4,096 ticks per revolution and 0.063 m wheel radius, 50 ticks/s corresponds to about 4.832 mm/s at the wheel rim. Base kinematics combines all three readings, so one wheel-speed change can appear in several channels: +50 ticks/s on the left wheel alone produces +2.790 mm/s in x, −1.611 mm/s in y, and +0.473 deg/s in yaw.

三只轮子各自都有超过 99% 的位置读数落在初始值 ±1 tick 内，且回合首尾位置读数完全相同。结合始终为零的速度请求，这组曲线表现为零指令下的小幅轮速反馈波动，没有显示持续的轮子转动。离散的速度读数及其运动学换算解释了绿线的尖峰形态；极小实际轮动与传感器读数波动各自的贡献，仍需独立运动测量才能区分。

For each wheel, more than 99% of position readings lie within one tick of its initial value, and the start and end readings are identical. Alongside the zero velocity requests, these traces show small wheel-speed feedback fluctuations without sustained wheel rotation. Discrete speed readings and their kinematic conversion explain the spikes in the green curves; separating very small physical wheel motion from sensor fluctuations requires an independent motion measurement.

静止标签与生成残差Stationary labels and generated residuals

示教中的底盘指令恒为零，连续生成模型仍可能输出接近零但不严格为零的数。18 维关节模型使用的处理器为避免除零，把极小范围通道的归一化跨度替换成 2。对于最小值和最大值均为零的底盘通道，其编码与解码实际退化为：

Although all demonstrated base commands are zero, a continuous generative model can output small nonzero values. The processor used by the 18-coordinate joint model replaces tiny normalization spans with 2 to avoid division by zero. For a base channel whose minimum and maximum are both zero, encoding and decoding reduce to:

$$\Delta=0\Rightarrow\Delta_{\mathrm{safe}}=2,\quad c=0,\qquad \widetilde a=(a-c)\frac{2}{\Delta_{\mathrm{safe}}}=a,\qquad \widehat a=\widehat{\widetilde a}\frac{\Delta_{\mathrm{safe}}}{2}+c=\widehat{\widetilde a}.$$

Mean absolute x/y residuals are approximately 5.7/5.6 mm/s: zero-labeled channels still generate nonzero velocity. The x predictions have both signs with a signed mean of −1.021 mm/s, without a persistent forward bias in this result. A stationary task needs an explicit zero-target execution constraint so individual sampling residuals do not directly drive the base.

桌面静止任务应在部署时将底盘速度固定为零，并在反归一化时把常量通道恢复为训练数据中的常量。80 维末端接口的解码已经按真实跨度恢复，跨度为零时输出严格恒定；两种动作表示对比时，应保持相同的底盘约束，避免把执行规则的差异算作表示方法的性能差异。

For a stationary tabletop task, deployment should fix base velocity at zero and denormalization should restore constant channels to their training-data constants. The 80-coordinate TCP decoder uses the true span, producing an exact constant when the span is zero. Both action representations should be compared under the same base constraint, so an execution-rule difference is not attributed to representation quality.
