Checkpoint selection finding: Falling training loss does not indicate better validation fit—Restarted 10k bottoms out earlier at 6k (0.016 vs 0.017), while π0.5’s old-validation loss climbs 2.4× from 1k to 21k (0.025 → 0.059). Earlier checkpoints (OpenWAM 6k and early π0.5) should enter the next live test alongside held-out corrective validation [19].

选点结论：训练 loss 持续下降并不对应更好的验证表现——重训 10k 的旧验证最低点更早出现在 6k（0.016 对 0.017），π0.5 同一旧验证在 1k 到 21k 间反升 2.4 倍（0.025 → 0.059）。后续应结合留出纠偏验证，把 OpenWAM 6k 与 π0.5 早期检查点一并纳入真机对照 [19]。
