Restarted 10k still mis-sorts in its second trial (13.7 s), and π0.5 makes early wrong drops in both double-crossed captures—confirming basket choice stays fragile across layouts.

重训 10k 另一段仍在 13.7 s 误投，π0.5 在两段双色交叉布局中也都出现早期错篮，说明“选对目标篮子”仍是主要瓶颈。

Effective control rate实测有效指令提交率

Against a 30 Hz target, command-sequence increments over Host time estimate 20.4–20.6 Hz on OpenWAM versus 27.6–27.9 Hz on π0.5 (including queued-chunk steps).

在 30 Hz 目标控制周期下，按指令序号增量除以 Host 时长估计，OpenWAM 约 20.4–20.6 Hz，π0.5 约 27.6–27.9 Hz（含队列步消费）。

Deployed checkpoints on the shared validation split实机检查点的训练与共同旧验证曲线

Checkpoint selection takeaway: Restarted 10k reaches its lowest old-validation action loss earlier at 6k (0.016 vs 0.017 at 10k), while π0.5’s training loss falls steadily through 21k even as its shared old-validation loss rises 2.4× (0.025 → 0.059). Next evaluations should pair old validation with held-out corrective layouts and test earlier π0.5 / OpenWAM 6k checkpoints directly on the robot [19].

选点结论：重训 10k 的旧验证最低点更早出现在 6k（0.016 对实机 10k 的 0.017）；π0.5 训练 loss 一路下降，同一旧验证却从 1k 到 21k 上升 2.4 倍（0.025 → 0.059）。后续选点不能只看训练 loss，应把旧验证、留出纠偏验证与 π0.5 早期 / OpenWAM 6k 检查点一并纳入真机对照 [19]。
