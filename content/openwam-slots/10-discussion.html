Action 1 · Preprocessing改进一 · 预处理

Fix near-constant channel scaling取消近常量通道紧 min/max

Replace data-driven min/max on fixed lift and stationary base channels with hardware range floors (e.g. 0–600 mm lift stroke), and freeze old stats when fine-tuning so 0.19 mm offsets never shift state tokens by 50%.

对固定升降轴和静止底盘改用硬件行程下限（如 0–600 mm），且基于旧权重微调时冻结原统计，杜绝 0.19 mm 复归微差被放大成 50% 状态跳变。

Action 2 · Optimization改进二 · 训练精度

Enable FP32 fine-tuning states续训启用 FP32 主权重/矩估计

Avoid fine-tuning pure-BF16 checkpoints at 1×10⁻⁵ with BF16 moments (sub-ULP rounding freezes >92% of weights). Use FP32 master states or higher lr, and test earlier validation minima (OpenWAM 6k; early π0.5).

避免纯 BF16 + 1×10⁻⁵ 续训（半步长舍入会冻结超 92% 参数）；续训启用 FP32 主权重/矩估计或提高学习率，并实测旧验证最低点（OpenWAM 6k 与 π0.5 早期步数）。

Action 3 · Data & prompts改进三 · 数据与指令

Balance layouts and paired rules扩充交叉布局与成对反规则

Collect crossed demos across all six blue/green × left/middle/right layouts and upweight corrective frames (currently 28.2%). For language-switchable sorting, include paired opposite-rule episodes instead of one fixed prompt.

补采“蓝/绿 × 左/中/右”六类交叉示教并提高纠偏采样权重（当前新增帧仅占 28.2%）；如需按文字切换左右规则，必须加入成对反规则示教而非单一固定模板。
