Implications and boundaries实验启发与证据边界

  1. Use hardware travel range on fixed-height channels and keep old stats when fine-tuning固定高度等几乎不动的通道改按硬件量程归一化，续训时沿用旧统计

    Replace data min/max on fixed lift and stationary base channels with hardware travel ranges (0–600 mm for lift), and reuse existing stats instead of recomputing them when fine-tuning so 0.19 mm offsets never shift state tokens by 50%.

    固定高度升降轴与静止底盘不按数据极值（min/max）缩放，改按硬件真实量程（升降 0–600 mm）归一化；基于旧模型续训时直接沿用旧检查点统计量，避免 0.19 mm 复归抖动引发 50% 状态跳变。

  2. Enable FP32 master weights/moments for both fine-tuning and restart, and verify early checkpoints续训与重训均启用 FP32 主权重/矩估计，并实测早期检查点

    Pure-BF16 training with BF16 moments stalls at 1×10⁻⁵ (93% locked) and still leaves ~62% of larger weights unchanged even at 5×10⁻⁵. Enable FP32 master weights and AdamW moments across runs, and evaluate earlier validation minima (OpenWAM 6k and early π0.5).

    纯 BF16 权重与动量在 1×10⁻⁵ 下几乎锁死（93% 未变），即便 5×10⁻⁵ 重训也仍有约 62% 较大权重受舍入截断；无论续训还是重训都应启用 FP32 主权重与优化器状态，并实测旧验证最低点（OpenWAM 6k 与 π0.5 早期步数）。

  3. Balance crossed layouts and add paired opposite-rule prompts扩充交叉布局与成对反规则示教

    Collect crossed demos across all six blue/green × left/middle/right layouts and upweight corrective frames (currently 28.2%). For language-switchable sorting, include paired opposite-rule episodes instead of one fixed prompt.

    补采“蓝/绿 × 左/中/右”六类交叉示教并提高纠偏采样权重（当前新增帧仅占 28.2%）；如需按文字切换左右规则，必须加入成对反规则示教。
