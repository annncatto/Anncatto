  • 数据采集：原失败发生在绿色物体靠近蓝色篮子的条件，下一轮优先补充蓝、绿物体位于左侧、中部和右侧的交叉示范。新增帧当前占训练集 28.24%，关键纠偏条件的采样权重决定它在混合训练中被反复看到的频率。

    Data collection: the observed error places a green object near the blue basket, motivating crossed demonstrations of both colors at left, middle, and right positions. Added frames currently account for 28.24% of training, so corrective-condition sampling controls their exposure within the mixture.

  • 训练选择：B 在新增窗口中拟合更好，A 节省了早期适配；本轮实际配置同时改变了起点、学习率和累计历史。状态尺度漂移与保守的参数改写已经被分别测量，后续训练对照应统一归一化、学习率和采样权重，再比较初始化。

    Training choice: B fits added windows better, while A saves early adaptation; the recipes also differ in initialization, learning rate, and history. State-scale shifts and conservative weight changes have been measured separately, motivating matched normalization, learning rate, and sampling when comparing initialization.

  • 网络拆解：中段与末段的视频—动作连接是本轮局部干预中更敏感的环节，颜色与状态的逐层响应提供了追踪条件融合的位置。后续可在这些层段继续比较关键纠偏场景中的隐藏响应与完整去噪动作。

    Network analysis: middle and final video–action connections are more sensitive under these local interventions; layerwise color and state responses locate condition fusion. Further probes can compare hidden responses and complete denoising in the critical correction scenes.

  • 真机执行：目标篮子选择、夹持状态、动作块生成和轮速反馈分别对应任务、接触、计算和传感环节。将抓取完成、正确投放、生成耗时与实际反馈分别记录，才能把网络变化连接到相应的执行结果。

    Robot execution: destination choice, holding state, chunk generation, and wheel feedback concern task selection, contact, computation, and sensing. Recording grasp completion, correct deposits, generation time, and feedback separately connects network changes to the relevant execution outcomes.
