训练从 OpenWAM-α 基座开始，重新初始化 18 维动作与状态投影，更新视频和动作 DiT，冻结 VAE 与文本编码器。全部示教的三个底盘动作通道均为零，升降保持固定工作高度。

Training starts from OpenWAM-α with new 18-coordinate action/state projections. Video and action DiTs are updated; VAE and text encoder are frozen. All demonstrated base commands are zero, with the lift at a fixed working height.

115 条示教 · 10k 步115 demonstrations · 10k steps

本组使用 18 维关节表示，训练数据划分为 109 条训练示教和 6 条验证示教。下面分别展示一次完成分类的记录，以及连续运行中出现的误投。

This group uses the 18-coordinate joint representation, with 109 training and six validation demonstrations. The clips show a completed sorting sequence and a wrong-basket deposit during continuous operation.

成功记录：Successful rollout:

两臂将桌面上的蓝、绿积木放入对应篮子，完成接近、夹持、搬运与释放，最终清空桌面散落积木。

Both arms place blue and green blocks into their assigned baskets, completing approach, grasping, transport, and release and clearing the loose tabletop objects.

失败记录：Failed rollout:

绿色积木被放到蓝色篮子前。机械臂夹住并抬起积木，随后将它送入蓝色篮子并松爪。

A green block is placed in front of the blue-filled basket. The arm grasps and lifts it, then carries it into that basket and releases it.

完整连续记录 · 4 min 45.6 sComplete continuous record · 4 min 45.6 s

过程中有人补放积木和调整场景，末段包含上面的绿色积木误投。

An operator replenishes blocks and adjusts the scene. The late segment includes the green-block error shown above.

成功与失败片段都完成了抓取、抬起和释放。两者的关键差别在于搬运目标：失败片段中物体没有滑落，夹爪也完成松开，绿色积木却沿动作轨迹进入了错误篮子。因此，抓取动作完成与颜色分类正确需要分别计分；只统计桌面是否清空，会把这类误投计入成功。

Both clips complete grasping, lifting, and release. The key difference is the transport destination: in the failed clip, the block does not slip and the gripper opens, but the planned transfer ends in the wrong basket. Grasp completion and correct color sorting therefore require separate scores. Counting only whether the tabletop is cleared would treat this error as a success.

这组记录使用固定的颜色分拣指令。短片表明模型能在所示布局中连续完成两类积木的操作，末段误投则说明这一结果没有覆盖绿色积木靠近蓝色篮子的情况。视频记录包含人工补放，没有统一的任务起止与试验分母，当前按案例分析操作阶段与目标选择。

The group uses a fixed color-sorting instruction. The short clip shows successive handling of both colors in that layout; the later error shows that this result does not extend to the green-block placement near the blue basket. The continuous footage includes manual replenishment rather than uniformly defined trials, so it is analyzed as cases of manipulation stages and destination choice.

后续评估应让蓝、绿积木分别出现在两个篮子前及桌面中部，保持篮子位置和任务指令不变。每种布局同时记录抓取完成率与目标篮子正确率，并保留搬运开始到释放的片段。这样能把操作失败与分类失败分开，也能直接检查补充数据是否改善了已经出现误投的条件。

Subsequent evaluation should place each color in front of either basket and in the middle of the table, keeping basket locations and the instruction fixed. Record both grasp-completion and destination-accuracy rates for each layout, retaining the transport-to-release segments. This separates manipulation errors from sorting errors and directly tests whether the additional data improves the observed failure condition.
