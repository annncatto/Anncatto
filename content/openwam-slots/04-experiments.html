AlohaMini 的任务是把桌面蓝色物体放入机器人左篮、绿色物体放入右篮。旧模型已经展示连续抓放能力，也出现了抓住绿色积木后送入错误篮子的情况。

AlohaMini must place blue tabletop objects in the robot-left basket and green objects in the right basket. The original model demonstrates successive manipulation, but also carries a grasped green block into the wrong basket.

补充 40 回合差异较大的示范后，本轮比较继续微调旧任务模型与从官方基座重训，关注哪条路线更充分地吸收纠偏示范。进一步通过条件替换与注意力通路干预，拆解输入、隐藏表征和动作输出之间的联系。

After adding 40 shifted demonstrations, the comparison asks whether fine-tuning the task model or restarting from the official foundation better assimilates corrective examples. Condition substitutions and attention-pathway interventions then trace their effects through inputs, hidden representations, and action outputs.

关键发现Key findings

  1. 新增轨迹拟合：三个新增训练窗口中，重训 B 的关节 MAE 为 3.96，微调 A 为 6.14；B 是优先进行纠偏实测的候选，A 的早期适配更快。训练路线对照。

    Added-trajectory fit: joint MAE is 3.96 for restarted B and 6.14 for fine-tuned A on three added-training windows. B is the candidate to test first for correction; A adapts faster initially. Training-route comparison.

  2. 状态条件漂移：升降统计边界约改变 0.19 mm，就使 320 个新增状态的投影 token 变化 49.47%；这个变化继续影响隐藏层和动作。状态尺度分析。

    State-context shift: an approximately 0.19 mm lift-statistic boundary change shifts projected tokens by 49.47% across 320 added states, with effects reaching hidden layers and actions. State-scale analysis.

  3. 条件读取：屏蔽整段文本造成大幅输出偏移，反转左右规则在所选短窗口中的动作变化较小；两项干预分别改变条件可见性与指令内容。语言与颜色干预。

    Conditional dependence: masking full text causes large output shifts, whereas rule reversal produces smaller action changes in the selected short windows. These interventions change context visibility and instruction content, respectively. Language and color interventions.

  4. 层段作用：屏蔽第 25–29 层的视频到动作通路，使 A/B 去噪速度场变化 35.05%/32.50%；第 5–9 层为 3.61%/3.90%。网络屏蔽图与测量解释。

    Layer-group effects: masking video-to-action attention at layers 25–29 changes A/B denoising velocity by 35.05%/32.50%, versus 3.61%/3.90% at layers 5–9. Network diagram and measurement.

成功与失败片段都完成了抓取、抬起和释放。两者的关键差别在于搬运目标：失败片段中物体没有滑落，夹爪也完成松开，绿色积木却沿动作轨迹进入了错误篮子。因此，抓取动作完成与颜色分类正确需要分别计分；只统计桌面是否清空，会把这类误投计入成功。

Both clips complete grasping, lifting, and release. The key difference is the transport destination: in the failed clip, the block does not slip and the gripper opens, but the planned transfer ends in the wrong basket. Grasp completion and correct color sorting therefore require separate scores. Counting only whether the tabletop is cleared would treat this error as a success.

这组记录使用固定的颜色分拣指令。短片表明模型能在所示布局中连续完成两类积木的操作，末段误投则说明这一结果没有覆盖绿色积木靠近蓝色篮子的情况。视频记录包含人工补放，没有统一的任务起止与试验分母，当前按案例分析操作阶段与目标选择。

The group uses a fixed color-sorting instruction. The short clip shows successive handling of both colors in that layout; the later error shows that this result does not extend to the green-block placement near the blue basket. The continuous footage includes manual replenishment rather than uniformly defined trials, so it is analyzed as cases of manipulation stages and destination choice.
