Experimental record实验记录

AlohaMini sorts tabletop blocks by placing blue objects in the robot-left basket and green objects in the right basket. The original 115-episode model grasps and transports blocks continuously, yet carries a green block placed near the blue basket into the wrong basket. After adding 40 shifted demonstrations (155 total), we first present six closed-loop robot rollouts alongside π0.5, then isolate the four mechanisms behind the gap: BF16 fine-tuning stagnation, tight min/max lift scaling, text/color pathways, and two-band video–action coupling.

AlohaMini 的任务是把桌面蓝色积木放入机器人左篮、绿色积木放入右篮。原 115 回合模型能连续完成抓取和搬运，但在绿色积木靠近蓝篮时出现误投。补充 40 条差异较大的纠偏示教（共 155 回合）后，本章先给出与 π0.5 同桌对比的 6 段真机复测结果，再逐项拆解背后的四个原因：BF16 微调参数停滞、升降紧 min/max 归一化、文本/颜色通路，以及视频—动作两段式耦合。

Core synthesis: Restarting from the foundation rewrites weights and clears cross-side green blocks cleanly once on the robot, whereas pure-BF16 fine-tuning at 1×10⁻⁵ freezes 93% of weights and repeats the wrong-basket drop. Across all models, text acts as a fixed template, color cues weaken when visible block area is small, and tight min/max on the fixed lift axis turns 0.19 mm of homing jitter into a 49.5% input shift.

总览结论：从基座重训能有效改写权重并在真机无介入完成跨侧绿块纠偏，而纯 BF16 下用 1×10⁻⁵ 微调几乎锁死 93% 参数并重复错篮。进一步拆解表明，单句文本仅起固定模板作用、前视颜色在小面积暴露下偏弱，且固定升降轴的紧 min/max 把 0.19 mm 复归误差放大成了 49.5% 的状态输入跳变。

The original 18-coordinate joint model trains on 109 demonstrations and validates on six. Below are a completed sorting rollout and the wrong-basket failure that motivated adding 40 corrective demonstrations.

原 18 维关节模型使用 109 条训练示教和 6 条验证示教。下面分别展示一次完成的分类记录，以及促使补充 40 条纠偏数据的误投案例。

Successful rollout:成功记录：

Both arms grasp, transport, and release blue and green blocks into their matching baskets, clearing the tabletop.

两臂将桌面蓝、绿积木分别放入对应篮子，完成抓取、搬运与释放并清空桌面。

Failed rollout:失败记录：

A green block placed in front of the blue basket is grasped cleanly, carried directly into the blue basket, and released. Grasp completion and destination correctness must therefore be scored separately.

绿色积木放在蓝篮前时，机械臂稳妥抓起并直接送入蓝篮松爪；物体没有滑落，失败点在于目标篮子选择，因此抓取完成与正确投放必须分开计分。
