Experimental record实验记录

AlohaMini sorts tabletop blocks by placing blue objects into the left basket and green objects into the right basket. The original 115-episode model grasps and transports blocks smoothly, yet drops a green block near the blue basket into the wrong basket. After adding 40 corrective demonstrations (155 total), we compare six live rollouts alongside π0.5 and isolate four mechanisms behind the behavior gap.

AlohaMini 的任务是把桌面蓝色积木放入左篮、绿色积木放入右篮。原 115 回合模型能连续完成抓取和搬运，但在绿色积木靠近蓝篮时出现误投。补充 40 条纠偏示教（共 155 回合）后，本章先呈现与 π0.5 同桌的 6 段真机复测结果，再拆解背后的四项核心机制。

    With weights and AdamW moments stored in BF16, Fine-tuned 10k’s 1×10⁻⁵ peak lr, 8.4× weaker starting gradients (0.76 vs 6.38), and 71.8% old-frame share push most updates below half a BF16 ULP, leaving 93% of DiT weights and 99.3% of state_proj bitwise unchanged.     Because tabletop sorting holds lift within a 0.19 mm band (599.54–599.73 mm), adding 40 episodes just 0.2 mm higher (599.86–599.93 mm) doubles the tight min/max denominator, jumping 599.73 mm from +1 to 0 and shifting 4096-dim state tokens by 49.5% (shifting actions by 5.0 / 3.0).   4. Single-prompt text serves only as a fixed template; forward-view color enters control but weakens under occlusion单句指令仅充当固定任务模板，前视颜色已读入但在小面积暴露下偏弱

    Reversing “blue-left / green-right” shifts actions only 2.6 / 1.5—below noise-seed variance (4.6 / 4.0)—showing no switchable language rule; the severe numerical crash from masking all text (151 / 144, nearly half out of bounds) is cross-attention collapse onto the single state token. Swapping forward foreground hue shifts arm joints 8.6 / 6.8 and right gripper 16.5 / 29.0 when the block is widely visible (ep. 135, 9,445 px), but only 1.6 / 0.6 when occluded (ep. 154, 770 px), directly explaining intermittent live wrong-basket drops (condition probes ↓).

    反转“蓝左绿右”规则动作仅变 2.6 / 1.5，低于同画面换噪声种子波动（4.6 / 4.0），模型并未学会按文字切换规则；清空全部文本引发的数值发散崩溃（151 / 144，近半越界）源于交叉注意力失去 512 维文本上下文后全部压向单个状态 token。交换前视蓝绿色相在积木暴露面积大的 ep. 135（9,445 像素）使臂与右爪剧烈改变（8.6 / 6.8，右爪 16.5 / 29.0），在遮挡较多的 ep. 154（770 像素）几乎无变化（1.6 / 0.6），直接解释了真机时对时错的错篮表现（条件探针 ↓）。

Successful rollout:成功记录：

Both arms grasp, transport, and release blue and green blocks into their matching baskets, clearing the tabletop.

两臂将桌面蓝、绿积木分别放入对应篮子，完成抓取、搬运与释放并清空桌面。

Failed rollout:失败记录：

A green block placed in front of the blue basket is grasped cleanly, carried directly into the blue basket, and released. Grasp completion and destination correctness must therefore be scored separately.

绿色积木放在蓝篮前时，机械臂稳妥抓起并直接送入蓝篮松爪；物体没有滑落，失败点在于目标篮子选择，因此抓取完成与正确投放必须分开计分。
