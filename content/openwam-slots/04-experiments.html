Experimental record实验记录

Experimental task实验任务

Bimanual sorting of blue and green tabletop blocks under the fixed prompt “Clear the tabletop by placing all blue objects into the basket on the robot's left and all green objects into the basket on the robot's right. Both baskets are in front of the robot.” The original 115-episode model grasps and transports blocks continuously but fails to sort them by color. After adding 40 corrective demonstrations (155 total), this section first presents six live robot rollouts comparing OpenWAM and π0.5, then breaks down the four underlying mechanisms.

双臂蓝绿色积木分类放置，语言文本为“Clear the tabletop by placing all blue objects into the basket on the robot's left and all green objects into the basket on the robot's right. Both baskets are in front of the robot.”。原 115 回合模型能连续完成抓取和搬运，但无法按颜色分类放置；补充 40 条纠偏示教（共 155 回合）后，本章先呈现 OpenWAM 与 π0.5 的 6 段真机复测结果，再拆解背后的四项核心机制。

    Both routes store weights and AdamW moments entirely in pure BF16 with no FP32 master copy. The 7% vs 38% update gap is gated first by the 5× learning-rate difference relative to BF16’s half-ULP rounding threshold, then amplified by starting gradients: Fine-tuned’s 1×10⁻⁵ peak lr sits below half a BF16 ULP for typical weights (6.1×10⁻⁵ at |w| ≈ 0.026; 4.9×10⁻⁴ in state_proj) and starts near the old minimum with 8.4× weaker gradients (0.76 vs 6.38) and 71.8% old frames, leaving 93% of DiT and 99.3% of state_proj unchanged. Restarted’s 5×10⁻⁵ peak lr clears the BF16 rounding threshold for small-to-medium weights while reinitialized 18D projections provide strong early gradients—rewriting 38% of DiT weights and reducing release-window MAE from 6.1 to 4.0 (ep. 115 2.4 vs 4.1; ep. 154 2.7 vs 7.5), though ~62% of larger weights still stay unchanged under pure BF16 (fit & weights ↓).

    微调 10k 与重训 10k 的参数和 AdamW 矩全为纯 BF16 存储（无 FP32 主权重）。更新比例相差逾 5 倍，核心受峰值学习率差 5 倍是否跨过 BF16 半步长门槛支配，并由起点梯度放大：微调峰值 lr 仅 1×10⁻⁵，低于常规权重的 BF16 半步长（|w| ≈ 0.026 时 6.1×10⁻⁵，state_proj 达 4.9×10⁻⁴），再叠加旧极小值附近小 8.4 倍的起始梯度（0.76 对 6.38）与 71.8% 旧帧占比，93% DiT 与 99.3% state_proj 逐位未变；重训用 5×10⁻⁵ 跨过中小幅值权重的 BF16 写入门槛，并从基座重建 18 维投影获得更强早期梯度，得以改写 38% DiT 参数，将右爪松开窗 12 关节误差降至 4.0（微调 6.1；ep. 115 2.4 对 4.1，ep. 154 2.7 对 7.5）——但纯 BF16 下仍有约 62% 较大权重未动，根本解决需启用 FP32 主权重与优化器状态（拟合与权重拆解 ↓）。

    Because lift barely moves during tabletop sorting, the old 109 episodes only span 0.19 mm of motor settling jitter (599.54–599.73 mm). Adding 40 episodes just 0.2 mm higher (599.86–599.93 mm) doubles that tiny denominator, shifting the same physical height (599.73 mm) from +1 to 0 and changing state tokens by 49.5% (shifting actions 5.0 / 3.0). Scaling by the 0–600 mm hardware travel range reduces that shift from 1.00 to 0.0006, and keeping old stats during fine-tuning preserves the adapted input scale (lift simulator ↓).

    桌面分拣时升降轴几乎不动，旧 109 条数据的极值差只是 0.19 mm 的电机复归抖动（599.54–599.73 mm）；补采 40 条仅高 0.2 mm（599.86–599.93 mm）就令分母翻倍，同一物理高度 599.73 mm 的输入坐标从 +1 跳至 0，引发状态特征变化 49.5%（恢复旧统计即致动作偏移 5.0 / 3.0）。建议改按 0–600 mm 硬件真实量程归一化（坐标变化从 1.00 降至 0.0006），基于旧权重续训时沿用旧统计量不重算（升降归一化演示 ↓）。

  4. Single-prompt text serves only as a fixed template; forward-view color enters control but weakens under occlusion单句指令仅充当固定任务模板，前视颜色已读入但在小面积暴露下偏弱

    Reversing “blue-left / green-right” shifts actions only 2.6 / 1.5—below noise-seed variance (4.6 / 4.0)—showing no switchable language rule; the large out-of-distribution divergence from masking all text (151 / 144, nearly half out of bounds) arises because cross-attention concentrates 100% weight onto the single remaining state token. Swapping forward foreground hue shifts arm joints 8.6 / 6.8 and right gripper 16.5 / 29.0 when the block is widely visible (ep. 135, 9,445 px), but only 1.6 / 0.6 when occluded (ep. 154, 770 px), directly explaining intermittent live wrong-basket drops (condition probes ↓).

    反转“蓝左绿右”规则动作仅变 2.6 / 1.5，低于同画面换噪声种子波动（4.6 / 4.0），模型并未学会按文字切换规则；清空全部文本引发的数值发散（151 / 144，近半越界）源于交叉注意力失去 512 维文本上下文后将全部权重集中到单个状态 token。交换前视蓝绿色相在积木暴露面积大的 ep. 135（9,445 像素）使臂与右爪明显改变（8.6 / 6.8，右爪 16.5 / 29.0），在遮挡较多的 ep. 154（770 像素）几乎无变化（1.6 / 0.6），直接解释了真机时对时错的错篮表现（条件探针 ↓）。

Successful rollout:成功记录：
