Experimental record实验记录

AlohaMini sorts tabletop blocks by placing blue objects in the robot-left basket and green objects in the right basket. The original 115-episode model grasps and transports blocks continuously, yet carries a green block placed near the blue basket into the wrong basket. After adding 40 shifted demonstrations (155 total), we present six closed-loop robot rollouts alongside π0.5, then isolate the causes behind the gap: BF16 fine-tuning stagnation, tight min/max lift scaling, text/color pathways, and two-band video–action coupling.

AlohaMini 的任务是把桌面蓝色积木放入机器人左篮、绿色积木放入右篮。原 115 回合模型能连续完成抓取和搬运，但在绿色积木靠近蓝篮时出现误投。补充 40 条差异较大的纠偏示教（共 155 回合）后，本章先给出与 π0.5 同桌对比的 6 段真机复测结果，再逐项拆解背后的四个原因：BF16 微调参数停滞、升降紧 min/max 归一化、文本/颜色通路，以及视频—动作两段式耦合。

    微调权重改写极小的原因（不止学习率低）：参数与 AdamW 一、二阶矩全存 BF16，微调峰值学习率低 5 倍（1×10⁻⁵ 对 5×10⁻⁵，衰减至 5×10⁻⁷），使多数单步更新小于半个 BF16 步长被直接舍入回原值；再叠加起点接近旧极小值（起始 grad_norm 仅 0.76，为重训 6.38 的 1/8.4）以及 71.8% 仍是旧数据帧，导致 93% DiT 与 99.3% state_proj 逐位未变，没能改写旧错篮习惯（拟合与权重拆解 ↓）。

  3. Tight data min/max amplifies 0.19 mm lift jitter into a 49.5% state-token jump → use hardware range floors紧 min/max 把 0.19 mm 升降复归微差放大为 49.5% 状态跳变 → 近常量通道改用硬件行程下限

    Cause & takeaway: Lift stayed practically fixed during sorting—old 109 episodes sat in 599.54–599.73 mm (0.19 mm span) and added 40 episodes in 599.86–599.93 mm (same desk height, 0.2 mm higher). Recomputing tight [−1, +1] min/max doubled the tiny denominator (0.19 → 0.38 mm), jumping the exact same height (599.73 mm) from +1 to 0 and shifting 4096-dim state tokens by 49.5% (restoring old stats shifts actions by 5.0 / 3.0). Engineering rule: never apply dataset min/max to near-constant channels—enforce physical range floors (0–600 mm lift stroke) and freeze old stats when fine-tuning (interactive simulator ↓).

    成因与思考：桌面分拣期间升降轴几乎静止，旧 109 条示教全落在 599.54–599.73 mm（跨度仅 0.19 mm），新增 40 条落在 599.86–599.93 mm（物理桌面相同，仅差 0.2 mm）。合并重算 [−1, +1] 紧 min/max 使极小分母翻倍（0.19 → 0.38 mm），同一高度 599.73 mm 的坐标从 +1 直接跳至 0，令 4096 维状态 token 相对 L2 剧变 49.5%（恢复旧统计即致动作偏移 5.0 / 3.0）。工程结论：固定高度或静止通道绝不能用紧数据 min/max，应强制设置硬件物理行程下限（0–600 mm），且微调时冻结旧统计而非重算（升降归一化交互演示 ↓）。

  4. Text acts as a fixed template rather than a switchable rule; forward color enters control but weakens under small visible area文本仅作固定模板而非可切换规则；前视颜色已读入但在可见面积小时偏弱

    Mask all text: Predictions explode to −424..+628 (arm shift 151 / 144, 49% / 47% out of bounds)—not proof of language understanding, but an OOD crash caused by cross-attention softmax dumping 100% weight onto the single state token after losing all 512 text slots. Reverse rule (“blue-left/green-right” → “blue-right/green-left”): Actions shift only 2.6 / 1.5, smaller than changing noise seed 17→29 (4.6 / 4.0), proving single-prompt training never taught switchable language rules. Swap foreground hue: Strongly shifts arm joints (8.6 / 6.8) and right-gripper release (16.5 / 29.0) when the block is widely exposed (ep. 135, 9,445 px), yet barely acts when occluded (ep. 154, 770 px: 1.6 / 0.6)—matching intermittent live wrong-basket drops (condition probes ↓).

    屏蔽全部文本：预测炸至 −424~+628（12 关节偏离 151 / 144，49% / 47% 越界），并不代表模型听懂文字，而是清空训练必带的 512 维文本后，交叉注意力 softmax 把 100% 权重压到单个状态 token 引发分布外崩溃。反转左右规则：把“蓝左绿右”改为“蓝右绿左”动作仅变 2.6 / 1.5，低于同画面换随机种子波动（4.6 / 4.0），说明单句固定指令训练未教会按文字切换规则。交换前视蓝绿色相：积木暴露面积大时（ep. 135，9,445 像素）臂变 8.6 / 6.8、右爪变 16.5 / 29.0，遮挡较多时（ep. 154，770 像素）仅变 1.6 / 0.6，说明颜色线索在小面积或视角冲突下偏弱，正对应真机“时对时错”的错篮表现（条件探针 ↓）。

    What “response” measures: With weights fixed, blocking video K/V → action Q (32×360) in one 5-layer group and running all 30 layers measures the relative L2 deviation of the final 32×14 denoising-velocity field. Deviation peaks at layers 25–29 (35.0% / 32.5%) and 10–19 (25–31%), versus 3.6% / 3.9% at layers 5–9—identical across all three checkpoints, indicating an inherited two-band backbone schedule. In full 10-step rollouts, blocking only generated future video → action shifts arm joints 16.9 / 18.5 and right gripper 39.4 / 35.7, showing execution directly tracks the model’s imagined future frames (interactive layer probe ↓).

    “响应”具体指什么：保持权重固定，每次仅在连续 5 层切断 视频 K/V → 动作 Q（32×360 子块），跑完 30 层后量测末端 32×14 动作去噪速度场相对正常基线的偏离幅度。切断 25–29 层偏离高达 35.0% / 32.5%、10–19 层偏离 25%–31%，而 5–9 层仅 3.6% / 3.9%（三份权重曲线重合，属基座固有结构）；完整去噪中仅切断生成中的未来视频→动作即令 12 关节偏离 16.9 / 18.5、右夹爪偏离 39.4 / 35.7，说明机械臂轨迹直接依赖模型想象出的未来视频帧（交互式层组探针 ↓）。
