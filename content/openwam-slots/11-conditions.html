Experimental conditions实验条件

Clear the tabletop by placing all blue objects into the basket on the robot's left and all green objects into the basket on the robot's right. Both baskets are in front of the robot.

All runs use AlohaMini2 Pro with 18-coordinate state/action (12 arm joints, 2 grippers, 3 base velocities, 1 lift height). OpenWAM reads forward, wrist_left, and wrist_right; π0.5 additionally reads chest. Complete dataset, training, probe, and evaluation tables are grouped below.
