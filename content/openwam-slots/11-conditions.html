Clear the tabletop by placing all blue objects into the basket on the robot's left and all green objects into the basket on the robot's right. Both baskets are in front of the robot.

实验使用 AlohaMini2 Pro，输入前视与双腕图像，输出双臂关节、夹爪、底盘和升降共 18 维动作。蓝色积木放入机器人左侧篮子，绿色积木放入右侧篮子。

The experiment uses AlohaMini2 Pro with forward and wrist images and 18 joint, gripper, base, and lift outputs. Blue blocks go in the robot-left basket and green blocks in the robot-right basket.

Training starts from OpenWAM-α with new 18-coordinate action/state projections. Video and action DiTs are updated; VAE and text encoder are frozen. All demonstrated base commands are zero, with the lift at a fixed working height.

训练起点Training initialization

  • 数据：原 115 回合增加到 155 回合，共 82,903 帧。训练为 149 回合、79,088 帧；6 条验证示教均来自原数据，共 3,815 帧。

    Data: 115 original episodes are expanded to 155, totaling 82,903 frames. Training uses 149 episodes and 79,088 frames; all six validation episodes come from the original data, totaling 3,815 frames.

  • 共同设置：两组使用同一划分和逐项相同的训练集 min/max，保持三路图像、18 维关节接口与 32 步动作窗口。有效 batch 32，seed 42，BF16 权重与计算。

    Shared settings: both runs use the same split and exactly matching training-set min/max, retaining three views, the 18-coordinate joint interface, and 32-step action chunks. Effective batch size is 32, seed is 42, and weights and computation use BF16.

离线探针协议Offline probe protocols
项目Item设置Setting
完整去噪Full denoising每模型 4 窗口 × 2 种子（17、29）× 8 条件＝64 次；三模型共 192 次。只输入当前观测，10 步 Euler、shift 5，生成 32 步动作与未来视频。Per model: 4 windows × 2 seeds (17, 29) × 8 conditions = 64 queries; 192 across three models. Current observations only, 10 Euler steps, shift 5, generating 32 actions and future video.
单步定位One-step localization每模型 4 窗口 × 15 条件；三模型共 180 次前向。未来示范视频和动作分别与 seed 7 噪声各混合 50%，时间 500，当前视频前缀保持固定。4 windows × 15 conditions per model; 180 forwards total. Demonstrated future video and actions are mixed equally with seed-7 noise at time 500; the observed video prefix remains clamped.
层组干预Layer-group intervention30 个配对层按连续五层分为六组；仅选定组中动作 Q 读取视频 K/V 的位置设为不可见，所有模型权重固定。Six consecutive groups of five out of 30 paired layers. Only action-Q/video-K-V entries are masked in the chosen group; model weights remain fixed.
动作指标Action metrics12 个臂关节用 Host range_m100_100；两夹爪用 range_0_100，分别统计。输出未经机器人限幅。Twelve arm coordinates use Host range_m100_100; grippers use range_0_100 and are measured separately. Generated outputs are not robot-clipped.
速度场 / 隐藏表征Velocity / hidden representations速度场采用 32×14 个双臂含夹爪的归一化通道；隐藏表征使用每层全部残差 token。均先算各窗口的相对 L2，再平均四个窗口。Velocity uses 32×14 normalized arm/gripper coordinates; hidden comparisons use all residual tokens at each layer. Compute relative L2 within each window, then average the four windows.
状态投影替换State-projection substitution每回合等距取 8 帧：原训练 872、新增训练 320、原验证 48；投影在 FP32 下计算，单独替换统计或权重。Eight uniform states per episode: 872 original-training, 320 added-training, 48 original-validation. Projection is evaluated in FP32, replacing statistics or weights separately.
计算环境Probe environment单张 RTX 4090 D，PyTorch 2.11.0+cu130 / CUDA 13.0，模型与计算 BF16；T5/VAE 编码缓存跨模型复用。One RTX 4090 D, PyTorch 2.11.0+cu130 / CUDA 13.0, BF16 weights and compute; frozen T5/VAE encodings are cached across models.

每个模型在首窗口、seed 17 的相同缓存条件下，探针采样与原生采样逐元素相同，最大绝对差为 0。逐层残差、完整动作与窗口指纹用于复算公开聚合。[18]

For each model, probe and native sampling are exactly equal at the first window and seed 17 under identical cached conditions, with maximum absolute difference zero. Layer residuals, full actions, and window fingerprints support reproduction of the public aggregates. [18]
