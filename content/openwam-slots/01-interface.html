关节与末端Joints and end effectors

动作表示决定策略预测哪些物理量。AlohaMini 的原始动作记录包含双臂各 6 个关节与 1 个夹爪、底盘 3 个速度以及升降高度，共 18 维。关节值采用 Host 的标定坐标，夹爪采用开合坐标；这些量分别对应关节位置目标、底盘速度目标和升降位置目标。

An action representation determines the physical quantities a policy predicts. AlohaMini records six joints and one gripper per arm, three base velocities, and lift height: 18 coordinates in total. Joints use calibrated Host coordinates and grippers use opening coordinates. These fields specify joint positions, base velocities, and lift position.

关节表示直接描述各电机的目标位置，动作可以经单位恢复后交给控制器。末端表示描述工具中心的位置与朝向，先由标定和正运动学（FK）把关节记录转换成末端位姿，执行时再通过逆运动学（IK）求关节目标。相同的末端移动可以由不同机器人的关节轨迹实现，因此末端表示更便于对齐不同本体的动作数据。

Joint representation directly specifies motor targets, which can be sent to the controller after restoring units. TCP representation specifies tool position and orientation: calibration and forward kinematics (FK) convert recorded joints to TCP poses, and inverse kinematics (IK) recovers joint targets for execution. Different robots can realize a similar TCP motion through different joint trajectories, making TCP data easier to align across embodiments.

末端位姿由位置与朝向组成。位置通常用三个坐标表示，朝向的编码则决定整个向量的长度。常见表示及 AlohaMini 各算法的适配如下。[8]

A TCP pose consists of position and orientation. Position normally uses three coordinates; the orientation encoding determines the total vector length. Common representations and their use in AlohaMini adapters are listed below. [8]

  • 位置＋欧拉角：6 维。三个位置坐标加三个依次旋转的角度，例如 roll、pitch、yaw。必须约定旋转顺序与角度单位；角度主值在边界处会换支，部分姿态还会出现万向节锁。Position + Euler angles: 6 coordinates.Three position coordinates and three ordered rotation angles, such as roll, pitch, and yaw. Rotation order and angular units must be specified. Principal angles wrap at their boundaries, and some orientations encounter gimbal lock.
  • 位置＋旋转向量：6 维。旋转向量的方向表示旋转轴，长度表示旋转角，单位为弧度；它的三个分量不能按三个欧拉角解释。Spirit 1.5 的适配默认采用这种编码，加上夹爪后，每只手占 7 维。Position + rotation vector: 6 coordinates.The vector direction specifies the rotation axis and its length the angle in radians. Its components are not three Euler angles. The Spirit 1.5 adapter uses this encoding by default, giving seven coordinates per hand with the gripper.
  • 位置＋单位四元数：7 维。三个位置坐标加四个旋转分量。四元数满足单位长度约束，并且 $q$ 与 $-q$ 表示同一朝向；数据还需约定 $xyzw$ 或 $wxyz$ 的排列顺序。欧拉角与四元数是常见的位姿表示，但当前共享 TCP 训练转换器未将它们提供为输出选项。Position + unit quaternion: 7 coordinates.Three position coordinates plus four rotation components. Quaternions have unit norm, and $q$ and $-q$ represent the same orientation. The component order, $xyzw$ or $wxyz$, must also be specified. Euler angles and quaternions are common pose formats, but are not output options in the current shared TCP training converter.
  • 位置＋行式 6D 旋转：9 维。取旋转矩阵的前两行，依次排列为 $(R_{00},R_{01},R_{02},R_{10},R_{11},R_{12})$。AlohaMini 的 rotation_6d 使用这一顺序，可用于 Hy-VLA 的末端适配和 GR00T 可选的相对末端配置；这些算法的默认关节配置不会自动进行此转换。Position + row-based 6D rotation: 9 coordinates.The first two matrix rows are ordered as $(R_{00},R_{01},R_{02},R_{10},R_{11},R_{12})$. AlohaMini's rotation_6d uses this convention for Hy-VLA TCP adaptation and the optional relative-TCP GR00T configuration. Their default joint configurations do not apply this conversion automatically.
  • 位置＋列式 6D 旋转：9 维。取旋转矩阵的前两列，依次排列为 $(R_{00},R_{10},R_{20},R_{01},R_{11},R_{21})$。OpenWAM-α 使用这一约定，AlohaMini 的 alpha80 适配对应 rotation_6d_columns。行式与列式编码长度相同，但数值顺序和解码方式不同，不能直接互换。[2][5]Position + column-based 6D rotation: 9 coordinates.The first two matrix columns are ordered as $(R_{00},R_{10},R_{20},R_{01},R_{11},R_{21})$. OpenWAM-α uses this convention, implemented by AlohaMini's alpha80 adapter as rotation_6d_columns. Row and column encodings have the same width but different ordering and decoding, so they are not interchangeable. [2][5]

旋转编码之外，还要区分绝对目标与相对增量。OpenWAM 的 alpha80 适配预测指定参考系中的绝对末端位姿；Spirit 的默认适配预测相对于本次观察中末端位姿的平移与旋转增量，夹爪仍使用绝对开合目标。因此，同样是“6 维末端位姿”，也必须交代旋转编码、参考系和目标类型，才能确定每个数的含义。

Absolute targets and relative increments are a separate choice from rotation encoding. OpenWAM's alpha80 adapter predicts absolute TCP poses in the declared reference frame. Spirit's default adapter predicts translation and rotation increments from the TCP pose in the current observation, while retaining absolute gripper targets. Even a “six-coordinate TCP pose” therefore needs an orientation encoding, reference frame, and target convention to define its meaning.

旋转编码Rotation encoding

刚体末端位姿有 6 个自由度：平移 3、旋转 3。官方旋转编码用 6 个数表达这 3 个旋转自由度，因此一只手的“位姿＋夹爪”占 10 个槽。双臂原始记录为 $2(6+1)+3+1=18$ 个数；经过 FK 与旋转编码后，有效量变为：

A rigid TCP pose has six degrees of freedom: three translational and three rotational. The official representation encodes the three rotational degrees of freedom with six numbers, so pose plus gripper occupies ten slots per hand. The original record contains $2(6+1)+3+1=18$ numbers. FK and rotation encoding produce:

$$D_{\mathrm{active}}=2\underbrace{(3+6+1)}_{\text{position + rotation + gripper}}+\underbrace{3+1}_{\text{base + lift}}=24,\qquad D_{\mathrm{interface}}=80.$$

24 表示有效标量数量，并未增加机器人自由度。对于旋转矩阵 $R\in SO(3)$，列式编码为 $r_6=[R_{:,0};R_{:,1}]$。解码时先单位化第一列，再从第二列中去除平行分量并单位化，第三列由叉积恢复。两个三维向量受单位长度和正交约束，其独立自由度仍为 3。官方列式顺序必须保留，不能与按行展开的实现混用。[2]

The 24 active scalars do not add mechanical degrees of freedom. For $R\in SO(3)$, the column representation is $r_6=[R_{:,0};R_{:,1}]$. Decoding normalizes the first column, orthogonalizes and normalizes the second, and reconstructs the third by a cross product. Unit-length and orthogonality constraints leave three rotational degrees of freedom. The official column ordering must be preserved rather than mixed with a row-based implementation. [2]

归一化与槽位Normalization and slots

双臂末端与辅助通道形成 24 个有效量后，还需要统一数值尺度和字段位置。位置与辅助量按训练集统计范围归一化，旋转矩阵列保持 [−1,1] 的物理范围，夹爪按闭合、张开端点映射。随后把每一类量放入固定索引，使不同数据源的“左手位置”“右手旋转”等字段保持相同含义。

The 24 valid TCP and auxiliary quantities still require consistent numerical scales and field positions. Position and auxiliaries are normalized by training ranges, rotation-matrix columns use physical bounds [−1,1], and grippers use closed/open endpoints. Each quantity is then assigned fixed indices so fields such as left-hand position and right-hand rotation retain their meaning across data sources.

OpenWAM-α 为每侧分配 34 个语义槽：位置 3、旋转 6、夹爪 1、手部关节 24；双侧共 68 个槽，另有 12 个预留位置。AlohaMini 使用双臂位姿与夹爪对应的 20 个槽，底盘与升降使用预留区中的 4 个槽，无灵巧手对应的通道被屏蔽。[1]

OpenWAM-α allocates 34 semantic slots per side: position 3, rotation 6, gripper 1, and hand joints 24. Together these occupy 68 slots, with 12 reserved. AlohaMini uses 20 pose/gripper slots and four reserved slots for base/lift; dexterous-hand channels are masked. [1]

底盘与升降的 68–71 映射是 AlohaMini 适配声明的扩展，官方预训练没有为这四个位置保证同样的含义。末端坐标也必须同时声明参考系：当前适配使用左右机械臂各自的基座坐标系，FK 工具帧由模型与标定配置确定。相同的槽位编号并不能消除坐标系、工具偏置和单位的差异。

Assigning base and lift to slots 68–71 is an explicit project extension; pretraining does not guarantee those meanings. TCP coordinates also require declared reference frames: this adapter uses the respective left/right arm base frames, with tool frames fixed by the robot model and calibration. Matching slot indices alone does not resolve frame, tool-offset, or unit differences.

特征投影Feature projections

输入线性层之前，每个槽位对应约定的物理量，例如末端位置、旋转编码或夹爪开度。线性层将这些数值加权组合为动作特征，供后续 DiT 处理；特征的每一维不再固定对应某个物理量。设 $a_k$ 为真实控制时刻 $k$ 的动作，$N$ 为归一化，$S$ 为槽位映射，$\tau$ 为流匹配的噪声时间，则动作分支可概括为：

Before the input linear layer, each slot represents an agreed physical quantity, such as TCP position, rotation components, or gripper opening. The layer forms learned weighted combinations of these values for the DiT; each resulting feature coordinate no longer has a fixed physical meaning. Let $a_k$ be the action at control time $k$, $N$ normalization, $S$ slot assignment, and $\tau$ the flow-matching noise time. The action branch can be summarized as:

$$u_k=S\,N(a_k),\qquad z_{k,\tau}=(1-\sigma_\tau)u_k+\sigma_\tau\epsilon_k,\qquad h_{k,\tau}=W_{\mathrm{in}}z_{k,\tau}+b_{\mathrm{in}},$$
$$\widehat v_{k,\tau}=W_{\mathrm{out}}F_{\theta}(h_{1:H,\tau},c,\tau)_k+b_{\mathrm{out}}.$$

$\epsilon_k$ 是标准高斯噪声，$\sigma_\tau$ 是噪声调度给出的混合比例；$c$ 包含图像、文本和状态条件，$H$ 是预测时域。$F_\theta$ 表示由注意力与 MLP 组成的动作主干。网络预测流速度 $\widehat v$，采样器沿噪声时间积分后才得到动作。这里的 $\tau$ 与机器人真实时间 $k$ 是两条不同的时间轴。对 80 维末端表示，$u_k\in\mathbb R^{80}$；对 18 维关节路径，$S$ 为恒等映射，$u_k\in\mathbb R^{18}$。两者都投影成一个 1024 维动作 token。

Here $\epsilon_k$ is standard Gaussian noise and $\sigma_\tau$ is the mixing weight given by the noise schedule. $c$ contains image, text, and state conditions, $H$ is the prediction horizon, and $F_\theta$ is the attention/MLP action backbone. The network predicts flow velocity $\widehat v$; the sampler integrates over noise time to obtain actions. Noise time $\tau$ and physical control time $k$ are distinct axes. The 80-coordinate TCP representation uses $u_k\in\mathbb R^{80}$; the 18-coordinate joint path uses an identity $S$ and $u_k\in\mathbb R^{18}$. Both become one 1024-dimensional action token per step.

状态向量记录生成动作时机器人自身的反馈。18 维关节实验中的 observation.state 包含双臂各 6 个关节位置与 1 个夹爪开度、底盘 x/y 线速度与偏航角速度，以及升降高度。这些值由 Host 上报，描述当前姿态与运动状态；前面的动作向量则描述模型要预测的控制目标。两者可以具有相同的字段排列，但在模型中的作用和对应时刻不同。

The state vector records the robot's own feedback when actions are generated. In the 18-coordinate joint experiment, observation.state contains six joint positions and one gripper opening per arm, base x/y velocity and yaw rate, and lift height. These Host-reported values describe the current configuration and motion; the action vectors above describe control targets to be predicted. State and action can share a field layout while serving different roles and referring to different times.

任务文本先经过分词器与 T5 文本编码器，得到一组文本 token 特征；状态数值经过归一化，再由独立的线性层 $18\to4096$ 映射为一个状态 token。随后将状态 token 追加到文本 token 序列中。忽略 batch 维，设文本序列长度为 $L$，归一化状态为 $\widetilde s_k$，则：

The task text passes through a tokenizer and T5 to produce a sequence of text features. Normalized state values pass through a separate $18\to4096$ linear layer to produce one state token, which is then appended to the text-token sequence. Omitting the batch dimension, let $L$ be the text sequence length and $\widetilde s_k$ the normalized state:

$$C_{\mathrm{text}}\in\mathbb R^{L\times4096},\qquad h_s=W_s\widetilde s_k+b_s\in\mathbb R^{4096},$$
$$C_{\mathrm{cond}}=\begin{bmatrix}C_{\mathrm{text}}\\h_s^\top\end{bmatrix}\in\mathbb R^{(L+1)\times4096}.$$

拼接增加的是 token 数量：从 $L$ 个变为 $L+1$ 个，每个 token 仍是 4096 维特征。状态直接通过数值投影接入这组条件特征，T5 只负责文本编码。切换到 alpha80 时，状态先经过 FK、归一化与槽位映射，输入投影变为 $80\to4096$，仍只生成一个状态 token。[3]

Concatenation increases the token count from $L$ to $L+1$; every token remains 4096-dimensional. State enters the conditioning sequence through numeric projection, while T5 encodes the text. With alpha80, state undergoes FK, normalization, and slot assignment before an $80\to4096$ projection, still producing one state token. [3]

这样合并可以复用 DiT 已有的条件注意力通路。视频与动作分支分别将条件序列投影到各自使用的特征宽度，再通过交叉注意力读取其中的信息。文本给出“蓝色放左篮、绿色放右篮”的任务要求，状态给出手臂当前位置、夹爪开合与底盘运动情况；图像则经 VAE 进入视频分支，提供积木与篮子的视觉信息。模型由此同时获得任务要求、机器人状态和场景观察。

This combination reuses the DiT's existing conditioning-attention path. The video and action branches project the shared sequence to their respective feature widths and read it through cross-attention. Text specifies the task—blue blocks to the left basket and green blocks to the right—while state supplies arm configuration, gripper opening, and base motion. Images enter the video branch through the VAE and provide visual information about blocks and baskets. The model thus receives task requirements, robot state, and scene observations together.

视频与动作分支有 30 组对应的 DiT 层，在共同的注意力空间中交换信息，而各自残差流的宽度可以不同。动作分支内部的 FFN 为 $1024\to4096\to1024$，这些 MLP 与注意力、时间条件参数都属于基座的一部分。[3]

Thirty paired DiT layers exchange world and action information in a common attention space while retaining different residual widths. The action FFN has shape $1024\to4096\to1024$. These MLPs, attention weights, and time-conditioning parameters are part of the foundation model. [3]

改变动作字段会改变输入输出投影的形状和含义，但不要求改变 DiT 的隐藏宽度。因此，适配新机器人时可以分别处理动作接口与网络主干的预训练参数。

Changing action fields changes the shapes and meanings of input/output projections without requiring a different DiT hidden width. Adapting a new robot can therefore treat pretrained interface and backbone parameters separately.
