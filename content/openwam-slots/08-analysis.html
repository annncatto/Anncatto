动作与执行分析Action and execution analysis

The five diagnostics below profile the original 115-episode, 10k-step OpenWAM model—offline accuracy on the six validation episodes (192 queries) plus two fixed-base live rollouts (517 frames / 31.5 s and 4,938 frames)—establishing the tracking, posture, and latency baseline behind the 155-episode comparison above.

以下五项诊断针对原 115 回合、10k 步 OpenWAM 模型，覆盖 6 条验证示教上的离线误差（192 次查询）与两段固定底盘真机记录（517 帧 / 31.5 s 与 4,938 帧），用于交代运动跟踪、抓取姿态与推理时序基线。

双臂轨迹Arm trajectories

  • Both arms execute sustained, asynchronous transfers双臂执行多段异步连续搬运

    Over 31.52 s (517 frames), shoulder lift and elbow trace multiple continuous large-amplitude transfers with intervening gripper transitions; the right arm returns near home at ~20 s and the left at ~26 s.

    31.52 s（517 帧）内肩抬与肘部完成多段大幅连续搬运并穿插夹爪开合，右臂约 20 s、左臂约 26 s 各自回到初始姿态附近，并非单次静态抓取。

  • Command–feedback gripper gaps match Host holding intervals夹爪指令与反馈分离对应 Host 夹持保持态

    Closing commands stay near zero while feedback retains a nonzero opening during Host-reported holding (L 7.5–9.9 s / 19.9–22.3 s; R 5.1–7.9 s / 13.8–17.0 s); scoring raw command–feedback distance as tracking error would misclassify normal grasping.

    Host 上报 holding 期间（左 7.5–9.9 / 19.9–22.3 s；右 5.1–7.9 / 13.8–17.0 s），闭合指令近零而反馈保持非零开口，属正常夹持状态，不能直接计作位置跟踪失败。

  • A few raw requests exceed calibrated bounds—keep deployment clipping logged少量生成指令超出标定范围，部署需保留限幅并分开记录

    Submitted requests occasionally step past [−100, 100] / [0, 100] (left elbow peak 103.4; grippers min −0.70 / −1.11). Deployment interfaces must keep range clipping and log pre-/post-clip values separately.

    提交请求中左肘 11 帧超过 100（最高 103.4），左右夹爪分别有 13 / 20 帧低于 0（最低 −0.70 / −1.11）；部署接口需保留输出限幅，并将生成原值与限幅后控制指令分开落盘。

右臂抓取姿态Right-arm grasp orientation

  • Offline sampling reproduces low-wrist transitions (41.6 → 17.3 vs demo 42.8 → 15.0)离线回放能复现示教的低腕与压腕变化（41.6 → 17.3 对 42.8 → 15.0）

    Across 10 demonstration states × 3 seeds, the model generates the demonstrated downward wrist-flex drop in calibrated Host coordinates—showing the action distribution itself retains low-wrist grasps.

    在 10 个示教状态 × 3 随机种子离线采样中，右腕屈伸均值由 41.6 降至 17.3（示教 42.8 → 15.0，Host 标定坐标），说明策略能够生成压腕动作。

  • Live right-arm grasps tilt 5.3° steeper downward yet stay inside demo P10–P90实机右臂中位数更偏俯抓（+5.3°），仍落在示教 P10–P90 区间内

    In the 4,938-frame fixed-base rollout (FK-selected closing candidates), left-arm median tilt is unchanged (80.5° demo vs 80.4° live), while the right arm shifts from 67.3° to 72.6° (narrowing the left–right gap from 13.2° to 7.8°). Because demo P10–P90 already spans 47.9°–76.9°, this reflects online trajectory-selection preference rather than missing wrist range.

    在 4,938 帧固定底盘记录（标定 FK 闭合候选片段）中，左臂中位数基本不变（示教 80.5° 对实机 80.4°），右臂由 67.3° 升至 72.6°（双臂中位数差从 13.2° 缩至 7.8°）。该值仍处在示教 P10–P90（47.9°–76.9°）内，反映的是在线姿态选择偏好而非动作范围缺失。

执行时序Execution timing

  • 3.5% chunk-inference calls account for 77.8% of total call time仅占 3.5% 的慢调用（动作块重算）占掉 77.8% 总耗时

    Across 517 calls, median duration is 5.51 ms and P95 is 6.84 ms (queue dequeues), while the 18 calls over 100 ms have a median of 513.4 ms (first call 991.2 ms). Because chunk generation and queue reads share one timing field, overall P95 hides the dominant inference wait.

    517 次调用中位数 5.51 ms、P95 仅 6.84 ms（队列出队），但 18 次超过 100 ms 的慢调用中位数达 513.4 ms（首次 991.2 ms）；动作块生成与队列取步共用同一耗时字段，导致总体 P95 掩盖了主要等待开销。

  • Effective send rate is ~20.3 Hz—always measure durations on the Host clock实际发送频率约 20.3 Hz，时长必须以 Host 单调时钟为准

    Recorded frame rate is 16.4 Hz and command-sequence send rate is 20.3 Hz (below the nominal 30 Hz demo rate); dividing frame count by 30 would compress a 31.5 s rollout to 17.2 s. Future timing comparisons should log chunk compute and queue reads separately.

    录制覆盖率 16.4 帧/s、按命令序号估计的发送频率 20.3 Hz（低于示教名义 30 Hz）；按帧数除以 30 会将 31.5 s 压缩成 17.2 s，后续需将动作块推理起止与队列取步分开计时。
