动作与执行分析Action and execution analysis

离线动作误差Offline action error

以下指标使用115 条示教、10k 步模型，在六条验证示教中每条选择 32 个观察，共进行 192 次独立查询，随机种子为 0。每次预测后续 32 步，去掉 episode 末尾越界部分后，每个坐标累计 5,891 个有效预测步。窗口可以重叠，这些预测步不等于 5,891 个独立样本。基线在整个预测窗口保持查询时的当前状态。

The 115-demonstration, 10k-step model is queried at 32 observations from each of six validation demonstrations: 192 queries with seed 0. Each predicts 32 future steps. Removing steps beyond episode ends leaves 5,891 valid predicted steps per coordinate. Windows may overlap, so these are not 5,891 independent samples. The baseline holds the observed state constant throughout each window.

12 个臂关节的平均 MAE 从保持状态基线的 9.406 降至 4.192，降低 55.4%。改善主要来自肩抬和肘部：左肩抬从 25.711 降至 7.614，左肘从 22.443 降至 7.597；右侧对应两项从 17.243、14.213 降至 5.676、5.381。这些关节承担了本组搬运动作中的大幅变化，模型的优势首先体现在预测这种变化，而不只是维持当前姿态。

Mean MAE across twelve arm joints falls from 9.406 for the hold-state baseline to 4.192, a 55.4% reduction. Shoulder lift and elbow account for the strongest gains: the left pair improves from 25.711 and 22.443 to 7.614 and 7.597; the right pair improves from 17.243 and 14.213 to 5.676 and 5.381. These joints carry large changes during transfers, showing that the model predicts motion beyond maintaining the current pose.

9 个关节优于基线，左腕 yaw、左腕 roll 与右腕 yaw 略差。整体均值因此不能代表每个动作通道都同等受益。双夹爪误差也明显低于保持状态，但抓取与投放发生在少数开合转换时刻，后续评估还应检查这些转换是否出现在正确位置。

Nine joints improve over the baseline; left wrist yaw/roll and right wrist yaw are slightly worse. The aggregate gain is therefore uneven across coordinates. Both grippers also improve substantially, but grasping and deposit depend on sparse opening/closing transitions whose timing and location need to be evaluated separately.

预测时域Prediction horizon

12 个臂关节在窗口首步的 MAE 为 1.984，保持状态基线为 3.192；到第 32 步分别增至 5.953 与 15.079。模型在远端预测上仍优于保持状态，但误差随预测距离增加。左右夹爪也呈现相同趋势：首步 MAE 为 2.735、3.394，末步增至 6.554、7.291。

At the first predicted step, joint MAE is 1.984 versus 3.192 for holding state; at step 32 it reaches 5.953 versus 15.079. The model remains better at distant predictions, while errors grow with prediction distance. Left/right gripper MAEs similarly rise from 2.735/3.394 at the first step to 6.554/7.291 at the last.

这些误差来自同一次观察条件下不同未来位置的预测，尚未经过实机执行后再观察的反馈过程。本组每次执行全部 32 步，预测尾部也会进入控制队列。比较较短执行窗口时，应同时测量目标选择、松爪时机、计算等待与完成时间：较早重观察减少了执行远端预测的长度，也增加了新预测的调用频率。

These are predictions at different future positions from one observation, before execution and subsequent feedback. This group executes all 32 steps, including the prediction tail. A shorter execution-window comparison should measure destination choice, release timing, computation waits, and completion time together: earlier observation reduces the length of distant predictions executed while increasing requests for fresh predictions.

离线动作误差与颜色误投衡量不同层面的结果。这里的目标是示教轨迹，MAE 按动作坐标和预测步平均；视频中的误投则取决于整段搬运最后落在哪个篮子。对补充数据模型，应保留同一验证划分比较动作拟合，同时在颜色与位置组合测试中直接统计正确投放，避免只用一个均值评价任务完成。

Offline action error and wrong-basket deposits describe different outcomes. MAE compares demonstration trajectories averaged across coordinates and predicted steps, while the video error concerns the destination reached by a complete transfer. The expanded-data model should keep the validation split for action fitting and directly score correct deposits in the crossed color/position tests, rather than using one mean to represent task completion.

双臂轨迹Arm trajectories

以下轨迹使用 11 月 3 日的固定底盘评估记录，模型训练条件同为 115 条示教、10k 步。共保存 517 帧，Host 单调时钟覆盖 31.520 s。曲线显示实际提交的动作与请求前反馈；这份控制记录独立于前面的手机拍摄片段。

The following traces use the November 3 fixed-base evaluation of the same 115-demonstration, 10k-step model: 517 frames over 31.520 s of Host monotonic time. Curves show submitted actions and pre-request feedback. This control record is separate from the phone recordings above.

肩抬和肘部形成多段连续的大幅运动，夹爪在其中完成开合转换。右臂约 20 s 后回到接近初始的肩肘位置，左臂约 26 s 后回到相近位置；两臂动作并未在同一时刻结束。这些曲线说明评估中实际执行了持续的搬运轨迹，而非仅输出一组静态抓取姿态。

Shoulder and elbow traces contain several large continuous movements with intervening gripper transitions. The right arm returns near its initial shoulder/elbow positions after roughly 20 s, and the left after roughly 26 s; the two arms do not finish simultaneously. Execution therefore includes sustained transfer trajectories rather than a single static grasp pose.

夹爪指令与反馈存在成段分离。左夹爪在约 7.54–9.86 s、19.90–22.28 s 处于 holding，右夹爪对应 5.06–7.94 s、13.82–16.96 s。此时闭合指令仍接近零，反馈保持非零开口，与 Host 的夹持保持状态一致。对这类阶段，应结合夹持状态解释开口差值；把指令与反馈的距离统一算作位置跟踪失败，会把夹持保持状态本身计入误差。

Gripper commands and feedback separate over sustained intervals. Host reports left holding at approximately 7.54–9.86 s and 19.90–22.28 s, and right holding at 5.06–7.94 s and 13.82–16.96 s. Closing commands remain near zero while feedback retains a nonzero opening, consistent with the holding state. The opening difference should therefore be interpreted with grip state; treating every command/feedback separation as position-tracking failure would count the holding state itself as an error.

生成指令也出现少量超出标定范围的值：左肘 11 帧高于 100，最高 103.374；左右夹爪分别有 13、20 帧低于零，最低 −0.699、−1.108。这些是提交请求中的数值，不能据此推断关节实际越过限位。它们说明平均动作误差下降后，输出范围仍需由部署接口检查；后续对照应保留限幅前后指令，分开记录生成误差和控制器处理。

A few generated requests exceed calibrated ranges: left elbow exceeds 100 in 11 frames, reaching 103.374; left/right grippers fall below zero in 13/20 frames, reaching −0.699/−1.108. These are request values, not evidence of physical limit violations. Lower average action error still leaves output-range checks to deployment. Subsequent comparisons should retain commands before and after limiting to distinguish generation errors from controller handling.

右臂抓取姿态Right-arm grasp orientation

姿态分析使用 11 月 2 日另一段固定底盘评估，共 4938 帧，同样采用 115 条示教微调至 10k 步的模型。该记录中左右腕字段保持独立，固定覆盖只作用于底盘三维速度。按同一闭合片段筛选得到的右臂末端倾角中位数，指令侧约 73.0°、反馈侧约 72.1°；向下抓取的姿态已经出现在下发目标中。

Orientation analysis uses a separate fixed-base evaluation from November 2, containing 4,938 frames from the same model fine-tuned on the 115-demonstration dataset to 10k steps. Left/right wrist fields remain independent, and fixed overrides affect only the three base velocities. The selected closing segments give right-arm median downward angles of approximately 73.0° for commanded poses and 72.1° for feedback poses: the downward orientation is already present in the submitted targets.

从训练示教中选取 10 个状态、每个使用 3 个随机种子离线生成动作，可以预测出原记录中的低腕姿态与压腕变化。其中一次 32 步窗口的右腕屈伸目标从 42.8 降至 15.0，模型三次预测的均值从 41.6 降至 17.3。这里使用 Host 标定后的关节坐标，数值不表示角度；这些查询用于检查模型在示教观察下能否产生相应动作。

Offline generation from ten selected demonstration states, with three random seeds each, reproduces low-wrist poses and wrist-flex transitions. In one 32-step window, the demonstrated wrist-flex target falls from 42.8 to 15.0, while the three-seed prediction mean falls from 41.6 to 17.3. These are calibrated Host joint coordinates, not angles. The queries test whether the corresponding actions can be generated from demonstration observations.

倾角统计先寻找夹爪指令由大于 35 转为小于 15 的闭合事件，取闭合前最多 11 个采样点；用标定后的 FK 计算工具朝向，保留工具高度中位数低于 0.51 m 的桌面附近候选片段。高度采用机器人模型基座坐标和固定升降零参考。示教左、右臂分别得到 179、215 个候选，实机得到 19、33 个；它们没有经过人工成功抓取标注，物体位置与场景分布也不相同。

The angle analysis detects gripper commands transitioning from above 35 to below 15 and considers up to 11 samples preceding closure. Calibrated FK estimates tool orientation, retaining tabletop-near candidates with median tool height below 0.51 m in the model base frame with a fixed lift-zero reference. This yields 179/215 left/right candidates from demonstrations and 19/33 from the live rollout. They are not manually labeled successful grasps, and object placements and scene distributions differ.

左臂倾角中位数为示教 80.49°、实机 80.42°，基本一致；右臂由 67.34° 变为 72.64°，增加 5.30°，两臂中位数差从 13.15° 缩小到 7.78°。这支持实机右臂更偏向俯抓的观察。右臂示教自身的 P10–P90 为 47.92°–76.94°，说明原数据包含较宽的姿态范围；实机中位数仍处于该范围内，差异主要体现在所选姿态的分布。

Left-arm medians are nearly unchanged: 80.49° in demonstrations and 80.42° live. The right-arm median rises from 67.34° to 72.64°, a 5.30° increase; the left–right median gap narrows from 13.15° to 7.78°. This supports the observed shift toward a more downward right-arm grasp. Demonstrated right-arm angles already span 47.92°–76.94° at P10–P90, and the live median remains within that range; the difference concerns the distribution of selected orientations.

离线回放和实机倾角分别检查了两个环节：示教观察下的动作生成，以及连续执行中实际进入的姿态。前者能生成压腕，不保证后者以相同频率选择这些轨迹。当前策略根据图像、任务和状态预测目标，没有强制右臂采用某个接近角的约束。曝光、初始姿态与执行节奏应在同一物体布局下分别控制，再比较倾角分布、抓取完成率和目标篮子正确率。切换到 80 维末端接口会让朝向监督更直接，但具体抓取姿态仍需由数据、目标或部署约束定义。

Offline replay tests action generation from demonstration observations; live angles describe the poses reached during sequential execution. Reproducing a wrist transition offline does not guarantee that the same trajectories will be selected with equal frequency online. The policy predicts targets from images, task text, and state, without enforcing a particular right-arm approach angle. Exposure, initial pose, and execution timing should be controlled separately under matched object layouts, measuring angle distributions, grasp completion, and basket correctness. An 80-coordinate TCP interface makes orientation supervision explicit, while the desired grasp orientation still needs to be defined by data, objectives, or deployment constraints.

执行时序Execution timing

517 次策略调用的中位数为 5.51 ms，P95 为 6.84 ms；其中 18 次超过 100 ms，这些慢调用的中位数为 513.39 ms，首次调用为 991.17 ms。慢调用仅占次数的 3.5%，却占总调用耗时的 77.8%，因此总体 P95 也未覆盖主要等待开销。

Across 517 policy calls, median duration is 5.51 ms and P95 is 6.84 ms. Eighteen calls exceed 100 ms, with a median of 513.39 ms; the first call takes 991.17 ms. These slow calls are only 3.5% of calls but account for 77.8% of total call time, so even the overall P95 omits the dominant waiting cost.

策略接口既负责生成动作块，也负责从已有队列取出单步动作。两类工作共用同一耗时字段，5.51 ms 不能作为一次完整模型推理的延迟。当前记录没有独立的动作块计算标记，下一轮应把观察采样、模型计算起止、队列长度和发送时间分别记录，再比较执行窗口带来的重规划收益与等待开销。

The policy interface both generates action chunks and dequeues individual actions. Those operations share one timing field, so 5.51 ms does not describe a complete model inference. The record lacks separate chunk-computation markers. The next run should log observation time, model start/end, queue length, and transmission time to compare replanning gains against waiting costs across execution windows.

本段录制覆盖率为 16.37 帧/s，按同一会话命令序号变化估计的发送频率为 20.27 Hz，均不同于示教数据的名义 30 Hz。按录制帧数除以 30 得到的 17.23 s 会压缩实际 31.52 s 的过程。分析动作持续时间和两次操作之间的间隔时，需沿用 Host 时间；记录中未保存每一条命令，命令序号间隙也不等于控制请求丢失。

The record captures 16.37 frames/s; command-sequence changes within the session estimate transmission at 20.27 Hz. Both differ from the demonstrations’ nominal 30 Hz. Dividing recorded frames by 30 compresses a 31.52 s process to 17.23 s. Motion durations and intervals therefore use Host time. Not every command is recorded, so sequence gaps do not establish dropped control requests.
