预训练范围Scope of pretraining

OpenWAM 继承视觉生成模型的表示与动力学先验。图像和视频经 Wan VAE 压缩为视觉 latent，任务指令经 umT5 编码为文本条件；动作与本体状态通过独立的数值投影进入网络。具身预训练冻结 VAE 与 T5，更新相互连接的视频和动作 DiT。[4]

OpenWAM inherits representations and dynamics priors from visual generation. Wan VAE compresses images/video into visual latents, umT5 encodes task instructions, and separate numeric projections encode actions and proprioception. Embodied pretraining freezes VAE and T5 while updating connected video and action DiTs. [4]

人类视频提供视觉变化监督，没有机器人动作与状态标签的通道被屏蔽；机器人示教为有效动作槽提供监督。由此形成的基座同时包含视觉与时间特征，以及与动作物理含义相关的输入输出映射。迁移到新机器人时，需要分别检查这些模块的形状和数据含义。

Human video supervises visual changes, with unlabeled robot action/state channels masked. Robot demonstrations supervise valid action slots. The resulting foundation includes visual and temporal features as well as input/output mappings tied to physical actions. Adapting it to another robot requires checking both module shapes and data meanings.

接口适配Interface adaptation

线性层或 MLP 是否包含预训练权重，由预训练参数的加载方式决定。当前 18 维适配在导入官方基座时，明确丢弃动作输入、动作输出、状态输入三个投影的 weight 和 bias，然后训练新投影；DiT 内部同形状参数继续加载。三个新投影共 115,730 个参数：

Whether a Linear layer or MLP contains pretrained weights depends on checkpoint loading. The 18-coordinate adapter explicitly discards the weight and bias of the action input, action output, and state input projections when importing the foundation checkpoint. Compatible DiT parameters are retained. The three new projections contain 115,730 parameters:

$$(18\cdot1024+1024)+(1024\cdot18+18)+(18\cdot4096+4096)=115{,}730.$$

输入投影的每一列作用于一个动作量，输出投影的每一行对应一个预测量。由末端字段改为关节字段后，这些位置的物理含义和矩阵形状随之改变，需要重新建立映射；DiT 主干仍接收同样宽度的隐藏特征，因此可继续加载原有参数。

Each input-projection column acts on one action quantity, and each output-projection row corresponds to one predicted quantity. Replacing TCP fields with joints changes both their meanings and matrix shapes, requiring a new mapping. The DiT backbone still receives the same hidden width, allowing its parameters to load unchanged.

关节空间微调Joint-space fine-tuning

更换动作投影后，训练目标变成根据图像、语言和当前状态预测未来关节序列。新投影学习关节坐标与隐藏特征之间的映射，DiT 内部参数同时调整；这由完整的非线性网络共同完成，单个线性投影并不承担 FK 或 IK。视觉编码器保留物体与场景特征，视频和动作分支保留生成时序结构，训练不必从随机特征重新建立整个控制模型。

After replacing the projections, the objective predicts future joint sequences from images, language, and current state. New projections and DiT parameters adapt jointly through the complete nonlinear network; a single linear projection does not perform FK or IK. Visual encoders retain object/scene features, and video/action branches retain temporal generative structure; the entire controller need not be rebuilt from random features.

在固定机器人和有限工作空间中，示教覆盖的关节轨迹与末端运动具有稳定关系，微调可以沿这一分布建立新的动作映射。这提供了关节表示能够有效学习的机制。预训练究竟减少了多少数据需求、改善了多少泛化能力，需要与相同训练预算下的随机初始化模型比较。

For one robot in a bounded workspace, demonstrated joint trajectories have a stable relationship with TCP motion, allowing fine-tuning to learn a new action mapping along that distribution. This explains a route to effective joint-space learning. Quantifying the data-efficiency or generalization gains from pretraining requires a random-initialization control under the same training budget.

末端空间微调TCP-space fine-tuning

末端空间微调进一步保留输入输出字段的物理含义。动作与状态继续使用官方 80 维宽度，因此可以连同原始投影加载基座。关节数据在进入网络前经 FK 转换，预测出的末端动作在执行前经 IK 恢复为关节目标，几何转换由标定接口承担。[13]

TCP-space fine-tuning additionally preserves the physical meanings of input/output fields. Keeping the official 80-coordinate width allows the original projections to load with the foundation. FK converts joints before the network, and IK recovers joint targets from predicted TCP actions; calibrated interfaces perform the geometric conversion. [13]

训练和部署保存同一份关节标定、参考系、工具帧与归一化统计。无效槽不参与动作损失；底盘与升降的预留槽含义由 AlohaMini 适配声明。保持槽位顺序只解决字段对齐，坐标变换和物理单位仍须一致。

Training and deployment share joint calibration, reference/tool frames, and normalization statistics. Invalid slots are excluded from action loss, and the project explicitly declares base/lift meanings in reserved slots. Matching indices aligns fields; coordinate transforms and physical units must also agree.

部署时先取回有效槽并反归一化，再把旋转 6D 恢复成合法姿态，以当前关节反馈为起点连续求解 IK。夹爪、底盘与升降走各自的物理接口，不参与手臂 IK。退化旋转、不可达目标和越过关节限位的动作块会被拒绝。FK/IK 路径把机械几何约束移到确定性的接口中，也引入了可达性、解支连续性与标定误差这些新的部署因素。

Deployment gathers valid slots, denormalizes them, reconstructs a rotation, and solves IK continuously from current joint feedback. Gripper, base, and lift use their own physical interfaces outside arm IK. Degenerate rotations, unreachable targets, and joint-limit violations reject the action chunk. FK/IK moves mechanical geometry into a deterministic interface while introducing deployment concerns around reachability, solution continuity, and calibration error.
