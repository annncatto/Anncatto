OpenWAM carries 1.80× more trainable parameters than full-FT π0.5 (6.021B out of 12.406B vs 3.353B out of 3.617B), so its speed advantage over the two main π0.5 runs comes from the compute path below rather than smaller parameter count.

OpenWAM 总参 12.406B、可训练 6.021B，约为全参 π0.5（总参 3.617B、可训练 3.353B）的 1.80 倍；因此它在前两组对比中的速度优势并非来自参数更少，而来自下节的三项计算路径差异。

计算路径Compute path

  1. VAE compression cuts OpenWAM’s backbone sequence to 392 tokens (vs 1,274 / 1,018)VAE 时空压缩将 OpenWAM 主干序列缩至 392 位置（对比 1,274 / 1,018）

    OpenWAM packs three cameras × 9 frames into a 384×320 canvas; the causal VAE compresses this to 3 × 24 × 20 latents and 2×2 patches yield 360 video tokens + 32 action tokens = 392 positions. π0.5 encodes each 224×224 view into 256 patches (14×14), plus 200 text/state and 50 action tokens, totaling 1,274 (4-cam) or 1,018 (3-cam) positions.

    OpenWAM 将三视图 9 帧拼入 384×320 画布，经 VAE 压缩为 3 × 24 × 20 latent 再按 2×2 分块得到 360 视频 token + 32 动作 token，共 392 位置；π0.5 每路 224×224 产生 256 视觉 token，叠加 200 文本/状态与 50 动作位置，四/三相机分别达 1,274 / 1,018 位置。

  2. Single 8-sample microbatch reduces FSDP2 gather/communication passes单次大微批减少 FSDP2 收集与通信频次

    With activation checkpointing trading compute for memory, OpenWAM runs 8 samples/GPU in one pass, whereas the two main π0.5 runs disable checkpointing and accumulate 4 passes of 2 samples/GPU—multiplying per-module FSDP2 parameter gathers, reshards, and gradient communications [16].

    OpenWAM 开启梯度重计算换取显存，每卡 8 样本 1 次完成前后向；两组 π0.5 主对照关闭重计算、每卡 2 样本需累积 4 次，成倍增加了 FSDP2 按模块收集/重分片与梯度通信次数 [16]。

  3. Full BF16 storage and PyTorch SDPA lower attention memory traffic全 BF16 与 PyTorch SDPA 降低注意力计算与访存开销

    OpenWAM stores all parameters in BF16 with BF16 autocast and dispatches attention via PyTorch SDPA; this π0.5 run uses mixed_precision=none (96.7% BF16 / 3.3% FP32 storage) and materializes the joint attention matrix and softmax explicitly [16].

    OpenWAM 全量采用 BF16 存储与 autocast 并调用 PyTorch SDPA；该版本 π0.5 配置为 mixed_precision=none（96.69% BF16 + 3.31% FP32）且显式计算联合注意力矩阵与 softmax，进一步拉大单步访存与计算开销 [16]。
