π0.7, §III

The likelihood treats the density assigned to a demonstrated action as a function of the parameters. Maximum likelihood estimation fits those parameters so the demonstrations become more plausible under their conditions. Logarithms turn products across samples into sums. Continuous actions use probability densities.

似然将模型为示范动作赋予的密度，看作参数的函数。最大似然估计拟合这些参数，让示范动作在对应条件下更可能出现；对数将跨样本的乘积变为求和。连续动作使用概率密度。

Other policies also fit this view. Categorical cross-entropy is negative log-likelihood for discrete actions; an L1 objective corresponds to a fixed-scale Laplace observation model. The main difference is how the action distribution is represented and trained. A flow model transforms noise into an action chunk through a learned vector field, allowing several plausible trajectories for the same observation.

其他策略也能放进这个视角。离散动作的交叉熵就是负对数似然，L1 则可以对应尺度固定的 Laplace 观测模型。区别主要在于如何表示和训练动作分布。流模型通过学到的向量场，将噪声变成动作块，使同一观测能够对应多种合理轨迹。

In a noise-to-data convention, $\tau=0$ is Gaussian noise and $\tau=1$ is the demonstrated action. For $\epsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$, linearly interpolate the whole action chunk and regress the velocity of that path:

从噪声到数据的约定中，$\tau=0$ 对应高斯噪声，$\tau=1$ 对应示范动作。采样 $\epsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$，对整个动作块做线性插值，再回归这条路径的速度：

$$\begin{aligned}x_\tau&=(1-\tau)\epsilon+\tau A_t,\\\mathcal L_{\mathrm{CFM}}&=\mathbb E_{A_t,\epsilon,\tau}\!\left[\left\|v_\theta(x_\tau,\tau;O_t,C_t)-(A_t-\epsilon)\right\|_2^2\right].\end{aligned}$$

The target $A_t-\epsilon$ is known, so training requires a sampled time, an interpolated input, and one vector-field prediction. At inference, start at noise and integrate $dx_\tau/d\tau=v_\theta(x_\tau,\tau;O_t,C_t)$ toward the action endpoint. The flow time $\tau$ describes generation; the index inside $A_t$ describes physical execution. Those are independent time axes. Reversing the interpolation convention reverses the target’s sign but not the underlying construction. Flow Matching for Generative Modeling · π0

目标 $A_t-\epsilon$ 已知，训练只需要采样一个时间、构造插值输入，再预测向量场。推理时从噪声出发，沿 $dx_\tau/d\tau=v_\theta(x_\tau,\tau;O_t,C_t)$ 积分到动作端点。流时间 $\tau$ 描述生成过程，$A_t$ 内部的下标描述真实执行，两者是独立的时间轴。若反过来定义插值方向，目标的符号也要相应改变，方法本身不变。Flow Matching for Generative Modeling（生成建模中的流匹配） · π0

An exact flow density is less convenient than a categorical softmax. It generally requires following the continuous transformation and accounting for volume expansion or contraction. For a smooth invertible flow, the change-of-variables relation is:

流模型的精确密度不像分类 softmax 那样容易直接得到。一般需要追踪连续变换，以及变换中体积的扩张和收缩。对光滑、可逆的流，变量变换关系为：

$$\log p_1(A_t\mid O_t,C_t)=\log p_0(x_0)-\int_0^1\nabla_x\!\cdot v_\theta(x_\tau,\tau;O_t,C_t)\,d\tau.$$

Here the trajectory ends at the observed action, and its noise endpoint must be obtained through the inverse flow. Evaluating the integral requires an ODE solve and divergence calculations. Conditional flow matching avoids that computation during training by fitting tractable conditional paths instead.

这条轨迹以观测到的动作为终点，噪声端点需要通过逆向流求出；积分还涉及 ODE 求解与散度计算。条件流匹配在训练时改为拟合容易构造的条件路径，省去了这套密度计算。

The paper’s “lower bound” language comes from the connection to latent-path diffusion objectives. Introducing an auxiliary path $Z$ and a distribution $q$ gives the variational relation below; for suitable paths and weights, its parameter-dependent terms become denoising or vector-field regression losses:

论文所说的“下界”，来自它与带潜在路径的扩散目标之间的联系。引入辅助路径 $Z$ 与分布 $q$，有下面的变分关系；在合适的路径和权重下，其中依赖参数的部分可以化为去噪或向量场回归损失：

$$\log p_\theta(A\mid O,C)\geq\mathbb E_{q(Z\mid A,O,C)}\!\left[\log\frac{p_\theta(A,Z\mid O,C)}{q(Z\mid A,O,C)}\right].$$

The relation between a surrogate loss and log-likelihood depends on the path, weighting, and probabilistic construction. π0.7 trains the continuous expert with flow matching and the backbone with discrete prediction.

代理损失与对数似然之间的对应关系，由路径、权重和概率构造决定。π0.7 使用流匹配训练连续动作专家，使用离散预测训练主干。

The role of a richer prompt can now be stated precisely. If $m$ is omitted, different execution styles share the mixture $p(A\mid O,\ell)=\sum_m p(A\mid O,\ell,m)p(m\mid O,\ell)$. Supplying $m$ makes the desired component addressable. A flexible generative model can preserve multiple modes, but conditions are still needed to select which behavior is wanted. This connects the loss to the dataset rather than treating flow matching alone as the source of generalization.

更丰富的 prompt 也就有了明确的作用。省略 $m$ 时，不同执行方式进入同一个混合分布：$p(A\mid O,\ell)=\sum_m p(A\mid O,\ell,m)p(m\mid O,\ell)$；提供 $m$ 后，才有条件指定其中想要的行为。生成模型能够保留多峰，条件则用于选择行为。这将损失与数据组织接了起来，泛化不再被简单归因于流匹配本身。

π0.7 Overview

Our previous models, π0, π0.5, and π0.6, use a short textual description of the task as the context. In training π0.7, we expand the context to include additional information and modalities: more expressive language commands, episode metadata, and subgoal images, making it possible to train on diverse and potentially suboptimal data.

此前的 π0、π0.5 和 π0.6，以简短的任务文本描述作为上下文。训练 π0.7 时，我们扩展了上下文，加入表达更丰富的语言指令、执行元数据和子目标图像，从而能够使用多样、可能次优的数据进行训练。

π0.7, §IV

The system has three roles: a high-level semantic policy proposes the next subtask, a world model depicts its visual outcome, and the VLA produces executable actions. The VLA itself has about 5B parameters: a Gemma3 4B backbone, including its 400M vision encoder, plus an 860M action expert. The BAGEL-based goal generator is a separate 14B model; it is not included in that 5B count. §IV

系统分成三个角色：高层语义策略提出下一项子任务，世界模型描绘对应的视觉结果，VLA 生成可执行动作。VLA 本身约 5B 参数，由 Gemma3 4B 主干和 860M 动作专家组成；400M 视觉编码器已经包含在主干参数量中。基于 BAGEL 的目标生成器是独立的 14B 模型，不计入这 5B。§IV

During training, observations and recorded actions are paired with descriptions of the behavior. At runtime, the context is assembled from desired metadata, a subtask supplied by the high-level policy or a human, and optionally a generated goal image. The low-level policy is shared across tasks; the conditions determine which part of its experience is relevant.

训练时，观测与已记录的动作，配上描述这段行为的条件。运行时，再由期望的元数据、高层策略或人工给出的子任务，以及可选的生成目标图组成上下文。低层策略在任务间共享，条件决定调用哪部分经验。

$$C_t=(\ell,\hat\ell_t,g_t,m,c),\qquad A_t\sim\pi_\theta(A\mid O_t,C_t).$$

$\ell$ is the overall task, $\hat\ell_t$ the current subtask, $g_t$ the multi-view goal, $m$ the execution metadata, and $c$ the control mode. Recent history explains how the scene has been changing; the prompt explains where it should go and how the task should be performed.

$\ell$ 是整体任务，$\hat\ell_t$ 是当前子任务，$g_t$ 是多视角目标图，$m$ 是执行元数据，$c$ 是控制模式。近期历史说明场景如何变化，prompt 则说明场景应达到的下一阶段，以及任务应该以什么方式完成。

Diversifying the Prompt

Subtask instructions

An overall task can remain unchanged while the required action reverses: before putting food into a microwave, open it; afterward, close it. The current subtask provides the missing phase information. Training segments receive detailed language labels, tying local actions to a specific intent instead of asking the model to infer the whole plan from a short task name.

整体任务没有变化，所需动作却可能相反：把食物放进微波炉之前要开门，之后要关门。当前子任务补上了阶段信息。训练片段配有详细语言标签，将局部动作与明确意图对应起来，而不必只凭一个简短任务名猜测整套流程。

The hierarchy continues π0.5: the high-level model generates the next instruction, and the low-level policy learns its connection to control. Annotated subtasks serve as training conditions and runtime interfaces. Human coaching and autonomous sequencing use this same interface.

这延续了 π0.5 的层级结构：高层模型生成下一条指令，低层策略学习它与控制的对应关系。带标注的子任务同时充当训练条件与运行接口，人工指导和自主步骤排序都通过这个接口调用动作。

Subgoal images

“Open the fridge” names the interaction but leaves the contact geometry vague. A base-view goal can show the door’s target configuration; wrist-view goals can show the gripper relative to the handle. The multi-view goal $g_t=(G_t^1,\ldots,G_t^n)$ supplies these spatial details as images.

“打开冰箱”说明了交互，却没有说清接触关系。主视角目标可以展示冰箱门应该打开到什么状态，腕部目标则展示夹爪与把手的关系。多视角目标 $g_t=(G_t^1,\ldots,G_t^n)$ 用图像补上这些空间细节。

For the goal generator, a well-annotated subtask segment supplies a training pair: current observation and instruction as input, segment-end camera images as the target $g_t^*=I_{t_{\mathrm{end}}}^{1:n}$. Its horizon follows the duration of the subtask segment. At runtime, those future images are unavailable, so the world model generates a goal from the current observation:

目标生成器的训练样本来自标注较好的子任务片段：输入当前观测与指令，目标是片段结束时的各相机图像 $g_t^*=I_{t_{\mathrm{end}}}^{1:n}$。目标的时间跨度由子任务片段的长度决定。运行时无法提前获得这些图像，就由世界模型根据当前观测生成目标：

Episode metadata

A key goal in expanding the context provided to the model is to train on a broader, more diverse dataset of trajectories. Instead of just using high-quality demonstration data, π0.7 leverages lower quality demonstrations (including failures) and even autonomous data from prior models. Since we still want π0.7 to perform the task as well as possible at test time, we need to appropriately label these diverse trajectories with information about how the task was performed so that the model can correctly contextualize them. Full prompt and training details

During training, we randomly drop out each part of the prompt, which provides π0.7 with the flexibility to use any subset of the prompt components at test time (e.g., running with or without subgoal images). First, we find that the model trains significantly faster when given the subgoal images — the action prediction task essentially becomes an “inverse dynamics” problem inferring the robot action between the current and future frames. Thus we only add the visual subgoal images to 25% of the examples in each batch in training. Among the examples with subgoal images we also drop out the subtask instruction $\hat\ell_t$ 30% of the time as often visual subgoal can substitute the equivalent textual subtask description in richer details. For episode metadata, we drop it entirely 15% of the time, and additionally each component (overall speed, overall quality, and mistake label) is dropped with 5% probability individually. We do not apply dropout for the control mode.

训练时随机丢弃 prompt 的各个部分，使 π0.7 在测试时能够灵活使用这些组件的任意子集，例如使用或不使用子目标图像。首先，我们发现提供子目标图像时，模型训练明显更快：动作预测近似成为根据当前帧与未来帧推断动作的“逆动力学”问题。因此，每个 batch 只在 25% 的样本中加入视觉子目标。在带目标图的样本中，还以 30% 的概率丢弃子任务指令 $\hat\ell_t$，因为视觉目标通常能以更丰富的细节替代对应的文本子任务。执行元数据以 15% 的概率整体丢弃，另外每个组件，即整体速度、整体质量和错误标签，分别以 5% 的概率丢弃。控制模式不使用 dropout。

Training datasets

The training mixture spans static and mobile robots, single and dual arms, laboratory and home environments, teleoperation, autonomous evaluation rollouts, human interventions, open robot datasets, and egocentric human video. 早期策略产生的经验尤其重要。π*0.6 强化学习期间收集的 rollout，会成为 π0.7 的训练样本。这是一种通过数据完成的行为蒸馏：通用模型拟合专用策略的执行，包括它访问的状态与纠偏动作，从而继承能力。元数据将成功、缓慢和失败的轨迹区分开，不把所有 rollout 都当成同样理想的行为。

Spatial layers share information across the image; temporal layers carry that information through time. The VLM subsequently fuses the compressed camera streams. A current-frame feature can therefore contain earlier visual evidence even after the earlier tokens have been removed.

空间层在画面内部交换信息，时间层将信息沿历史传递，VLM 随后融合各相机的压缩表示。因此，即使较早的 tokens 已被移除，当前帧特征仍能携带过去的视觉证据。

The video encoder interleaves spatial attention with temporal attention every fourth ViT layer. With $K$ frames and $N$ patches per frame, factorization reduces the attention interaction count from $O(K^2N^2)$ to $O(KN^2+NK^2)$. Past-frame tokens are dropped in upper layers after they have contributed to the current representation, so the VLM receives the same token count as for one frame. History is compressed into features rather than simply discarded. Modified attention patterns and fixed temporal embeddings reuse the pretrained ViT weights without adding learnable parameters. The major architectural modifications of π0.7 model compared to previous π0.5 and π0.6 models include the use of the history vision encoder from MEM [37] and visual subgoal images in the context. 相对 π0.5 和 π0.6，π0.7 架构的主要修改是采用 MEM [37] 的历史视觉编码器，并在上下文加入视觉子目标。模型最多接收四个相机视角：前视、两个腕部视角，以及可选的后视，每个相机最多六帧历史；还可以接收最多三张子目标图像，不包含后视。历史帧经过视觉编码器，压缩到与单帧相同的 token 数量，子目标图像也由同一个编码器处理。相机观测和目标图像都先缩放到 448 × 448。历史帧采样间隔为一秒，整组历史帧以 0.3 概率丢弃，可用的后视图像也以 0.3 概率丢弃。

The proprioceptive vector $q_t$ describes the robot’s own configuration, such as joint positions. Force and touch require additional sensor channels. A linear map $e_t=W_q q_t+b_q$ produces one backbone-width state token per sampled time. Unlike representing every joint value as text, this keeps state-history token count proportional to the number of observations. Dropping a history frame also masks the matching state token.

本体状态 $q_t$ 描述机器人自身的配置，例如关节位置，力与触觉需要额外的传感器通道。线性映射 $e_t=W_q q_t+b_q$ 将每个采样时刻的状态变成一个与主干等宽的 token。相比将每个关节数值编码成文本，状态历史的 token 数量只随观测次数增长。丢弃某个历史画面时，也会掩掉对应的状态 token。

Each Gemma block updates the whole token sequence through attention, then a gated feed-forward network. With $N_1,\ldots,N_4$ denoting RMSNorm operations, its residual computation is:

$$\begin{aligned}\tilde X^{(l)}&=X^{(l)}+N_2\!\left(\operatorname{Attn}_l(N_1(X^{(l)}))\right),\\X^{(l+1)}&=\tilde X^{(l)}+N_4\!\left(\operatorname{FFN}_l(N_3(\tilde X^{(l)}))\right),\\\operatorname{FFN}(z)&=\bigl(\operatorname{GELU}(zW_g)\odot zW_u\bigr)W_d.\end{aligned}$$

Attention mixes information between token positions. The FFN transforms each position’s feature channels with shared weights, and residual connections carry the existing representation into the next block. Gemma 3 uses a GELU-gated FFN, with separate projections for its gate and value path.

注意力在 token 位置之间交换信息，FFN 则用共享权重变换每个位置的特征通道；残差连接将已有表示带到下一层。Gemma 3 使用 GELU 门控 FFN，门控与数值路径分别投影。

Grouped-query attention shares each key/value head between two query heads. For the 4B configuration, $W_Q$ maps width 2560 to $8\times256=2048$, while $W_K$ and $W_V$ each map to $4\times256=1024$. Grouped-query attention 让两个查询头共享一组键值头。4B 配置中，$W_Q$ 将 2560 维映射到 $8\times256=2048$ 维，$W_K$ 与 $W_V$ 分别映射到 $4\times256=1024$ 维，注意力结果再投影回 2560 维。QK normalization 稳定查询与键的尺度，RoPE 按 token 位置旋转它们，再计算点积。

我们采用块因果掩码：观测 tokens 与子目标图像 tokens 在各自内部使用双向注意力，目标图像 tokens 还可以读取观测。后续文本 tokens 使用因果注意力，注意力掩码可视化见附录。机器人的本体状态 $q_t$，包括历史状态，也输入模型主干。π0.6 将 $q_t$ 表示为离散文本 tokens；π0.7 则沿用 MEM，以线性投影将状态维度映射到主干维度。每个历史状态对应一个独立 token；如果历史帧被丢弃，对应状态 token 也会被掩掉。

The attention mask controls information access. For query tokens $Q$ reading keys and values $K,V$, the scaled dot-product scores pass through softmax. The mask adds zero at permitted positions and negative infinity at blocked positions:

注意力掩码决定能读取什么。 查询 $Q$ 读取键值 $K,V$ 时，softmax 计算前，允许的位置加零，禁止的位置加负无穷：

$$\operatorname{Attention}(Q,K,V)=\operatorname{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt d}+M\right)V,\qquad M_{ij}\in\{0,-\infty\}.$$

In a causal text block, token $i$ can read earlier tokens and itself, while later tokens are masked. A bidirectional image block allows every token in that block to read every other token. Training with subgoal images

When training π0.7 to handle subgoal images, we need the model to accommodate goals with different delays and different levels of image quality, including images generated by our world model. This requires carefully selecting which subgoals are provided as context to the model when training. We train on a combination of real images from future timesteps of the training trajectory and generated images. We found the following sampling scheme to be effective for selecting the timesteps for the real images: with probability 0.25, we sample the end-of-segment images (consistent with the prediction target for the world model), and with probability 0.75 we sample future images uniformly from 0–4 seconds ahead of the current timestep. In addition to these real images, we mitigate the train-test mismatch between real and generated images by also sampling a large number of subgoal images from the world model, and constructing additional training examples with these generated images added into the context of π0.7 instead of the real future images.

训练 π0.7 使用子目标图像时，需要让模型适应不同时间跨度和图像质量的目标，包括世界模型生成的图像。这要求仔细选择作为上下文的子目标。训练同时使用示范轨迹中真实的未来图像与生成图像。对于真实图像，我们发现以下采样方案有效：以 0.25 概率取片段结束时的图像，与世界模型的预测目标一致；以 0.75 概率从当前时刻之后 0–4 秒均匀采样未来图像。除真实图像外，还从世界模型大量采样子目标，构造额外训练样本，用这些生成图像替代真实未来图像加入 π0.7 的上下文，以减轻训练与测试之间的分布差异。

Discussion

π0.7 brings heterogeneous experience into one policy by describing the conditions under which each behavior occurred. Text specifies intent, history supplies recent changes, goals specify spatial outcomes, and metadata describes execution quality and style. This raises a representation question: what should these different sources share?

π0.7 用条件描述每种行为发生的背景，将异质经验汇入同一个策略。文本给出意图，历史提供近期变化，目标图描述空间结果，元数据标记执行质量与方式。这也引出了一个表示问题：这些信息应该在潜空间中共享什么？

Shared Representations

Projecting an image and a sentence into 1024-dimensional tokens gives them compatible shapes. A shared semantic space additionally needs relationships between their meanings; a useful dynamics space needs relationships between states, actions, and subsequent changes. These requirements lead to five complementary routes: alignment, fusion, reconstruction, prediction, and latent-action learning. A single model can combine several of them. Input modalities and modes of an action distribution describe different things: one names sources of information, the other names alternative behaviors under the same conditions.

将图像和句子都投影成 1024 维 token，统一的是输入形状。语义共享需要建立含义之间的对应，动力学表示则需要建立状态、动作与后续变化的联系。由此可以归纳出五条相互补充的路线：对齐、融合、重建、预测，以及潜动作学习。同一个模型可以同时采用其中几种。 输入的多模态与动作分布的多峰性也分别描述两件事：前者指信息来源，后者指同一条件下存在多种行为。

Fusion is the most direct connection to π0.7. A generic fusion model encodes images $I$, language $\ell$, proprioception $q$, and metadata $m$, then allows their tokens to interact:

融合与 π0.7 的联系最直接。一般的融合模型分别编码图像 $I$、语言 $\ell$、本体状态 $q$ 和元数据 $m$，再让这些 tokens 交换信息：

$$\begin{aligned}Z_v&=E_v(I),&Z_l&=E_l(\ell),\\Z_q&=E_q(q),&Z_m&=E_m(m),\\Z_{\mathrm{context}}&=T([Z_v;Z_l;Z_q;Z_m]).\end{aligned}$$

The encoders above describe input roles. In π0.7, metadata is serialized as text, proprioception uses a linear projection, and the block mask controls which groups can exchange information. Action prediction trains this context to support control. VIMA offers an earlier example of multimodal task specification: interleaved words and object images form a prompt, a T5 encoder contextualizes it, and a causal controller reads that prompt through cross-attention. Both designs make task conditions available to the policy; their attention interfaces differ.

上式按输入的作用划分编码器。在 π0.7 中，元数据写成文本，本体状态经过线性投影，block mask 决定各组信息怎样交互，动作预测则让这些上下文服务于控制。VIMA 更早展示了多模态任务提示：将文字与物体图像交错排列，经 T5 编码后，由因果控制器通过交叉注意力读取。两种设计都让策略利用任务条件，具体的信息接口有所不同。

Alignment gives similarity across modalities an explicit training objective. ImageBind aligns images, text, audio, depth, thermal images, and IMU signals. Images serve as an anchor: image–audio and image–text pairs can support audio–text retrieval even without directly paired audio and text. The correspondence still comes from paired observations, such as synchronized video and audio. For normalized image and text embeddings, a one-way contrastive objective is:

对齐进一步用训练目标约束不同模态的相似性。ImageBind 将图像、文本、音频、深度、热成像与 IMU 信号对齐。图像充当锚点：图像—音频、图像—文本配对可以支持音频—文本检索，减少对所有模态两两配对的需求。对应关系仍来自配对观测，例如同步的视频与音频。以归一化的图像、文本表示为例，单向对比目标可以写成：

$$\begin{aligned}z_i^v&=f_v(I_i),\qquad z_i^l=f_l(\ell_i),\\\mathcal L_{\mathrm{align}}&=-\log\frac{\exp(\operatorname{sim}(z_i^v,z_i^l)/\tau)}{\sum_{j=1}^{B}\exp(\operatorname{sim}(z_i^v,z_j^l)/\tau)}.\end{aligned}$$

Here $i$ identifies a matched pair, $j$ runs over the $B$ candidates in the batch, $\operatorname{sim}$ is cosine similarity, and $\tau>0$ controls the concentration of the softmax. Increasing similarity to the matched description reduces the loss. The training target determines which distinctions survive: “a cup on a table” can align semantically across views while leaving velocity, contact, and friction unresolved. Control adds a demand for spatial detail and action-dependent change.

$i$ 表示匹配的一对样本，$j$ 遍历 batch 中的 $B$ 个候选，$\operatorname{sim}$ 为余弦相似度，$\tau>0$ 控制 softmax 的集中程度。图像与对应描述越相似，损失越小。训练目标决定哪些差异会被保留：“杯子在桌上”可以在不同视角下保持语义接近，而速度、接触与摩擦状态仍需其他信号来区分。控制还要求表示保留空间细节，以及动作引起的变化。

World Models

Reconstruction asks a representation to preserve enough information to recover an observation. With an encoder $E$ and decoder $D$, a schematic image autoencoder is $z_t=E(I_t)$, $\hat I_t=D(z_t)$. A VAE further specifies a distribution over the latent variable and regularizes it against a prior. Adding an action-conditioned transition model gives a generative world model:

重建要求表示保留足以恢复观测的信息。用编码器 $E$ 和解码器 $D$ 表示，图像自编码器可以简写为 $z_t=E(I_t)$、$\hat I_t=D(z_t)$。VAE 还为潜变量定义概率分布，并用先验加以约束。加入以动作为条件的转移模型，就得到一种生成式世界模型：

$$\hat z_{t+1}=F_\theta(z_t,a_t),\qquad\hat I_{t+1}=D(\hat z_{t+1}).$$

The decoded future makes prediction inspectable, at the cost of modeling visual detail such as wood grain, reflections, and lighting. BAGEL combines semantic features from a vision encoder with VAE features used for image generation. These pathways preserve different information. In π0.7, the BAGEL-based model generates a subgoal from observations, instructions, and metadata; the equation above instead describes predicting the consequence of a supplied action. Connecting the two would let a proposed goal be checked against candidate actions.

未来可以解码成图像，预测便于直接检查，模型也需要处理木纹、反射和光照等视觉细节。BAGEL 同时使用视觉编码器的语义特征与用于图像生成的 VAE 特征，两条路径保留的信息有所侧重。π0.7 中的 BAGEL 模型根据观测、指令和元数据生成子目标；上式描述的则是给定动作后预测其后果。将两者连接起来，可以进一步检查候选动作能否到达提出的目标。

Prediction can supervise future features directly. A general action-conditioned formulation is:

预测也可以直接以未来特征作为监督。以动作为条件的一般形式为：

$$\begin{aligned}z_t&=E(o_t),\\\hat z_{t+k}&=F_\theta(z_t,a_{t:t+k-1},\ell),\\\mathcal L_{\mathrm{pred}}&=d\!\left(\hat z_{t+k},\operatorname{sg}[E_{\mathrm{target}}(o_{t+k})]\right).\end{aligned}$$

$k$ is the prediction horizon, $d$ measures feature discrepancy, and $\operatorname{sg}$ stops gradients through the target. This is a common schematic; individual methods choose different histories, conditioning, targets, and distances. A frozen pretrained target fixes the space to be predicted. When learning the encoder jointly, target updates and constraints must also keep representations informative: constant features could otherwise produce a small matching loss.

$k$ 是预测跨度，$d$ 衡量特征差异，$\operatorname{sg}$ 阻断目标分支的梯度。这是一种概括性写法，各方法使用的历史、条件、目标与距离函数有所不同。冻结预训练目标编码器，可以固定预测所依赖的表示空间。若同时学习编码器，还需要通过目标更新方式与训练约束维持信息量，否则常量特征也可能得到很小的匹配损失。

V-JEPA 2 first learns video features by predicting masked representations. Its action-conditioned extension, V-JEPA 2-AC, freezes the visual encoder and learns transitions from robot state and actions, with both one-step and rollout losses. At deployment it searches for action sequences whose predicted features approach the goal. V-JEPA 2.1 strengthens dense features through supervision on visible as well as masked tokens and across intermediate layers. Spatially organized features matter when the task depends on where an object or contact is.

V-JEPA 2 先通过预测被掩掉的表示来学习视频特征，再由 V-JEPA 2-AC 冻结视觉编码器，使用机器人状态与动作学习转移，同时优化单步预测和 rollout 损失。部署时搜索动作序列，让预测特征接近目标。V-JEPA 2.1 则通过监督可见与被掩掉的 tokens，并在中间层加入监督，改善稠密特征。任务涉及物体位置或接触时，这种空间组织尤其重要。

DINO-WM uses frozen DINOv2 patch features, fits an action-conditioned predictor on offline trajectories, and optimizes actions toward goal features at runtime. The encoder supplies a spatial prior; the transition model supplies action-dependent change. A decoder can help inspect predictions, while planning operates on feature distances.

DINO-WM 固定 DINOv2 的 patch 特征，利用离线轨迹学习动作条件预测器，并在运行时优化动作，使预测到达目标特征。编码器提供空间先验，转移模型学习动作引起的变化。解码器可以用于检查预测，规划本身在特征距离上进行。

A second use of prediction is to shape the policy during training. FLARE adds future tokens to a diffusion/flow policy and aligns their intermediate activations with frozen embeddings of future observations. JEPA-WAM: Learning Vision-Language-Action Policies with Joint-Embedding World Modeling uses frozen V-JEPA 2.1 features to define a dense target that jointly encodes current and future observations. Transition prediction and action generation train a shared predictor; the transition head is removed for deployment. These are two ways to bring future supervision into the representations used for action.

另一种用法是在训练中用未来预测塑造策略。FLARE 在 diffusion／flow 策略中加入 future tokens，将其中间激活与未来观测的冻结特征对齐。JEPA-WAM: Learning Vision-Language-Action Policies with Joint-Embedding World Modeling（利用联合嵌入世界建模学习视觉—语言—动作策略） 则使用冻结的 V-JEPA 2.1，将当前与未来观测联合编码为稠密目标。转移预测和动作生成共同训练一个预测主干，部署时移除转移预测头。两者都让未来监督影响用于生成动作的表示。

The deployment choice is therefore separate from the representation choice. DINO-WM and V-JEPA 2-AC spend inference time on predicted rollouts and action search. FLARE integrates future-feature prediction into action generation, and JEPA-WAM retains its training benefit after removing the prediction head. Compared with generating subgoal images, these designs offer different exchanges between visual inspectability, search cost, and direct policy execution.

表示形式与部署方式是两项独立的选择。DINO-WM 和 V-JEPA 2-AC 在推理时进行预测 rollout 与动作搜索；FLARE 将未来特征预测融入动作生成；JEPA-WAM 移除预测头后保留训练带来的收益。与生成子目标图像相比，它们在可视化检查、搜索开销与直接执行策略之间作出了不同取舍。

For π0.7, a useful extension would compare image goals and predictive features with the same demonstrations and low-level control budget. Hold the observation fixed and vary candidate actions: does the predicted object motion change in the correct direction, including at contact? Then compare closed-loop success and latency. Goal accuracy, feature prediction error, and control success measure different parts of this connection.

沿着 π0.7 继续研究，可以固定示范数据与低层控制的计算预算，比较图像目标和预测特征。固定观测、改变候选动作，检查预测的物体运动是否沿正确方向变化，尤其是接触发生之后，再比较闭环成功率与延迟。目标准确性、特征预测误差和控制成功率，分别衡量这条链路的不同部分。

Latent Actions

Latent-action learning addresses the missing interface in human videos: the scene changes over time, while robot control commands are absent. An inverse model can encode that change into $u_t$, and a forward model can use it to predict the next state:

潜动作学习补上人类视频中缺少的控制接口：画面随时间变化，却没有机器人的动作命令。逆模型将变化编码为 $u_t$，前向模型再利用它预测后续状态：

$$u_t=E_{\mathrm{inv}}(I_t,I_{t+1}),\qquad\hat z_{t+1}=F_\theta(z_t,u_t).$$

This separates a representation of change from the joint coordinates of a particular robot. The inverse model sees the future during training; execution needs a way to obtain $u_t$ from information available now. Two possible interfaces are a policy that predicts $u_t$ and a decoder that translates it into robot commands, or a projector that maps candidate robot commands into $u_t$ for world-model evaluation:

这样可以将变化的表示与具体机器人的关节坐标分开。训练时逆模型能够看到未来，执行时则需要根据已有信息获得 $u_t$。一种接口是让策略预测 $u_t$，再由解码器转成机器人命令；另一种接口是将候选机器人命令投影到 $u_t$，交给世界模型评价：

$$\begin{aligned}\text{Policy:}\quad u_t&\sim\pi_u(\cdot\mid o_t,\ell),\\a_t&=D_{\mathrm{act}}(u_t,q_t,e),\\\text{Planning:}\quad u_t&=P_e(a_t),\\\hat z_{t+1}&=F_\theta(z_t,u_t).\end{aligned}$$

$e$ identifies the embodiment or control interface. These are design patterns, with different implementations across papers. World-action models (WAMs) couple predictive world modeling with action generation. Latent actions offer one shared interface for human videos and heterogeneous robot trajectories.

$e$ 表示机器人本体或控制接口。这两种是设计思路，各论文的具体实现不同。World-action model（WAM，世界—动作模型）将世界预测与动作生成结合，潜动作则为人类视频和不同机器人的轨迹提供了一种共享接口。

LAPA follows the video-first route described in Related Work: learn discrete latent actions from visual transitions, pretrain prediction of those codes, and fine-tune for real robot actions. CLAP anchors the space from the other direction. It first learns an action codebook from labeled robot commands, then maps visual transitions into that vocabulary. Robot pairs provide action alignment; unlabeled human videos use teacher-derived targets. Separate action-relevant and nuisance streams help keep appearance changes from dominating the action code.

LAPA 沿用 Related Work 中介绍的视频路线：从视觉转移学习离散潜动作，预训练对这些编码的预测，再用真实机器人动作微调。CLAP 从另一端建立锚点：先用有标注的机器人命令学习动作码本，再把视觉变化映射进这套词表。机器人样本提供动作对齐，人类视频则使用教师模型产生的目标；动作相关与干扰信息分流，减少外观变化对动作编码的影响。

LAC-WM learns continuous latent actions with both future-feature prediction and motion-decoding supervision, using cross-augmentation to discourage copying future appearance. To adapt the world model to an unseen robot, it learns a projector from that robot’s raw actions into the latent space. A VLA can then propose commands, and the world model can rank their imagined outcomes. This direction of adaptation is useful when planning already operates on executable robot commands.

LAC-WM 结合未来特征预测与运动解码监督，学习连续潜动作，并通过交叉增强减少对未来外观的直接复制。适配新机器人时，学习从该机器人实际动作到潜动作的投影。VLA 提出候选命令，世界模型根据预测结果排序；当规划已经使用可执行命令时，这一映射方向便于直接接入。

Learning Latent Action World Models in the Wild studies what the bottleneck should retain. In its experiments, continuous latent actions constrained by noise or sparsity capture richer video changes than the tested vector-quantized alternative. Without a shared embodiment, transferred motion often remains localized relative to the camera. This makes the coordinate frame part of the learned interface, even when the representation is shared across videos.

Learning Latent Action World Models in the Wild（在自然视频中学习潜动作世界模型） 进一步研究瓶颈应该保留什么。论文实验中，使用噪声或稀疏约束的连续潜动作，比所测试的向量量化方案表达了更丰富的视频变化。缺少共同本体时，迁移的运动往往仍相对于相机位置局部生效。即使不同视频共享表示，坐标系仍然是接口的一部分。

A useful bottleneck must carry enough information to explain motion while restricting future-frame leakage. Otherwise $u_t$ can encode the answer to the prediction task, or absorb camera motion and lighting changes. Cross-video action transfer, matched scenes with different actions, and physical execution test whether the retained change is reusable. FAST addresses a different data interface: it compresses known robot action chunks. Latent-action inference supplies a representation of change when those labels are missing.

有效的瓶颈需要保留足以解释运动的信息，同时限制未来画面泄漏。否则，$u_t$ 可能直接编码预测答案，也可能混入相机运动与光照变化。跨视频迁移动作、同场景下改变动作，以及真实执行，可以检验留下的变化能否复用。FAST 面向的则是另一种数据接口：压缩已知的机器人动作块；潜动作推断让缺少这些标签的视频也能提供变化的表示。

Learning from Experience

These objectives make different parts of heterogeneous experience usable. Fusion exposes conditions, alignment relates meanings, prediction uses temporal transitions, and latent actions connect visual change to control. Failed attempts can still teach transitions and recovery. Selecting better behavior from that experience needs an outcome signal.

这些目标让异质经验的不同部分可以被利用：融合提供条件，对齐建立语义联系，预测利用时间转移，潜动作连接视觉变化与控制。失败轨迹仍然可以提供转移与恢复经验。要从中选择更好的行为，还需要结果评价。

π0.7 already absorbs rollouts from RL-trained specialists. RECAP: RL with Experience & Corrections via Advantage-conditioned Policies explains how such policies can improve: collect autonomous rollouts and human corrections, fit a value model, assign advantage conditions, and train the policy to select better behavior. Episode quality summarizes an execution; advantage compares a local decision with the expected return from its state.

π0.7 已经吸收 RL 专用策略的 rollout。RECAP: RL with Experience & Corrections via Advantage-conditioned Policies（通过优势条件策略，利用经验与纠错进行强化学习） 说明了这类策略怎样改善：采集自主 rollout 与人工纠正，拟合价值模型，赋予优势条件，再训练策略选择更好的行为。Episode quality 概括整次执行的质量，优势则比较局部决策与该状态下的预期回报。

RECAP trains a distributional value function with cross-entropy over 201 return bins and takes its expectation to obtain $V$. An undiscounted $n$-step advantage estimate and its binary condition can be written as:

RECAP 将回报离散到 201 个区间，用交叉熵训练价值分布模型，再取期望得到 $V$。不使用折扣时，$n$ 步优势估计与二值条件可以写成：

$$\begin{aligned}\hat A_t&=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}r_{t+j}+V(o_{t+n},\ell)-V(o_t,\ell),\\I_t&=\mathbf 1[\hat A_t>\delta_\ell].\end{aligned}$$

$\delta_\ell$ is a task-dependent threshold. The policy trains with and without the advantage condition, then uses conditioning and guidance to favor high-advantage behavior. Human corrective actions receive a positive indicator. Repeated collection, value fitting, relabeling, and policy training turn outcomes into a policy-improvement signal. π0.7’s human-scored metadata and its use of specialist rollouts provide different parts of this loop. RECAP, §III–IV

$\delta_\ell$ 是任务相关阈值。策略同时学习带优势条件和不带条件的预测，再通过条件与 guidance 偏向高优势行为；人工纠正动作被赋予正向标记。反复进行采集、价值拟合、重新标注和策略训练，就能将执行结果变成策略改进信号。π0.7 的人工元数据评分与专用策略 rollout，提供了这一循环中的不同部分。RECAP，§III–IV

A data flywheel connects collection to a measurable improvement: deploy, record outcomes and corrections, update labels or representations, retrain, and evaluate again.
