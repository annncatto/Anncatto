Both projects provide data collection, training and deployment toolchains. AlohaMini maintains a mobile-bimanual development ecosystem with native control, data and training modules, while reusing LeRobot v3 conventions and upstream policy code.两者均提供采集、训练与部署工具链。AlohaMini 提供移动双臂开发生态，包含独立的控制、数据与训练模块，并复用 LeRobot v3 规范及上游策略代码。

Engineering stage工程环节LeRobotAlohaMini
Hardware and teleoperation硬件与遥操作Robot / Teleoperator / Camera / Motors interfaces; leader-arm and other supported teleoperators.Robot／Teleoperator／Camera／Motors 接口；主臂及其他已接入遥操设备。HostClient and robot profiles; dual-arm, base and lift control; leader-arm/gamepad teleoperation.HostClient 与机型配置；双臂、底盘和升降控制；主臂／手柄遥操作。
Data collection and tools采集与数据处理LeRobot v3 and Hub tools; record, edit, split, compute statistics and visualize episodes.LeRobot v3 与 Hub 工具；录制、编辑、划分、统计及回合可视化。LeRobot v3; record, edit, split and compute statistics; add capture timing, calibration and feedback masks.LeRobot v3；录制、编辑、划分、统计；附加采样时间、标定与反馈掩码。
Policies and training策略与训练Imitation/RL tools; ACT, diffusion, VLA and WAM policies; multi-GPU training, mixed precision and resume.模仿／强化学习工具；ACT、扩散、VLA、WAM；多卡训练、混合精度与续训。ACT/AM-ACT, diffusion/DiT, VLA and WAM adapters; DDP/FSDP2, mixed precision, EMA and resume.ACT／AM-ACT、扩散／DiT、VLA、WAM 适配；DDP／FSDP2、混合精度、EMA 与续训。
Action processing动作预后处理Processor pipelines: normalization and joint, TCP and relative-action transforms.Processor 流水线：归一化、关节／TCP／相对动作转换。Normalization and joint/TCP/relative-action transforms; bind local calibration, URDF and physical units.归一化、关节／TCP／相对动作转换；绑定本机标定、URDF 与物理单位。
Deployment策略部署Local inference, remote inference and RTC; policy client/server and action-chunk execution.本地推理、远程推理与 RTC；策略客户端／服务端及动作块执行。Local inference, remote inference and RTC; action queues, named targets and Host execution.本地推理、远程推理与 RTC；动作队列、具名目标和 Host 执行。
Simulation and analysis仿真与分析Simulation evaluation interfaces and benchmark integrations; dataset and policy-rollout visualization.仿真评估接口与基准接入；数据集及策略运行可视化。MuJoCo/ManiSkill/RoboTwin integration; ROS2/MoveIt and Rerun trajectory/feedback analysis.MuJoCo／ManiSkill／RoboTwin 接入；ROS2／MoveIt 与 Rerun 轨迹／反馈分析。
