Reach and task feasibility可达范围与任务条件

  • Joint limits determine geometric reach.关节限位确定几何可达范围。
  • Tool orientation sets grasping and placement directions.末端朝向对应抓取与放置方向。
  • Collision checks constrain body, arms and scene clearance.碰撞检查约束机身、双臂与环境间隙。
  • Gravity torque and temperature constrain sustained payload.重力矩与温升约束持续作业负载。

The overlapping arm workspaces provide the geometric basis for handover and bimanual tasks.双臂工作空间的交叠区域为交接与协同操作提供几何基础。
