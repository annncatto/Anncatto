Optimization and Validation优化与验证

The previous choices become a design process when loads, models and measurements form a loop. Start with the task and duty cycle, derive the loads at the component interfaces, propose a structure and compare its predictions with tests. Removing mass changes the dynamics and can change the loads used to justify the removal.

载荷、模型与测量形成闭环，前面的选择才成为设计流程。从任务和工作周期出发，推导部件接口载荷，提出结构，再用试验检查预测。移除质量会改变动力学，也可能改变原先用来支持减重的载荷。

From Motion to Loads从运动到载荷

A motion specification should include useful payload, tool mass, reachable postures, acceleration, contacts and repetition. Multibody simulation can turn these conditions into joint and interface loads. A leg requires stance and swing cases, touchdown and plausible disturbances; an arm requires extended poses, acceleration and braking, and tool forces. A single vertical static force seldom represents all controlling cases.

运动要求应包含有效负载、工具质量、可达姿态、加速度、接触与重复次数。多体仿真可以将这些条件转换为关节和接口载荷。腿部需要支撑、摆动、触地与合理扰动工况；机械臂需要伸展姿态、加速制动和工具作用力。单一竖直静载很少覆盖所有主导工况。

For a rigid body, the force and moment balances about its centre of mass are:

对刚体，绕质心的力与力矩平衡为：

$$\sum\mathbf F=m\mathbf a_C,\qquad\sum\boldsymbol\tau_C=I_C\boldsymbol\alpha+\boldsymbol\omega\times(I_C\boldsymbol\omega).$$

Each interface must transmit the forces and moments required by these balances. Reaction loads depend on constraints, contact and the controller. Ideal position constraints can create unrealistic impact peaks; compliant contact and time resolution need to match the phenomenon being studied. A measured load history is valuable for calibrating the model and establishing fatigue cycles.

各接口需要传递满足这些平衡关系的力与力矩。反力取决于约束、接触和控制器；理想位置约束可能产生不真实的冲击峰值，接触柔顺性和时间分辨率需要匹配所研究的现象。实测载荷历程可以校准模型，也为疲劳循环提供依据。

After changing a link or moving a motor, update mass, centre of mass and inertia in the multibody model. Recompute trajectories and reactions if the design change affects them. Structural optimization under frozen loads can be a useful inner loop, followed by a dynamic update in an outer loop. Gravity, inertia, contact and preload should remain traceable to their origins.

改变连杆或移动电机后，应更新多体模型中的质量、质心与惯量。修改影响运动和反力时，还要重新计算。固定载荷下的结构优化可以作为内循环，再由外循环更新动力学。重力、惯性、接触和预紧都应能够追溯到各自来源。

From Loads to Deformation从载荷到变形

Linear elasticity couples equilibrium to strain and material response. For displacement $\mathbf u$, small strain $\boldsymbol\varepsilon$ and stress $\boldsymbol\sigma$, the corresponding continuum equations are:

线弹性通过应变与材料响应连接平衡。设位移为 $\mathbf u$、小应变为 $\boldsymbol\varepsilon$、应力为 $\boldsymbol\sigma$，相应连续体方程为：

$$\boldsymbol\varepsilon=\tfrac12(\nabla\mathbf u+\nabla\mathbf u^T),\qquad\boldsymbol\sigma=\mathbb C:\boldsymbol\varepsilon,\qquad\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma+\mathbf b=\mathbf0.$$

$\mathbb C$ is the constitutive stiffness tensor and $\mathbf b$ is body force per volume. Displacements are prescribed on one boundary portion and tractions on another. The weak form balances internal and external virtual work. Finite-element interpolation then gives a system for nodal displacement $\mathbf d$:

$\mathbb C$ 是本构刚度张量，$\mathbf b$ 是单位体积体力。边界的一部分规定位移，另一部分规定面力。弱形式令内外虚功平衡，有限元插值进一步得到节点位移 $\mathbf d$ 的方程组：

$$\int_\Omega\boldsymbol\varepsilon(\delta\mathbf u):\mathbb C:\boldsymbol\varepsilon(\mathbf u)\,d\Omega=\int_\Omega\delta\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf b\,d\Omega+\int_{\Gamma_t}\delta\mathbf u\cdot\bar{\mathbf t}\,d\Gamma,\qquad K\mathbf d=\mathbf f.$$

An element contributes $K_e=\int_{\Omega_e}B^T\mathbb CB\,d\Omega$, where $B$ maps nodal displacement to strain. This makes the material–geometry connection explicit: constitutive behavior enters through $\mathbb C$, geometry and interpolation through $B$ and the integration domain. Structural finite elements develop this discretization.

单元刚度为 $K_e=\int_{\Omega_e}B^T\mathbb CB\,d\Omega$，其中 $B$ 将节点位移映射为应变。材料通过 $\mathbb C$ 进入，几何与插值通过 $B$ 和积分区域进入，材料—几何关系由此具体化。结构有限元介绍了这一离散过程。

Choose beams, shells or solids according to the required detail. A beam can capture global member bending; a shell resolves a thin wall and its stability; a local solid model can examine a bearing seat or insert. Connections need their own stiffness, contact and preload representation. A fully fixed hole over its entire surface can make a bracket artificially stiff and conceal the real bearing load distribution.

按所需细节选择梁、壳或实体：梁适合整体杆件弯曲，壳能够解析薄壁与稳定性，局部实体模型可以检查轴承座或嵌件。连接还需要相应的刚度、接触和预紧模型。将整个孔面完全固定，可能让支架显得过硬，也掩盖真实轴承载荷的分布。

Check reaction balance, rigid-body modes, energy and a simple hand calculation before interpreting the color map. Refine the mesh until the quantities used for a decision converge. Point loads and perfectly sharp re-entrant corners may create mathematical stress singularities; a growing peak under refinement then calls for a better local model or an appropriate averaged quantity. A global displacement can converge while a local stress remains unresolved.

解释云图之前，先检查反力平衡、刚体模态、能量，并与简单手算比较。加密网格，直到决策所用的量收敛。点载荷与理想尖锐内凹角可能造成数学应力奇异；峰值随加密不断增长时，需要改进局部模型，或采用适当的平均量。整体位移已经收敛，局部应力仍可能没有解析清楚。

For a ductile isotropic metal, a common static yield comparison uses von Mises equivalent stress:

延性各向同性金属的静态屈服，常用 von Mises 等效应力比较：

$$\sigma_{\mathrm{vm}}=\sqrt{\frac{(\sigma_1-\sigma_2)^2+(\sigma_2-\sigma_3)^2+(\sigma_3-\sigma_1)^2}{2}}.$$

The principal stresses $\sigma_i$ come from the local stress tensor. Buckling, fatigue, brittle fracture and composite failure need their corresponding criteria. Compressive preload and geometric slenderness may require nonlinear stability analysis; contact, large motion and material plasticity change the governing system beyond linear $K\mathbf d=\mathbf f$.

主应力 $\sigma_i$ 来自局部应力张量。屈曲、疲劳、脆性断裂与复合材料失效，需要相应准则。压缩预载和细长几何可能要求非线性稳定分析；接触、大变形与材料塑性，也会使系统超出线性 $K\mathbf d=\mathbf f$。

Topology and Sensitivity拓扑与灵敏度

Density-based topology optimization assigns each design element a variable $\theta_e\in[0,1]$. This is a relative material variable; the solid’s physical density remains $\rho_s$. In SIMP, stiffness is interpolated with a penalty exponent $p$:

密度法拓扑优化为各设计单元分配变量 $\theta_e\in[0,1]$。它表示相对材料用量，实体材料本身的物理密度仍为 $\rho_s$。SIMP 用惩罚指数 $p$ 插值刚度：

$$E_e(\theta_e)=E_{\min}+\theta_e^p(E_0-E_{\min}),\qquad m(\boldsymbol\theta)=\rho_s\sum_e\theta_eV_e.$$
{{lightweight:simp}}

The small $E_{\min}$ keeps an empty region from making the linear system singular. For $p>1$, intermediate densities receive relatively little stiffness for their material budget, encouraging a more distinct solid–void design. The resulting optimization remains nonconvex, and filtering or projection can change which local solution is reached. The COMSOL topology-optimization introduction describes this density formulation.

很小的 $E_{\min}$ 避免空区域令线性系统奇异。$p>1$ 时，中间密度对应的单位材料刚度较低，促使结果趋向更明确的实体与空域。优化仍是非凸问题，滤波和投影会影响最后到达的局部解。COMSOL 拓扑优化介绍给出了这一密度形式。

A standard stiffness problem minimizes compliance for fixed external forces and a volume budget. For several load cases:

一种标准刚度优化，是在固定外力和体积预算下最小化柔度。对多个载荷工况：

$$\begin{aligned}\min_{\boldsymbol\theta}\quad &c=\sum_{r=1}^{n_L}w_r\mathbf f_r^T\mathbf d_r,\\\text{subject to}\quad &K(\boldsymbol\theta)\mathbf d_r=\mathbf f_r,\quad \sum_e\theta_eV_e\le V^*,\quad0\le\theta_e\le1.\end{aligned}$$

Compliance $\mathbf f^T\mathbf d$ measures load-weighted deformation and equals twice the elastic strain energy for a linear system under the stated force loading. Lower compliance means a stiffer response to those loads. The weights $w_r$ express design priorities; a worst-case objective can replace the weighted sum when one severe case must not be traded away. A mass-minimization problem with displacement limits is another formulation.

柔度 $\mathbf f^T\mathbf d$ 描述载荷加权的变形；在线性系统及上述力加载条件下，它等于弹性应变能的两倍。柔度越低，结构对这些载荷的响应越刚。权重 $w_r$ 表示设计侧重；若不能牺牲某个严苛工况，可以用最坏情况目标替代加权和。也可以改成有位移约束的质量最小化。

$$c=\mathbf f^T\mathbf d=\mathbf d^TK\mathbf d=2U_{\mathrm{elastic}}.$$

For example, $V^*=0.4V_{\mathrm{design}}$ permits material in 40% of the chosen design domain. Protected bearing seats and attachments may remain outside that variable domain. The final assembly also contains motors and other fixed parts, so this volume fraction does not directly specify a whole-robot mass reduction.

例如，$V^*=0.4V_{\mathrm{design}}$ 允许在选定设计域中保留 40% 的材料体积。受保护的轴承座和连接区，可以位于变量设计域之外。最终装配体还包含电机等固定零件，因此这一体积分数不能直接换算为整机减重。

Under design-independent forces, the single-load sensitivity is:

外力与设计无关时，单载荷工况的灵敏度为：

$$\frac{\partial c}{\partial\theta_e}=-\mathbf d_e^T\frac{\partial K_e}{\partial\theta_e}\mathbf d_e.$$

This follows by differentiating equilibrium and eliminating the displacement derivative. It identifies where added stiffness most reduces compliance. Self-weight, centrifugal force and other design-dependent loads introduce additional derivative terms. Sensitivity filtering, density filtering and a physical minimum length scale help control checkerboards and mesh dependence. A minimum member size and a minimum hole size are separate requirements.

对平衡方程求导并消去位移导数，就得到这一表达式，说明在哪增加刚度最能降低柔度。自重、离心力等随设计变化的载荷，还会带来额外导数项。灵敏度滤波、密度滤波与物理最小长度尺度，有助于控制棋盘格和网格依赖；最小杆件尺寸与最小孔洞尺寸则是两个独立要求。

Stress constraints and eigenfrequency constraints add different difficulties. Stress in a nearly empty element is sensitive to interpolation, and many local stress constraints may need aggregation. Frequency optimization must track changing modes. Castability, milling access, extrusion direction, additive supports, symmetry and protected attachment regions should constrain the optimization when they are required in production.

应力和固有频率约束各有难点。近空单元的应力对插值敏感，大量局部应力约束可能需要聚合；频率优化还需跟踪变化中的模态。生产要求涉及铸造、铣削、挤出、增材支撑、对称性和保留连接区时，应将它们纳入约束。

Uncertainty and Physical Tests不确定性与实物试验

A nominal optimum can sit close to several constraints. Wall-thickness variation, modulus scatter, uncertain payload, assembly preload and contact conditions can shift the feasible region. Sensitivity studies identify dominant variables; tolerance cases or robust optimization can then preserve margins. A Pareto frontier displays the remaining tradeoffs among mass, deformation, cost and energy instead of hiding them in one unexplained score.

名义最优解可能紧贴多项约束。壁厚偏差、模量离散、负载不确定、装配预紧与接触条件，都可能移动可行域。灵敏度分析可找出主导变量，再通过公差工况或鲁棒优化保留余量。Pareto 前沿可以展示质量、变形、成本与能量之间剩余的取舍。

Once the topology is rebuilt as CAD, solve the manufactured geometry again with its actual fillets, holes, supports and joints. Compare component mass, centre of mass, stiffness, strains and modes with measurements. Static loading and strain gauges check load transfer; modal tests check frequencies and damping; repeated loading and impact tests address life and damage. Thermal tests should include representative motion and sustained holds.

拓扑重建为 CAD 后，需要用实际圆角、孔、支承和接头重新求解。将部件质量、质心、刚度、应变与模态同实测比较：静载和应变片检查传力，模态试验检查频率与阻尼，循环加载和冲击试验检查寿命与损伤。热试验应包含典型运动和持续保持。

Use measured discrepancies to update the model. A joint may contribute more compliance than the link, a fastener may slip, or a cable may create a load path that was omitted. Recheck the controller after structural changes: lower inertia can improve responsiveness, while a lower flexible-mode frequency can narrow the stable bandwidth. The finished design is the tested assembly, including connections, wiring, cooling and service access.

根据测量差异更新模型：接头可能比连杆更柔，紧固件可能滑移，线缆也可能形成被遗漏的传力路径。修改结构后还要重新检查控制器。惯量下降可能改善响应，柔性模态频率下降却可能压缩稳定带宽。最终需要验证的是包含连接、走线、散热与维修空间的装配体。
