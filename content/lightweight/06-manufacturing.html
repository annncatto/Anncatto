Manufacturing and Connections制造与连接

The light section chosen on paper needs a manufacturing route that preserves its dimensions and usable properties. Thin walls, fibre directions, integrated ribs and internal cavities are only design opportunities when they can be produced, assembled and inspected. Manufacturing belongs in the material–shape decision from the beginning.

纸面上的轻质截面，需要通过制造保留尺寸与可用性能。薄壁、纤维方向、集成筋条和内腔，只有能够制造、装配和检测，才成为有效的设计机会。因此，制造从一开始就需要进入材料—形状决策。

Metal Processing金属加工

Machining permits accurate bearing seats and datum surfaces, but thin walls can deflect under cutting forces, residual stresses can distort the part, and deep pockets demand access. Near-net casting can combine ribs and attachment features, while porosity, wall transitions, feeding and subsequent heat treatment affect strength and fatigue. Stamping is efficient for thin sheet parts when bend radii, springback and local thinning are controlled.

切削加工便于获得准确的轴承座与基准面，薄壁却可能在切削力下变形，残余应力会让零件翘曲，深腔还需要刀具能够到达。近净成形铸造可以集成筋条与安装特征，孔隙、壁厚过渡、补缩和后续热处理则影响强度与疲劳。冲压适合薄板零件，需要控制弯曲半径、回弹与局部减薄。

For a bored tube, controlling bore diameter alone does not guarantee uniform wall thickness. Inner and outer surfaces need a consistent axis; eccentricity makes one side thinner and the opposite side thicker. Workholding, tool deflection, heat and released residual stress can alter the final geometry. The machining sequence, supported cutting conditions and thickness measurements should address the critical wall rather than checking only the finished hole.

镗削管件时，只控制孔径，并不能保证壁厚均匀。内外表面需要采用一致的轴线，偏心会让一侧变薄、另一侧变厚。装夹、刀具挠曲、温升和残余应力释放，还会改变最终几何。因此，加工顺序、切削支承条件和壁厚测量，应面向关键薄壁，而不只检查完成后的孔。

Standard tube, extrusion, stamping or near-net casting can reduce the need to remove a large solid core. The appropriate route depends on section continuity, access for tools and cores, tolerances and production volume. Heat treatment and surface processing then establish the material condition used in the design. A grade label without its product form and condition does not finish this specification.

标准管材、挤出、冲压或近净铸造，可以减少从大块实体中挖空的需求。选择取决于截面连续性、刀具与型芯可达性、公差和批量。热处理与表面处理，进一步确定进入设计的材料状态；仅有牌号，还不足以完成产品形态和状态的定义。

Additive manufacturing enables internal passages, lattices and integrated features that may be difficult to machine. Build direction, supports, rough surfaces, defects and residual stress constrain the result. Removing powder from an enclosed cavity and inspecting an internal load path are real requirements. A smoother optimized contour has little value if the critical surface cannot be manufactured or checked.

增材制造能够实现难以切削的内流道、晶格与集成特征，也受构建方向、支撑、粗糙表面、缺陷和残余应力限制。封闭腔体的清粉、内部传力路径的检测，都是实际要求。关键表面无法制造或检查时，优化轮廓再平滑也难以使用。

Composite Processing复合材料加工

Composite manufacture must both place reinforcement and consolidate the matrix. Automated tape laying (ATL) and automated fibre placement (AFP) control orientation and placement; pultrusion suits a continuous section; winding follows a path around a tool. Resin-transfer molding (RTM) places a dry preform in a closed mold and injects resin before cure. Each route creates different practical limits on curvature, local thickness, fibre continuity and integrated features.

复合材料制造，需要同时安排增强体并完成基体固结。自动铺带 ATL、自动铺丝 AFP 控制方向与位置，拉挤适合连续截面，缠绕沿模具路径布置；树脂传递模塑 RTM 则把干态预成形体放进闭模，注入树脂后固化。不同工艺，对曲率、局部厚度、纤维连续性和集成特征提出不同限制。

The RTM process chain in the course extends beyond injection: cut and store reinforcement, handle and drape it, tack and pre-consolidate the preform, place it in the mold, inject, cure, trim and inspect. Automation reduces handling variability, while near-net molding can reduce subsequent machining. Isothermal process control changes thermal cycling and can affect tooling and cycle cost. These benefits follow from the complete process chain.

课程中的 RTM 工艺链覆盖注射前后：裁切与储存增强体、抓取铺覆、定位与预压实、入模、注射、固化、修边和检测。自动化减少操作离散，近净成形减少后加工；等温控制改变热循环，也会影响模具与节拍成本。这些收益来自完整工艺链。

Vacuum-assisted infusion uses a pressure difference to move resin through the reinforcement. Flow paths and temporary channels affect filling time and the risk of dry regions. Fibre compaction changes permeability and thickness during filling. Tool sealing, resin viscosity and cure timing therefore influence both process repeatability and the properties of the final laminate.

真空辅助浸渍利用压差，使树脂穿过增强体。流道与临时通道影响充模时间和干斑风险，纤维压实又会改变渗透率和厚度。因此，密封、树脂黏度与固化时序，同时影响工艺一致性和最终层合板性能。

For continuous-fibre additive feedstock, impregnation must bring thermoplastic matrix between the fibres rather than leave an incompletely wetted bundle. The course’s ultrasonic-assisted impregnation example addresses this interface and feedstock-production step. Process-development research discusses this manufacturing route. It is a way to improve the input material and process economics, alongside the geometric freedom of printing.

连续纤维增材制造的丝材，需要让热塑性基体进入纤维束内部，避免浸润不充分。课件中的超声辅助浸渍，改善的就是这一界面与丝材制备环节。相关工艺研究介绍了这一制造路线。除了打印的几何自由度，输入材料质量与制备成本也在其中改进。

Fibre volume fraction alone does not establish a good laminate. Voids, waviness, resin-rich regions, cure and interface quality can govern compression, shear or damage tolerance. The course gives a fibre-volume example around 65%; that figure still needs the process and achieved quality alongside it. Production should preserve the property that justified reducing the section.

纤维体积分数本身不足以判断层合板优劣。孔隙、波曲、富树脂区、固化和界面质量，都可能主导压缩、剪切或损伤容限。课件列出了约 65% 的体积分数示例，这个数字仍需要与对应工艺和成形质量一起阅读。生产要保留的，是原先支持减薄的那项性能。

Connections and Load Introduction连接与载荷引入

Light skins and tubes transfer load through fittings, bearing seats, flanges, inserts and adhesive overlaps. These regions need sufficient area and stiffness to spread concentrated force into the thin structure. The mass of an efficient member can be outweighed by heavy end fittings, so connection design belongs in the initial comparison.

轻质蒙皮和管件，通过接头、轴承座、法兰、嵌件和胶接搭接传力。这些区域需要足够面积与刚度，将集中力扩散到薄壁。高效杆件的收益可能被重端接头抵消，因此连接设计应进入初步比较。

Bolts clamp mating surfaces through preload. The joint should remain closed and resist the intended slip, while flange bending and preload variation are included in the check. Tightening torque is affected by friction and does not measure bolt tension exactly. Thin walls may need bosses, backing plates or inserts to introduce the load without local crushing.

螺栓通过预紧夹住配合面。需要检查接头保持闭合、抵抗预期滑移的能力，也要计入法兰弯曲与预紧离散。拧紧力矩受摩擦影响，无法精确代表螺栓拉力；薄壁可能需要凸台、背板或嵌件，避免局部压溃。

Adhesive joints distribute load over an overlap and can avoid drilling through fibres. The ends of the overlap can develop peel and shear concentrations; surface preparation, bond-line thickness, cure and environment govern repeatability. A composite insert must spread bearing and through-thickness loads into the laminate without triggering crushing or delamination.

胶接能够沿搭接面分布传力，也可以减少纤维钻孔。搭接端部仍可能出现剥离与剪切集中，表面处理、胶层厚度、固化和环境共同决定一致性。复合材料嵌件需要将孔边承压与厚度方向载荷扩散进铺层，同时控制压溃和分层。

A bonded metallic sleeve can spread the load into a CFRP tube. A potted insert can provide a threaded attachment in a sandwich. Threads cut directly into a thin laminate interrupt fibres and provide little bearing area, while an insert adds its own pull-out, shear and thermal-mismatch requirements. The connection should be sized with the local laminate, rather than selected after the global member is finished.

胶接金属套筒可以向 CFRP 管件扩散载荷，灌封嵌件可以为夹层提供螺纹接口。直接在薄铺层上攻丝，会切断纤维，也缺少承压面积；嵌件又带来拔出、剪切与热失配要求。接头应与局部铺层一起设计。

Dimensions, Form and Position尺寸、形状与位置精度

Size tolerance controls dimensions such as a shaft diameter; geometric tolerances control form, orientation and position relative to chosen datums. Coaxiality, flatness, circularity and runout affect bearing preload, seal friction, encoder alignment and gear engagement. Making every tolerance tighter adds cost without necessarily improving the function. The important surfaces should be identified through the assembly and measurement chain.

尺寸公差控制轴径等尺寸，几何公差控制形状、方向与相对指定基准的位置。同轴度、平面度、圆度和跳动，会影响轴承预紧、密封摩擦、编码器对中与齿轮啮合。所有公差一起收紧，会增加成本，却未必改善功能；关键表面应沿装配与测量链确定。

A dimension can be correct while the load path is misaligned. Bearing-seat axis error introduces unwanted bending or friction; uneven mounting faces change clamping and preload; wall variation changes local stiffness and buckling resistance. Precision requirements should therefore follow the function of each datum and interface.

尺寸正确，传力路径仍可能没有对准。轴承座轴线误差会引入额外弯曲或摩擦，安装面不平改变夹紧与预紧，壁厚不均改变局部刚度和屈曲能力。因此，精度要求应跟随各基准与接口的功能确定。全部公差一起收紧，可能增加成本，却没有处理主导误差。

The manufacturing definition should connect each important requirement to a measurement: wall thickness and minimum section, hole and bearing-seat geometry, laminate orientation, bond quality and assembly preload. Inspection and testing confirm whether the physical part has the geometry and properties assumed by the calculation. Batch size, inspection effort, repair and replacement complete the cost comparison.

生产定义需要将重要要求连接到测量方法：壁厚与最小截面、孔与轴承座几何、铺层方向、胶接质量和装配预紧。检测与试验，确认实际零件是否具有计算假定的几何和性能。批量、检验工作、维修与更换，则补全成本比较。
