Manufacturing and Interfaces制造与连接

A lightweight concept becomes a working structure through manufacturing and assembly. Minimum walls, fibre placement, tool access, surface finish and tolerances can change both the achievable geometry and the properties used in analysis. These constraints should enter the design before a detailed shape is optimized.

轻量化概念通过制造和装配变成实际结构。最小壁厚、纤维铺放、刀具可达性、表面状态与公差，会同时改变可实现的几何和分析所用的性能。这些约束应在详细形状优化之前进入设计。

Designing for a Process面向工艺设计

Machining permits accurate bearing seats and datum surfaces, but thin walls can deflect under cutting forces, residual stresses can distort the part, and deep pockets demand access. Near-net casting can combine ribs and attachment features, while porosity, wall transitions, feeding and subsequent heat treatment affect strength and fatigue. Stamping is efficient for thin sheet parts when bend radii, springback and local thinning are controlled.

切削加工便于获得准确的轴承座与基准面，薄壁却可能在切削力下变形，残余应力会让零件翘曲，深腔还需要刀具能够到达。近净成形铸造可以集成筋条与安装特征，孔隙、壁厚过渡、补缩和后续热处理则影响强度与疲劳。冲压适合薄板零件，需要控制弯曲半径、回弹与局部减薄。

A bored thin-wall tube needs inner and outer surfaces referenced to a consistent axis. With a small axis offset $e$, local wall thickness changes approximately as $t(\psi)=t_0-e\cos\psi$: a uniform bore diameter can coexist with a nonuniform wall. Workholding, tool deflection and stress release should be controlled through the machining sequence. Wall-thickness measurements and datum-based inspection then check the actual result. Standard tube, extrusion or near-net manufacture can avoid removing a large solid core, if their tolerances and material condition meet the task.

镗削薄壁管件，需要让内外表面基于一致的轴线。存在小偏心 $e$ 时，局部壁厚近似为 $t(\psi)=t_0-e\cos\psi$，因此孔径均匀仍可能伴随壁厚不均。加工顺序需要控制装夹、刀具挠曲和应力释放，再通过壁厚测量与基准检测检查结果。标准管材、挤出或近净成形可以减少实体挖空，但公差与材料状态仍需满足任务。

Additive manufacturing enables internal passages, lattices and integrated features that may be difficult to machine. Build direction, supports, rough surfaces, defects and residual stress constrain the result. Removing powder from an enclosed cavity and inspecting an internal load path are real requirements. A smoother optimized contour has little value if the critical surface cannot be manufactured or checked.

增材制造能够实现难以切削的内流道、晶格与集成特征，也受构建方向、支撑、粗糙表面、缺陷和残余应力限制。封闭腔体的清粉、内部传力路径的检测，都是实际要求。关键表面无法制造或检查时，优化轮廓再平滑也难以使用。

Composite manufacture adds fibre handling, impregnation and cure. Hand layup, resin-transfer molding, automated fibre placement and thermoplastic forming offer different combinations of labour, tooling, porosity and repeatability. Fibre waviness can reduce compressive performance; resin-rich areas and poorly consolidated interfaces alter damage behavior. Process selection can improve the usable structural properties as well as production cost.

复合材料制造还包括纤维处理、浸渍与固化。手工铺层、树脂传递模塑、自动铺丝和热塑成形，在人工、模具、孔隙与一致性方面各有取舍。纤维波曲可能降低压缩性能，富树脂区和压实不足的界面会改变损伤行为。选择工艺也在改变可用的结构性能，而不只改变生产成本。

A material designation should include its processing condition. In 6061-T6, 6061 identifies the aluminium alloy and T6 specifies solution heat treatment followed by artificial ageing; T651 adds stress relief by stretching. 7075-T6 is another alloy–temper combination, while Ti-6Al-4V is a titanium alloy designation. Heat treatment changes strength, ductility and residual stress much more than it changes Young’s modulus. Product form, thickness and the purchasing specification determine which properties can be guaranteed. Solution treatment and ageing describes the process sequence.

材料标注需要包含加工状态。6061-T6 中，6061 表示铝合金牌号，T6 表示固溶处理后人工时效；T651 还包含拉伸消除应力。7075-T6 是另一种牌号与状态的组合，Ti-6Al-4V 则是钛合金名称。热处理对强度、延性和残余应力的影响，通常远大于对杨氏模量的影响。能够保证哪些性能，还取决于产品形态、厚度与采购规范。固溶与时效工艺介绍了这一处理过程。

A single integrated part may remove several fasteners, sealing faces and assembly operations. It can also require an expensive tool or make a small damaged feature costly to replace. Batch size changes the economics: $C_{\mathrm{unit}}\approx C_{\mathrm{tooling}}/n+C_{\mathrm{process}}+C_{\mathrm{inspection}}+C_{\mathrm{assembly}}$. Service and replacement costs extend this model over the robot’s life.

一个集成零件可能替代多颗紧固件、密封面和装配工序，也可能需要昂贵模具，或让局部损伤的更换代价升高。批量会改变经济性：$C_{\mathrm{unit}}\approx C_{\mathrm{tooling}}/n+C_{\mathrm{process}}+C_{\mathrm{inspection}}+C_{\mathrm{assembly}}$。加入服务与更换费用后，才覆盖机器人的全寿命成本。

Connections and Tolerances连接与公差

A robot link transfers its loads through joints. Thin material away from a connection may be efficient, but bearing seats, inserts, bolt flanges and adhesive overlaps need enough stiffness and area to spread forces. Their added mass belongs to the design from the start. A light tube with heavy end fittings can lose much of its nominal advantage.

机器人连杆通过接头传递载荷。远离连接区的薄壁可能很高效，轴承座、嵌件、螺栓法兰和胶接搭接仍需要足够刚度与面积来扩散载荷。这部分质量应从一开始计入设计。轻质管件配上很重的端接头，可能损失大部分名义优势。

Bolt preload keeps mating faces clamped. In a simple elastic joint under a separating axial load $P$, let bolt stiffness be $k_b$ and clamped-part stiffness be $k_c$. Before separation:

螺栓预紧使配合面保持压紧。简单弹性接头承受轴向分离载荷 $P$ 时，设螺栓刚度为 $k_b$、被夹件刚度为 $k_c$，在接合面尚未分离的条件下：

$$\chi=\frac{k_b}{k_b+k_c},\qquad F_b=F_0+\chi P,\qquad F_c=F_0-(1-\chi)P.$$

$F_0$ is assembly preload, $F_b$ bolt tension and $F_c$ remaining compression. Separation, slip and preload scatter change the model. A thin flange can bend and create prying forces. Preload affects stiffness and fatigue, while tightening friction makes torque an imperfect measurement of bolt tension. Bolted-joint mechanics provides the corresponding spring model.

$F_0$ 是装配预紧力，$F_b$ 是螺栓拉力，$F_c$ 是剩余压紧力。分离、滑移和预紧离散会改变模型，薄法兰弯曲还可能产生撬力。预紧影响刚度与疲劳，拧紧摩擦又使力矩难以精确代表螺栓拉力。螺栓接头力学给出了相应弹簧模型。

Adhesive joints distribute load over an overlap and can avoid drilling through fibres. The ends of the overlap can develop peel and shear concentrations; surface preparation, bond-line thickness, cure and environment govern repeatability. A composite insert must spread bearing and through-thickness loads into the laminate without triggering crushing or delamination.

胶接能够沿搭接面分布传力，也可以减少纤维钻孔。搭接端部仍可能出现剥离与剪切集中，表面处理、胶层厚度、固化和环境共同决定一致性。复合材料嵌件需要将孔边承压与厚度方向载荷扩散进铺层，同时控制压溃和分层。

For a CFRP tube, a bonded metallic sleeve can spread force over a longer overlap; a potted insert can introduce a threaded attachment into a sandwich. Direct threads cut into a thin laminate leave little bearing area and interrupt fibres. The sleeve, adhesive, potting and local plies must be sized together, including pull-out, shear, torque and thermal mismatch. The ECSS Insert Design Handbook develops load-transfer and failure models for sandwich inserts; Hart-Smith’s scarf and stepped-lap joint analysis examines overlap geometry, adhesive plasticity and thermal mismatch. These are useful starting points for a composite robot adapter.

CFRP 管件可以用胶接金属套筒沿较长搭接面扩散载荷；夹层板可以通过灌封嵌件引入螺纹连接。直接在薄铺层上攻丝，既缺少承压面积，也会切断纤维。套筒、胶层、灌封料与局部铺层，需要一起按拔出、剪切、转矩和热膨胀失配设计。ECSS 嵌件设计手册给出了夹层嵌件的传力与失效模型；Hart-Smith 的斜接与阶梯搭接分析研究搭接几何、胶层塑性与热失配。这些工作可以作为复合材料机器人转接头的设计起点。

Size tolerance controls dimensions such as a shaft diameter; geometric tolerances control form, orientation and position relative to chosen datums. Coaxiality, flatness, circularity and runout affect bearing preload, seal friction, encoder alignment and gear engagement. Making every tolerance tighter adds cost without necessarily improving the function. The important surfaces should be identified through the assembly and measurement chain.

尺寸公差控制轴径等尺寸，几何公差控制形状、方向与相对指定基准的位置。同轴度、平面度、圆度和跳动，会影响轴承预紧、密封摩擦、编码器对中与齿轮啮合。所有公差一起收紧，会增加成本，却未必改善功能；关键表面应沿装配与测量链确定。

Joint-angle error and structural deformation both enter positioning error. A first-order expression is $\Delta x\approx J(q)\Delta q+\Delta x_{\mathrm{elastic}}$. Repeatability under one load does not measure deformation under a different load, and machining accuracy alone does not determine whole-arm accuracy. Thermal expansion and bearing wear can change the result after assembly.

关节角误差与结构变形都会进入定位误差。一阶表达为 $\Delta x\approx J(q)\Delta q+\Delta x_{\mathrm{elastic}}$。一种载荷下的重复定位精度，不能代表另一种载荷下的弹性变形，单个零件的加工精度也不能直接决定整臂精度。热膨胀和轴承磨损还会改变装配后的结果。

Fatigue and Thermal Limits疲劳与热约束

Static strength addresses an individual load state. A robot repeats cycles, changes direction and experiences impacts. The stress history should be converted into relevant cycle amplitudes and means, with a material- and process-appropriate fatigue model. Rainflow counting is one way to extract cycles from irregular histories.

静强度面向单次载荷状态，机器人则反复循环、换向并承受冲击。需要从应力历程中提取相关的循环幅值与均值，再使用适合材料和工艺的疲劳模型。雨流计数是处理不规则载荷历程的一种方法。

$$\sigma_a=\frac{\sigma_{\max}-\sigma_{\min}}2,\qquad\sigma_m=\frac{\sigma_{\max}+\sigma_{\min}}2,\qquad D_{\mathrm{Miner}}=\sum_i\frac{n_i}{N_i}.$$

$N_i$ is the life associated with the specified stress level and test conditions; $n_i$ is the number of applied cycles. Miner’s rule is a linear damage approximation. Load order, overloads and evolving cracks can matter, so a damage sum is not a universal prediction of the exact failure cycle. Surface finish, notches, corrosion and size affect the fatigue data that can be used.

$N_i$ 是指定应力与试验条件下的寿命，$n_i$ 是实际施加的循环数。Miner 法则采用线性损伤近似，载荷顺序、过载和裂纹演化仍可能影响结果，损伤和无法普遍预测精确失效循环。表面状态、缺口、腐蚀与尺寸，也会改变可采用的疲劳数据。

A nominally low-stress link can fail at a fastener hole or a sharp pocket corner. The elastic stress concentration $K_t$ and fatigue notch factor $K_f$ describe different effects. Smooth transitions, appropriate fillets and local reinforcement should be evaluated against actual loading and manufacturing constraints. For composites, cyclic degradation can involve matrix cracks, delamination and fibre damage rather than one metal-like fatigue limit.

名义应力不高的连杆，也可能在紧固孔或尖锐腔角处断裂。弹性应力集中系数 $K_t$ 与疲劳缺口系数 $K_f$ 描述不同作用，平滑过渡、合适圆角与局部补强应结合实际载荷和制造约束评估。复合材料循环退化还可能包含基体开裂、分层与纤维损伤，难以用单一的金属疲劳极限概括。

Cracks and Impact裂纹与冲击

Fatigue strength is the stress a material can sustain for a specified number of cycles, stress ratio and environment. Fracture toughness characterizes resistance to crack extension. Their density-normalized ratios can help screen materials, but a crack size, geometry and failure model are still needed to derive a minimum-mass index. High static strength alone does not specify either fatigue life or crack tolerance.

疲劳强度是在指定循环数、应力比和环境下能够承受的应力；断裂韧度描述抵抗裂纹扩展的能力。将它们除以密度，可以辅助筛选材料；要推导最小质量指数，仍需给出裂纹尺寸、几何和失效模型。仅有较高静强度，无法确定疲劳寿命和容限。

For an opening-mode crack under linear-elastic conditions, stress intensity grows with nominal stress and the square root of crack length:

在线弹性条件下，张开型裂纹的应力强度因子随名义应力和裂纹长度的平方根增长：

$$K_I=Y\sigma\sqrt{\pi a},\qquad K_I

$a$ is crack length and $Y$ represents geometry. $K_{Ic}$ is a valid plane-strain mode-I fracture toughness; $E\prime=E$ in plane stress and $E/(1-\nu^2)$ in plane strain. $G$ is energy release rate per new crack area. These connect stress-based and energy-based descriptions. Composite delamination also needs mode-II and mixed-mode behavior. Fracture mechanics develops these quantities.

$a$ 为裂纹长度，$Y$ 表示几何影响。$K_{Ic}$ 是满足有效性条件的平面应变 I 型断裂韧度；平面应力下 $E\prime=E$，平面应变下为 $E/(1-\nu^2)$。$G$ 是单位新增裂纹面积的能量释放率，将应力描述与能量描述联系起来。复合材料分层还需要考虑 II 型和混合型断裂。相关量可见断裂力学。

For an axial tie with fixed crack length $a$ and geometry factor $Y$, the fracture constraint gives $m_{\min}=\rho FLY\sqrt{\pi a}/K_{Ic}$, so $K_{Ic}/\rho$ is a relevant index under those assumptions. At a specified fatigue life and stress ratio, the corresponding axial index uses allowable stress amplitude $\sigma_{a,\mathrm{allow}}(N,R)/\rho$. Letting the crack scale with member size, or changing from axial loading to bending, changes the derivation.

对裂纹长度 $a$ 和几何系数 $Y$ 固定的轴向拉杆，断裂约束给出 $m_{\min}=\rho FLY\sqrt{\pi a}/K_{Ic}$，因此在这些假设下可以使用 $K_{Ic}/\rho$。指定疲劳寿命和应力比后，相应轴向指数可采用许用应力幅 $\sigma_{a,\mathrm{allow}}(N,R)/\rho$。如果裂纹随杆件尺寸缩放，或改为弯曲受载，推导也会改变。

A common intermediate-growth fatigue approximation is Paris’ law, $da/dN=C(\Delta K)^m$. It predicts crack growth per cycle within its applicable range, using experimentally fitted $C$ and $m$. Combining a detectable initial crack, service loads and a critical crack size can connect material selection to inspection intervals. Small cracks, thresholds and rapid final fracture require additional treatment.

疲劳裂纹稳定扩展区常用 Paris 关系 $da/dN=C(\Delta K)^m$，用试验拟合的 $C$ 和 $m$ 描述每循环的裂纹增长。将可检测的初始裂纹、使用载荷与临界尺寸结合，可以把材料选择连接到检查间隔。小裂纹、扩展门槛与最终快速断裂，还需要另外处理。

Impact loads act over a short duration and can excite flexible modes or produce high strain rates. Their severity depends on energy, momentum, contact stiffness and stopping distance. The absorbed work is the area under a force–displacement curve:

冲击在短时间内施加载荷，可能激发柔性模态，也可能产生高应变率。严重程度取决于能量、动量、接触刚度与制动距离。吸收功对应力—位移曲线下的面积：

$$W_{\mathrm{abs}}=\int F\,dx,\qquad \mathrm{SEA}=\frac{W_{\mathrm{abs}}}{m},\qquad u_{\mathrm{elastic}}=\frac{\sigma^2}{2E}.$$

SEA is specific energy absorption for the tested component and loading mode. The final expression gives recoverable elastic energy per volume under uniaxial linear stress. Plastic crushing, viscoelasticity and controlled damage can dissipate additional energy. A Charpy absorbed-energy result belongs to its notched specimen and test conditions; fracture toughness, tensile toughness and whole-robot crash energy use different definitions. For a falling robot, sacrificial covers and compliant feet may protect a stiff load-bearing frame more efficiently than making every member soft.

SEA 是指定构件和加载方式下的比吸能。最后一式是单轴线弹性应力下，单位体积可恢复的弹性能。塑性压溃、黏弹性和受控损伤可以进一步耗散能量。Charpy 冲击功对应指定缺口试样与试验条件；断裂韧度、拉伸韧性和整机碰撞能量各有定义。对可能跌倒的机器人，用可更换外罩与柔顺足垫保护高刚度承力框架，可能比所有杆件一起变软更有效。

Heat Flow and Expansion导热与热膨胀

Thermal design connects structure to actuator capability. Winding loss contains $I^2R$, while a simple thermal network gives $\Delta T\approx P_{\mathrm{loss}}R_{\mathrm{th}}$ in steady state. Integrating a motor housing with a conductive link may improve the heat path; insulating polymer or adhesive interfaces may interrupt it. Continuous torque can be limited by temperature long before a short peak-torque test reaches its mechanical limit.

热设计将结构连接到执行器能力。绕组损耗包含 $I^2R$，稳态简单热网络有 $\Delta T\approx P_{\mathrm{loss}}R_{\mathrm{th}}$。电机壳体与导热连杆集成，可能改善热路径；隔热聚合物和胶层则可能阻断它。持续转矩可能先被温度限制，而短时峰值试验尚未触及机械极限。

Thermal conductivity $\kappa$, thermal expansion $\alpha$ and heat capacity describe different behavior. For a uniform conduction path and a free bar under a uniform temperature change:

热导率 $\kappa$、热膨胀系数 $\alpha$ 与热容描述不同性质。对均匀导热路径，以及温度均匀变化且可自由伸长的杆：

$$R_{\mathrm{th}}=\frac{L}{\kappa A},\qquad \Delta L=\alpha L\Delta T,\qquad C_{\mathrm{th}}\frac{dT}{dt}=P_{\mathrm{loss}}-\frac{T-T_\infty}{R_{\mathrm{th}}}.$$

Contact resistance and convection add to the heat network. Frictional heating includes bearing, seal and transmission losses. Aluminium housings often offer a useful conductive path; CFRP conductivity depends strongly on fibre type, direction and laminate, and an epoxy-rich through-thickness path may conduct poorly. A metal insert or dedicated spreader can bridge that bottleneck. Inertia savings and continuous-torque capability should therefore be evaluated together.

接触热阻与对流还需要加入热网络，轴承、密封与传动摩擦也会生热。铝壳常能提供有效导热路径；CFRP 的导热率则强烈依赖纤维种类、方向与铺层，富环氧的厚度方向路径可能导热较差。金属嵌件或专用均热件可以跨过这一瓶颈，因此惯量收益应与持续转矩能力一起评估。

Heat also changes clearances, preload and alignment. Dissimilar materials in a bonded or bolted joint expand differently, potentially introducing thermal stress. Repeated hot–cold cycles become another fatigue and sealing load. A good lightweight assembly retains its performance across the intended temperature range and after realistic assembly, service and repair.

热还会改变间隙、预紧和对中。胶接或螺接中的不同材料膨胀量不同，可能引入热应力；反复冷热循环，又成为疲劳与密封工况。有效的轻量化装配，需要在预期温度范围内，以及实际装配、服务和维修后，仍然保持性能。
