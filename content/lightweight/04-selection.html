Material Selection材料选择

The mechanical requirements now make a mass comparison possible. Specify the function, loads and constraints, then identify the geometric variable that may change. The objective is minimum mass. Solving the controlling requirement for that variable separates the fixed task from the material-dependent part of the result.

明确力学要求后，质量比较才有了基础。先规定功能、载荷与约束，再确定允许改变哪个几何变量，目标是质量最小。根据主导要求求出这个变量，就能把任务中固定的部分，与材料相关的部分分开。

Specific Properties and Material Indices比性能与材料指数

Specific modulus $E/\rho$ and specific strength $\sigma/\rho$ compare a property with density. A material index is derived for a particular design problem and may use a different power of that property. The difference matters when a material change is accompanied by a change of section dimensions. An Ashby chart displays the data needed for this comparison; it does not choose the governing requirement for the designer.

比模量 $E/\rho$、比强度 $\sigma/\rho$，将一种性能与密度比较。材料指数则由具体设计问题推导，可能采用该性能的不同幂次。换材料时如果还会改变截面尺寸，这种区别就很重要。Ashby 图提供比较所需的数据，但主导约束仍需要由设计要求确定。

A material can first be screened against fixed requirements such as maximum temperature, corrosion resistance, minimum ductility or an available joining process. The remaining candidates are ranked with the index relevant to the free dimensions and load case. The final shortlist is checked against detailed properties, product form, manufacturing cost and test evidence.

可以先按温度、耐腐蚀、最低塑性或连接工艺等固定要求筛选，再用对应自由尺寸和工况的指数排序。最后检查候选材料的详细性能、产品形态、制造成本与试验依据。

Stiffness-Limited Members刚度主导的构件

Start with a tie of fixed length $L$. It must carry $F$ with extension no greater than $\delta_{\max}$; area $A$ is free. The axial relation already derived gives the smallest permissible area. Substituting that area into mass makes the reason for $E/\rho$ explicit:

从长度 $L$ 固定的拉杆开始。它需要承受力 $F$，伸长不超过 $\delta_{\max}$，面积 $A$ 可变。前面得到的轴向关系给出最小面积；将它代入质量，就能看出为什么出现 $E/\rho$：

$$\begin{aligned}A_{\min}&=\frac{FL}{E\delta_{\max}},\\m_{\min}&=\rho A_{\min}L=\frac{FL^2}{\delta_{\max}}\frac\rho E.\end{aligned}$$

The load, length and displacement limit are fixed by the task. Only $\rho/E$ changes between candidate materials. Maximizing $E/\rho$ therefore minimizes the mass of this axial-stiffness-limited tie. The specific modulus is the correct index here because area enters both axial stiffness and mass linearly.

载荷、长度与位移限制都由任务固定，候选材料之间只有 $\rho/E$ 改变。因此，最大化 $E/\rho$，就能最小化这根轴向刚度受限拉杆的质量。比模量在这里恰好就是材料指数，因为面积对轴向刚度与质量都是一次方关系。

For a bending beam, the geometry changes differently. If its section stays similar while both dimensions scale, its second moment can be written $I_A=\beta A^2$, with shape constant $\beta$. Substituting this relation into the cantilever stiffness $k_b=3EI_A/L^3$ and requiring $k_b\ge k_0$ gives:

受弯梁的几何关系不同。截面形状保持相似、两个尺寸一起缩放时，二次矩可以写成 $I_A=\beta A^2$，$\beta$ 为形状常数。代入悬臂刚度 $k_b=3EI_A/L^3$，再要求 $k_b\ge k_0$：

$$\begin{aligned}A_{\min}&=\sqrt{\frac{k_0L^3}{3\beta E}},\\m_{\min}&=L\sqrt{\frac{k_0L^3}{3\beta}}\frac\rho{\sqrt E}.\end{aligned}$$

The resulting beam index is $E^{1/2}/\rho$. The square root comes from how area scales with bending geometry: section dimensions change area quadratically but the second moment quartically. It is a consequence of the design freedom, rather than an empirical correction to specific modulus.

由此得到受弯梁指数 $E^{1/2}/\rho$。平方根来自截面缩放：截面尺寸以二次方影响面积，以四次方影响二次矩。因此，它来自允许改变的几何关系，而非对比模量做经验修正。

A plate of fixed length and width has thickness as its free dimension. Its isotropic thin-plate rigidity is $D=Et^3/[12(1-\nu^2)]$. At fixed load pattern, supports and allowed displacement, the required rigidity is fixed, so the permitted thickness and mass scale as:

长宽固定的板，以厚度为自由尺寸。各向同性薄板的抗弯刚度为 $D=Et^3/[12(1-\nu^2)]$。载荷形式、支承和允许位移固定时，所需板刚度也固定，因此厚度与质量满足：

$$t_{\min}\propto\left(\frac{1-\nu^2}{E}\right)^{1/3},\qquad m_{\min}\propto\rho\left(\frac{1-\nu^2}{E}\right)^{1/3}.$$

The plate index is $[E/(1-\nu^2)]^{1/3}/\rho$, commonly simplified to $E^{1/3}/\rho$ when candidate Poisson ratios are sufficiently similar. Density carries more relative weight than in the axial tie. This is why the same material substitution can provide little benefit in a tie and a larger benefit in a panel.

板的指数是 $[E/(1-\nu^2)]^{1/3}/\rho$。候选材料泊松比足够接近时，常简化为 $E^{1/3}/\rho$。与拉杆相比，密度的相对影响更大。这解释了同样的材料替换，为什么在拉杆上收益很小，在板件上却可能更明显。

Changing the Controlling Requirement改变主导要求

If allowable stress controls the tie, the starting relation is $F/A\le\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$, so minimum mass is proportional to $\rho/\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$. For a geometrically similar bending section, section modulus scales as $Z\propto A^{3/2}$; imposing $M_b/Z\le\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$ instead makes minimum mass proportional to $\rho/\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}^{2/3}$. A bending plate with thickness free gives the exponent $1/2$, because surface stress varies with $1/t^2$.

如果拉杆由许用应力主导，就从 $F/A\le\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$ 出发，最小质量与 $\rho/\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$ 成比例。几何相似的受弯截面满足 $Z\propto A^{3/2}$，代入 $M_b/Z\le\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$，得到最小质量与 $\rho/\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}^{2/3}$ 成比例。厚度可变的受弯板，则因为表面应力与 $1/t^2$ 成比例，得到 $1/2$ 次方。

The final row fixes the outer envelope of a thin closed tube and changes only its wall. Both mass and torsion constant then vary approximately linearly with wall thickness. The twist requirement therefore produces $G/\rho$, while the shear-strength requirement produces $\tau_{\mathrm{allow}}/\rho$. Changing the free dimension changes the index: an axial, bending or torsion label alone is not enough to select the exponent.

表格最后一行固定薄壁闭口管的外包络，只改变壁厚。质量与扭转常数都近似随壁厚线性变化，因此扭转变形要求给出 $G/\rho$，剪切强度要求给出 $\tau_{\mathrm{allow}}/\rho$。允许变化的尺寸一变，指数也会改变，仅有“拉伸、弯曲或扭转”这个名称，还不足以确定幂次。

Reading the Charts读懂材料图

An Ashby chart groups material-property data on two axes, usually logarithmic because the ranges span several orders of magnitude. Family envelopes display a range of compositions and conditions. A specific grade or laminate occupies only part of that range. The official chart below places Young’s modulus against density and includes the minimum-mass guide lines used by the preceding derivations.

Ashby 图将两种材料性能放在两个坐标轴上。性能跨度通常达到几个数量级，因此多采用对数坐标。材料族包络显示成分与状态的范围，具体牌号或铺层只占其中一部分。下面的官方图绘制杨氏模量与密度，也包含前面推导所对应的最小质量引导线。

For an index $E^a/\rho$, constant-index lines satisfy $E=C^{1/a}\rho^{1/a}$. Their log–log slopes are 1 for a tie, 2 for the similar-section beam and 3 for plate-thickness design. Move a line parallel toward higher modulus at the same density to find higher index. A rectangular screening region and an index line do different jobs: the region enforces independent limits, while the line ranks the surviving candidates.

对指数 $E^a/\rho$，等指数线满足 $E=C^{1/a}\rho^{1/a}$。因此，双对数图上的斜率分别为：拉杆 1、截面相似缩放的受弯梁 2、厚度可变的板 3。保持斜率、向相同密度下模量更高的方向平移，就得到更高指数。矩形筛选区与指数线承担不同任务：前者施加独立限值，后者对保留下来的材料排序。

Steel, aluminium, magnesium and titanium have very different moduli and densities, yet their specific moduli are relatively close. Under an axial stiffness constraint, the larger area allowed by a lighter metal may mostly offset its lower modulus. When a beam or plate may expand its efficient section, low density can contribute much more strongly. The comparison below shows minimum mass relative to aluminium under each of these ideal constraints.

钢、铝、镁和钛的模量与密度差别很大，比模量却比较接近。轴向刚度约束下，轻金属可以采用更大面积，这部分收益可能大体被较低模量抵消。梁或板能够扩大高效截面时，低密度的收益就更加明显。下面比较各理想约束下，以铝为基准的最小质量。

The strength–density chart uses an applicable strength in place of modulus. Its interpretation must retain the chart’s definitions: yield or elastic limit is useful for some families, while brittle materials and composites need their relevant failure measures. The official chart is a screening overview; the allowable for the actual product, direction and service condition is used in the final calculation.

强度—密度图将模量换成适用的强度。阅读时需要保留图中定义：部分材料族使用屈服或弹性极限，脆性材料与复合材料则需要相应的失效量。官方图用于初筛，最终计算仍采用实际产品、方向和使用条件下的许用值。

This also clarifies the difference between strengthening and stiffening. Two aluminium alloys or tempers can have similar Young’s moduli and quite different yield strengths. A stronger alloy can reduce a yield-limited section; a displacement-limited section may retain almost the same dimensions. If local buckling, fatigue or a connection controls the part, that requirement must be introduced before the material ranking is finalized.

这也区分了强化与增刚。不同铝合金或热处理状态，可能具有相近的杨氏模量，却有明显不同的屈服强度。高强合金可以缩小屈服主导的截面，位移主导的截面则可能基本不变。如果局部屈曲、疲劳或连接决定尺寸，就要先引入相应要求，再完成排序。
