Lightweight indices轻量化指标

From material modulus to member stiffness从材料模量到构件刚度

A tensile specimen provides the simplest connection. Apply axial force $F$ to a uniform member of initial length $L$ and area $A$, and measure its extension $\delta$. Dividing force by area gives nominal normal stress; dividing extension by length gives axial strain. Within the linear elastic range, the slope of the stress–strain curve is Young’s modulus:

拉伸试样提供了最简单的联系。对初始长度 $L$、面积 $A$ 均匀的杆件施加轴向力 $F$，测量伸长 $\delta$。力除以面积，得到名义正应力；伸长除以长度，得到轴向应变。在线弹性范围内，应力—应变曲线的斜率就是杨氏模量：

$$\sigma=\frac FA,\qquad\varepsilon=\frac\delta L,\qquad\sigma=E\varepsilon.$$
{{course:stressstrain}}

Substitution converts the material law into a force–displacement relation. This is the step that introduces structural stiffness:

代入后，材料关系转化为力—位移关系，结构刚度由此出现：

$$F=\frac{EA}{L}\delta,\qquad k=\frac F\delta=\frac{EA}{L}.$$

$E$ is a material modulus, measured in Pa; $k$ is the stiffness of this particular member, measured in N/m. Increasing area raises stiffness, while increasing length lowers it. Compliance describes displacement per force: $s=\delta/F=1/k$ in the linear case. A lightweight design can preserve stiffness through material, dimensions or both. Young’s modulus describes the material part of this relation.

$E$ 是材料模量，单位为 Pa；$k$ 是这根具体杆件的刚度，单位为 N/m。增大面积可以提高刚度，增加长度则降低刚度。柔度描述单位力引起的位移，在线性情形下为 $s=\delta/F=1/k$。轻量化可以通过材料、尺寸，或两者共同变化来保持刚度。杨氏模量描述的是其中的材料部分。

Shear introduces a second modulus, $G$, through $\tau=G\gamma$, where $\tau$ is shear stress and $\gamma$ shear strain. Poisson’s ratio $\nu$ describes transverse strain accompanying axial loading. These quantities matter because real members carry combinations of tension, compression, bending and torsion. Fibre laminates also require the appropriate directions: a large longitudinal modulus does not establish equally large shear or transverse stiffness.

剪切变形通过 $\tau=G\gamma$ 引入剪切模量 $G$，其中 $\tau$ 是剪应力，$\gamma$ 是剪应变。泊松比 $\nu$ 描述轴向加载伴随的横向应变。真实构件承受拉伸、压缩、弯曲和扭转的组合，因此需要这些不同性能。纤维层合板还必须对应方向：较高的纵向模量，并不能直接代表同样高的剪切和横向刚度。

Normal stress and bending stiffness正应力与抗弯刚度

A transverse load bends a beam rather than stretching its entire section uniformly. One side lengthens and the other shortens; the neutral axis separates them. Material farther from that axis experiences a larger longitudinal strain. Under small-deflection Euler–Bernoulli assumptions, bending normal stress is proportional to that distance:

横向载荷会让梁弯曲，截面上的材料不再均匀伸长。一侧拉长，另一侧缩短，中性轴将两者分开。材料离中性轴越远，纵向应变越大。在小变形 Euler–Bernoulli 梁假设下，弯曲正应力与这一距离成比例：

$$\sigma_x(y)=\frac{M_b y}{I_A},\qquad I_A=\int_A y^2\,dA.$$

$M_b$ is bending moment, $y$ distance from the neutral axis, and $I_A$ the second moment of area, with units m⁴. Thus $M_b/I_A$ gives the stress gradient through the section. The largest normal stress is $M_bc/I_A$, where $c$ is the outer-fibre distance. The section modulus $Z=I_A/c$ packages the geometry for a strength comparison.

$M_b$ 是弯矩，$y$ 是到中性轴的距离，$I_A$ 是截面二次矩，单位为 m⁴。因此，$M_b/I_A$ 表示应力沿截面高度的变化率。最大正应力为 $M_bc/I_A$，$c$ 是最外侧纤维距离；截面模量 $Z=I_A/c$ 则将强度比较所需的几何信息合在一起。

Deformation introduces the modulus again. Using $\varepsilon_x=\sigma_x/E$ and the beam relation $\varepsilon_x=\kappa y$ gives curvature $\kappa=M_b/(EI_A)$. The product $EI_A$ is bending rigidity. Material and geometry appear together because the same material amount can be placed closer to or farther from the neutral axis.

计算变形时，模量再次进入。将 $\varepsilon_x=\sigma_x/E$ 与梁的几何关系 $\varepsilon_x=\kappa y$ 结合，得到曲率 $\kappa=M_b/(EI_A)$。乘积 $EI_A$ 是抗弯刚度。材料与几何共同出现，是因为相同的材料用量，也可以布置在距中性轴不同的位置。

The stress relation and Hooke’s law give ε_x = M_z y/(EI_z). Plane sections remaining plane give ε_x = κy, so equating them yields κ = M_z/(EI_z). Thus M_z/I_z describes the stress gradient, while M_z/(EI_z) describes curvature. The second moment I_z has units m⁴; the mass moment of inertia in robot dynamics has units kg·m². Euler–Bernoulli beam theory.

由正应力关系与胡克定律得到 $\varepsilon_x=M_zy/(EI_z)$；平截面假设给出 $\varepsilon_x=\kappa y$，两者相等得到 $\kappa=M_z/(EI_z)$。因此，$M_z/I_z$ 描述应力梯度，$M_z/(EI_z)$ 描述弯曲曲率。截面二次矩 $I_z$ 的单位为 m⁴，机器人动力学中的质量转动惯量单位为 kg·m²。参见 Euler–Bernoulli 梁理论。

For a uniform cantilever, the support imposes zero displacement and slope. Integrating curvature with those conditions gives the deflection. Moving the load or distributing it changes the bending moment along the beam, even with the same total force.

均匀悬臂梁的固定端位移和转角为零。沿梁长积分曲率，并代入这两个边界条件，得到挠度。即使总力相同，载荷位置和分布改变，梁上的弯矩分布也会改变。

For a plate, bending acts in two in-plane directions. Taking a unit-width strip gives a through-thickness second moment t³/12. If m_x is the bending moment per unit width, the corresponding surface stress magnitude is 6|m_x|/t². Plate deflection also couples the two directions through Poisson’s ratio; an isotropic thin plate has rigidity D = Et³/[12(1−ν²)]. A laminate instead requires its actual directional bending matrix. Plate theory.

板在两个面内方向上承受弯曲。取单位宽度条带，其厚度方向截面二次矩为 $t^3/12$；若 $m_x$ 为单位宽度弯矩，相应表面正应力大小为 $6|m_x|/t^2$。板的变形还通过泊松比耦合两个方向，各向同性薄板的抗弯刚度为 $D=Et^3/[12(1-\nu^2)]$；层合板则需要实际方向相关的弯曲刚度矩阵。参见板理论。

Specific properties and material indices比性能与材料指数

Specific modulus $E/\rho$ and specific strength $\sigma/\rho$ compare a property with density. A material index is derived for a particular design problem and may use a different power of that property. The difference matters when a material change is accompanied by a change of section dimensions. An Ashby chart displays the data needed for this comparison; it does not choose the governing requirement for the designer.

比模量 $E/\rho$、比强度 $\sigma/\rho$，将一种性能与密度比较。材料指数则由具体设计问题推导，可能采用该性能的不同幂次。换材料时如果还会改变截面尺寸，这种区别就很重要。Ashby 图提供比较所需的数据，但主导约束仍需要由设计要求确定。

A material can first be screened against fixed requirements such as maximum temperature, corrosion resistance, minimum ductility or an available joining process. The remaining candidates are ranked with the index relevant to the free dimensions and load case. The final shortlist is checked against detailed properties, product form, manufacturing cost and test evidence.

可以先按温度、耐腐蚀、最低塑性或连接工艺等固定要求筛选，再用对应自由尺寸和工况的指数排序。最后检查候选材料的详细性能、产品形态、制造成本与试验依据。

Deriving the tensile-member index拉杆指数的完整推导

Fix the length L, load F and maximum extension δ_max. The required axial stiffness is S₀ = F/δ_max; cross-sectional area A is the free variable. The minimum-mass design uses just enough area to reach S₀:

固定长度 $L$、载荷 $F$ 和允许伸长 $\delta_{\max}$，目标轴向刚度为 $S_0=F/\delta_{\max}$，截面积 $A$ 是自由变量。最小质量对应刚好达到这一刚度的面积：

$$\begin{aligned}S&=\frac{EA}{L}\ge S_0,\\A_{\min}&=\frac{S_0L}{E}=\frac{FL}{E\delta_{\max}},\\m_{\min}&=\rho A_{\min}L=S_0L^2\frac\rho E=\frac{FL^2}{\delta_{\max}}\frac\rho E.\end{aligned}$$

S₀ and L are fixed by this design. Only ρ/E changes with the candidate material, so minimum mass is equivalent to maximum E/ρ. The derivation eliminates the area through the stiffness constraint; it does not assume that different materials keep the same area.

$S_0$ 与 $L$ 已由此项设计固定，候选材料只改变 $\rho/E$，因此质量最小等价于 $E/\rho$ 最大。这里通过刚度约束消去了面积，不同材料各自采用满足要求的面积。

Beam and plate indices梁与板的材料指数

For a bending beam, the geometry changes differently. If its section stays similar while both dimensions scale, its second moment can be written $I_A=\beta A^2$, with shape constant $\beta$. Substituting this relation into the cantilever stiffness $k_b=3EI_A/L^3$ and requiring $k_b\ge k_0$ gives:

受弯梁的几何关系不同。截面形状保持相似、两个尺寸一起缩放时，二次矩可以写成 $I_A=\beta A^2$，$\beta$ 为形状常数。代入悬臂刚度 $k_b=3EI_A/L^3$，再要求 $k_b\ge k_0$：

$$\begin{aligned}A_{\min}&=\sqrt{\frac{k_0L^3}{3\beta E}},\\m_{\min}&=L\sqrt{\frac{k_0L^3}{3\beta}}\frac\rho{\sqrt E}.\end{aligned}$$

The resulting beam index is $E^{1/2}/\rho$. The square root comes from how area scales with bending geometry: section dimensions change area quadratically but the second moment quartically. It is a consequence of the design freedom, rather than an empirical correction to specific modulus.

由此得到受弯梁指数 $E^{1/2}/\rho$。平方根来自截面缩放：截面尺寸以二次方影响面积，以四次方影响二次矩。因此，它来自允许改变的几何关系，而非对比模量做经验修正。

A plate of fixed length and width has thickness as its free dimension. Its isotropic thin-plate rigidity is $D=Et^3/[12(1-\nu^2)]$. At fixed load pattern, supports and allowed displacement, the required rigidity is fixed, so the permitted thickness and mass scale as:

长宽固定的板，以厚度为自由尺寸。各向同性薄板的抗弯刚度为 $D=Et^3/[12(1-\nu^2)]$。载荷形式、支承和允许位移固定时，所需板刚度也固定，因此厚度与质量满足：

$$t_{\min}\propto\left(\frac{1-\nu^2}{E}\right)^{1/3},\qquad m_{\min}\propto\rho\left(\frac{1-\nu^2}{E}\right)^{1/3}.$$

The plate index is $[E/(1-\nu^2)]^{1/3}/\rho$, commonly simplified to $E^{1/3}/\rho$ when candidate Poisson ratios are sufficiently similar. Density carries more relative weight than in the axial tie. This is why the same material substitution can provide little benefit in a tie and a larger benefit in a panel.

板的指数是 $[E/(1-\nu^2)]^{1/3}/\rho$。候选材料泊松比足够接近时，常简化为 $E^{1/3}/\rho$。与拉杆相比，密度的相对影响更大。这解释了同样的材料替换，为什么在拉杆上收益很小，在板件上却可能更明显。

For metal sheet with fixed plan dimensions and variable thickness, this gives the bending index E¹ᐟ³/ρ. A high-strength aluminium alloy may improve yield capacity without changing E appreciably; if deflection controls thickness, its higher strength alone provides little reduction. A beam whose two section dimensions scale together instead uses E¹ᐟ²/ρ. These different freedoms explain why the word “bending” does not determine one universal index.

对于平面尺寸固定、厚度可变的金属薄板，抗弯指数为 $E^{1/3}/\rho$。高强铝合金可提高屈服承载能力，却很少改变 $E$；厚度由挠度控制时，仅提高强度带来的减重有限。两个截面尺寸一起缩放的梁则采用 $E^{1/2}/\rho$，具体几何自由度决定幂次。

Strength and torsion indices强度与扭转指数

If allowable stress controls the tie, the starting relation is $F/A\le\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$, so minimum mass is proportional to $\rho/\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$. For a geometrically similar bending section, section modulus scales as $Z\propto A^{3/2}$; imposing $M_b/Z\le\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$ instead makes minimum mass proportional to $\rho/\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}^{2/3}$. A bending plate with thickness free gives the exponent $1/2$, because surface stress varies with $1/t^2$.

如果拉杆由许用应力主导，就从 $F/A\le\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$ 出发，最小质量与 $\rho/\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$ 成比例。几何相似的受弯截面满足 $Z\propto A^{3/2}$，代入 $M_b/Z\le\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$，得到最小质量与 $\rho/\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}^{2/3}$ 成比例。厚度可变的受弯板，则因为表面应力与 $1/t^2$ 成比例，得到 $1/2$ 次方。

The final row fixes the outer envelope of a thin closed tube and changes only its wall. Both mass and torsion constant then vary approximately linearly with wall thickness. The twist requirement therefore produces $G/\rho$, while the shear-strength requirement produces $\tau_{\mathrm{allow}}/\rho$. Changing the free dimension changes the index: an axial, bending or torsion label alone is not enough to select the exponent.

表格最后一行固定薄壁闭口管的外包络，只改变壁厚。质量与扭转常数都近似随壁厚线性变化，因此扭转变形要求给出 $G/\rho$，剪切强度要求给出 $\tau_{\mathrm{allow}}/\rho$。允许变化的尺寸一变，指数也会改变，仅有“拉伸、弯曲或扭转”这个名称，还不足以确定幂次。

For tension, Young’s modulus measures resistance to axial elastic strain; for torsion, the shear modulus controls shear deformation. A circular shaft obeys θ = TL/(GJ_t). In a thin closed tube with fixed outer dimensions, both mass and torsional rigidity vary approximately linearly with wall thickness, producing G/ρ. For isotropic linear elasticity G = E/[2(1+ν)]; fibre laminates require independent directional properties.

拉伸结构用杨氏模量描述轴向弹性变形，扭转结构由剪切模量控制剪切变形。圆轴扭转有 $\theta=TL/(GJ_t)$。薄壁闭口管外包络固定时，质量与抗扭刚度都近似随壁厚线性变化，得到 $G/\rho$。各向同性线弹性材料满足 $G=E/[2(1+\nu)]$，纤维层合板则需分别确定各方向性能。

Specific tensile fracture strength σ_f/ρ compares tensile failure capacity per mass; specific shear fracture strength τ_f/ρ does the same for shear. Yield-limited ductile-metal design uses the appropriate allowable yield stress, while brittle and composite failure uses an applicable failure measure. With similar beam sections, strength enters as σ_f²ᐟ³/ρ; with plate thickness free, it enters as σ_f¹ᐟ²/ρ.

比拉伸断裂强度 $\sigma_f/\rho$ 比较单位质量的拉伸失效能力，比剪切断裂强度 $\tau_f/\rho$ 对应剪切。延性金属屈服设计采用相应许用屈服应力，脆性材料与复合材料采用适用的失效量。截面相似缩放的梁使用 $\sigma_f^{2/3}/\rho$，板厚自由时使用 $\sigma_f^{1/2}/\rho$。

As the exponent of E decreases from 1 to 1/2 to 1/3, low density becomes more influential in the minimum-mass expression. This favors magnesium and sandwich constructions for plate-like components, while high-modulus fibres are especially useful in slender members with aligned loads. Stability, joints and damage tolerance can impose an additional constraint before the ideal index benefit is reached.

$E$ 的幂次从 1 降到 1/2、1/3 时，低密度在最小质量表达式中的相对作用增大。镁合金和夹层构造因此适合板件，高模量纤维则适合载荷方向明确的细长构件。稳定性、接头和损伤容限可能在理想指数收益实现之前提出额外尺寸要求。

Ashby charts and index comparisonsAshby 图与指数比较

An Ashby chart groups material-property data on two axes, usually logarithmic because the ranges span several orders of magnitude. Family envelopes display a range of compositions and conditions. A specific grade or laminate occupies only part of that range. The official chart below places Young’s modulus against density and includes the minimum-mass guide lines used by the preceding derivations.

Ashby 图将两种材料性能放在两个坐标轴上。性能跨度通常达到几个数量级，因此多采用对数坐标。材料族包络显示成分与状态的范围，具体牌号或铺层只占其中一部分。下面的官方图绘制杨氏模量与密度，也包含前面推导所对应的最小质量引导线。

For an index $E^a/\rho$, constant-index lines satisfy $E=C^{1/a}\rho^{1/a}$. Their log–log slopes are 1 for a tie, 2 for the similar-section beam and 3 for plate-thickness design. Move a line parallel toward higher modulus at the same density to find higher index. A rectangular screening region and an index line do different jobs: the region enforces independent limits, while the line ranks the surviving candidates.

对指数 $E^a/\rho$，等指数线满足 $E=C^{1/a}\rho^{1/a}$。因此，双对数图上的斜率分别为：拉杆 1、截面相似缩放的受弯梁 2、厚度可变的板 3。保持斜率、向相同密度下模量更高的方向平移，就得到更高指数。矩形筛选区与指数线承担不同任务：前者施加独立限值，后者对保留下来的材料排序。

Steel, titanium, aluminium and magnesium have nearly equal E/ρ, yet different E¹ᐟ²/ρ. Representative rounded tie indices are 27, 26, 26 and 25; beam indices are about 1.9, 2.4, 3.0 and 3.7. Quasi-isotropic and longitudinal unidirectional CFRP give about 39 and 90 for the tie index, and 5.0 and 7.6 for the beam index. Switching the geometric freedom changes the ranking and the possible mass saving.

钢、钛、铝、镁的 $E/\rho$ 几乎相同，$E^{1/2}/\rho$ 却不同。代表性取整拉杆指数为 27、26、26、25，梁指数约为 1.9、2.4、3.0、3.7；准各向同性与纵向单向 CFRP 的拉杆指数约为 39、90，梁指数约为 5.0、7.6。改变几何自由度，会改变排序与可实现的减重幅度。

The strength–density chart uses an applicable strength in place of modulus. Its interpretation must retain the chart’s definitions: yield or elastic limit is useful for some families, while brittle materials and composites need their relevant failure measures. The official chart is a screening overview; the allowable for the actual product, direction and service condition is used in the final calculation.

强度—密度图将模量换成适用的强度。阅读时需要保留图中定义：部分材料族使用屈服或弹性极限，脆性材料与复合材料则需要相应的失效量。官方图用于初筛，最终计算仍采用实际产品、方向和使用条件下的许用值。

This also clarifies the difference between strengthening and stiffening. Two aluminium alloys or tempers can have similar Young’s moduli and quite different yield strengths. A stronger alloy can reduce a yield-limited section; a displacement-limited section may retain almost the same dimensions. If local buckling, fatigue or a connection controls the part, that requirement must be introduced before the material ranking is finalized.

这也区分了强化与增刚。不同铝合金或热处理状态，可能具有相近的杨氏模量，却有明显不同的屈服强度。高强合金可以缩小屈服主导的截面，位移主导的截面则可能基本不变。如果局部屈曲、疲劳或连接决定尺寸，就要先引入相应要求，再完成排序。

Fatigue, fracture and impact疲劳、断裂与冲击

Static strength covers one loading condition. Fatigue addresses repeated loading: stress amplitude, mean stress, number of cycles, surface condition and environment determine the relevant fatigue strength. A gait, a machine cycle or repeated tool reversal can accumulate damage even when each individual load stays below static yield. The appropriate comparison uses fatigue data for the required life and load ratio.

静强度面对一次加载，疲劳面对反复加载。应力幅、平均应力、循环次数、表面状态与环境，共同决定相关的疲劳强度。步态、机器工作循环和工具反复换向，即使单次载荷低于静态屈服，也可能积累损伤。因此，应采用对应目标寿命与应力比的疲劳数据。

Fracture toughness describes resistance to crack extension. A scratch, hole edge, manufacturing defect or impact can introduce a crack whose behavior is not captured by nominal stress alone. Toughness, fatigue strength and their density-normalized values are useful selection properties when crack tolerance or cyclic life controls the design. Their test conditions must accompany the number. Fracture mechanics gives the corresponding crack-based viewpoint.

断裂韧度描述抵抗裂纹扩展的能力。划痕、孔边、制造缺陷或冲击都可能引入裂纹，其行为无法仅由名义应力概括。当裂纹容限或循环寿命主导设计时，韧度、疲劳强度及相应的密度归一化性能，就成为选材依据，数值需要附带试验条件。断裂力学提供了这种基于裂纹的分析视角。

Impact applies load over a short time and can excite structural vibration or produce a high local strain rate. The absorbed work is the area under a force–displacement curve. A stiff member can store elastic energy; plastic crushing, damping and controlled damage can dissipate energy. The useful choice depends on whether the part should recover, protect another component or be replaced after an impact.

冲击在短时间内加载，可能激发结构振动，也可能产生较高局部应变率。吸收功对应力—位移曲线下的面积：高刚度构件可以储存弹性能，塑性压溃、阻尼与受控损伤可以耗散能量。怎样选择，取决于构件需要恢复形状、保护其他部件，还是在冲击后更换。

Specific fatigue strength uses fatigue strength at a stated life, stress ratio and environment, divided by density. A specific fracture-toughness ratio can provide a preliminary comparison, but crack size and crack geometry also enter fracture design. Toughness, fatigue strength and energy absorption describe different responses; their allowable values must correspond to the load history that sizes the part.

比疲劳强度采用指定寿命、应力比和环境下的疲劳强度除以密度。比断裂韧度可用于初步比较，裂纹尺寸与几何仍需进入断裂设计。韧度、疲劳强度和吸能能力描述不同响应，各自许用值应对应决定零件尺寸的载荷历程。

Thermal properties and manufacturing state热性能与制造状态

Temperature introduces physical properties alongside mechanical ones. Thermal conductivity determines how easily heat reaches a cooling surface; thermal expansion changes dimensions and fits; heat capacity affects transient heating. Friction, motors and other heat sources can make a lightweight housing part of the thermal path. An insulating interface can interrupt that path even when each adjacent part conducts well.

温度将物理性能带入力学设计。热导率决定热量多容易传到散热表面，热膨胀改变尺寸与配合，热容影响暂态升温。摩擦、电机等热源，可能让轻质壳体同时成为热路径；即使相邻零件都导热，隔热接口仍可能阻断这条路径。

Different expansion in a metal–composite connection can change preload or introduce thermal stress. CFRP conductivity depends on fibre type, direction and laminate, with the through-thickness path often limited by the matrix and interfaces. Corrosion, moisture and oxidation further change usable performance. A material selected for low mass must retain its required function in this actual environment.

金属与复合材料连接中的膨胀差异，可能改变预紧或引入热应力。CFRP 导热率依赖纤维种类、方向与铺层，厚度方向路径常受基体和界面限制。腐蚀、湿度与氧化，又会改变可用性能。因此，按质量选择的材料，还需要在实际环境中维持功能。

A heat-treated aluminium designation combines alloy and condition. In 6061-T6, 6061 identifies the alloy and T6 specifies solution heat treatment followed by artificial ageing. T651 also includes stress relief by stretching. The designation therefore carries information relevant to strength and machining distortion. Heat treatment generally changes strength much more than Young’s modulus. Solution treatment and ageing describes this processing sequence.

热处理铝合金的标注同时包含牌号与状态。6061-T6 中，6061 表示合金牌号，T6 表示固溶处理后人工时效；T651 还包含拉伸消除应力。因此，这个标注也提供了强度和加工变形方面的信息。热处理对强度的影响，通常远大于对杨氏模量的影响。固溶与时效工艺介绍了这一过程。

For a CFRP tube, a bonded metallic sleeve can spread force over a longer overlap; a potted insert can introduce a threaded attachment into a sandwich. Direct threads cut into a thin laminate leave little bearing area and interrupt fibres. The sleeve, adhesive, potting and local plies must be sized together, including pull-out, shear, torque and thermal mismatch. The ECSS Insert Design Handbook develops load-transfer and failure models for sandwich inserts; Hart-Smith’s scarf and stepped-lap joint analysis examines overlap geometry, adhesive plasticity and thermal mismatch. These are useful starting points for a composite robot adapter.

CFRP 管件可以用胶接金属套筒沿较长搭接面扩散载荷；夹层板可以通过灌封嵌件引入螺纹连接。直接在薄铺层上攻丝，既缺少承压面积，也会切断纤维。套筒、胶层、灌封料与局部铺层，需要一起按拔出、剪切、转矩和热膨胀失配设计。ECSS 嵌件设计手册给出了夹层嵌件的传力与失效模型；Hart-Smith 的斜接与阶梯搭接分析研究搭接几何、胶层塑性与热失配。这些工作可以作为复合材料机器人转接头的设计起点。

The useful research question is how the fitting introduces force without concentrating peel, bearing or through-thickness stress in an inefficient way. Overlap length, taper, insert shape and local reinforcement can be compared under the same pull-out, shear and torque requirements. That connects joint research to the mass saved in the complete component, rather than judging the tube wall alone.

有用的研究问题，是接头怎样引入载荷，同时减少低效的剥离、孔边承压与厚度方向应力集中。可以在相同拔出、剪切和转矩要求下，比较搭接长度、锥度、嵌件形状与局部铺层。这样，接头研究就连接到完整构件的减重，而不只评价管壁。
