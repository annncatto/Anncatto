Geometry and Load Paths几何与传力

Material properties set what a unit volume can do. Geometry decides whether that material sits where the load needs it. A useful structural concept provides a short, continuous path from the point of loading to the supports, with enough depth to resist bending and a closed path for torsion when required.

材料性能规定单位体积能够做什么，几何决定材料是否处在载荷需要的位置。有效的结构概念，应从加载点到支承建立短而连续的传力路径，提供抵抗弯曲所需的截面高度，并在需要抗扭时形成闭合路径。

Section Efficiency截面效率

At fixed area, redistributing material changes the second moment. A square reference section has $I_{A,0}=A^2/12$. Define a dimensionless bending shape factor for this reference:

面积固定时，重新分配材料仍然能够改变截面二次矩。以实心正方形为参考，$I_{A,0}=A^2/12$，可以定义无量纲弯曲形状因子：

$$\phi_b=\frac{I_A}{I_{A,0}}=\frac{12I_A}{A^2}.$$

Combining this shape factor with the bending material index gives $M=\sqrt{\phi_bE}/\rho$ when area can scale while the chosen shape remains similar. Material and shape then enter the same expression. The attainable $\phi_b$ is constrained by the available envelope, local stability and manufacturing.

截面积可以缩放、选定形状保持相似时，将形状因子代入抗弯材料指数，可得 $M=\sqrt{\phi_bE}/\rho$。材料与形状进入同一个表达式；空间包络、局部稳定和制造，则限制实际能够获得的 $\phi_b$。

For a circular tube with mean radius $r$ and wall $t$, the exact factor relative to an equal-area solid square is $\phi_b=3(r/t+t/(4r))/\pi$. In the thin-wall limit it approaches $3r/(\pi t)$. Thus $r/t=5$ and $10$ give approximately 4.82 and 9.57, respectively. A solid circle gives $3/\pi\approx0.955$. These compare bending stiffness at equal area and modulus.

圆管的平均半径为 $r$、壁厚为 $t$ 时，相对于等面积实心正方形的精确形状因子为 $\phi_b=3(r/t+t/(4r))/\pi$，薄壁近似为 $3r/(\pi t)$。因此，$r/t=5$ 与 $10$ 分别得到约 4.82 和 9.57；实心圆为 $3/\pi\approx0.955$。它们比较的是面积与模量相同时的抗弯刚度。

For the same bending moment, $M_b/I_A$ falls by the reciprocal of $\phi_b$. It is the stress gradient across the section; maximum stress also contains the outer-fibre distance $c$. For the equal-area circular tube with $r/t=10$, $M_b/I_A$ is about 1/9.57 of the square’s value, while $\sigma_{\max}=M_bc/I_A$ is about 1/3.61 because the tube extends farther from the neutral axis. Stiffness efficiency and strength efficiency therefore use different section measures: $I_A$ and section modulus $Z=I_A/c$.

相同弯矩下，$M_b/I_A$ 按 $\phi_b$ 的倒数下降。它表示应力沿截面的变化率；最大应力还包含最外侧距离 $c$。对等面积、$r/t=10$ 的圆管，$M_b/I_A$ 约为实心方形的 1/9.57，最大应力 $\sigma_{\max}=M_bc/I_A$ 则约为 1/3.61，因为圆管的外缘也离中性轴更远。刚度效率和强度效率因此分别使用 $I_A$ 与截面模量 $Z=I_A/c$。

A 40 × 60 mm rectangular tube with 2 mm walls has area 384 mm² and bending second moment 193,152 mm⁴ about its strong axis. A solid square of the same area has side 19.60 mm and second moment 12,288 mm⁴. The tube offers about 15.7 times the bending second moment at equal material volume. It occupies a larger envelope, so the gain is available only if that envelope fits.

40 × 60 mm、壁厚 2 mm 的矩形管，截面积为 384 mm²，强轴截面二次矩为 193,152 mm⁴。相同面积的实心正方形边长为 19.60 mm，二次矩为 12,288 mm⁴。在材料体积相同的条件下，矩形管得到约 15.7 倍的弯曲二次矩，但它占用更大的外包络，空间允许时才能使用这项收益。

Removing material near the neutral axis is efficient in bending. Moving the same material outward increases the section’s surface area, which can help cooling, but it also changes manufacturing, local wall stability and impact resistance. An extremely thin, deep tube may be limited by local buckling or a bolted end fitting before the global beam reaches its nominal stress limit.

弯曲时，移走中性轴附近的材料通常很有效。将材料向外移动，也会增加表面积，可能有利于散热，同时改变制造、局部壁板稳定性与抗冲击能力。非常薄而高的管件，可能先受局部屈曲或螺栓端部接头限制，无法达到整体梁的名义应力极限。

The best section depends on the entire load set. A rectangular tube can favor one bending direction; a circular tube is insensitive to rotation about its axis and carries torsion efficiently. An I-section places material well for one bending plane but has low open-section torsional stiffness. A robot link carrying cables, bearings and off-axis contact forces rarely has a single ideal load.

最佳截面由完整载荷集合决定。矩形管可以偏向一个弯曲方向；圆管绕自身轴旋转时性能不变，抗扭也比较高效；工字截面适合一个主弯曲平面，开口截面的扭转刚度却偏低。携带线缆、轴承并承受偏轴接触力的机器人连杆，很少只有一种理想载荷。

Section efficiency also interacts with dynamics. For a uniform prismatic link spinning about its own longitudinal axis, mass inertia is $J_x=\rho L(I_y+I_z)$. Moving material outward at fixed area increases this roll inertia along with the bending benefit. For rotation about a transverse root axis, the longitudinal distance to the joint often dominates. The relevant joint axis therefore determines how a new section changes motion inertia.

截面效率也与动力学相连。均匀棱柱形连杆绕自身纵轴旋转时，质量惯量为 $J_x=\rho L(I_y+I_z)$。面积固定、材料向外移动，在提高抗弯效率的同时，也会增加这项滚转惯量。绕根部横向轴旋转时，沿杆长到关节的距离往往占主导。采用新的截面后，运动惯量怎样改变，需要根据实际关节轴判断。

A direct load path also avoids unnecessary lever arms: an offset $e$ adds a moment $Fe$. Triangulation can carry a load mainly through axial forces when the joint and support geometry permits it. A well-utilized structure spreads demand across its available material over the full load set. Low stress in one snapshot alone is insufficient reason to remove a region that provides stability, supports a joint or carries another case.

直接传力还可以减少额外力臂，偏置 $e$ 会增加弯矩 $Fe$。连接与支承几何允许时，三角化可以让载荷主要通过轴力传递。材料利用率需要在完整工况集合下评估；某一帧应力较低的区域，也可能负责稳定、支撑接头或承担另一工况。

Tailored Members and Sandwiches变截面与夹层结构

A tip-loaded cantilever has a bending moment that decreases toward its free end. With a solid rectangular section, fixed width $b$ and variable height $h(x)$, equal nominal bending stress gives:

末端受力的悬臂梁，弯矩沿杆长逐渐减小。实心矩形截面保持宽度 $b$、改变高度 $h(x)$，按名义弯曲应力相等设计可得：

$$M_b(x)=F(L-x),\qquad h(x)=\sqrt{\frac{6F(L-x)}{b\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}}}.$$

This ideal shape allocates more depth near the root. Its predicted height reaches zero at the tip, where the moment vanishes; a real member retains minimum dimensions for load application, joining, buckling and manufacturing. The equal-strength construction controls nominal stress. The resulting deflection must still be checked, since an allowable stress does not set an allowable displacement.

该形状在根部布置更多高度。理想公式在末端弯矩为零时给出零高度，实际杆件仍需保留加载、连接、稳定与制造要求的最小尺寸。等强度构形控制名义应力，完成后还需要检查挠度，许用应力本身并不规定允许位移。

Ribs and local reinforcements are another way to tailor a member. Material close to a bearing seat can spread concentrated forces into a shell, while ribs can stabilize panels. The reinforcement must actually connect to the load path. A decorative lattice on a low-stress surface can add manufacturing complexity without improving the controlling requirement.

加强筋与局部补强也能定制传力。轴承座附近的材料可以将集中力扩散进壳体，筋条可以稳定壁板，前提是它们确实接入传力路径。在低应力表面增加装饰性晶格，可能只提高制造复杂度，而没有改善主导约束。

A sandwich uses two stiff faces separated by a light core. If each face has thickness $t_f$, their centre separation is $h$, and width is $b$, a simple face-dominated beam estimate is:

夹层结构用轻质芯材分隔两层高刚度面板。设每层面板厚度为 $t_f$、面板中心间距为 $h$、宽度为 $b$，按面板主导的梁模型估计：

$$(EI)_{\mathrm{faces}}\approx\frac{E_fbt_fh^2}{2},\qquad \frac mL\approx2\rho_fbt_f+\rho_cbt_c.$$

The separation increases the bending leverage of the faces while the core transfers shear and stabilizes them. Core shear deformation, face wrinkling, local indentation, debonding and fastener inserts can control the result. A sandwich cover carrying distributed loads is a different problem from a small joint adapter carrying concentrated bearing forces.

分离面板增大了弯曲力臂，芯材负责传递剪力并稳定面板。芯材剪切变形、面板起皱、局部压陷、脱粘和紧固件嵌件，都可能决定结果。承受分布载荷的夹层外盖，与承受集中轴承力的小型关节转接件，适用的结构形式并不相同。

Lattices and foams can likewise provide separation and distributed support. Their effective stiffness depends on cell geometry, relative density and whether deformation is stretching- or bending-dominated. Cell size also determines whether a homogenized material model is meaningful. A lattice with only two cells across a highly loaded connection should be examined as a discrete structure.

晶格与泡沫同样可以提供间距和分布支承。等效刚度取决于胞元几何、相对密度，以及变形主要由拉伸还是弯曲承担。胞元尺寸还决定均匀化材料模型是否适用；高载荷连接区如果横向只有两个胞元，就应按离散结构检查。

Size, Shape and Topology尺寸、形状与拓扑

Sizing changes dimensions within a chosen concept: wall thickness, rib depth or bolt diameter. Shape optimization moves boundaries while preserving a chosen connectivity. Topology optimization changes where load-bearing material exists and which paths connect the supports. These variables operate at different stages, and all depend on supports and loads.

尺寸优化在既定概念内改变壁厚、筋高或螺栓直径；形状优化移动边界，保留既定连接关系；拓扑优化改变承载材料存在的位置，以及支承之间的连接路径。这些变量对应不同设计层次，也都依赖载荷与边界条件。

For an early robot concept, actuator locations, joint axes and required clearances should precede fine topology. A support modeled as perfectly rigid can produce a light optimized bracket that overloads the real bearing or amplifies joint motion. Several contact directions, acceleration cases and assembly preload should be represented when they can determine the design.

机器人概念设计应先确定执行器位置、关节轴线与必要间隙，再细化拓扑。将支承建模为完全刚性，可能得到一个很轻的支架，却让实际轴承过载或放大关节位移。可能决定设计的接触方向、加速度工况和装配预紧，都应进入载荷集合。

Detailed topology output becomes a manufacturable part through interpretation: minimum member sizes, machining access, casting draft, additive-build orientation and inspectable joints. The material left after interpretation needs a fresh analysis. Optimization identifies an efficient force path within its model; production design gives that path a reliable physical realization.

拓扑结果需要经过工程化解释，才能变成可制造零件：最小杆件尺寸、刀具可达性、铸造拔模、增材制造方向与接头可检测性都要考虑。解释和重建后的零件需要重新分析。优化在模型内寻找高效传力路径，生产设计则让这条路径能够可靠地实现。
