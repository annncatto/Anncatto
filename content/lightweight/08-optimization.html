Optimization and Validation优化与验证

The material, section, process and system choices now form a candidate structure. Optimization refines that structure against the requirements established in the introduction. Its value depends on the engineering model: design variables, load cases, supports, material properties and manufacturing limits determine the problem being solved.

材料、截面、工艺和系统选择，共同形成候选结构。优化根据导论中确定的要求，进一步细化这个结构。它的价值取决于工程模型：设计变量、载荷工况、支承、材料性能和制造限制，共同规定正在求解的问题。

Concept, Scheme and Detail概念、方案与详细设计

The course separates concept design, scheme design, detailed design and verification. Concept design fixes the main architecture and functional sharing. Scheme design compares materials, load paths and structural forms. Detailed design sets dimensions, transitions and process features. Verification returns evidence about stiffness, strength, life and behavior under the intended loads. Later calculations refine decisions whose largest effects can arise at the concept stage.

课程区分概念设计、方案设计、详细设计与验证。概念阶段确定主要构型和功能共用，方案阶段比较材料、传力与结构形式，详细阶段确定尺寸、过渡和工艺特征，验证再给出预期载荷下的刚度、强度、寿命和行为证据。后续计算细化这些决策，而最大的影响可能在概念阶段已经出现。

{{course:stages}}

A numerical optimizer needs that engineering brief translated into a model. Fixed quantities describe the task and known conditions; free variables describe what may change. Constraints state acceptable performance. The objective selects among feasible alternatives. An optimizer can efficiently search this defined space, but a missing load case or an unrealistic support changes which design appears best.

数值优化需要先把工程要求翻译成模型。固定量描述任务与已知条件，自由变量描述允许改变的部分，约束规定可接受表现，目标则在可行方案中作出选择。优化器能够高效搜索这个空间，遗漏工况或不真实的支承，也会改变它认为最好的设计。

Loads Before Stress先确定载荷

Static loading gives only part of the demand. Where motion is relevant, multibody analysis can estimate dynamic forces and interface reactions, which are then transferred to the structural model. The mass distribution used for those loads should be consistent with the current design. Changing a member or relocating equipment can require updating the load calculation.

静载只覆盖部分需求。涉及运动时，可以通过多体分析估计动态力和接口反力，再传入结构模型。计算载荷所用的质量分布，需要与当前设计一致。改变构件或移动设备后，可能需要更新载荷计算。

The structural analysis checks how the loads enter through attachments and supports, how the material carries them, and how much the structure deforms. A coarse beam model can establish the main load path; shell or solid analysis resolves details where needed. Bearing contact, preload and connection stiffness should correspond to the real assembly. A perfectly fixed boundary can otherwise hide the deformation that controls the design.

结构分析检查载荷怎样通过连接与支承进入、材料怎样承载，以及结构变形多少。粗梁模型可以建立主传力路径，需要时再用壳或实体解析细节。轴承接触、预紧和连接刚度，应对应真实装配；完全固支边界可能掩盖真正主导设计的变形。

Stress plots are interpreted alongside displacement, load balance, stability and service life. Mesh refinement should check the quantity used to decide the design. A sharp idealized corner or a point load can create a local singularity that should not be mistaken for a converged physical peak. The detailed geometry and the actual area over which a force enters often resolve the relevant question.

应力云图需要与位移、载荷平衡、稳定性和寿命一起解释。网格加密应检查决策所用的量。理想尖角或点载荷可能形成局部奇异，不能将其当成已经收敛的物理峰值。详细几何与力实际进入的面积，往往决定需要采用的局部模型。

Topology Optimization拓扑优化

Topology optimization distributes material within an allowed domain. The course uses the SIMP density formulation: each element receives a relative density $\rho_e$, and its elastic modulus is penalized by a power $p$. Here $\rho_e$ is a dimensionless design variable; the material’s physical density remains a separate property.

拓扑优化在允许区域内分配材料。课程采用 SIMP 密度形式：给各单元分配相对密度 $\rho_e$，再用幂次 $p$ 惩罚其弹性模量。这里的 $\rho_e$ 是无量纲设计变量，材料本身的物理密度仍是另一项性能。

$$\begin{aligned}\min_{\{\rho_e\}}\quad &c=\mathbf U^TK(\boldsymbol\rho)\mathbf U,\\\text{subject to}\quad &K(\boldsymbol\rho)\mathbf U=\mathbf F,\\&\sum_e\rho_eV_e\le V^*,\qquad0<\rho_{\min}\le\rho_e\le1,\\&E_e=\rho_e^pE_0.\end{aligned}$$

$K$ is assembled stiffness, $\mathbf U$ nodal displacement, $\mathbf F$ the specified nodal load, $V_e$ element volume and $V^*$ the available material volume. Under the stated linear force loading, compliance $c$ also equals $\mathbf F^T\mathbf U$. Reducing it reduces load-weighted displacement, so the formulation seeks stiffness within a volume budget.

$K$ 是装配刚度，$\mathbf U$ 是节点位移，$\mathbf F$ 是规定节点载荷，$V_e$ 是单元体积，$V^*$ 是材料体积预算。在上述线性力加载条件下，柔度 $c$ 也等于 $\mathbf F^T\mathbf U$。降低柔度，就是减少载荷加权的位移，因此这一形式在给定体积内寻找更高刚度。

With $p$ near 3, an intermediate-density element contributes much less stiffness than its material fraction. The penalty encourages material to collect into clearer load paths instead of remaining uniformly grey. A small lower density keeps the stiffness problem solvable. Filtering and a minimum feature length help control checkerboards and mesh dependence. The penalty curve explains this tendency; it is not itself a solved component.

$p$ 接近 3 时，中间密度单元的刚度贡献，相对于其材料份额明显更低。惩罚促使材料聚成更明确的传力路径，而不均匀停留在灰色状态。较小的密度下限让刚度方程保持可解，滤波与最小特征尺寸有助于控制棋盘格和网格依赖。惩罚曲线解释这一趋势，真正的结构还需要求解。

The material moves toward the outer regions that provide bending leverage and into diagonals that transfer force toward the supports. This connects numerical optimization to the section-efficiency and direct-load-path ideas already introduced. 材料向提供抗弯力臂的外侧区域集中，并形成将力送往支承的斜向路径。这将数值优化与前面的截面效率、直接传力联系起来。课件截图中的迭代次数和柔度数值，对应它自己的模型；下面的计算是相同载荷思路的另一组算例。

A 40% design-domain volume fraction does not imply a 60% whole-product mass reduction. Protected attachments, other components and required assembly features remain. A result also needs checks for other load directions, stress, stability and manufacturing. Changing those conditions can change the preferred topology.

设计域保留 40% 材料体积，并不代表完整产品减重 60%。受保护的连接区、其他部件和必要装配特征仍然存在。结果还需要检查其他载荷方向、应力、稳定和制造，改变这些条件，也可能改变优选拓扑。

From Optimized Shape to Tested Structure从优化外形到实物

Rebuild the result as manufacturable geometry with the required walls, fillets, holes, joining surfaces and tool access. Re-analyze this rebuilt part rather than assuming that it retains the numerical result. Casting, machining or additive manufacture can impose different practical interpretations of the same load path.

将结果重建为可制造几何，加入必要壁厚、圆角、孔、连接面和刀具空间，再分析重建后的零件。铸造、切削或增材制造，对同一传力路径可能提出不同的工程化实现。

The course’s verification loop combines simulation with specimen and component tests. Static loading checks stiffness and load transfer. Strain measurements test whether forces follow the predicted path. Modal tests check vibration and damping. Repeated loading, impact and thermal exposure examine the service conditions that justified the design allowance. The measurements then update the model before another design comparison.

课程中的验证闭环，将仿真与试样、构件试验结合。静载检查刚度与传力，应变测量检查力是否沿预测路径传递，模态试验检查振动与阻尼，循环加载、冲击和热环境检查原先支持设计许用值的使用条件。测量结果再用于更新模型，支持下一次设计比较。

The final decision includes dimensions, tolerances, process repeatability, inspection and reliability. A smaller nominal mass is useful when the finished structure continues to satisfy the original requirements. This returns the optimization result to the definition of lightweight design, rather than treating the density plot as the end of the process.

最终判断需要包含尺寸、公差、工艺一致性、检测与可靠性。完成后的结构继续满足原定要求，较小的名义质量才具有意义。这也让优化结果回到轻量化设计的定义。
