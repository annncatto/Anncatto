Energy methods and sizing能量法与尺寸优化

Stress checks determine whether a section carries a load; deformation checks calculate the resulting extension, deflection or rotation. Strain energy connects internal forces to displacement and makes it possible to compare different material distributions. Sizing optimization then chooses dimensions that minimize mass while satisfying those response limits.

应力校核判断截面能否承载，变形校核计算载荷造成的伸长、挠度或转角。应变能将内力与位移联系起来，可以用来比较不同的材料分布；尺寸优化则选择满足这些响应限值的最轻尺寸组合。

Elastic strain energy弹性应变能

Strain energy is the recoverable internal energy stored by elastic deformation. For quasi-static loading without dissipation, the external work equals the increase in strain energy. In a linear elastic system loaded from zero, a force rising to $F$ produces displacement $\delta$, so $U=\tfrac12F\delta$. The factor $1/2$ accounts for the loading path; the work is not $F\delta$.

应变能是结构因弹性变形而储存、卸载时可以释放的内能。 准静态且没有耗散时，外力所做的功等于应变能增量。线弹性系统由零开始加载，力增至 $F$、位移增至 $\delta$，因此 $U=\tfrac12F\delta$；系数 $1/2$ 来自加载过程，不能将外力功写成 $F\delta$。

For a straight, uncoupled beam described along its centroidal axis $x$, axial force $N$, bending moment $M_y$, torque $T$ and shear force $V$ contribute the following energies. The bending term is written for one principal plane; a second plane adds its own term.

对沿形心轴 $x$ 描述、各变形模式不耦合的直梁，轴力 $N$、弯矩 $M_y$、扭矩 $T$ 和剪力 $V$ 分别贡献以下能量。弯曲项先写一个主平面，另一主平面可增加相应项。

$$U=\int_0^L\left[\frac{N^2}{2EA}+\frac{M_y^2}{2EI_y}+\frac{T^2}{2GJ_t}+\frac{V^2}{2\kappa GA}\right]\,dx.$$

Axial extension轴向拉压$EA$$U_N=N^2L/(2EA)$

Torsion扭转$GJ_t$$U_T=T^2L/(2GJ_t)$

Here $E$ and $G$ are Young’s and shear moduli, $A$ is area, $I_y$ is the bending second moment, $J_t$ is the Saint-Venant torsion constant, and $\kappa$ is the shear correction factor under the convention $V=\kappa GA\gamma$. All four energies have units of joules. For a circular section, $J_t$ equals the polar area moment; this substitution is generally incorrect for an open I-section. Restrained warping or material coupling requires additional terms.

其中 $E$、$G$ 分别为杨氏模量与剪切模量，$A$ 为面积，$I_y$ 为弯曲截面二次矩，$J_t$ 为 Saint-Venant 扭转常数；$\kappa$ 采用 $V=\kappa GA\gamma$ 约定下的剪切修正系数。四项能量的单位均为焦耳。圆截面的 $J_t$ 等于面积极惯性矩，开口工字截面通常不能这样替代；翘曲受约束或材料存在耦合时，还需补充相应能量项。

The integral form permits $E$, $A$ and the section properties to vary along the member. Add the energies of all members in a frame, retaining any deformation mode that materially contributes. A short beam may need shear energy even when a slender beam can omit it. Use $U$ for stored energy: total potential energy also contains the potential of the applied loads.

积分形式允许 $E$、$A$ 与截面性质沿杆长变化；框架则对各杆件求和，并保留影响显著的变形模式。细长梁可以忽略的剪切能，在短梁中可能不可忽略。这里用 $U$ 表示储能；总势能还包含外载荷的势，不能与应变能混为一谈。

Castigliano’s theorem卡氏定理求位移

Express the energy in terms of independent applied loads. For a stable, small-deformation linear elastic structure, differentiating with respect to a force gives displacement at that force’s point and in its direction; differentiating with respect to a moment gives the corresponding rotation. Other independent loads remain fixed during differentiation.

将能量写成独立外载荷的函数。对稳定、小变形的线弹性结构，能量对某个力求偏导，得到该力作用点沿其方向的位移；对某个力矩求偏导，得到对应转角。求导时，其余独立外载荷保持不变。

$$\delta_i=\frac{\partial U}{\partial F_i},\qquad\theta_i=\frac{\partial U}{\partial Q_i}.$$

Strictly, the force-derivative theorem uses complementary energy $U^*$, which equals $U$ under the linear elastic assumptions above. Geometric nonlinearity, plasticity or load-dependent contact prevents direct use of these simple quadratic expressions. See Schoeftner’s formulation of Castigliano’s method.

严格而言，对力求导的定理使用余能 $U^*$；在上述线弹性条件下，它与应变能 $U$ 相等。存在几何非线性、塑性或随加载改变的接触时，不能直接套用这些简单的二次表达式。见 Schoeftner 对卡氏方法的表述。

Example: a tip-loaded cantilever例题：悬臂梁端部挠度

A cantilever of length $L$ and constant rigidity $EI_y$ carries a transverse force $F$ at its free end. Neglect shear deformation and find the tip deflection in the force direction.

长 $L$、抗弯刚度 $EI_y$ 不变的悬臂梁，自由端承受横向集中力 $F$。忽略剪切变形，求自由端沿载荷方向的挠度。

Measure $x$ from the clamp. The bending-moment magnitude is $|M_y(x)|=F(L-x)$. Substituting its square into the energy gives the displacement in the force direction:

令 $x$ 从固支端量起，弯矩大小为 $|M_y(x)|=F(L-x)$。将其平方代入能量式，求得沿外力方向的位移：

$$U=\int_0^L\frac{F^2(L-x)^2}{2EI_y}\,dx=\frac{F^2L^3}{6EI_y},\qquad\delta=\frac{\partial U}{\partial F}=\frac{FL^3}{3EI_y}.$$

This is the same result as integrating $EI_yw''$ twice with zero clamp displacement and slope. Energy differentiation is especially useful when only a few displacement components are required, or when a frame contains several members and piecewise section properties. It still requires correct internal forces and support compatibility.

这一结果与对挠曲微分方程积分、代入固支端位移与转角均为零所得结果一致。当只需求少数位移分量，或框架包含多个杆件和分段截面时，能量求导尤其方便；内力与支承相容条件仍须正确建立。

The root half stores $1-(1/2)^3=87.5\%$ of the bending energy. Increasing $EI_y$ where the bending moment is large can therefore reduce this load-point displacement efficiently. Changing the load changes the moment distribution and the regions where added rigidity is most effective.

根部半跨储存的弯曲能量占 $1-(1/2)^3=87.5\%$。因此，在弯矩较大的区域提高 $EI_y$，能较有效地降低这一加载点的位移。改变载荷后，弯矩分布及最值得加强的位置也会改变。

A displacement without an applied load无外载处的位移

When no actual force acts at the requested point and direction, introduce a dummy force $P$, form $U(F,P)$, differentiate, and only then set $P=0$. A dummy moment does the same for rotation. This order retains the cross terms needed for the result. Jönköping University: energy methods.

若待求位移的位置与方向没有实际外力，就引入虚拟力 $P$，建立 $U(F,P)$，先求导，再令 $P=0$；求转角时同理引入虚拟力矩。这一顺序保留了结果所需的交叉项。Jönköping 大学：能量法。

For the same cantilever, choose a dummy tip moment $Q$ in the tip-rotation direction. The bending energy and resulting rotation are:

对上述悬臂梁，在自由端沿待求转角方向增加虚拟力矩 $Q$，弯曲能与端部转角为：

$$U(F,Q)=\int_0^L\frac{[F(L-x)+Q]^2}{2EI_y}\,dx,\qquad\theta=\left.\frac{\partial U}{\partial Q}\right|_{Q=0}=\frac{FL^2}{2EI_y}.$$

Exercise: a centre-loaded simply supported beam练习：简支梁中点挠度

A simply supported beam of length $L$ and constant $EI_y$ carries a central point force $F$. Derive its midpoint deflection by the energy method.

长 $L$、抗弯刚度 $EI_y$ 不变的简支梁，中点承受集中力 $F$。用能量法推导中点挠度。

Symmetry gives reactions $F/2$ at both supports. On the left half, $|M_y|=Fx/2$; the right half stores equal energy. The midpoint displacement in the load direction is:

对称性给出两端支反力各为 $F/2$。左半跨弯矩大小为 $|M_y|=Fx/2$，右半跨储能相同，沿载荷方向的中点位移为：

$$U=2\int_0^{L/2}\frac{(Fx/2)^2}{2EI_y}\,dx=\frac{F^2L^3}{96EI_y},\qquad \delta_{\mathrm{mid}}=\frac{\partial U}{\partial F}=\frac{FL^3}{48EI_y}.$$

At equal $F$, $L$ and $EI_y$, this displacement is one-sixteenth of the cantilever’s tip displacement. Support conditions change the internal moment distribution and energy; identical material and section dimensions do not imply identical structural stiffness.

在 $F$、$L$ 和 $EI_y$ 相同的条件下，中点挠度只有悬臂梁端挠度的 $1/16$。支承条件改变内弯矩分布与储能；材料和截面尺寸相同，并不意味着结构刚度相同。

I-section efficiency工字截面效率

At fixed material, area and member length, mass stays constant while the section can redistribute material. For bending about $y$, $I_y=\int_Az^2\,dA$: material farther from the neutral axis contributes more to bending rigidity. The corresponding strength measure is $Z_y=I_y/c$, where $c$ is the farthest fibre distance. Increasing depth changes both $I_y$ and $c$, so the stiffness and strength gains differ.

材料、截面积和杆长固定时，质量不变，但截面仍可重新分配材料。绕 $y$ 轴弯曲时，$I_y=\int_Az^2\,dA$，远离中性轴的材料对抗弯刚度贡献更大。相应的强度几何量是 $Z_y=I_y/c$，其中 $c$ 为最远纤维距离；增高截面会同时改变 $I_y$ 与 $c$，所以刚度增益与强度增益不同。

Compare a solid 20 × 20 mm square with a symmetric I-section of total depth $h=48$ mm, flange width $b=30$ mm, flange thickness $t_f=4$ mm and web thickness $t_w=4$ mm. Both have sharp ideal corners. Compare their areas, strong-axis second moments and elastic section moduli.

比较 20×20 mm 实心正方形与双对称工字截面。工字截面总高 $h=48$ mm、翼缘宽 $b=30$ mm、翼缘厚 $t_f=4$ mm、腹板厚 $t_w=4$ mm，两者均按理想直角计算。求截面积、强轴二次矩与弹性截面模量。

$$\begin{aligned}A_I&=2bt_f+t_w(h-2t_f)=2\times30\times4+4\times40=400\ \mathrm{mm^2},\\I_{y,I}&=\frac{bh^3-(b-t_w)(h-2t_f)^3}{12}=137\,813.3\ \mathrm{mm^4},\\Z_{y,I}&=\frac{2I_{y,I}}h=5\,742.2\ \mathrm{mm^3}.\end{aligned}$$

The square also has area 400 mm², with $I_{y,\square}=20^4/12=13\,333.3$ mm⁴ and $Z_{y,\square}=20^3/6=1\,333.3$ mm³. The I-section therefore gives 10.336 times the strong-axis bending rigidity at equal modulus, and 4.307 times the elastic bending-moment capacity at equal allowable stress.

正方形面积也为 400 mm²，$I_{y,\square}=20^4/12=13\,333.3$ mm⁴，$Z_{y,\square}=20^3/6=1\,333.3$ mm³。因此，同模量下工字截面的强轴抗弯刚度为 10.336 倍，同许用应力下的弹性弯矩承载能力为 4.307 倍。

Equal area alone does not determine an optimum depth-to-width ratio. The design must also specify the envelope, how material is divided between flanges and web, minimum thickness, load directions and stability limits. Making a web deeper and thinner can increase global bending efficiency while reducing local plate-buckling resistance. An open I-section also needs checks for weak-axis bending, torsion and lateral-torsional buckling.

仅有等面积约束，不能确定唯一的最优高宽比。还须明确空间包络、翼缘与腹板的材料分配、最小厚度、载荷方向和稳定性限制。腹板加高、变薄，可以提高整体抗弯效率，同时降低局部板屈曲承载能力；开口工字截面还须校核弱轴弯曲、扭转与侧向扭转屈曲。

Variables, objective and constraints变量、目标与约束

A sizing problem specifies adjustable dimensions $\mathbf x$, an objective such as mass $m(\mathbf x)$, and admissible-response and manufacturing constraints. The feasible set contains all designs satisfying every constraint. A mass contour contains designs with equal objective value.

尺寸优化需要明确可调尺寸 $\mathbf x$、质量等目标函数 $m(\mathbf x)$，以及响应限值和制造约束。可行域由同时满足全部约束的设计组成；质量等值线上的各设计具有相同目标值。

$$\min_{\mathbf x}\ m(\mathbf x),\qquad g_i(\mathbf x)\le0,\qquad h_j(\mathbf x)=0.$$

If reducing a dimension lowers mass until a response limit is reached, that limit becomes active at the optimum. Two competing limits may select an intersection, but an optimum can also lie on a smooth boundary, inside the feasible region, or along a whole set of equivalent designs. The objective and constraints must establish which case occurs.

若减小尺寸能持续减重，直到某个响应达到限值，该约束就会在最优点取等号。两个竞争约束可能确定一个交点，但最优点也可能位于光滑边界、可行域内部，或形成一组等效设计。具体属于哪一种，需要由目标与约束共同判断。

Example: minimum-mass tensile tube例题：受拉圆管最小质量

A uniform circular tube carries a concentric tensile force $F$. Choose outside diameter $D$ and wall thickness $t$ to minimize $m=\rho AL$ while satisfying $F/A\le\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$ and $t\le D/10$. Then add an axial-extension limit $\delta\le\delta_0$. Assume small-strain linear elasticity; joints are outside this bar model.

均匀圆管承受通过形心的轴向拉力 $F$。选择外径 $D$ 与壁厚 $t$，使 $m=\rho AL$ 最小，并满足 $F/A\le\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$ 与 $t\le D/10$；再加入轴向伸长约束 $\delta\le\delta_0$。按小应变线弹性计算，接头不包含在杆件模型内。

The exact area is $A=\pi(D-t)t$. Introduce mean diameter $d_m=D-t$, so $A=\pi d_mt$ is exact and $t\le D/10$ becomes $t\le d_m/9$. Replacing $d_m$ with outside diameter gives the thin-wall approximation $A\approx\pi Dt$; at $t/D=0.1$, that approximation overestimates area by 11.1%.

精确面积为 $A=\pi(D-t)t$。引入平均直径 $d_m=D-t$，则 $A=\pi d_mt$ 是精确式，$t\le D/10$ 等价于 $t\le d_m/9$。将平均直径换成外径才得到薄壁近似 $A\approx\pi Dt$；在 $t/D=0.1$ 时，这一近似将面积高估 11.1%。

$$\frac FA\le\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}},\qquad\delta=\frac{FL}{EA}\le\delta_0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad A\ge A_{\mathrm{req}}=\max\left(\frac F{\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}},\frac{FL}{E\delta_0}\right).$$

If the geometric and manufacturing bounds admit this area, the minimum mass is $\rho LA_{\mathrm{req}}$. Several diameter–thickness pairs can attain it. A tighter extension limit moves the minimum-area contour upward, but does not by itself select a unique diameter. For illustration, take $F=10$ kN, $L=0.5$ m, $E=70$ GPa, $\rho=2700$ kg/m³ and $\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}=100$ MPa.

若几何与制造边界允许达到这一面积，最小质量就是 $\rho LA_{\mathrm{req}}$，多个直径—壁厚组合都可能实现。更严格的伸长限制会使最小面积曲线上移，却不会单独确定唯一的直径。作数值演示，取 $F=10$ kN、$L=0.5$ m、$E=70$ GPa、$\rho=2700$ kg/m³、$\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}=100$ MPa。

第二种情况下，$d_m=60$ mm、$t=0.758$ mm 与 $d_m=40$ mm、$t=1.137$ mm，在圆整前具有相同的最小质量与伸长。空间包络、接头、搬运损伤和可采购管材规格，可以进一步区分两者；尺寸圆整到可制造规格后，应重新计算精确面积并逐项验算。

Pure axial tension does not introduce an axial-compression buckling constraint. A compressed, bent, externally pressurized or twisted tube is a different load problem: global column buckling and local shell instability may become governing, and shell imperfections matter. NASA SP-8007 treats those load cases and their design limitations.

纯轴向拉伸不引入轴压屈曲约束。若圆管承受压缩、弯曲、外压或扭转，则是另一组载荷问题，整体压杆屈曲和局部壳体失稳可能成为主导，几何缺陷也会影响结果。NASA SP-8007 分别讨论了这些工况及设计适用范围。

Convex optimization凸优化

A set is convex if the segment between any two of its points remains in the set. A function is convex if its value on that segment lies no higher than the interpolated endpoint values. A standard convex minimization problem has a convex objective and convex inequality functions on a convex domain, with affine equalities. Every local minimum is then global; uniqueness needs further conditions. Boyd and Vandenberghe: Convex Optimization.

若集合内任意两点的连线都仍在集合内，该集合就是凸集。若函数在这条连线上的值不高于两端函数值的线性插值，它就是凸函数。标准凸最小化问题要求定义域为凸集、目标和不等式约束函数为凸函数、等式约束为仿射关系；此时局部最优也是全局最优，但唯一性还需额外条件。Boyd 与 Vandenberghe：《凸优化》。

$$f(\lambda\mathbf x+(1-\lambda)\mathbf y)\le\lambda f(\mathbf x)+(1-\lambda)f(\mathbf y),\qquad0\le\lambda\le1.$$

Convexity depends on the formulation. In the tensile-tube example, mass is proportional to $d_mt$: this product is not a convex function of the two raw dimensions. If area is the only free variable, however, minimizing $\rho LA$ subject to $A\ge A_{\mathrm{req}}$ is a linear problem. Retaining both dimensions also permits a useful reformulation.

凸性取决于问题的表述。受拉圆管的质量正比于 $d_mt$，这个乘积并不是两个原始尺寸的凸函数；但若只把面积作为自由变量，最小化 $\rho LA$、满足 $A\ge A_{\mathrm{req}}$，就是线性问题。保留两个尺寸，也可以进行适当变换。

Choose positive reference lengths $d_*$ and $t_*$ and set $u=\ln(d_m/d_*)$, $v=\ln(t/t_*)$. Minimizing mass is equivalent to minimizing its logarithm, hence $u+v$. The area and thin-wall constraints become:

选取正参考长度 $d_*$、$t_*$，令 $u=\ln(d_m/d_*)$、$v=\ln(t/t_*)$。最小化质量与最小化其对数等价，因此目标可以写成 $u+v$。面积与薄壁条件变为：

$$\min_{u,v}(u+v),\qquad u+v\ge\ln\frac{A_{\mathrm{req}}}{\pi d_*t_*},\qquad v-u\le\ln\frac{d_*}{9t_*}.$$

Positive upper and lower bounds on mean diameter $d_m$ and wall thickness $t$ become affine bounds on $u$ and $v$, so this particular sizing problem is a linear program. The logarithms use dimensionless ratios. This reformulation does not establish convexity for general structural design: discrete materials, changing connectivity, penalized density interpolation, contact and many shape or stability constraints can produce nonconvex problems. A numerically converged design therefore needs both response verification and an appropriate account of optimization limits.

平均直径 $d_m$ 和壁厚 $t$ 的正值上下限也变成 $u$、$v$ 的仿射边界，因此这一特定尺寸问题可化为线性规划；对数中使用的均为无量纲比值。这不代表一般结构设计都是凸问题：离散材料选择、连接关系变化、带惩罚的密度插值、接触及许多形状或稳定性约束，都可能产生非凸问题。数值收敛后的设计仍须核验物理响应，并说明优化结果的适用边界。
