Mechanical Performance力学性能

A material property becomes a structural requirement through a load case. A member may be limited by permanent deformation, by excessive elastic displacement, by instability or by damage accumulated during service. The controlling requirement determines how much material must remain. Before choosing a lightweight material, identify the response that is setting the dimensions.

材料性能需要通过载荷工况，转化为结构要求。构件可能受永久变形限制，也可能受弹性位移过大、失稳或使用中的损伤限制。主导要求决定需要保留多少材料。选择轻质材料之前，先确认究竟是哪种响应决定了尺寸。

From Material Modulus to Structural Stiffness从材料模量到结构刚度

A tensile specimen provides the simplest connection. Apply axial force $F$ to a uniform member of initial length $L$ and area $A$, and measure its extension $\delta$. Dividing force by area gives nominal normal stress; dividing extension by length gives axial strain. Within the linear elastic range, the slope of the stress–strain curve is Young’s modulus:

拉伸试样提供了最简单的联系。对初始长度 $L$、面积 $A$ 均匀的杆件施加轴向力 $F$，测量伸长 $\delta$。力除以面积，得到名义正应力；伸长除以长度，得到轴向应变。在线弹性范围内，应力—应变曲线的斜率就是杨氏模量：

$$\sigma=\frac FA,\qquad\varepsilon=\frac\delta L,\qquad\sigma=E\varepsilon.$$
{{course:stressstrain}}

Substitution converts the material law into a force–displacement relation. This is the step that introduces structural stiffness:

代入后，材料关系转化为力—位移关系，结构刚度由此出现：

$$F=\frac{EA}{L}\delta,\qquad k=\frac F\delta=\frac{EA}{L}.$$

$E$ is a material modulus, measured in Pa; $k$ is the stiffness of this particular member, measured in N/m. Increasing area raises stiffness, while increasing length lowers it. Compliance describes displacement per force: $s=\delta/F=1/k$ in the linear case. A lightweight design can preserve stiffness through material, dimensions or both. Young’s modulus describes the material part of this relation.

$E$ 是材料模量，单位为 Pa；$k$ 是这根具体杆件的刚度，单位为 N/m。增大面积可以提高刚度，增加长度则降低刚度。柔度描述单位力引起的位移，在线性情形下为 $s=\delta/F=1/k$。轻量化可以通过材料、尺寸，或两者共同变化来保持刚度。杨氏模量描述的是其中的材料部分。

Shear introduces a second modulus, $G$, through $\tau=G\gamma$, where $\tau$ is shear stress and $\gamma$ shear strain. Poisson’s ratio $\nu$ describes transverse strain accompanying axial loading. These quantities matter because real members carry combinations of tension, compression, bending and torsion. Fibre laminates also require the appropriate directions: a large longitudinal modulus does not establish equally large shear or transverse stiffness.

剪切变形通过 $\tau=G\gamma$ 引入剪切模量 $G$，其中 $\tau$ 是剪应力，$\gamma$ 是剪应变。泊松比 $\nu$ 描述轴向加载伴随的横向应变。真实构件承受拉伸、压缩、弯曲和扭转的组合，因此需要这些不同性能。纤维层合板还必须对应方向：较高的纵向模量，并不能直接代表同样高的剪切和横向刚度。

Strength and Stiffness Set Different Dimensions强度与刚度分别决定什么

The tensile curve continues beyond its initial slope. Yield strength identifies the onset of the specified permanent deformation, while ultimate strength and fracture describe later parts of the response. Ductility describes how much deformation can occur before failure. A high-strength alloy may retain almost the same elastic modulus as a lower-strength alloy, so its advantage depends on what limits the part.

拉伸曲线在初始直线段之后继续发展。屈服强度对应规定永久变形的出现，极限强度和断裂则描述之后的响应；塑性反映失效前能够经历多少变形。高强合金的弹性模量，可能与较低强度合金几乎相同，它的优势因此取决于零件受什么限制。

For a strength-limited tie, area must keep the stress below its allowable value. For a stiffness-limited tie, area must keep extension below its allowed displacement. The two checks share $F$ and $A$, but compare different responses:

强度主导的拉杆，需要用足够面积将应力限制在许用值内；刚度主导的拉杆，需要用足够面积将伸长限制在允许位移内。两个检查使用相同的 $F$ 与 $A$，比较的响应却不同：

$$\frac FA\le\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}},\qquad\frac{FL}{EA}\le\delta_{\max}.$$

If elastic displacement controls the design, increasing yield strength alone does not permit a smaller section. If yield controls it, higher allowable strength can reduce area until displacement, stability or another requirement becomes active. This is why the selection method later uses different indices for stiffness- and strength-limited members.

弹性位移决定尺寸时，只提高屈服强度，不能直接缩小截面。屈服决定尺寸时，提高许用强度可以减少面积，直到位移、稳定性或其他要求接替成为主导。这就是后文对刚度主导和强度主导构件使用不同材料指数的原因。

Bending: Why Section Geometry Appears弯曲为什么引入截面几何

A transverse load bends a beam rather than stretching its entire section uniformly. One side lengthens and the other shortens; the neutral axis separates them. Material farther from that axis experiences a larger longitudinal strain. Under small-deflection Euler–Bernoulli assumptions, bending normal stress is proportional to that distance:

横向载荷会让梁弯曲，截面上的材料不再均匀伸长。一侧拉长，另一侧缩短，中性轴将两者分开。材料离中性轴越远，纵向应变越大。在小变形 Euler–Bernoulli 梁假设下，弯曲正应力与这一距离成比例：

$$\sigma_x(y)=\frac{M_b y}{I_A},\qquad I_A=\int_A y^2\,dA.$$

$M_b$ is bending moment, $y$ distance from the neutral axis, and $I_A$ the second moment of area, with units m⁴. Thus $M_b/I_A$ gives the stress gradient through the section. The largest normal stress is $M_bc/I_A$, where $c$ is the outer-fibre distance. The section modulus $Z=I_A/c$ packages the geometry for a strength comparison.

$M_b$ 是弯矩，$y$ 是到中性轴的距离，$I_A$ 是截面二次矩，单位为 m⁴。因此，$M_b/I_A$ 表示应力沿截面高度的变化率。最大正应力为 $M_bc/I_A$，$c$ 是最外侧纤维距离；截面模量 $Z=I_A/c$ 则将强度比较所需的几何信息合在一起。

Deformation introduces the modulus again. Using $\varepsilon_x=\sigma_x/E$ and the beam relation $\varepsilon_x=\kappa y$ gives curvature $\kappa=M_b/(EI_A)$. The product $EI_A$ is bending rigidity. Material and geometry appear together because the same material amount can be placed closer to or farther from the neutral axis.

计算变形时，模量再次进入。将 $\varepsilon_x=\sigma_x/E$ 与梁的几何关系 $\varepsilon_x=\kappa y$ 结合，得到曲率 $\kappa=M_b/(EI_A)$。乘积 $EI_A$ 是抗弯刚度。材料与几何共同出现，是因为相同的材料用量，也可以布置在距中性轴不同的位置。

For the course’s cantilever example, the support and load position fix the bending-moment distribution. A tip force produces the greatest moment at the root. Integrating the curvature along a uniform beam gives its tip deflection:

课程中的悬臂梁算例，由支承和载荷位置确定弯矩分布。末端集中力使根部弯矩最大；沿均匀梁积分曲率，可以得到末端挠度：

{{lightweight:beam}}
$$\delta=\frac{FL^3}{3EI_A},\qquad k_b=\frac{3EI_A}{L^3}.$$

Length now enters cubically: doubling $L$ at fixed load and section multiplies displacement by eight. Increasing section depth can be much more effective than increasing area near the neutral axis. These geometric freedoms explain why a bending material index differs from the axial index, even though both use the same Young’s modulus.

长度在这里以三次方进入：载荷与截面不变，长度翻倍，位移增加到八倍。增加截面高度，可能比在中性轴附近增加面积更有效。正是这些几何自由度，让受弯构件与轴向构件使用不同的选材指数，即使两者采用相同的杨氏模量。

A thin plate carries bending in two in-plane directions. Its thickness affects bending rigidity cubically, so reducing a wall slightly can impose a large displacement penalty. Torsion instead depends on shear stiffness and whether the section provides a closed shear-flow path. Those distinctions guide both the section choice and which modulus belongs in the selection index.

薄板通过两个面内方向的弯曲承载，厚度以三次方影响抗弯刚度，因此少量减薄，也可能明显增加位移。扭转则依赖剪切刚度，以及截面是否提供闭合剪流路径。这些区别同时影响截面形式和选材时采用的模量。

Stability and Dynamic Response稳定性与动态响应

A slender compressed member may buckle before its material yields. A wide, thin wall may buckle locally while the overall beam still appears adequate. Moving material outward improves bending efficiency but also makes wall stability more important. The useful wall thickness is therefore set by several conditions together, including load introduction at joints and resistance to local denting.

细长受压杆可能在材料屈服前屈曲，宽而薄的壁板也可能先局部失稳。材料外移提高抗弯效率，同时让壁板稳定性更加重要。因此，有效壁厚需要由多项条件共同确定，也包括接头加载和局部抗凹陷能力。

Dynamic stiffness adds another reason to preserve geometry. In the course’s simplified vibration model, the natural angular frequency depends on stiffness relative to participating mass:

动态响应进一步要求保持几何刚度。课程中的简化振动模型，用刚度与参与质量的比值描述固有角频率：

$$\omega_n=\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}.$$

Here $m$ is the mass participating in that model, not automatically the entire assembly mass. Halving it at unchanged stiffness raises the frequency by $\sqrt2$; reducing stiffness at the same time can cancel the benefit. A lighter wall supporting the same heavy attachment may reduce frequency because most of the attached mass remains.

$m$ 是参与这一模型的质量，并不自动等于整个装配体质量。刚度不变、质量减半时，频率提高到 $\sqrt2$ 倍；同时降低刚度，就可能抵消收益。薄壁上如果仍安装相同的重附件，减薄可能降低频率，因为大部分附加质量仍然保留。

In a controlled mechanism, a flexible mode changes the response near resonance. Higher feedback gain can excite that motion while a motor-side sensor still reports an accurate motor angle. Tracking depends on the structural response between actuator and working point, as well as damping, delay and sensor location. This connects the course’s stiffness requirement to useful operating bandwidth; there is no universal fixed resonance-to-bandwidth ratio.

受控机构的柔性模态，会改变共振附近的响应。提高反馈增益可能激发这种运动，电机侧传感器却仍报告准确的电机角度。跟踪能力取决于执行器与工作点之间的结构响应，也取决于阻尼、延迟与测量位置。这将课程中的刚度要求连接到可用工作带宽，共振频率与带宽之间没有通用的固定比例。

Fatigue, Fracture and Impact疲劳、断裂与冲击

Static strength covers one loading condition. Fatigue addresses repeated loading: stress amplitude, mean stress, number of cycles, surface condition and environment determine the relevant fatigue strength. A gait, a machine cycle or repeated tool reversal can accumulate damage even when each individual load stays below static yield. The appropriate comparison uses fatigue data for the required life and load ratio.

静强度面对一次加载，疲劳面对反复加载。应力幅、平均应力、循环次数、表面状态与环境，共同决定相关的疲劳强度。步态、机器工作循环和工具反复换向，即使单次载荷低于静态屈服，也可能积累损伤。因此，应采用对应目标寿命与应力比的疲劳数据。

Fracture toughness describes resistance to crack extension. A scratch, hole edge, manufacturing defect or impact can introduce a crack whose behavior is not captured by nominal stress alone. Toughness, fatigue strength and their density-normalized values are useful selection properties when crack tolerance or cyclic life controls the design. Their test conditions must accompany the number. Fracture mechanics gives the corresponding crack-based viewpoint.

断裂韧度描述抵抗裂纹扩展的能力。划痕、孔边、制造缺陷或冲击都可能引入裂纹，其行为无法仅由名义应力概括。当裂纹容限或循环寿命主导设计时，韧度、疲劳强度及相应的密度归一化性能，就成为选材依据，数值需要附带试验条件。断裂力学提供了这种基于裂纹的分析视角。

Impact applies load over a short time and can excite structural vibration or produce a high local strain rate. The absorbed work is the area under a force–displacement curve. A stiff member can store elastic energy; plastic crushing, damping and controlled damage can dissipate energy. The useful choice depends on whether the part should recover, protect another component or be replaced after an impact.

冲击在短时间内加载，可能激发结构振动，也可能产生较高局部应变率。吸收功对应力—位移曲线下的面积：高刚度构件可以储存弹性能，塑性压溃、阻尼与受控损伤可以耗散能量。怎样选择，取决于构件需要恢复形状、保护其他部件，还是在冲击后更换。

{{course:energy}}

These mechanisms explain the course’s emphasis on material beyond initial modulus. Matrix toughening and thin plies act on damage formation and propagation. A sandwich needs faces that resist bending and a core that carries shear without crushing. A thin composite panel can suffer hidden delamination after impact, reducing its subsequent compressive capacity. The relevant property is the performance that remains under the intended service history.

这也解释了课程为何关注初始模量之外的材料性能。基体增韧与薄层化改变损伤形成和扩展；夹层需要面板抗弯、芯材承受剪切而不压溃；薄壁复合板受到冲击后，可能产生难以从外观识别的分层，降低后续抗压能力。真正相关的是，在预期使用历程下还剩多少性能。

Thermal and Environmental Performance热与环境性能

Temperature introduces physical properties alongside mechanical ones. Thermal conductivity determines how easily heat reaches a cooling surface; thermal expansion changes dimensions and fits; heat capacity affects transient heating. Friction, motors and other heat sources can make a lightweight housing part of the thermal path. An insulating interface can interrupt that path even when each adjacent part conducts well.

温度将物理性能带入力学设计。热导率决定热量多容易传到散热表面，热膨胀改变尺寸与配合，热容影响暂态升温。摩擦、电机等热源，可能让轻质壳体同时成为热路径；即使相邻零件都导热，隔热接口仍可能阻断这条路径。

Different expansion in a metal–composite connection can change preload or introduce thermal stress. CFRP conductivity depends on fibre type, direction and laminate, with the through-thickness path often limited by the matrix and interfaces. Corrosion, moisture and oxidation further change usable performance. A material selected for low mass must retain its required function in this actual environment.

金属与复合材料连接中的膨胀差异，可能改变预紧或引入热应力。CFRP 导热率依赖纤维种类、方向与铺层，厚度方向路径常受基体和界面限制。腐蚀、湿度与氧化，又会改变可用性能。因此，按质量选择的材料，还需要在实际环境中维持功能。

The mechanical checks have now identified the quantities that matter: modulus for deformation, allowable strength for failure, shear modulus for torsion, and fatigue, toughness and thermal properties when service conditions require them. The next step is to combine the controlling property with density and the dimensions that are free to change. That combination produces a material index.

这些检查已经指出各项性能的用途：模量用于限制变形，许用强度用于限制失效，剪切模量用于扭转；使用条件需要时，再引入疲劳、韧度和热性能。接下来，将主导性能与密度、允许变化的尺寸组合，就能得到材料指数。
