Sandwich structures夹层结构

A sandwich structure combines two thin, stiff faces with a light core. Under bending, one face is mainly in tension and the other in compression. The core keeps them apart, transfers transverse shear and supports the compressed face against local buckling. Adhesive interfaces transmit loads between the layers; a separation there changes the load path.

夹层结构由两层薄而刚的面板和中间的轻质芯材组成。受弯时，一侧面板主要受拉，另一侧主要受压；芯材维持两面板的间距、传递横向剪力，并为受压面板提供局部支承。胶层负责层间传力，一旦脱粘，原有传力关系便会改变。

Faces and core面板与芯材

Let each face have thickness $t_f$, the core thickness be $h_c$, and the beam width be $b$. The total thickness $h=h_c+2t_f$ differs from the face-centroid separation $d=h_c+t_f$. In the thin-face approximation, bending moment $M$ is carried mainly by a force couple $N_fd=M$. Increasing $d$ reduces the face force required for the same moment.

设每层面板厚度为 $t_f$，芯材厚度为 $h_c$，梁宽为 $b$。总厚度 $h=h_c+2t_f$ 与面板中面间距 $d=h_c+t_f$ 是两个不同的尺寸。薄面板近似下，弯矩 $M$ 主要由两面板的轴向力偶承担，即 $N_fd=M$。间距增大后，承担同一弯矩所需的面板轴向力减小。

$$h=h_c+2t_f,\qquad d=h_c+t_f,\qquad N_f\simeq\frac{M}{d},\qquad |\sigma_f|\simeq\frac{|M|}{bt_fd}.$$
{{course:sandwichsection}}

For bonded layers in small pure bending, axial strain is continuous and varies linearly through depth: $\varepsilon_x=-\kappa z$. Normal stress is $\sigma_x=-E(z)\kappa z$, so it jumps where the modulus changes. These are axial stresses, not interface-normal tractions. With unchanged face material and thickness, the dominant bending rigidity grows approximately as $d^2$. A low-density core makes that separation economical, but adding core thickness still adds mass $\rho_c bL\,\Delta h_c$.

各层粘接可靠、发生小变形纯弯曲时，轴向应变沿高度连续且呈线性分布：$\varepsilon_x=-\kappa z$。正应力则为 $\sigma_x=-E(z)\kappa z$，因此在模量改变的界面处发生跳变；这里讨论的是轴向正应力，并非界面法向牵引。面板材料和厚度不变时，主导抗弯刚度近似随 $d^2$ 增长。低密度芯材使增大间距的代价较小，但增加芯厚仍会增加 $\rho_c bL\,\Delta h_c$ 的质量。

Layered section rigidity分层截面刚度

The elastic contributions of the layers are added using their respective moduli $E_k$ along the beam axis. For layer $k$, let $A_k=b\Delta h_k$, $I_{c,k}$ be its second moment about its centroid, and $z_k$ its distance from the neutral axis. Axial rigidity has unit N; bending rigidity $D$ has unit N·m²:

各层的弹性贡献需要按各自沿梁轴方向的模量 $E_k$ 加权。对第 $k$ 层，面积为 $A_k=b\Delta h_k$，绕自身形心轴的截面二次矩为 $I_{c,k}$，形心距中性轴为 $z_k$。等效拉压刚度的单位为 N，抗弯刚度 $D$ 的单位为 N·m²：

$$(EA)_{\mathrm{eq}}=\sum_kE_kA_k,\qquad D=(EI)_{\mathrm{eq}}=\sum_kE_k\left(I_{c,k}+A_kz_k^2\right).$$

For the symmetric three-layer section, the neutral axis lies at the midplane, and the two face centroids are at $z=\pm d/2$. The result separates each face’s own bending contribution, its parallel-axis contribution, and the core contribution:

对称三层截面的中性轴位于中面，两面板形心位于 $z=\pm d/2$。将各层面积和距离代入，可分清面板自身的抗弯贡献、平行轴项以及芯材贡献：

$$\begin{aligned}(EA)_{\mathrm{eq}}&=2E_fbt_f+E_cbh_c,\\D&=\frac{E_fbt_f^3}{6}+\frac{E_fbt_fd^2}{2}+\frac{E_cbh_c^3}{12}.\end{aligned}$$

For an unsymmetric section, first find the stiffness-weighted neutral axis, $z_0=\sum E_kA_k\bar z_k/\sum E_kA_k$, and use distances from that axis. In a thin-face, low-core-modulus sandwich, the face-separation term often dominates. This does not mean that core shear deformation can also be discarded.

非对称截面需要先求刚度加权的中性轴 $z_0=\sum E_kA_k\bar z_k/\sum E_kA_k$，再以该轴计算距离。薄面板、低模量芯材的夹层梁通常由面板间距项主导抗弯刚度，但这并不意味着芯材剪切变形也可以忽略。

Section example截面刚度例题

A symmetric sandwich has two aluminium faces, each $t_f=1$ mm thick, and a foam core $h_c=20$ mm thick with a much smaller Young’s modulus. Explain why the core contribution to bending rigidity may be neglected.

对称夹层板的两层铝面板各厚 $t_f=1$ mm，泡沫芯材厚 $h_c=20$ mm，芯材杨氏模量很小。说明为什么计算抗弯刚度时可以忽略芯材的贡献。

No numerical moduli or width are specified, so the comparison should retain $E_c/E_f$. Here $h=22$ mm and $d=21$ mm. Dividing the second moments by the common width gives:

题目未给出具体模量和宽度，因此保留模量比 $E_c/E_f$ 进行比较。此时总厚度为 22 mm，面板中面距为 21 mm；将截面二次矩除以共同的宽度：

$$\begin{aligned}\frac{I_f}{b}&=2\left[\frac{1^3}{12}+1\left(\frac{21}{2}\right)^2\right]=220.667\ \mathrm{mm^3},\\\frac{I_c}{b}&=\frac{20^3}{12}=666.667\ \mathrm{mm^3},\\\frac{D_c}{D_f}&=\frac{E_cI_c}{E_fI_f}=3.02115\frac{E_c}{E_f}.\end{aligned}$$

If, for illustration, $E_c/E_f=10^{-3}$, the core adds about 0.302% of the faces’ bending rigidity. Neglecting it is then reasonable for a percent-level estimate. The numerical criterion is the rigidity ratio, not merely the statement that the core lies near the neutral axis. Its shear modulus still controls part of the deflection and stability.

例如再取 $E_c/E_f=10^{-3}$，芯材抗弯贡献约为两面板的 0.302%，在百分比精度的估算中可以忽略。判断依据是刚度比，而不能仅凭“芯材靠近中性轴”下结论。芯材的剪切模量仍会影响挠度与稳定性。

Shear deformation and buckling剪切变形与屈曲

Separating the faces can produce high bending rigidity while the soft core still permits appreciable shear deformation. Write the effective shear rigidity as $S$, with unit N. A simple uniform-core-shear model takes $S\simeq\kappa_sG_cb h_c$ and $\kappa_s\simeq1$; a different shear distribution requires its corresponding effective stiffness. For a simply supported beam under a central force $P$:

面板分开后，梁可以具有很高的抗弯刚度，但软芯材仍可能产生明显剪切变形。记有效剪切刚度为 $S$，单位为 N。将芯材剪切视为均匀分布的简化模型取 $S\simeq\kappa_sG_cb h_c$、$\kappa_s\simeq1$；采用其他剪切分布时，应使用相应的等效刚度。对跨中承受集中力 $P$ 的简支梁：

$$\delta=\delta_b+\delta_s\simeq\frac{PL^3}{48D}+\frac{PL}{4S}.$$

Shear flexibility also reduces the axial buckling load. The usual shear-corrected column approximation combines the Euler load $P_E$ and the effective shear rigidity as follows. Both terms on the right have unit N⁻¹:

剪切柔度也会降低轴向受压时的屈曲载荷。常用的剪切修正梁柱近似，将 Euler 临界力 $P_E$ 与有效剪切刚度组合为下式，右侧两项的单位均为 N⁻¹：

$$\frac1{P_{\mathrm{cr}}}\simeq\frac1{P_E}+\frac1S,\qquad P_{\mathrm{cr}}\simeq\frac{P_E}{1+P_E/S},\qquad P_E=\frac{\pi^2D}{(\mu L)^2}.$$

When $S\gg P_E$, the Euler result is recovered. If $S=P_E$, the estimate is halved. For very large bending rigidity at fixed $S$, the critical force approaches $S$ rather than increasing indefinitely. The effective-length factor $\mu$ below belongs to ideal Euler end conditions; actual joints must supply the assumed rotational restraint.

当 $S\gg P_E$ 时，结果接近 Euler 解；当 $S=P_E$ 时，临界力约减半。如果不断提高抗弯刚度而保持 $S$ 不变，临界力将趋近 $S$，不会无限增长。下表的有效长度系数 $\mu$ 对应理想 Euler 边界，实际连接需要能够提供假定的转动约束。

Local failure modes局部失效模式

A section can satisfy the global buckling check yet fail locally. Four distinct checks are face yielding or material failure, core shear failure, face wrinkling on the elastic core, and face dimpling between honeycomb cell walls. Dimpling is a face instability; core crushing under a concentrated bearing load is a different mechanism. The Hexcel design guide also includes adhesive load transfer, overall stiffness and local compression in its design checks.

即使满足整体屈曲要求，截面仍可能先发生局部失效。需要区分面板屈服或材料破坏、芯材剪切破坏、面板在弹性芯材上的皱褶，以及面板在蜂窝胞壁之间的局部凹陷。胞格凹陷属于面板失稳，不能与集中承压引起的芯材压溃混为一谈。Hexcel 设计指南还将胶层传力、整体变形与局部承压列入校核。

Face wrinkling is a short-wavelength instability of a compressed face supported by the core. For a common elastic, sufficiently thick-core approximation, its critical stress is estimated using the face modulus in the loading direction $E_f$, core through-thickness modulus $E_{c,z}$ and core transverse shear modulus $G_{c,xz}$:

面板皱褶是受压面板在芯材支承上的短波失稳。在常用的线弹性、足够厚芯材近似下，临界应力由面板沿载荷方向的模量 $E_f$、芯材厚度方向模量 $E_{c,z}$ 和横向剪切模量 $G_{c,xz}$ 估算：

$$\sigma_{\mathrm{wr}}=C\left(E_fE_{c,z}G_{c,xz}\right)^{1/3}.$$

The coefficient depends on the core model, boundary assumptions and imperfections. Hexcel’s preliminary example uses $C=0.5$. The NASA review of facesheet wrinkling distinguishes thick- and finite-core models and their different coefficients. For an isotropic core with fixed Poisson’s ratio, $G_c=E_c/[2(1+\nu_c)]$ reduces the expression to:

系数取决于芯材模型、边界假设和初始缺陷，Hexcel 的初筛示例采用 $C=0.5$。NASA 面板皱褶综述区分了厚芯材、有限芯厚等模型及其不同系数。若芯材各向同性且泊松比固定，由 $G_c=E_c/[2(1+\nu_c)]$ 可化为：

$$\sigma_{\mathrm{wr}}=B E_f^{1/3}E_c^{2/3},\qquad B=\frac{C}{[2(1+\nu_c)]^{1/3}}.$$

Wrinkling sensitivity皱褶应力例题

If the core Young’s modulus doubles, by what percentage does the wrinkling critical stress increase? Use the relation proportional to $E_c^{2/3}$.

若芯材杨氏模量提高一倍，皱褶临界应力提高百分之几？按临界应力与 $E_c^{2/3}$ 成正比的关系计算。

Keep the face modulus, core Poisson’s ratio and coefficient unchanged. The core shear modulus then increases in the same proportion as its Young’s modulus:

保持面板模量、芯材泊松比和系数不变，芯材剪切模量便随杨氏模量同比例增加：

$$\frac{\sigma_{\mathrm{wr,new}}}{\sigma_{\mathrm{wr,old}}}=2^{2/3}=1.5874,\qquad\frac{\Delta\sigma_{\mathrm{wr}}}{\sigma_{\mathrm{wr,old}}}=58.74\%.$$

The exponents compare modulus changes, not thickness changes. In this model $E_f$ has exponent $1/3$; that does not say that adding face thickness has a $1/3$ effect. Thicker faces reduce working stress and can alter other failure limits. Likewise, a denser core changes mass as well as stiffness, so its benefit must be evaluated with the actual property–density relation. An anisotropic honeycomb does not generally permit $G_c$ to be inferred from a single $E_c$.

这些指数比较的是模量变化，不是厚度变化。$E_f$ 的指数为 $1/3$，并不表示面板加厚只有 $1/3$ 次方的作用；加厚面板会降低工作应力，也可能改变其他失效限值。提高芯材密度则会同时改变质量与刚度，需要结合实际性能—密度关系评价。方向性蜂窝通常不能仅凭一个 $E_c$ 推算 $G_c$。

Thickness optimization厚度优化

With materials, width and span fixed, take $h_c$ and $t_f$ as design variables. The structural mass is $m=bL(2\rho_ft_f+\rho_ch_c)$. For bending and shear, a first sizing model checks face stress against both material strength and wrinkling, and checks core shear. Manufacturing limits bound the available thicknesses:

固定材料、宽度和跨度，以 $h_c$、$t_f$ 为设计变量，结构质量为 $m=bL(2\rho_ft_f+\rho_ch_c)$。对受弯、受剪梁，初步尺寸模型同时检查面板材料强度、面板皱褶和芯材剪切，再加入可制造的厚度范围：

$$\begin{aligned}\min_{h_c,t_f}\quad &m=bL(2\rho_ft_f+\rho_ch_c),\\\text{subject to}\quad &\frac{M_{\max}}{bt_f(h_c+t_f)}\le\min(\sigma_{f,\mathrm{allow}},\sigma_{\mathrm{wr}}),\\&\frac{V_{\max}}{bh_c}\le\tau_{c,\mathrm{allow}},\qquad t_f\ge t_{\min},\quad h_{\min}\le h_c\le h_{\max}.\end{aligned}$$

These stresses use a thin-face force couple and a uniform-core-shear approximation. If the materials and thick-core wrinkling model remain fixed, material strength and wrinkling are simply two limits on the same face stress; the smaller limit governs. The lower limit therefore defines the governing face-thickness boundary.

上式采用薄面板力偶与均匀芯材剪切近似。固定材料和厚芯皱褶模型后，材料强度与皱褶只是同一面板工作应力的两个上限，由较低者确定面板厚度的约束边界。

A computed sizing example尺寸优化算例

For an illustrative simply supported sandwich beam, let $L=1$ m, $b=0.10$ m and the central point load be $P=500$ N. Treat the core as isotropic and use the assumed properties below. Require $t_f\ge0.30$ mm and $1\le h_c\le60$ mm, and minimize face-plus-core mass subject to the stated stress constraints.

某夹层简支梁的跨度 $L=1$ m、宽度 $b=0.10$ m，跨中集中力 $P=500$ N。芯材按各向同性近似，采用下表假定性能；要求每层面板 $t_f\ge0.30$ mm、芯厚 $1\le h_c\le60$ mm；在上述应力约束下，使面板与芯材的总质量最小。

Set $C=0.5$ in the wrinkling estimate. Equilibrium gives $M_{\max}=PL/4=125$ N·m and $V_{\max}=P/2=250$ N. The estimated wrinkling stress is 275.89 MPa, so the 150 MPa face-material limit governs. Core shear requires $h_c\ge3.125$ mm.

皱褶估算取 $C=0.5$；由平衡得到 $M_{\max}=PL/4=125$ N·m、$V_{\max}=P/2=250$ N。皱褶临界应力约为 275.89 MPa，因此面板由较低的 150 MPa 材料应力限值控制；芯剪切则要求 $h_c\ge3.125$ mm。

$$\begin{aligned}K&=\frac{M_{\max}}{b\sigma_{f,\mathrm{allow}}}=8.33333\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m^2},\\t_{\mathrm{req}}(h_c)&=\frac{\sqrt{h_c^2+4K}-h_c}{2},\\t_f(h_c)&=\max\left(t_{\min},t_{\mathrm{req}}(h_c)\right).\end{aligned}$$

This reduces the problem to choosing $h_c$. Along the active face-stress boundary, setting the mass derivative to zero gives an interior candidate. It must then be compared with the thickness bounds and the point where the minimum face thickness becomes active:

问题由此化为选择芯厚 $h_c$。沿面板应力边界对质量求导，令导数为零，可得到一个内部候选点；随后还需与厚度边界，以及最小面板厚度开始起作用的位置比较：

$$\begin{aligned}\frac{1}{bL}\frac{\mathrm dm}{\mathrm dh_c}&=\rho_c-\rho_f+\rho_f\frac{h_c}{\sqrt{h_c^2+4K}}=0,\\a&=1-\frac{\rho_c}{\rho_f},\qquad h_c^*=\frac{2\sqrt K\,a}{\sqrt{1-a^2}},\\h_c^*&=23.187\ \mathrm{mm},\qquad t_f^*=0.354\ \mathrm{mm},\qquad m^*=0.37665\ \mathrm{kg}.\end{aligned}$$

At this point, core shear is about 0.108 MPa, below 0.8 MPa, and the face thickness is above its 0.30 mm manufacturing minimum. The equal-mass line is tangent to one active stress boundary. The stress curve meets the minimum-thickness line later, at $h_c=27.478$ mm, but that intersection is heavier. Changing density, the minimum gauge or the shear limit can move the optimum onto an intersection or a bound.

此时芯剪应力约为 0.108 MPa，低于 0.8 MPa，面板也厚于 0.30 mm 的制造下限。最优点是等质量线与单一应力边界的切点。应力曲线在 $h_c=27.478$ mm 处才与最小厚度线相交，但该交点的质量更大。改变密度、最小板厚或剪应力限值后，最优点也可能移到交点或尺寸边界。

Deflection and global buckling were not imposed in this sizing example. Substituting the resulting section into the layered rigidity and uniform-core-shear model gives approximately 14.99 mm bending deflection plus 1.80 mm shear deflection; a 10 mm limit would reject this design. A complete design therefore adds the required stiffness, axial stability, cell-scale instability, local bearing, bond, fatigue and manufacturing constraints before choosing dimensions.

本算例没有施加挠度与整体屈曲约束。将所得尺寸代入分层刚度和均匀芯剪切模型，弯曲挠度约为 14.99 mm，剪切挠度约为 1.80 mm；若要求挠度不超过 10 mm，这一尺寸就不合格。实际选定尺寸前，还需按工况加入刚度、轴向稳定性、胞格局部失稳、局部承压、胶接、疲劳和制造约束。
