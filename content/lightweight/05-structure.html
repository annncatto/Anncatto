Structural Design结构设计

A selection index compares materials under a stated geometric freedom. Structural design decides how to use that freedom. The course summarizes the main opportunities as thin efficient sections, direct load paths and appropriate use of the material across the controlling load cases. These choices can change the required mass before a detailed optimization begins.

材料指数在规定几何自由度下比较材料，结构设计则决定怎样使用这些自由度。课程将主要机会概括为高效薄壁截面、直接传力和面向主导工况的材料利用。详细优化之前，这些选择已经能够改变完成同一承载任务所需的质量。

Section Efficiency截面效率

At fixed area, redistributing material changes the second moment. A square reference section has $I_{A,0}=A^2/12$. Define a dimensionless bending shape factor for this reference:

面积固定时，重新分配材料仍然能够改变截面二次矩。以实心正方形为参考，$I_{A,0}=A^2/12$，可以定义无量纲弯曲形状因子：

$$\phi_b=\frac{I_A}{I_{A,0}}=\frac{12I_A}{A^2}.$$

Combining this shape factor with the bending material index gives $M=\sqrt{\phi_bE}/\rho$ when area can scale while the chosen shape remains similar. Material and shape then enter the same expression. The attainable $\phi_b$ is constrained by the available envelope, local stability and manufacturing.

截面积可以缩放、选定形状保持相似时，将形状因子代入抗弯材料指数，可得 $M=\sqrt{\phi_bE}/\rho$。材料与形状进入同一个表达式；空间包络、局部稳定和制造，则限制实际能够获得的 $\phi_b$。

For a circular tube with mean radius $r$ and wall $t$, the exact factor relative to an equal-area solid square is $\phi_b=3(r/t+t/(4r))/\pi$. In the thin-wall limit it approaches $3r/(\pi t)$. Thus $r/t=5$ and $10$ give approximately 4.82 and 9.57, respectively. A solid circle gives $3/\pi\approx0.955$. These compare bending stiffness at equal area and modulus.

圆管的平均半径为 $r$、壁厚为 $t$ 时，相对于等面积实心正方形的精确形状因子为 $\phi_b=3(r/t+t/(4r))/\pi$，薄壁近似为 $3r/(\pi t)$。因此，$r/t=5$ 与 $10$ 分别得到约 4.82 和 9.57；实心圆为 $3/\pi\approx0.955$。它们比较的是面积与模量相同时的抗弯刚度。

{{lightweight:section}}

For the same bending moment, $M_b/I_A$ falls by the reciprocal of $\phi_b$. It is the stress gradient across the section; maximum stress also contains the outer-fibre distance $c$. For the equal-area circular tube with $r/t=10$, $M_b/I_A$ is about 1/9.57 of the square’s value, while $\sigma_{\max}=M_bc/I_A$ is about 1/3.61 because the tube extends farther from the neutral axis. Stiffness efficiency and strength efficiency therefore use different section measures: $I_A$ and section modulus $Z=I_A/c$.

相同弯矩下，$M_b/I_A$ 按 $\phi_b$ 的倒数下降。它表示应力沿截面的变化率；最大应力还包含最外侧距离 $c$。对等面积、$r/t=10$ 的圆管，$M_b/I_A$ 约为实心方形的 1/9.57，最大应力 $\sigma_{\max}=M_bc/I_A$ 则约为 1/3.61，因为圆管的外缘也离中性轴更远。刚度效率和强度效率因此分别使用 $I_A$ 与截面模量 $Z=I_A/c$。

A 40 × 60 mm rectangular tube with 2 mm walls has area 384 mm² and bending second moment 193,152 mm⁴ about its strong axis. A solid square of the same area has side 19.60 mm and second moment 12,288 mm⁴. The tube offers about 15.7 times the bending second moment at equal material volume. It occupies a larger envelope, so the gain is available only if that envelope fits.

40 × 60 mm、壁厚 2 mm 的矩形管，截面积为 384 mm²，强轴截面二次矩为 193,152 mm⁴。相同面积的实心正方形边长为 19.60 mm，二次矩为 12,288 mm⁴。在材料体积相同的条件下，矩形管得到约 15.7 倍的弯曲二次矩，但它占用更大的外包络，空间允许时才能使用这项收益。

Removing material near the neutral axis is efficient in bending. Moving the same material outward increases the section’s surface area, which can help cooling, but it also changes manufacturing, local wall stability and impact resistance. An extremely thin, deep tube may be limited by local buckling or a bolted end fitting before the global beam reaches its nominal stress limit.

弯曲时，移走中性轴附近的材料通常很有效。将材料向外移动，也会增加表面积，可能有利于散热，同时改变制造、局部壁板稳定性与抗冲击能力。非常薄而高的管件，可能先受局部屈曲或螺栓端部接头限制，无法达到整体梁的名义应力极限。

The best section depends on the entire load set. A rectangular tube can favor one bending direction; a circular tube is insensitive to rotation about its axis and carries torsion efficiently. An I-section places material well for one bending plane but has low open-section torsional stiffness. A structural member carrying cables, bearings and off-axis contact forces rarely has a single ideal load.

最佳截面由完整载荷集合决定。矩形管可以偏向一个弯曲方向；圆管绕自身轴旋转时性能不变，抗扭也比较高效；工字截面适合一个主弯曲平面，开口截面的扭转刚度却偏低。携带线缆、轴承并承受偏轴接触力的构件，很少只有一种理想载荷。

Mass inertia remains a separate consideration when the member moves. Distributing material outward may improve bending while increasing inertia about its own longitudinal axis. About a transverse support axis, distance along the member can dominate. Geometry should be judged against the actual load and motion, rather than maximizing one section quantity in isolation.

构件运动时，还要单独考虑质量惯量。材料向外分布，有利于抗弯，也可能增加绕构件纵轴的惯量；绕横向支承轴转动时，沿杆长的距离则可能占主导。几何应对应真实载荷与运动评价，而不是孤立地最大化一个截面量。

Load Paths and Local Reinforcement传力与局部加强

A load path connects the point of application to the supports. Offsets introduce additional moments, while triangulation can convert much of a bending demand into axial force when the connections permit it. Straightening a path, separating tension and compression paths, and avoiding an unnecessary load detour can reduce the material needed to carry the same external force.

传力路径连接加载点与支承。偏置引入额外弯矩，连接允许时，三角化可以将较多弯曲需求转化为轴力。缩短路径、分开拉压路径、减少不必要的载荷绕行，都可能降低传递相同外力所需的材料用量。

Material utilization is evaluated across the complete load set. A region with low stress in one case can stabilize a thin wall, support a connection or carry another direction. A well-utilized design places sufficient material where the governing demand occurs and avoids excessive reserves elsewhere. “Fully stressed” is a useful design idea, but stiffness and stability can still require material below the strength limit.

材料利用率需要在完整工况集合下评价。一个工况中应力较低的区域，可能负责稳定薄壁、支撑接头，或承受另一方向。合理设计在主导需求处保留足够材料，减少其他位置的过度余量。“满应力”提供一种设计思路，刚度和稳定性仍可能要求材料保留在强度极限以下。

A tip-loaded cantilever illustrates this distribution. Moment decreases from the root toward the free end, so a deeper root and a lighter outer region may use material more efficiently than a uniform member. The end still needs enough section to introduce the load and form a connection. Changing the section to equalize nominal stress also changes deflection, which must remain within its own limit.

末端受力的悬臂梁能够说明这种分布。弯矩从根部向自由端降低，因此加深根部、减轻外侧，可能比均匀杆件更高效。末端仍需足够截面来引入载荷和形成连接。按名义应力均匀分配材料，也会改变挠度，位移限制仍需满足。

Ribs spread concentrated loads from a bearing or fastener into a shell and can stabilize a panel. They need to connect to a real load path. Local metal, thicker laminate or a bonded insert can provide the bearing, shear or through-thickness performance needed at an interface. The mass saving is the complete supported component’s saving, including those reinforcements.

加强筋可以把轴承或紧固件的集中力扩散进壳体，也可以稳定壁板，前提是它们接入真实传力路径。局部金属、加厚铺层或胶接嵌件，可以提供接口需要的承压、剪切和厚度方向性能。减重收益需要包括这些补强后，按完整受支承构件核算。

Sandwiches and Laminates夹层与铺层

A sandwich places stiff faces on either side of a light core. Separation gives the faces bending leverage; the core transfers shear and stabilizes them. This is the same section-efficiency idea as a tube, implemented with different materials. Face wrinkling, core shear, crushing, debonding and attachment loads can set the thickness before the faces reach their nominal strength.

夹层用轻质芯材分隔两层高刚度面板。间距为面板提供抗弯力臂，芯材负责传递剪力并稳定面板。这与空心管的截面效率思路相同，只是通过不同材料实现。面板起皱、芯材剪切、压溃、脱粘和连接载荷，都可能在面板达到名义强度前决定厚度。

A fibre laminate adds directional placement. Reinforcement near the faces contributes strongly to bending, while off-axis plies can supply shear and torsional capability. A symmetric layup can avoid some extension–bending coupling; a different stacking sequence can intentionally introduce coupling. For the present design sequence, the important point is that fibre direction and through-thickness position are structural variables. A detailed laminate calculation uses the actual stacking sequence and material directions.

纤维层合板进一步增加了方向布置。靠近表面的增强层对抗弯贡献较大，偏轴铺层可以提供剪切与抗扭能力。对称铺层可以避免部分拉伸—弯曲耦合，不同叠层顺序也可以有意引入耦合。在当前设计流程中，关键是把纤维方向和厚度方向位置视为结构变量；详细计算再采用实际铺层顺序与材料方向。

Thin plies offer more layers within a given laminate thickness and can alter the onset and growth of matrix cracks and delamination. Matrix toughening targets another part of the same damage chain. The course presents these as ways to increase usable performance and reduce the thickness required by damage tolerance. Their benefit depends on the load and failure mode that previously controlled the design.

薄层化让同样层合板厚度内能够安排更多层，并改变基体开裂与分层的起始和扩展；基体增韧则作用于这条损伤链的另一环节。课程将它们作为提高可用性能、减少损伤容限所需厚度的途径。收益取决于原来究竟由哪种载荷和失效控制设计。

Openings show why laminate design and load paths must be planned together. A door-surround structure (DSS) redirects loads around a large cut-out, where shear reinforcement, concentrated loads and thickened corners can dominate mass. The course’s MAAXIMUS example links savings to the redesign of these details and assembly. Double-double laminates use repeated groups of two angle pairs; their distribution can simplify reinforcement transitions and thickness tailoring. The benefit depends on the selected angles, taper and load cases.

开口说明了铺层与传力路径为什么需要一起安排。舱门周边结构（DSS）需要让载荷绕过大开口，抗剪加强、集中载荷与角部加厚往往占用较多质量。课件中的 MAAXIMUS 案例将收益联系到这些细节与装配的重设计。double-double 铺层使用两组角度对的重复组合，其分布可以简化补强层过渡与厚度裁剪；效果取决于角度、过渡方式与工况。

Fibre–metal laminates alternate metal and fibre-composite layers. The metal contributes ductility and useful isotropic behavior, while the reinforcement can slow crack growth and tailor stiffness. Glass-fibre/aluminium GLARE is one such combination. With carbon fibre and aluminium, electrical isolation and corrosion protection become part of the interface design.

纤维金属层板交替组合金属与纤维复合层。金属提供塑性及相对均匀的方向性能，纤维增强可以阻碍裂纹扩展并定制刚度。玻璃纤维—铝合金 GLARE 是其中一种组合。碳纤维与铝组合时，还需要把电隔离与防腐纳入界面设计。

Size, Shape and Topology尺寸、形状与拓扑

Sizing changes dimensions within a chosen concept: wall thickness, rib depth or bolt diameter. Shape optimization moves boundaries while preserving a chosen connectivity. Topology optimization changes where load-bearing material exists and which paths connect the supports. These variables operate at different stages, and all depend on supports and loads.

尺寸优化在既定概念内改变壁厚、筋高或螺栓直径；形状优化移动边界，保留既定连接关系；拓扑优化改变承载材料存在的位置，以及支承之间的连接路径。这些变量对应不同设计层次，也都依赖载荷与边界条件。

The concept should establish attachment locations, clearances, main load paths and joining methods before fine topology is optimized. A visually efficient organic shape can still be a poor component if it cannot be machined, cast, joined or inspected. The next stage therefore places manufacturing constraints alongside the geometric opportunities.

概念阶段应先确定连接位置、间隙、主传力路径和连接方法，再细化拓扑。外形看起来高效的有机结构，如果无法加工、铸造、连接或检测，也可能不适合作为实际零件。因此，下一步需要将制造约束与几何机会一起考虑。
