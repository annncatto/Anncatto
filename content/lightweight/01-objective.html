Designing with Constraints约束下的设计

A robot carries its structure through every movement. A lighter wrist reduces the torque demanded from the elbow; a lighter leg changes swing dynamics and impact response. The benefit depends on where material is removed and which function it was serving. A thin link that bends under load can cost more in tracking error than it saves in mass.

机器人在每次运动中都要带着自身结构一起运动。手腕减轻，肘关节需要的力矩会下降；腿部减轻，摆腿动力学与落地响应也会改变。收益取决于材料从哪里移走，以及它原本承担什么功能。杆件过薄、受载后明显弯曲，可能用跟踪误差换来了质量下降。

Lightweight structural design combines material selection, load paths, geometry and manufacturing to meet a task with less mass. The requirements include strength, stiffness, stability, fatigue life, vibration, thermal conditions, interfaces and cost. The design variable is therefore an entire load-bearing system, including joints, fasteners, sensors, actuators and the parts added to make it manufacturable.

轻量化结构设计将材料、传力路径、几何与制造放在一起考虑，以更少的质量完成任务。约束包括强度、刚度、稳定性、疲劳寿命、振动、温度、连接接口与成本。因此，设计对象是一整套承载系统，也包括关节、紧固件、传感器、执行器，以及让结构能够制造和装配的附加零件。

$$\begin{aligned}\min_{x}\quad &m(x),\\\text{subject to}\quad &\sigma(x,\mathcal L)\leq\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}},\\&\delta(x,\mathcal L)\leq\delta_{\max},\quad f_1(x)\geq f_{\min},\\&\mathcal R(x,\mathcal L,\mathcal U)\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{required}}.\end{aligned}$$

$x$ collects geometry, material, actuator placement and manufacturing choices. $\mathcal L$ is the set of load cases, while $\mathcal U$ describes uncertainty in loads, dimensions and properties. $\mathcal R$ represents the remaining requirements, such as life, temperature, range of motion and cost. A mass value becomes useful only when these conditions are specified.

$x$ 包含几何、材料、执行器位置与制造方案，$\mathcal L$ 是载荷工况集合，$\mathcal U$ 描述载荷、尺寸和材料性能的不确定性，$\mathcal R$ 表示寿命、温度、运动范围和成本等其余要求。确定这些条件后，质量数值才具有可比性。

Components and Systems零件与系统

Two complementary routes appear throughout this article. Component design makes each necessary part more efficient: a hollow section, a suitable alloy, a load-aligned laminate. System design changes which parts are necessary: a motor housing carries structural loads, a joint combines sensing and electronics, or a shell also supplies cooling area. Integration can remove brackets and interfaces, but it also couples repair, heat flow and failure modes.

贯穿全文的设计路线有两条。零件设计提高必要零件的承载效率，例如空心截面、合适的合金、沿载荷方向铺设的复合材料。系统设计改变哪些零件必须存在，例如让电机壳体承载，让关节集成传感与电子设备，或者让外壳兼任散热表面。集成可以省去支架和接口，也会把维修、热流和失效模式联系起来。

The mass spiral connects these routes. Reducing distal mass can lower upstream torque demands, allowing smaller actuators and lighter supports. This second saving requires redesigning those components. Moving a motor to the body reduces swing inertia immediately, while the added transmission may increase total mass. Both changes belong in the same mass budget.

质量螺旋将两条路线连接起来。远端减重可能降低上游力矩需求，进而允许使用更小的执行器和更轻的支承；第二部分收益需要重新设计才能兑现。将电机移到机身，能够直接降低摆动惯量，新增传动却可能增加总质量。两者需要记入同一份质量预算。

A design brief should fix the workspace, payload, movement cycle, mounting conditions, allowable deformation, life and environment before comparing concepts. A manipulator holding a tool, a quadruped landing and a humanoid standing continuously impose different requirements. The same material or transmission can rank differently in each task.

比较方案之前，应先明确工作空间、负载、运动循环、安装边界、允许变形、寿命与环境。机械臂持握工具、四足机器人落地、人形机器人持续站立，对结构的要求各不相同。同一种材料或传动，在这些任务中的排序也可能改变。

The Robot Structure Studio at the end of the article provides a running example. Its parameters drive a three-dimensional robot and analytical calculations together. Mass, limb inertia, member deformation and rotor inertia are kept separate, so a favorable change in one metric remains visible alongside its cost in another.

文末的机器人结构实验室提供了贯穿全文的算例。同一组参数同时驱动三维机器人与解析计算。质量、肢体惯量、单杆变形和转子惯量分别显示，便于观察一个指标改善时，其他指标付出了什么代价。
