The course principles can now be applied to more specific choices. The examples in this section keep their additional assumptions explicit: which tube dimension is free, how motion is transmitted, and what a joint must transfer. They extend the main sequence without changing its selection logic.

A Tube with Different Free Dimensions不同尺寸自由度下的圆管

A thin circular robot link gives another useful contrast. With outer diameter $D$ and wall $t\ll D$, $I_A\approx\pi D^3t/8$ and $m\approx\rho\pi DtL$. Keeping the envelope fixed and changing only the wall gives $m_{\min}\propto\rho/E$ under a bending-stiffness requirement. Keeping the wall fixed and allowing diameter to change instead gives $D\propto E^{-1/3}$ and $m_{\min}\propto\rho/E^{1/3}$.

薄壁圆形连杆还能说明几何约束的影响。外径为 $D$、壁厚满足 $t\ll D$ 时，$I_A\approx\pi D^3t/8$，质量 $m\approx\rho\pi DtL$。外包络固定、只改变壁厚，在抗弯刚度约束下得到 $m_{\min}\propto\rho/E$；壁厚固定、允许改变外径，则有 $D\propto E^{-1/3}$，进而 $m_{\min}\propto\rho/E^{1/3}$。

The two indices are $E/\rho$ and $E^{1/3}/\rho$, even though both members are bending tubes. They follow different geometric freedoms. The thin-wall assumption must remain valid after resizing, and the chosen diameter must fit the joint interfaces. This is how the section second moment enters a material-index derivation before the geometric variable is eliminated.

两者都是受弯圆管，材料指数却分别为 $E/\rho$ 与 $E^{1/3}/\rho$，因为允许改变的几何变量不同。调整尺寸后仍需满足薄壁近似，直径也要适配关节接口。截面二次矩先参与刚度计算，再通过消去几何变量进入材料指数。

Take a 0.35 m aluminium tube with 60 mm outer diameter and 2 mm wall as a bending-stiffness reference. Use $E=69$ GPa and $\rho=2700$ kg/m³ for aluminium, 114 GPa and 4430 kg/m³ for titanium, and an illustrative effective laminate modulus of 60 GPa with density 1600 kg/m³ for CFRP. These rounded values make the geometry comparison concrete.

以长 0.35 m、外径 60 mm、壁厚 2 mm 的铝管为抗弯刚度基准。取铝合金 $E=69$ GPa、$\rho=2700$ kg/m³，钛合金 114 GPa、4430 kg/m³，CFRP 示例等效铺层模量 60 GPa、密度 1600 kg/m³。用这些取整参数，将几何比较具体化。

With diameter fixed, the ideal mass order is CFRP, titanium, aluminium; titanium and aluminium are nearly tied because their specific moduli are close. With wall fixed and diameter free, the order becomes CFRP, aluminium, titanium. Lower-density material gains more from the larger efficient section. The numerical CFRP ranking depends on its actual laminate properties. Joining, heat transfer, impact tolerance or cost can reverse a mass-only selection for a real joint-bearing link.

外径固定时，理想质量从低到高为 CFRP、钛合金、铝合金；钛和铝的比模量接近，因此差别很小。壁厚固定、外径自由时，排序变为 CFRP、铝合金、钛合金。低密度材料能够用更大的高效截面获益。CFRP 的具体排序取决于实际铺层性能；对带轴承接口的真实连杆，连接、导热、抗冲击或成本，也可能改变仅按质量得到的选择。

The material rank changes because a fixed wall permits a larger diameter to provide bending leverage, while a fixed diameter permits only a change in wall area. For the same bending moment, the stress comparison also includes outer-fibre distance through $Z=I_A/c$. Keeping stress equal and keeping bending rigidity equal are different constraints, so one index cannot answer both questions.

材料排序改变，是因为固定壁厚时，可以扩大直径来获得抗弯力臂；固定外径时，则只能改变壁面积。相同弯矩下，强度比较还需要通过 $Z=I_A/c$ 计入最外侧距离。相同应力与相同抗弯刚度，是两个不同约束，一项指数不能同时回答这两个问题。

Proximal Actuation and Transmission近端驱动与传动

Moving an actuator toward a fixed support can reduce the mass carried by the outer mechanism. Its output still has to reach the driven joint. The comparison must include the new transmission’s mass, compliance, friction, preload and maintenance. Relocation is useful when those added costs are smaller than the benefit of removing distal moving mass.

将执行器移向固定支承，可以降低外侧机构携带的质量，但其输出仍需传到被驱动关节。比较需要包括新增传动的质量、柔性、摩擦、预紧与维护。只有新增代价小于移走远端运动质量的收益，近端布置才有价值。

Timing belts transmit motion by tooth engagement. Pulley tooth counts set the transmission ratio, while rotation remains continuous. The driven joint can stop between tooth pitches; its useful precision depends on sensing, transmission error, elasticity and control.

同步带通过齿啮合传递运动，带轮齿数决定传动比，转动本身连续。关节可以停在齿距之间，实际精度取决于测量、传动误差、弹性与控制。

The main belt limitations are load-dependent stretch, tooth deformation, preload requirements, bearing side load, hysteresis and the risk of tooth jumping under unsuitable loading or tension. An output encoder measures the actual joint angle and can improve feedback, while the mechanical compliance remains. Belt width, span, tooth profile and engaged tooth count must be sized with the manufacturer’s data. The timing-belt design handbook explains these mechanisms.

同步带的主要限制是随载荷变化的伸长、带齿变形、预紧需求、轴承侧向载荷、滞后，以及不合适载荷或张紧条件下的跳齿风险。输出编码器测量实际关节角度，有助于反馈，机械弹性仍然存在。带宽、跨距、齿形与啮合齿数，需要结合厂家数据选型。同步带设计手册介绍了这些机制。

A rigid linkage uses bars and pivots to relate actuator motion to joint motion. Its mechanical advantage varies with posture. A configuration near a toggle may provide large force but little displacement, reduced usable travel or sensitivity to clearance. Link loads, pin bearing, interference and the complete range of motion need to be checked together.

刚性连杆通过杆与铰链，建立驱动运动和关节运动的关系。机械增益随姿态变化；接近死点的构型可能放大力、减小位移，同时压缩有效行程，或放大间隙的影响。杆件载荷、销轴承压、干涉与完整运动范围，需要一起检查。

A screw converts motor rotation into linear travel, and a rocker or linkage converts that travel into joint motion. Screw lead sets the translation per revolution; the current lever arm sets how axial force produces joint torque. Ball screws and roller screws can combine useful force density with efficient motion, while supports, reversal, preload, buckling and lubrication remain design requirements. A highly efficient screw can backdrive, so holding behavior follows the complete mechanism.

丝杆将电机旋转转换为直线运动，再通过摇杆或连杆转换为关节转动。导程决定每转的直线行程，当前力臂决定轴向力怎样形成关节转矩。滚珠丝杆和行星滚柱丝杆能够结合较高轴向力与传动效率，也需要检查支承、换向、预紧、屈曲和润滑。高效率丝杆可以被反向驱动，因此保持能力需要按完整机构判断。

These alternatives distribute compromises differently. Belts and cables can keep moving mass low but require tension and attention to elasticity. Linkages define the motion geometrically but add pivots and posture-dependent leverage. Screws offer a compact route to substantial axial force but add linear travel and support requirements. Their robot implementations are compared in the final section.

几种方案把取舍放在不同位置：带与绳索可以保持较低运动质量，但依赖张紧，也需要处理弹性；连杆以几何规定运动，但增加铰链和姿态相关的杠杆；丝杆提供较紧凑的大轴向力路线，也增加直线行程与支承要求。它们在机器人中的具体实现，放在最后的调研中比较。

Research on Composite Connections复合材料接头研究

For a CFRP tube, a bonded metallic sleeve can spread force over a longer overlap; a potted insert can introduce a threaded attachment into a sandwich. Direct threads cut into a thin laminate leave little bearing area and interrupt fibres. The sleeve, adhesive, potting and local plies must be sized together, including pull-out, shear, torque and thermal mismatch. The ECSS Insert Design Handbook develops load-transfer and failure models for sandwich inserts; Hart-Smith’s scarf and stepped-lap joint analysis examines overlap geometry, adhesive plasticity and thermal mismatch. These are useful starting points for a composite robot adapter.

CFRP 管件可以用胶接金属套筒沿较长搭接面扩散载荷；夹层板可以通过灌封嵌件引入螺纹连接。直接在薄铺层上攻丝，既缺少承压面积，也会切断纤维。套筒、胶层、灌封料与局部铺层，需要一起按拔出、剪切、转矩和热膨胀失配设计。ECSS 嵌件设计手册给出了夹层嵌件的传力与失效模型；Hart-Smith 的斜接与阶梯搭接分析研究搭接几何、胶层塑性与热失配。这些工作可以作为复合材料机器人转接头的设计起点。

The useful research question is how the fitting introduces force without concentrating peel, bearing or through-thickness stress in an inefficient way. Overlap length, taper, insert shape and local reinforcement can be compared under the same pull-out, shear and torque requirements. That connects joint research to the mass saved in the complete component, rather than judging the tube wall alone.

有用的研究问题，是接头怎样引入载荷，同时减少低效的剥离、孔边承压与厚度方向应力集中。可以在相同拔出、剪切和转矩要求下，比较搭接长度、锥度、嵌件形状与局部铺层。这样，接头研究就连接到完整构件的减重，而不只评价管壁。
