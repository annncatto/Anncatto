Introduction概论

Lightweight structural design asks how much material a function actually needs. The structure must carry its loads, retain its shape, survive repeated use and remain affordable to manufacture. Reducing mass becomes useful when those functions are preserved. A thin part that requires a heavy support, or an expensive alloy used in an inefficient section, can move the problem elsewhere in the assembly.

轻量化结构设计研究的是：完成一种功能，究竟需要多少材料。结构需要承载、保持形状、经受反复使用，并能以合理成本制造。保留这些功能，减重才有意义。薄零件如果需要很重的支架，高价合金如果用在低效截面里，都可能只是把问题转移到装配体的其他位置。

The design objective is minimum structural mass under specified requirements. Stiffness controls deformation, strength controls material failure, durability controls service life, and vibration, noise, impact, environment and cost complete the conditions. These requirements establish the comparison: an unsupported mass number says little about the quality of a design.

设计目标是在规定要求下，使结构质量最小。刚度限制变形，强度限制材料失效，耐久性限制使用寿命；振动、噪声、冲击、环境与成本则补全使用条件。这些要求确定之后，质量才具有可比性。

Materials, Structure and Manufacturing材料、结构与制造

Three decisions work together. Material selection changes the available properties per unit mass. Structural design places material along the required load paths and chooses section, thickness and shape. Manufacturing makes that arrangement achievable, including the joints, surface condition and property changes introduced by processing. A successful material substitution usually requires reconsidering all three.

三项决策需要一起考虑。选材改变单位质量能够提供的性能；结构设计沿传力路径布置材料，确定截面、壁厚与形状；制造让这些布置能够实现，也带来连接、表面状态和加工后的性能变化。有效的材料替换，通常需要重新考虑这三件事。

The course also distinguishes making necessary parts lighter from making fewer parts necessary. A hollow beam improves an individual member; a shell that also carries loads, routes services and spreads heat can remove separate brackets and covers. The first route improves components. The second changes the system boundary and the number of interfaces. Both must count the complete assembly.

课程还区分了“必要零件更轻”与“必要零件更少”。空心梁提高单个构件的效率；承载外壳同时走线、导热，就可能省去独立支架和盖板。前者改善零件，后者改变系统边界和接口数量，两者都需要按完整装配体核算。

This distinction matters early. The concept fixes the main load paths, available space, material families and joining strategy. Detailed sizing then refines a structure whose basic efficiency may already be largely determined. An efficient concept leaves room for several viable materials and processes; premature commitment can make later optimization work around an avoidable constraint.

这也说明概念阶段的重要性。主要传力路径、可用空间、材料类别和连接方案，在这一阶段确定。后续尺寸优化面对的结构，其基本效率可能已经大体确定。高效概念应保留几种可行的材料与工艺组合，过早锁定方案，会让后面的优化围绕本可避免的约束展开。

Weight and Cost质量与成本

The lightest feasible design and the best economic design need not coincide. Thinner walls may reduce material consumption until extra machining, tooling, inspection and assembly effort outweigh that saving. More advanced material can reduce service energy but increase purchasing or repair cost. The comparison should include the relevant production volume and operating life.

最轻的可行方案，与经济性最好的方案未必重合。减薄可以节省材料，但额外加工、模具、检测和装配成本也可能超过收益。先进材料能够降低使用能耗，也可能增加采购与维修费用。因此，比较需要覆盖相应的生产批量与使用寿命。

{{course:cost}}

Point A represents a balance in design and production cost. If saving mass also has an operating or mission value, an acceptable extra cost per kilogram saved shifts the preferred design toward B. The two points depend on that valuation. The cost boundary includes research, development, material, tooling, production and repair; affordability and sustainability also depend on production volume, service life and end-of-life recovery.

A 点表示设计与生产成本的平衡。减重如果还具有运行或任务价值，可以接受的单位减重附加成本，就会将取舍推向 B 点。两点的位置取决于怎样评价这些收益。成本边界包括研究、开发、材料、模具、生产与维修；可负担性和可持续性，还与批量、寿命及报废回收有关。

Requirements and Verification要求与验算

A load enters a structure through supports and interfaces. Verification includes static and dynamic force transfer, stiffness, load capacity, stability, remaining strength after damage, service life and crack growth. Reliability connects these checks to variation in loads, geometry, material properties and defects. Manufacturing surface condition, climate and temperature affect the evidence used in each check.

载荷通过支承和接口进入结构。验算包括静动态传力、刚度、承载能力、稳定性、损伤后残余强度、寿命与裂纹扩展。可靠性进一步将这些检查联系到载荷、几何、材料性能和缺陷的离散性；制造后的表面状态、气候与温度，也会影响各项验算的依据。

{{course:verification}}

The technical brief identifies loads and boundaries, allowed displacement, life, vibration limits, temperature, interfaces, production volume and cost. Requirements guide concept design and scheme comparison, then detailed sizing and production preparation. Manufacturing, prototype assembly, testing and delivery follow; the results can send the design back to an earlier decision.

设计要求明确载荷与边界、允许位移、寿命、振动限制、温度、接口、批量和成本，再指导概念设计与方案比较，进入详细尺寸设计和生产准备。之后开展制造、原型装配、试验与交付，结果可以反馈到前面的设计决策。

{{course:workflow}}

The course’s “virtual–physical” method closes that loop. Analysis helps select sensor locations and test loads. Tests identify real support stiffness, material scatter and unmodeled behavior. A calibrated model can then compare variants with greater confidence. Numerical optimization belongs inside this loop, between a well-defined engineering problem and a physically checked structure.

课程中的“虚实结合”让这条流程闭合。分析用于选择测点和试验载荷，试验则识别真实支承刚度、材料离散与未建模行为；校准后的模型，再用于比较设计变体。数值优化处在这个闭环中，连接明确的工程问题与实物验证。

The material provides the available properties; the structure turns those properties into load capacity, stiffness and service life. Understanding that connection begins with how a material is made and what its measured properties actually describe.

材料提供可用性能，结构则将这些性能转化为承载、刚度与寿命。理解这条联系，先从材料怎样制成、测得的性能又描述什么开始。
