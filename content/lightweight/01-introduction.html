Introduction导论

Structural lightweight design combines appropriate material selection, structural optimization and manufacturing processes while meeting requirements for stiffness, strength, durability, vibration and noise, passive safety and cost. It places suitable component shapes, dimensions and materials where and when they are needed, making full use of each part’s load-bearing, reinforcing and energy-absorbing functions.

结构轻量化设计是在满足机械结构刚度、强度、耐久性、振动噪声、被动安全性和成本的前提下，通过合理的选材，优化结构，采用合理的工艺，将合理的部件结构形状、尺寸和材料在合适的时间应用到合适的位置，充分发挥各部分的承载、加强、吸能等作用。

Material performance and cost材料性能与成本

Material selection offers several distinct routes to lower structural mass. Strengthening changes the stress a material can sustain, low density changes the volume available for a given mass, and fibre orientation changes which directions carry load efficiently. Their benefits depend on the deformation or failure that sets the component dimensions.

选材有几条不同的减重路径：强化改变材料可承受的应力，低密度改变给定质量下可用的材料体积，纤维方向则改变各方向的承载效率。哪条路径有效，取决于什么变形或失效决定了构件尺寸。

  • High-strength steel modifies microstructure. In dual-phase steel, harder martensite and a more ductile ferrite matrix share the load. This raises resistance to plastic deformation while leaving density and elastic modulus close to those of conventional steel. Strength-limited parts can use less section; a deflection-limited part still needs adequate elastic stiffness. Dual-phase steel metallurgy.
  • Light alloys provide more material volume at the same mass. That volume can be used to increase section depth and improve bending leverage. Their lower modulus must be included: a thinner or identically shaped aluminium replacement does not automatically retain the steel part’s stiffness.
  • Fibre composites place reinforcement along the load directions. A tube carrying bending and torque needs longitudinal and off-axis reinforcement in different proportions. Material composition, layup and geometry are consequently designed together.
  • 高强钢通过组织调控提高抵抗塑性变形的能力。例如，双相钢由较硬的马氏体与较有延展性的铁素体共同承载，密度和弹性模量仍与普通钢接近。强度主导的零件可因此缩小截面；挠度主导的零件仍需足够的弹性刚度。双相钢冶金。
  • 轻合金在相同质量下提供更多材料体积，可以用来增大截面高度、提高抗弯力臂。较低的模量也需同时计入，换成同形状或更薄的铝件，并不能自动保留原钢件的刚度。
  • 纤维复合材料可沿载荷方向布置增强体。承受弯曲和扭转的管件，需要不同比例的纵向与偏轴纤维，材料组成、铺层与几何由此共同参与设计。

Balancing lightweight strategies轻量化策略平衡

{{course:cost}}

At A, the design-cost curve C(m) has its minimum. Moving left saves mass but can add development, processing and inspection expense. If carrying one unit of mass also incurs an operating cost, approximate that contribution locally by λm, with λ > 0. Minimizing C(m) + λm selects B, where the slope of C(m) equals −λ. The dashed line is tangent at B; it represents the marginal value of the mass saving, rather than a second measured cost curve.

A 点是设计成本曲线 $C(m)$ 的最低点。继续向左减重，可能增加研发、加工与检测费用。如果携带单位质量还带来运行成本，可在比较范围内用 $\lambda m$ 近似，$\lambda>0$。此时最小化 $C(m)+\lambda m$，得到 B 点，其设计成本曲线斜率为 $-\lambda$。虚线在 B 点与曲线相切，表示减重的边际价值。

The horizontal axis increases toward heavier structures. A lower mass at B therefore costs more to design and manufacture than A, but can be preferable once operating savings are counted. The balance depends on the selected material, shape, manufacturing route and service life.

横轴向右表示结构更重。因此，B 比 A 更轻，设计制造成本也更高；计入运行节省后，它仍可能更划算。具体取舍取决于材料、形状、制造路线与使用寿命。

Composition and structure determine available properties; processing changes that structure, and service loads expose its limits. Heat treatment changes metal microstructure and strength, fibre orientation changes laminate stiffness, and consolidation controls voids and interfaces. These links connect physics, chemistry, mechanics and mathematics with metallurgy, ceramics, polymers and manufacturing engineering.

成分与组织决定可用性能，加工改变组织，使用条件决定哪些性能首先达到极限。热处理改变金属组织和强度，纤维方向改变层合板刚度，压实与固化影响孔隙和界面。这些联系将物理、化学、力学、数学，与冶金、陶瓷、高分子和制造工程连接起来。

Static and dynamic forces pass through the structure and its joints. Verification covers stiffness, load capacity, residual strength after damage, service life, crack growth and reliability; geometry, material, surface condition, manufacturing and environment supply the inputs.

静态力和动态力通过结构与连接传递。验算包括刚度、承载能力、损伤后残余强度、使用寿命、裂纹扩展与结构可靠性；几何、材料、表面状态、制造和环境共同提供验算依据。

{{course:verification}}

Boundary conditions are clarified before a scheme is selected. Material, structural units, joining, geometry, assembly and production conditions are then turned into a plan and production documentation. Prototype manufacture and trial tests feed corrections back to the scheme and production definition.

先解释边界条件，再寻找方案，将力、材料、结构单元、连接技术、几何尺寸、装配与生产条件转化为设计计划和生产档案。原型制造与试制的结果，反馈到方案和生产定义中。

{{course:workflow}}

Mathematical modelling fixes the known parameters p and selects design variables x. Solving the model gives x*(p); engineering evaluation may then revise either the parameters or the model. Optimization algorithms operate within this loop.

数学建模确定固定参数 $\mathbf p$ 与设计变量 $\mathbf x$，求解得到 $\mathbf x^*(\mathbf p)$，再评价其实际效果，据此调整参数或改进模型。优化算法在这一闭环内求解。

{{course:modeling}}

Finite-element analysis guides the test setup and strain-gauge placement. Component bench tests establish baseline behavior and check predicted strain, deformation and load paths; the comparison updates model fidelity before the next design is assessed.

有限元分析指导台架方案与传感器、应变片布置；零件台架试验建立原方案基准，并核对预测的应变、变形与传力。仿真与试验对标后，修正模型，再用于评价下一轮设计。
