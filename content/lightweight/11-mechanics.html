Mechanics foundations力学基础

A lightweight component must carry its load with acceptable deformation and life. The calculation proceeds from the free-body diagram to section resultants, local stress and strain, and then strength, stiffness and stability constraints. This order connects a robot’s joint loads to material and geometry choices.

轻质构件需要在允许变形和寿命范围内承载。计算从受力图出发，求截面内力，再求局部应力与应变，最后建立强度、刚度和稳定性约束。由此可以把机器人的关节载荷落实到材料与几何选择。

Loads and section resultants载荷与截面内力

{{course:sectionloads}}

Cut a member at the section of interest and replace the removed side with statically equivalent forces and moments. In three dimensions, these are axial force $N$, shear forces $V_y,V_z$, torque $T$ and bending moments $M_y,M_z$. Forces have unit N and moments N·m. Their distribution along the member follows equilibrium and the actual load path. A robot link commonly carries several components simultaneously.

在关注截面切开构件，用等效力与力矩代替被移去的一侧。三维截面内力包括轴力 $N$、剪力 $V_y,V_z$、扭矩 $T$ 和弯矩 $M_y,M_z$；力的单位为 N，力矩为 N·m。内力沿构件的分布由平衡关系和实际传力路径决定，机器人连杆通常同时承受其中多项。

For a moving link, first calculate the external, gravitational and inertial loads at the selected time or use a multibody inverse-dynamics solution. Apply the corresponding forces and moments to the structural model with matching joint constraints. A maximum actuator torque alone omits radial bearing loads and bending caused by offset forces; contact and emergency-stop cases can change the governing section.

运动连杆应先求指定时刻的外载、重力与惯性载荷，或使用多体逆动力学结果，再按对应关节约束将力与力矩施加到结构模型。单独使用最大执行器力矩会遗漏轴承径向力和偏置力造成的弯曲；接触与急停工况还可能改变危险截面。

Stress and strain应力与应变

Stress describes internal force per unit area, while strain describes relative deformation. Uniform axial stress is $\sigma=N/A$; engineering normal strain is $\varepsilon=\Delta L/L_0$. In simple shear, $\gamma\simeq\Delta x/h$ for a small angle. $V/A$ is an average shear stress; a beam’s actual cross-sectional shear stress is generally nonuniform.

应力描述单位面积上的内力，应变描述相对变形。均匀轴向应力为 $\sigma=N/A$，工程正应变为 $\varepsilon=\Delta L/L_0$；小角度简单剪切下，$\gamma\simeq\Delta x/h$。$V/A$ 只是平均剪应力，梁截面的实际剪应力通常并不均匀。

$$\sigma=\frac{N}{A},\qquad\tau_{\mathrm{avg}}=\frac{V}{A},\qquad\varepsilon=\frac{\Delta L}{L_0},\qquad\gamma\simeq\frac{\Delta x}{h}.$$
{{course:strainbasics}}

Stress is measured in Pa; $1$ MPa equals $1$ N/mm². Normal strain and engineering shear strain are dimensionless. Dividing by area or initial length makes stress–strain data useful for material characterization. Under the same force, a thinner bar still has a higher stress, and holes, notches and contacts create additional local concentrations.

应力单位为 Pa，$1$ MPa 等于 $1$ N/mm²；正应变和工程剪应变均无量纲。用面积与初始长度归一化后，应力—应变数据便于表征材料。在相同拉力下，杆件变细仍会提高应力，孔、缺口和接触还会造成局部集中。

Elastic constitutive relations弹性本构关系

Within the small-strain, linear-elastic range, Young’s modulus $E$ relates normal stress to axial strain, and shear modulus $G$ relates shear stress to engineering shear strain. For an isotropic material, Poisson’s ratio $\nu$ couples axial extension and transverse contraction. The relations are:

在小应变、线弹性范围内，杨氏模量 $E$ 联系正应力与轴向应变，剪切模量 $G$ 联系剪应力与工程剪应变。各向同性材料的泊松比 $\nu$ 则联系轴向伸长与横向收缩：

$$\sigma=E\varepsilon,\qquad\tau=G\gamma,\qquad G=\frac{E}{2(1+\nu)}.$$

For multiaxial loading, the isotropic relation becomes $\varepsilon_x=[\sigma_x-\nu(\sigma_y+\sigma_z)]/E$, with cyclic permutations for the other directions and $\gamma_{xy}=\tau_{xy}/G$. Fibre composites require directional moduli and coupling terms. In a tensile curve, $E$ is the initial elastic slope; yield strength controls the onset of permanent deformation. A higher yield strength does not by itself reduce elastic deflection.

多轴受力时，各向同性关系为 $\varepsilon_x=[\sigma_x-\nu(\sigma_y+\sigma_z)]/E$，其他方向按循环置换得到，且 $\gamma_{xy}=\tau_{xy}/G$。纤维复合材料则需要方向相关的模量与耦合项。在拉伸曲线上，$E$ 是初始弹性斜率，屈服强度控制永久变形的开始；提高屈服强度本身不会减小弹性挠度。

Axial extension example轴向伸长例题

A circular aluminium bar has diameter 10 mm, length 1 m, tensile load 10 kN and $E=74$ GPa. Find axial stress, strain and extension.

圆截面铝杆直径 10 mm、长 1 m，承受 10 kN 拉力，$E=74$ GPa。求轴向应力、应变和伸长量。

$$\begin{aligned}A&=\frac{\pi d^2}{4}=78.54\ \mathrm{mm^2},\\\sigma&=\frac{10\,000}{78.54}=127.32\ \mathrm{MPa},\\\varepsilon&=\frac{127.32}{74\,000}=1.7206\times10^{-3},\\\Delta L&=\varepsilon L=1.721\ \mathrm{mm}.\end{aligned}$$

The nominal stress is below the illustrative 364 MPa proof stress, but a 1.721 mm extension may still violate positioning requirements. Strength and stiffness therefore impose separate conditions on the same rod.

名义应力低于示例材料的 364 MPa 规定塑性延伸强度，但 1.721 mm 的伸长仍可能超过定位允许值。同一根杆需要分别满足强度与刚度条件。

Euler–Bernoulli beam theoryEuler–Bernoulli 梁理论

A slender beam can be described by its centreline deflection $w(x)$. Euler–Bernoulli theory assumes that cross-sections remain plane and perpendicular to the deformed centreline; it neglects transverse shear deformation. Under small rotations, a point at height $z$ from the neutral axis has axial displacement $u_x=-zw^{\prime}$ and axial strain $\varepsilon_x=-zw^{\prime\prime}$. Fibres on opposite sides of the neutral axis extend and shorten.

细长梁可用中心线挠度 $w(x)$ 描述。Euler–Bernoulli 理论假定截面变形后仍保持平面，并垂直于变形后的中心线，忽略横向剪切变形。小转角下，距中性轴高度 $z$ 处的轴向位移为 $u_x=-zw^{\prime}$，轴向应变为 $\varepsilon_x=-zw^{\prime\prime}$；中性轴两侧纤维分别伸长与缩短。

{{course:planesections}}

Let $x$ run along the beam, with $z$ and $w$ positive upward. Define the section moment by $M_y=\int_A z\sigma_x\,\mathrm dA$. For a homogeneous beam under pure bending, the neutral axis passes through the centroid and the second moment of area is $I_y=\int_A z^2\,\mathrm dA$. Combining the strain field with Hooke’s law gives:

取 $x$ 沿梁长，$z$ 与挠度 $w$ 向上为正，并定义截面弯矩 $M_y=\int_A z\sigma_x\,\mathrm dA$。均质梁纯弯曲时，中性轴通过形心，截面二次矩为 $I_y=\int_A z^2\,\mathrm dA$。将应变场与胡克定律结合：

$$\sigma_x(z)=-Ez\,w^{\prime\prime},\qquad M_y=-EI_yw^{\prime\prime},\qquad \sigma_x(z)=\frac{M_yz}{I_y},\qquad |\sigma|_{\max}=\frac{|M_y|}{W_y},\quad W_y=\frac{I_y}{z_{\max}}.$$

$I_y$ has unit m⁴ and section modulus $W_y$ has unit m³. $EI_y$ is bending rigidity, combining material and section geometry. Moving material away from the neutral axis increases $I_y$, but the resulting walls must resist local buckling. Unsymmetric sections or coupled bending require the full section relations. The strain and equilibrium assumptions are developed in MIT’s structural mechanics notes.

$I_y$ 的单位为 m⁴，抗弯截面模量 $W_y$ 的单位为 m³。抗弯刚度 $EI_y$ 同时包含材料与截面几何；材料移离中性轴可以增大 $I_y$，形成的薄壁仍需抵抗局部屈曲。非对称截面或耦合弯曲需要使用完整截面关系。MIT 结构力学讲义进一步给出应变与平衡假设。

Cantilever internal forces and deflection悬臂梁的内力与挠度

{{course:cantileverfields}}

For a cantilever of length $L$ with a downward tip force of magnitude $F$, section shear has constant magnitude $F$, while bending moment magnitude is $F(L-x)$. The root has the largest moment; the free end has the largest deflection. The small-deflection Euler–Bernoulli solution is:

长 $L$ 的悬臂梁自由端承受大小为 $F$ 的向下集中力时，剪力大小恒为 $F$，弯矩大小为 $F(L-x)$。根部弯矩最大，自由端挠度最大。Euler–Bernoulli 小挠度解为：

$$|w(x)|=\frac{Fx^2(3L-x)}{6EI_y},\qquad\delta_b=|w(L)|=\frac{FL^3}{3EI_y},\qquad |\sigma|_{\max,\mathrm{root}}=\frac{FL}{W_y}.$$

Doubling length at unchanged force and section multiplies tip deflection by eight and root bending stress by two. This explains why a longer robot arm often needs a deeper section even if the material strength is sufficient. On a rectangular section, bending normal stress is linear through depth and greatest at the outer fibres; transverse shear stress is parabolic and greatest at the neutral axis. Their maxima occur at different points.

载荷和截面不变时，长度加倍使端部挠度增至八倍、根部弯曲应力增至两倍。因此机械臂加长后，即使材料强度足够，也常需增大截面高度。矩形截面的弯曲正应力沿高度线性变化，在外缘最大；横向剪应力呈抛物线分布，在中性轴最大，两者峰值位于不同位置。

Shear deformation and beam models剪切变形与梁模型

A short beam, a sandwich with a low-shear-modulus core, or a higher-frequency mode can have appreciable shear deformation. Timoshenko theory allows the section rotation to differ from the centreline slope, adding effective shear rigidity $k_sGA$; its dynamic form also includes rotary inertia. The shear correction factor $k_s$ relates the assumed section shear to its elastic energy. NPTEL’s derivation explains this relaxation of the plane-normal assumption.

短梁、低剪切模量芯材夹层梁以及较高频率模态可能具有显著剪切变形。Timoshenko 理论允许截面转角与中心线斜率不同，引入有效剪切刚度 $k_sGA$；动力学形式还计入截面转动惯性。剪切修正系数 $k_s$ 将假定截面剪切与实际弹性能联系起来。NPTEL 推导说明了这一平面法线假设的放宽。

$$\delta=\delta_b+\delta_s=\frac{FL^3}{3EI}+\frac{FL}{k_sGA},\qquad\frac{\delta_s}{\delta_b}=\frac{3EI}{k_sGAL^2}.$$

For a homogeneous rectangular section, $I=bh^3/12$ and $A=bh$. With $\nu=0.30$ and $k_s=5/6$, the shear-to-bending deflection ratio becomes $0.78(h/L)^2$: 0.78% at $L/h=10$, 3.12% at 5, and 19.5% at 2. These values follow this load and section model; the allowable error and the actual $EI/(GA)$ determine which theory is adequate. Sandwich beams can require shear deformation even when their geometric slenderness is large.

均质矩形截面有 $I=bh^3/12$、$A=bh$。取 $\nu=0.30$、$k_s=5/6$，剪切挠度与弯曲挠度之比为 $0.78(h/L)^2$：当 $L/h$ 为 10、5、2 时，分别为 0.78%、3.12%、19.5%。这些数值对应当前载荷与截面模型，理论选择取决于允许误差和实际 $EI/(GA)$；夹层梁即使几何上细长，也可能需要计入剪切变形。

{{course:shearmodels}}

For a rectangular homogeneous section, elementary beam equilibrium gives $\tau_{xz}(z)=3V[1-4z^2/h^2]/(2A)$, zero on the upper and lower free surfaces. Levinson’s higher-order rectangular beam theory introduces cross-section warping so that shear strain varies through depth and satisfies the free-surface conditions without an empirical shear correction factor. It is useful when that distribution matters; Levinson (1981) gives the formulation.

均质矩形截面由初等梁平衡得到 $\tau_{xz}(z)=3V[1-4z^2/h^2]/(2A)$，上下自由表面的剪应力为零。Levinson 高阶矩形梁理论允许截面翘曲，使剪应变沿高度变化并满足自由表面条件，无需经验剪切修正系数；当截面分布本身重要时，可以采用这一模型。具体形式见 Levinson（1981）。

Combined stress and yield组合应力与屈服

A link under bending and torsion has normal and shear stress at the same material point. For a ductile isotropic metal, von Mises equivalent stress compares the combined state with uniaxial yield. In principal stresses it is:

连杆同时受弯与受扭时，同一材料点可能具有正应力与剪应力。对于延性各向同性金属，von Mises 等效应力将组合应力状态与单轴屈服强度比较。用主应力表示为：

$$\sigma_{\mathrm{VM}}=\sqrt{\frac{(\sigma_1-\sigma_2)^2+(\sigma_2-\sigma_3)^2+(\sigma_3-\sigma_1)^2}{2}}\le\frac{R_{p0.2}}{n}.$$

With one axial normal stress $\sigma$ and one shear stress $\tau$, this reduces to $\sigma_{\mathrm{VM}}=\sqrt{\sigma^2+3\tau^2}$. Here $n$ is the selected strength safety factor. Pure shear therefore reaches von Mises yield at $\tau_y=R_{p0.2}/\sqrt3$. Tresca’s maximum-shear criterion gives $\tau_y=R_{p0.2}/2$, producing a more restrictive pure-shear limit.

只有一个轴向正应力 $\sigma$ 和一个剪应力 $\tau$ 时，上式化为 $\sigma_{\mathrm{VM}}=\sqrt{\sigma^2+3\tau^2}$，其中 $n$ 为选用的强度安全系数。因此纯剪切下，von Mises 屈服对应 $\tau_y=R_{p0.2}/\sqrt3$；Tresca 最大剪应力准则对应 $\tau_y=R_{p0.2}/2$，纯剪切限值更保守。

{{course:yieldloci}}

Evaluate all components at the same point before forming equivalent stress. Combining the outer-fibre maximum bending stress with a neutral-axis maximum transverse shear stress would mix different material points. Check multiple sections and points, including holes and fillets. Fatigue, buckling, composite failure and brittle fracture require their own criteria in addition to a metal yield check.

计算等效应力前，应先取得同一点的全部应力分量。将外缘最大弯曲应力与中性轴最大横向剪应力直接组合，会混合不同材料点。应检查多个截面和位置，包括孔与圆角；疲劳、屈曲、复合材料失效和脆性断裂还需要相应准则。

Load capacity and self-weight承载能力与自重

A dimensionless structural measure is load capacity divided by self-weight. For a uniform axial bar of length $L$, area $A$ and density $\rho$, define $M_L=F_0/F_G$, where $F_G=\rho gAL$. A ratio of 1000 means that the specified bar load capacity is 1000 times its own weight. The length, boundary conditions and failure criterion determine the result.

可以用承载能力与自重之比评价结构效率。对于长 $L$、面积 $A$、密度 $\rho$ 的均匀轴向杆，定义 $M_L=F_0/F_G$，其中 $F_G=\rho gAL$。比值为 1000，表示规定承载力为杆件自重的 1000 倍；长度、边界条件和失效准则共同决定这一数值。

$$\frac{F_0}{A}\le\frac{R_{p0.2}}{n}\ \Longrightarrow\ A_{\min}=\frac{nF_0}{R_{p0.2}}\ \Longrightarrow\ F_{G,\min}=\frac{\rho gLnF_0}{R_{p0.2}}\ \Longrightarrow\boxed{M_L=\frac{R_{p0.2}}{n\rho gL}}.$$

For $L=1$ m, $n=1$ and the illustrative properties above, the resulting values are approximately 5,136 for stainless steel, 13,493 for aluminium and 16,267 for titanium. At the same tensile load and length, aluminium needs about 38.1% of the steel bar’s mass, and titanium about 31.6%. Stiffness or fatigue can require a larger area than this strength-only minimum.

取 $L=1$ m、$n=1$，代入上表示例值，不锈钢、铝合金与钛合金的比值约为 5,136、13,493 和 16,267。在相同拉力与长度下，铝杆所需质量约为钢杆的 38.1%，钛杆约为 31.6%。刚度或疲劳约束可能要求比这一静强度最小值更大的面积。

For an ideal slender column, $F_{cr}=\pi^2EI/(KL)^2$, where $K$ is the effective-length factor. With geometrically similar sections, $I=\eta A^2$ and $\eta$ is constant. Solving the buckling constraint for area gives $A_{\min}\propto\sqrt{F_0/E}$ and mass $m_{\min}\propto\rho/\sqrt E$. Density alone therefore does not rank buckling resistance. Fixed section height or wall thickness gives a different derivation.

理想细长压杆的临界力为 $F_{cr}=\pi^2EI/(KL)^2$，$K$ 为有效长度系数。几何相似截面满足 $I=\eta A^2$，其中 $\eta$ 为常数。由屈曲约束求得 $A_{\min}\propto\sqrt{F_0/E}$，所以最小质量 $m_{\min}\propto\rho/\sqrt E$。屈曲选材需要同时考虑模量与密度；截面高度或壁厚固定时，应重新推导。

{{course:capacityindices}}

The same three example materials change order with the constraint. Titanium leads the tensile-strength index; aluminium leads both axial-stiffness and similar-section buckling indices. Once a candidate is selected, verify stiffness, local stability, fatigue, joints, temperature and manufacturing cost on the actual geometry. The material index narrows the choice; the complete structural calculation determines the usable dimensions.

同样三种示例材料，主导约束改变后排序也改变：钛合金的拉伸强度指数最高，铝合金的轴向刚度指数和相似截面屈曲指数最高。选出候选材料后，还需在实际几何上校核刚度、局部稳定性、疲劳、连接、温度与制造成本。材料指数用于缩小范围，完整结构计算决定可用尺寸。
