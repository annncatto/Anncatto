Mass and Motion质量与运动

Mass enters robot design through several quantities. Total mass sets what the base must support and what the battery must transport. The centre of mass sets gravity moments. Rotational inertia describes resistance to angular acceleration about a specified axis. These quantities can change independently when an actuator moves along a limb.

质量通过不同的量影响机器人。总质量决定基座要支撑多少重量、电池要搬运多少负担；质心位置决定重力矩；转动惯量描述绕指定轴加速的难易。沿肢体移动一个执行器，这几个量可以分别改变。

From Kinetic Energy to Joint Torque从动能到关节力矩

For a rigid link, split its motion into translation of the centre of mass and rotation about that centre. With mass $m$, centre velocity $v_c$, angular velocity $\omega$ and inertia tensor $\mathcal I_c$, its kinetic energy is:

刚性杆件的运动可以拆成质心平动和绕质心转动。设质量为 $m$、质心速度为 $v_c$、角速度为 $\omega$、质心惯性张量为 $\mathcal I_c$，动能为：

$$T=\frac12 m v_c^T v_c+\frac12\omega^T\mathcal I_c\omega.$$

The inertia tensor describes an entire three-dimensional mass distribution. Its diagonal entries refer to chosen axes; its off-diagonal entries describe products of inertia. A uniform slender rod of length $L$ has $J_c=mL^2/12$ about a transverse centre axis. About an end, the parallel-axis theorem gives $J_O=J_c+m(L/2)^2=mL^2/3$. The reference axis is part of every inertia value.

惯性张量描述三维质量分布。对角项对应所选坐标轴，非对角项描述惯性积。质量均匀、长度为 $L$ 的细杆，绕质心横轴的惯量为 $J_c=mL^2/12$；绕端点的平行轴，利用平行轴定理得到 $J_O=J_c+m(L/2)^2=mL^2/3$。每个惯量数值都需要指定参考轴。

$$\mathcal I_c=\int_V\rho\big((r^T r)\mathbf 1-r r^T\big)\,dV,\qquad J_O=J_c+mr^2.$$

The scalar formula uses parallel axes separated by perpendicular distance $r$. Moving 0.2 kg from 0.50 m to 0.10 m from an axis reduces its point-mass contribution from 0.050 to 0.002 kg·m². The component still weighs 0.2 kg. This is why a distal motor can dominate a light link’s motion even when it is a modest fraction of the robot’s total mass.

标量公式中的两根轴互相平行，$r$ 是轴间垂直距离。将 0.2 kg 的质量从距轴 0.50 m 移到 0.10 m，其质点惯量贡献从 0.050 降至 0.002 kg·m²，零件仍然重 0.2 kg。因此，一个占整机质量比例不算大的远端电机，也可能主导轻质连杆的运动。

For an articulated robot, link velocities depend on joint coordinates. Writing $v_{c,i}=J_{v,i}(q)\dot q$ and $\omega_i=J_{\omega,i}(q)\dot q$, summing link energies produces the joint-space mass matrix:

关节机器人的杆件速度由关节坐标决定。将质心速度与角速度写成 $v_{c,i}=J_{v,i}(q)\dot q$、$\omega_i=J_{\omega,i}(q)\dot q$，累加各杆件的动能，就得到关节空间质量矩阵：

$$\begin{aligned}T&=\frac12\dot q^T M(q)\dot q,\\M(q)&=\sum_i\left(m_iJ_{v,i}^T J_{v,i}+J_{\omega,i}^T\mathcal I_i J_{\omega,i}\right).\end{aligned}$$

All inertia tensors must be expressed in frames consistent with their Jacobians. $M$ is symmetric and positive definite for independent movable coordinates with positive inertia. Its diagonal entry $M_{ii}$ gives the coefficient of $\ddot q_i$ in joint $i$’s inertia torque; off-diagonal entries couple accelerations across joints. An end mass contributes through several Jacobians, explaining why distal mass affects upstream joints.

惯性张量与相应雅可比必须使用一致的坐标表达。对于独立的可动坐标与正惯量，$M$ 对称正定。对角项 $M_{ii}$ 是关节 $i$ 惯性力矩中 $\ddot q_i$ 的系数，非对角项则耦合不同关节的加速度。末端质量通过多组雅可比进入矩阵，因此会影响多个上游关节。

Choose $f_{\mathrm{ext}}$ as the environment’s force on the robot. A consistent manipulator equation is:

规定 $f_{\mathrm{ext}}$ 表示环境施加给机器人的力，一种符号一致的动力学方程为：

$$M(q)\ddot q+C(q,\dot q)\dot q+g(q)+\tau_f=\tau_{\mathrm{act}}+J(q)^T f_{\mathrm{ext}}.$$

$C\dot q$ contains Coriolis and centrifugal terms, $g$ gravity torque, and $\tau_f$ friction. The mass distribution enters $M$, the velocity-dependent terms and $g$; $J$ itself depends on geometry. Mass also changes the contact forces produced during an interaction, but it does not directly multiply every externally specified force. The transpose mapping $J^T f$ follows from power balance, $f^T\dot x=\tau^T\dot q$. Modern Robotics, Chapter 8 develops these quantities from rigid-body dynamics.

$C\dot q$ 包含科里奥利项与离心项，$g$ 是重力矩，$\tau_f$ 是摩擦。质量分布影响 $M$、速度相关项和 $g$；雅可比 $J$ 本身由几何决定。质量还会影响交互过程中产生的接触力，但不会直接乘在每一个已经给定的外力上。$J^T f$ 的映射来自功率一致性：$f^T\dot x=\tau^T\dot q$。Modern Robotics 第 8 章从刚体动力学推导了这些量。

A Two-Link Limb二连杆肢体

A planar two-link limb makes the effect of mass placement explicit. Let lengths be $L_1,L_2$, link masses $m_1,m_2$, centre distances $r_1,r_2$, centre inertias $J_1,J_2$, and relative second-joint angle $q_2$. Before actuator rotor inertia is added:

平面二连杆能够直接展示质量位置的作用。设杆长为 $L_1,L_2$，质量为 $m_1,m_2$，质心距各自近端关节为 $r_1,r_2$，质心惯量为 $J_1,J_2$，第二关节相对角为 $q_2$。加入电机转子惯量之前：

$$\begin{aligned}M_{11}&=J_1+J_2+m_1r_1^2+m_2(L_1^2+r_2^2+2L_1r_2\cos q_2),\\M_{12}&=M_{21}=J_2+m_2(r_2^2+L_1r_2\cos q_2),\\M_{22}&=J_2+m_2r_2^2.\end{aligned}$$

A motor housing mounted at the second joint travels with the first link, adding approximately $m_mL_1^2$ to the root-joint inertia. Mounting that housing near the root removes most of this contribution. A remote transmission adds its own distributed mass and rotational components. The calculation must place every housing, pulley, bearing and shaft on the body that actually carries it.

安装在第二关节处的电机壳体随第一根杆运动，约为根关节增加 $m_mL_1^2$ 的惯量贡献。将壳体安装在根部附近，可以移走这部分贡献；远程传动又会增加分布质量与旋转部件。电机壳体、带轮、轴承与传动轴，都需要归到实际承载它的刚体上。

Gravity introduces a different dependence. Measure $q_1$ from the horizontal, with $q_2$ relative to the first link. For the same limb, the holding torques are:

重力呈现出另一种关系。令 $q_1$ 从水平方向量起，$q_2$ 是相对第一根杆的角度，这条肢体的静态保持力矩为：

$$\begin{aligned}g_1&=g_0\left[(m_1r_1+m_2L_1)\cos q_1+m_2r_2\cos(q_1+q_2)\right],\\g_2&=g_0m_2r_2\cos(q_1+q_2).\end{aligned}$$

$g_0$ is gravitational acceleration. A limb can have a small holding torque in one posture and a large one in another. Low static current in a hanging posture therefore says little about swing acceleration or horizontal reach. A design comparison should specify both posture and movement, including acceleration and the end force.

$g_0$ 是重力加速度。同一条肢体在某个姿态中保持力矩很小，换一个姿态却可能很大。因此，垂直悬垂时的低电流，不能说明快速摆动或水平伸展时也轻松。比较方案时需要同时指定姿态与运动，包括加速度和末端载荷。

The Mass Felt at Contact接触点感受到的质量

At a contact point, the relevant inertia depends on direction and posture. For an unconstrained robot with translational Jacobian $J_v$, the inverse effective mass along a unit direction $n$ is:

接触点的惯性响应与方向、姿态有关。对于没有额外运动约束的机器人，设平动雅可比为 $J_v$，沿单位方向 $n$ 的等效质量满足：

$$\frac1{m_{\mathrm{eff}}}=n^T J_vM^{-1}J_v^T n,\qquad E_{\mathrm{kin}}=\frac12m_{\mathrm{eff}}v_n^2.$$

This expression describes instantaneous inertial response while other joint directions are free. Additional contacts, locked joints, floating-base motion or active control change the effective model. At a directional singularity the reciprocal can vanish, so the ideal directional effective mass becomes unbounded. It is a posture-dependent mechanical quantity, rather than the robot’s mass divided by a fixed number.

该式描述其他关节方向能够自由运动时的瞬时惯性响应。额外接触、关节锁止、浮动基座运动和主动控制，都会改变等效模型。处于方向奇异位形时，右侧倒数可能趋于零，对应理想方向等效质量趋于无穷。这是与姿态有关的机械量，不能用整机质量除以固定比例代替。

For a fixed effective mass, doubling contact speed quadruples kinetic energy. Reducing moving mass can reduce the energy available to an impact, but peak force also depends on compliance, damping, contact geometry and stopping distance. The energy calculation is useful for comparing designs; contact pressure, trapping and the behavior of the safety system require their own analysis.

固定等效质量时，接触速度翻倍，动能增加到四倍。减轻运动质量可以降低碰撞能够释放的能量，峰值接触力还取决于柔性、阻尼、接触几何与停止距离。动能适合比较设计，接触压力、夹挤以及安全系统的行为仍需要分别分析。

Lower inertia and greater damping are separate changes. Inertia governs acceleration for a given torque; damping dissipates motion-dependent energy. A light link can oscillate strongly when its damping is small. Actuator placement, transmission friction and structural modes determine how these effects appear together.

降低惯量与增加阻尼是两件事。惯量决定给定力矩产生多大加速度，阻尼耗散与运动有关的能量。阻尼很小时，轻质杆件也可能发生明显振动。执行器布置、传动摩擦和结构模态，决定这些作用怎样共同出现。
