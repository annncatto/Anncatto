Materials材料基础

At fixed volume, lower density directly reduces mass. A working structure rarely keeps every dimension fixed, however: a different material may require a thicker wall, a larger section or a reinforced connection. Material selection therefore begins with a property set and a manufacturing condition. Density is one entry in that set.

体积固定时，降低密度可以直接减重。但工作结构很少能保持所有尺寸不变：换一种材料，可能需要更厚的壁、更大的截面，或更强的连接。因此，选材首先要确定一组性能及其制造状态，密度只是其中一项。

Material Families材料类别

Metals, polymers, ceramics and composites offer different combinations of stiffness, strength, ductility, temperature capability and processing. Metals provide useful bearing and joining surfaces and can often carry concentrated loads. Polymers offer low density and complex molded geometry. Ceramics provide high stiffness and temperature resistance but need careful treatment of tensile fracture. Composites combine constituents and orient their reinforcement to suit the load.

金属、聚合物、陶瓷与复合材料，提供不同的刚度、强度、塑性、耐温与加工组合。金属便于形成轴承和连接表面，也适合承受集中载荷；聚合物密度低，便于模塑复杂几何；陶瓷具有较高刚度与耐温能力，需要认真处理拉伸断裂；复合材料则组合不同组分，并按载荷安排增强方向。

These categories are starting points. An aluminium alloy does not have one universal strength, and “carbon fibre” does not define a finished structural material. Composition, temper, fibre fraction, orientation, voids and environment determine the values available in the final part. Comparing a fibre’s longitudinal modulus with a quasi-isotropic laminate’s modulus would mix two different structural definitions.

这些类别只是选材的起点。铝合金没有唯一的强度，“碳纤维”也不足以定义完整结构材料。成分、热处理状态、纤维体积分数、方向、孔隙与环境，共同决定最终零件的可用性能。将纤维纵向模量与准各向同性层合板模量直接比较，会混用两种结构定义。

Processing, Structure and Performance工艺、组织与性能

The materials framework in the course connects composition and structure, synthesis and processing, properties, and service behavior. Changing heat treatment changes microstructure and strength. Changing fibre direction changes the directional stiffness. Changing consolidation changes voids and interlaminar behavior. The property used in a calculation must correspond to the material that the selected process actually produces.

课程中的材料框架，将成分与结构、合成与加工、性能、使用行为联系起来。热处理改变组织和强度，纤维方向改变方向刚度，压实与固化改变孔隙和层间行为。进入计算的性能，需要对应所选工艺真正能够制造出的材料。

Basic properties cover mechanical strength and ductility, magnetic, thermal and electrical behavior, and chemical resistance to corrosion and oxidation. Engineering performance places them in a service condition: static strength, cyclic fatigue, high-temperature creep or low-temperature embrittlement. Process properties describe whether the material can be formed, cast, welded, machined or heat-treated in the intended way. All three groups affect achievable mass.

基本性能包括力学中的强度与塑性，物理中的磁、热、电，以及化学中的耐腐蚀与抗氧化。工程性能将它们放进使用条件，例如静强度、循环疲劳、高温蠕变与低温脆化；工艺性能则说明能否按预期进行变形、铸造、焊接、切削和热处理。这三组性能共同影响可以实现的结构质量。

The course’s discipline map places physics, chemistry, mechanics and mathematics at the theoretical level, followed by applied fields such as metallurgy, ceramics, polymer science, life science and earth science. Mechanical, chemical, civil, aerospace, nuclear, mining and surface engineering put those ideas into components. Lightweight design crosses these levels: chemistry and processing establish the material, mechanics determines its structural use, and manufacturing turns the selected concept into a repeatable part.

课程的学科图从物理、化学、力学与数学，延伸到冶金、陶瓷、高分子、生命与地球科学，再进入机械、化工、土木、宇航、原子、地矿与表面工程。轻量化贯穿这些层次：化学与加工建立材料，力学决定材料怎样承载，制造再将所选概念变成能够稳定生产的零件。

A heat-treated aluminium designation combines alloy and condition. In 6061-T6, 6061 identifies the alloy and T6 specifies solution heat treatment followed by artificial ageing. T651 also includes stress relief by stretching. The designation therefore carries information relevant to strength and machining distortion. Heat treatment generally changes strength much more than Young’s modulus. Solution treatment and ageing describes this processing sequence.

热处理铝合金的标注同时包含牌号与状态。6061-T6 中，6061 表示合金牌号，T6 表示固溶处理后人工时效；T651 还包含拉伸消除应力。因此，这个标注也提供了强度和加工变形方面的信息。热处理对强度的影响，通常远大于对杨氏模量的影响。固溶与时效工艺介绍了这一过程。

Fibre, Matrix and Laminate纤维、基体与铺层

In a fibre-reinforced polymer, fibres carry much of the load along their direction; the matrix transfers shear, holds the fibres in place and participates in compression and damage resistance. A unidirectional ply favors one direction. A laminate stacks plies at selected angles to carry several load components. Its mass efficiency comes from matching reinforcement to the actual load set.

纤维增强聚合物中，纤维沿自身方向承担较多载荷；基体传递剪切、固定纤维，并参与抗压与损伤抵抗。单向层偏向一个方向，层合板则按选定角度叠放铺层，承担多种载荷分量。其质量效率来自增强方向与实际载荷集合的匹配。

That match extends to the section and its connections. A broad thin face, a torsion tube and a concentrated bearing mount need different reinforcement arrangements. Local metal inserts, adhesive overlaps and additional plies can make the interfaces work, but their mass belongs to the composite design. The finished laminate and attachment should be compared with the complete alternative metal part.

这种匹配还要延伸到截面和连接。宽薄面板、受扭管与集中承载的轴承座，需要不同的增强布置。局部金属嵌件、胶接搭接与附加铺层，能够让接口工作，其质量也需要计入复合材料方案。比较对象应是完整铺层与接头，以及对应的完整金属零件。

The course’s material-development examples follow the same logic: matrix toughening acts on crack growth, thin plies alter damage initiation and propagation, and fibre–metal laminates combine ductile metal sheets with reinforcement. These approaches can increase usable performance without simply increasing pristine fibre modulus. Their structural role becomes clearer after distinguishing the failure and deformation modes that set the design dimensions.

课程中的材料改进例子，也沿着这条逻辑展开：基体增韧作用于裂纹扩展，薄层化改变损伤起始与传播，纤维金属层板组合延性金属薄片与纤维增强。这些方法可以在不单纯追求完好纤维模量的情况下，提高可用性能。先区分究竟由哪种变形或失效决定尺寸，才能理解它们为什么有利于轻量化。
