Topology optimization拓扑优化

Topology optimization distributes material within a design domain while keeping load application, supports and protected interfaces fixed. At scheme-design level, it can reveal load-bearing connectivity before dimensions and surface details are finalized. Sizing changes dimensions within a chosen layout; shape optimization moves its boundaries; topology optimization can also create or remove connecting paths.

拓扑优化在设计域中分配材料，同时固定加载、支承与受保护接口。在方案设计阶段，它可以先揭示承载连接关系，再细化尺寸和表面细节。尺寸优化改变既定布局中的尺寸，形状优化移动边界，拓扑优化还允许创建或去除连接路径。

SIMP modelSIMP 模型

SIMP, Solid Isotropic Material with Penalization, assigns each finite element a relative density ρ_e from nearly zero to one. It is a dimensionless design variable; the physical density of the selected solid material is a separate quantity. The stiffness-maximizing formulation under a volume budget is:

SIMP（Solid Isotropic Material with Penalization）为每个有限元单元分配接近 0 到 1 的相对密度 $\rho_e$。这是无量纲设计变量，所选实体材料的物理密度是另一项量。在给定体积预算下，提高结构刚度可写为：

$$\begin{aligned}\min_{\{\rho_e\}}\quad &c=\mathbf U^TK(\boldsymbol\rho)\mathbf U,\\\text{subject to}\quad &K(\boldsymbol\rho)\mathbf U=\mathbf F,\\&\sum_e\rho_eV_e\le V^*,\qquad0<\rho_{\min}\le\rho_e\le1,\\&E_e=\rho_e^pE_0.\end{aligned}$$

$K$ is assembled stiffness, $\mathbf U$ nodal displacement, $\mathbf F$ the specified nodal load, $V_e$ element volume and $V^*$ the available material volume. Under the stated linear force loading, compliance $c$ also equals $\mathbf F^T\mathbf U$. Reducing it reduces load-weighted displacement, so the formulation seeks stiffness within a volume budget.

$K$ 是装配刚度，$\mathbf U$ 是节点位移，$\mathbf F$ 是规定节点载荷，$V_e$ 是单元体积，$V^*$ 是材料体积预算。在上述线性力加载条件下，柔度 $c$ 也等于 $\mathbf F^T\mathbf U$。降低柔度，就是减少载荷加权的位移，因此这一形式在给定体积内寻找更高刚度。

With $p$ near 3, an intermediate-density element contributes much less stiffness than its material fraction. The penalty encourages material to collect into clearer load paths instead of remaining uniformly grey. A small lower density keeps the stiffness problem solvable. Filtering and a minimum feature length help control checkerboards and mesh dependence. The penalty curve explains this tendency; it is not itself a solved component.

$p$ 接近 3 时，中间密度单元的刚度贡献，相对于其材料份额明显更低。惩罚促使材料聚成更明确的传力路径，而不均匀停留在灰色状态。较小的密度下限让刚度方程保持可解，滤波与最小特征尺寸有助于控制棋盘格和网格依赖。惩罚曲线解释这一趋势，真正的结构还需要求解。

For p = 3, density 0.5 gives only 0.125 of the solid stiffness while consuming half the material volume. This discourages intermediate densities. Each iteration solves equilibrium, calculates the sensitivity of compliance to material distribution, filters local numerical effects and updates densities while enforcing the volume budget. The density pattern evolves through repeated structural analysis.

当 $p=3$ 时，相对密度 0.5 只提供实体的 0.125 倍刚度，却消耗一半材料体积，因此中间密度受到惩罚。每轮迭代求解平衡，计算柔度对材料分布的敏感度，过滤局部数值影响，再在体积约束下更新密度。密度图由反复结构分析逐步形成。

Material collects near the outer fibres to form a bending couple and along diagonals that carry the central load toward the supports. Low-contribution regions lose material. This connects the optimized pattern with thin-wall leverage and direct load paths. Changing the support, load direction, volume fraction or minimum feature size can change the topology.

材料在外缘集中形成抗弯力偶，沿斜向路径将中央载荷传到支承，贡献较低区域逐渐减少材料。这将优化结果与薄壁力臂、直接传力路径连接起来。改变支承、加载方向、体积分数或最小特征尺寸，拓扑也会改变。

A 40% design-domain volume is a material budget, not a complete product mass ratio. Connection regions, bearings and other non-design parts remain. The result minimizes linear compliance for the stated load; stress, buckling, fatigue, impact and other load cases require their own constraints or subsequent checks.

设计域 40% 体积表示材料预算，连接区、轴承与其他非设计部件仍然存在。该结果针对给定载荷最小化线性柔度，应力、屈曲、疲劳、冲击和其他工况需要各自约束或后续校核。

From density field to component从密度场到零件

Rebuild the result as manufacturable geometry with the required walls, fillets, holes, joining surfaces and tool access. Re-analyze this rebuilt part rather than assuming that it retains the numerical result. Casting, machining or additive manufacture can impose different practical interpretations of the same load path.

将结果重建为可制造几何，加入必要壁厚、圆角、孔、连接面和刀具空间，再分析重建后的零件。铸造、切削或增材制造，对同一传力路径可能提出不同的工程化实现。

Reanalyse the reconstructed geometry with realistic interfaces and verify the quantities that determine the design: displacement, stress away from numerical singularities, stability, modes and fatigue. Mesh refinement should stabilize those quantities. Static, modal and cyclic component tests then check the load paths and stiffness assumed in the model, closing the analysis–test loop.

以真实接口重新分析重建几何，核对决定设计的位移、数值奇异区之外的应力、稳定性、模态与疲劳。网格加密应使这些量稳定，再通过构件静载、模态和循环试验检查模型假定的传力与刚度，闭合分析—试验流程。

Further reading延伸阅读

Materials Selection in Mechanical Design, by Michael F. Ashby, organizes selection around function, constraints, material indices, charts and material–shape combinations. Leichtbau: Elemente und Konstruktion, by Johannes Wiedemann, develops lightweight members, panels, sandwich construction and structural load introduction. Their published contents provide two complementary routes through the subject.

Michael F. Ashby 的 Materials Selection in Mechanical Design，围绕功能、约束、材料指数、图表与材料—形状组合组织选材。Johannes Wiedemann 的 Leichtbau: Elemente und Konstruktion，展开轻量构件、板、夹层与结构载荷引入。两本书的公开目录提供了互补的学习路线。

For material and process background, Lightweight Materials: Understanding the Basics connects material families with manufacturing and joining. For a reproducible optimization example, the DTU topology optimization teaching resource connects the SIMP formulation to an executable finite-element and optimization loop. These references continue the same design question: which material, geometry and process meet the required function with less complete-system mass?

材料与工艺方面，Lightweight Materials: Understanding the Basics 将材料族连接到制造与连接方法。可复现的优化入门，可以参考 DTU 拓扑优化教学资源，将 SIMP 写成可执行的有限元—优化循环。这些资料继续讨论同一个设计问题：怎样组合材料、几何与工艺，以更低的完整系统质量完成所需功能。
