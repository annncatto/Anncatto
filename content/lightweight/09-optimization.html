Topology optimization拓扑优化

Topology optimization distributes material within a design domain while keeping load application, supports and protected interfaces fixed. At scheme-design level, it can reveal load-bearing connectivity before dimensions and surface details are finalized. Sizing changes dimensions within a chosen layout; shape optimization moves its boundaries; topology optimization can also create or remove connecting paths.

拓扑优化在设计域中分配材料，同时固定加载、支承与受保护接口。在方案设计阶段，它可以先揭示承载连接关系，再细化尺寸和表面细节。尺寸优化改变既定布局中的尺寸，形状优化移动边界，拓扑优化还允许创建或去除连接路径。

SIMP modelSIMP 模型

SIMP, Solid Isotropic Material with Penalization, assigns each finite element a relative density ρ_e from nearly zero to one. It is a dimensionless design variable; the physical density of the selected solid material is a separate quantity. The stiffness-maximizing formulation under a volume budget is:

SIMP（Solid Isotropic Material with Penalization）为每个有限元单元分配接近 0 到 1 的相对密度 $\rho_e$。这是无量纲设计变量，所选实体材料的物理密度是另一项量。在给定体积预算下，提高结构刚度可写为：

$$\begin{aligned}\min_{\{\rho_e\}}\quad &c=\mathbf U^TK(\boldsymbol\rho)\mathbf U,\\\text{subject to}\quad &K(\boldsymbol\rho)\mathbf U=\mathbf F,\\&\sum_e\rho_eV_e\le V^*,\qquad0<\rho_{\min}\le\rho_e\le1,\\&E_e=\rho_e^pE_0.\end{aligned}$$

$K$ is assembled stiffness, $\mathbf U$ nodal displacement, $\mathbf F$ the specified nodal load, $V_e$ element volume and $V^*$ the available material volume. Under the stated linear force loading, compliance $c$ also equals $\mathbf F^T\mathbf U$. Reducing it reduces load-weighted displacement, so the formulation seeks stiffness within a volume budget.

$K$ 是装配刚度，$\mathbf U$ 是节点位移，$\mathbf F$ 是规定节点载荷，$V_e$ 是单元体积，$V^*$ 是材料体积预算。在上述线性力加载条件下，柔度 $c$ 也等于 $\mathbf F^T\mathbf U$。降低柔度，就是减少载荷加权的位移，因此这一形式在给定体积内寻找更高刚度。

For $p=3$, an element at density 0.5 uses half of its solid volume but contributes only $0.5^3=0.125$ of its solid stiffness. Intermediate densities become an inefficient way to spend the material budget. Repeated analysis then redistributes material into more useful paths. A density floor keeps the stiffness matrix solvable; sensitivity filtering couples neighboring updates and suppresses checkerboard patterns.

当 $p=3$ 时，相对密度 0.5 的单元占用一半实体体积，只贡献 $0.5^3=0.125$ 倍实体刚度。在相同材料预算下，中间密度的刚度效率较低，反复分析与更新会将材料集中到更有效的路径。密度下限维持刚度矩阵可解，敏度滤波让相邻单元的更新相互关联，抑制棋盘格。

Simply supported beam evolution简支梁的迭代演化

In a rectangular design domain, place a pin at the lower-left corner and a roller restraining vertical motion at the lower-right corner. Apply a downward unit force at the top centre. Keep 40% of the domain’s material volume and find a distribution with minimum load-point displacement.

在矩形设计域左下角设置铰支承，右下角设置限制竖向位移的滚动支承，顶部中央施加向下单位力。在只保留设计域 40% 材料体积的条件下，寻找使加载点位移最小的材料分布。

This is a plane-stress model with 120 × 36 four-node elements. Solid modulus, thickness and force are normalized to one; Poisson’s ratio is 0.3. Relative density ranges from 0.001 to 1, $p=3$, and the sensitivity filter spans 2.2 element widths. The roller permits horizontal movement, so it avoids imposing an artificial horizontal restraint on the beam. The computation follows the finite-element and optimality-criteria approach in Sigmund / DTU TopOpt.

算例采用 120×36 个四节点平面应力单元，实体模量、厚度和载荷均归一化为 1，泊松比取 0.3。相对密度范围为 0.001～1，$p=3$，敏度滤波半径为 2.2 个单元宽度。滚动支承允许梁端水平移动，避免额外施加水平约束。计算沿用 Sigmund／DTU TopOpt 的有限元与最优性准则思路。

What changes in one iteration一次迭代究竟改变什么

  • Assemble and solve. Convert each density to its modulus, assemble K, and solve KU = F. The new displacement field determines the current compliance.
  • Evaluate material sensitivity. Estimate how much compliance would decrease if a small amount of material were added to each element. Filter those values across nearby elements.
  • Redistribute under the volume constraint. Increase useful densities and reduce less useful ones. The optimality-criteria multiplier is adjusted by bisection until total material remains at 40%; the density change in one update is limited to 0.2.
  • 组装并求解。由密度计算各单元模量，组装刚度矩阵 $K$，求解 $K\mathbf U=\mathbf F$；本轮位移场决定当前柔度。
  • 计算材料增益。估计向各单元增加少量材料时，柔度可以降低多少，再对邻近单元的敏度滤波。
  • 在体积约束下重新分配。提高增益较大区域的密度，减少增益较低区域的材料；用二分法调整最优性准则中的乘子，使总体积保持为 40%，单轮密度变化限制为 0.2。

Before filtering, the linear fixed-load problem gives the following material sensitivity. $\mathbf u_e$ contains an element’s nodal displacements, and $K_e^0$ is its solid-material stiffness matrix. A larger positive value means that adding material there lowers compliance more strongly.

对于线性、固定载荷问题，滤波前的材料增益敏度可写为下式。$\mathbf u_e$ 为单元节点位移，$K_e^0$ 为实体材料的单元刚度矩阵；正值越大，表示在此增加材料能更有效地降低柔度。

$$-\frac{\partial c}{\partial\rho_e}=p\rho_e^{p-1}\mathbf u_e^TK_e^0\mathbf u_e.$$

The gain is recomputed after every equilibrium solve. Removing a weakly loaded region changes the remaining structure’s stiffness and forces, so the process cannot be reproduced by cutting away the lowest-stress pixels from one initial image. The density and sensitivity views below show the two quantities side by side with the compliance history.

每次重新求解平衡后，材料增益都会改变。去除一个区域，会改变剩余结构的刚度和受力，因此需要重新计算，再决定下一轮材料分配。下图可切换材料密度与增益敏度，并将任意迭代帧与柔度曲线对应起来。

Reading the evolving load paths沿迭代观察传力路径

Iteration 0 assigns density 0.4 everywhere. Under the SIMP law, each element initially has only $0.4^3=0.064$ of the solid stiffness. During the first updates, the sensitivity field highlights where material is most useful under the central load and supports. The total material stays constant while its spatial distribution changes.

第 0 次在所有单元上均匀赋予密度 0.4，按 SIMP 插值，每个单元的初始刚度只有实体的 $0.4^3=0.064$。最初几轮中，敏度场显示中央载荷与支承条件下更值得保留材料的位置。总材料体积保持不变，改变的是材料的空间分布。

By about iteration 15, a continuous lower band, an upper arch-like band and connecting diagonals are evident. In a truss interpretation of this vertical load case, the lower band acts as a tie, the upper path mainly carries compression, and diagonals transfer load between them. Their separation supplies a bending lever arm. The continuum still has finite-width joints and local bending, which the finite-element solve retains.

到第 15 次附近，底部连续带、上部拱形带和连接两者的斜向路径已经清晰。在这一竖向工况的桁架近似下，底部路径起拉杆作用，上部路径主要承压，斜向路径在两者之间传力；上下间距提供抗弯力臂。实际密度模型仍有有限宽度节点与局部弯曲，这些响应保留在有限元求解中。

Later iterations mainly refine member width, junctions and boundary densities. From iteration 40 to 80, compliance falls from 36.12 to 36.02, about 0.27%, while the overall topology changes little. The maximum density update at iteration 80 is still about 0.011, so the plotted endpoint records a slowly changing solution rather than proving a global optimum.

后续迭代主要调整杆件宽度、连接节点与边界密度。第 40 次至第 80 次，柔度从 36.12 降到 36.02，约降低 0.27%，整体拓扑变化已经较小；第 80 次的最大密度更新仍约为 0.011，表明这是变化趋缓的计算截点，不能据此证明全局最优。

The final load-point compliance is about 13.0% of its initial value at the same 40% material budget. This quantifies the benefit of redistribution for the stated load. A 40% domain fraction does not imply a complete robot or product loses 60% of its mass: non-design joints, bearings and other components have not entered that denominator.

同样保持 40% 材料预算，最终加载点柔度约为初始值的 13.0%，量化了此工况下重新分配材料的收益。设计域体积分数的分母只包含可优化区域；连接、轴承和其他部件仍需另外计入整机质量。

A new load direction, additional load case or different support can change the useful paths. Strength, buckling, fatigue and impact also add constraints that minimum compliance alone does not enforce. These checks determine whether the proposed topology can become a working lightweight component.

加载方向、附加工况或支承改变，材料增益与传力路径也会改变。强度、屈曲、疲劳和冲击还会加入单一柔度目标未施加的约束，这些校核决定该拓扑能否成为可用的轻质零件。

From density field to component从密度场到零件

Rebuild the result as manufacturable geometry with the required walls, fillets, holes, joining surfaces and tool access. Re-analyze this rebuilt part rather than assuming that it retains the numerical result. Casting, machining or additive manufacture can impose different practical interpretations of the same load path.

将结果重建为可制造几何，加入必要壁厚、圆角、孔、连接面和刀具空间，再分析重建后的零件。铸造、切削或增材制造，对同一传力路径可能提出不同的工程化实现。

Use realistic interfaces to verify the quantities that determine the design: displacement, stress away from numerical singularities, stability, modes and fatigue. Mesh refinement should stabilize those quantities. Static, modal and cyclic component tests then check the load paths and stiffness assumed in the model, closing the analysis–test loop.

采用真实接口条件，核对决定设计的位移、数值奇异区之外的应力、稳定性、模态与疲劳。网格加密应使这些量稳定，再通过构件静载、模态和循环试验检查模型假定的传力与刚度，闭合分析—试验流程。
