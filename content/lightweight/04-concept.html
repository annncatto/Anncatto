Concept design概念设计

Concept design determines mass distribution and functional integration. The positions of actuators and payloads, major load paths, joint arrangement and shared functions establish which components must move and which components must support them. Wall thickness and local fillets are refined within this architecture.

概念设计：质量分布与功能集成在此阶段确定。执行器和负载位置、主传力路径、关节布置与功能共用，决定哪些零件需要运动、哪些零件需要支承它们；壁厚和局部圆角则在这一构型内继续细化。

A static support carries its own weight and external loads. A robot repeatedly accelerates its links, holds them against gravity, changes direction and makes contact. Moving mass therefore contributes to torque demand, motor current and heating, energy use and impact. Distal mass is especially costly because it loads several upstream joints at long lever arms.

静态支架承担自重和外载荷；机器人还反复加速连杆、抵抗重力保持姿态、换向并发生接触。因此，运动质量同时影响扭矩、电流与发热、能耗和冲击。远端质量尤其昂贵，因为它以较长力臂加载到多个上游关节。

The design routes act at two levels: lighter necessary components, and fewer necessary components through integration. Hollow sections, material selection and proximal actuation address the first; structural springs, integrated fluid passages and joint electronics address the second. Early choices can remove repeated supports, flanges and wiring before detailed optimization begins.

设计分为两个层次：让必要零件更轻，以及通过集成让必要零件更少。空心截面、选材与驱动近端布置作用于前者；结构弹簧、集成流道与关节电子系统作用于后者。早期方案可以直接省去重复支架、法兰与线束，再进入详细优化。

From motion to structural requirements从运动到结构要求

The objective is minimum structural mass subject to stiffness, strength, dynamics, safety and cost constraints. Stiffness is expressed through allowable tool deflection and structural modes; strength through static, fatigue and impact limits; dynamics through required torque, speed and bandwidth. Contact force and energy govern the relevant safety checks, while process, production volume and maintenance determine cost.

轻量化设计的目标，是在刚度、强度、动力学、安全与成本约束下，使结构质量最小。刚度落实到工具允许偏移和结构模态，强度落实到静强度、疲劳和冲击极限，动力学落实到所需扭矩、速度和带宽；接触力与能量进入相应安全校核，工艺、批量与维修决定成本。

A multibody dynamics model uses the intended motion, mass, centres of mass and contact conditions to calculate joint forces, torques and time-varying interface loads. These loads are transferred to beam, shell or solid models to obtain their spatial distribution. Static-strength analysis checks critical snapshots; dynamic-strength and fatigue checks retain the load history, stress amplitude, cycles and impact response. Stress analysis identifies load introduction, concentration and local failure risks, while deformation analysis checks position error.

多体动力学分析根据预定运动、质量、质心和接触条件，计算关节力、力矩与随时间变化的接口动载荷，再将这些载荷传入梁、壳或实体模型，得到结构内的载荷分布。静强度分析检查危险时刻，动强度与疲劳校核保留载荷历程、应力幅、循环次数和冲击响应。应力分析定位载荷引入、集中与局部失效风险，变形分析则检查位置误差。

Dimensions, form and relative position connect the model to the manufactured assembly. Shaft and bore diameters set bearing fits; geometric tolerances control the surfaces and datums that establish alignment. Coaxiality aligns bearing seats and transmission axes; flatness controls mounting contact; roundness controls local fit; runout constrains variation during rotation. Errors can introduce bending, friction, uneven preload or encoder misalignment even when nominal strength is adequate.

尺寸、形状与相对位置精度，将计算模型连接到实际装配。轴径与孔径决定轴承配合，几何公差控制建立对准关系的表面与基准。同轴度关系到轴承座和传动轴线对准，平面度关系到安装接触，圆度关系到局部配合，跳动限制旋转中的偏差。即使名义强度足够，这些误差仍会引入附加弯曲、摩擦、不均匀预紧或编码器偏心。

Tolerances follow the functional chain: identify the tool or contact accuracy required, allocate error among joints, bearings and interfaces, and choose measurable datums. Thin-wall machining and heat treatment can release residual stress and change these surfaces. A lighter section therefore needs a process and inspection plan that preserve its critical geometry.

公差沿功能链分配：先确定工具或接触位置需要的精度，再将误差分配到关节、轴承和接口，并选择可测量的基准。薄壁加工和热处理可能释放残余应力、改变这些表面，因此轻质截面还需要能保持关键几何的工艺与检测方案。

Robot and manipulator designs机器人与机械臂调研

Moving an actuator, changing a link’s section and combining a battery enclosure with a frame solve different design problems. The following mechanisms show where load travels, which mass moves and how parts are manufactured. These details explain the lightweighting opportunity more directly than a list of robot masses.

移动电机、改变连杆截面、让电池壳体兼作机架，分别解决不同的设计问题。以下从传力、运动质量与制造细节比较具体机构，再用负载规格检查这些结构究竟服务于什么任务。

Cable-driven SCARA: a lighter wrist and a different support绳驱 SCARA：减轻手腕，重新安排支承

Shi and colleagues’ nine-DoF cable-driven SCARA moves six wrist/gripper actuators to the shoulder. The moving wrist and gripper weigh 222 g and 113 g. Cables carry motion outward, leaving the distal assembly with substantially less actuator mass to accelerate.

Shi 等的九自由度绳驱 SCARA将腕部与夹爪的六个执行器移到肩部，运动端的手腕和夹爪分别为 222 g、113 g。绳索向外传递运动，远端随动的执行器质量减少，换向时需要加速的质量也随之降低。

Its closed-section frame connects the arm to a vertical tower and routes payload bending and torsion into that support. A 3 kg load at 0.5 m creates about 14.7 N·m of gravitational bending moment; the frame and bearings must carry it even when the drive only overcomes rolling resistance in the relevant motion. The 4.5 kg prototype carries that 3 kg load at 0.5 m reach. This is both a mass-placement change and a load-path change.

它还通过闭口截面框架将机械臂连接到竖直塔架，把负载弯矩与扭矩传入静态支承。3 kg 负载位于 0.5 m 处，仅重力就产生约 14.7 N·m 的弯矩；即使相应运动中的驱动主要克服滚动阻力，框架和轴承仍需承担这个弯矩。论文原型质量为 4.5 kg，可在 0.5 m 臂展下搬运 3 kg。这里同时改变了运动质量的位置与结构反力的路径。

Swinging legs: actuator packaging and transmission摆动腿：执行器封装与传动布置

Mini Cheetah packages a motor, planetary gear, encoder and controller into a replaceable joint unit. The MIT actuator study develops its rotor and module arrangement for higher torque density within a similar envelope. Low reduction limits the inertia reflected to the leg and lets contact push the transmission backward. Link mass and motor torque must be designed together: a low-ratio motor cannot support an arbitrarily heavy swinging leg.

Mini Cheetah 将电机、行星齿轮、编码器和驱动器封装成可更换关节。MIT 执行器研究通过转子和模组布局，在相近封装尺寸内提高转矩密度。低减速比限制折算到腿部的转子惯量，也允许接触反过来推动传动。轻腿与电机能力需要共同设计：降低减速比以后，电机必须直接提供更多输出转矩，摆动构件的质量就更加敏感。

LOLA’s leg design uses a roller screw and linkage to turn linear force into knee torque. This permits the motor to sit higher on the leg instead of adding its full mass at the knee. The screw and linkage have their own mass, and their moment arm changes with posture; the benefit is assessed through the entire swing, including peak actuator force and inertia about the hip.

LOLA 的腿部设计通过滚柱丝杆与连杆，将直线力转换为膝关节力矩，使电机可以布置在腿部较高的位置，减少膝部随动的电机质量。丝杆和连杆本身也有质量，作用力臂还会随姿态改变；应在整个摆腿过程中比较髋部惯量与执行器峰值力，才能判断近端布置的收益。

Cassie: elasticity inside the load pathCassie：把弹性放进传力链

Cassie has five actuated joints and two leaf-spring-connected passive joints per leg. The springs participate in the closed-chain leg mechanism, carrying load while allowing compression and elastic energy return. Its spring-mass locomotion model uses this response in motion planning. A separate rigid support and a separate energy-storage element need not duplicate every part of that path.

Cassie 每条腿有五个主动关节，以及由板簧连接的两个被动关节。板簧参与腿部闭链传力，在承载的同时允许压缩，并储存、释放弹性能。其弹簧—质量步行模型将这一响应用于运动规划，同一机械路径兼具支承与弹性储能功能。

This changes the control problem as well as the component count. During stance, spring deflection influences ground reaction force; during swing and landing, the same elasticity creates additional motion to track. Spring stiffness is chosen for the intended gait and load, so replacing it with a rigid link to improve positioning would also change the mechanical behavior the walking design uses.

这一选择也改变了控制对象。支撑相中，弹簧变形影响地面反力；摆动和落地时，同一弹性又引入需要控制的附加运动。弹簧刚度应对应步态与负载，若为提高位置刚度而直接替换成刚性杆，也会改变原步行设计所利用的机械响应。

LWR III: size the joints along the armLWR III：沿臂长分配电机与结构

DLR’s LWR III uses motor diameters of 85, 70 and 50 mm as required torque decreases toward the wrist. Smaller distal drives also reduce the load carried by upstream joints. Joint units integrate gearing, sensing, electronics and bearings, with internal cabling rather than separate external support packages.

DLR 的 LWR III 随着腕端所需转矩降低，分别采用直径 85、70、50 mm 的电机。远端驱动缩小，也减少上游关节需要携带的质量。关节内部共同封装减速器、传感器、电子系统和轴承，并采用内部布线。

Its structural links use load-oriented carbon-fibre preforms, epoxy impregnation and pressing to remove excess resin. This produces shaped links with reinforcement following the expected forces. The 14 kg arm, 14 kg maximum-payload specification describes the resulting assembly; its joint sizing and fibre placement show where the material and packaging decisions are made.

承力连杆采用按载荷方向安排的碳纤维预成形体，浸渍环氧后压制、去除多余树脂，形成纤维沿预期受力路径分布的曲面连杆。14 kg 臂体、最大 14 kg 负载是完整装配体的规格；电机沿臂长分级、纤维按力流布置，则具体说明了质量如何分配。

Humanoid torsos: material zones and shared enclosures人形躯干：材料分区与壳体共用

The TianGong 3.0 description places magnesium-alloy and carbon-fibre components in regions including the body, soles and head shell, while retaining high-strength aluminium in the exoskeleton and chest shell for impact resistance. Finite-element analysis and topology optimization remove material from selected structural regions. This is a regional allocation of stiffness, impact capacity and mass, with the load-bearing and protective parts sized for different duties.

天工 3.0在机身、足底、头部外壳等部位采用镁合金与碳纤维，同时在外骨骼和胸腔外壳保留高强度铝合金以承受冲击，并结合有限元和拓扑优化调整局部材料分布。不同区域分别承担承载、运动与防护任务，材料分区由这些任务决定。

Figure’s F.03 battery enclosure combines stamped high-strength steel, die-cast aluminium and structural adhesive. It both protects the cells and serves as a torso structural member; cooling features are built into the casting. Sharing these paths saves the separate support and cooling parts that would otherwise surround the battery. This is enclosure-level integration, distinct from the structural electrochemical materials discussed earlier.

Figure 的 F.03 电池壳体组合高强度冲压钢、压铸铝与结构胶，既保护电芯，又作为躯干承力构件，冷却结构也直接集成到铸件中。承力和导热路径共用，减少了原本围绕电池另设的支承与冷却零件。这属于封装层面的集成，与前面由电极、电解质本身参与承载的结构储能材料有明确层次差别。

Electrical architecture can remove physical parts too. Figure’s production account describes eliminating a forearm distribution PCB and related dynamic cabling by letting wrist controllers communicate directly with the host. The removed items include connectors, moving wire and their mounting provisions, rather than a uniform reduction in every link’s wall thickness.

电气架构也会改变必要的机械零件。Figure 的生产记录介绍了让腕部控制器直接与主机通信，从而移除前臂配电板及相关动态线缆的修改。减少的对象包括连接器、随动线束和固定它们的安装结构，其作用位置与单纯减薄连杆不同。

Teleoperation: tool, payload and platform遥操：工具、负载与平台

A leader arm is moved by a person; a follower carries the tool. In ALOHA 2, gravity compensation reduces the force needed to operate the leader, while frame and camera changes support robust data collection. Compensation reduces holding effort but can add spring or counterweight mass. For lightweighting, operator effort and moving inertia therefore need separate measurements.

遥操主臂由人推动，从臂携带工具执行任务。ALOHA 2通过重力补偿降低操作主臂的用力，框架与相机布置则服务于稳定采集。补偿可以减小保持姿态所需的力，却也可能增加弹簧或配重质量，因此操作省力与运动惯量需要分别测量。

OpenArm 2.0 explicitly includes the end effector in its payload budget. Under its 4.1 kg nominal condition, a 1.5 kg tool leaves 2.6 kg for the workpiece. Here a lighter gripper directly increases available workpiece mass before any arm redesign. Its 6 kg peak condition has a shorter duty cycle, so it answers a different thermal question.

OpenArm 2.0 明确将末端执行器计入负载预算：在 4.1 kg 名义负载条件下，1.5 kg 工具留下 2.6 kg 工件余量。此处减轻夹爪，可直接增加可用工件质量，无需先重做整条机械臂。6 kg 峰值负载对应更短工作周期，回答的是另一项热负荷问题。

The Galaxea A1X specification lists a 4.2 kg arm with 3 kg rated load at 600 mm; the LEMO configuration uses a different A1Z generation. Unitree’s seven-axis R1 arm lists 5.5 kg and notes position-stiffness limits with low reduction. These figures help set comparison conditions; joint geometry, included tools and load duration are needed before inferring how one structure saves mass.

Galaxea A1X 标注臂体 4.2 kg、600 mm 处额定负载 3 kg，LEMO 配置则采用另一代 A1Z。宇树七轴 R1 机械臂标注 5.5 kg，并说明低减速比下的位置刚度限制。这些数据用于确定比较条件；结合关节几何、所含工具和负载持续时间，才可进一步分析结构减重。
