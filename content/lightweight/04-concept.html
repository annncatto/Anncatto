Concept design概念设计

Concept design determines mass distribution and functional integration. The positions of actuators and payloads, major load paths, joint arrangement and shared functions establish which components must move and which components must support them. Wall thickness and local fillets are refined within this architecture.

概念设计：质量分布与功能集成在此阶段确定。执行器和负载位置、主传力路径、关节布置与功能共用，决定哪些零件需要运动、哪些零件需要支承它们；壁厚和局部圆角则在这一构型内继续细化。

A static support carries its own weight and external loads. A robot repeatedly accelerates its links, holds them against gravity, changes direction and makes contact. Moving mass therefore contributes to torque demand, motor current and heating, energy use and impact. Distal mass is especially costly because it loads several upstream joints at long lever arms.

静态支架承担自重和外载荷；机器人还反复加速连杆、抵抗重力保持姿态、换向并发生接触。因此，运动质量同时影响扭矩、电流与发热、能耗和冲击。远端质量尤其昂贵，因为它以较长力臂加载到多个上游关节。

The design routes act at two levels: lighter necessary components, and fewer necessary components through integration. Hollow sections, material selection and proximal actuation address the first; structural springs, integrated fluid passages and joint electronics address the second. Early choices can remove repeated supports, flanges and wiring before detailed optimization begins.

设计分为两个层次：让必要零件更轻，以及通过集成让必要零件更少。空心截面、选材与驱动近端布置作用于前者；结构弹簧、集成流道与关节电子系统作用于后者。早期方案可以直接省去重复支架、法兰与线束，再进入详细优化。

From motion to structural requirements从运动到结构要求

The objective is minimum structural mass subject to stiffness, strength, dynamics, safety and cost constraints. Stiffness is expressed through allowable tool deflection and structural modes; strength through static, fatigue and impact limits; dynamics through required torque, speed and bandwidth. Contact force and energy govern the relevant safety checks, while process, production volume and maintenance determine cost.

轻量化设计的目标，是在刚度、强度、动力学、安全与成本约束下，使结构质量最小。刚度落实到工具允许偏移和结构模态，强度落实到静强度、疲劳和冲击极限，动力学落实到所需扭矩、速度和带宽；接触力与能量进入相应安全校核，工艺、批量与维修决定成本。

A multibody dynamics model uses the intended motion, mass, centres of mass and contact conditions to calculate joint forces, torques and time-varying interface loads. These loads are transferred to beam, shell or solid models to obtain their spatial distribution. Static-strength analysis checks critical snapshots; dynamic-strength and fatigue checks retain the load history, stress amplitude, cycles and impact response. Stress analysis identifies load introduction, concentration and local failure risks, while deformation analysis checks position error.

多体动力学分析根据预定运动、质量、质心和接触条件，计算关节力、力矩与随时间变化的接口动载荷，再将这些载荷传入梁、壳或实体模型，得到结构内的载荷分布。静强度分析检查危险时刻，动强度与疲劳校核保留载荷历程、应力幅、循环次数和冲击响应。应力分析定位载荷引入、集中与局部失效风险，变形分析则检查位置误差。

Dimensions, form and relative position connect the model to the manufactured assembly. Shaft and bore diameters set bearing fits; geometric tolerances control the surfaces and datums that establish alignment. Coaxiality aligns bearing seats and transmission axes; flatness controls mounting contact; roundness controls local fit; runout constrains variation during rotation. Errors can introduce bending, friction, uneven preload or encoder misalignment even when nominal strength is adequate.

尺寸、形状与相对位置精度，将计算模型连接到实际装配。轴径与孔径决定轴承配合，几何公差控制建立对准关系的表面与基准。同轴度关系到轴承座和传动轴线对准，平面度关系到安装接触，圆度关系到局部配合，跳动限制旋转中的偏差。即使名义强度足够，这些误差仍会引入附加弯曲、摩擦、不均匀预紧或编码器偏心。

Tolerances follow the functional chain: identify the tool or contact accuracy required, allocate error among joints, bearings and interfaces, and choose measurable datums. Thin-wall machining and heat treatment can release residual stress and change these surfaces. A lighter section therefore needs a process and inspection plan that preserve its critical geometry.

公差沿功能链分配：先确定工具或接触位置需要的精度，再将误差分配到关节、轴承和接口，并选择可测量的基准。薄壁加工和热处理可能释放残余应力、改变这些表面，因此轻质截面还需要能保持关键几何的工艺与检测方案。

Legs: Inertia and Elasticity腿部：惯量与弹性

MIT’s Mini Cheetah uses modular electric actuators to support rapid, dynamic motion. The actuator development combines a motor, planetary reduction, sensing and control in a compact assembly. The design links torque density to impact tolerance, thermal behavior and manufacturing. Low reduction helps limit reflected inertia and permits backdriving, while the joint must still deliver the required peak and continuous torque. This is the actuation tradeoff behind the robot’s dynamic behavior, rather than a benefit of a light shell alone.

MIT Mini Cheetah 用模块化电驱动支持快速动态运动。其执行器设计将电机、行星减速、传感与控制装进紧凑模组，把转矩密度与抗冲击、热性能、制造联系起来。低减速比有助于限制反射惯量，并允许反向驱动；关节仍需提供所需的峰值和持续转矩。这种驱动取舍与动态表现直接相关，轻质外壳只是其中一部分。

Cassie approaches leg dynamics with compliant mechanisms and springs. The spring-mass walking study uses this leg architecture to exploit passive dynamics. Springs can store and return energy and reshape contact dynamics, while joint coordination determines how that compliance appears at the foot. Mass placement, link geometry and stiffness should be evaluated together. A spring that reduces peak power during one gait can also introduce a mode that the controller must manage.

Cassie 则用柔顺机构与弹簧组织腿部动力学。弹簧—质量行走研究利用这一构型发挥被动动力学的作用。弹簧能够储存和释放能量，改变接触动态；关节协调又决定这些柔顺性怎样表现到足端。质量分配、连杆几何与刚度需要一起评估：一种步态下降低峰值功率的弹簧，也可能引入需要控制器处理的模态。

LOLA illustrates a different transmission choice. Its leg mechanisms include screw-driven actuation, discussed in the robot’s design thesis. A screw and linkage convert linear force into posture-dependent joint torque. Mounting a motor toward the upper leg can reduce moving distal mass, but the screw, supports and linkage still contribute inertia and must withstand reversal, buckling and fatigue. The useful comparison is the complete mechanism over its working range.

LOLA 展示了另一种传动选择，其设计论文介绍了腿部的丝杆驱动机构。丝杆与连杆把直线力转换为随姿态变化的关节转矩。电机靠近大腿上部，有助于减少远端运动质量；丝杆、支承与连杆仍然贡献惯量，也要承受换向、屈曲和疲劳。比较应覆盖完整机构及其工作范围。

Arms: Structure and Mechatronics机械臂：结构与机电协同

The DLR LWR III has a published arm mass of 14 kg, maximum payload of 14 kg and reach of 936 mm. Its carbon-fibre links work with compact motors, Harmonic Drive reducers and integrated electronics. The mechatronic design places motor-side and link-side position sensing alongside joint torque sensing. This connects light structures to vibration damping and impedance control: elasticity is measured and managed, while the physical load path remains stiff enough for the task.

DLR LWR III公布的臂体质量为 14 kg，最大负载 14 kg，臂展 936 mm。碳纤维连杆与紧凑电机、谐波减速器及集成电子系统共同工作。其机电设计结合电机侧与连杆侧位置传感，以及关节转矩传感，将轻质结构连接到振动抑制与阻抗控制：测量并管理弹性，同时保持任务所需的物理传力刚度。

The carbon-fibre structure also illustrates process-led geometry. DLR describes oriented fibre preforms impregnated with epoxy and pressed to remove excess resin. Fibre placement follows expected loads, producing curved structural links rather than replacing a metal shape with an arbitrary laminate. Mechanical interfaces, internal wiring and joint modules complete the assembly.

碳纤维结构也展示了工艺如何参与几何设计。DLR 采用定向纤维预成形体，浸渍环氧后压制，去除多余树脂。纤维沿预期载荷布置，形成曲面承力连杆；机械接口、内部走线与关节模组共同完成装配。

A 2025 cable-driven SCARA study relocates six wrist and gripper actuators to the shoulder. It reports a 222 g wrist, 113 g gripper and 3 kg payload at 0.5 m reach. A closed-section frame transfers bending and torsion into the stationary support, changing the shoulder motor’s load path. The reported large torque reduction belongs to this architecture and its comparison. Cable routing, pretension, stretch, pulley friction and joint sensing remain part of the mechanism’s design.

一项 2025 年的绳驱 SCARA 研究将六个腕部与夹爪执行器移到肩部，报告腕部质量 222 g、夹爪 113 g，并在 0.5 m 臂展下承载 3 kg。闭口截面框架将弯矩与扭矩传递到固定支承，改变了肩部电机承担的载荷路径。文中较大的转矩降幅对应这一构型及其比较条件；绳索走线、预紧、伸长、滑轮摩擦和关节测量，仍然需要设计。

This SCARA combines two independent gains: less moving mass at the tool and a different path for structural reactions. Moving a motor without changing the support cannot remove the gravitational work needed to raise a payload. A good remote-drive design states which mass is relocated, which load is redirected and which transmission losses are added.

该 SCARA 结合了两项独立收益：工具端运动质量减少，以及结构反力的路径改变。仅移动电机、不改变支承，无法消除抬升负载所需的重力做功。近端驱动设计应说明移走了哪些质量、重定向了哪些载荷，以及增加了哪些传动损耗。

Humanoids: Materials and Integration人形：材料与集成

The official TianGong 3.0 introduction describes magnesium-alloy parts, carbon-fibre components and topology optimization supported by finite-element analysis. Different body regions use different materials according to load and impact requirements. This is a material–geometry combination: local shell and frame decisions contribute to whole-body mass, while the joint layout must preserve workspace, assembly and structural continuity.

天工 3.0 官方介绍列出了镁合金零件、碳纤维部件，以及有限元支持的拓扑优化。不同部位根据承载与冲击要求选用材料，体现材料与几何的联合设计。局部壳体和框架共同影响整机质量，关节布局还需兼顾工作空间、装配与结构连续性。

Figure 03 provides an example of manufacturing and electrical integration. Alongside its structural battery enclosure, the robot introduction describes a shift toward casting, stamping and injection molding, with reduced part count and assembly effort. The production report also describes removing a forearm distribution board and dynamic cabling, with wrist motor controllers communicating directly with the host. Reducing interfaces can improve packaging and reliability as well as mass.

Figure 03 展示了制造与电气集成。除了结构化电池壳体，产品介绍还说明了向铸造、冲压和注塑转变，以及零件数量与装配工序的减少。生产报告介绍了移除前臂配电板与动态线缆、让腕部电机控制器直接与主机通信的修改。接口减少，同时影响布置、可靠性与质量。

These examples are compatible strategies. A topology-optimized component can be cast; a battery enclosure can use lightweight materials; a simpler electrical architecture can reduce the brackets and connectors required to support it. The system benefit emerges when one change removes several dependent parts. Manufacturer descriptions establish the design choices, while attributing a measured whole-robot mass reduction to any single choice requires a component mass budget.

这些策略可以组合使用：拓扑优化零件可以铸造，电池壳体可以采用轻质材料，简化电气架构又可能减少支架与连接器。一次修改连带省去多项配套零件，才形成系统收益。厂商资料能够确认设计选择；要把整机减重归因到某一项选择，还需要部件质量账目。

Teleoperation: Leader, Follower and Platform遥操：主臂、从臂与平台

A teleoperation leader is manipulated by a person, while the follower carries out the task. The leader benefits from low friction, low moving inertia and comfortable workspace. Counterweights or springs can reduce the force needed to hold it, yet add inertia or change the force felt during motion. The follower instead needs to carry the tool and payload at the required speed and precision. A light-looking leader does not establish the mass or load capacity of the follower.

遥操主臂由人操作，从臂负责执行任务。主臂需要低摩擦、低运动惯量与舒适工作空间；配重和弹簧可以降低维持姿态所需的力，也可能增加惯量，或改变运动中的手感。从臂则需要按要求的速度与精度携带工具和负载。主臂看起来轻巧，无法直接说明从臂的质量或负载能力。

Galaxea’s A1X specification gives 4.2 kg arm mass and a rated 3 kg load at 600 mm. These are follower-arm figures. Its current LEMO product page describes a different A1Z-based configuration. Arm generation and whether a gripper is included need to accompany any comparison; neither page gives enough information to infer the leader’s inertia from appearance.

Galaxea 的 A1X 规格给出臂体质量 4.2 kg、600 mm 处额定负载 3 kg，这些数字对应从臂。其当前 LEMO 页面采用另一套基于 A1Z 的配置。比较需要注明臂体代际，以及是否包含夹爪；这两个页面也不足以支持仅凭外观推断主臂惯量。

OpenArm makes load conditions explicit. The 2.0 specification states a nominal 4.1 kg load in the worst posture for one minute and a peak 6 kg condition consisting of a three-second move followed by a one-second hold. The version notes include the end effector in that payload. A 1.5 kg tool therefore leaves 2.6 kg for the workpiece under the nominal condition. Payload, peak torque and continuous duty are related through the thermal and structural model.

OpenArm 将负载条件写得比较明确。2.0 规格中的名义负载为最不利姿态下承载 4.1 kg 一分钟，峰值 6 kg 则对应运动三秒、保持一秒。版本说明将末端执行器计入负载，因此在名义条件下，1.5 kg 工具会留下 2.6 kg 工件余量。负载、峰值转矩和持续工作能力，需要通过热与结构模型联系起来。

The Unitree R1 robotic arm specifies a 5.5 kg, seven-axis arm and discusses the limited position-control stiffness associated with its low-reduction design. This is a useful reminder that backdrivability, reflected inertia and position stiffness belong to one joint-level tradeoff. Control tuning and load-dependent behavior matter alongside nominal payload. This product is the R1 arm; the R1 humanoid is a separate system.

宇树 R1 机械臂公布了 5.5 kg、七轴的规格，并说明低减速比设计对应的位置控制刚度限制。可反驱性、反射惯量与位置刚度，属于同一个关节层面的取舍；除了名义负载，还需要关注控制调节和载荷相关表现。这款产品是 R1 机械臂，R1 人形机器人属于另一套系统。

ALOHA 2 treats teleoperation as an integrated platform: improved gravity compensation and grippers reduce operator effort, while the frame supports camera placement and task-space access. Arms, supports, cameras, cables and mobile equipment should be counted according to the question being asked. A frame that increases total mass can still improve data collection by preserving camera alignment and reducing vibration.

ALOHA 2按完整遥操平台设计：改进重力补偿与夹爪，降低操作者负担；框架则支持相机布置和任务空间。机械臂、支架、相机、线缆与移动设备，应根据比较问题分别计入。增加总质量的框架，也可能通过保持相机对准、减少振动来改善数据采集。
