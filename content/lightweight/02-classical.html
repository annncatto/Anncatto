Materials材料

CFRP combines low density with high properties along the fibre direction. Load-aligned fibres supply tensile stiffness and strength; the matrix transfers shear and stabilizes them. A multidirectional laminate sacrifices some longitudinal performance to carry transverse and shear loads. Its useful performance is therefore specified by fibre, resin, volume fraction, orientation and stacking sequence.

CFRP 将低密度与沿纤维方向的高性能结合。沿载荷布置的纤维提供拉伸刚度与强度，基体传递剪切并固定纤维；多向层合板则用一部分纵向性能换取横向与剪切承载能力。因此，材料性能需要由纤维、树脂、体积分数、方向与铺层顺序共同定义。

CFRP also supports functional integration: conductive, sensing and actuating elements can be embedded in the composite. Large, continuous moulded skins reduce steps and gaps, helping maintain aerodynamic surface quality. Its corrosion and fatigue behavior is attractive in aircraft structures; Airbus lists CFRP as 53% of the A350 airframe’s material mass. Airbus A350.

CFRP 还适合功能集成：导电、传感与驱动元件可以嵌入复合结构；大尺寸整体蒙皮减少台阶与间隙，有利于保持气动表面质量。耐腐蚀与疲劳性能使其适合航空结构，空客 A350 的 CFRP 占机体结构材料质量约 53%。

Life-cycle accounting explains why energy-intensive manufacture may still be worthwhile. One aircraft assessment assigns only about 0.1% of lifetime carbon emissions to manufacture for long-haul service and 0.2% for short/medium-haul service: lower operating fuel consumption dominates that comparison. These shares depend on aircraft utilization, lifetime and accounting boundary.

全寿命周期分析解释了高能耗制造仍可能值得采用的原因。一组民机算例中，制造阶段约占远程飞机全寿命碳排放的 0.1%、短中程飞机的 0.2%，使用阶段的节油收益占据主导。这些比例对应算例的航程、利用率、寿命与核算范围。

Fibre–metal laminates alternate metal and fibre-composite layers. The metal contributes ductility and useful isotropic behavior, while the reinforcement can slow crack growth and tailor stiffness. Glass-fibre/aluminium GLARE is one such combination. With carbon fibre and aluminium, electrical isolation and corrosion protection become part of the interface design.

纤维金属层板交替组合金属与纤维复合层。金属提供塑性及相对均匀的方向性能，纤维增强可以阻碍裂纹扩展并定制刚度。玻璃纤维—铝合金 GLARE 是其中一种组合。碳纤维与铝组合时，还需要把电隔离与防腐纳入界面设计。

Glass-fibre/aluminium GLARE is used on the A380. Metal supplies toughness, electrical conduction and comparatively isotropic behavior; fibres supply directional stiffness, strength, fatigue and corrosion resistance. Carbon fibre in contact with aluminium requires electrical isolation against galvanic corrosion. EPDM elastomer interlayers can absorb impact energy; a tested hybrid reduced delaminated area by about 35%, and such compliant interlayers can also support morphing structures.

A380 使用的 GLARE 将玻璃纤维与铝箔结合。金属贡献韧性、导电性和较均匀的方向性能，纤维贡献定向高刚高强、抗疲劳与耐腐蚀能力。碳纤维与铝接触时需要电隔离，避免电偶腐蚀。EPDM 弹性体夹层可以吸收冲击能量，一组混杂结构试验的分层面积减少约 35%，这类柔性夹层也可用于变形结构。

Matrix modification targets crack initiation and propagation. Small, well-dispersed particles create a large interfacial area; their chemistry and bonding determine whether cracks are deflected, pinned or accompanied by additional dissipation. Nano-boehmite modified epoxy showed fracture toughness +39%, critical energy-release rate +66% and stiffness +17% in the reported tests. Mineral nanofiller at 30% loading raised thermal conductivity by 40% and reduced flame propagation to one third; taurine-modified boehmite also improved fatigue behavior. These changes refer to the tested resin systems.

基体改性针对裂纹的起始与扩展。细小且分散良好的颗粒提供较大界面面积，界面化学与结合状态决定裂纹偏转、钉扎及附加耗能。纳米勃姆石改性环氧的试验中，断裂韧度提高 39%、临界能量释放率提高 66%、刚度提高 17%；另一组 30% 矿物纳米填充使热导率提高 40%、火焰传播速率降至三分之一。牛磺酸改性勃姆石还可改善疲劳行为。这些数值对应受测树脂体系。

Thin-ply reinforcement reduces ply areal mass from conventional 160–600 g/m² to about 20–100 g/m². More orientations fit within a given thickness, and thinner individual layers can delay matrix cracking and delamination. Mechanical and fatigue performance can improve and delaminated areas can shrink; placing and processing more layers requires additional time and cost.

薄层化将单层面密度从常规 160～600 g/m² 降到约 20～100 g/m²。给定总厚度内可以布置更多方向，较薄单层还会改变基体开裂与分层的起始和传播，改善力学与疲劳性能、减小分层面积；更多层数也增加铺放、加工时间与成本。

Structural performance结构性能

Damage, manufacturing scatter and imperfect failure models reduce the performance available to a structure. A knockdown factor (KDF) translates reference test performance into a design value:

损伤、制造离散与失效模型误差会降低结构可用的性能。折减系数 KDF 将参考试验值转换为设计值：

$$X_{\mathrm{design}}=X_{\mathrm{ref}}\,\mathrm{KDF}_1\,\mathrm{KDF}_2\cdots,\qquad 0<\mathrm{KDF}_i\le1.$$

Temperature, moisture, defects and uncertainty may each require an allowance. Better failure calculations, production quality assurance and detectable damage can justify less conservative factors. Raising a KDF toward one increases usable performance and can reduce required thickness without changing the nominal material. Factors must represent distinct effects so that the same uncertainty is not counted twice.

温度、湿度、缺陷与模型不确定性都可能需要折减。更准确的失效计算、更稳定的制造质保与可检出的损伤，可以支持较少保守的折减。KDF 向 1 接近意味着可用性能提高，在名义材料不变时也可能减薄。各项系数应对应不同影响，避免重复计入同一不确定性。

Compression after impact (CAI) often governs CFRP damage tolerance: a small visible mark can hide extensive internal delamination and reduced compressive strength. Impact between stiffeners allows the skin to absorb some energy through elastic deflection; impact directly over a stiffener can cause much more localized damage. A component-level assessment separates recoverable elastic energy from damaging energy and preserves the actual support and impact location.

冲击后压缩（CAI）常决定 CFRP 的损伤容限：表面小痕迹可能伴随较大内部分层和抗压能力下降。加强件之间的蒙皮受冲击时，一部分能量由弹性变形吸收；正对加强件受冲击时，局部损伤可能大得多。构件级分析应区分可恢复弹性能与造成损伤的能量，并保留真实支承和冲击位置。

Resonance fatigue testing accelerates high-cycle tests by loading near a structural mode. It can reach more than 10⁷ cycles in practical test times; a GFRP/epoxy example sustained 2×10⁷ cycles at 1700 microstrain. The strain amplitude, stress ratio and mode shape define what that result establishes.

共振疲劳试验利用接近结构模态的加载提高高周试验效率，在合理时间内完成 10⁷ 次以上循环。一组 GFRP／环氧试验在 1700 微应变下完成 2×10⁷ 次循环；应变幅、应力比与模态形状共同限定这一结果的意义。

Structural health monitoring (SHM) can make hidden damage observable. Bonded or embedded piezoelectric elements transmit and receive guided ultrasound; changes in stiffness or a debond alter transmission, reflection and scattering. Comparing signals with an intact baseline helps locate damage, with temperature and sensor condition compensated. UC San Diego adhesive-bond inspection.

结构健康监测（SHM）使隐蔽损伤可被检测。贴附或嵌入的压电元件发射并接收导波超声；局部刚度变化或脱粘改变透射、反射与散射。与完好基准比较，可识别并定位损伤，同时需要补偿温度和传感器状态的影响。UC San Diego 胶接检测研究。

Detectable damage can support a revised allowable strain and reduce structural thickness. A vertical-tail case reports at least 5% net mass reduction after including the SHM system’s own mass. Monitoring serves the design here; embedding its sensors and wiring into the load-bearing structure also connects it to active integration.

损伤可检出后，可以用相应证据修订许用应变，减少结构厚度。一个垂尾算例计入 SHM 系统自重后，净减重仍达到至少 5%。监测在这里服务于设计；将传感器和走线进一步嵌入承力结构，又连接到主动功能集成。

Design concepts设计概念

Composite-oriented design combines load-aligned reinforcement, large integrated parts and suitable joints from the concept stage. Sandwich skins separate stiff faces with a light core; integral frames and stringers carry loads without repeated overlap flanges and fasteners. The resulting load paths differ from a metal assembly copied in composite.

面向复合材料的设计，在概念阶段就共同安排载荷导向铺层、大尺寸整体零件与连接。夹芯蒙皮用轻芯材分隔高刚度面板；整体框、纵桁减少重复搭接法兰和紧固件，重新组织传力路径。

Manufacturing制造

Composite manufacture must both place reinforcement and consolidate the matrix. Automated tape laying (ATL) and automated fibre placement (AFP) control orientation and placement; pultrusion suits a continuous section; winding follows a path around a tool. Resin-transfer molding (RTM) places a dry preform in a closed mold and injects resin before cure. Each route creates different practical limits on curvature, local thickness, fibre continuity and integrated features.

复合材料制造，需要同时安排增强体并完成基体固结。自动铺带 ATL、自动铺丝 AFP 控制方向与位置，拉挤适合连续截面，缠绕沿模具路径布置；树脂传递模塑 RTM 则把干态预成形体放进闭模，注入树脂后固化。不同工艺，对曲率、局部厚度、纤维连续性和集成特征提出不同限制。

{{course:rtm}}

The RTM chain includes cutting, storage, grabbing, draping, tacking, pre-consolidation, inspection, final contour definition, injection and curing. Automation reduces manual variation; near-net manufacture reduces subsequent edge sealing and machining. Isothermal RTM cuts repeated heating and cooling: one fuselage-frame process reports a 60% cycle-time reduction. Its stable temperature also permits cheaper tooling metals where a large thermal cycle would require low-expansion Invar.

RTM 工艺链包括裁切、储存、抓取、铺覆、定位、预压实、检测、最终轮廓、注射与固化。自动化减少人工离散，近净成形减少封边与后加工。等温 RTM 减少反复升降温，一组机身框段工艺的节拍缩短约 60%；稳定温度也放宽了模具低膨胀要求，使部分工况可以使用比 Invar 更便宜的模具金属。

Vacuum-assisted infusion uses a pressure difference to move resin through the reinforcement. Flow paths and temporary channels affect filling time and the risk of dry regions. Fibre compaction changes permeability and thickness during filling. Tool sealing, resin viscosity and cure timing therefore influence both process repeatability and the properties of the final laminate.

真空辅助浸渍利用压差，使树脂穿过增强体。流道与临时通道影响充模时间和干斑风险，纤维压实又会改变渗透率和厚度。因此，密封、树脂黏度与固化时序，同时影响工艺一致性和最终层合板性能。

Vacuum-difference processing uses a pressure difference to open a temporary flow channel for infusion; changing the pressure closes the channel again. A reusable, switchable channel reduces flow-distribution consumables while retaining control of resin access to the fibre preform.

真空差压工艺通过压差打开临时流道，供树脂浸润增强体；改变压差后，流道重新闭合。可复用、可切换的树脂通道减少导流耗材，并控制树脂进入纤维预成形体的路径。

Continuous-fibre printing first requires a uniformly impregnated thermoplastic feedstock. Ultrasonic assistance improves penetration into fibre bundles; a reported feedstock process reduced filament cost by about 80%. Fibre volume fractions can reach about 65% in suitable composite processes, while voids, waviness and cure quality still control usable compression and shear performance. ATL, AFP, RTM, pultrusion and winding address different geometries and production volumes. Ultrasonic impregnation study.

连续纤维打印首先需要浸润均匀的热塑性丝材。超声辅助使基体更充分进入纤维束，一组丝材工艺的成本降低约 80%。适当工艺下纤维体积分数可达到约 65%，孔隙、波曲和固化质量仍决定可用抗压与剪切性能。ATL、AFP、RTM、拉挤与缠绕，分别适应不同几何和生产批量。超声浸渍研究。

Light skins and tubes transfer load through fittings, bearing seats, flanges, inserts and adhesive overlaps. These regions need sufficient area and stiffness to spread concentrated force into the thin structure. The mass of an efficient member can be outweighed by heavy end fittings, so connection design belongs in the initial comparison.

轻质蒙皮和管件，通过接头、轴承座、法兰、嵌件和胶接搭接传力。这些区域需要足够面积与刚度，将集中力扩散到薄壁。高效杆件的收益可能被重端接头抵消，因此连接设计应进入初步比较。

Bolts clamp mating surfaces through preload. The joint should remain closed and resist the intended slip, while flange bending and preload variation are included in the check. Tightening torque is affected by friction and does not measure bolt tension exactly. Thin walls may need bosses, backing plates or inserts to introduce the load without local crushing.

螺栓通过预紧夹住配合面。需要检查接头保持闭合、抵抗预期滑移的能力，也要计入法兰弯曲与预紧离散。拧紧力矩受摩擦影响，无法精确代表螺栓拉力；薄壁可能需要凸台、背板或嵌件，避免局部压溃。

Adhesive joints distribute load over an overlap and can avoid drilling through fibres. The ends of the overlap can develop peel and shear concentrations; surface preparation, bond-line thickness, cure and environment govern repeatability. A composite insert must spread bearing and through-thickness loads into the laminate without triggering crushing or delamination.

胶接能够沿搭接面分布传力，也可以减少纤维钻孔。搭接端部仍可能出现剥离与剪切集中，表面处理、胶层厚度、固化和环境共同决定一致性。复合材料嵌件需要将孔边承压与厚度方向载荷扩散进铺层，同时控制压溃和分层。

A bonded metallic sleeve can spread the load into a CFRP tube. A potted insert can provide a threaded attachment in a sandwich. Threads cut directly into a thin laminate interrupt fibres and provide little bearing area, while an insert adds its own pull-out, shear and thermal-mismatch requirements. The connection should be sized with the local laminate, rather than selected after the global member is finished.

胶接金属套筒可以向 CFRP 管件扩散载荷，灌封嵌件可以为夹层提供螺纹接口。直接在薄铺层上攻丝，会切断纤维，也缺少承压面积；嵌件又带来拔出、剪切与热失配要求。接头应与局部铺层一起设计。
