Load efficiency and energy absorption承载效率与吸能

Capacity under different constraints不同约束下的承载能力

The load-capacity-to-self-weight ratio $M_L=F_0/F_G$ measures a component under a specified requirement. A larger value means more useful load per unit self-weight. For a uniform tensile bar of fixed length, the strength constraint gives $M_L=\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}/(\rho gL)$. With the three illustrative materials above and the same strength factor, titanium leads this comparison: its ratio is 3.17 times the steel value, while aluminium reaches 2.63 times.

承载能力与自重之比 $M_L=F_0/F_G$ 用于评价规定要求下的构件，比值越大，单位自重承担的有效载荷越多。对于长度固定的均匀拉杆，强度约束给出 $M_L=\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}/(\rho gL)$。采用前文三种材料的示例值及相同强度安全系数，钛合金的比值为不锈钢的 3.17 倍，铝合金为 2.63 倍。

If the same bar must limit its axial extension to $\delta_0$, area $A$ is sized from stiffness. Here $F_0$ is the required tensile load, $L$ the bar length, $E$ Young’s modulus, $\rho$ density and $g$ gravitational acceleration. Taking the minimum area satisfying this constraint gives:

同一根拉杆若要求轴向伸长不超过 $\delta_0$，就需要按刚度确定截面积 $A$。设要求承受的拉力为 $F_0$，杆长为 $L$，杨氏模量为 $E$，密度为 $\rho$，重力加速度为 $g$。取满足伸长约束的最小面积，有：

$$\begin{aligned}\delta=\frac{F_0L}{EA}\le\delta_0 &\quad\Longrightarrow\quad A_{\min}=\frac{F_0L}{E\delta_0},\\ F_G=\rho gA_{\min}L=\frac{\rho gF_0L^2}{E\delta_0} &\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \boxed{M_L=\frac{E\delta_0}{\rho gL^2}}. \end{aligned}$$

At equal length and allowable extension, the material term is $E/\rho$. The same example values give 25.00 for steel, 26.91 for aluminium and 22.34 for titanium in GPa·cm³/g. Aluminium now ranks first, but its advantage over steel is much smaller than under the strength constraint. When both strength and extension matter, the larger required area governs:

长度与允许伸长相同时，需要比较的材料项为 $E/\rho$。同一组示例值给出不锈钢 25.00、铝合金 26.91、钛合金 22.34，单位为 GPa·cm³/g。此时铝合金排在前面，但它相对钢的优势远小于强度控制时。若强度与伸长均有要求，应取两项约束中较大的截面积：

$$A_{\min}=\max\!\left(\frac{F_0}{\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}},\frac{F_0L}{E\delta_0}\right),\qquad M_L=\min\!\left(\frac{\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}}{\rho gL},\frac{E\delta_0}{\rho gL^2}\right).$$

If area and length are already fixed and each candidate satisfies the same design load, mass is simply $\rho AL$: the lower-density material is lighter. This is a comparison within the feasible set, rather than a new strength law. The relevant index also depends on deformation mode. An axial tie uses $E/\rho$; a plate sized by bending stiffness through thickness uses $[E/(1-\nu^2)]^{1/3}/\rho$, where $\nu$ is Poisson’s ratio.

若截面积和长度已经固定，且候选材料均能满足同一设计载荷，质量就是 $\rho AL$，密度较小的方案较轻。这是在满足要求的方案之间比较质量。选材指标还取决于变形方式：轴向拉杆采用 $E/\rho$，通过厚度满足弯曲刚度的板采用 $[E/(1-\nu^2)]^{1/3}/\rho$，其中 $\nu$ 为泊松比。

A wing skin is governed by stiffness. Should steel or aluminium be selected, and why?

机翼蒙皮由刚度要求控制，应选择钢还是铝？为什么？

The loading and adjustable dimension complete the problem statement. If equal in-plane extensional stiffness is required and thickness may vary, $Et$ is fixed and areal mass $\rho t$ is proportional to $\rho/E$: the example aluminium is slightly lighter. For a bending panel, $D=Et^3/[12(1-\nu^2)]$ fixes a different thickness and leads to the plate index above. These models explain why “stiffness controlled” alone does not specify the calculation.

这道题还需要明确载荷形式与允许调整的尺寸。若要求相同面内拉伸刚度、厚度 $t$ 可变，则 $Et$ 固定，单位面积质量 $\rho t$ 与 $\rho/E$ 成正比，示例铝合金略轻。若蒙皮按弯曲板计算，则由 $D=Et^3/[12(1-\nu^2)]$ 确定厚度，得到上面的板材指数。“刚度控制”需要落实到具体变形模型，才能完成比较。

Energy absorption per unit mass吸能与比吸能

An energy absorber deforms through a usable stroke while limiting the force transmitted to the protected structure. Let $F(\delta)$ be its resisting force and $\delta_f$ the end of the evaluated stroke. The work put into the component is the area under its force–displacement curve. Dividing that work by component mass $m$ gives specific energy absorption, abbreviated SEA here:

吸能构件通过一段可用行程发生变形，同时限制传给被保护结构的力。设变形过程中的阻力为 $F(\delta)$，评价行程的终点为 $\delta_f$，输入构件的功等于力—位移曲线下的面积。将这部分功除以构件质量 $m$，得到比吸能，以下记为 SEA：

$$\Pi(\delta_f)=\int_0^{\delta_f}F(\delta)\,\mathrm d\delta,\qquad \boxed{\mathrm{SEA}(\delta_f)=\frac{\Pi(\delta_f)}m}\quad[\mathrm{J/kg}].$$

For a uniform bar, use engineering stress $\sigma=F/A_0$ and strain $\varepsilon=\delta/L_0$, based on initial area $A_0$ and length $L_0$. With reference volume $V_0=A_0L_0$, mass $m=\rho_0V_0$ and terminal strain $\varepsilon_f=\delta_f/L_0$, substitution gives:

对于均匀变形的拉杆，取基于初始截面积 $A_0$ 和初始长度 $L_0$ 的工程应力 $\sigma=F/A_0$、工程应变 $\varepsilon=\delta/L_0$。初始体积为 $V_0=A_0L_0$，质量为 $m=\rho_0V_0$，终止应变为 $\varepsilon_f=\delta_f/L_0$。代入可得：

$$\Pi=V_0\int_0^{\varepsilon_f}\sigma(\varepsilon)\,\mathrm d\varepsilon,\qquad w=\frac{\Pi}{V_0},\qquad \mathrm{SEA}=\frac1{\rho_0}\int_0^{\varepsilon_f}\sigma(\varepsilon)\,\mathrm d\varepsilon.$$

The stress–strain area is work per reference volume $w$, measured in J/m³, equivalent to Pa. Density converts it to J/kg. Under elastic unloading, some or all of the input work is returned. The loading and unloading curves therefore determine how much is stored and how much is dissipated, as explained in MIT’s stress–strain notes.

应力—应变曲线下的面积是单位初始体积上的功 $w$，单位为 J/m³，也就是 Pa；再除以密度才得到 J/kg。弹性卸载会返还部分或全部输入功，因此储存了多少能量、耗散了多少能量，需要结合加载与卸载曲线判断。MIT 应力—应变讲义给出了这一面积关系。

Elastic storage and plastic dissipation弹性储能与塑性耗能

For linear-elastic tension, $\sigma=E\varepsilon$ and the area is triangular. The bar stores $\Pi=F\delta/2$; all of this energy is recoverable under ideal elastic unloading. At a given final stress:

线弹性拉伸满足 $\sigma=E\varepsilon$，曲线下面积为三角形。杆件储存的能量为 $\Pi=F\delta/2$，理想弹性卸载可将其全部返还。加载至应力 $\sigma$ 时：

$$\Pi_{\mathrm e}=\frac12F\delta=\frac{\sigma^2}{2E}V_0,\qquad \mathrm{SEA}_{\mathrm e}=\frac{\sigma^2}{2E\rho_0}.$$

An ideal elastic–perfectly-plastic bar follows this elastic line up to yield stress $\sigma_y$ and yield strain $\varepsilon_y=\sigma_y/E$, then deforms at constant stress. For total terminal strain $\varepsilon_f\ge\varepsilon_y$, the loading work contains the elastic triangle and the plastic plateau:

理想弹性—完全塑性拉杆先沿弹性直线加载，到达屈服应力 $\sigma_y$、屈服应变 $\varepsilon_y=\sigma_y/E$ 后，在恒定应力下继续变形。若终点总应变 $\varepsilon_f\ge\varepsilon_y$，加载功由弹性三角形与塑性平台面积组成：

$$\begin{aligned} w_{\mathrm{load}}&=\frac12\sigma_y\varepsilon_y+\sigma_y(\varepsilon_f-\varepsilon_y) =\sigma_y\varepsilon_f-\frac{\sigma_y^2}{2E},\\ w_{\mathrm{return}}&=\frac{\sigma_y^2}{2E},\qquad w_{\mathrm{plastic}}=\sigma_y\varepsilon_p,\qquad \varepsilon_p=\varepsilon_f-\varepsilon_y,\\ w_{\mathrm{load}}&=w_{\mathrm{return}}+w_{\mathrm{plastic}}. \end{aligned}$$

Here $\varepsilon_p$ is the permanent strain remaining after unloading. Dividing each energy density by $\rho_0$ gives the corresponding specific energy. If plastic strain greatly exceeds elastic strain, neglecting the small elastic interval gives the rigid-perfectly-plastic approximation $\mathrm{SEA}\approx\sigma_y\varepsilon_f/\rho_0$. This product shows the roles of stress level, usable strain and density.

其中 $\varepsilon_p$ 是卸载后保留的塑性应变。将各能量密度除以 $\rho_0$，即可得到相应的单位质量能量。当塑性应变远大于弹性应变时，可以忽略短小的弹性区间，采用理想刚塑性近似 $\mathrm{SEA}\approx\sigma_y\varepsilon_f/\rho_0$。这个乘积同时体现了承载应力、可用变形和密度的作用。

Aluminium energy-absorption example铝合金比吸能例题

An aluminium alloy has $R_{p0.2}=300$ MPa, terminal strain $\varepsilon_f=0.12$ and density $\rho=2.7$ g/cm³. Estimate its ideal specific energy absorption in kJ/kg.

某铝合金的 $R_{p0.2}=300$ MPa，终止应变 $\varepsilon_f=0.12$，密度 $\rho=2.7$ g/cm³。求其理想比吸能，单位为 kJ/kg。

Use the stated proof stress as the constant stress in the ideal plastic approximation. Convert density to 2700 kg/m³ and stress to $300\times10^6$ Pa. The stress–strain area is $36\times10^6$ J/m³, so:

在理想塑性近似中，将给定的规定塑性延伸强度作为恒定应力。先把密度换算为 2700 kg/m³、应力换算为 $300\times10^6$ Pa，应力—应变面积为 $36\times10^6$ J/m³，于是：

$$\mathrm{SEA}\approx\frac{R_{p0.2}\varepsilon_f}{\rho} =\frac{300\times10^6\times0.12}{2700} =13\,333\ \mathrm{J/kg} =\boxed{13.33\ \mathrm{kJ/kg}}.$$

This is the uniform-material estimate for the stated ideal model. In a thin-walled crash box or honeycomb, folding and local collapse determine the measured force–displacement curve. Evaluate that component with $\Pi=\int F\,d\delta$ and its actual mass. At a fixed usable stroke, the mean crushing force is $\overline F=\Pi/\delta_f$; the peak force must also fit the protected structure’s load capacity. Material strength and ductility work together with the collapse geometry.

这一结果对应题目中的均匀材料理想模型。薄壁吸能盒或蜂窝的折叠与局部塌缩决定实际力—位移曲线，应以 $\Pi=\int F\,d\delta$ 和构件实际质量计算比吸能。在给定可用行程内，平均压溃力为 $\overline F=\Pi/\delta_f$，峰值力也需要与被保护结构的承载能力匹配。材料的强度、延性与结构的塌缩形式共同决定吸能表现。
