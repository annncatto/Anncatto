Actuation and Transmission驱动与传动

Actuator choice changes the mass carried by every upstream joint and the inertia felt through the transmission. Motor placement, reduction, force capacity, heat rejection and structural stiffness therefore belong in one design loop. A transmission can move a heavy motor to a favorable location while adding mass, friction and elastic modes along the limb.

执行器选择同时改变上游关节搬运的质量，以及经过传动后感受到的惯量。电机位置、减速比、输出能力、散热与结构刚度，需要进入同一轮设计。传动可以将重电机移到有利位置，也会沿肢体增加质量、摩擦和弹性模态。

Rotors and Reduction转子与减速比

The stator carries the windings, while the rotor turns in the magnetic field. With an external rotor, rotating material sits at a larger radius than in many internal-rotor arrangements. For a simplified annular rotor, $J_m=m_m(r_o^2+r_i^2)/2$. Torque capacity also benefits from air-gap radius, so rotor topology should be compared using actual torque, mass and inertia data rather than a categorical label.

定子承载绕组，转子在磁场中转动。外转子结构的旋转材料通常分布在比内转子更大的半径处。将转子简化为圆环，有 $J_m=m_m(r_o^2+r_i^2)/2$；气隙半径也有利于输出转矩，因此应比较实际的转矩、质量与惯量数据，不能仅凭内外转子的名称判断优劣。

A planetary stage shares load among several planet gears and can package reduction around the motor axis. Reduction transforms torque and speed. Defining $N=\omega_m/\omega_j>1$, with driving efficiency $\eta$, gives:

行星减速级通过多颗行星轮分担载荷，也便于围绕电机轴紧凑布置。规定 $N=\omega_m/\omega_j>1$，正向传动效率为 $\eta$，力矩与速度变换为：

$$\omega_j=\frac{\omega_m}{N},\qquad\tau_j\approx\eta N\tau_m,\qquad J_{\mathrm{ref}}=N^2J_m.$$

The inertia expression follows from $T_m=J_m(N\dot q)^2/2$. It is an ideal constant-ratio result; real multi-joint transmissions can also introduce coupling terms. A high reduction can make a small rotor dominate the output inertia. It also increases output torque for the same motor torque, so acceleration must be evaluated from the complete torque and inertia model. Modern Robotics, §8.9 derives this effect.

惯量关系来自 $T_m=J_m(N\dot q)^2/2$。这是理想恒定传动比的结果，多关节传动还可能带来耦合项。大减速比可以让小转子主导输出端惯量，同时也会提高给定电机力矩下的输出，因此加速能力需要结合完整力矩与惯量模型判断。Modern Robotics §8.9给出了相应推导。

Backdrivability describes how readily an external load can move the actuator through its transmission. Reflected inertia, Coulomb friction, viscous losses, preload and seals all affect it. Low reduction can improve mechanical transparency, while demanding a larger motor torque and current. Torque estimated from motor current also depends on the motor constant and transmission losses; accurate force control must account for that relationship.

反向驱动性描述外载荷能否容易地通过传动推动执行器。反射惯量、库仑摩擦、黏性损耗、预紧和密封都会影响它。低减速比通常有利于机械透明性，也要求电机提供更大的转矩与电流。由电流估算输出力矩，还依赖转矩常数与传动损耗，精确力控需要处理这一关系。

Quasi-direct drives combine a relatively high-torque motor with a modest reduction. The approach suits dynamic limbs when output speed, low inertia and impact response are valuable. Its continuous capability is constrained by winding temperature and cooling. A series-elastic actuator deliberately inserts a compliant element to measure or shape force; its compliance is part of the control design, with a corresponding effect on position bandwidth.

准直驱将较高转矩电机与较小减速比组合，适合重视输出速度、低惯量和冲击响应的动态肢体，持续能力仍受绕组温度与散热限制。串联弹性执行器则主动加入柔性元件，用来测量或塑造力；这部分柔性属于控制设计，也会影响位置带宽。

Moving the Motor Proximally电机近端布置

Relocating a knee motor toward the hip reduces the mass carried at the knee, but its power still has to reach the knee. The replacement includes pulleys or linkages, shafts, bearings, supports and any extra encoders. Coaxial motor placement describes where axes sit; serial or parallel kinematics describes the mechanism’s constraints. One does not determine the other.

将膝电机移向髋部，可以减少膝部搬运的质量，但动力仍然需要传到膝关节。新增部分包括带轮或连杆、传动轴、轴承、支承，以及可能需要的输出编码器。同轴布置说明轴线的位置，串联或并联运动学说明机构约束，两者分别描述不同的设计选择。

Timing belts transmit motion by tooth engagement. The pulley teeth set the transmission ratio, while rotation remains continuous. For a 14-bit motor encoder and an ideal 2:1 reduction, the output quantization interval is $360^\circ/(2^{14}\cdot2)\approx0.01099^\circ$. A 40-tooth output pulley does not make motion occur in 9° increments.

同步带通过齿形啮合传递运动，带轮齿数确定传动比，转动仍然连续。采用 14 bit 电机编码器和理想 2:1 减速时，输出量化间隔为 $360^\circ/(2^{14}\cdot2)\approx0.01099^\circ$。40 齿输出带轮并不会让关节按 9° 一步运动。

The main belt limitations are load-dependent stretch, tooth deformation, preload requirements, bearing side load, hysteresis and the risk of tooth jumping under unsuitable loading or tension. An output encoder measures the actual joint angle and can improve feedback, while the mechanical compliance remains. Belt width, span, tooth profile and engaged tooth count must be sized with the manufacturer’s data. The timing-belt design handbook explains these mechanisms.

同步带的主要限制是随载荷变化的伸长、带齿变形、预紧需求、轴承侧向载荷、滞后，以及不合适载荷或张紧条件下的跳齿风险。输出编码器测量实际关节角度，有助于反馈，机械弹性仍然存在。带宽、跨距、齿形与啮合齿数，需要结合厂家数据选型。同步带设计手册介绍了这些机制。

A rigid linkage transmits forces through bars and pivots. If joint angle $q$ depends on motor angle $\theta_m$, virtual work gives its instantaneous torque transformation:

刚性连杆通过杆件与铰链传力。若关节角 $q$ 由电机角 $\theta_m$ 决定，根据虚功原理，瞬时转矩变换为：

$$\tau_m\,d\theta_m=\tau_j\,dq,\qquad\tau_j=\tau_m\frac{d\theta_m}{dq}.$$

Geometry can provide high mechanical advantage near a support posture and greater joint speed elsewhere. Near a dead point, the ratio and internal forces can become extreme. Link buckling, pivot clearances, motion range and collisions constrain the usable region. A mechanism’s full angle-dependent Jacobian is more informative than one nominal ratio.

机构几何可以在支撑姿态附近提供较大机械优势，在其他区间提供较高关节速度。接近死点时，传动比与内部载荷可能急剧增大。杆件屈曲、铰链间隙、活动范围与碰撞共同限制可用区间，完整的角度相关雅可比比单个名义传动比更有意义。

Screws and Rockers丝杠与摇臂

A screw converts rotation into translation; a rocker or linkage then converts translation into joint motion. With lead $p$ in metres per revolution, motor torque $\tau_m$ and angular speed $\omega_m$, a simple driving relation is:

丝杠将转动变成直线运动，再由摇臂或连杆变成关节转动。设导程 $p$ 以每转前进的米数表示，电机力矩为 $\tau_m$、角速度为 $\omega_m$，简单正向关系为：

$$F_s\approx\frac{2\pi\eta\tau_m}{p},\qquad v_s=\frac{p\omega_m}{2\pi}.$$

A 5 mm lead, 90% driving efficiency and 1 N·m input give about 1131 N axial force. With an instantaneous perpendicular rocker arm of 30 mm, the joint torque is about 33.9 N·m. Reducing lead increases force at the same torque while reducing linear speed at the same motor speed. The calculation is local to that posture and excludes other losses.

导程 5 mm、正向效率 90%、输入 1 N·m 时，轴向力约为 1131 N；若当前姿态的垂直摇臂力臂为 30 mm，关节力矩约为 33.9 N·m。减小导程，会在同一输入转矩下增加推力，也会在同一电机转速下降低直线速度。这是当前姿态下的局部算例，未包含其他损耗。

Let screw extension be $x(q)$. Its effective lever arm and motor-to-joint ratio are:

令丝杠伸长量为 $x(q)$，其有效力臂与电机到关节的瞬时传动比为：

$$r_{\mathrm{eff}}=\frac{dx}{dq},\qquad N_{\mathrm{eff}}=\frac{2\pi}{p}\frac{dx}{dq},\qquad J_{\mathrm{ref}}(q)\approx N_{\mathrm{eff}}(q)^2J_m.$$

Large mechanical advantage can therefore bring substantial reflected inertia. Ball screws and roller screws also require checks of support bearings, screw critical speed, compressive buckling, lubrication, contact fatigue and shock loads. Efficient rolling screws can be backdriven; their ability to hold a load must be evaluated separately. THK’s ball-screw introduction describes forward and reverse conversion.

较大机械优势也可能带来较大反射惯量。滚珠和滚柱丝杠还需要检查支承轴承、临界转速、受压屈曲、润滑、接触疲劳与冲击载荷。高效率滚动丝杠能够被反向驱动，保持载荷的能力需要单独评估。THK 滚珠丝杠介绍说明了正、反向转换。

LOLA’s mechanical design demonstrates how motor location, linear drives and leg inertia are considered together. Linkage and screw mechanisms can be combined with belts or gears. The useful comparison is the completed leg under its load cycle, including mass, stiffness, speed, losses, maintenance and collision clearance.

LOLA 的机械设计将电机位置、直线驱动与腿部惯量放在一起考虑。连杆和丝杠也可以与同步带、齿轮组合。真正需要比较的是整个载荷循环中的完整腿部，包括质量、刚度、速度、损耗、维护与运动间隙。

A coupled transmission also changes coordinates. If motor coordinates satisfy $\theta=f(q)$, then $\dot\theta=A(q)\dot q$ and joint torques are $\tau_q=A^T\tau_m$. Rotor inertia contributes $A^TJ_mA$, generally a matrix with off-diagonal terms. Routing a drive across a moving hip must account for this coupling when the knee is commanded to remain fixed.

耦合传动还会改变坐标关系。若电机坐标满足 $\theta=f(q)$，则 $\dot\theta=A(q)\dot q$，关节力矩为 $\tau_q=A^T\tau_m$。转子惯量贡献为 $A^TJ_mA$，通常包含非对角项。传动跨过运动中的髋关节时，即使要求膝角保持不变，也需要在控制中处理这种耦合。
