Robot design and functional integration机器人设计与功能集成

Robot lightweight design combines material selection, efficient structure, manufacturing and function sharing. A support may also guide cables or conduct heat; a load-bearing leg member may also store elastic energy. The useful comparison is the complete assembly that performs the required functions: shared geometry saves mass by removing duplicated walls, brackets, housings and connections.

机器人轻量化设计将选材、高效结构、制造与功能共用结合起来。支承件可以同时引导线束或导热，承力腿部构件也可以储存弹性能。比较对象是完成所需功能的完整装配：共用几何通过减少重复壁面、支架、壳体和连接来减重。

Passive integration被动集成

Passive integration combines functions that are provided by material, shape and layout without additional powered actuation or control. A load-bearing leaf spring also stores and returns elastic energy; a hollow tube also guides cables; an aluminium joint housing also conducts motor heat to its surface. Shared geometry reduces separate components and their connecting interfaces.

被动集成通过材料、形状和布置，组合无需额外驱动或控制的功能。承力板簧同时储存并释放弹性能，空心管同时引导线缆，铝制关节壳体同时将电机热量传到表面。共用几何减少独立零件及其连接接口。

Active integration主动集成

Active integration embeds actuators, sensors and electronics in the structure. Its mass savings follow three routes:

主动集成将执行器、传感器和电子器件嵌入结构，通过三条途径减重：

  • Fewer housings: sensors and electronics move into the supporting structure, reducing their separate enclosures.
  • Shorter wiring: a drive beside the motor uses power and communication connections in place of multiple long, heavy motor leads.
  • Lighter structure: sensing and control compensate allowable link flexibility and detect contact, supporting a more compliant structural design.
  • 壳体更少：传感器和电子器件移入承力结构，减少独立封装。
  • 线束更短：驱动器紧邻电机，用电源和通信总线替代多根长而粗的电机线。
  • 结构更轻：感知与控制补偿允许范围内的连杆柔性并检测接触，支持更柔的结构设计。

An integrated joint combines drive electronics, encoder, motor stator and rotor, reduction, output torque sensing, output flange and a load-bearing housing that also spreads heat. A central passage routes wiring through the joint. Thermal management, electromagnetic compatibility and service access are designed together with the load path.

集成关节组合驱动电子、编码器、电机定子与转子、减速机构、输出力矩感知、输出法兰，以及兼具承载与散热功能的壳体；中心通道用于关节内部走线。热管理、电磁兼容和维修空间需要与传力路径共同设计。

Legged robots腿部机器人

Legs repeatedly accelerate and decelerate during swing, then transmit ground reaction forces during stance. Distal mass therefore affects swing torque, contact response and the upstream actuator sizes. Functional design concentrates on the combination of mass placement, compliant load paths and integrated leg joints.

腿部在摆动期反复加减速，在支撑期传递地面反力。远端质量同时影响摆腿力矩、触地响应与上游执行器尺寸，因此功能设计需要结合质量布置、弹性传力路径和腿部关节集成。

MIT Cheetah: tendon–bone load sharingMIT Cheetah：腱—骨协同承载

The early MIT Cheetah leg uses a tension member alongside a compression-bearing member. The tendon changes the foot-to-leg load path and reduces bending in the bone-like structure. The rigid member can then carry a larger share of load axially, allowing its bending-driven section to be reduced. This is structural design plus passive integration (D + P). The original leg-design paper develops this tendon–bone co-location principle.

早期 MIT Cheetah 腿部将受拉的腱与承压的骨状构件协同布置。腱改变足端向腿部传力的路径，减小骨状构件承受的弯矩，使刚性构件更多承担轴向压力，从而缩减原先由弯曲控制的截面。这对应结构设计与被动集成（D＋P），具体原理见腱—骨协同布置的原始论文。

For a section carrying normal force $N$ and bending moment $M$, the outer-fibre stress contains $N/A+Mc/I$. Reducing $M$ attacks the bending term directly. Tendon anchors, pretension and load reversal must be designed so that the intended tension path remains effective; the compressed member still requires a buckling check.

对于同时承受轴力 $N$ 与弯矩 $M$ 的截面，外缘应力包含 $N/A+Mc/I$，减小 $M$ 就直接削减弯曲项。腱的锚固、预紧与反向载荷需要保证受拉路径有效，承压构件仍需校核屈曲。

Mini Cheetah: proximal actuation and low reductionMini Cheetah：近端驱动与低减速比

Mini Cheetah uses the same motor-module design 12 times. Each module combines a large-diameter motor, a 6:1 planetary reduction, position sensing and an onboard drive. The knee actuator is placed near the hip and transmits motion through a belt, reducing the mass that swings at the knee radius. The common module integrates the mechanical and electrical package, while proximal placement improves mass distribution. MIT’s module description identifies these components. The main mechanisms are structural layout and active integration (D + A).

Mini Cheetah 将同一种电机模组重复使用 12 次，每个模组组合大直径电机、6∶1 行星减速器、位置感知与板载驱动器。膝执行器布置在髋部附近，再通过同步带传递运动，减少随膝部半径摆动的质量。共用模组实现机电封装集成，近端布置则改善质量分布。MIT 的模组说明列出了这些组成。其主要机制是结构布置与主动集成（D＋A）。

With motor inertia $J_m$, the joint-side reflected inertia is $N^2J_m$: the 6:1 reduction contributes $36J_m$. Lower reflected inertia and transmission friction improve backdrivability and permit motor current to inform a contact-force estimate. In a simple model, $\boldsymbol\tau_{ext}=J^T\mathbf F_{contact}$; estimating contact force also requires the motion model, gravity and friction compensation. The design therefore couples a light moving leg with a low-impedance drive, supporting rapid swing and impact response.

转子惯量为 $J_m$ 时，折算到关节侧的反射惯量为 $N^2J_m$，6∶1 减速比对应 $36J_m$。较低的反射惯量与传动摩擦提高反向驱动性，也支持利用电机电流估计接触力。简化关系为 $\boldsymbol\tau_{ext}=J^T\mathbf F_{contact}$，实际估计还需结合运动模型，补偿重力与摩擦。轻质运动腿与低阻抗驱动共同服务于快速摆腿和触地响应。

Consider a fully extended two-link leg: both links are 0.30 m long, the thigh is 0.5 kg, the shank is 0.3 kg and the knee motor is 0.6 kg. Approximate each link by a point mass at its midpoint and compare hip inertia before and after moving the motor from the knee to the hip.

考虑完全伸直的二连杆腿：两段连杆均长 0.30 m，大腿质量 0.5 kg，小腿质量 0.3 kg，膝电机质量 0.6 kg。将连杆近似为位于各自中点的点质量，比较电机从膝部移到髋部前后的髋轴惯量。

{{course:leglayout}}
$$\begin{aligned}I_0&=0.5(0.15)^2+0.3(0.45)^2+0.6(0.30)^2=0.126\ \mathrm{kg\,m^2},\\ I_1&=0.5(0.15)^2+0.3(0.45)^2+0.6(0)^2=0.072\ \mathrm{kg\,m^2},\\\Delta I&=0.054\ \mathrm{kg\,m^2},\qquad\frac{\Delta I}{I_0}=42.9\%.\end{aligned}$$

The total mass is unchanged in this point-mass model; relocating the motor removes 0.054 kg·m², or 42.9%, of the initial hip inertia. An implemented remote drive must also include the added transmission mass and inertia.

该点质量模型的总质量保持不变，电机移位使髋轴惯量降低 0.054 kg·m²，约为初始值的 42.9%；实际远置驱动还应计入新增传动质量与惯量。

ANYmal: series elastic actuationANYmal：串联弹性驱动

{{course:seapath}}

A series elastic actuator (SEA) places an elastic element between the drive and load. Measuring spring deflection gives a force or torque estimate. With calibrated linear stiffness $k$ or torsional stiffness $k_\theta$, the relations are:

串联弹性执行器（SEA）在驱动与负载之间串入弹性元件，测量弹簧变形即可估计力或力矩。线性刚度 $k$ 或扭转刚度 $k_\theta$ 标定后，有：

$$F=k\Delta x,\qquad \tau=k_\theta\Delta\theta,\qquad E_s=\frac12k_\theta(\Delta\theta)^2.$$

Elastic travel separates short-duration contact motion from motor motion and makes force measurement possible through deformation. It also introduces a resonance: for a simplified load inertia $J_L$ against a fixed drive side, $\omega_n=\sqrt{k_\theta/J_L}$. Lower spring stiffness increases deflection sensitivity but reduces this frequency. ETH’s ANYmal leg actuators use series elasticity, while the lab also develops quasi-direct drives for other joints. SEA and low-ratio rigid transmission solve different combinations of sensing, impact and bandwidth requirements.

弹性行程使短时接触运动与电机运动分离，并通过变形实现测力；它同时引入共振。将驱动侧视为固定、负载等效为惯量 $J_L$ 时，简化固有频率为 $\omega_n=\sqrt{k_\theta/J_L}$。弹簧越软，变形测量灵敏度越高，这一频率也越低。ETH 的 ANYmal 腿部执行器采用串联弹性，实验室也发展用于其他关节的准直驱。两类驱动分别权衡测力、冲击和带宽要求。

ANYdrive packages the motor, reduction, elastic torque measurement and control electronics into a sealed unit. This reduces separate sensor mounts and external wiring while retaining a replaceable actuator module. Its benefit differs from Mini Cheetah’s low-ratio drive: the elastic element supplies measurable deflection and contact compliance; its finite stiffness also limits load-side position bandwidth.

ANYdrive 将电机、减速、弹性测力与控制电子器件封装为密封单元，减少独立传感器支架与外部线束，同时保留可更换的执行器模组。它与 Mini Cheetah 低减速比驱动的侧重不同：弹性元件提供可测变形和接触柔度，有限刚度也约束负载侧位置带宽。

Cassie: load-bearing leaf springsCassie：承力板簧与弹性储能

Cassie combines linkage geometry with compliant leg elements. Its shin and heel springs participate in the leg’s load path and provide passive joint motion, so one structural assembly also supplies spring behaviour. Each leg has five actuated and two passive joints; the standing and walking model distinguishes these joints and their springs. Actuation is distributed along the leg, with distal mass reduced through linkage and actuator layout.

Cassie 将连杆几何与腿部弹性元件结合，胫部与跟部弹簧参与承载路径并提供被动关节运动，使一套结构同时具有弹簧功能。每条腿具有五个驱动关节和两个被动关节，站立与行走模型给出了这些关节与弹簧。驱动沿腿部分布，连杆和执行器布局共同减少远端运动质量。

{{course:legspring}}

During leg compression, spring energy increases by $E_s=\tfrac12 kx^2$ in a linear equivalent model. During unloading, part of that energy returns to body motion. Greater elastic travel can spread the deceleration over a longer displacement and reduce the drive’s instantaneous demand. Joint torques and gearbox loads still depend on the transmission, damping and gait. The spring–mass model used in Cassie’s control design connects this mechanical compliance to the desired body dynamics.

腿部压缩时，在线性等效模型中弹性能按 $E_s=\tfrac12 kx^2$ 增加；卸载时，部分能量返回躯干运动。较大的弹性行程可以延长减速距离，减小驱动的瞬时需求；关节力矩和减速器载荷仍取决于传动、阻尼与步态。Cassie 控制设计所使用的弹簧—质量模型，将这种机械柔度与期望躯干动力学联系起来。

The lightweight contribution is the shared structural and elastic function (D + P). Spring stiffness, linkage geometry and gait timing are selected together: excessively stiff legs provide little compression travel, while excessively soft legs can exceed travel limits or lower the usable response frequency. Energy return, spring fatigue and contact stability are therefore evaluated in the same gait.

其轻量化机制是承载与弹性功能共用（D＋P）。弹簧刚度、连杆几何和步态时序需要共同确定：过硬的腿压缩行程小，过软的腿则可能超过行程或降低可用响应频率，因此储能回收、弹簧疲劳与触地稳定性要在同一步态下评价。

Locomotion energy and COT行走能耗与 COT

Leg design is evaluated over a gait cycle, including swing, stance and landing. Cost of transport (COT) measures energy per unit weight and travel distance:

腿部设计应在完整步态周期中评价，包含摆动、支撑与落地。运输成本（COT）表示单位重量、单位行走距离消耗的能量：

$$\mathrm{COT}=\frac{E}{mgd}=\frac{\bar P}{mg\bar v}.$$

$E$ is energy consumed over distance $d$, $m$ robot mass and $g$ gravitational acceleration. Use the same energy boundary—electrical input or mechanical output—when comparing. At fixed speed, reducing power by 15% and mass by 10% gives $\mathrm{COT}_{new}/\mathrm{COT}_{old}=0.85/0.90=0.944$, a 5.6% improvement. Reducing mass alone, with unchanged power, increases COT because its denominator decreases. Low distal inertia is valuable when it also reduces the work and electrical losses required by the gait.

$E$ 是行走距离 $d$ 消耗的能量，$m$ 为机器人质量，$g$ 为重力加速度。比较时应统一能量边界，例如电气输入或机械输出。在速度相同的条件下，功率降低 15%、质量降低 10%，则 $\mathrm{COT}_{new}/\mathrm{COT}_{old}=0.85/0.90=0.944$，改善约 5.6%。如果只减质量而功率不变，COT 会因分母减小而升高。降低远端惯量的价值在于同时减少步态所需的做功和电气损耗。

Manipulators机械臂

A serial arm carries its distal joints, links and tool through every upstream motion. Functional design therefore combines lightweight links with compact joints, internal routing and selected remote actuation. Payload-to-arm-mass ratio evaluates the assembled result, while trajectory torque, stiffness and thermal duty determine how much of that payload is usable.

串联机械臂的远端关节、连杆和工具都由上游关节带动，因此功能设计需要结合轻质连杆、紧凑关节、内部走线与适当的远置驱动。负载自重比评价装配结果，轨迹力矩、刚度与热工作制则决定可用负载。

DLR LWR III: lightweight links and integrated jointsDLR LWR III：轻质连杆与集成关节

DLR LWR III combines load-oriented CFRP link shells with seven integrated joints. Its published specification gives a 14 kg arm mass and a 14 kg maximum payload, producing a payload-to-mass ratio of 1.00. The structural and electromechanical choices can be separated into five contributions:

DLR LWR III 将按载荷布置纤维的 CFRP 连杆外骨骼与七个集成关节结合。官方规格给出臂体质量 14 kg、最大负载 14 kg，负载自重比为 1.00。其结构与机电设计可以分为五项具体作用：

  • M · CFRP carries the link loads with low structural mass.
  • D · Free-form shells and motor sizes matched to proximal and distal torque demand avoid uniform oversizing.
  • F · Fibre preforms are sewn onto a carrier, impregnated with epoxy and pressed to remove excess resin.
  • P · The load-bearing shell also forms the link enclosure; internal passages carry supply lines.
  • A · Motors, gears, brakes, sensing and power electronics share joint packaging.
  • M · CFRP 承担连杆载荷，降低运动结构质量。
  • D · 自由曲面壳体与按近、远端力矩需求配置的电机尺寸，减少全臂统一放大造成的冗余。
  • F · 将纤维预成形体缝在载体上，浸渍环氧后压制排出多余树脂，兼顾铺层方向与含胶量。
  • P · 承力外骨骼同时形成连杆外壳，内部通道容纳供给线。
  • A · 电机、减速器、制动器、感知与功率电子器件共用关节封装。

The mechatronic design places an output torque sensor between the Harmonic Drive gear and link structure, and includes motor-side and output-side position sensors. Three motor diameters—85, 70 and 50 mm—follow the decreasing torque demand along the arm. Local power conversion and internal communication remove bulky external arm cabling. Torque feedback supports compliant interaction, while the CFRP shells preserve structural stiffness with lower moving mass.

其机电设计将输出力矩传感器布置在谐波减速器与连杆结构之间，并设置电机侧与输出侧位置传感器。85、70、50 mm 三种电机直径对应沿机械臂逐渐降低的力矩需求；局部功率变换与内部通信减少臂外粗重线缆。力矩反馈支持柔顺交互，CFRP 壳体则以较低运动质量维持结构刚度。

Arm mass-layout example机械臂质量分布例题

A horizontal 0.40 m forearm has mass 3 kg and its centre of mass at 0.20 m. A 1 kg wrist actuator and a 2 kg payload are at 0.40 m. Replace the forearm with a 1.5 kg CFRP member having the same centre-of-mass position, and move the actuator to 0.10 m from the elbow. Find the change in elbow gravity torque. Use $g=9.81$ m/s².

水平前臂长 0.40 m、质量 3 kg，重心距肘轴 0.20 m；1 kg 腕部执行器和 2 kg 负载均位于 0.40 m 处。将前臂换为重心位置相同的 1.5 kg CFRP 构件，并将执行器移至距肘轴 0.10 m 处。取 $g=9.81$ m/s²，求肘关节重力矩变化。

$$\begin{aligned}\tau_{g,0}&=9.81(3\times0.20+1\times0.40+2\times0.40)=17.658\ \mathrm{N\,m},\\\tau_{g,1}&=9.81(1.5\times0.20+1\times0.10+2\times0.40)=11.772\ \mathrm{N\,m},\\\frac{\tau_{g,0}-\tau_{g,1}}{\tau_{g,0}}&=33.3\%.\end{aligned}$$

Separating the contributions clarifies the mechanism. Forearm lightening removes $1.5\times0.20\times9.81=2.943$ N·m; actuator relocation removes $1\times(0.40-0.10)\times9.81=2.943$ N·m. The payload remains unchanged. Added remote-transmission mass $m_t$ at distance $r_t$ would add $m_tgr_t$ to the redesigned torque.

将贡献分开，可以看清两种设计的作用。前臂减重降低 $1.5\times0.20\times9.81=2.943$ N·m，执行器移位降低 $1\times(0.40-0.10)\times9.81=2.943$ N·m，负载项保持不变。如果远置传动新增质量 $m_t$，重心距肘轴 $r_t$，新方案还应增加 $m_tgr_t$。

For a uniformly distributed forearm and point-mass actuator and payload, the elbow inertia changes from $3L^2/3+1(0.40)^2+2(0.40)^2=0.640$ kg·m² to $1.5L^2/3+1(0.10)^2+2(0.40)^2=0.410$ kg·m², a 35.9% reduction. The static and acceleration terms therefore improve through different lever-arm laws, $mr$ and $mr^2$. Motor, brake and reducer resizing follows the complete trajectory torque and thermal duty.

进一步将前臂近似为均匀杆，执行器和负载近似为点质量，肘轴惯量从 $3L^2/3+1(0.40)^2+2(0.40)^2=0.640$ kg·m² 降为 $1.5L^2/3+1(0.10)^2+2(0.40)^2=0.410$ kg·m²，降低 35.9%。重力项与加速项分别按 $mr$ 和 $mr^2$ 改变。电机、制动器与减速器的重新选型，应依据完整轨迹力矩和热工作制。

If the reduced elbow demand permits a smaller drive after stiffness and thermal checks, the shoulder also carries less mass; its torque demand can then fall in turn. This is the next step of the mass spiral. The redesign is repeated upstream with actual actuator and transmission masses until the complete arm, rather than the isolated forearm, meets the target.

如果刚度与热校核后，较低肘部需求允许采用更小驱动，肩部携带的质量也会下降，肩部力矩需求便可能继续降低，这构成质量螺旋的下一步。应将实际执行器与传动质量逐级代回，直到完整机械臂满足目标。

$T_c$ is the work-cycle duration. With a fixed motor torque constant and transmission, RMS torque determines RMS current and hence resistive heating. A lighter distal link reduces acceleration torque, while moving its centre of mass also changes gravity torque. Re-evaluating the entire cycle establishes whether the motor can be downsized without exceeding continuous temperature or transient current limits.

$T_c$ 为工作循环周期。在电机力矩常数与传动关系固定时，均方根力矩决定均方根电流，进而影响电阻发热。远端连杆减重降低加速力矩，重心移位还会改变重力矩。重新计算完整循环后，才能判断较小电机是否同时满足持续温升和瞬时电流要求。

Design trade-offs设计权衡

  • Stiffness and precision: reducing section size without compensating its stiffness increases deformation and can lower natural frequencies.
  • Cost and process: composites and additive manufacture can increase unit cost and require suitable joining and inspection.
  • Maintenance and modification: damage may require replacement of the complete integrated part; later modifications are harder.
  • Explicit trade-offs: specify which requirement is relaxed, by how much, and how control compensates it.
  • 刚度与精度：减小截面而未补偿刚度时，变形增大，固有频率可能降低。
  • 成本与工艺：复合材料和增材制造可能提高单件成本，并需要相应的连接与检测方法。
  • 维修与修改：集成件损坏可能需要整体更换，后期修改更困难。
  • 说明权衡：明确放宽哪项要求、放宽多少，以及控制如何补偿。

Choose shared parts where functions use compatible geometry and material. A stiff bearing seat and a compliant force-sensing flexure often require separate regions in the same assembly. Heat dissipation must preserve sensor accuracy and lubricant temperature. Inspection access and replaceable wear parts preserve serviceability. These requirements become explicit load, deformation and stress checks in the mechanics model.

当功能对几何和材料的要求相容时，共用零件才有效。刚性的轴承座与柔性的测力弹性体通常需要在同一装配内划分不同区域；散热设计需要维持传感精度和润滑温度，检测通道与可更换易损件则维持维修性。这些要求最终转化为力学模型中的载荷、变形和应力校核。
