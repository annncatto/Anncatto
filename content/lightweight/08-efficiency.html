Thin-walled sections: use the available depth薄壁截面：利用可用高度

Bending is resisted by tension on one side and compression on the other. Their separation supplies a moment arm. Tubes, box sections, shells and sandwich plates place more material away from the neutral axis, producing a larger second moment with the same cross-sectional area. Section depth is therefore a design resource: a deep hollow member may be lighter than a shallow solid one at the same deflection limit.

弯曲由一侧拉伸、另一侧压缩共同抵抗，两者的间距提供力臂。管、箱形截面、壳体和夹层板将更多材料放在远离中性轴的位置，在相同截面积下得到较大的二次矩。因此，可用高度是一项设计资源：同样挠度限制下，较深的空心构件可能比浅实心构件更轻。

A box section closes the shear-flow path and can resist torsion efficiently. An open I-section concentrates material in flanges for a preferred bending plane, but its thin walls can twist easily. A shell distributes load over a surface, with curvature and edge restraint influencing stability. Choosing the section starts from bending directions, torque, envelope and how loads enter its ends.

箱形截面闭合剪流路径，可较高效地抗扭；工字截面将材料集中到翼缘，适合一个主要弯曲平面，但薄壁开口截面容易扭转。壳体沿曲面分散载荷，曲率与边界约束又影响稳定性。截面选择需要同时考虑弯曲方向、扭矩、空间和端部载荷引入。

In a sandwich, faces carry most of the bending normal stress and the core transfers transverse shear and stabilizes the faces. Increasing separation helps only while the core and interfaces can transfer these loads. Face wrinkling, core shear, indentation and debonding are separate checks; a large nominal bending rigidity can coexist with a weak local attachment.

夹层面板承担大部分弯曲正应力，芯材传递横向剪力并稳定面板。增加间距的收益依赖芯材和界面能够传递这些载荷；面板起皱、芯材剪切、局部压陷与脱粘需要分别检查，较大的名义抗弯刚度也可能伴随薄弱的局部连接。

Thin-wall efficiency ends where local instability or manufacturing becomes dominant. Shortening an unsupported panel with a well-connected stiffener can improve its buckling resistance; thickening a whole shell may waste material away from the critical region. The stiffener’s own mass, attachment and load path remain part of that comparison.

薄壁效率最终会受到局部失稳与制造限制。连接合理的加强筋可缩短无支承板格、提高抗屈曲能力；整体加厚壳体则可能在非危险区域增加较多材料。加强筋自身质量、连接与传力仍需共同计入。

Direct load paths: reduce avoidable bending直接传力路径：减少附加弯曲

Trace each load from its application point through the structure to a support. An eccentricity e creates an additional moment Fe, requiring a bending couple and often more section depth or thickness. Aligning the reaction with the applied load can replace part of that bending demand with axial force. A short geometric path is useful only if it also transmits the required directions and moments.

从加载点沿结构追踪载荷，直到支承。偏置 $e$ 会增加弯矩 $Fe$，需要额外拉压偶和相应截面高度或壁厚；使反力与载荷对准，可以将部分弯曲需求转为轴力。几何路径较短还需同时满足所需方向和力矩的传递。

An ideal pin-jointed truss carries axial force in its members. Triangulation removes a rectangular frame’s shear mechanism and directs loads into tension and compression members. Actual joints have finite size and stiffness; off-node loads or rigid joints introduce bending. Compression members also need buckling checks. MIT: Truss Design and Convex Optimization.

理想铰接桁架的杆件主要承受轴力。三角化约束矩形框架的剪切机构运动，使载荷沿拉杆与压杆传递；实际接头具有尺寸和刚度，节点外加载或刚性连接还会引入弯曲，受压杆也需要校核屈曲。MIT：桁架设计与凸优化。

A bracket demonstrates why supports matter. Moving bolt rows apart increases the lever arm resisting an overturning moment; placing a rib between the load and those rows can transfer force more directly. A rib that stops before the mounting interface instead loads a thin wall locally. Follow the path through bolts, contact surfaces and the supporting body, including their flexibility.

支架可以说明支承的重要性。拉开螺栓排间距，可增大抵抗倾覆力矩的力臂；在载荷与螺栓排之间接入筋条，可以更直接地传力。筋条若在安装接口前终止，反而可能将力集中到薄壁。传力路径需要继续经过螺栓、接触面和支承本体，并计入它们的柔性。

Multiple load cases can compete: an efficient diagonal for one force may obstruct a mechanism or become ineffective for the opposite direction. Space-frame and shell layouts distribute paths for the complete load set. Joint motion, tool access and assembly order constrain which of those paths can actually be built.

多工况之间可能相互竞争：对一个方向高效的斜撑，可能妨碍机构运动，或在反向载荷下失效。空间桁架与壳体要为完整载荷集合安排路径，关节运动、刀具空间与装配顺序则限制哪些路径能够实现。

Fully stressed design and material utilization满应力设计与材料利用

For a stress-limited axial member, reducing excess area raises its stress toward the allowable value. Applying that idea across a structure leads to fully stressed design: material is placed where the governing load cases require it, instead of keeping a uniform section throughout. A cantilever’s bending moment falls toward the free end, so a deeper root and lighter outer region can use material more efficiently.

轴向杆由强度控制时，减少多余面积会使应力接近许用值。将这一思路用于整个结构，形成满应力设计：按主导工况需求分配材料，而不沿全长保留相同截面。悬臂梁弯矩向自由端降低，根部加深、远端减轻，可更充分地利用材料。

Low stress alone is insufficient grounds for removal. Material near the neutral axis may transmit shear, restrain buckling, maintain torsional closure or spread a concentrated joint load. A region may also be lightly loaded in one case but critical in another. Utilization must be evaluated against the constraint that actually controls each region.

低应力本身不足以决定去除材料。中性轴附近材料可能传递剪力、抑制屈曲、维持闭口抗扭或扩散接头集中力；一个工况中载荷较低的区域，也可能在另一工况中关键。材料利用率需要相对各区域真正的主导约束评价。

Displacement-limited structures may need sections whose stresses remain well below failure. Removing material changes stiffness and can redistribute internal force in redundant structures, so simply making every element reach the same stress need not minimize mass. NASA’s fully utilized design study explicitly adds displacement constraints to the stress-based approach.

位移受限结构可能需要让应力保持在失效极限以下。去除材料会改变刚度，并在超静定结构中重新分配内力，因此让各单元达到相同应力，未必得到最小质量。NASA 的充分利用设计研究将位移约束纳入了应力设计方法。

Topology optimization extends material redistribution to the connectivity itself. Its result can reveal efficient ribs, flanges and diagonal paths, but the final component still needs continuous connections, practical radii, minimum walls and all relevant strength, stiffness and stability checks.

拓扑优化进一步允许改变材料的连接关系，可揭示高效筋条、翼缘与斜向路径。最终构件仍需要连续连接、合理圆角和最小壁厚，并完成相关强度、刚度与稳定性校核。
