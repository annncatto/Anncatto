Functional Integration功能集成

Component optimization asks how to make each required part lighter. Functional integration asks whether separate parts are needed at all. If the same structure can support a load, provide protection, guide a service line or spread heat, several supports and interfaces may disappear. The comparison includes the complete integrated assembly and the functions it replaces.

零件优化研究怎样让必要零件更轻，功能集成则研究是否仍需要这些独立零件。同一结构如果能够承载、防护、引导管线或散热，就可能省去几套支架与接口。比较需要覆盖完整集成装配体，以及它替代的全部功能。

Passive Integration被动功能集成

Passive integration lets one geometry serve several mechanical or physical functions. A sealed hollow link can carry load and route cables; a motor housing can be a structural bearing carrier and a heat spreader. A compliant member can both guide motion and store elastic energy. Each example eliminates an interface but introduces coupling: a hot bearing housing can shift its fits, and a load-bearing cable duct must retain adequate section around openings.

被动功能集成让一种几何承担多种机械或物理功能。密封空心连杆可以承载并走线，电机壳体可以同时支承轴承与导热，柔性杆件可以同时导向与储能。省去接口的同时，也会引入耦合：轴承壳体升温会改变配合，承载线槽的开口附近仍需保留足够截面。

Compliance mechanisms use elastic deformation in place of some pin joints. They can reduce backlash, lubrication needs and part count, while shifting the limits toward strain, fatigue and out-of-plane stability. Large rotations require nonlinear geometry. A flexible hinge sized from a small-deflection approximation can reach unexpectedly high local strain over its full travel. Compliant-mechanism design treats deformation as part of the intended motion.

柔顺机构用弹性变形替代部分转动铰链，可以减少回差、润滑需求与零件数量，同时将限制转向应变、疲劳和面外稳定性。大转角需要考虑几何非线性，按小变形近似设计的柔性铰链，在完整行程中可能出现很高的局部应变。柔顺机构设计将变形本身作为预期运动的一部分。

Equal-strength substitution is one way to compare concepts, but it is only the first check. A replacement must also provide the required stiffness, life, damage tolerance and interfaces. Material-oriented construction can outperform a direct substitution because the section, layup and manufacturing route are redesigned together. Integrated composite frames and shells in the course illustrate this concept-level opportunity.

等强度替换是一种比较概念的方法，但只是第一项检查。替代方案还需满足刚度、寿命、损伤容限和接口要求。按材料特点重新构造，可能优于直接替换，因为截面、铺层和制造路线能够一起变化。课件中的复合材料一体框架与壳体，展示了概念阶段的这种机会。

A one-piece shell can remove fasteners and overlapping flanges, while an integrated frame can combine several former load paths. The saved parts and assembly operations create the benefit; the redesigned shell still needs inspectable load introduction, repair access and a practicable manufacturing sequence. Integration can also make a small defect require replacing a larger unit.

整体壳体能够减少紧固件和重叠法兰，集成框架能够合并原先独立的传力路径。省去的零件与装配工序形成收益；新壳体仍然需要可检测的载荷引入、维修空间与可行工艺。集成也可能让一个小缺陷，要求更换更大的单元。

Sensing, Actuation and Adaptive Behavior感知、驱动与自适应

The course extends integration to sensing, actuation and adaptive functions. A load-bearing member can carry embedded sensors, while an actuator or smart material changes its deformation or vibration response. The added electronics, power and interfaces remain part of the mass budget. Functional sharing is useful when the complete system provides the required performance with less supporting structure.

课程进一步将感知、驱动与自适应功能纳入结构。承力构件可以嵌入传感器，执行器或智能材料可以改变其变形和振动响应。新增电子设备、供能与接口，仍需进入质量预算。只有完整系统以更少的配套结构满足要求，功能共用才形成收益。

Vibration treatment can act on excitation, transmission, damping or controlled counterforces. Active vibration control measures the response and applies a force with suitable magnitude and phase. Active noise control uses a secondary acoustic field to reduce sound at selected locations. These mechanisms address different parts of the physical problem: reducing vibration at one structural sensor does not automatically reduce all radiated noise.

减振可以作用于激励、传播、阻尼或主动抵消力。主动减振测量响应，再施加幅值与相位适当的力；主动降噪则通过次级声场，降低指定位置的声音。两者作用于不同物理环节：一个结构测点的振动下降，不必然代表所有辐射噪声都下降。

Morphing structures intentionally change shape or stiffness between operating conditions. Elastic deformation, actuation and locks can work together, for example to permit motion in one phase and carry a larger load in another. The useful comparison spans the full cycle and includes the mass and reliability of the mechanism needed to make that change.

变形结构有意在不同工况间改变形状或刚度。弹性变形、驱动与锁止可以配合工作，例如在一个阶段允许运动，在另一阶段承担较大载荷。比较应覆盖完整循环，也包括实现变化所需机构的质量和可靠性。

Structure and Energy Storage结构与储能

A conventional battery pack can be integrated through its enclosure, allowing the casing to carry external loads. Structural energy-storage material goes further by making constituents participate in both mechanical function and electrochemical storage. Structural batteries and structural supercapacitors belong to this broader idea, with different energy and power characteristics.

传统电池可以通过壳体集成，让封装承担外部载荷；结构储能材料则进一步让组分同时参与机械功能与电化学储能。结构电池与结构超级电容，都属于这一思路，但具有不同的能量和功率特征。

The energy–power plot in the course separates two demands: how much energy is stored per mass, and how quickly it can be delivered per mass. A high-power device can support a short pulse without storing much total energy. The structural design adds stiffness, strength and damage tolerance to that comparison. The whole system may benefit even if the multifunctional material performs less well than the best separate material in each individual property.

课件中的能量—功率图，区分了单位质量能存储多少能量，以及单位质量能多快输出能量。高功率器件能够支持短时脉冲，并不一定储存很多总能量。结构设计又在其中加入刚度、强度和损伤容限要求。即使多功能材料在每项独立性能上都不及专用材料，完整系统仍可能因省去独立结构而受益。

Research on multifunctional carbon-fibre structural batteries demonstrates this material-level approach. Its useful system comparison includes the energy and stiffness requirements together. A lower specific energy can still be worthwhile if enough separate structure is removed, while damage to a load-bearing battery also couples mechanical repair to electrical isolation and thermal protection.

多功能碳纤维结构电池研究展示了材料层面的集成。系统比较需要同时满足储能与刚度要求；即使比能量较低，只要能够省去足够的独立结构，也可能有利。承载电池损伤时，机械维修又会与电隔离和热防护联系起来。

For a structural supercapacitor, electrodes, separator and electrolyte must also maintain the mechanical load path and electrical isolation. A load-bearing energy-storage demonstration is valuable because it tests the two functions together. The course’s LATP/CNT example belongs to this material-level route: a solid electrolyte supports mechanical integrity and the electrode structure contributes electrochemical surface area. Its volumetric capacitance and its mass/volume comparison are different quantities.

结构超级电容的电极、隔膜和电解质，还需要维持机械传力路径与电隔离。承载同时储能的演示，可以直接检查这两项功能。课件中的 LATP/CNT 例子属于材料层面的路线：固态电解质参与机械完整性，电极结构提供电化学比表面积。体积电容与系统质量、体积比较，是不同的评价量。

Usable Performance and Monitoring可用性能与监测

A lightweight structure is sized using a design value, not simply a pristine specimen’s best result. For a property $X$, the course represents the reduction from reference test value to usable design value with knockdown factors:

轻量化结构采用的是设计值，而不只是完好试样的最佳结果。对某项性能 $X$，课程用折减系数 KDF，将参考试验值转换为可用设计值：

$$X_{\mathrm{design}}=X_{\mathrm{ref}}\,\mathrm{KDF}_1\,\mathrm{KDF}_2\cdots.$$

Temperature, moisture, manufacturing variation, imperfections and damage can each affect the allowance. A more reliable material process or damage model can recover usable performance without changing the nominal modulus. Factors should follow their test basis, and the same uncertainty should not be counted twice.

温度、湿度、制造离散、初始缺陷与损伤，都可能影响许用值。更稳定的工艺或更可靠的损伤模型，可以在不改变名义模量的情况下恢复可用性能。系数需要对应试验依据，相同不确定性也不应重复计入。

Compression after impact (CAI) illustrates the issue. A composite panel can contain hidden internal delamination while its outer appearance changes little. Impact position matters: a flexible skin region and a reinforced location can absorb and transmit the same impact differently. Damage tolerance therefore depends on the actual structural region and support condition as well as the laminate.

冲击后压缩 CAI 可以说明这一问题。复合板外观变化很小，内部仍可能存在分层。冲击位置也很重要：柔性蒙皮区域与补强位置，对相同冲击的吸收和传递可能不同。因此，损伤容限既取决于铺层，也取决于实际结构区域和支承条件。

The test method also matters. Resonance-based fatigue testing can reach many cycles efficiently, but the resulting strain, mode and failure location must represent the question being studied. Guided-wave or strain-based structural health monitoring adds another route: detect a change during service, locate it and decide what inspection or repair follows.

试验方法同样参与设计。共振疲劳试验能够高效达到较多循环，所形成的应变、模态与失效位置，需要对应正在研究的问题。结构健康监测则提供另一条路线：服役中检测变化、定位，再决定检查或维修。

{{course:monitoring}}

Piezoelectric transducers can send guided waves through a composite panel. A debond changes transmission, reflection or scattering, allowing comparison with a baseline. Temperature and sensor condition also affect the signal. UC San Diego’s adhesive-bond inspection work shows how wave mode, transducer configuration and temperature compensation enter that method.

压电换能器可以在复合板内激励导波。脱粘改变透射、反射或散射，使信号能够与基准比较，温度和传感器状态也会影响结果。UC San Diego 的胶接检测研究展示了波模态、换能器布置与温度补偿在其中的作用。

{{course:guidedwave}}

Monitoring can support a lighter structure when detection coverage and the response to detected damage justify a different design allowance. Sensors, wiring and protection add mass too. This closes the material–structure–manufacturing loop: reducing uncertainty and making damage observable can increase usable efficiency, while the final benefit is evaluated at assembly level.

检测覆盖与故障响应如果能够支持新的设计许用值，监测就有机会帮助减轻结构。传感器、走线和防护也增加质量，因此仍需按装配体计算收益。这将材料、结构和制造重新连接起来：降低不确定性、让损伤可观察，都可能提高可用效率。
