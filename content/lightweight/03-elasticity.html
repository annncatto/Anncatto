Stiffness and Strength刚度与强度

A link can remain far below its failure stress while moving enough to spoil an end-effector trajectory. Strength constrains failure; stiffness constrains deformation under load. Stability constrains a loss of equilibrium, and fatigue constrains damage over repeated loading. Lightweight design must identify which of these limits actually sets the section size.

连杆的应力远低于失效水平，末端位移仍可能大到破坏轨迹精度。强度约束失效，刚度约束受载变形，稳定性约束平衡状态的丧失，疲劳则约束反复加载产生的损伤。轻量化首先要判断，究竟是哪一个条件决定了截面尺寸。

Elastic Response弹性响应

For a uniform axial member in small-strain linear elasticity, force $F$ causes extension $\delta$, normal stress $\sigma$ and strain $\varepsilon$:

对于小应变、线弹性、均匀的轴向杆件，力 $F$ 引起伸长 $\delta$，正应力与应变满足：

$$\sigma=\frac FA,\qquad\varepsilon=\frac\delta L,\qquad\sigma=E\varepsilon,\qquad k=\frac F\delta=\frac{EA}L.$$

$E$ is Young’s modulus, a material property with units Pa. The axial stiffness $k$ also depends on geometry and has units N/m. For a linear spring, compliance is $s=1/k$, with units m/N. For coupled displacements, $f=K_su$ and $u=Sf$, where $S=K_s^{-1}$ after rigid modes have been constrained.

$E$ 是杨氏模量，属于材料性能，单位为 Pa；轴向刚度 $k$ 还受几何影响，单位为 N/m。线性弹簧的柔度为 $s=1/k$，单位为 m/N。多自由度下，$f=K_su$、$u=Sf$；约束刚体模态后，柔度矩阵 $S=K_s^{-1}$。

For a nonlinear force–displacement curve, tangent stiffness and tangent compliance are derivatives of the response: $k_t=dF/d\delta$ and $s_t=d\delta/dF$. Compliance is the derivative of displacement with respect to force, not the derivative of stiffness. A derivative $dk/d\delta$ instead describes how stiffness changes along the response. The stored elastic energy is $U=\int_0^\delta F(u)\,du$, giving $U=k\delta^2/2$ in the linear case.

对于非线性的力—位移曲线，切线刚度与切线柔度分别为 $k_t=dF/d\delta$、$s_t=d\delta/dF$。柔度是位移对力的导数，而刚度的导数 $dk/d\delta$ 描述刚度随位移怎样改变。储存的弹性能为 $U=\int_0^\delta F(u)\,du$，在线性情形下得到 $U=k\delta^2/2$。

Shear uses $\tau=G\gamma$, with shear modulus $G$. An isotropic linear elastic solid satisfies $G=E/[2(1+\nu)]$, where $\nu$ is Poisson’s ratio. A fibre laminate generally needs direction-dependent properties and a constitutive matrix; the isotropic relation cannot replace its laminate analysis.

剪切变形满足 $\tau=G\gamma$，其中 $G$ 为剪切模量。各向同性线弹性材料有 $G=E/[2(1+\nu)]$，$\nu$ 为泊松比。纤维层合板通常需要方向相关的性能与本构矩阵，各向同性关系不能替代铺层分析。

Bending and Section Geometry弯曲与截面几何

A long robot link often behaves approximately as a beam. Under Euler–Bernoulli assumptions, cross-sections remain plane and normal to the deflected centreline, deformations are small, and shear deformation is neglected. With coordinate $y$ measured from the neutral axis:

细长机器人连杆通常可以先近似为梁。Euler–Bernoulli 模型假定变形较小、横截面保持平面并垂直于变形后的中心线，同时忽略剪切变形。令 $y$ 为到中性轴的距离：

$$\sigma_x=\frac{M_b y}{I_A},\qquad I_A=\int_A y^2\,dA,\qquad\kappa=\frac{M_b}{EI_A}.$$

$I_A$ is the second moment of area, with units m⁴. Rotational mass inertia has units kg·m². The former measures how area resists bending; the latter measures how mass resists angular acceleration. Confusing them hides the central opportunity of a hollow link: moving material away from the bending neutral axis can improve stiffness per mass.

$I_A$ 是截面二次矩，单位为 m⁴；质量转动惯量的单位为 kg·m²。前者衡量面积如何抵抗弯曲，后者衡量质量如何抵抗角加速度。空心杆件的关键机会正来自两者的差异：将材料移离弯曲中性轴，能够提高单位质量的弯曲刚度。

For a cantilever fixed at $x=0$, with a transverse point load $F$ at $x=L$, integration of curvature gives the bending displacement and maximum stress:

对于在 $x=0$ 固支、长度为 $L$、末端承受横向集中力 $F$ 的悬臂梁，对曲率积分得到弯曲位移与最大应力：

{{lightweight:beam}}
$$\begin{aligned}w(x)&=\frac{Fx^2(3L-x)}{6EI_A},\\\delta_b=w(L)&=\frac{FL^3}{3EI_A},\qquad k_b=\frac{3EI_A}{L^3},\\\sigma_{\max}&=\frac{FLc}{I_A}.\end{aligned}$$

$c$ is the distance to the outermost fibre. Length enters deflection cubically, whereas the load enters linearly. Doubling a cantilever’s length with everything else fixed increases its bending deflection eightfold. Shortening a load path can therefore achieve more than a modest improvement in material modulus.

$c$ 是到最外侧纤维的距离。长度以三次方进入挠度，载荷以一次方进入。其余条件不变时，悬臂长度翻倍，弯曲挠度增加到八倍。因此，缩短传力路径可能比小幅提高材料模量更有效。

For a rectangle of width $b$ and bending depth $h$, $I_A=bh^3/12$ while mass is $\rho bhL$. Increasing depth is especially effective when packaging allows it. For a rectangular closed tube with uniform wall $t$:

矩形截面宽为 $b$、弯曲方向高度为 $h$ 时，$I_A=bh^3/12$，质量为 $\rho bhL$。空间允许时，增加截面高度尤其有效。对于壁厚均匀为 $t$ 的矩形闭口管：

$$A=bh-(b-2t)(h-2t),\qquad I_A=\frac{bh^3-(b-2t)(h-2t)^3}{12}.$$

The equation refers to bending about the axis parallel to the width. Rotating the same section by 90° generally changes its stiffness. A multi-axis limb needs both bending directions and torsion checked. Thin walls also introduce local instability, denting and connection loads that a section formula alone does not capture.

该式对应绕平行于宽度方向的轴弯曲。同一个截面旋转 90°，刚度通常会改变。多轴受力的肢体需要同时校核两个弯曲方向与扭转。薄壁还会引入局部失稳、凹陷和连接载荷，截面公式无法独自描述这些问题。

A cut through a loaded link exposes axial force $N$, transverse shear $V$, bending moment $M_b$ and torque $T$. In a linear elastic beam, axial and bending normal stresses superpose. A transverse shear estimate for a suitable prismatic section uses the first area moment $Q$ and local section width $b_{\mathrm{loc}}$:

将受载连杆截开，可以得到轴力 $N$、横向剪力 $V$、弯矩 $M_b$ 和扭矩 $T$。在线弹性梁中，轴向与弯曲正应力可以叠加。对适用的等截面梁，横向剪应力还可用面积一次矩 $Q$ 与局部截面宽度 $b_{\mathrm{loc}}$ 估计：

$$\sigma_x(y)=\frac NA\pm\frac{M_by}{I_A},\qquad \tau_{xy}(y)\approx\frac{VQ(y)}{I_Ab_{\mathrm{loc}}(y)}.$$

$Q(y)$ integrates distance from the neutral axis over the area above or below the chosen cut. The bending stress is greatest at an outer fibre; transverse shear can be greatest nearer the neutral axis. A sandwich core or box web therefore carries a different demand from its outer faces. Around bearings and holes, the local force introduction needs a more detailed model. Material should be assigned according to the local stress state, including compression, shear and interfacial loads.

$Q(y)$ 对所选位置上方或下方的面积，积分其到中性轴的距离。弯曲正应力在外侧纤维较大，横向剪应力却可能在靠近中性轴的位置较大。因此，夹层芯材或箱形腹板，与外侧面板承担不同的要求。轴承和孔附近的局部加载，需要更细致的模型；材料应根据局部的压缩、剪切和界面载荷等应力状态选择。

For short or deep beams, shear deformation adds to bending. A Timoshenko-type screening expression is $\delta\approx FL^3/(3EI_A)+FL/(\kappa_sGA)$, where $\kappa_s$ depends on the section. Joint and bearing flexibility can contribute still more. A beam estimate should be compared with a refined model whenever those contributions are significant.

短梁或深梁还需要考虑剪切变形。Timoshenko 型的初步表达为 $\delta\approx FL^3/(3EI_A)+FL/(\kappa_sGA)$，$\kappa_s$ 随截面而变。关节与轴承柔性还会继续叠加；这些贡献明显时，应使用更细致的模型检查梁估计。

Plates and Shells板与壳

A thin metal panel carries transverse load through bending in two in-plane directions. For a homogeneous isotropic Kirchhoff–Love plate of thickness $t$, its bending rigidity and small-deflection equation are:

薄金属板通过两个面内方向的弯曲承受横向载荷。对于厚度为 $t$、均匀各向同性的 Kirchhoff–Love 薄板，弯曲刚度与小挠度方程为：

$$D=\frac{Et^3}{12(1-\nu^2)},\qquad D\nabla^4w=p(x,y).$$

$p$ is transverse pressure, and support conditions specify displacement and rotation or the corresponding moments and shear forces. A clamped edge, a simply supported edge and a free edge produce different solutions. For moment resultants $M_x,M_y$ per unit width, bending stresses at thickness coordinate $z$ are $\sigma_x=12M_xz/t^3$ and $\sigma_y=12M_yz/t^3$. At a surface their magnitudes are $6|M_x|/t^2$ and $6|M_y|/t^2$.

$p$ 为横向面载荷，支承条件规定边界的位移、转角，或相应的弯矩与剪力。固支、简支和自由边会给出不同解。设单位宽度弯矩为 $M_x,M_y$，厚度坐标为 $z$，则弯曲正应力为 $\sigma_x=12M_xz/t^3$、$\sigma_y=12M_yz/t^3$；在表面，其幅值为 $6|M_x|/t^2$、$6|M_y|/t^2$。

The cubic thickness dependence explains why a small wall reduction can create a large deformation penalty. Curving a panel into a shell may allow membrane forces to carry part of the load, but shell stability then becomes important. Large deflection, thick plates and laminates require the appropriate nonlinear, shear-deformable or anisotropic models. Kirchhoff–Love plate theory supplies the thin-plate formulation.

厚度的三次方关系，解释了为什么小幅减薄也可能明显增加变形。将平板弯成壳体，可能利用膜内力承担部分载荷，同时也需要重视壳稳定性。大挠度、厚板与层合板，分别需要相应的非线性、剪切变形或各向异性模型。Kirchhoff–Love 板理论给出了薄板表达。

Torsion and Stability扭转与稳定性

A straight uniform shaft under torque $T$ has twist $\theta=TL/(GJ_t)$, where $J_t$ is the Saint-Venant torsion constant. For a circular tube it equals the polar area moment; for a general section it does not. With outer diameter $D$ and inner diameter $d$:

均匀直轴承受扭矩 $T$ 时，扭转角为 $\theta=TL/(GJ_t)$，其中 $J_t$ 为 Saint-Venant 扭转常数。圆管的扭转常数等于截面极惯性矩，一般截面则需要另行计算。设外径为 $D$、内径为 $d$：

$$I_A=\frac\pi{64}(D^4-d^4),\qquad J_t=\frac\pi{32}(D^4-d^4),\qquad k_\theta=\frac{GJ_t}L.$$

For a thin-walled single-cell closed section with median enclosed area $A_m$, $J_t\approx4A_m^2/\oint ds/t$. Its torque is carried by a circulating shear flow. An open strip instead has a torsion constant scaling with $t^3$. Cutting a long opening in a closed robot link may leave its bending performance respectable while sharply reducing torsional stiffness. Warping and the restraint at end fittings also matter.

对于中线包围面积为 $A_m$ 的单腔薄壁闭口截面，$J_t\approx4A_m^2/\oint ds/t$，扭矩由环绕截面的剪流传递。开口薄条的扭转常数则与 $t^3$ 成比例。在闭口连杆上开一道长槽，弯曲性能可能仍然不错，扭转刚度却会明显下降。端部接头对翘曲的约束也需要考虑。

A compressed slender member can buckle before its material reaches yield. The ideal Euler critical load is:

细长受压杆可能在材料屈服前发生屈曲。理想 Euler 临界载荷为：

$$P_{\mathrm{cr}}=\frac{\pi^2EI_{A,\min}}{(K_LL)^2}.$$

$K_L$ expresses end restraint: 1 for ideal pin–pin ends and 2 for a fixed–free column. The weaker bending axis governs. This model assumes an initially straight, elastic slender column under axial load. Shell buckling, local plate buckling and eccentric compression require different models. Manufacturing imperfections can make thin shells particularly sensitive, motivating experimentally supported knockdown factors.

$K_L$ 表示端部约束的作用：理想两端铰支取 1，一端固支、一端自由取 2。较弱的弯曲轴控制结果。模型假定杆件初始笔直、弹性、细长并承受轴向载荷；壳体屈曲、局部板屈曲和偏心受压需要其他模型。制造缺陷会让薄壳尤其敏感，因此需要有试验依据的折减系数。

Structural Modes and Control结构模态与控制

A single flexible mode can be represented by effective mass $m_e$, stiffness $k_e$ and damping $c_e$:

一个柔性模态可以用等效质量 $m_e$、刚度 $k_e$ 和阻尼 $c_e$ 表示：

$$m_e\ddot x+c_e\dot x+k_ex=F,\qquad\omega_n=\sqrt{\frac{k_e}{m_e}},\qquad\zeta=\frac{c_e}{2\sqrt{k_em_e}}.$$

Reducing effective mass by half while retaining stiffness raises the undamped natural frequency by $\sqrt2$. Reducing stiffness at the same time can cancel or reverse that gain. A thin link carrying a substantial motor or tool may lose frequency quickly because its stiffness falls while the attached mass remains.

刚度保持不变、等效质量减半时，无阻尼固有频率提高到 $\sqrt2$ 倍；同时降低刚度，则可能抵消甚至反转这项收益。薄连杆上携带较重的电机或工具时，杆件刚度下降，附加质量仍然保留，频率就可能很快降低。

In a multi-degree structure, modes satisfy $K_s\phi_i=\omega_i^2M_s\phi_i$. The mass participating in a mode is therefore more specific than total robot mass. Joint compliance, bearing preload and transmission elasticity can place a low-frequency mode below the link’s own bending resonance.

多自由度结构的模态满足 $K_s\phi_i=\omega_i^2M_s\phi_i$。参与某个模态的质量，比整机总质量更具体。关节柔性、轴承预紧与传动弹性，还可能在杆件弯曲共振之前引入更低频的模态。

High servo gain asks the actuator to correct motion rapidly. An unmodeled flexible mode introduces additional amplitude and phase changes near resonance; increasing feedback gain may excite that mode instead of correcting the tool pose. Sensor location matters: a motor encoder can report an accurate motor angle while the flexible end of the link vibrates. Delay, damping, model-based compensation and sensor placement together determine the usable bandwidth. There is no universal ratio between the first resonance and allowable servo bandwidth.

提高伺服增益，是要求执行器更快地修正运动。未建模的柔性模态在共振附近引入额外的幅值与相位变化，继续提高增益可能激发振动，反而难以修正工具姿态。传感器位置也很关键：电机编码器报告的角度可以很准确，柔性连杆的末端却仍在振动。延迟、阻尼、模型补偿与测量位置共同决定可用带宽，第一阶共振与伺服带宽之间没有通用的固定比例。

This explains the recurring stiffness constraint in robot lightweighting. A structure must preserve the shape through which its controller acts. Strength, buckling and fatigue remain binding wherever their margins are lower. Beam theory, Euler buckling and structural dynamics give complementary models for deciding which limit comes first.

机器人轻量化经常由刚度主导，原因是结构需要保持控制器赖以施力的几何关系。强度、屈曲与疲劳在各自余量更小时仍然决定尺寸。梁理论、Euler 屈曲与结构动力学提供了互补的判断工具。
