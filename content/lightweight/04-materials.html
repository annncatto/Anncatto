Material Selection材料选择

Density and modulus appear together because geometry can compensate for a material change. The useful question is how much material is needed to meet a requirement. A heavier material with high modulus can outperform a lighter one in one geometry and lose in another. A material index follows from the load, the constraint and the geometric variable allowed to change.

密度与模量总是一起出现，因为几何可以补偿材料变化。真正需要计算的是：满足要求究竟需要多少材料。密度较大的高模量材料，在一种几何约束下可能占优，在另一种约束下又会落后。材料指数来自载荷、约束，以及允许改变的几何变量。

Deriving a Material Index推导材料指数

Consider a tensile tie carrying force $F$, with fixed length $L$ and allowable extension $\delta_{\max}$. The area $A$ can vary. Eliminate it from the stiffness constraint and the mass expression:

考虑承受拉力 $F$ 的拉杆，长度 $L$ 固定、允许伸长为 $\delta_{\max}$，截面面积 $A$ 可以改变。从刚度约束和质量表达式中消去 $A$：

$$\begin{aligned}\delta&=\frac{FL}{EA}\leq\delta_{\max}\quad\Rightarrow\quad A\geq\frac{FL}{E\delta_{\max}},\\m_{\min}&=\frac{FL^2}{\delta_{\max}}\frac\rho E.\end{aligned}$$

The functional terms are fixed, leaving $\rho/E$ as the material-dependent penalty. Maximizing $E/\rho$ minimizes mass. This is the specific modulus for an axial-stiffness-limited tie. If the tie is strength-limited, $F/A\leq\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$ instead gives $m_{\min}=FL\rho/\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$, so the appropriate index becomes $\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}/\rho$.

功能相关项已经固定，材料相关的代价只剩 $\rho/E$。最大化 $E/\rho$，就能最小化质量，这也是轴向刚度受限拉杆的比模量。若由强度约束，改用 $F/A\leq\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$，则 $m_{\min}=FL\rho/\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$，应比较 $\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}/\rho$。

For a bending beam whose cross-section scales with a constant shape, area and second moment satisfy $I_A=\beta A^2$, where $\beta$ is a shape constant. A prescribed cantilever stiffness $k_0$ requires:

对于保持形状、按比例缩放的受弯梁，截面面积与二次矩满足 $I_A=\beta A^2$，其中 $\beta$ 是形状系数。要求悬臂刚度达到 $k_0$：

$$\begin{aligned}\frac{3E\beta A^2}{L^3}&\geq k_0,\\m_{\min}&=L\sqrt{\frac{k_0L^3}{3\beta}}\frac\rho{\sqrt E}.\end{aligned}$$

The beam index is $E^{1/2}/\rho$. This result assumes that both cross-section dimensions may scale together. If the width is fixed and only bending depth may change, $I_A=bh^3/12$ leads to an index $E^{1/3}/\rho$. That same exponent occurs in a plate of fixed planform whose thickness is the free variable, because its bending rigidity scales with $Et^3$.

因此，受弯梁的材料指数为 $E^{1/2}/\rho$。这个结果允许截面的两个尺寸一起缩放；若宽度固定、只允许改变弯曲方向高度，代入 $I_A=bh^3/12$ 后得到 $E^{1/3}/\rho$。平面外形固定、只改变厚度的板也出现同一指数，因为弯曲刚度与 $Et^3$ 成比例。

A thin circular robot link gives another useful contrast. With outer diameter $D$ and wall $t\ll D$, $I_A\approx\pi D^3t/8$ and $m\approx\rho\pi DtL$. Keeping the envelope fixed and changing only the wall gives $m_{\min}\propto\rho/E$ under a bending-stiffness requirement. Keeping the wall fixed and allowing diameter to change instead gives $D\propto E^{-1/3}$ and $m_{\min}\propto\rho/E^{1/3}$.

薄壁圆形连杆还能说明几何约束的影响。外径为 $D$、壁厚满足 $t\ll D$ 时，$I_A\approx\pi D^3t/8$，质量 $m\approx\rho\pi DtL$。外包络固定、只改变壁厚，在抗弯刚度约束下得到 $m_{\min}\propto\rho/E$；壁厚固定、允许改变外径，则有 $D\propto E^{-1/3}$，进而 $m_{\min}\propto\rho/E^{1/3}$。

The two indices are $E/\rho$ and $E^{1/3}/\rho$, even though both members are bending tubes. They follow different geometric freedoms. The thin-wall assumption must remain valid after resizing, and the chosen diameter must fit the joint interfaces. This is how the section second moment enters a material-index derivation before the geometric variable is eliminated.

两者都是受弯圆管，材料指数却分别为 $E/\rho$ 与 $E^{1/3}/\rho$，因为允许改变的几何变量不同。调整尺寸后仍需满足薄壁近似，直径也要适配关节接口。截面二次矩先参与刚度计算，再通过消去几何变量进入材料指数。

For a strength-limited, geometrically similar bending section, section modulus $Z=I_A/c$ scales with $A^{3/2}$. Requiring $M_b/Z\leq\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$ produces $m_{\min}\propto\rho/\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}^{2/3}$. The corresponding index is $\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}^{2/3}/\rho$. The exponent must be derived from the actual constraint; it cannot be copied from a stiffness formula.

强度受限、几何相似的受弯截面，截面模量 $Z=I_A/c$ 与 $A^{3/2}$ 成比例。由 $M_b/Z\leq\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$ 得到 $m_{\min}\propto\rho/\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}^{2/3}$，相应指数为 $\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}^{2/3}/\rho$。指数必须从实际约束推导，不能直接套用刚度公式中的幂次。

For a metal plate of fixed planform dimensions $a,b$, a fixed pressure pattern and fixed supports, maximum deflection has the form $\delta=C_p p_0a^4/D$. The coefficient $C_p$ contains aspect ratio and boundary conditions. Eliminating thickness makes the plate index explicit:

对于平面尺寸 $a,b$ 固定、面载荷形式和支承固定的金属薄板，最大挠度可写为 $\delta=C_p p_0a^4/D$，$C_p$ 包含长宽比与边界条件。消去厚度后，板的材料指数明确为：

$$\begin{aligned}t_{\min}&=\left[\frac{12C_p p_0a^4(1-\nu^2)}{E\delta_{\max}}\right]^{1/3},\\m_{\min}&=\rho abt_{\min},\qquad M_p=\frac{[E/(1-\nu^2)]^{1/3}}\rho.\end{aligned}$$

With similar Poisson ratios, this reduces to $E^{1/3}/\rho$. At fixed bending moment per unit width, the surface stress $6M_x/t^2$ instead yields $t_{\min}\propto\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}^{-1/2}$ and the strength index $\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}^{1/2}/\rho$. The index identifies an ideal geometric trade; local instability, joints and fatigue can impose another thickness floor.

候选材料泊松比接近时，可简化为 $E^{1/3}/\rho$。若固定的是单位宽度弯矩，由表面应力 $6M_x/t^2$ 得到 $t_{\min}\propto\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}^{-1/2}$，强度指数为 $\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}^{1/2}/\rho$。指数给出理想几何权衡，局部失稳、连接与疲劳还可能规定另一项最小厚度。

Reading an Ashby Chart阅读 Ashby 图

An Ashby chart places material properties on logarithmic axes. With modulus on the vertical axis and density on the horizontal axis, constant $E^a/\rho$ lines satisfy:

Ashby 图将材料性能放在对数坐标中。以模量为纵轴、密度为横轴，固定 $E^a/\rho$ 的等值线满足：

$$\log E=\frac1a\log\rho+\mathrm{constant}.$$

The slopes are 1 for axial stiffness, 2 for similar-section beam bending, and 3 for plate-thickness design. Parallel lines move toward higher performance as their intercept increases. A point’s position must be evaluated against the relevant slope. The chart below uses representative values for four named material systems.

轴向刚度、截面相似缩放的受弯梁、只改变厚度的板，对应斜率分别为 1、2、3。平行线的截距越高，性能指数越大。材料点需要沿相应斜率比较。下图采用四种具名材料体系的代表值。

Steel, aluminium and titanium have Young’s moduli and densities of different magnitudes, but their specific axial moduli are remarkably close at the representative values used here. A change to aluminium does not multiply the axial stiffness available per kilogram. Under beam or plate scaling, lower density can provide a stronger advantage because the necessary section can grow.

在此采用的代表值下，钢、铝和钛的模量、密度相差明显，比轴向模量却很接近。换成铝合金，并不会让每千克可提供的轴向刚度成倍增加。梁或板允许改变截面时，较低密度才可能通过更大的几何尺寸获得更明显的优势。

For example, with $E_{\mathrm{Al}}=69$ GPa, $\rho_{\mathrm{Al}}=2700$ kg/m³, $E_s=210$ GPa and $\rho_s=7850$ kg/m³, the aluminium-to-steel index ratio is about 0.955 for an axial tie, 1.67 for a similarly scaled bending beam and 2.01 for a thickness-varied plate. These ratios assume that the prescribed geometric change is permitted. A fixed envelope can remove that advantage.

例如，取 $E_{\mathrm{Al}}=69$ GPa、$\rho_{\mathrm{Al}}=2700$ kg/m³，$E_s=210$ GPa、$\rho_s=7850$ kg/m³，铝对钢的指数比约为：受拉杆 0.955、截面相似缩放的受弯梁 1.67、只改变厚度的板 2.01。前提是允许相应的几何调整；固定外包络可能使这项优势无法兑现。

Two aluminium tempers or alloys can have very similar Young’s moduli and quite different yield strengths. Replacing 6061-T6 with a stronger alloy may help a yield-limited bracket but do little for a deflection-limited link in the same envelope. Joint details, fatigue and corrosion can change the eventual ranking.

不同铝合金或热处理状态，杨氏模量可能接近，屈服强度却相差明显。用更高强度合金替换 6061-T6，可能改善屈服受限支架，对同外包络、挠度受限的连杆则未必有效。连接细节、疲劳与腐蚀还会改变最终排序。

Composites and Design Values复合材料与设计值

A carbon-fibre composite combines stiff fibres with a matrix that transfers shear, protects fibres and stabilizes their positions. Unidirectional material can be very efficient along the fibre direction while remaining much weaker or more compliant transversely. A quasi-isotropic laminate uses several orientations to spread in-plane loads, with a lower directional modulus than a fibre-aligned strip.

碳纤维复合材料由高刚度纤维与基体组成。基体负责传递剪力、保护纤维并稳定其位置。单向材料沿纤维方向可以很高效，横向性能则明显不同；准各向同性层合板通过多种铺层方向分担面内载荷，某一方向的模量通常低于沿纤维布置的单向条带。

Classical laminate theory relates membrane forces $N$ and bending moments $M$ to mid-plane strain $\varepsilon_0$ and curvature $\kappa$ through the laminate $A$, $B$ and $D$ matrices:

经典层合板理论利用 $A$、$B$、$D$ 矩阵，将面内力 $N$、弯矩 $M$ 与中面应变 $\varepsilon_0$、曲率 $\kappa$ 联系起来：

$$\begin{bmatrix}N\\M\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}A&B\\B&D\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}\varepsilon_0\\\kappa\end{bmatrix}.$$

Ply orientation and position enter these matrices. In an unsymmetric layup, the coupling matrix $B$ can make in-plane loading produce curvature. Fibre strength alone cannot determine the allowable load at a bolted insert or across a delaminated interface. Bearing, interlaminar shear, compression after impact and environmental degradation require their own data.

铺层方向与位置共同进入这些矩阵。非对称铺层的耦合矩阵 $B$，可能让面内载荷同时引起弯曲。仅凭纤维强度，无法确定螺栓嵌件或分层界面的承载能力；孔边承压、层间剪切、冲击后压缩与环境退化，都需要相应的数据。

Material development changes these limits. Tougher matrices can improve resistance to crack growth; thin plies alter damage initiation and propagation; fibre–metal laminates combine metal ductility with fibre reinforcement. Their structural benefit depends on layup, processing, interfaces and the failure mechanism. Electrical insulation and corrosion protection become part of the design when carbon fibres contact aluminium.

材料改性会改变这些限制。增韧基体可以改善裂纹扩展阻力，薄层铺层改变损伤起始与传播，纤维金属层板则结合金属延性与纤维增强。结构收益取决于铺层、工艺、界面和主导失效机制。碳纤维与铝接触时，电绝缘与腐蚀防护也属于设计内容。

A design value should identify the material condition, product form, temperature, direction and statistical basis. Yield strength, ultimate strength, fatigue strength and an allowable stress are different quantities. The studio uses rounded representative properties to explore trends; a final part needs traceable supplier and test data for its actual material system. Material selection and laminate theory provide useful notation for extending the calculations.

设计值应说明材料状态、产品形态、温度、方向与统计依据。屈服强度、极限强度、疲劳强度与许用应力各有含义。实验室采用取整的代表性能来比较趋势，实际零件还需要对应材料体系的可追溯供方与试验数据。材料选择与层合板理论可以作为继续推导的术语入口。
