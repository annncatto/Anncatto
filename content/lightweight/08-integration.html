Functional Integration功能集成

A system can lose mass because fewer independent parts are needed. A load-bearing shell may also locate sensors, spread heat and protect cables. The structural question becomes which functions can share a material volume or interface while preserving all their requirements. The mass comparison must cover both the removed parts and everything added to make integration work.

减少独立零件，也能减少系统质量。承载外壳可以同时定位传感器、扩散热量和保护线缆。结构问题因此变成：哪些功能能够共享材料体积或接口，并同时满足各自要求？质量比较需要覆盖移走的零件，以及让集成能够成立的全部新增部分。

$$\Delta m_{\mathrm{sys}}=m_{\mathrm{separate\ functions}}-m_{\mathrm{integrated\ assembly}}.$$

A useful baseline includes fasteners, brackets, seals, cooling hardware, wiring and protective parts. Thermal isolation, service access and replaceable wear surfaces can justify keeping functions separate. The best boundary follows the product’s use and repair cycle rather than a target part count.

基准方案应包括紧固件、支架、密封、散热件、线缆与防护件。热隔离、维修空间和可更换磨损表面，也可能要求保留功能分离。合理边界取决于产品使用与维修循环，而不只取决于零件数量目标。

Sharing Structure共享承载结构

Passive integration lets one geometry serve several mechanical or physical functions. A sealed hollow link can carry load and route cables; a motor housing can be a structural bearing carrier and a heat spreader. A compliant member can both guide motion and store elastic energy. Each example eliminates an interface but introduces coupling: a hot bearing housing can shift its fits, and a load-bearing cable duct must retain adequate section around openings.

被动功能集成让一种几何承担多种机械或物理功能。密封空心连杆可以承载并走线，电机壳体可以同时支承轴承与导热，柔性杆件可以同时导向与储能。省去接口的同时，也会引入耦合：轴承壳体升温会改变配合，承载线槽的开口附近仍需保留足够截面。

Compliance mechanisms use elastic deformation in place of some pin joints. They can reduce backlash, lubrication needs and part count, while shifting the limits toward strain, fatigue and out-of-plane stability. Large rotations require nonlinear geometry. A flexible hinge sized from a small-deflection approximation can reach unexpectedly high local strain over its full travel. Compliant-mechanism design treats deformation as part of the intended motion.

柔顺机构用弹性变形替代部分转动铰链，可以减少回差、润滑需求与零件数量，同时将限制转向应变、疲劳和面外稳定性。大转角需要考虑几何非线性，按小变形近似设计的柔性铰链，在完整行程中可能出现很高的局部应变。柔顺机构设计将变形本身作为预期运动的一部分。

A battery can join the load path at different levels. A structural enclosure uses conventional cells inside a pack whose casing also carries robot loads. A structural battery composite goes further: electrodes, electrolyte and reinforcement participate in both energy storage and mechanical function. These concepts have different manufacturing, damage and service implications.

电池可以在不同层次进入传力路径。承载电池壳体在传统电芯外设置兼任机器人结构的封装；结构电池复合材料则更进一步，让电极、电解质与增强体同时参与储能和承载。两种概念的制造、损伤和维修要求各不相同。

Research on multifunctional carbon-fibre structural batteries demonstrates this material-level approach. Its useful system comparison includes the energy and stiffness requirements together. A lower specific energy can still be worthwhile if enough separate structure is removed, while damage to a load-bearing battery also couples mechanical repair to electrical isolation and thermal protection.

多功能碳纤维结构电池研究展示了材料层面的集成。系统比较需要同时满足储能与刚度要求；即使比能量较低，只要能够省去足够的独立结构，也可能有利。承载电池损伤时，机械维修又会与电隔离和热防护联系起来。

At enclosure level, Figure’s F.03 battery combines stamped steel, die-cast aluminium and structural adhesive, and uses its pack as part of the torso structure. Cooling features are integrated into the casting. This is a practical example of function sharing even when some local material is steel. The company describes the mechanism; the exact mass saving attributable to that integration needs a complete before-and-after system budget.

在壳体层面，Figure F.03 电池组合使用冲压钢、压铸铝和结构胶，让电池封装参与躯干承载，并把冷却特征集成进压铸件。即使局部采用钢，也能通过共享功能减少其他结构。官方说明了机制，要独立核算这项集成的具体减重，仍需要完整的前后系统质量预算。

Actuation, Sensing and Vibration驱动、感知与振动

Active integration combines structural parts with actuation, sensing or control. A joint module can contain a motor, reduction, encoder, torque sensor, driver and communications. Compact integration can reduce connectors and wiring, but it also concentrates heat and makes electromagnetic compatibility, sealing and replacement more demanding.

主动功能集成将结构与驱动、传感或控制结合。关节模组可以容纳电机、减速级、编码器、力矩传感器、驱动器和通信。紧凑集成可能减少连接器与线缆，也会集中热量，让电磁兼容、密封与更换更具挑战。

An elastic element used as a torque sensor illustrates the coupling. If its torque–deflection calibration is known, $\tau\approx k_\theta\Delta\theta$ provides a measurement. Temperature, hysteresis, cross-axis loads and sensor resolution affect its accuracy. Increasing stiffness reduces deflection under the same torque, which can make the measurement harder to resolve. Structural stiffness and sensing sensitivity must be selected together.

用弹性元件测量力矩，能够直接说明这种耦合。已知转矩—变形标定后，可以通过 $\tau\approx k_\theta\Delta\theta$ 获得测量；温度、滞后、交叉轴载荷与传感器分辨率都会影响精度。提高刚度会减小相同力矩下的变形，也可能让测量更难分辨，结构刚度与感知灵敏度需要一起选择。

Vibration can be reduced by changing the excitation, isolating its transmission, adding damping or applying control forces. A passive damper dissipates energy; active vibration control uses measured motion and actuators to counter a mode. The latter adds electronics, power demand and phase constraints. Feedback cannot be treated as an unlimited replacement for a mechanically sound structure.

减振可以从改变激励、隔断传播、增加阻尼或施加控制力入手。被动阻尼器耗散能量，主动减振则利用测量与执行器抵消某个模态；后者需要电子设备、能耗并受到相位约束。反馈能力不能视为无限的机械补偿资源。

Active noise control similarly produces a secondary acoustic field to reduce sound at chosen locations. It is most effective for predictable low-frequency components and within a limited spatial region. Gear mesh, motor electromagnetic excitation and structural radiation may require different treatments. Reducing acceleration at one sensor does not automatically reduce all radiated sound.

主动降噪通过次级声场降低特定位置的声音，通常更适合可预测的低频成分与有限空间区域。齿轮啮合、电机电磁激励和结构辐射，可能需要不同措施。一个加速度测点的响应下降，也不必然代表整体辐射噪声下降。

Morphing structures deliberately change shape or stiffness to adapt to different operating phases. Their benefit should be evaluated across the complete cycle: a structure that folds compactly may need locks to carry a high load, and those locks add mass and interfaces. Geometry, actuation, stored energy and control reliability become a combined design problem.

变形结构主动改变形状或刚度，以适应不同工作阶段。收益需要按完整循环评估：能够折叠收纳的结构，在高载荷时可能需要锁止，锁止件又会增加质量和接口。几何、驱动、储能与控制可靠性因此构成联合设计问题。

Damage, Uncertainty and Monitoring损伤、不确定性与监测

A tested material property is usually reduced before it becomes a structural design value. Temperature, moisture, manufacturing variation, defects and impact damage can all matter. A schematic strength allowance is $\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}=k_{\mathrm{env}}k_{\mathrm{process}}k_{\mathrm{damage}}\sigma_{\mathrm{reference}}/n_s$. Each factor needs an appropriate test basis, and correlated effects should not be counted twice.

试验测得的材料性能，通常还要折减后才能用作结构设计值。温度、湿度、工艺波动、缺陷和冲击损伤都可能参与。一种概括表达为 $\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}=k_{\mathrm{env}}k_{\mathrm{process}}k_{\mathrm{damage}}\sigma_{\mathrm{reference}}/n_s$。各系数需要对应的试验依据，相关作用也不能重复折减。

Thin composite structures can lose compression strength after an impact that leaves little visible evidence. Improving toughness, making damage inspectable or reducing manufacturing variability can recover usable strength without a dramatic change in pristine modulus. For buckling-sensitive shells, NASA’s shell-buckling guidance shows why imperfection sensitivity requires dedicated design treatment.

薄壁复合结构受到冲击后，可能外观变化很小，压缩性能却明显下降。提高韧性、让损伤可检测或降低制造波动，可以在不显著改变完好模量的情况下恢复可用承载能力。对于对缺陷敏感的薄壳，NASA 壳屈曲指南说明了为何需要专门处理初始缺陷。

Structural health monitoring uses embedded or attached sensors to track changes during service. Piezoelectric transducers can launch guided waves and measure changes in scattering; strain and temperature sensors can identify shifts in load or stiffness. NASA’s guided-wave monitoring project combines piezoelectric arrays and optical sensing. Temperature drift and sensor aging can resemble damage, so baseline compensation and detection coverage are part of the system.

结构健康监测利用嵌入或附着传感器，在服役中跟踪变化。压电换能器可以激励导波并测量散射变化，应变与温度传感器可以观察载荷或刚度改变。NASA 导波监测项目结合了压电阵列与光学测量。温漂和传感器老化也可能类似损伤，因此基准补偿与检测覆盖属于系统设计。

Monitoring can support a lighter design only when its detection capability, inspection interval and response to a detected fault justify the changed allowance. The sensors, wiring, electronics and protective measures also add mass. This puts quality assurance inside the structural optimization: more consistent, observable parts may require less uncertainty margin, while missed damage can remove the intended benefit.

监测要支持减轻结构，必须由检测能力、检查间隔与故障响应证明新的折减依据；传感器、线缆、电子设备与防护也要计入质量。这将质量保证带回结构优化：一致性与可观测性更好的零件，可能减少不确定性余量，漏检损伤则会破坏预期收益。
