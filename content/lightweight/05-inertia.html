Moving a link’s centre of mass changes inertia, gravity and velocity coupling together. A lightweight redesign therefore updates the multibody model before recomputing motor requirements. The sign of the external-force term follows its definition: a wrench applied by the environment to the robot appears on the opposite side of the balance.

移动连杆质心会同时改变惯性、重力与速度耦合。结构修改后，需要更新多体模型，再重新计算电机需求。外力项正负号取决于定义：若采用环境作用于机器人的力旋量，应放到平衡方程的另一侧。

The mass spiral and distal placement质量螺旋与远端布置

A lighter distal link needs less joint torque for the same motion. Resizing the motor and reducer can then reduce the mass carried by the next link, allowing that link and its support to shrink as well. The saving can propagate toward the battery, base or mobile platform. Additional wrist mass propagates in the reverse direction.

同样运动下，末端连杆更轻，所需关节扭矩更小；重新选择较小的电机和减速器，又减轻上游连杆的负载，使其结构和支承继续减重。这种节省可以传到电池、基座或移动平台。手腕处增加质量时，代价也沿相反方向逐级放大。

Distance matters particularly strongly in rotation. The parallel-axis theorem relates inertia about parallel axes, one through the centre of mass and one a perpendicular distance $r$ away:

距离对转动尤其重要。平行轴定理将质心轴惯量，与相距 $r$ 的平行轴惯量联系起来：

$$J=J_{\mathrm{cm}}+mr^2.$$

Doubling the distance multiplies the offset contribution mr² by four. Feet, shanks, wrists and grippers sit far from several driving joints, so moving motors toward the torso or base is often effective. A 0.6 kg knee motor moved through a 0.30 m offset removes 0.054 kg·m² from that contribution about the hip. In the illustrated two-link comparison, total hip inertia changes from about 0.126 to 0.072 kg·m² before adding the new transmission’s contribution.

距离加倍，偏置项 $mr^2$ 增加到四倍。足端、小腿、手腕和夹爪离多个驱动关节较远，因此常将电机移向躯干或基座，再向外传递运动。0.6 kg 的膝电机移去 0.30 m 偏置，可减少其绕髋轴惯量中的 0.054 kg·m²；图示二连杆比较的总惯量约从 0.126 降到 0.072 kg·m²，新增传动还需另行计入。

Moving an actuator toward a fixed support can reduce the mass carried by the outer mechanism. Its output still has to reach the driven joint. The comparison must include the new transmission’s mass, compliance, friction, preload and maintenance. Relocation is useful when those added costs are smaller than the benefit of removing distal moving mass.

将执行器移向固定支承，可以降低外侧机构携带的质量，但其输出仍需传到被驱动关节。比较需要包括新增传动的质量、柔性、摩擦、预紧与维护。只有新增代价小于移走远端运动质量的收益，近端布置才有价值。

Timing belts transmit motion by tooth engagement. Pulley tooth counts set the transmission ratio, while rotation remains continuous. The driven joint can stop between tooth pitches; its useful precision depends on sensing, transmission error, elasticity and control.

同步带通过齿啮合传递运动，带轮齿数决定传动比，转动本身连续。关节可以停在齿距之间，实际精度取决于测量、传动误差、弹性与控制。

The main belt limitations are load-dependent stretch, tooth deformation, preload requirements, bearing side load, hysteresis and the risk of tooth jumping under unsuitable loading or tension. An output encoder measures the actual joint angle and can improve feedback, while the mechanical compliance remains. Belt width, span, tooth profile and engaged tooth count must be sized with the manufacturer’s data. The timing-belt design handbook explains these mechanisms.

同步带的主要限制是随载荷变化的伸长、带齿变形、预紧需求、轴承侧向载荷、滞后，以及不合适载荷或张紧条件下的跳齿风险。输出编码器测量实际关节角度，有助于反馈，机械弹性仍然存在。带宽、跨距、齿形与啮合齿数，需要结合厂家数据选型。同步带设计手册介绍了这些机制。

A rigid linkage uses bars and pivots to relate actuator motion to joint motion. Its mechanical advantage varies with posture. A configuration near a toggle may provide large force but little displacement, reduced usable travel or sensitivity to clearance. Link loads, pin bearing, interference and the complete range of motion need to be checked together.

刚性连杆通过杆与铰链，建立驱动运动和关节运动的关系。机械增益随姿态变化；接近死点的构型可能放大力、减小位移，同时压缩有效行程，或放大间隙的影响。杆件载荷、销轴承压、干涉与完整运动范围，需要一起检查。

A screw converts motor rotation into linear travel, and a rocker or linkage converts that travel into joint motion. Screw lead sets the translation per revolution; the current lever arm sets how axial force produces joint torque. Ball screws and roller screws can combine useful force density with efficient motion, while supports, reversal, preload, buckling and lubrication remain design requirements. A highly efficient screw can backdrive, so holding behavior follows the complete mechanism.

丝杆将电机旋转转换为直线运动，再通过摇杆或连杆转换为关节转动。导程决定每转的直线行程，当前力臂决定轴向力怎样形成关节转矩。滚珠丝杆和行星滚柱丝杆能够结合较高轴向力与传动效率，也需要检查支承、换向、预紧、屈曲和润滑。高效率丝杆可以被反向驱动，因此保持能力需要按完整机构判断。

Motor, transmission and backdrivability电机、传动与反向驱动性

The stator is the stationary electromagnetic assembly; the rotor turns relative to it. An inner rotor places rotating material inside the stator, while an outer rotor places it around the outside. A larger electromagnetic radius can provide higher torque for a given tangential force, but moving rotor mass outward also increases its rotational inertia. Motor diameter, rotor inertia, thermal path and packaging must therefore be selected together.

定子是固定的电磁组件，转子相对它旋转。内转子将旋转部分放在定子内部，外转子则包围定子。较大的电磁作用半径可以在相同切向力下产生更大转矩，但转子质量外移也会提高转动惯量。因此，电机直径、转子惯量、散热路径与封装需要一起选择。

A planetary reducer transmits torque through a sun gear, planet gears and a ring gear. With N = motor speed / joint speed, ideal output torque is N times motor torque. Yet the rotor must rotate N times as fast, so its kinetic energy gives the inertia reflected to the joint:

行星减速器通过太阳轮、行星轮与齿圈传递转矩。定义 $N=\omega_m/\omega_j$，理想输出转矩为电机转矩的 $N$ 倍；转子也必须以关节的 $N$ 倍速度旋转，由其动能得到折算到关节侧的惯量：

$$\frac12J_m(N\dot q)^2=\frac12(N^2J_m)\dot q^2,\qquad J_{\mathrm{joint}}\approx J_{\mathrm{link}}+N^2J_m.$$
{{lightweight:reflection}}

With J_m = 10⁻⁴ kg·m², ratios 6, 10, 30, 50 and 100 reflect 0.0036, 0.01, 0.09, 0.25 and 1 kg·m² respectively. A high ratio reduces the motor torque needed to accelerate a given link, but increases the rotor’s contribution to output-side inertia. During an impact, accelerating that rotor can require appreciable joint torque.

取 $J_m=10^{-4}$ kg·m²，减速比 6、10、30、50、100 分别对应 0.0036、0.01、0.09、0.25、1 kg·m² 的反射惯量。高减速比降低加速同一连杆所需的电机转矩，却提高转子在输出侧的惯量贡献；冲击时带动转子加速，可能产生明显的附加关节力矩。

Backdrivability is the ability of an output-side force to drive the transmission backward and move the motor. Slow motion depends strongly on friction, seals, preload and transmission geometry; rapid contact also has to accelerate reflected inertia. A transmission can therefore move readily when pushed slowly yet resist a fast impact. N²J_m explains the inertial part, while friction and efficiency determine additional resistance.

反向驱动性指输出端受力时，能否反过来带动传动和电机运动。缓慢推动时，摩擦、密封、预紧与传动几何影响很大；快速接触时，还要加速反射惯量。因此，一个能被缓慢推动的关节，面对快速冲击仍可能表现出较大阻力。$N^2J_m$ 解释惯性部分，摩擦和传动效率则决定额外阻力。

Quasi-direct drive pairs a high-torque motor with a low reduction ratio. Lower reflected inertia and transmission loss improve mechanical transparency and make current-based torque estimation more useful. A motor current gives electromagnetic torque through the torque constant; output torque still includes transmission loss, acceleration and calibration error. Dedicated torque sensing remains valuable where these errors matter.

准直驱将高转矩电机与低减速比结合，较小的反射惯量和传动损耗改善机械透明性，也使基于电流的力矩估计更有用。电流通过转矩常数给出电磁转矩，输出力矩仍受传动损耗、加速度与标定误差影响；精度要求较高时，独立扭矩传感仍有价值。

关节模组还包括承受外载荷的轴承、维持对准并导热的壳体，以及测量电机侧或输出侧运动的编码器。整个模组近端化可以减少远端惯量；只降低转子惯量，壳体、轴承和传动的质量仍留在原处。Mini Cheetah 执行器设计体现了这种协同，LWR III 机电系统则将轻质连杆、关节扭矩传感与电子系统集成。

Cassie combines compliant legs and leaf springs with deliberate actuator placement. The springs share structural and elastic functions; the motors are not all located in the pelvis. The useful design principle is to assign mass and compliance around the intended motion and contact behavior.

Cassie 将柔顺腿、板簧与执行器布置共同设计。板簧同时承担结构和弹性功能，电机也分布在不同关节，并非全部位于骨盆。其可借鉴之处，是围绕预期运动和接触行为安排质量与柔顺性。

Stiffness, compliance and bandwidth刚度、柔度与控制带宽

Young’s modulus relates stress to strain in the material; structural stiffness relates force to deflection for a particular geometry. For a uniform tensile member k = EA/L. In a linear scalar model, compliance is 1/k; in a multi-axis model it is the inverse of the nonsingular stiffness matrix. For a nonlinear force–displacement curve, tangent stiffness is dF/dx and tangent compliance is dx/dF.

杨氏模量描述材料应力与应变的关系，结构刚度描述特定几何的力与变形关系。均匀拉杆有 $k=EA/L$。线性标量模型中，柔度为 $1/k$；多轴模型中，对应非奇异刚度矩阵的逆。非线性力—位移曲线上的切线刚度为 $dF/dx$，切线柔度为 $dx/dF$。

A single-mode approximation connects stiffness with participating mass:

单模态近似将刚度与参与振动的质量联系起来：

$$\omega_n=\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}},\qquad \frac{\omega_{n,\;m/2}}{\omega_{n,\;m}}=\sqrt2\approx1.414\quad(k\ \mathrm{fixed}).$$

Halving mass with stiffness unchanged raises natural frequency by about 41%. Removing material also lowers stiffness, so that gain is not automatic. Flexible links can meet strength limits yet deflect enough to spoil tool accuracy; their resonances can limit feedback gain and tracking bandwidth. The first relevant structural resonance should be separated from the operating bandwidth, with the needed separation depending on damping, sensing, delay and controller design.

刚度不变、质量减半时，固有频率提高约 41%；去除材料也会降低刚度，因此收益需要同时计算。柔软连杆可能满足强度，却已弯曲到影响工具精度，其共振还会限制反馈增益和跟踪带宽。相关结构共振应与工作带宽保持足够间隔，具体间隔取决于阻尼、测量、延迟与控制器设计。

Contact energy and safety接触能量与安全

$$E_k=\frac12m_{\mathrm{eff}}v^2.$$
{{lightweight:kinetic}}

Effective mass depends on posture, contact point and direction. At a given speed, reducing it lowers the associated kinetic energy; at a fixed energy allowance, lower effective mass permits a higher speed. Doubling speed multiplies energy by four. Collaborative manipulators combine this mechanical advantage with torque sensing and control.

等效质量随姿态、接触点和方向改变。同样速度下，等效质量降低，相应动能降低；给定能量上限时，较小等效质量允许更高速度。速度加倍，动能增加到四倍。协作机械臂将这一机械优势与扭矩传感、控制结合使用。

ISO/TS 15066:2016 describes safety requirements for collaborative industrial robot applications, including power and force limiting. Its biomechanical framework distinguishes body regions and force/pressure limits. Transient contact depends on effective mass and speed; sustained clamping additionally depends on geometry and stopping behavior. Contact area, compliance and the human body also affect peak force, so kinetic energy alone does not establish application safety.

ISO/TS 15066:2016规定协作工业机器人应用的安全要求，包含功率与力限制，并区分身体部位及相应力、压强判据。瞬态接触与等效质量、速度相关，持续夹持还取决于几何和停止行为。接触面积、柔顺性与人体响应共同影响峰值力，应用安全需要结合这些因素评价。
