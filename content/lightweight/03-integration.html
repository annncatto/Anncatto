System (integrated) lightweight methods系统（集成）轻量化方法

System lightweighting combines functions so that fewer parts and interfaces are needed. A component can carry load while also acting as a spring, duct, cable route or heat path; sensors, actuators and electronics can be built into the structure. The two routes complement material, geometry and manufacturing improvements.

系统轻量化通过共用功能，减少必要零件和接口。承力件可以同时充当弹簧、流道、线缆通路或散热路径，传感器、执行器和电子器件也可以集成到结构中。这两条路线与材料、几何和制造改进共同发挥作用。

Active integration主动集成

Active integration brings actuation, sensing and electronics into load-bearing components. Joint modules combine motor, transmission, bearings, encoders, torque sensing and control electronics. Series-elastic actuators (SEA) add a deliberately compliant element; tactile skins distribute contact measurement. DLR LWR III joints and Mini Cheetah actuators illustrate how compact packaging reduces wiring and interfaces while force-aware control supports lighter members.

主动集成将执行器、传感器与电子器件纳入承力构件。关节模组共同封装电机、传动、轴承、编码器、扭矩传感与控制电路；串联弹性执行器（SEA）加入受控的弹性元件，触觉皮肤则分布测量接触。DLR LWR III 关节与 Mini Cheetah 执行器展示了紧凑封装如何减少线束和接口，并由力感知控制支持较轻的连杆。

Vibration treatment can act on excitation, transmission, damping or controlled counterforces. Active vibration control measures the response and applies a force with suitable magnitude and phase. Active noise control uses a secondary acoustic field to reduce sound at selected locations. These mechanisms address different parts of the physical problem: reducing vibration at one structural sensor does not automatically reduce all radiated noise.

减振可以作用于激励、传播、阻尼或主动抵消力。主动减振测量响应，再施加幅值与相位适当的力；主动降噪则通过次级声场，降低指定位置的声音。两者作用于不同物理环节：一个结构测点的振动下降，不必然代表所有辐射噪声都下降。

Morphing structures intentionally change shape or stiffness between operating conditions. Elastic deformation, actuation and locks can work together, for example to permit motion in one phase and carry a larger load in another. The useful comparison spans the full cycle and includes the mass and reliability of the mechanism needed to make that change.

变形结构有意在不同工况间改变形状或刚度。弹性变形、驱动与锁止可以配合工作，例如在一个阶段允许运动，在另一阶段承担较大载荷。比较应覆盖完整循环，也包括实现变化所需机构的质量和可靠性。

A structural battery combines load bearing and electrochemical storage in the material system. A battery enclosure that supports external loads is a first level of integration; making electrodes, electrolyte and separator themselves participate in mechanical load transfer goes further. Structural supercapacitors apply the same principle to high-power storage.

结构电池将承载与电化学储能放进同一材料系统。电池壳体承担外载荷，是封装层面的集成；让电极、电解质与隔膜本身参与机械传力，则进一步进入材料层面。结构超级电容也采用这一原则，偏重短时高功率储能。

Research on multifunctional carbon-fibre structural batteries demonstrates this material-level approach. Its useful system comparison includes the energy and stiffness requirements together. A lower specific energy can still be worthwhile if enough separate structure is removed, while damage to a load-bearing battery also couples mechanical repair to electrical isolation and thermal protection.

多功能碳纤维结构电池研究展示了材料层面的集成。系统比较需要同时满足储能与刚度要求；即使比能量较低，只要能够省去足够的独立结构，也可能有利。承载电池损伤时，机械维修又会与电隔离和热防护联系起来。

LATP, a solid ion-conducting electrolyte, can contribute to mechanical integrity; carbon nanotubes (CNT) provide a large electrically connected surface. A LATP/CNT structural-supercapacitor system achieved a volumetric capacitance of 11.59 mF/cm³. An aerospace system comparison reports 73% lower mass, 78% lower volume and 80% retention of mechanical load-bearing capability compared with separate storage and structural arrangements. These are different measurements: capacitance describes storage response, while the percentages describe the specified system comparison.

LATP 固态离子导体可参与保持机械完整性，碳纳米管（CNT）提供较大的导电接触面积。LATP／CNT 结构超级电容体系实现了 11.59 mF/cm³ 的体积电容；一组航天系统比较中，相对独立储能与承载方案，质量降低 73%、体积降低 78%，机械承载能力保持约 80%。体积电容描述储能响应，三个百分数则对应指定的系统比较。

Electrical actuation and onboard equipment increase storage demand. If secondary aircraft structures also store several kWh, part of the dedicated battery’s support and enclosure mass can be eliminated. Achieving that benefit requires the structure to retain mechanical and electrical function over charge cycles, impact, temperature change and repair. Structural power composites.

动力电气化与机载设备提高储能需求。如果飞机二次结构还能承担数 kWh 储能，就有机会省去部分独立电池的支承与封装质量。兑现这种收益，需要结构在充放电、冲击、温度变化和维修后继续维持机械与电气功能。结构储能复合材料。

Passive integration被动集成

Passive integration lets one geometry serve several mechanical or physical functions. A sealed hollow link can carry load and route cables; a motor housing can be a structural bearing carrier and a heat spreader. A compliant member can both guide motion and store elastic energy. Each example eliminates an interface but introduces coupling: a hot bearing housing can shift its fits, and a load-bearing cable duct must retain adequate section around openings.

被动功能集成让一种几何承担多种机械或物理功能。密封空心连杆可以承载并走线，电机壳体可以同时支承轴承与导热，柔性杆件可以同时导向与储能。省去接口的同时，也会引入耦合：轴承壳体升温会改变配合，承载线槽的开口附近仍需保留足够截面。

Compliance mechanisms use elastic deformation in place of some pin joints. They can reduce backlash, lubrication needs and part count, while shifting the limits toward strain, fatigue and out-of-plane stability. Large rotations require nonlinear geometry. A flexible hinge sized from a small-deflection approximation can reach unexpectedly high local strain over its full travel. Compliant-mechanism design treats deformation as part of the intended motion.

柔顺机构用弹性变形替代部分转动铰链，可以减少回差、润滑需求与零件数量，同时将限制转向应变、疲劳和面外稳定性。大转角需要考虑几何非线性，按小变形近似设计的柔性铰链，在完整行程中可能出现很高的局部应变。柔顺机构设计将变形本身作为预期运动的一部分。

Cassie’s leaf springs participate in both leg structure and elastic energy storage. Atlas integrates hydraulic passages into components, reducing separate routing and connectors. Other combinations include a load-bearing shell that guides cables, a sealed hollow member carrying fluid, or an actuator housing that supports bearings and dissipates heat. These functions work through geometry and material response without an additional feedback loop.

Cassie 的板簧同时参与腿部结构和弹性储能，Atlas 的集成液压通道减少独立管路与接头。类似组合还包括承载外壳走线、密封空心构件输送流体、执行器壳体同时支承轴承和散热。这些功能通过几何与材料响应工作，无需额外反馈控制。

Shared geometry couples the requirements. A cable opening changes the load path; a hot bearing housing changes its fit; a spring used as a link changes both deflection and fatigue. Integration therefore includes interfaces, sealing, heat paths, assembly and repair from the start. Eliminating a cover or fastener is useful only when the remaining structure can take over its function.

共用几何也会耦合要求：走线开口改变传力，轴承壳体升温改变配合，兼作连杆的弹簧同时改变变形和疲劳。接口、密封、导热、装配与维修因此需要一起设计；省去盖板或紧固件时，剩余结构必须接管相应功能。

A one-piece shell can remove fasteners and overlapping flanges, while an integrated frame can combine several former load paths. The saved parts and assembly operations create the benefit; the redesigned shell still needs inspectable load introduction, repair access and a practicable manufacturing sequence. Integration can also make a small defect require replacing a larger unit.

整体壳体能够减少紧固件和重叠法兰，集成框架能够合并原先独立的传力路径。省去的零件与装配工序形成收益；新壳体仍然需要可检测的载荷引入、维修空间与可行工艺。集成也可能让一个小缺陷，要求更换更大的单元。
