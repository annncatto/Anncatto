Shape形状

Section shape and bending efficiency截面形状与抗弯效率

At fixed area, redistributing material changes the second moment. A square reference section has $I_{A,0}=A^2/12$. Define a dimensionless bending shape factor for this reference:

面积固定时，重新分配材料仍然能够改变截面二次矩。以实心正方形为参考，$I_{A,0}=A^2/12$，可以定义无量纲弯曲形状因子：

$$\phi_b=\frac{I_A}{I_{A,0}}=\frac{12I_A}{A^2}.$$

Combining this shape factor with the bending material index gives $M=\sqrt{\phi_bE}/\rho$ when area can scale while the chosen shape remains similar. Material and shape then enter the same expression. The attainable $\phi_b$ is constrained by the available envelope, local stability and manufacturing.

截面积可以缩放、选定形状保持相似时，将形状因子代入抗弯材料指数，可得 $M=\sqrt{\phi_bE}/\rho$。材料与形状进入同一个表达式；空间包络、局部稳定和制造，则限制实际能够获得的 $\phi_b$。

For a circular tube with mean radius $r$ and wall $t$, the exact factor relative to an equal-area solid square is $\phi_b=3(r/t+t/(4r))/\pi$. In the thin-wall limit it approaches $3r/(\pi t)$. Thus $r/t=5$ and $10$ give approximately 4.82 and 9.57, respectively. A solid circle gives $3/\pi\approx0.955$. These compare bending stiffness at equal area and modulus.

圆管的平均半径为 $r$、壁厚为 $t$ 时，相对于等面积实心正方形的精确形状因子为 $\phi_b=3(r/t+t/(4r))/\pi$，薄壁近似为 $3r/(\pi t)$。因此，$r/t=5$ 与 $10$ 分别得到约 4.82 和 9.57；实心圆为 $3/\pi\approx0.955$。它们比较的是面积与模量相同时的抗弯刚度。

{{lightweight:section}}

For the same bending moment, $M_b/I_A$ falls by the reciprocal of $\phi_b$. It is the stress gradient across the section; maximum stress also contains the outer-fibre distance $c$. For the equal-area circular tube with $r/t=10$, $M_b/I_A$ is about 1/9.57 of the square’s value, while $\sigma_{\max}=M_bc/I_A$ is about 1/3.61 because the tube extends farther from the neutral axis. Stiffness efficiency and strength efficiency therefore use different section measures: $I_A$ and section modulus $Z=I_A/c$.

相同弯矩下，$M_b/I_A$ 按 $\phi_b$ 的倒数下降。它表示应力沿截面的变化率；最大应力还包含最外侧距离 $c$。对等面积、$r/t=10$ 的圆管，$M_b/I_A$ 约为实心方形的 1/9.57，最大应力 $\sigma_{\max}=M_bc/I_A$ 则约为 1/3.61，因为圆管的外缘也离中性轴更远。刚度效率和强度效率因此分别使用 $I_A$ 与截面模量 $Z=I_A/c$。

A 40 × 60 mm rectangular tube with 2 mm walls has area 384 mm² and bending second moment 193,152 mm⁴ about its strong axis. A solid square of the same area has side 19.60 mm and second moment 12,288 mm⁴. The tube offers about 15.7 times the bending second moment at equal material volume. It occupies a larger envelope, so the gain is available only if that envelope fits.

40 × 60 mm、壁厚 2 mm 的矩形管，截面积为 384 mm²，强轴截面二次矩为 193,152 mm⁴。相同面积的实心正方形边长为 19.60 mm，二次矩为 12,288 mm⁴。在材料体积相同的条件下，矩形管得到约 15.7 倍的弯曲二次矩，但它占用更大的外包络，空间允许时才能使用这项收益。

Removing material near the neutral axis is efficient in bending. Moving the same material outward increases the section’s surface area, which can help cooling, but it also changes manufacturing, local wall stability and impact resistance. An extremely thin, deep tube may be limited by local buckling or a bolted end fitting before the global beam reaches its nominal stress limit.

弯曲时，移走中性轴附近的材料通常很有效。将材料向外移动，也会增加表面积，可能有利于散热，同时改变制造、局部壁板稳定性与抗冲击能力。非常薄而高的管件，可能先受局部屈曲或螺栓端部接头限制，无法达到整体梁的名义应力极限。

The best section depends on the entire load set. A rectangular tube can favor one bending direction; a circular tube is insensitive to rotation about its axis and carries torsion efficiently. An I-section places material well for one bending plane but has low open-section torsional stiffness. A structural member carrying cables, bearings and off-axis contact forces rarely has a single ideal load.

最佳截面由完整载荷集合决定。矩形管可以偏向一个弯曲方向；圆管绕自身轴旋转时性能不变，抗扭也比较高效；工字截面适合一个主弯曲平面，开口截面的扭转刚度却偏低。携带线缆、轴承并承受偏轴接触力的构件，很少只有一种理想载荷。

Mass inertia remains a separate consideration when the member moves. Distributing material outward may improve bending while increasing inertia about its own longitudinal axis. About a transverse support axis, distance along the member can dominate. Geometry should be judged against the actual load and motion, rather than maximizing one section quantity in isolation.

构件运动时，还要单独考虑质量惯量。材料向外分布，有利于抗弯，也可能增加绕构件纵轴的惯量；绕横向支承轴转动时，沿杆长的距离则可能占主导。几何应对应真实载荷与运动评价，而不是孤立地最大化一个截面量。

A thin wall’s radius-to-thickness ratio r/t is a geometric opportunity and a stability limit. Increasing it moves material outward, increasing I_z for the same area, but makes the wall more sensitive to local buckling, dents and joint loads. The optimal tube therefore balances global bending, local stability, contact and manufacturable minimum thickness.

薄壁的半径壁厚比 $r/t$ 同时代表几何机会和稳定性限制。增大这一比值，在相同面积下将材料外移、提高 $I_z$，也使管壁更容易局部屈曲、凹陷或受接头集中力影响。因此，圆管需要共同满足整体抗弯、局部稳定、接触与可制造最小壁厚要求。

Manufacturing a uniform thin metal wall薄壁金属的加工与均匀性

A continuous tube can begin as drawn, extruded or seamless stock, preserving material around the circumference before machining. A changing section, flange or bearing interface may instead need turning, milling, boring, casting or additive manufacture. Choosing the stock form early changes machining volume, tool access and the thinnest unsupported wall encountered during production.

连续管件可从拉制、挤压或无缝管材开始，在加工前保留周向连续材料；变截面、法兰与轴承接口则可能需要车削、铣削、镗削、铸造或增材制造。尽早确定毛坯形态，会改变切削量、刀具可达性，以及加工过程中最薄的无支承壁。

Boring enlarges or finishes an existing hole around a controlled axis. Uniform wall thickness also requires the bore and outside surface to share an axis: a correct bore diameter can coexist with an eccentric, dangerously thin side. Locate both surfaces from a consistent datum, control clamping distortion, and measure thickness at several angles and axial positions. Roundness, coaxiality and wall thickness answer different inspection questions.

镗削绕受控轴线扩大或精加工已有孔。壁厚均匀还要求内孔与外表面共轴：孔径正确的同时，一侧仍可能因偏心而过薄。内外表面应采用一致基准定位，控制装夹变形，并在多个周向和轴向位置测量壁厚；圆度、同轴度与壁厚分别回答不同的检测问题。

As material is removed, the workpiece becomes more flexible and its vibration characteristics change. Retaining temporary support, leaving finish allowance and shortening the unsupported cutting span can help; excessive clamping force can simply machine a round hole into a temporarily distorted part. Sharp tooling, suitable engagement and controlled overhang reduce cutting-force deflection and chatter. Sandvik Coromant shoulder and thin-wall milling.

去除材料时，工件刚度与振动特性不断改变。保留临时支承和精加工余量、缩短无支承切削区有助于控制变形；过大的夹紧力则可能使工件在变形状态下加工成圆孔，松夹后又恢复失圆。锋利刀具、合适切入量与受控悬伸，有助于减少切削力变形和颤振。Sandvik Coromant 肩铣与薄壁加工。

Deep bores make the tool flexible as well as the workpiece. Short, stiff tool setups or damped boring bars address tool vibration; they do not remove wall eccentricity or residual-stress distortion. Roughing, intermediate stress stabilization where appropriate, and finishing from functional datums separate these problems. Final inspection should be performed after unclamping and thermal stabilization. Sandvik Coromant damped boring.

深孔中，刀具与工件都可能柔软。缩短、加刚刀具系统，或采用阻尼镗杆，可以抑制刀具振动，但不能消除壁厚偏心和残余应力变形。粗加工、适当的中间稳定处理与基于功能基准的精加工，分别处理这些问题；最终检测应在松夹、温度稳定后进行。Sandvik Coromant 阻尼镗削。

Metal additive manufacturing enables integral ribs, curved load paths and internal channels that may be inaccessible to a cutter. Build orientation, supports, powder removal and accessible finishing surfaces must be designed with the geometry. Thermal gradients create residual stress and distortion; a printed near-net shape may still need heat treatment and machining of bearing seats and datums. NIST residual-stress measurements and modelling.

金属增材制造可以实现刀具难以进入的整体筋条、曲面传力路径与内流道。构建方向、支撑、清粉通道和可精加工表面，需要与几何一起设计。热梯度会产生残余应力和变形，打印后的近净外形仍可能需要热处理，以及轴承座和基准面的切削加工。NIST 残余应力测量与建模。

Example: material selection for an upper-arm tube例题：上臂圆管选材

Consider a stiffness-limited, 0.35 m cantilever tube. First fix its outside diameter at 60 mm and allow wall thickness to vary; then fix thickness and allow diameter to vary. Rank Al 6061, Ti-6Al-4V and quasi-isotropic CFRP by minimum mass, using the same required bending rigidity.

考虑长 0.35 m、刚度受限的悬臂圆管。首先固定外径 60 mm、允许壁厚变化，再固定壁厚、允许直径变化；在相同所需抗弯刚度下，按最小质量对 Al 6061、Ti-6Al-4V 与准各向同性 CFRP 排序。

For outside diameter D and wall t, d = D−2t. The exact annulus properties and thin-wall approximations are:

外径为 $D$、壁厚为 $t$ 时，内径 $d=D-2t$。圆环的精确几何量与薄壁近似为：

$$I_z=\frac\pi{64}\big[D^4-(D-2t)^4\big]\approx\frac{\pi D^3t}{8},\qquad A=\frac\pi4\big[D^2-(D-2t)^2\big]\approx\pi Dt.$$

The tip deflection δ = FL³/(3EI_z) fixes EI_z when F, L and δ are given. With D fixed, I_z varies linearly with t: Et is constant, so t ∝ 1/E and m ≈ ρπDtL ∝ ρ/E. The index is E/ρ.

端部挠度 $\delta=FL^3/(3EI_z)$ 表明，$F$、$L$ 与 $\delta$ 给定时，需要相同的 $EI_z$。固定 $D$ 后，$I_z$ 近似随 $t$ 线性变化，因此 $Et$ 为常数，$t\propto1/E$，$m\approx\rho\pi DtL\propto\rho/E$，指数为 $E/\rho$。

With t fixed, I_z varies with D³: ED³ is constant, D ∝ E⁻¹ᐟ³ and m ∝ ρ/E¹ᐟ³. The index is E¹ᐟ³/ρ. The second moment is included in both derivations; eliminating the allowed dimension produces the different exponents.

固定 $t$ 后，$I_z$ 近似随 $D^3$ 变化，因此 $ED^3$ 为常数，$D\propto E^{-1/3}$，$m\propto\rho/E^{1/3}$，指数为 $E^{1/3}/\rho$。两次推导都包含截面二次矩，消去不同的自由尺寸后才得到不同幂次。

Use an Al 6061 reference of D = 60 mm and t = 2 mm. Take E = 68.9, 113.8 and 50 GPa, and ρ = 2700, 4430 and 1550 kg/m³ for aluminium, titanium and quasi-isotropic CFRP respectively. Thin-wall sizing gives:

以外径 60 mm、壁厚 2 mm 的 Al 6061 为基准。铝、钛与准各向同性 CFRP 分别取 $E=68.9,113.8,50$ GPa，$\rho=2700,4430,1550$ kg/m³。按薄壁近似计算：

At fixed diameter the order is CFRP, titanium, aluminium, with titanium and aluminium almost tied. At fixed thickness it becomes CFRP, aluminium, titanium. Enlarging diameter improves bending leverage more efficiently than adding thickness, favoring lower density. Joint dimensions, impact resistance, thermal conduction or cost can change the final selection. Actual CFRP sizing uses its laminate bending properties; the estimates above also require the thin-wall approximation to remain suitable.

固定外径时，排序为 CFRP、钛、铝，其中钛与铝几乎并列；固定壁厚时，排序变为 CFRP、铝、钛。增大直径比增加壁厚更高效地提供抗弯力臂，因此低密度材料获益更多。接头尺寸、抗冲击、导热或成本都可能改变最终选择。实际 CFRP 采用层合板弯曲性能验证，上述估算还要求薄壁近似保持适用。

Using only M_z/I_z would compare stress gradients. Multiplying by the outer radius gives maximum stress; dividing by E gives curvature. The stated example limits deflection, so EI_z determines its index. A strength-limited redesign instead begins with M_z/Z ≤ σ_allow and may rank the materials differently.

只比较 $M_z/I_z$，得到的是应力梯度；乘外缘半径得到最大应力，除以 $E$ 才得到曲率。此题限制挠度，因此由 $EI_z$ 推导指数；改为强度受限时，应从 $M_z/Z\le\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$ 出发，材料排序也可能改变。
