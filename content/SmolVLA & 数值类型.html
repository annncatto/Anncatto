同一个玩偶收集任务，120 条示教训练出的 SmolVLA 已经能够完成抓取与投放；补充数据继续训练后，却出现了漏抓、后退过大和原有表现下降。换用 π0.5 后，实机动作看起来更连贯。这些变化把三个问题连在一起：视觉信息怎样进入动作生成、微小的参数更新能否真正写入权重，以及预测出的动作怎样按时送到机器人。

On the same toy-collection task, a SmolVLA trained on 120 demonstrations could grasp and deposit toys. Continued training with additional data brought missed grasps, excessive retreats, and poorer behavior in familiar settings. A later π0.5 run looked more continuous. These observations connect three questions: how visual information reaches action generation, whether small updates actually reach stored weights, and how predicted actions arrive at the robot on time.
