## Images and computations

Official Ansys Granta charts retain their copyright marks and original property definitions. DLR provides the FML image; NTPT / SuCoHS provides the thin-ply slide; Greenhalgh et al. provides the Ragone chart under CC BY 4.0. Credits are placed at the figures.

The four introductory relationship diagrams preserve the source nodes, routing and feedback loops as bilingual SVG. The cost curve is qualitative. Scientific curves are evaluated from their stated equations. The simply supported SIMP example is computed with Q4 plane-stress elements; its PNG stages, animated GIF and equilibrium/volume history are generated by `scripts/figures/lightweight-topology.py`.

## Corrections retained

- Compliance is the reciprocal of scalar linear stiffness; nonlinear tangent compliance is dx/dF.
- Reflected rotor inertia is N²Jm at the output; backdrivability also depends on friction and transmission geometry.
- Cassie does not place every motor in the pelvis.
- Surface metal foil improves the reported specific energy absorption; this is not an interlaminar shear-strength claim.
- Material indices depend on the geometric variable allowed to change. The thin-tube exercise distinguishes fixed diameter from fixed wall thickness.
- The SIMP density is dimensionless and distinct from material mass density.
